# Names

One thing, one name.

**Qualify on the axis that separates the set — enough words to be unique, no more.** Two displays
in two places take the place: `faucet display`, `enclosure display`. Several plates sharing one
place take the position: `mounting plate`, `under-counter plate`. There is one gooseneck, so
`gooseneck` stands alone.

**A file's path is a qualifier.** Under `hardware/printed-parts/faucet/`, `the shell` is the
faucet's and needs no prefix. Anywhere a reader can arrive at one line without the rest — chat, a
README spanning areas, a commit message — the term carries its qualifier.

**A glossary entry is the full name.** The bare short form is what the path licenses, not what the
entry says: `faucet shell` is the name, and `the shell` is correct inside
`hardware/printed-parts/faucet/` and nowhere else.

The **Not** column is the routing rule: those forms are retired, and the name beside them is what
they became.
