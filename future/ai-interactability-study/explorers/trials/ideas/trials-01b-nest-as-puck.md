# trials-01b-nest-as-puck: the smallest version of the puck

Branch of `trials-01-puck-swap` (no scene of its own; the puck scene's branch radio, "tube only (current nest)", shows the present state).

## Picture it

Nothing new is added to the rig except three ball-and-dowel seats where the nest's three M3 screws are today. The nest is already a replaceable part with an ID pilot and an OD guide and three radial adjusters **[repo]**; now it can be lifted off with its tube in it and another nest set down in its place.

## The proposal

Keep the nest exactly as designed. Replace the three M3 screws that fix it to the turntable by three balls under the nest and three dowel pairs in the turntable, with magnets. Everything else in trials-01 (seat certificate, phase from asymmetric spacing, index magnet, tag, prep stand) is optional and can come later. The gain is the swap of a *nest* (with its tube already indicated) instead of a tube in a screwed-down nest.

## Carries, locates, free

The turntable carries the nest and tube through the seats; the nest's own datums (0.20 mm pilot, 0.40 mm guide, three adjusters) still locate the tube in it.

## Software

Same as trials-01 but without the extras: rotator speed; the judge cameras see the tube-in-nest eccentricity. Manual: the lift; indicating at a prep stand.

## Tried to break it

1. **It changes the turntable.** Conflict: the study does not change current hardware. Assumption: this is idea-level only. What it leaves: the turntable's 3 M3 wells in the pilot and the register socket under the nest **[repo]** are load-bearing details; the seats must not undo them.
2. **A second nest is a second nest.** Each puck needs its own nest, adjusters and shim history. Leaves: cost in print time only.
3. **No index, no tag.** Without the extras the software cannot tell which nest it is. Leaves: a visible label the camera reads is the cheapest replacement.

## Unresolved

Q: Would you want a second and third nest, or would a lift-out of the tube from one nest do? (Ten re-seats with the indicator settle it.)
