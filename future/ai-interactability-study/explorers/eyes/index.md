# eyes: Seeing first (index of every arrangement held)

Framing: begin from what can be observed about the dot, the seam, the gun's pose, the tube and the wire (cameras, markers, light, touch, electrical continuity, sound, inertial sensors): what can be seen from where, what is hidden, which measurements are direct and which inferred. Nothing here is ranked or scored. Maturity is depth of work, not a verdict. "Lens" marks a scene that is an analysis, not one arrangement.

## Questions that need Derek's observation, by idea

- **eyes-01:** hold a phone about 10 cm from the nozzle looking at the corner past the wire, red dot on: does the corner line show? Is the dot visible on the plate? Does the red dot stay on while the trigger is held? (wave 3) Does a green line look like a kink to your own eye from where you stand? What is the umbilical's pull along and across its exit at three poses (a spring scale), and does the support move when the wire feeds or the gas starts (a dial gauge on the shell, laser disabled)?
- **eyes-02:** from about 25 cm to the side of the tube and 6 cm above the rim, can he see the red dot in the corner at all?
- **eyes-03:** can a printed cube of PETG or PA-CF stand 5 cm from the nozzle during a bead without warping?
- **eyes-04:** is the copper nozzle removable, and what does the work-contact circuit connect to at the gun end?
- **eyes-05:** does the yellow Ready LED change when the nozzle touches the tube with the laser off? What closes the work-contact circuit at the gun end? (wave 3) Does a steel ball at a fraction of a newton mark the inside of a 316L bore?
- **eyes-06b:** with the dot a few mm short of the wall, is there a second red spot on the wall?
- **eyes-07:** on a sawn half-tube, how much of the corner does the dot show along the tangent?
- **eyes-09:** does the gun's support or shell move when you jog the wire or open the gas, and when emitting starts? (the number the scene's bead-start step asks for)
- **eyes-11b:** the umbilical's pull at three poses (as eyes-01); how does it hang in the current hand-held use?
- **eyes-13:** a photo of the dot on the plate at 30 cm: how big is the spot, does it centroid to a tenth of a millimetre?
- **eyes-14:** what does the tail actuator hold (a point or a tangent) and what rod is meant? With the trigger held does the red dot stay on?
- **eyes-15:** which side does he stand and which hand holds the gun?
- **eyes-16:** a phone photo from across the bore, 10 to 15 degrees above the rim: is the plate-wall corner a clean edge on stainless? Plate seat depth on a dozen tubes, rim to plate face.
- **eyes-17 and eyes-18:** which side do you stand on and which hand holds the gun; do you wear eyewear that passes a green or an amber LED near the nozzle; would you accept a wall you can always push through at about 2 N; how far does a relaxed hand yield per newton at the grip; does your hand slow down or stop when the gun resists; which would you rather have wrong, a light you can look away from or a wall you can feel? A phone video of the dot against the corner during a real hand-held bead gives the hand's speed, tremor and how it steers.
- **eyes-19:** where would a stylus fit beside the nozzle in the canyon; beep, lamp or click?
