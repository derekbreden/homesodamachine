# room: every arrangement held (end of wave 3)

Framing: **the room is the first stage.** Positioning begins in the space the gun and rotator already occupy: bench, table, opening, wall, ceiling, floor, shelf, frame, rails. The coarse stage may be furniture or building; the fine stage is whatever is left. Nothing here is ranked; maturity is depth only. Questions that need Derek's observation are in each idea file under "Unresolved problems"; the short list at the end points to them.

Wave 2 partner: **use**. Wave 3: my answers to eyes are in `exchange/room--reply-to-eyes-w3.md`; the new direction (the work in another orientation, and the station around it) is room-16 to room-18.
