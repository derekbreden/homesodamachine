## What the wave found, in a few lines

- Every handover shifts the gun, and a look taken before the last one does not see it. With illustrative numbers travel-05b's clamp leaves 81 percent of closures inside +-0.10 mm with no look after it and 95 percent with one; a mean shift can be learned from logged closures, a scatter cannot (`use-14`).
- An arm may let go of soft supports only at zero arm force, and a soft support cannot hold the weld: 1 N moves a 0.15 N/mm bungee 6.7 mm (`use-14`, Derek's suspension).
- In travel-01 the fine Z (+-6 mm) is larger than the swap margin (badge red from Z = +2 mm in their own scene), the end of a bead has no owner, and one closure needs two approaches (`use-13`).
- Parking the gun is a state that costs each of Derek's examples something different, and none of them touches the hand's minutes at the tube (`use-15`).
- The written sequence engages the ground shoe after indicating: a centring done shoe-off is not the weld state's centring; presetting frees the station, a driver frees the hand (`use-17`).
- The lift-away at the end of a bead is not in guide 46 although several files cite it there: every escape idea is conditional on it.

## Questions that need Derek's observation
