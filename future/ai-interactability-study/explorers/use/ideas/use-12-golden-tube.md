# use-12-golden-tube: a reference tube runs the whole cycle every morning

No scene yet (arrangement A10 of the notebook). Origin: swarm. Maturity: sketch. A day-scale idea that the lanes scene does not draw.

## Picture it

Before any real tube of the day, a scrap tube with a scrap plate, kept for the purpose, is seated, indicated, and run through the dry cycle with the same cables and hoses. Its dot-versus-seam trace is compared with the one stored the last time everything was known to be good. The comparison says, in numbers, what moved overnight: the gun's shell on the arm, the seat, the camera, the cable's set, the nest screws.

## The proposal

Make drift visible before it costs a tube. A **golden tube** stays in its case, is the same in every session, and runs the full dry sequence first. The AI (or the person) compares today's trace and readings with the baseline: rim height on the gauge, indicated TIR, seat continuity, the seat-to-seat spread, the dot's mean offset against the printed or scribed seam, the rotator's degrees against a mark. Differences beyond what repeated runs on the same day show are named as *the campaign's pose changed*: re-trim before welding.

It is a calibration-artefact idea in the sense of another explorer's `trials-05-artefact-ladder` (known to me only by name; not read); what this framing adds is that it is a **step in the day**, at a fixed time, with an output that gates the first real tube.

## What carries the loads, what establishes position, what is free or restrained

Nothing new. The golden tube sits in the nest like any tube; what it establishes is a reference for the whole chain (nest, register, gun support, seat, camera) against its own previous record.

## What software could command, observe, and what stays manual

- **Command:** the same as the arrangement it is used with (a swing head runs its cycle; a hand-held day has no command).
- **Observe:** the day-start record and its difference from the baseline.
- **Manual:** seating the golden tube, and deciding what to do with a difference.

## What was tried to break it

1. **The golden tube is a real tube.** *Conflict:* it is reused, so it ages: scuffed plate, warped nest, rust. *Assumption:* it stays the same. *Change:* a second sealed copy kept unused, compared with occasionally; scrap it when the two disagree. *Leaves:* how often.
2. **One tube does not span the tube-to-tube spread.** *Conflict:* the golden tube is one tube's length and roundness; real tubes vary. *Assumption:* drift is the same for every tube. *Change:* it checks the *station*, not the spread; the spread is checked per tube (`use-03-preset-cartridge`). *Leaves:* the spread is unknown (measure ten tubes).
3. **Time.** *Conflict:* a full cycle each morning costs minutes. *Change:* only the dry states run (no laser); with a swing head (`use-02-swing-head`) they run unattended under policy C of `use-05-gates`; on a hand-held day it is a five minute dry lap with the camera. *Leaves:* the time.
4. **What counts as a difference.** *Conflict:* without a repeated-run noise floor any difference looks meaningful. *Change:* run the golden cycle three times on the first day to get a spread, and treat only differences larger than it as change. *Leaves:* nothing.
