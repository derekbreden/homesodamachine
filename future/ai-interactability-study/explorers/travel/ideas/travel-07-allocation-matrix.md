# travel-07-allocation-matrix: who supplies each relative motion

**Picture it.** A table. Rows are arrangements: Derek's monitor arm, his hole-in-the-table gantry, his suspension of rings, wire and bungees, then the twelve arrangements of this notebook. Columns are the relative motions at the dot. Each cell is coloured by who supplies the motion (motor, hand, spring, gravity, a seat, a lock) and says in a few words what does it. A dotted blank means the source did not name that motion. Flip between setup and weld and the arm's cells change from hand to locked.

Scene: `scenes/travel-07-allocation-matrix` (a lens, not an arrangement).

## The proposal

Treat every arrangement as an allocation of relative motion between gun, tube and room, and make the allocation visible so blanks become questions. It applies Derek's own remark that the monitor arm and the table were illustrations of how much room there is even within positioning the gun.

The columns are: radial (into the wall), plan angle / tangent, vertical, pitch (hole axis), beam roll (grip axis), swap / retract, and what carries the cables. Two motions are special. Along-seam translation is redundant (the rotator supplies it), so it has no column; plan angle can be produced by a tangent shift of the work at 1/r (`travel-01`).

## What carries the loads, what establishes position, what is free

One column names what carries the cables; the details panel for the selected row names what carries the gun and what locates. Setup / weld toggles which cells are locked.

## What software could command, observe, what stays manual

Nothing is commanded. The details panel lists, for the selected row, what software could command and observe.

## What was tried to break it

**1. A table can pretend to rank.** *Conflict:* colour and density can read as quality. *Change:* the only encodings are who supplies the motion; there is no score column, and the "highlight" filter dims cells, it does not sort. *Leaves:* a viewer may still read fullness as merit.

**2. Blank cells.** *Conflict:* Derek's gantry and suspension rows have unnamed cells because he did not describe them, not because the arrangement lacks them. *Change:* they are dotted and labelled "not named". *Leaves:* filling them is the work of other scenes.

**Wave 3 entry, from use's exchange ("Also noticed", `use-15-examples-as-days`).** **The matrix has columns for motions and only one state axis (use).** *Conflict:* setup versus weld is the only state axis; states have columns too (park, escape, snip). *Assumption behind it:* two states. *What the change alters:* a PARK column (the recess open for indicating, seating the plate and the swap) fills three cells the blanks marked "not named": parking is free in the table opening (the hand works under the table), a swing held by a friction window on the monitor arm, and a lift of the whole hanging assembly in the suspension; rows T1b, T13, T14 and T15 (this wave's arrangements) are added, and the scene is tagged a lens. `use-15` draws the eleven state columns. *What it leaves uncertain:* the cell wording is mine.
