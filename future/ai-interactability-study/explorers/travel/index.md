# travel: split the travel. Every arrangement held (through wave 3)

Framing: the relative pose between gun, tube and room can be produced by moving the gun, the tube, a support or a setup adjustment; by motor, hand, spring or gravity; at setup or during the weld. A small angle at the grip is a long travel at the dot. Nothing here is ranked or scored; depth is depth of work only. No budget applies.

## What the wave found, in a few lines

- The crown's seat decides its radial error: pads that centre the ring pass the tube's out-of-round at gain 1 (three pads pass ovality); pads soft enough to seat are as stiff as the ring; lock after seating. The n=1 offset of the outside from the bore passes every seat.
- A stiff constraint on the free axis costs nothing at the seam, but a tab tether turns a torque into a force 3.07 times the drag on the seat; a wire pair wrapped on the ring is a couple.
- The signature's replay is only as good as the hold between the dry turn and the weld turn: change of force times compliance (6 to 128 micron per newton); a second dry turn measures it. The constants are two thirds of the rms; against a +-0.30 mm window a static trim is nearly the whole job.
- A touch made with the stage that then applies it is a null measurement; the work moving into a fixed stylus answers datum-07's "what moves the tip", and the stylus-to-dot vector is the real largest term.
- Removing the wire from the gun frees a family of attitudes, not yaw; it does not fix the orbiting cable (the exit direction is the same), but it allows a weld either way round, which does (3.4 m against 5.8).
- A wedge under the base pivots at the bench, 232 mm below the dot; the station azimuth chooses which rotation it supplies.

## What wave 3 found, in a few lines

- Answering use: a drop tier under the fine Z on a three-ball seat, its bottom-stop switch the shuttle interlock (`travel-19`); the clamp and the crown's lock are handovers with a closing shift, and the last look comes after them (`travel-05b`, `travel-14`); the arm can dock, float, read and re-teach (`travel-22`); the shoe's push is trimmed and only its turning part matters (`travel-06`); the lift-away at the end of a bead is unconfirmed and every escape slider starts at 0.
- New direction (bought arms and positioners): the tube's rotation makes the seam's whole path, so an arm is asked for one pose, held; the datasheet's repeatability is the return figure the closure uses, and stiffness at the flange, lost motion and drift, which no datasheet states, are what the weld needs. Where the base stands decides less than expected (61 to 100 micrometres for the same 0.01 degree per joint); the wrist has the gun's length as its lever; a SCARA on the radial line has two joints whose error costs nothing; joint resolution is a floor, not the problem (19 bits 0.4 to 3 micrometres, 12 bits 0.1 to 0.5 mm); a coarse arm with a fine stack under the work separates the two jobs. No Prime listing carries the gun (payloads 0.25 to 0.5 kg); the two 3 kg arms found are sold by their makers.
