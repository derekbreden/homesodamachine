**Prime rule.** Every Amazon entry below appeared under Amazon's "All Prime" delivery filter (search URL parameter `rh=p_85:2470955011`) and its product page carried the Prime logo element (`a-icon-prime`) beside the buy box. The buy-box text itself prints the delivery promise ("FREE delivery Tomorrow, September 29") and "Ships from ..."; it does not print the word Prime. Non-Prime listings were not read.

**Volume evidence** is what the page shows: rating count, "bought in past month". A familiar-looking product or a listing's existence is not evidence of volume; entries with few ratings say so.

Prices are listing prices on that day, not quotes. Delivery is the date the page offered.
