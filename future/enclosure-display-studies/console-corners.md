# Console corner controls

[`console-corners.html`](console-corners.html) places Settings at the bottom of the left flavor
rail and Done at the top-right of each task. Fill, Prime and Clean remain in the top navigation.
The resting “On tap.” screen reserves the same top-right space without showing a Done control.

The flavor rail contains two picture choices and a persistent 104 × 64 Settings button with a
36-pixel gear. The rail has no heading. Both choices and the large selected image keep their
positions across every screen; “✓ Selected” appears below the large image.

Quiet's resting body and image picker retain their layout, colors and spacing. The picker shows
four customer images followed by four factory images, with Previous, Next and position feedback.
Selecting an image updates the chosen flavor immediately. Done returns to the resting screen.
The review controls can change the completion label between Done and Close.

Settings highlights the bottom-left gear. Pump service retains its Settings back action; Done
returns directly to the resting screen. During an operation, Stop stays available while Done,
Settings and flavor switching are disabled. All operation states are local mock interactions.

Browser review covers all eight screens: image and control bounds, fixed gear and Done positions,
task completion, Settings access, image paging and assignment, and operation navigation controls.
The functional and artwork basis is described in [Console completion](console-completion.md).
