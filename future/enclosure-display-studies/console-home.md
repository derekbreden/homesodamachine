# Console Home

[`console-home.html`](console-home.html) contains four iterations of Console's Home and middle
image column. The top navigation is Home, Fill, Prime and Clean. Each iteration retains the
Console action panes for Fill, Prime and Clean.

| Iteration | Home | Middle column | Settings access |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Quiet | A short heading, restrained reservoir reading, image and ratio links | Image without captions | Persistent gear in the top bar |
| Reservoir | Four large discrete level segments, image and ratio links | One “Selected” label above the image | Persistent button below the flavor choices |
| Simple list | Reservoir, Change image, Settings, and a smaller Mixing ratio row | “Your drink” above the image | Home row |
| Small footer | Pouring prompt, restrained level, image and Settings buttons, small tappable ratio | Dark image column with a lower selection label | Home button |

The ratio number appears on Home only in Small footer. Every iteration exposes the full ratio
control when Ratio is selected. Both flavor selectors and the large current image retain their
positions across pages and selections.

## Image selection

Change image opens four portrait previews in the action pane. Choosing a picture assigns it to
the current flavor and updates its selector thumbnail, large image and faucet preview together.
The sample library contains two compositions of each of the two brand marks used in the
[logo studies](README.md#the-image-as-identity). Add images in the app opens a short description
of the existing phone workflow: choose, fit to the faucet preview, send, then select on the machine.

## Machine settings

Settings shows illustrative states of ten reed switches: four for each flavor reservoir and two
for the carbonator. Pump service opens the instruction to place a container under the faucet and
dry the lines before pulling the pump cartridge. Drying and Stop are local mock states.

Image and ratio controls remain flavor controls. The machine Settings area contains system
status and pump service, matching the current enclosure firmware's functions.

## Review

Browser review covers Home, image selection, ratio, Settings, pump service, image-upload
instructions, Fill, Prime and Clean. Checks cover control bounds and size, stable image
positions, image assignment through both displays, Settings access and all four navigation tabs.
The Fill, Prime and Clean pane markup matches the accepted Console panes in every iteration.

The functional basis and artwork sources are in the [logo studies](README.md#basis-and-scope).
