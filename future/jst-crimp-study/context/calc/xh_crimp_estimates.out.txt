========================================================================
4. Crimp force, bounded
========================================================================
conductor barrel at bottom of stroke: 0.75 to 2.30 kN
insulation barrel wing forming: 33 to 132 N
carrier cut-off shear: 48 to 158 N
peak total, one stroke doing both barrels (+cut-off at high end): 0.79 to 2.59 kN
design figure for a machine: plan on 3 kN at bottom dead centre, 4-5 kN capacity

Where in the stroke the force is needed [estimate]:
  conductor punch travel from first touch of wing tips to bottom ~0.92 mm
  first ~70%: wings curl inward along the punch arch -> tens to a few hundred N
  last ~0.10-0.20 mm: strands compact and bronze coins -> force climbs to peak at BDC
  energy per crimp at 1.5 kN peak ~0.27 J (tiny; any slow drive with mechanical advantage suffices)
  energy per crimp at 3.0 kN peak ~0.38 J (tiny; any slow drive with mechanical advantage suffices)
  position accuracy that matters: BDC height to ~+/-0.02-0.05 mm (sets crimp height);
  the rest of the stroke can be sloppy

5. What slow actuators deliver (sanity check, [calc] with assumed parts)

  NEMA23 ~1.5 N*m, 2 mm lead screw, trapezoid eta 0.3: 1.4 kN at stall torque

  NEMA23 ~1.5 N*m, 5:1 belt, 4 mm ball screw eta 0.85: 10.0 kN at stall torque
