Derek's signed-in Chrome. No amazon.com page was fetched to make this list.

opened. In the idea files these parts are written "Amazon, Prime to be
confirmed". Non-Amazon sources (Digi-Key, LCSC, Harbor Freight, eBay, ATO,
Würth) are cited in the idea files with the date observed, 2026-09-28.
