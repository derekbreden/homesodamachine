==============================================================================

  ram  6 mm above BDC: upstroke 236.8 deg, the same height on the downstroke at 123.2 deg
  ram 15 mm above BDC: upstroke 274.3 deg, the same height on the downstroke at  85.7 deg
  ram 20 mm above BDC: upstroke 293.4 deg, the same height on the downstroke at  66.6 deg
  pre-feed advance at 15-20 mm up on the upstroke [b1b assumption] -> 274-293 deg (b1c's table: ~266-285);

==============================================================================

     pull; 20 N there is an unrated retention test on the part that must later latch [xh-facts §3].
