1. Post capture at the box entry
==============================================================================
The pin passes the box's formed entry (0.60-0.70 mm on clone drawings), not the box inside.
[w2 5] used the inside (~1.45 x 1.80) and gave +/-0.41 / +/-0.58; that figure is withdrawn.
  entry 0.60, pin tip chamfer 0.10/side (flat 0.44): capture +/-0.08 mm
  entry 0.60, pin tip chamfer 0.20/side (flat 0.24): capture +/-0.18 mm
  entry 0.60, pin tip chamfer 0.28/side (flat 0.08): capture +/-0.26 mm
  entry 0.70, pin tip chamfer 0.10/side (flat 0.44): capture +/-0.13 mm
  entry 0.70, pin tip chamfer 0.20/side (flat 0.24): capture +/-0.23 mm
  entry 0.70, pin tip chamfer 0.28/side (flat 0.08): capture +/-0.31 mm
Where the pin must arrive, for a head steered by the backlit outline of the contact:
  axis +/-0.02, outline fit +/-0.02, entry-to-outline +/-0.03 -> +/-0.04 mm (rss)
  axis +/-0.05, outline fit +/-0.03, entry-to-outline +/-0.03 -> +/-0.07 mm (rss)
-> +/-0.04-0.07 mm needed against +/-0.08-0.31 captured: a 0.20-0.28 mm chamfer (near-pointed
   tip) keeps a margin of ~3x. What breaks the margin is height, not aim (section 2).

2. A bare contact lying barrels-up: rest on the lance, and the lance groove
==============================================================================
The lance hangs 0.6-0.9 mm below the floor side, its tip 2.24-2.64 mm behind the front; the
centre of mass is ~2.75 mm behind the front [ith-w3 E], so on a flat floor the contact rocks:
  nose-up 8-16 deg (box-front floor 0.9-1.7 mm high) or nose-down 13-22 deg [ith-w3 E].
  0.9-1.7 mm is 4-20x the capture of section 1: the pin meets the box face, not the entry.
A groove along the whole channel floor, under the lance:
  groove 0.8 wide, box 1.85: box floor rests on ledges 0.53 mm each side; lance 0.5-0.8 clears by 0.00-0.15/side
  groove 0.8 wide, box 1.90: box floor rests on ledges 0.55 mm each side; lance 0.5-0.8 clears by 0.00-0.15/side
  groove 1.0 wide, box 1.85: box floor rests on ledges 0.43 mm each side; lance 0.5-0.8 clears by 0.10-0.25/side
  groove 1.0 wide, box 1.90: box floor rests on ledges 0.45 mm each side; lance 0.5-0.8 clears by 0.10-0.25/side
  depth >= 1.0 mm clears a 0.9 mm lance by >= 0.1 mm.
  With the floor down flat, the entry centre stands at floor + box height / 2:
    box 2.20 tall: entry centre 1.10 mm above the floor (lot spread +/-0.05 [estimate])
    box 2.35 tall: entry centre 1.18 mm above the floor (lot spread +/-0.05 [estimate])
-> the pin's height is set from the channel floor to ~+/-0.05 mm, inside the capture.
   The same groove belongs in every bare-contact pocket: a6's pocket plate, x1's spear
   block, a4's loading nest, the shuttle nests of a4b, a5, x3 and a4c.

3. The push after a crimp at or outside the housing, and where it is stored
==============================================================================
Seated box front 6.75-6.95 mm inside the rear face (housing 7.75 [mfr] - front wall (0.8, 1.0) [assumption]); same as [ith-w3 A]
  a5 staged 1.0 mm: push after the crimp 5.75-5.95 mm
  a5 staged 1.5 mm: push after the crimp 5.25-5.45 mm
  a5 staged 2.0 mm: push after the crimp 4.75-4.95 mm
  a4b post 9.0 proud, box on 1.5: box front 7.5 outside -> push 14.25-14.45 mm
  a4b post 9.0 proud, box on 1.8: box front 7.2 outside -> push 13.95-14.15 mm
One web clamp, square-cut ends, each contact seated taut: a contact crimped T short of its
seat needs its conductor T longer at the crimp. As a shallow arch over span L, sag ~sqrt(3LT/8):
  a5, 1.5 staged   T  5.35: sag L 20:  6.3, L 25:  7.1, L 30:  7.8, L 40:  9.0 mm
  a4b, 9 mm post   T 14.20: sag L 20: 10.3, L 25: 11.5, L 30: 12.6, L 40: 14.6 mm
  [ith-w3 B] gives the clamped hump: 7-8 mm (a5) and 12-13 mm (a4b), tightest radius 2.6-5.7 mm,
  past the strands' ~67 mm yield radius, so the hump keeps its shape and the push draws it
  straight with a few hundredths of a newton.
If the housing steps in X under a fixed web after the first latch, seated conductors drag:
  XHP-4: up to 7.5 mm sideways -> the web clamp rides the housing's stage
  XHP-5: up to 10.0 mm sideways -> the web clamp rides the housing's stage
  XHP-7: up to 15.0 mm sideways -> the web clamp rides the housing's stage
  XHP-9: up to 20.0 mm sideways -> the web clamp rides the housing's stage
Or the web follows each push: the web clamp's Y advances T with the fork, so conductor k is
straight at its crimp; every seated conductor bows by T while the web is forward, and straightens
when it returns. Strand strain in that bow, and bending cycles to failure (Coffin-Manson,
eps_f' 0.3-0.6, c -0.6 [estimate]):
  tightest radius 2.6 mm: strand strain 1.5 % -> ~224-712 bows (a housing asks at most 8)
  tightest radius 5.7 mm: strand strain 0.7 % -> ~830-2634 bows (a housing asks at most 8)
  -> fatigue is not what limits it; the waiting conductors are: they advance T too, and must wait
     lifted above the housing's top so they do not reach its rear face.
A single push after every crimp stores nothing: every conductor gets its T at once (x3, a4c).
Any Y stagger between neighbours at the crimp is stored in the finished loom by that push
(the housing seats the nearer contacts first and then carries them on), so all contacts of
one housing are crimped at one Y when one move seats them.

8. a4c: bare contacts crimped on a post bed through the housing, one push
==============================================================================
Posts 0.64 mm square steel, cantilevered from a bed behind the mating face, through the
housing (front opening loose, cavity larger than the post), standing ~9 mm out of the rear face:
  free length 17 mm: 1.71 N/mm sideways; tip-loaded Euler buckling 24 N (push-on 0.2-2 N)
  free length 19 mm: 1.22 N/mm sideways; tip-loaded Euler buckling 19 N (push-on 0.2-2 N)
  -> soft: the die centres the barrels for 0.2-0.3 N of post bending; the push-on is safe.
Where the dies work, relative to the post tips (Y from the tip, + away from the housing):
  box -1.5 to +0.5; lance tip +0.9 to +1.1; conductor barrel +0.9 to +2.6; insulation barrel ~+3.0 to +4.5 mm
  empty neighbour posts end at 0.0, so an anvil whose front edge stands behind the lance
  (>= +1.0) never meets them: in the odd pass the dies' width is limited only by the waiting
  conductors, which wait lifted.
Loading nest for the even pass, between crimped neighbours whose boxes stand at +/-2.5:
  neighbour box edge 1.525 mm from the station axis -> a tongue nest <= 2.75 mm wide
  (odd pass: bare neighbour posts at 2.5 - 0.32 = 2.18 -> a walled nest <= 4.06 wide)
Housing travel: 13.95-14.45 mm, once, for every contact (section 3; [ith-w3 A]).
Post pitch: the bed must be 2.50 mm. A 2.54 mm header is off at the ends by:
  4 posts: +/-0.06 mm
  5 posts: +/-0.08 mm
  7 posts: +/-0.12 mm
  9 posts: +/-0.16 mm
