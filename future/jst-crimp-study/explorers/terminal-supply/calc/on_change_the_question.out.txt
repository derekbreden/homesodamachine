2. The Wurth WR-WTB 2.50 contact is not an XH-sized box [mfr-an]
==============================================================================
Wurth section C-C box 1.45 x 2.0 mm; XH box 1.85-1.95 x 2.2-2.4 mm
  narrower by 0.40-0.50 mm, lower by 0.20-0.40 mm
  length box front to insulation-barrel rear 7.0 mm (XH 6.1/6.5 JST, 5.8-6.73 clones)
  tab (10.8 overall - 7.0 contact - 3.00 carrier) = 0.80 mm
  pilot-hole centre to contact rear = 3.00/2 + tab = 2.30 mm
  lance 0.65 +/-0.1 proud on the floor side, tip 2.3 mm behind the front
  -> its 2.3 mm open wings belong to a smaller box; the 0.20 mm 'Wurth' gap at 2.5 mm pitch
     in [ctq s3] is a property of a different connector, not of an XH-compatible option.
     646 101 137 22 and 646 001 137 22 are the same contact on 1,000- and 10,000-piece reels.
