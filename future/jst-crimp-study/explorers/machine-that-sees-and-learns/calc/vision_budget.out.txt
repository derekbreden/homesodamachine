3. Crimp height by silhouette: repeatability and the non-telecentric error
============================================================================
Edge-fit repeatability on a backlit silhouette, assumed 0.05-0.3 px per edge
(0.3 px allows for MJPG compression on the ELP's USB 2.0 link [repo: targets.conf]).
Height = two edges, so sigma_h = sqrt(2) * sigma_edge.
  A  ELP stock lens at 100 mm                    sigma(CH)  1.6 -  9.4 um  vs +/-50 um tolerance
  B  ELP stock lens at 70 mm (if AF reaches)     sigma(CH)  1.1 -  6.4 um  vs +/-50 um tolerance
  C  ELP + clip-on +20 D close-up lens           sigma(CH)  0.8 -  4.9 um  vs +/-50 um tolerance
  C' ELP + +30 D close-up lens                   sigma(CH)  0.5 -  3.3 um  vs +/-50 um tolerance
  D  16MP M12 board, 12 mm M12 lens, 100 mm      sigma(CH)  0.6 -  3.5 um  vs +/-50 um tolerance
  E  RPi HQ + 16 mm C-mount at f/5.6, 100 mm     sigma(CH)  0.6 -  3.5 um  vs +/-50 um tolerance

Non-telecentric scale error: a crimp sitting dz off the calibration plane is scaled by dz/d.
  d =  50 mm, dz = 0.2 mm ->  3.5 um on a 0.88 mm crimp height
  d =  50 mm, dz = 0.5 mm ->  8.8 um on a 0.88 mm crimp height
  d = 100 mm, dz = 0.2 mm ->  1.8 um on a 0.88 mm crimp height
  d = 100 mm, dz = 0.5 mm ->  4.4 um on a 0.88 mm crimp height
  d = 150 mm, dz = 0.2 mm ->  1.2 um on a 0.88 mm crimp height
  d = 150 mm, dz = 0.5 mm ->  2.9 um on a 0.88 mm crimp height
A gauge pin of known diameter resting on the same support, in the same frame, removes
most of the scale error; a point micrometer on ~10 crimps calibrates the silhouette
against the industry's own measurement (flash at the crimp sides shows in silhouette
but not under a point micrometer).  [calc / assumption]

4. Alignment tolerances the camera must hold at the nest
============================================================================
  cond. barrel 1.25, window 0.5: brush ranges +0.65 .. +1.15 mm as the insulation edge moves across the window
  cond. barrel 1.25, window 1.0: brush ranges +0.15 .. +1.15 mm as the insulation edge moves across the window
  cond. barrel 1.50, window 0.5: brush ranges +0.40 .. +0.90 mm as the insulation edge moves across the window
  cond. barrel 1.50, window 1.0: brush ranges -0.10 .. +0.90 mm as the insulation edge moves across the window
The insulation edge may sit anywhere in the window, so the axial room is the window
length itself: ~0.5-1.0 mm total (+/-0.25 to +/-0.5 mm) on the clone-drawing estimates.
The brush must also stay clear of the box, which caps the far end; that clearance is
not dimensioned anywhere read [xh-facts]. A strip 0.3 mm off uses most of the room,
so the axial target is set from the measured strip, and a strip outside ~2.2-2.7 mm
is sent back to be re-stripped rather than forced.  [calc on xh-facts estimates]

Lateral: a 0.72 mm bundle in a ~1.7 mm open conductor barrel leaves ~0.49 mm each side.
Holding +/-0.15 mm keeps the bundle clear of the wing tips with margin.
  A  ELP stock lens at 100 mm                    +/-0.15 mm =   6.8 px; 0.05 mm stage step =  2.3 px
