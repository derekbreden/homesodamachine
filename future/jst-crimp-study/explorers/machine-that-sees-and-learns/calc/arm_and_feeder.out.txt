1. SO-101 tip resolution and what the XH steps need
============================================================================
  one encoder count = 0.0879 deg
    at  50 mm from the joint: 0.08 mm per count
    at 100 mm from the joint: 0.15 mm per count
    at 250 mm from the joint: 0.38 mm per count
    at 350 mm from the joint: 0.54 mm per count
  Gear backlash and servo compliance are not published [source: Waveshare wiki]; with
  shoulder and elbow each worth ~0.4-0.5 mm per count at the tip, a tip repeatability of
  ~1-2 mm is the working [estimate]. Dobot's MG400 states +/-0.05 mm [source: dobot].
    conductor over the open barrel, lateral          +/-0.15 mm -> arm error/tolerance ~10.0x
    insulation edge in the window, axial             +/-0.25 mm -> arm error/tolerance ~ 6.0x
    contact into an XHP cavity, lateral              +/-0.20 mm -> arm error/tolerance ~ 7.5x
    clip into a chamfered dock (capture range)       +/-2.50 mm -> arm error/tolerance ~ 0.6x
  Everything but the dock is out of the arm's reach by 5-10x: the arm carries, a dock
  locates, and a station's own small stage or guide does the last fraction of a mm.

2. Transfers and teaching time for the arm (v2)  [estimate]
============================================================================
  14 ribbon ends x 5 station visits = 70 transfers per unit
  at 15-30 s each: 18-35 min of arm time per unit
  teaching: 5 subtasks x 50 demonstrations x 1.0-1.5 min = 4.2-6.2 h of Derek at the leader arm
  Training: 'several hours' per policy [source: LeRobot docs], unattended, on the Mac (mps)
  or a rented GPU. Harness labour today is 45 attended min per unit [repo: labor.md], so
  the teaching costs roughly the harness labour of 5-8 units, spent once.
