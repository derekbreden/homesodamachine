3. The proof pull: what is behind the box, and where a pull can react
==============================================================================
neck (box rear to conductor-barrel front) from clone lengths: -0.20 .. +2.18 mm
brush reaches into it by 0.2-0.5 mm -> free gap -0.70 .. +1.98 mm  (undetermined; one kit contact under a caliper settles it)
lance tip 0.4-0.6 mm behind the box rear, 0.6-0.9 mm below the floor: it crosses the plane of any plate set against the box rear
   a plate with a slot open upward (v5: crimp dropped in from above) has material under the floor strip -> it meets the lance unless the slot runs >= 0.9 mm below the floor
   loaded at its tip, the lance carries the retention load; clone retention min 19.6 N = the 20 N proof; v3's pull to failure goes to 100 N
   plate that works: thickness <= free gap - 0.2 mm placement; slot wider than the floor strip and the lance, narrower than the box less its 0.2 mm walls (~1.4 mm); slot depth past the lance; bearing on the side walls (and top wall if the slot opens downward)
     side-wall bearing 0.88 mm^2 at 20 N: 23 MPa (bronze yields ~450+)
     side-wall bearing 0.88 mm^2 at 100 N: 114 MPa (bronze yields ~450+)
Push sleeve on the rear of the insulation crimp (to avoid the box altogether):
   insulation crimp 1.7-1.9 x <= 2.05 mm against a 1.7 mm jacket: radial step 0.00-0.17 mm -> nothing to bear on
carrier tab 0.8 x 0.2 mm, bronze yield 450 MPa [assumption]: axial yield 72 N; plastic moment 3.6 N mm
   pull line 0.40 mm above the tab plane, no hold-down: tab bends at 9.0 N of pull
   pull line 0.85 mm above the tab plane, no hold-down: tab bends at 4.2 N of pull
carrier tab 0.8 x 0.2 mm, bronze yield 600 MPa [assumption]: axial yield 96 N; plastic moment 4.8 N mm
   pull line 0.40 mm above the tab plane, no hold-down: tab bends at 12.0 N of pull
   pull line 0.85 mm above the tab plane, no hold-down: tab bends at 5.6 N of pull
   pull line 0.40 mm up, hold-down 3 mm ahead of the tab, support under it: hold-down = 0.13 x pull -> 2.7 N at 20 N, 13.3 N at 100 N; tab in compression only
   pull line 0.40 mm up, hold-down 5 mm ahead of the tab, support under it: hold-down = 0.08 x pull -> 1.6 N at 20 N, 8.0 N at 100 N; tab in compression only
   pull line 0.85 mm up, hold-down 3 mm ahead of the tab, support under it: hold-down = 0.28 x pull -> 5.7 N at 20 N, 28.3 N at 100 N; tab in compression only
   pull line 0.85 mm up, hold-down 5 mm ahead of the tab, support under it: hold-down = 0.17 x pull -> 3.4 N at 20 N, 17.0 N at 100 N; tab in compression only
   -> the tab, held against pitch, reacts a proof pull and pull-outs up to ~70-100 N; a crimp that survives to tab yield is already 1.8x the 39.2 N minimum
