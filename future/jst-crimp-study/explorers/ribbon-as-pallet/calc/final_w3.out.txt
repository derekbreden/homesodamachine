1. Kinematic seats: contact stress at the balls, and which seat parts must be hardened
==============================================================================
Preload P = the magnets' total pull on the pallet, 10-40 N [est: 3-4 N52 10x3 discs
across a small gap]. Three balls share it; each ball bears on two faces.
  P   10 N, ball on two 6 mm 304 pins (Prime row: precision ground, NOT hardened):  2.4 N per contact, p0  1071 MPa
  P   10 N, ball on two touching 6 mm balls (all 52100):  1.9 N per contact, p0  1301 MPa
  P   10 N, ball on two 8 mm case-hardened chrome rods:  2.4 N per contact, p0  1057 MPa
  P   40 N, ball on two 6 mm 304 pins (Prime row: precision ground, NOT hardened):  9.4 N per contact, p0  1701 MPa
  P   40 N, ball on two touching 6 mm balls (all 52100):  7.7 N per contact, p0  2066 MPa
  P   40 N, ball on two 8 mm case-hardened chrome rods:  9.4 N per contact, p0  1678 MPa
  first yield under a point contact at p0 ~ 1.6 x yield strength:
    304 stainless 215-310 MPa annealed, ~500-700 cold-drawn [asm] -> yields at p0 ~350-1100 MPa
    hardened 52100 / case-hardened rod ~60 HRC: static contact to ~4000 MPa (bearing static rating)
-> the Prime stainless dowels dent under the balls at any useful preload (~1100-1700 MPa);
   a seat of balls on ball pairs, or on cut lengths of case-hardened rod, stays elastic.

3. Proof pull: two load paths that keep the carrier and the tab out of it
==============================================================================
(a) blade in the neck bearing on the box's rear face from 1.2 mm up:
    wall edges in contact ~0.81 mm^2 -> 25 MPa at 20 N (phosphor bronze yields ~450-650)
    it needs the neck between box and conductor barrel, t, to take a ~0.3 mm blade [w3 s5]
(b) pad down, tab 0.8x0.2 mm, 0.7 mm long, in compression: 125 MPa; fixed-fixed buckling 4727 N
(b) pad down, tab 0.8x0.2 mm, 1.15 mm long, in compression: 125 MPa; fixed-fixed buckling 1751 N
    the pad (3.8-6.3 N [w3 s4]) stops the contact pitching about its tab; slot pins at
    +/-3.55 mm hold the carrier to ~1 um [P3 s3]. End grips alone let a 2.5-3 mm carrier
    bow 0.2-2.7 mm [P3 s3].

4. Docking when the open insulation barrel is narrower than the jacket
==============================================================================
Capture read from the clone drawings [P3 s2]: +0.51 mm a side if the drawn widths are inside
widths; +0.01 mm worst if they are outside widths; -0.08 mm if JST's 1.95 mm envelope is the
open wing. Placement error ~0.16 RSS, 0.32 worst [P3 s2].
  interference 0.05 mm a side: wing 12.0 N/mm, silicone 3-7 N/mm; push to seat ~0.5-0.9 N per conductor
  interference 0.08 mm a side: wing 12.0 N/mm, silicone 3-7 N/mm; push to seat ~0.8-1.4 N per conductor
  interference 0.15 mm a side: wing 12.0 N/mm, silicone 3-7 N/mm; push to seat ~1.4-2.6 N per conductor
-> if docking has to press, a comb of fingers on the ribbon pallet pushes each jacket past
   the wing tips with ~0.5-2 N (5-10 N for a 5P), taken by the shelf or rail under the
   barrels, never by the tabs (which yield at 0.5-0.9 N at the box [X s5]).

5. Two consistent orders, and the length each leaves in the finished loom
==============================================================================
Order A: flush-cut and strip at the fan block face after the fan (a1, a2, a2e, a10).
  Tips and insulation edges lie on one line in the crimp pose. The outer conductors are
  longer than the centre one by their fan recession. Closed to 2.5 mm, and latched at one
  depth, that excess stays in the loom: e = r(p) - r(2.5) for the outer conductor.
   3P fanned to 5.0 mm: outer excess 0.65 mm; over a 20 mm split a shallow arc stands 2.2 mm
   3P fanned to 5.0 mm: outer excess 0.65 mm; over a 30 mm split a shallow arc stands 2.7 mm
   3P fanned to 7.1 mm: outer excess 1.21 mm; over a 20 mm split a shallow arc stands 3.0 mm
   3P fanned to 7.1 mm: outer excess 1.21 mm; over a 30 mm split a shallow arc stands 3.7 mm
   4P fanned to 5.0 mm: outer excess 1.00 mm; over a 20 mm split a shallow arc stands 2.7 mm
   4P fanned to 5.0 mm: outer excess 1.00 mm; over a 30 mm split a shallow arc stands 3.4 mm
   4P fanned to 7.1 mm: outer excess 1.85 mm; over a 20 mm split a shallow arc stands 3.7 mm
   4P fanned to 7.1 mm: outer excess 1.85 mm; over a 30 mm split a shallow arc stands 4.6 mm
   5P fanned to 5.0 mm: outer excess 1.34 mm; over a 20 mm split a shallow arc stands 3.2 mm
   5P fanned to 5.0 mm: outer excess 1.34 mm; over a 30 mm split a shallow arc stands 3.9 mm
   5P fanned to 7.1 mm: outer excess 2.46 mm; over a 20 mm split a shallow arc stands 4.3 mm
   5P fanned to 7.1 mm: outer excess 2.46 mm; over a 30 mm split a shallow arc stands 5.3 mm
Order B: flush-cut and strip before the split (procedure-is-the-machine p7), then an
  equal-path fan (humps in the inner grooves [P3 s1]) (a9). Every conductor is one length
  from the root, so the finished loom carries no excess; the humps' set is pulled straight
  by the insertion clamp's forward push.
   hump 2.6 mm: straightening tension ~M_p/h = 0.12-0.23 N
   hump 3.3 mm: straightening tension ~M_p/h = 0.09-0.18 N
   hump 5.1 mm: straightening tension ~M_p/h = 0.06-0.12 N
Order C: one conductor at a time, each tip found by touch (a4 with a8, or a8b with a snout).
  Any fan; each conductor's own Y is read and used. Excess as in order A if the fan is
  closed afterwards.
Housing pitch only (2.5 mm): recession 0.11-0.31 mm, and either order is the same.

==============================================================================
6. Insertion from a fan closed to 2.5 mm: the natural staircase and what the load cell sees
==============================================================================
Fronts left free after closing (order A, or order B with the humps not pressed flat)
[P3 s10]: 4P from 7.1: outer pair +1.85, inner pair 0; 5P from 7.1: outer +2.47, next +1.22,
centre 0; from 5.0: 4P +1.00/0; 5P +1.34/+0.65/0.
  3P: latch events in order: outer pair, then centre  (2 events for 3 contacts)
  4P: latch events in order: outer pair, then inner pair  (2 events for 4 contacts)
  5P: latch events in order: outer pair, then next pair, then centre  (3 events for 5 contacts)
-> the load cell counts events, not contacts; a pair is two lances in one event.
   The spring-limited pull-back and the camera confirm each contact.
