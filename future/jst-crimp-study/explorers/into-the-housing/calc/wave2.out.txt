A. Pin maps as layers: which looms need a crossing, and how many levels
==============================================================================
A crossing between ribbon and housing is one conductor lying over another in the
split. Any pin map splits into layers, each laid in order without crossing; the
minimum number of layers is the longest run of conductors whose pins DEcrease in
web order (Dilworth). Layer 0 lies flat; each higher layer lies over the ones below.

C. Stage high, place low: how high the staged row must sit
==============================================================================
The crimped row waits in web order in a staging comb directly above the target comb,
at the same Y, raised h. A carrier moves each contact down into its target slot. A placed
conductor lies flat (Z 0..1.7); a staged one rises linearly from its root to height h.
Where their plan views cross at fraction f of the span from the root, the staged one's
underside is at f*h. It must clear the placed one's top by 0.3 mm: h >= 2.0 / f.
   J4, staged at 2.5 mm pitch:
      web order, lower layer first                    : tightest f = 0.40 (placed IO25 under staged 3V3) -> h >=  4.9 mm
      inside out, lower layer first                   : tightest f = 0.40 (placed IO25 under staged 3V3) -> h >=  4.9 mm
      most clearance (exhaustive), lower layer first  : tightest f = 0.40 (placed IO25 under staged 3V3) -> h >=  4.9 mm
         order: ['V5', 'IO25', 'IO26', 'IO27', 'IO23', '3V3', 'GND']
   J4, staged at 5.0 mm pitch:
      web order, lower layer first                    : tightest f = 0.18 (placed V5 under staged IO25) -> h >= 10.8 mm
      inside out, lower layer first                   : tightest f = 0.25 (placed IO25 under staged 3V3) -> h >=  7.9 mm
      most clearance (exhaustive), lower layer first  : tightest f = 0.25 (placed IO25 under staged 3V3) -> h >=  7.9 mm
         order: ['IO25', 'V5', 'IO26', 'IO27', 'IO23', '3V3', 'GND']

==============================================================================
G. The lance condition belongs to the contact, not to i2
==============================================================================
A contact held by its box, with a flat anvil under its conductor barrel and the lance hanging
free, needs the anvil's front edge behind the lance tip (+0.1 mm) and in front of the
conductor barrel's front. Measured from the contact's front, that is:
   lance_tip + 0.1 <= box + t   ->   t >= lance_tip + 0.1 - 2.0
   lance tip 2.24 mm behind the front: transition t >= 0.34 mm
   lance tip 2.44 mm behind the front: transition t >= 0.54 mm
   lance tip 2.64 mm behind the front: transition t >= 0.74 mm
The box depth in a cavity (i2), in a stub (i2d) or in front of a hand tool's jaw drops out:
the same inequality governs every locator that holds the box and leaves the lance free.
-> The iCrimp SN-2549 already crimps these contacts. If its XH anvil is a plain block with
   no slot or step where the lance hangs, today's crimps show t is long enough. If it has a
   relief, every machine anvil needs the same relief. One look at the jaw settles which.
