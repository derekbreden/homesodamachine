14. a2b: does its own weight straighten a hanging split conductor?
============================================================================
 linear mass ~4.9 mg/mm [estimate]
  25 mm of split conductor weighs 1.2 mN; as a horizontal cantilever its own weight makes 0.015 N*mm at the root
  35 mm of split conductor weighs 1.7 mN; as a horizontal cantilever its own weight makes 0.029 N*mm at the root
  holding a 20 mm radius elastically takes EI/r = 0.72-0.91 N*mm
  holding a 40 mm radius elastically takes EI/r = 0.36-0.45 N*mm
  holding a 67 mm radius elastically takes EI/r = 0.22-0.27 N*mm
-> gravity is ~2-13 % of the moments in play, and a bend the copper has set has no
   restoring moment at all. The hanging tip goes where its history puts it; the fork and
   a close guide set its line, not its weight.
