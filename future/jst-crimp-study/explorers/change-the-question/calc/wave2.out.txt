1. The Wurth 646 001 137 22 contact is not XH-sized
==============================================================================
  Wurth box 1.45 x 2.0 mm; XH box 1.85-1.95 x 2.2-2.4 mm
  Wurth is 0.40-0.50 mm narrower and 0.20-0.40 mm lower: it would sit loose in an XHP cavity.
  Its open wings (2.3 x 2.15), its 7.10 mm carrier pitch and its 1.50 mm pilot hole describe a
  smaller 2.5 mm-pitch contact, not an XH one; it is kept out of every XH gap table.

3. Forming the pre-form: force per wing, die force, springback (c6)
==============================================================================
  phosphor bronze C5191 spring temper [estimate 450-650]: plastic hinge moment 2.92-11.80 N*mm per wing; force 1.5-11.8 N per wing
  brass C2680 half-hard, if a clone uses it [assumption 300-420]: plastic hinge moment 1.94-7.62 N*mm per wing; force 1.0-7.6 N per wing
  two wings, friction on the die and an overbend allowance x2-3 -> die force ~10-80 N per contact [estimate]
  (context calc: insulation forming with a wire in it is 33-132 N per barrel; no wire here, so lower)
  springback PB 550 MPa, bend radius 0.78 mm: K = 0.942 -> the wing opens back 5.8% of its bend angle
  springback PB 550 MPa, bend radius 1.0 mm: K = 0.925 -> the wing opens back 7.5% of its bend angle
  springback PB 650 MPa, bend radius 0.78 mm: K = 0.931 -> the wing opens back 6.9% of its bend angle
  springback PB 650 MPa, bend radius 1.0 mm: K = 0.911 -> the wing opens back 8.9% of its bend angle
  springback brass 400 MPa, bend radius 0.78 mm: K = 0.953 -> the wing opens back 4.7% of its bend angle
  springback brass 400 MPa, bend radius 1.0 mm: K = 0.940 -> the wing opens back 6.0% of its bend angle
  a 30 deg inward bend springs back ~2 deg: the die overbends by that much; the ID grows ~0.02-0.05 mm

4. Snap-in: pushing the jacket through the throat, retention, axial grip (c6)
==============================================================================
  silicone Shore 50A -> E ~ 2.5 MPa   [Shore 50-70A: source Primasil via ribbon-as-pallet]
  silicone Shore 60A -> E ~ 3.6 MPa   [Shore 50-70A: source Primasil via ribbon-as-pallet]
  silicone Shore 70A -> E ~ 5.5 MPa   [Shore 50-70A: source Primasil via ribbon-as-pallet]
  jacket wall 0.45-0.47 mm over a 0.75-0.8 mm strand core
  push-in through a throat g: per-side interference d = (1.7 - g)/2, wall strain d/wall,
  contact chord a = 2 sqrt(0.85 d), normal force N = k E strain a L (k = 0.5-1 for bulge relief),
  push F = 2 N (tan(alpha) + mu)/(1 - mu tan(alpha)), alpha 30-40 deg, mu 0.3-0.8 [all estimate]
  throat 1.2 mm: interference 0.25/side, N 0.48-4.2 N/side, push-in ~1.0-42 N
  throat 1.3 mm: interference 0.20/side, N 0.34-3.0 N/side, push-in ~0.7-30 N
  throat 1.4 mm: interference 0.15/side, N 0.22-2.0 N/side, push-in ~0.5-20 N
  throat 1.5 mm: interference 0.10/side, N 0.12-1.1 N/side, push-in ~0.3-11 N
  retention against lifting out is taken as 0.5-1x the push-in force [assumption]: ~0.3-10 N for g = 1.3

  axial grip of the C on the jacket (the flag's axial hold):
  C inner diameter 1.50: squeeze 11.8% of OD, pressure 0.52-1.23 MPa, axial grip ~0.29-5.5 N
  C inner diameter 1.55: squeeze  8.8% of OD, pressure 0.39-0.92 MPa, axial grip ~0.23-4.3 N
  C inner diameter 1.60: squeeze  5.9% of OD, pressure 0.26-0.61 MPa, axial grip ~0.16-3.0 N
  C inner diameter 1.65: squeeze  2.9% of OD, pressure 0.13-0.31 MPa, axial grip ~0.08-1.5 N
  force-and-form's thin-layer model gives 0.4-9 N at 10-30 % squeeze [ff wave2 section 5]; same order.

  what a snapped flag has to resist: its weight 0.42 mN; a keyed slot 0.1-0.5 N [ts otq section 7];
  a 0.64 mm post push-on 0.2-1.6 N [ts section 7]. A 1.55-1.60 mm C clears the slot case at central values.

5. Pre-formed contacts stack nose to tail: no nesting (c6)
==============================================================================
  box 1.85-1.95 wide vs pre-formed throat 1.2-1.4 and C bore 1.55-1.65: the next contact's box can enter neither from above nor from behind.
  open clone wings (2.46-3.25 wide) admit a box (1.85-1.95): that is why loose contacts nest [hand-tool-as-press].
  stick for one T4 end                          4 contacts: 0.02-0.03 m
  stick for J1                                  9 contacts: 0.05-0.06 m
  stick for one unit                           53 contacts: 0.31-0.36 m
  stick for a long T4 spool run (21 ends)      84 contacts: 0.49-0.57 m
  stick for a 100-piece strip                 100 contacts: 0.58-0.67 m
  short loom-order sticks (4-9 contacts, 23-61 mm) are the handy unit; a unit is 14 of them, or 10 per housing.
