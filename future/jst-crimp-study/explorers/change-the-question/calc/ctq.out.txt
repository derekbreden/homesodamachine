3. Can open contacts sit side by side? gap between open insulation wings at a pitch
============================================================================
wing width                                      2.5      3.4      5.0   (gap mm; negative = collide)
JST catalog end-view envelope (1.95)           0.55     1.45     3.05
HDGC / JXT clone (2.7-2.8 nominal)            -0.30     0.60     2.20
clone worst case (3.0 + 0.25)                 -0.75     0.15     1.75

Most a crimp punch can spread either side of its contact centreline without touching an
OPEN neighbour's wings (0.1 mm clearance), vs a CRIMPED neighbour (1.95 envelope):
  pitch 2.5  JST catalog end-view envelope (1.95)     beside open: 1.42  beside crimped: 1.42
  pitch 2.5  HDGC / JXT clone (2.7-2.8 nominal)       beside open: 1.00  beside crimped: 1.42
  pitch 2.5  clone worst case (3.0 + 0.25)            beside open: 0.78  beside crimped: 1.42
  pitch 3.4  JST catalog end-view envelope (1.95)     beside open: 2.32  beside crimped: 2.32
  pitch 3.4  HDGC / JXT clone (2.7-2.8 nominal)       beside open: 1.90  beside crimped: 2.32
  pitch 3.4  clone worst case (3.0 + 0.25)            beside open: 1.67  beside crimped: 2.32
  pitch 5.0  JST catalog end-view envelope (1.95)     beside open: 3.93  beside crimped: 3.93
  pitch 5.0  HDGC / JXT clone (2.7-2.8 nominal)       beside open: 3.50  beside crimped: 3.93
  pitch 5.0  clone worst case (3.0 + 0.25)            beside open: 3.27  beside crimped: 3.93
A conductor-crimp punch with ~1.0 mm legs around a ~1.5-1.8 mm profile is ~1.75-1.9 mm
half-width [estimate]: it fits beside open neighbours at 5.0, is marginal at 3.4, fails at 2.5.

5. Tack first (c1b): gang-close the insulation barrels, crimp conductor barrels later
============================================================================
  half-row of 2: gang tack   66- 264 N   vs   gang conductor crimp 1.50- 4.60 kN
  half-row of 3: gang tack   99- 396 N   vs   gang conductor crimp 2.25- 6.90 kN
  half-row of 4: gang tack  132- 528 N   vs   gang conductor crimp 3.00- 9.20 kN
  half-row of 5: gang tack  165- 660 N   vs   gang conductor crimp 3.75-11.50 kN
  NEMA 23 1.5 N*m through a 2 mm trapezoid screw at eta 0.3: ~1.4 kN [calc C1 section 5] -> ample for a tack gang.
  tack 33 N on 2.3 mm^2 barrel floor ->  14.5 MPa (PETG yields ~45-50 MPa [estimate]; steel anvil strip)
  tack 132 N on 2.3 mm^2 barrel floor ->  57.9 MPa (PETG yields ~45-50 MPa [estimate]; steel anvil strip)

6. Solder-set joint (c3): fold wings without coining, then solder
============================================================================
  plastic hinge moment per wing 7.70 N*mm; force at 0.8 mm arm   9.6 N per wing
  plastic hinge moment per wing 7.70 N*mm; force at 1.2 mm arm   6.4 N per wing
  curling both wings against a punch arch with friction: tens to ~300 N total [calc C1 section 4 range],
  i.e. roughly 3-10x less than coining the conductor barrel (0.75-2.3 kN).
  heat to lift contact + 10 mm of conductor by 230 K:  6.2 J (a 60-70 W iron supplies this in well under a second;
  real dwell 1-3 s is set by conduction into the wire and tip contact) [calc]

7. Carrier strip pitch against the housing (the strip cannot load several cavities)
============================================================================
  2 x 2.5 =  5.0 mm  (strip 7.1 mm, mismatch +2.10 mm)
  3 x 2.5 =  7.5 mm  (strip 7.1 mm, mismatch -0.40 mm)
  4 x 2.5 = 10.0 mm  (strip 7.1 mm, mismatch -2.90 mm)
  4 x 1.7 =  6.8 mm  (ribbon every 4th conductor; mismatch +0.30 mm)
  7.10 mm belongs to Wurth's smaller WR-WTB 2.50 contact (box 1.45 mm), not to an XH contact.
  JST's own SXH pitch is licence-gated; clone drawings scale to 7-9.5 mm [xh-facts section 1].
