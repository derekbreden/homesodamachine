
==============================================================================
1. What the final stroke does to a round keyhole (c6): side squeeze pinches the throat
==============================================================================
The keyhole's outside (bore + 2 x stock) is wider than the die's insulation section, so the
die's side walls, which reach down to near the anvil at the bottom of the stroke [htq section 3,
edge model], push the ring's widest point inward by delta per side. Each wing then turns
about its root near the floor corner, and the tip, higher up than the widest point, moves
inward by k x delta, k = (tip height - pivot height) / (widest-point height - pivot height).
The tip also moves slightly DOWN (it lies inboard of the pivot), so the die's arches, which
close at ~1.8 mm, never reach it; the die's cusp meets the jacket top instead [htq section 1].

-> With an insulation die 1.80-1.90 mm wide the stroke pinches the throat from 1.3-1.5 mm down
   to roughly 0.75-1.4 mm, pressing the tips into the jacket's shoulders: an O-shaped crimp
   with a narrowed gap on top, not a B. With a 2.00 mm die the ring is barely touched and the
   insulation grip stays the snap's. The tips are not turned over the jacket in either case
   (agreeing with htq section 1); how far the throat closes depends on the SN-2549's
   insulation width, which nobody has measured. Rigid-wing kinematics [estimate]; the jacket
   resists the closure, and elastic recovery of the wings reopens it a few percent [w2 section 3].

2. The tool-made pre-form (c6c): a narrowed U, and the snap and grip it gives
==============================================================================
At the end of the insulation-first window the wings are inside the die width, roots near
vertical, tips 2.3-3.0 mm above the underside and starting to curl inward [htq section 3].
Inside width of the U = die width + springback - 2 x stock.
  wall stiffness check: 3EI/L^3, E 110 GPa, 1.2 x 0.2 mm, L 2.0 mm -> 33.0 N/mm
  wall stiffness check: 3EI/L^3, E 110 GPa, 1.2 x 0.2 mm, L 2.8 mm -> 12.0 N/mm

  die W | U inside | jacket OD 1.60 / 1.70 / 1.80: interference per side
  1.80  | 1.38-1.49  | +0.06 to +0.11 / +0.10 to +0.16 / +0.16 to +0.21
  1.90  | 1.48-1.59  | +0.01 to +0.06 / +0.06 to +0.11 / +0.11 to +0.16
  2.00  | 1.58-1.69  | -0.04 to +0.01 / +0.01 to +0.06 / +0.06 to +0.11

  lateral grip of the U walls on the jacket, walls and silicone in series:
  silicone side stiffness k_s = E x contact width (0.6-0.9 mm) x barrel length / wall (0.45-0.47 mm)
  k_s 2.5-16.6 N/mm; in series with a wall of 12-33 N/mm -> 2.1-11.0 N/mm
  interference 0.02 per side: normal 0.04-0.2 N per side, axial grip 0.02-0.4 N
  interference 0.05 per side: normal 0.10-0.6 N per side, axial grip 0.06-0.9 N
  interference 0.10 per side: normal 0.21-1.1 N per side, axial grip 0.12-1.8 N
  interference 0.15 per side: normal 0.31-1.7 N per side, axial grip 0.19-2.6 N
  interference 0.20 per side: normal 0.41-2.2 N per side, axial grip 0.25-3.5 N
  -> about the round keyhole's band (0.08-3.8 N at OD 1.70 [htq section 2]); the silicone, not
     the softer wall, still sets most of it at the low end.

  snap through the tips' gap g_t (tips vertical: g_t = U inside; curl started: 0.1-0.3 mm less):
  g_t 1.65 on a 1.70 jacket: interference 0.03/side, push-in 0.11-2.7 N, retention against lift ~0.06-2.7 N [assumption 0.5-1x push-in]
  g_t 1.50 on a 1.70 jacket: interference 0.10/side, push-in 0.44-11.0 N, retention against lift ~0.22-11.0 N [assumption 0.5-1x push-in]
  g_t 1.40 on a 1.70 jacket: interference 0.15/side, push-in 0.66-16.5 N, retention against lift ~0.33-16.5 N [assumption 0.5-1x push-in]
  g_t 1.30 on a 1.70 jacket: interference 0.20/side, push-in 0.88-22.0 N, retention against lift ~0.44-22.0 N [assumption 0.5-1x push-in]
  g_t 1.20 on a 1.70 jacket: interference 0.25/side, push-in 1.10-27.5 N, retention against lift ~0.55-27.5 N [assumption 0.5-1x push-in]
  -> with the tips still vertical (g_t = U inside, 1.4-1.65) there is no undercut: the U holds
     the jacket against lift only by wall friction, the same ~0.02-3.5 N as its axial grip.
     Once the click has started the curl (g_t 1.2-1.4) the tips form an undercut; push-in is
     ~0.7-27 N and retention ~0.3-27 N, the keyhole's band [assumption 0.5-1x push-in].

  nesting: a box 1.85-1.95 mm wide cannot enter a U 1.42-1.85 mm inside whose tips curl in;
  a pre-formed contact stands 2.3-3.0 mm tall at the insulation barrel against a 2.2-2.4 mm box,
  so a stick channel is ~2.1-2.2 mm wide x ~3.1-3.2 mm tall (c6's 2.1 x 2.6 is for the round keyhole).

3. Choosing the mandrel against the measured jacket OD (c6, c6b): bore, grip and pins
==============================================================================
Rule [htq H2 repair]: bore after springback 0.08-0.12 mm under the measured jacket OD;
mandrel = bore - springback (0.02-0.05); go pin just under the smallest bore; no-go pin
0.05-0.08 mm under the jacket OD.
  jacket OD | bore target | mandrel    | axial grip at that bore (w2 model) | go pin | no-go pin
   1.60    | 1.48-1.52   | 1.46-1.47  |             0.13-3.7 N              | 1.47   | 1.52-1.55
   1.65    | 1.53-1.57   | 1.51-1.52  |             0.13-3.6 N              | 1.52   | 1.57-1.60
   1.70    | 1.58-1.62   | 1.56-1.57  |             0.13-3.5 N              | 1.57   | 1.62-1.65
   1.75    | 1.63-1.67   | 1.61-1.62  |             0.12-3.4 N              | 1.62   | 1.67-1.70
   1.80    | 1.68-1.72   | 1.66-1.67  |             0.12-3.4 N              | 1.67   | 1.72-1.75
  the Prime-confirmed Accusize set stops at 1.52 mm [sourcing/amazon-prime.md]: it holds the
  mandrel and both pins only if the jacket measures ~1.60 mm; at 1.70 the pins are a Wave 3 request.
  Grip at a bore the rule leaves: ~0.1-4 N, the same band, but no longer zero at the low edge
  of the ribbon's 1.7 +/-0.1 mm [source S29], because the bore follows the measured jacket.
  If the OD varies by +/-0.1 mm along one spool, one mandrel cannot follow it; the grip then
  spans ~0-6 N across the spool [htq section 2].

5. Row B's stored length in c1/c1c's two-push ending: forming the bow at the park
==============================================================================
Two sequential pushes into one housing need one row to carry ~7 mm of stored length
(w3 section 7). If the housing moves onto row A, only row B stores it: pallet B draws back
7 mm as it parks, and its conductors bow below the working plane over the split.
Buckling load of one free conductor, Euler scaling from 6 N over 5 mm [digest]:
  split chord  6 mm: buckling load ~4.17 N per conductor
  split chord 12 mm: buckling load ~1.04 N per conductor
  split chord 20 mm: buckling load ~0.38 N per conductor
  split chord 26 mm: buckling load ~0.22 N per conductor
  bow height for 7 mm stored over 6-26 mm: 4.6-8.6 mm [w3 section 7]
-> drawing pallet B back costs well under the pallet's own friction; what matters is that
   the bow goes down (the park fold sets it) and stays clear of row A's wires above.

  J7 board now   RB1 RB2 RB3 RB4 X CLO CHI GND                     1       0      (3, 0)     (a') GND on the 3P [procedure-is-the-machine]
  J7 GND to 5    RB1 RB2 RB3 RB4 GND CLO CHI X                     1       0      (0, 0)     (a) 5P column+GND | 3P CLO, CHI, X
  'off-parity' after a trimmed conductor is a per-loom split-jaw pattern, not a crossing.

  (b) one ribbon, one housing: split each pair housing. Housing overall width C = A + 4.8,
  A = (n-1) x 2.5 [mfr S2]; a row of housings needs the sum of their C plus a gap [assumption 0.5-1 mm]:
  J1: XHP-9 C 24.8 mm -> XHP-5 + XHP-4 = 27.1 mm: +2.3 mm plus one gap
  J2: XHP-6 C 17.3 mm -> XHP-3 + XHP-2 = 17.1 mm: -0.2 mm plus one gap
  J4: XHP-7 C 19.8 mm -> XHP-4 + XHP-3 = 22.1 mm: +2.3 mm plus one gap
  J7: XHP-7 C 19.8 mm -> XHP-5 + XHP-2 = 22.1 mm: +2.3 mm plus one gap
  total +6.7 mm of housing row plus 4 gaps -> ~9-11 mm more board edge [estimate]
  4P into XHP-4                 7 ends: J3, J5, J9, J11, J13, J1b, J4a
  5P into XHP-5                 3 ends: J6, J1a, J7a
  3P into XHP-3                 2 ends: J2b, J4b
  3P into XHP-2, one trimmed    2 ends: J2a, J7b
  14 ends, 14 housings, 53 crimps unchanged; 4P into XHP-4 becomes 7 ends and 28 crimps (53 %).
  J2 as XHP-3 + XHP-2 drops the empty-cavity guard: each 3P fills its own housing, so landing
  one position off can only happen by using the wrong housing.

7. Pre-forming in a host's own press (c6c): pitch at which the tongue clears open neighbours
==============================================================================
  open neighbour wings 2.46 mm: tongue half-width 2.23 + 0.1 clearance -> pitch >= 3.56 mm
  open neighbour wings 2.80 mm: tongue half-width 2.23 + 0.1 clearance -> pitch >= 3.73 mm
  open neighbour wings 3.25 mm: tongue half-width 2.23 + 0.1 clearance -> pitch >= 3.95 mm
  -> at 3.4 mm the tongue meets open neighbours' wing tips; at 5.0 mm, or on strip at its
     carrier pitch (7-9.5 mm [xh-facts section 1]), it clears them.
