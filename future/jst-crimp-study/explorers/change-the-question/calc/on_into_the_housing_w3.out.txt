1. The stepped crimper's insulation step has to swallow the open wings (i2/K1, i1b, i2b)
==============================================================================
The stepped crimper's mouth is its lower edge. Wing tips must land inside it at first touch;
a tip that lands on the wall's end face is folded outward, not curled in. The insulation
wings stand taller (2.75-3.20 open [source S19-S22]) than the conductor wings (1.50-1.60), so
the insulation step touches first, before the conductor step's flare has centred anything.
Needed outer width at the mouth = wing spread s + 2 x capture margin m + 2 x wall land l.
That outer width passes down beside the neighbour to the barrel floor.

  i2/K1: beside a seated neighbour wire (one side): free half-width 1.65 mm
    wing spread                         need (m 0.05, l 0.05, c 0.05)  need (m 0.15, l 0.10, c 0.10)
    pre-formed keyhole (c6), low  1.96     1.13 -> margin +0.52          1.33 -> margin +0.32
    pre-formed keyhole (c6), high  2.14     1.22 -> margin +0.43          1.42 -> margin +0.23
    clone open, drawing min   2.46     1.38 -> margin +0.27          1.58 -> margin +0.07
    clone open, typical       2.80     1.55 -> margin +0.10          1.75 -> margin -0.10
    clone open, drawing max   3.00     1.65 -> margin -0.00          1.85 -> margin -0.20
    clone open, max + tol     3.25     1.78 -> margin -0.13          1.98 -> margin -0.33

  i2b pass 2: between crimped insulation barrels 2.0 wide: free half-width 1.50 mm
    wing spread                         need (m 0.05, l 0.05, c 0.05)  need (m 0.15, l 0.10, c 0.10)
    pre-formed keyhole (c6), low  1.96     1.13 -> margin +0.37          1.33 -> margin +0.17
    pre-formed keyhole (c6), high  2.14     1.22 -> margin +0.28          1.42 -> margin +0.08
    clone open, drawing min   2.46     1.38 -> margin +0.12          1.58 -> margin -0.08
    clone open, typical       2.80     1.55 -> margin -0.05          1.75 -> margin -0.25
    clone open, drawing max   3.00     1.65 -> margin -0.15          1.85 -> margin -0.35
    clone open, max + tol     3.25     1.78 -> margin -0.28          1.98 -> margin -0.48

  The step's outer width 2.5-2.7 mm [ff; i2] leaves a mouth of at most 2.3-2.6 mm with a
  0.05-0.10 mm land: it swallows a pre-formed keyhole (1.96-2.14) and the narrowest clone
  wings (2.46) only. Widening the step to swallow 3.0-3.25 mm wings needs a half-width of
  1.70-1.95 mm, over the 1.65 mm to the neighbour's jacket (i2) and 1.50 mm between crimped
  neighbours (i2b pass 2).
  The conductor step (3.1 mm, 0.8 mm walls) has no such problem: open conductor wings are
  1.43-2.15 mm [source S19-S22], its mouth can flare to ~2.7-2.9 mm.

  Loading the evens of i2b between crimped odds (gap = 2.5 - s/2 - 1.0):

    clone open, typical                gap +0.10 mm

3. Conductor-barrel growth against a rigid stop at the box nose (i2d, i2d'', c1c, c6b)
==============================================================================
Coining after full fill lengthens the conductor barrel; the forward half pushes the
transition and box toward +Y. A stop at the box nose blocks it; a stop behind (tab stub,
box rear shoulder) does not.
   forward growth of the barrel front: 0.03-0.11 mm [estimate]
   axial force the flow can push with, bounded by friction on the dies: 80-520 N [estimate]
   transition yield (floor strip only, 0.2 x 1.0-1.4): 90-182 N (450-650 MPa [estimate])
   transition yield (U-section with 0.5 mm side walls): 180-312 N (450-650 MPa [estimate])
   box nose end face ~1.7 mm^2: at 90-290 N it bears 54-173 MPa
   on the stop: a PA6 stub face (yield ~50-90 MPa [estimate]) dents; a steel stop does not,
   so with steel the transition takes the growth.
   growth 0.03 mm taken as a buckle in a 0.34 mm transition: lateral amplitude ~0.07 mm
   growth 0.03 mm taken as a buckle in a 0.80 mm transition: lateral amplitude ~0.10 mm
   growth 0.11 mm taken as a buckle in a 0.34 mm transition: lateral amplitude ~0.12 mm
   growth 0.11 mm taken as a buckle in a 0.80 mm transition: lateral amplitude ~0.19 mm
-> Overlapping ranges: a rigid nose stop may bow the transition by ~0.05-0.2 mm (JST's
   bend fault); a PA6 stop dents instead and walks forward. A stop that holds only until
   capture, or a sprung stop (10-30 N), or a rear reference avoids it. Unmeasured.

7. The feed-length rule applied to c1/c1c's two row pushes with housing and web still
==============================================================================
Each row is pushed ~7 mm from its pallet into the cavities [c1 step 8]. With the web clamp
and housing still, every conductor of a pushed row must carry 7 mm of extra length, as a
bow over its split. Circular-arc bow of arc length c + s over chord c:
   split chord    6 mm, stored 5 mm: bow height  3.7 mm, radius  3.1 mm
   split chord    6 mm, stored 7 mm: bow height  4.6 mm, radius  3.3 mm
   split chord   12 mm, stored 5 mm: bow height  5.0 mm, radius  6.1 mm
   split chord   12 mm, stored 7 mm: bow height  6.1 mm, radius  6.0 mm
   split chord   20 mm, stored 5 mm: bow height  6.3 mm, radius 11.1 mm
   split chord   20 mm, stored 7 mm: bow height  7.6 mm, radius 10.4 mm
   split chord   26 mm, stored 5 mm: bow height  7.2 mm, radius 15.4 mm
   split chord   26 mm, stored 7 mm: bow height  8.6 mm, radius 14.1 mm
-> A 4-9 mm bow per conductor between the web root and the pallet, where c1c's presser comb,
   pallets and row B's carriers work. The alternative is a gang push with the housing moving
   onto one merged row (i3, i6), which stores nothing.
