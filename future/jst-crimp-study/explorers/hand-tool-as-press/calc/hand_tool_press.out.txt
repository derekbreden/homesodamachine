4. The handle as a magnifier of die position
============================================================================
  gain 15: last 0.10 mm at the die =  1.5 mm at the grip
  gain 15: last 0.20 mm at the die =  3.0 mm at the grip
  gain 30: last 0.10 mm at the die =  3.0 mm at the grip
  gain 30: last 0.20 mm at the die =  6.0 mm at the grip
  gain 40: last 0.10 mm at the die =  4.0 mm at the grip
  gain 40: last 0.20 mm at the die =  8.0 mm at the grip
  Tr8x2 pusher resolution: 0.62 um per microstep at the grip
  -> 0.021 um at the die through a gain of 30, before compliance
  Compliance (handles bending, pin play, pusher mount) adds grip travel that
  is not die travel. It is measured by closing the empty tool and by closing
  on a contact without wire; the difference curve is what the crimp did.

6. Overtravel spring: force-limited full closure (the ratchet's job, done by a spring)
============================================================================
  preload 3.0 kN: crimp up to 3.0 kN happens rigid; any extra eccentric travel compresses the stack instead of the frame
  preload 3.5 kN: crimp up to 3.5 kN happens rigid; any extra eccentric travel compresses the stack instead of the frame
  preload 4.0 kN: crimp up to 4.0 kN happens rigid; any extra eccentric travel compresses the stack instead of the frame
  Series stack of 3-4 discs (20-25 mm OD, 1-1.25 mm) gives ~0.3-0.6 mm of travel
  over ~1-2 kN rise [estimate, disc-spring catalogues]. With ~0.15 mm of eccentric
  overtravel past die contact, the dies meet every stroke and the peak is ~4-5 kN.

7. Where the contact must sit along its axis, and what can reference it
============================================================================
  allowable axial error at the conductor die: about +/-0.10 mm [estimate]
  box front against a stop (contact length +/-0.25 on clone drawings)            +/-0.250  outside
  box rear shoulder against a flap (box length 2.0; tolerance assumed +/-0.05)   +/-0.080  fits
  pilot hole on a pin (pin 1.45 in hole 1.50; hole-to-barrel assumed +/-0.05)    +/-0.075  fits
  0.64 mm post in the box, box front on the post shoulder                        +/-0.250  outside
  camera measures, carriage corrects (0.02 mm/px at ~20 mm field)                +/-0.030  fits
  Where the +/-0.25 on overall length sits (tab, box front, or between box and
  barrels) is not on the drawings; a caliper on kit contacts measures it.

============================================================================

  tab 0.8 x 0.20 mm, shear 400 MPa ->    64 N
  tab 1.0 x 0.20 mm, shear 250 MPa ->    50 N
  tab 1.0 x 0.20 mm, shear 400 MPa ->    80 N
  tab 1.2 x 0.20 mm, shear 250 MPa ->    60 N
  tab 1.2 x 0.20 mm, shear 400 MPa ->    96 N
  proof pull at 50% of JST's 39.2 N minimum = 19.6 N
  proof pull at 60% of JST's 39.2 N minimum = 23.5 N
  A carriage pulling through a spring to ~20-25 N screens gross failures without
  destroying a good crimp [assumption; proof loads are not a JST procedure].
