1. a4 / a4b / a4c / a4d: where to set die contact when the frame is not rigid
==============================================================================
Model [calc]: eccentric e, angle phi before BDC, ram travel still to go d = e(1 - cos phi).
Nominal die contact (zero load) is set y_c above BDC; s = y_c - d is travel past it.
Crimp: force rises linearly over the last 0.2 mm of die gap to Fc at die contact [xh-facts §4].
Frame loop k_f (shaft, bushings, holders, button cell) 15-30 kN/mm [estimate, sl w3 §6];
disc stack preload P = 3.5 kN, rate k_s = 3 kN/mm [estimate]. Past P the loop is k_f in series with k_s.

  e   Fc   k_f | y_c 0.15 (rigid-frame setting)          | y_c = P/k_f + 0.05 (stack engages)

   2.6 kN crimp's dies apart or leaves the stack idle, so height follows frame stiffness.
   Set die contact 0.17-0.28 mm above BDC (P/k_f + 0.05); the BDC force is ~3.6 kN, the
   peak shaft torque ~1.6-2.0 N*m before friction, ~2.0-3.0 N*m with 30-50 % friction.

3. The pull plate: what has to fit in the neck and what does not
==============================================================================
Crimped contact, end view, box 1.85-1.95 wide, walls 0.2 [xh-facts §1]; crimped conductor
barrel ~1.5 wide and ~0.9 tall (crimp width 1.5 [mfr S14 analog], height ~0.88 [xh-facts §1]).
  box 1.85, crimped barrel 1.40: the box's rear side-wall edges stand +0.23 mm outside the barrel each side
  box 1.85, crimped barrel 1.60: the box's rear side-wall edges stand +0.12 mm outside the barrel each side
  box 1.95, crimped barrel 1.40: the box's rear side-wall edges stand +0.28 mm outside the barrel each side
  box 1.95, crimped barrel 1.60: the box's rear side-wall edges stand +0.17 mm outside the barrel each side
  above the crimped barrel (z > ~1.1 mm) the box's rear edges (top wall, upper side walls)
  face free space: a plate there can be any thickness and can be backed from behind.

Three plates, 20 N pull, two tines [calc; stress = 6 M / (b t^2)]:
  (b) backer to 1 mm above floor: tine t 0.15, width 0.6:  2.8 N each,    630 MPa
  (b) backer to 1 mm above floor: tine t 0.15, width 1.0:  2.8 N each,    378 MPa
  (b) backer to 1 mm above floor: tine t 0.20, width 0.6:  2.8 N each,    354 MPa
  (b) backer to 1 mm above floor: tine t 0.20, width 1.0:  2.8 N each,    213 MPa
  (b) backer to 1 mm above floor: tine t 0.30, width 0.6:  2.8 N each,    157 MPa
  (b) backer to 1 mm above floor: tine t 0.30, width 1.0:  2.8 N each,     94 MPa
  (c) stepped plate: thick (0.5-1.0) and backed wherever it bears on the box outside the
      barrel's width or above its height; thin only where it faces the barrel's front edge,
      where it bears nothing. The neck then limits only the thin tongue, not the load path.
      barrel inside the box by 0.05 a side -> backer clearance to the barrel side 0.05 mm (tight)
      barrel inside the box by 0.10 a side -> backer clearance to the barrel side 0.10 mm 
      barrel inside the box by 0.25 a side -> backer clearance to the barrel side 0.25 mm 
-> [sl w3 §2 a6] holds for a flat plate: it needs a transition of ~0.35-0.5 mm.
   A stepped plate needs only that the crimped barrel be narrower than the box by ~0.1 mm a
   side and the box's rear edges be a clean step, not a sloped transition. The same neck
   photo settles both [estimate]. In a crimp jig the contact is alone, so the space outside
   the box is free.

5. The flag seat on the SN-2549 (a6b, a6c): jaw opening at the nest
==============================================================================
Flag carried level with its floor at h above the anvil; h >= lance 0.6-0.9 + 0.2 on entry,
and the crimp needs a 1.0-1.7 mm lift before drawing back [ith ex §2]. Opening = h + the
flag's tallest part above its floor + 0.2 margin [calc, as htq §4].
  h 1.1: tool-made pre-form (a6b) 3.6-4.3 | v8 tack (a6c) 3.6-3.8 | open contact, for comparison 4.0-4.5
  h 1.4: tool-made pre-form (a6b) 3.9-4.6 | v8 tack (a6c) 3.9-4.1 | open contact, for comparison 4.4-4.8
  h 1.7: tool-made pre-form (a6b) 4.2-4.9 | v8 tack (a6c) 4.2-4.4 | open contact, for comparison 4.7-5.1
  springs under the ledge and rear guide 1-3 N; the wings start to curl at tens of N [xh-facts §4],
  so the punch seats the flag on the anvil before it forms anything [estimate].
-> a flag needs ~3.6-4.9 mm of opening at the nest; the SN-2549's full opening is unmeasured.

What holds the flag on its wire against the person's push to the stop:
  tool-made pre-form: 0.08-3.8 N [htq §2 model]; v8 tack: 0.2-1.5 N wanted, 0.4-9 N estimated
  lance fold force 1-5 N [ith ex §3]: a flag dragged on the anvil loses its place on the wire
-> green (box on the wired stop) means stop pushing; the seat carries the flag clear of the anvil.
