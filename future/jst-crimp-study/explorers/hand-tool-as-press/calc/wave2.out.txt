1. The neck: what fits between the box's rear face and the conductor barrel
==============================================================================
room left for the brush = transition - blade stack (negative = the blade does not fit)
  blade stack                          t=0.2  t=0.3  t=0.4  t=0.5
  0.30 steel (wave 1)                 -0.10  +0.00  +0.10  +0.20
  0.20 steel                          +0.00  +0.10  +0.20  +0.30
  0.15 steel                          +0.05  +0.15  +0.25  +0.35
  0.10 steel + 0.025 polyimide        +0.08  +0.17  +0.28  +0.38
  0.10 steel + 0.05 polyimide         +0.05  +0.15  +0.25  +0.35
-> a brush of 0.1-0.2 mm plus a blade needs a transition of >= 0.25-0.35 mm even
   with a 0.1 mm blade. A 0.2 mm transition leaves no room for any blade with a
   brush: the contact is then located some other way (pilot pin, camera, box front).

5. The treadle (a6): a foot closes the hand tool through a cord
==============================================================================
foot force and foot travel for a heel-hinged treadle of ratio r (toe arm / cord arm)
  grip 100 N, r 1.5: foot    74 N
  grip 100 N, r 2.0: foot    55 N
  grip 100 N, r 3.0: foot    37 N
  grip 175 N, r 1.5: foot   129 N
  grip 175 N, r 2.0: foot    97 N
  grip 175 N, r 3.0: foot    65 N
  grip 220 N, r 1.5: foot   163 N
  grip 220 N, r 2.0: foot   122 N
  grip 220 N, r 3.0: foot    81 N
  cord travel = grip travel: 51-56 mm with the tool's full opening [wave-1 calc §3];
  ~25-35 mm with a printed opening limiter [estimate: the jaw needs ~5 mm of gap
  (3.35-4.10 mm passage [ith ex §2] + ~1 mm lift before drawing back), and the
  open-end gain is assumed 3-6]
  cord travel 30 mm -> foot travel 45 / 60 / 90 mm at r 1.5 / 2 / 3
  cord travel 55 mm -> foot travel 82 / 110 / 165 mm at r 1.5 / 2 / 3
-> r = 2 with an opening limiter: <= ~120 N at the toe over ~60 mm. Presses per unit
   ~106 (a half-press to hold and a full press to crimp, 53 crimps) [calc].
   Seated ankle pedals are comfortable to tens of newtons for frequent use and leg-
   driven pedals take several hundred [estimate]; this is occasional work.
  two-stage treadle: stage 1 stop at the first ratchet tooth (half-press = hold),
  stage 2 through a stronger spring (full press = crimp) [design, unmeasured tooth position]

6. Sensing a foot-closed crimp: load cell in the cord, angle sensor on the pulley
==============================================================================
  AS5600 on a 22 mm pulley: 16.9 um of cord per count
  through a gain of 15: 1.12 um of die per count, before compliance
  through a gain of 30: 0.56 um of die per count, before compliance
  S-type 50 kg cell: 490 N capacity; HX711 at 10-80 SPS; a 3 s full press at 80 SPS
  gives ~240 samples over the stroke and ~15-40 in compaction (last 1.5-8 mm of grip).
-> the same force-against-travel curve a1 logs, with a foot as the motor.

7. The pull jig (a6): capstan and a readable spring
==============================================================================
capstan: hold force = pull x exp(-mu*theta)
  mu 0.3, 1 turn(s): 20 N needs   3.04 N of hold at the tail
  mu 0.3, 2 turn(s): 20 N needs   0.46 N of hold at the tail
  mu 0.3, 3 turn(s): 20 N needs   0.07 N of hold at the tail
  mu 0.6, 1 turn(s): 20 N needs   0.46 N of hold at the tail
  mu 0.6, 2 turn(s): 20 N needs   0.01 N of hold at the tail
  mu 0.6, 3 turn(s): 20 N needs   0.00 N of hold at the tail
spring-steel leaf as the gauge, cantilever L, width b, thickness t, load 20 N at the tip
  leaf 40x10x0.8: k  4.00 N/mm, 20 N ->  5.00 mm, stress  750 MPa
  leaf 40x10x1.0: k  7.81 N/mm, 20 N ->  2.56 mm, stress  480 MPa
  leaf 30x10x1.0: k 18.52 N/mm, 20 N ->  1.08 mm, stress  360 MPa
  leaf 40x12x1.2: k 16.20 N/mm, 20 N ->  1.23 mm, stress  278 MPa
-> a 40 x 10 x 1.0 mm leaf reads 20 N as ~2.6 mm of tip travel at ~480 MPa; a printed
   pointer or the camera reads it. A digital luggage scale on the hook does the same job.

8. A keyhole fit gauge (a6, a1b)
==============================================================================
  closed insulation barrel 1.95 wide x 2.26 tall: passes a 1.95 x 2.4 opening
  closed insulation barrel 1.90 wide x 2.33 tall: passes a 1.95 x 2.4 opening
  closed insulation barrel 1.80 wide x 2.46 tall: stops at a 1.95 x 2.4 opening
  side slot 1.3 mm: the 1.7 mm silicone wire squeezes out sideways (24 % diametral squeeze) [estimate]; a 1.9-2.0 mm crimp cannot
  side slot 1.5 mm: the 1.7 mm silicone wire squeezes out sideways (12 % diametral squeeze) [estimate]; a 1.9-2.0 mm crimp cannot
-> a stencil-steel keyhole (cavity section, lance notch, side slot) passes a good crimp
   nose first and lets the wire out sideways. It stops flared wings, strands outside,
   a spike of tab and a half-crimp. It says nothing about crimp height. The opening is
   copied from a sliced kit housing measured under the ELP, not from the catalog.
