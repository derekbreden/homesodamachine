-> size the drive for ~5.4 N*m: mid-stroke spring work, not the crimp, sets the torque

2. Frame stretch at crimp force (O-frame: two steel plates, four tie rods)
============================================================================
plates 12.7 mm x 100 wide over 170 span, 4 rods d16: k_total ~13 kN/mm; stretch at 3 kN 235 um; crimp-to-crimp scatter from +/-15% force: +/-23.5 um
plates 25.4 mm x 100 wide over 170 span, 4 rods d16: k_total ~39 kN/mm; stretch at 3 kN 77 um; crimp-to-crimp scatter from +/-15% force: +/-7.7 um
plates 12.7 mm x 100 wide over 110 span, 4 rods d16: k_total ~29 kN/mm; stretch at 3 kN 103 um; crimp-to-crimp scatter from +/-15% force: +/-10.3 um
plates 15 mm x 100 wide over 150 span, 4 rods d16: k_total ~23 kN/mm; stretch at 3 kN 130 um; crimp-to-crimp scatter from +/-15% force: +/-13.0 um
plates 20 mm x 120 wide over 150 span, 4 rods d20: k_total ~38 kN/mm; stretch at 3 kN 79 um; crimp-to-crimp scatter from +/-15% force: +/-7.9 um
-> mean stretch is dialled out once on the applicator's crimp-height dial; the scatter sits inside
   the +/-0.05 mm J.S.T. UK tolerance [facts], comfortably so from 15 mm plates up.
   12.7 mm (0.500 in) is the thickest mild steel a quick laser-cut service offers [source SendCutSend];
   shortening the span between rods is the cheap way to stiffen it (stiffness ~ 1/span^3), but the
   rods must clear the applicator (a WERI mini-applicator is 155 x 150 x 110 mm [prior-art s3]), so
   ~170 mm is the realistic span one way; the 25.4 mm row is two 12.7 mm plates bolted/bonded as one [estimate].
   A printed frame would not hold it: PETG-CF E ~ 4-8 GPa [estimate], and it creeps under load
  printed 30 mm-thick plate, E=4 GPa: k ~12.8 kN/mm -> 234 um at 3 kN, creeps
  printed 30 mm-thick plate, E=8 GPa: k ~25.6 kN/mm -> 117 um at 3 kN, creeps

3. Alternatives: ball screw with an over-travel spring; air cylinders
============================================================================
NEMA23 1.2 N*m, SFU1605 direct: 1.4 kN thrust at stall
NEMA23 1.2 N*m, SFU1605 via 3:1 belt: 3.8 kN thrust at stall
NEMA23 1.2 N*m, SFU1204 via 5:1 belt: 8.0 kN thrust at stall
Over-travel spring: the nut drives the ram through a disc-spring stack preloaded above the crimp peak
(>3 kN). The ram lands on a hard stop at shut height; the nut runs on ~0.3-0.5 mm into the stack and
stops by step count. BDC is set by the stop, not by the screw [estimate, standard press practice].

air cylinder 50 mm bore at 6 bar: 1.18 kN direct; 3.5 kN through a 3:1 lever  (DeWalt 200 PSI compressor on hand [repo]; regulated to 6 bar)
air cylinder 63 mm bore at 7 bar: 2.18 kN direct; 6.5 kN through a 3:1 lever  (DeWalt 200 PSI compressor on hand [repo]; regulated to 7 bar)
air cylinder 80 mm bore at 7 bar: 3.52 kN direct; 10.6 kN through a 3:1 lever  (DeWalt 200 PSI compressor on hand [repo]; regulated to 7 bar)
-> a hard stop sets BDC; the cylinder only has to exceed the crimp peak plus the stop's share
