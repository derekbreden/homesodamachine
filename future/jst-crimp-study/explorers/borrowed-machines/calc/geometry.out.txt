3. Laser scoring from above and below (idea b4)
============================================================================
conductor OD 1.7 mm, bundle 0.72 mm, wall 0.49 mm [facts]
Model: vertical beam; removal along the beam scales with cos(incidence) [estimate];
radial depth ~ d0*cos^2(phi), phi = angle of the surface normal from vertical.
  score depth at top/bottom 60% of wall: ligament area 1.18 mm^2 of 1.86 mm^2 unscored; tear-off by tension 4.7-13.0 N (4-11 MPa), unscored 7.5-20.5 N
  score depth at top/bottom 80% of wall: ligament area 1.00 mm^2 of 1.86 mm^2 unscored; tear-off by tension 4.0-11.0 N (4-11 MPa), unscored 7.5-20.5 N
  score depth at top/bottom 95% of wall: ligament area 0.87 mm^2 of 1.86 mm^2 unscored; tear-off by tension 3.5-9.6 N (4-11 MPa), unscored 7.5-20.5 N
The thin ligament sits at the flanks (phi near 90 deg), where the beam grazes. Tilting the fixture
+/-45 deg for two more passes scores the flanks too:
  with the extra 45 deg passes: ligament 0.40 mm^2 -> 1.6-4.4 N
Compare: a 22 AWG conductor breaks at ~85-100 N [facts]; the cassette clamps the insulation, so a
slug pull of 5-15 N per conductor is carried by the clamp, not the strands.
