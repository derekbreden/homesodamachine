5. The reject turn: blow-off window between 'crimpers clear' and 'feed moves'
==============================================================================
  10 s per turn: crimpers clear at 220 deg, feed starts at ~266 deg: 1.27 s, or any length if the shaft stops there
  40 s per turn: crimpers clear at 220 deg, feed starts at ~266 deg: 5.06 s, or any length if the shaft stops there
  A crimped-empty contact weighs 0.42 mN [facts: 0.043 g]. An air jet from a 1 mm nozzle at
    1 bar gives ~39 mN at the nozzle [estimate: ~p*A/2]: 93 x its weight
    3 bar gives ~118 mN at the nozzle [estimate: ~p*A/2]: 279 x its weight
  -> the jet has force to spare; what can stop it is a contact still gripped by the stripper plate or
     wedged in the anvil nest [assumption]; the after-picture shows the anvil empty or not.
