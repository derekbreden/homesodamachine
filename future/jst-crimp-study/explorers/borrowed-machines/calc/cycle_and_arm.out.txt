2. Cheap servo arm: tool-tip wander from joint backlash (idea b5)
============================================================================
backlash 0.87 deg per joint: worst stack 11.2 mm, RSS 6.0 mm at the tool tip
repeatability 0.17 deg per joint: worst stack 2.2 mm, RSS 1.2 mm at the tool tip
Published SO-101 repeatability: +/-2-4 mm [source]. Dobot MG400: +/-0.05 mm [source].
XH targets: conductor-barrel opening ~1.7-1.9 mm wide; housing cavity entry ~2 mm; strip 2.4 mm [facts].
-> the cheap arm can only feed docks and funnels with >= 5 mm lead-in; the precision must live in the
   station it docks into. The MG400 class can present a conductor directly.
