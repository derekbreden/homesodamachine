==============================================================================
3. Frame stiffness for crimp-height repeatability
==============================================================================
  force scatter +/-300 N, crimp-height budget +/-0.01 mm -> loop stiffness >= 30 kN/mm
  force scatter +/-300 N, crimp-height budget +/-0.02 mm -> loop stiffness >= 15 kN/mm
  force scatter +/-600 N, crimp-height budget +/-0.01 mm -> loop stiffness >= 60 kN/mm
  force scatter +/-600 N, crimp-height budget +/-0.02 mm -> loop stiffness >= 30 kN/mm
  A printed PETG loop is tens to a hundred times softer than steel [estimate]; the
  force loop (anvil -> frame -> eccentric bearing -> rod -> punch) is steel plate and
  bearings, and printed parts carry only the light cams.

==============================================================================
4. One revolution per conductor: timing
==============================================================================
    0-30  deg    3.3 s  index ribbon carriage to conductor k                 stepper, cam switch triggers
   30-70  deg    4.4 s  selector lifts k; fork closes behind strip line      face cam, printed
   70-130 deg    6.7 s  strip jaws close, pull 3 mm, open (slug drops)       two face cams
  130-170 deg    4.4 s  lay-in finger sets conductor into contact            face cam
  170-240 deg    7.8 s  punch down: wings curl, compaction, BDC at 240       steel eccentric
  230-240 deg    1.1 s  carrier tab sheared at BDC                           same stroke
  240-290 deg    5.6 s  punch up                                             steel eccentric
  290-330 deg    4.4 s  camera frame + proof pull 15-20 N                    cam switch + spring
  330-360 deg    3.3 s  fork opens, selector down, strip feed pawl advances  face cam + pawl
  compaction (last 22 deg) lasts 2.4 s at 40 s/rev: slow enough to log
  force against angle with an HX711 load-cell amplifier at its 80 Hz setting
  -> 196 samples through compaction [source: prior-art §6, SparkFun HX711 guide]
  per unit: 53 revolutions = 35 min of cycling, plus cassette changes
  mean shaft power over the compaction: 0.16 W
