1. Continuity through the spool
==============================================================================
  15.24 m of one conductor: 0.89 ohm
   8.00 m of one conductor: 0.47 ohm
   2.00 m of one conductor: 0.12 ohm
  slip ring contact: tens to hundreds of milliohm [assumption; Adafruit does not state]
  -> open (infinite) vs good (<~1.5 ohm) is trivially separated by a GPIO + resistor
     divider or an ADC; adjacent short (a stray strand bridging two contacts) shows as
     a low resistance between two conductors that should read open.
  -> a crimp's own resistance (1-10 milliohm, 10-20 max per JST rating [mfr S1]) is
     invisible in series with ~0.9 ohm of spool; this test sees opens, shorts and
     which conductor went to which contact, not crimp quality.

2. Spool turns per unit, and over a spool's life
==============================================================================
  hub 60 mm, full 140 mm (mean 100 mm) [assumption]:
    3P:  5.9 turns per unit,   49 turns per spool
    4P:  8.9 turns per unit,   49 turns per spool
    5P:  4.3 turns per unit,   49 turns per spool
  hub 100 mm, full 160 mm (mean 130 mm) [assumption]:
    3P:  4.5 turns per unit,   37 turns per spool
    4P:  6.9 turns per unit,   37 turns per spool
    5P:  3.3 turns per unit,   37 turns per spool
  -> a lead from the spool's inner end would wind up 30-80 turns over a spool: a slip
     ring on the spool axle carries the test circuits (5 conductors on a 6-circuit ring).

3. Guillotine force, cutting the loom free after feed-out
==============================================================================
  tau 120 MPa, 3 conductors: straight blade ~  115 N
  tau 120 MPa, 4 conductors: straight blade ~  153 N
  tau 120 MPa, 5 conductors: straight blade ~  191 N
  tau 120 MPa, 9 conductors: straight blade ~  344 N
  tau 200 MPa, 3 conductors: straight blade ~  187 N
  tau 200 MPa, 4 conductors: straight blade ~  250 N
  tau 200 MPa, 5 conductors: straight blade ~  312 N
  tau 200 MPa, 9 conductors: straight blade ~  562 N
  A scissor (angled) blade cuts one conductor at a time: ~40-65 N peak [calc, per conductor].
  NEMA 17 at 0.3 N*m on a Tr8 lead 8 mm (eta 0.3): 71 N
  NEMA 17 at 0.3 N*m on a Tr8 lead 2 mm (eta 0.3): 283 N
  -> a Tr8x2 lead screw or a lever does a straight cut; an angled blade needs almost nothing.
