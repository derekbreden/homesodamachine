
==============================================================================
1. What each transfer delivers, and what a lead-in captures
==============================================================================
  dowel into 4 mm hole, pointed to 1 mm tip: captures +/-1.5 mm
  dowel into 6 mm hole, pointed to 2 mm tip: captures +/-2.0 mm
  dowel into 8 mm hole, pointed to 2 mm tip: captures +/-3.0 mm
  three balls into three printed V-grooves (Maxwell coupling): captures about +/- half
  the groove width, e.g. 8 mm grooves -> +/-3 mm; repeats to ~0.01-0.05 mm when the
  grooves are steel or hard inserts [estimate; principle from Wikipedia 'Kinematic coupling']

Transfer                               delivers (+/- mm)   note
  person drops cassette in a nest        2-5                 hands are fine at this
  carousel, NEMA17 + GT2 belt on rim, R=200 0.20 step, ~0.5 play rim ratio 31:1
  carousel + spring ball detent          0.1-0.3             detent notch in rim
  linear belt conveyor, stepper          0.5-1               
  SO-101 class printed arm               1-3                 [estimate]; hobby servos
  Dobot MG400                            0.05                [source: Pololu listing, repeatability]
-> every transfer above lands inside a +/-1.5-3 mm lead-in; none of them needs to be
   precise if the station lifts the cassette onto its own pins before it works.

==============================================================================
2. Lateral chain: cassette datum -> conductor over the open contact
==============================================================================
allowed: strands into conductor barrel +/-0.47 mm; insulation (1.7+0.1)
into the narrowest clone insulation barrel +/-0.33 mm
  station slide, lead screw, per step                        +/-0.02
  cassette on station dowels (steel pins in printed bores)   +/-0.05
  comb slot 1.85 wide on a 1.7+/-0.1 conductor               +/-0.10
  conductor tip wander, 8 mm stick-out, light touch          +/-0.20
  contact on the anvil (strip track, pilot hole)             +/-0.05
  worst case +/-0.42, RSS +/-0.24 against +/-0.33
-> the free tip is the largest term; a fork or funnel closing 2-4 mm behind the strip
   line at the crimp station replaces it with that tool's own +/-0.05.
  with a fork: worst +/-0.27, RSS +/-0.13

==============================================================================
3. Axial chain: where the insulation edge lands between the barrels
==============================================================================
window: insulation edge between conductor barrel and insulation barrel, strands
visible past the front of the conductor barrel; ~+/-0.3 mm usable [estimate from
clone barrel lengths ~1.25-1.5 mm and a ~0.5-0.8 mm window]
  trim blade to cassette datum                               +/-0.05
  cassette to station                                        +/-0.05
  strip length (V-jaw at 2.4 mm, slug tears)                 +/-0.20
  lift retraction scatter (s=20, h=8, split +/-3 mm)         +/-0.27
  wire stop in the die (if used, replaces lift scatter)      +/-0.05
  no wire stop: worst +/-0.57, RSS +/-0.34
  with a wire stop: worst +/-0.35, RSS +/-0.22
-> the strip length and the split-point scatter matter most. Trimming and stripping
   in the pose the crimp uses (same station, same lift) removes the scatter term.
