-> A rewind reel with an 80 mm hub radius (OD ~240 mm full, 15.24 m of any width) adds no set
   and brings the inner end out through the hub. It does not remove curl already in the

   or at the machine's feed, does. A clamp holds the ribbon flat up to its face, so curl
   matters only in the free length past the clamp: 20-40 mm of split conductors.

==========================================================================================

  wave 1 p1, strip elsewhere, no wire stop                               worst +/-0.65  RSS +/-0.35
  lift once, strip in pose, contact by box front                         worst +/-0.47  RSS +/-0.32
  lift once, strip in pose, contact by neck blade                        worst +/-0.29  RSS +/-0.21
  lift once, camera measures bare length, Y corrects; neck blade         worst +/-0.14  RSS +/-0.09
  lift once, camera; contact on a post, offset measured on the post (x1) worst +/-0.12  RSS +/-0.07
  touch-off on the strand tips (no camera), neck blade                   worst +/-0.35  RSS +/-0.22
-> Doing trim, strip and crimp to conductor k in one pose removes the split-point term. The
   strip-length tear is then the largest term left, and only a measurement of where the edge
   actually is (camera on a backlit tip, or a scored edge) takes it out. Touch-off finds the
   strand tips, which is the wrong end for the insulation edge.

  F 220 N, roller 16 mm dia x 8 mm on a R60 lobe, E 8 GPa: peak contact  106 MPa
  F 220 N, roller 16 mm dia x 16 mm on a R60 lobe, E 5 GPa: peak contact   59 MPa

==========================================================================================

conductor centre plane. Each crown is cut to a chord; what is left to tear is the band |z|<zs
of every jacket, plus the web in the valley (web neck thickness unmeasured). Strand bundle
radius 0.36 mm, jacket OD 1.7 mm (wall 0.49 mm) [digest].

  Tearing from the two score lines takes less than the tension bound: the digest gives 3-15 N per
  conductor for a 0.15-0.3 mm ligament cut all round, and borrowed-machines 4-11 N scored top and
  bottom to 80 % [calc geometry §3]. A 5P end is ~20-75 N of pull: a lead screw or a hand lever.

Margins that set the ligament (straight blades):
   strand bundle off-centre in its jacket [assumption]          +/-0.05-0.10 mm
   blade stop to channel floor (steel stop, printed floor)      +/-0.02-0.03 mm
   jacket OD tolerance / 2 (1.7 +/- 0.1 -> centre height)       +/-0.05-0.05 mm
   channel floor flatness under the clamp                       +/-0.02-0.03 mm
   RSS +/-0.08-0.12 mm  -> a 0.15 mm ligament is inside the scatter at the high end; 0.20-0.30 mm keeps the blade off the strands

Pitch error: straight blades cut every crown at the same height whatever the conductor's x,
so the ribbon's +/-0.1 mm per conductor (0.43 mm worst from a centred datum on 9 [ribbon-as-
pallet calc §1]) does not matter. Scalloped cutters (US 4,046,045) cut closer at the flanks but
their notches must sit on each conductor: an offset d moves the flank cut d toward the strands.
   scallop ligament 0.15 mm, notch offset 0.10 mm -> flank ligament +0.05 mm
   scallop ligament 0.15 mm, notch offset 0.20 mm -> flank ligament -0.05 mm  (cuts strands)
   scallop ligament 0.15 mm, notch offset 0.43 mm -> flank ligament -0.28 mm  (cuts strands)
   scallop ligament 0.25 mm, notch offset 0.10 mm -> flank ligament +0.15 mm
   scallop ligament 0.25 mm, notch offset 0.20 mm -> flank ligament +0.05 mm
   scallop ligament 0.25 mm, notch offset 0.43 mm -> flank ligament -0.18 mm  (cuts strands)
   scallop ligament 0.35 mm, notch offset 0.10 mm -> flank ligament +0.25 mm
   scallop ligament 0.35 mm, notch offset 0.20 mm -> flank ligament +0.15 mm
   scallop ligament 0.35 mm, notch offset 0.43 mm -> flank ligament -0.08 mm  (cuts strands)

Strip line after the fan: the strip line is straight across the webbed ribbon; fanning 1.7 ->
2.5 mm afterwards pulls the outer conductors' fronts back [calc P exchange_borrowed §7]:
