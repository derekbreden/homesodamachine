pilot holes on each contact's centreline under the wire's path [digest]. So every conductor

strip, track and shear there: no neighbour can DROP under the anvil level. Above is the punch.

13. b5: SO-101 backlash if every dock is approached the same way
==============================================================================
  borrowed calc: backlash 0.87 deg/joint -> 6.0 mm RSS; repeatability 0.17 deg -> 1.2 mm RSS
  approaching every dock from above with the same payload keeps each joint loaded the same way,
  so taught waypoints absorb the backlash and ~1.2 mm RSS (2.2 worst) remains: inside 5 mm lead-ins.
  A flip reverses gravity on the wrist only: 0.87 deg x ~10-80 mm = 0.15-1.2 mm.
