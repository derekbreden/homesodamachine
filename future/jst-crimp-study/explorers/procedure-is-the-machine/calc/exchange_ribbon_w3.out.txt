1. Equal-path fan: grooves of equal length so tips and strip lines stay on one line
========================================================================================
ribbon-as-pallet's fan rule (R 5 mm, 30 deg S, then straight), each ribbon fanned alone.
Every groove spans the outer conductor's axial length L_f. An inner groove gets a
vertical cosine hump of height H over the whole L_f (overlapping its own lateral S) so
its path equals the outer one's. Then a flush cut and strip made before the fan stay on
one line after it; every tip recedes by the same r_max, which the split must allow.
  3P at 2.5 mm: L_f   3.9 mm, all tips recede 0.11 mm | offset 0.0: +0.11 mm -> hump 0.4 mm, R_min 1.9; offset 0.8: 0
  4P at 2.5 mm: L_f   4.7 mm, all tips recede 0.20 mm | offset 0.4: +0.16 mm -> hump 0.6 mm, R_min 2.0; offset 1.2: 0
  5P at 2.5 mm: L_f   5.5 mm, all tips recede 0.31 mm | offset 0.0: +0.31 mm -> hump 0.8 mm, R_min 1.8; offset 0.8: +0.20 mm -> hump 0.7 mm, R_min 2.2; offset 1.6: 0
  3P at 5.0 mm: L_f   8.4 mm, all tips recede 0.76 mm | offset 0.0: +0.76 mm -> hump 1.7 mm, R_min 2.1; offset 3.3: 0
  4P at 5.0 mm: L_f  11.3 mm, all tips recede 1.20 mm | offset 1.6: +0.88 mm -> hump 2.1 mm, R_min 3.0; offset 4.9: 0
  5P at 5.0 mm: L_f  14.1 mm, all tips recede 1.65 mm | offset 0.0: +1.65 mm -> hump 3.2 mm, R_min 3.2; offset 3.3: +0.88 mm -> hump 2.4 mm, R_min 4.3; offset 6.6: 0
  3P at 7.1 mm: L_f  12.0 mm, all tips recede 1.32 mm | offset 0.0: +1.32 mm -> hump 2.6 mm, R_min 2.8; offset 5.4: 0
  4P at 7.1 mm: L_f  16.7 mm, all tips recede 2.05 mm | offset 2.7: +1.45 mm -> hump 3.3 mm, R_min 4.3; offset 8.1: 0
  5P at 7.1 mm: L_f  21.4 mm, all tips recede 2.77 mm | offset 0.0: +2.77 mm -> hump 5.1 mm, R_min 4.5; offset 5.4: +1.45 mm -> hump 3.7 mm, R_min 6.2; offset 10.8: 0
-> at 7.1 mm strip pitch a 5P's centre groove needs a ~5.1 mm hump over ~21 mm, bent at
   ~R 4.5, near the fan rule's own R 5. At 5 mm a ~3.2 mm hump; at 2.5 mm under 1 mm. The
   hump costs fan-block height and leaves the same class of set the S-bends already leave.

How much the fan's shape matters (outer conductor recession, lateral move delta):
  5P to 2.5 mm (delta 1.60) over  8.0 mm: compact R5/30 S 0.31 mm (S itself 5.5 mm long); gentle S over the whole length 0.19 mm; straight diagonal 0.16 mm
  5P to 2.5 mm (delta 1.60) over 15.0 mm: compact R5/30 S 0.31 mm (S itself 5.5 mm long); gentle S over the whole length 0.10 mm; straight diagonal 0.09 mm
  5P to 7.1 mm (delta 10.80) over 21.0 mm: compact R5/30 S 2.77 mm (S itself 21.4 mm long); gentle S over the whole length 3.09 mm; straight diagonal 2.99 mm
  5P to 7.1 mm (delta 10.80) over 30.0 mm: compact R5/30 S 2.77 mm (S itself 21.4 mm long); gentle S over the whole length 2.27 mm; straight diagonal 2.01 mm
  4P to 2.5 mm (delta 1.20) over 15.0 mm: compact R5/30 S 0.20 mm (S itself 4.7 mm long); gentle S over the whole length 0.06 mm; straight diagonal 0.05 mm
-> ribbon-as-pallet's 0.31 mm (5P at 2.5 mm) is the compact S; this explorer's wave-2
   0.09-0.16 mm (exchange_borrowed §7) is the straight diagonal over the split. Both are
   right for their shapes; the groove shape is a design choice that sets the recession.

========================================================================================

each +/-0.25 [source S19-S22]; JST catalog end-view envelope 1.95 x 2.4 [mfr S1].
Whether a drawn width is the inside opening or the outside of the wings is not stated.
  conductor barrel / strands   drawn = inside  : half-gap nominal +0.54 mm, worst (min W - tol, max OD) +0.34 mm
  conductor barrel / strands   drawn = outside : half-gap nominal +0.34 mm, worst (min W - tol, max OD) +0.14 mm
  insulation barrel / jacket   drawn = inside  : half-gap nominal +0.51 mm, worst (min W - tol, max OD) +0.20 mm
  insulation barrel / jacket   drawn = outside : half-gap nominal +0.31 mm, worst (min W - tol, max OD) +0.01 mm
  JST 1.95 mm envelope read as open insulation wings: inside 1.55 mm against a 1.7 mm jacket -> -0.08 mm (interference; the jacket must be pressed in)

-> a2's '+/-0.5 mm, five times the placement error' holds at the clone drawings' nominal read
   as inside widths. Read as outside widths at the low tolerance the jacket's capture falls to
   ~0.1 mm or below, under the RSS placement error. The $4.71 strip measures which.

5. a2e's press: the applicator's standard crank against a short eccentric
========================================================================================

contact's wings (2.75-3.2 mm) plus margin. Force model [estimate, digest]: compaction
F_peak falling linearly to 0 over the last dc mm; 300 N of wing curl from 0.2 to 0.9 mm;
ram return spring F_s acting throughout. Torque = F x throw x sin(theta) (rod obliquity
ignored). 10 s per revolution, HX711 at 80 Hz.
