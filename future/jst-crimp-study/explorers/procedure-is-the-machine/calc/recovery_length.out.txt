1. Cut-back for one whole-end redo
==============================================================================
contact rear sits 3.5-4.7 mm behind the conductor tip
cut-back per redo = 4.5 to 6.7 mm; design figure 6 mm [calc]
the web split behind the housing must also be extended by the same amount

3. Cut-first orders: a loom carries a redo reserve; past it the loom is scrap
==============================================================================
reserve = how much shorter than its cut length a loom may end up (a Derek question)
reserve    0 mm -> 0 redo(s) allowed; p= 2.0%: expected scrapped looms per unit 1.067 (per 61-unit program 65.1)
reserve    0 mm -> 0 redo(s) allowed; p=10.0%: expected scrapped looms per unit 4.720 (per 61-unit program 287.9)
reserve    6 mm -> 1 redo(s) allowed; p= 2.0%: expected scrapped looms per unit 0.084 (per 61-unit program 5.1)
reserve    6 mm -> 1 redo(s) allowed; p=10.0%: expected scrapped looms per unit 1.625 (per 61-unit program 99.1)
reserve   12 mm -> 2 redo(s) allowed; p= 2.0%: expected scrapped looms per unit 0.007 (per 61-unit program 0.4)
reserve   12 mm -> 2 redo(s) allowed; p=10.0%: expected scrapped looms per unit 0.570 (per 61-unit program 34.8)
reserve   20 mm -> 3 redo(s) allowed; p= 2.0%: expected scrapped looms per unit 0.001 (per 61-unit program 0.0)
reserve   20 mm -> 3 redo(s) allowed; p=10.0%: expected scrapped looms per unit 0.204 (per 61-unit program 12.4)
A scrapped loom loses its ribbon (100-600 mm) and, if the far end was made first, its
Fastons/ferrules/IDC too. Doing the XH end first puts the risk before the hand work.

==============================================================================
4. Cut-last order (terminate at the spool): a redo costs spool, never a loom
==============================================================================
p= 0.5%: expected spool consumed by redos    1.7 mm per unit (0.03% of ~6.0 m of ribbon per unit)
p= 2.0%: expected spool consumed by redos    6.9 mm per unit (0.12% of ~6.0 m of ribbon per unit)
p= 5.0%: expected spool consumed by redos   18.8 mm per unit (0.31% of ~6.0 m of ribbon per unit)
p=10.0%: expected spool consumed by redos   43.4 mm per unit (0.72% of ~6.0 m of ribbon per unit)
No reserve is carried on any loom, no loom length depends on how many redos it took,
and a pair's two ribbons stay equal length because each is cut after it passes.

==============================================================================
5. Crimps wasted by a fault, by when it is found (p = 5%, 5P end)
==============================================================================
checked after every crimp:   0.66 crimps made then cut off, per end attempt
checked after the whole end: 1.13 crimps made then cut off, per end attempt
Contacts cost ~$0.01-0.05 [mfr/source, xh-facts §6]; the waste that matters is machine
time and, in cut-first orders, the loom's length reserve, which is the same either way.
Checking after every crimp matters most when a fault means the machine is out of
adjustment, so the next crimps would fail too.
