C. Direct screw (no linkage): force at the die equals screw thrust
==============================================================================
  NEMA 23 1.9 N*m, 2 mm trapezoid           :  1.07 kN at 60 % of holding torque
  NEMA 23 1.9 N*m, 1605 ball screw (5 mm)   :  1.29 kN at 60 % of holding torque
  NEMA 23 + 5:1 belt, 1605 ball screw       :  6.12 kN at 60 % of holding torque
  NEMA 17 0.4 N*m, 2 mm trapezoid           :  0.23 kN at 60 % of holding torque
  BDC sensitivity: 1:1. A lost step or frame stretch moves BDC directly.
  2 mm lead, 200 full steps: 10 um/step; with 1/16 microstepping 0.6 um nominal,
  but microstep position under kN load is not trustworthy [estimate]. A direct
  screw needs a hard stop or a gauge on the die to set crimp height.

D. Arbor press (rack and pinion) driven at its handle
==============================================================================
    500 N pushed at the handle end (20:1):  10.0 kN at the ram (if pushed square to the handle)
   1000 N pushed at the handle end (20:1):  20.0 kN at the ram (if pushed square to the handle)
   1500 N pushed at the handle end (20:1):  30.0 kN at the ram (if pushed square to the handle)
  actuator stroke 50 mm at a 250 mm handle radius -> pinion turns 11.5 deg -> ram moves  2.5 mm (pinion radius 12.5 mm, assumed)
  actuator stroke 100 mm at a 250 mm handle radius -> pinion turns 22.9 deg -> ram moves  5.0 mm (pinion radius 12.5 mm, assumed)
  No geometric BDC: rack position follows the handle 1:1 through the pinion.
  -> the crimp height has to come from a hard stop at the die, not from the drive.
