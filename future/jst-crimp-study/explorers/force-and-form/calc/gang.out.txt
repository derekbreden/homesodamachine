1. Station pitch
============================================================================
insulation crimper outer width 3.5-4.4 mm; open insulation wings 2.46-3.0 mm
-> minimum station pitch ~3.8-4.7 mm with 0.3 mm air [estimate]
-> 2.5 mm (the housing pitch) is impossible; 5.0 mm (every other cavity) fits;
   the carrier strip's own pitch (unmeasured; 7-9.5 mm read off clone drawings)
   fits with room to spare.
  3P at 5.0 mm pitch: span 10.0 mm, outer conductor moves  3.30 mm from its place in the ribbon
  3P at 7.5 mm pitch: span 15.0 mm, outer conductor moves  5.80 mm from its place in the ribbon
  3P at 9.0 mm pitch: span 18.0 mm, outer conductor moves  7.30 mm from its place in the ribbon
  4P at 5.0 mm pitch: span 15.0 mm, outer conductor moves  4.95 mm from its place in the ribbon
  4P at 7.5 mm pitch: span 22.5 mm, outer conductor moves  8.70 mm from its place in the ribbon
  4P at 9.0 mm pitch: span 27.0 mm, outer conductor moves 10.95 mm from its place in the ribbon
  5P at 5.0 mm pitch: span 20.0 mm, outer conductor moves  6.60 mm from its place in the ribbon
  5P at 7.5 mm pitch: span 30.0 mm, outer conductor moves 11.60 mm from its place in the ribbon
  5P at 9.0 mm pitch: span 36.0 mm, outer conductor moves 14.60 mm from its place in the ribbon

2. Stroke force for a whole ribbon end
============================================================================
  3 stations, central:   5.0 kN at BDC
  3 stations, high   :   7.3 kN at BDC
  4 stations, central:   6.7 kN at BDC
  4 stations, high   :   9.7 kN at BDC
  5 stations, central:   8.4 kN at BDC
  5 stations, high   :  12.2 kN at BDC
  VEVOR 12 t shop press ~118 kN [calc C1]; 1 t arbor press ~9 kN [source HF 59766]
  -> the shop press does any ribbon end with huge margin; a 1 t arbor press does
     3 stations at the high estimate, 5 at the central one only.

3. Unbalanced stroke in a two-post cassette
============================================================================
  3 st. at 5.0 mm: outer station short by  1216 N -> moment  6.08 N*m, post side load ~ 101 N; clearance tilt at the outer station  2.5 um
  3 st. at 9.0 mm: outer station short by  1216 N -> moment 10.94 N*m, post side load ~ 182 N; clearance tilt at the outer station  4.5 um
  5 st. at 5.0 mm: outer station short by  1216 N -> moment 12.16 N*m, post side load ~ 203 N; clearance tilt at the outer station  5.0 um
  5 st. at 9.0 mm: outer station short by  1216 N -> moment 21.89 N*m, post side load ~ 365 N; clearance tilt at the outer station  9.0 um
  -> a two-post cassette with 10 um clearance keeps station-to-station height
     within a few um; the stations' own die heights have to match to ~0.01 mm,
     which means grinding the anvils as one block or shimming each [estimate].

4. Does one bad station show in the summed force?
============================================================================
  3 stations, one with no conductor keeping 40 % of its force: sum drops 20.0 %
  3 stations, one with no conductor keeping 60 % of its force: sum drops 13.3 %
  4 stations, one with no conductor keeping 40 % of its force: sum drops 15.0 %
  4 stations, one with no conductor keeping 60 % of its force: sum drops 10.0 %
  5 stations, one with no conductor keeping 40 % of its force: sum drops 12.0 %
  5 stations, one with no conductor keeping 60 % of its force: sum drops  8.0 %
  force without conductor, as a share of force with it, is unknown for XH
  ("headroom"); 40-60 % is an assumption. Industrial monitors work in a ~+/-4 %
  band [source: Assembly Magazine]. A summed signal sees a missing conductor
  in a 3-station gang and probably a 5-station gang; it cannot say which one,
  and it sees nothing finer. The camera, before the stroke, is the station check.
