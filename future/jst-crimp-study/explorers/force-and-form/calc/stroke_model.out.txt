3. What a slow stroke changes (bounded, each with its label)
============================================================================
flow stress at press speed is ~5 % above quasi-static -> a slow stroke needs ~4.8 % less peak force for the same geometry
flow stress at press speed is ~7.7 % above quasi-static -> a slow stroke needs ~7.1 % less peak force for the same geometry
elastic springback scales with flow stress/E: a few % smaller when slow (a thousandth of a mm on ~0.01-0.03 mm) [estimate]
central: adiabatic upper bound on temperature rise 16 K (at production speed); a stroke of seconds conducts it away [calc]
high: adiabatic upper bound on temperature rise 26 K (at production speed); a stroke of seconds conducts it away [calc]
forming travel 0.92 mm in   0.1 s ->    9.200 mm/s (production press ~ 500 mm/s peak)
forming travel 0.92 mm in   1.0 s ->    0.920 mm/s (production press ~ 500 mm/s peak)
forming travel 0.92 mm in  10.0 s ->    0.092 mm/s (production press ~ 500 mm/s peak)
forming travel 0.92 mm in  60.0 s ->    0.015 mm/s (production press ~ 500 mm/s peak)
inertia: a crank press reaches BDC with ram and flywheel momentum; a slow drive reaches it quasi-statically, so BDC is set only by geometry and stiffness [estimate]
