# Pump-first upper bay

This native layout study places the scanned diaphragm pump across X and grows
the funnel 60 mm aft above the cold-core lid, within the **215 mm enclosure width**.
The funnel holds **715.057 mL to the brim** and **463.244 mL with 10 mm headroom**.
The frozen installed reference holds 455.182 mL to the brim; the geometric
increase is 259.875 mL, 57.09%. A 500 mL fill fits below the cover's skirt;
a 600 mL fill exceeds the closed-cover headspace.

Open [the component viewer](index.html) to rotate the complete arrangement,
compare the reference, hide the funnel/roof, and inspect individual fittings,
tubes, insulation, looms, printed hosts and fasteners. Coordinates are the
enclosure's world millimetres: X is width, +Y is aft, and Z is height.
The **Tubes** and **Wires** controls independently toggle tube runs with their
insulation and wire looms. Both are hidden by default; fittings, connectors and
mounts remain visible. Inspecting a hidden tube or wire enables its category.

The study boundary is the upper bay aft of the enlarged funnel, including its
side/rear walls, roof and cold-core lid. The floor is Z 253.4. The lower cold core
and forward cartridge interfaces provide fixed routing boundaries.

## Arrangement

The pump remains upright on its four feet, with its motor toward +X. Its
15.1 mm outer-diameter braided suction and discharge hoses retain exact 15.9 mm
centreline bends, clamps and engaged barb sleeves. The suction fittings sit on
a lid-rooted seat with a separate bolted upper key. The discharge chain has a
roof-rooted upper capture and removable lower key. The scanned valve and source
manifolds sit ahead of the pump. The water tee's main axis runs fore/aft in the
low east lane; its upward branch feeds the widthwise flavor needle, whose
adjuster faces forward.

The horizontal Mean Well supply sits above both relay modules at the rear of
the lid. Its four native mounting stations have blind insert pilots, washers
and explicit bridge/load webs. The populated controller faces down from the
roof on four blind insert posts with 3 mm end caps; the eight distribution/signal
junctions occupy the west roof platform. Protective earth uses the roof ring
fan at X 51.2, Y 430.5, Z 341.2, clocked 50 degrees. The Class II IRM supply has no earth
terminal.

The ASSE assembly spans X ahead of the pump above its sensed, west-extracting
tray. Two keyed saddles and complete dressed tie bands retain the brass barrel
in a removable carrier. Two independent roof blocks and M3×12 bearing clamps
secure the carrier. The bare valve enters rolled through the fore aperture,
unrolls and lowers before the carrier is inserted and fastened; the purchased
VK valve is installed afterward. The rolled
carbonated-water meter and full 25.4 mm insulation use the east side. The CO₂
regulator occupies the diagonal west lane. The rear water, carbonated-water,
CO₂, C14 and keystone interfaces include their complete fitted receivers and
identification pieces. The complete accepted nameplate/receiver is on the west
wall, with its mating dimensions preserved by a rigid transform. The meter's
two fixed collars have roof-rooted seats and narrow tie bands, including the
locking-head envelopes and the actual lead-root escape.
The Water5 clearance clips each seat's upper east corner. The retained profile
has a complete 24.05 mm crown strip, 3 mm lower bearing walls and unchanged roof
roots; the [fastener check](structure/fastener-native-check.json) records the
3.739614584 mm³ relief in each original 24.8 mm rectangular crown witness and
proves it lies wholly inside that declared tube-clearance tool. This supports a
geometric attachment claim; loaded capacity and lifetime remain unqualified.

The flush tray's [rounded pull-face opening](structure/pan-slot.json) follows
the complete held pan silhouette through the west wall, with 0.25 mm side/top
clearance and 0.5 mm below. Its opening stays above the cold-core lid. The
[discharge support key opening](structure/water5-key-pocket.json) follows the
key's curved bearing with 0.25 mm running clearance and a 12 mm entry sweep.
It preserves the complete fixed seat and blind fastener stock, with 5.316 mm
of exterior east wall remaining.

The [funnel geometry and casting tooling](funnel/README.md) and
[electronics mounts](mounts/README.md) describe the native interfaces. Circuit
routes and pin schedules are in the [wiring manifest](wiring/candidate.json).
The full compressor jacket and lower earth leads terminate at explicit bay
boundaries; their factory interfaces below this study are unlocated in the
reference.

## Evidence and scope

The [integrated native audit](native-audit.json) tests actual B-reps, including
retained neighboring parts. Named barb/terminal contacts and joined print roots
are distinguished from unintended overlap. The
[power member proof](wiring/power-members-check.json) checks each exact cylinder
and arc separately and publishes those same conductor members, with continuous
full-section seams and complete clearance-cutter containment. The
[control terminal proof](wiring/control-terminal-owner-check.json) checks each
route against its own purchased or retained endpoint bodies, with explicit
local continuities at nominal fanout joins. The
[joined print stock](structure/print-parts.json) binds the complete printed
hosts and recut blind pilots to their parent parts. Native slice and emitted
support records are in [manufacturing](manufacturing/).
The [final material certificate](structure/print-material-union-check.json)
checks that every cold-imported print retains the complete union of its
declared parents and hosts after the exact pilot and clearance recuts.
It also checks that all six unfused scene parents match the recut parent
material in those print articles.

The rear shell's two local loom pockets retain 3.53 mm and 4.83 mm of roof
stock. Their complete cutter clearance, retained shell material and protected
mounting stock are recorded in `shell_checks.wire_roof_finishes` in the
[shell manifest](structure/candidate.json). These are geometric stock checks;
the remaining roof thickness has no physical load qualification.

The [front contact lead staging](wiring/front-loom-handling.json) preserves the
complete modeled J13 run through the front's 102.2 mm closing travel. The
contact-side joints stay on the front while the free board dressing end is
parked through the empty display passage. The display and J9 loom are installed
after mechanical closure. The viewer's **Front closure staging** arrangement
shows that temporary article. Individual conductor dressing, board termination
and manual access through the open funnel/display apertures remain physical
qualifications.

The factory sequence and roof motion are recorded in
[factory-slide-check.json](structure/factory-slide-check.json). Flexible hose,
vent and wire handling have a different evidentiary scope from rigid clearance.
The unchanged PRV outlet has an explicit temporary tucked pose and pull-loop
reseating method in [prv-factory-handling.json](structure/prv-factory-handling.json).
The [rear roof hatch](structure/roof-hatch.json) provides the supply's vertical
tool approach and carries the discharge anchor and earth fan. Its four recessed
M3 fasteners close on a 4 mm lap. A continuous cast silicone seam and sealed head
recesses finish flush with the roof; bonding, cure and water exclusion remain
physical qualifications. The [hatch hose family](structure/hatch-hose-check.json)
preserves the 193.381496 mm native braid length through its 14 mm landing travel.

This is a geometric layout candidate. Clear solids and successful slicing do
not establish insert pullout, loaded brim support, creep, vibration, molded fit,
drain rate, thermal performance, actual wire dressing or lifetime. Nominal
terminal/fanout envelopes are labeled in their manifests. Existing accepted
articles retain the scope in the
[mechanical qualification records](../../hardware/mechanical-qualification/README.md).

The [IRM-90 controlled drawing](https://www.meanwell.com/Upload/PDF/IRM-90/IRM-90-spec.pdf)
sets the 109×52×33.5 mm supply body and four mounting stations. The
[Mean Well installation guidance](https://www.meanwell.com/Upload/PDF/PCB_EN.pdf)
calls for ventilation, 5 mm under-unit insulation distance with Mylar, and
10 mm surrounding insulation distance for general units. It also calls for
clearance from adjacent heat sources and derating outside the standard mounting
orientation. This candidate has 10 mm native air to
the west junction platform, 10.194 mm to the pump and 6.958 mm to the populated
controller; the supply's rear body is 2 mm from the inner rear-wall plane. It
does not meet the generic 10 mm surrounding-distance guidance throughout.
Supply installation, enclosure ventilation and current derating remain
unqualified; geometric fit does not establish the full-current rating.
The [supply clearance search](routing/psu-surround-feasibility.json) records
the local alternatives. A back-wall orientation requires a broader arrangement
of the relays, rear interfaces and fluid routes to meet the surrounding-distance
guidance.

The [45 mm extension and fore-shifted package probe](routing/psu-coupled-probe.json)
holds 650.106 mL to the brim. Moving the rigid package 8 mm fore and the controller
14 mm fore gives 10 mm rear supply clearance and 11.017 mm to the controller.
That bounded alternative still requires revised suction and backflow supports,
core-mouth clearance, supply-side restraints and boundary routing. Its rigid
preview does not carry the selected arrangement's routing or assembly proofs.

## Reproduce the reviewed geometry

The [reference snapshot](inputs/baseline.json) pins both the original native
STEP and its inspection mesh. The [candidate checkpoint](inputs/candidate-native.json)
pins the reviewed native B-reps and scene manifests. These archives remove any
dependency on a concurrently changing production CAD export.

From the repository root:

```sh
tools/cad-venv/bin/python future/pump-first-layout-study/checkpoint.py restore
tools/cad-venv/bin/python future/pump-first-layout-study/verify_evidence.py
tools/cad-venv/bin/python future/pump-first-layout-study/build_viewer.py
```

Restore writes only `.cache/pump-first-layout/`. Parameterized producers remain
beside each subsystem. The checkpoint is the exact reviewed arrangement;
changing a producer requires fresh integrated occupancy, connected stock,
factory motion and manufacturing checks before a changed arrangement can carry
those results.
The final print producer is `structure/assemble_prints_verified.py`.
