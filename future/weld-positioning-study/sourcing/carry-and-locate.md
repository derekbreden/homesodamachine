Representative parts only: one or a few per role, enough to show that an idea
has a practical route. Observed 2026-09-28 in Derek's Chrome (Amazon ships to
Lincoln 68520). Only Prime listings are recorded. "Product page" means the
listing itself was opened and its Prime badge, price, delivery and stock read
there; "search page" means the values come from the Prime-filtered search
results only.
