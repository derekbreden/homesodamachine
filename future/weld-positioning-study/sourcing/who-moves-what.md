  - Source: https://www.thorlabs.com/item/KB75_M (family page https://www.thorlabs.com/kinematic-bases2?pn=KB75/M) · Observed: 2026-09-28 · Price: $117.27 · Prime: NA
  - Stock/delivery signal: "Available Today"
  - Volume/interchangeability evidence: released June 6 2000, and a lifetime catalogue item. Ball-and-V kinematic bases are a standard optomechanical product from several vendors.
  - Observation vs estimate: read directly: "magnetically coupled using two pairs of high-strength magnets which provide a holding force of 6.5 lbs"; angular repeatability over 50 reseats "mean of 21.20 µrad … maximum of 81.90 µrad". An estimate: at 250 mm from the dot, 82 µrad is 0.02 mm. The 75 mm span needs 40–67 N preload against a 10 N cable pull at 150–250 mm (calc 3), which exceeds its 29 N. That is why the seat in idea 4 is drawn larger (150–200 mm span).
