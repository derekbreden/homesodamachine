# Lincoln advertising options

Research checked **28 September 2026**. These are public-page findings and planning
judgments. A published rate is distinguished from a broker estimate or a price that
requires a quote. Availability, minimum commitments and total delivered costs need
confirmation at booking. The [local plan](README.md) defines the proposed pilot.

## Shortlist

Suggested order reflects the ability to learn from a small number of installations,
show an unfamiliar product and control the commitment. It is not a measured ranking
of advertising performance.

| Option | Price evidence | Role in this plan |
|---|---|---|
| Owner introductions and a local demonstration | Founder time and demo supplies; no purchased-media rate | Establish the offer and hear objections directly |
| Car decals and topper | Local graphics available; custom quote; topper source unverified | Make delivery and installation visible and give neighbors a way to inquire |
| Nextdoor / Facebook / Instagram | Self-service advertising; actual campaign price and delivery need account setup | Small, adjustable local test once the inquiry page and offer exist |
| StarTran through Houck | Direct operator quote required; broker reference below | Investigate promptly because of Derek's interest; buy only against the complete commitment |
| USPS Every Door Direct Mail | Published retail postage **$0.26/piece**, plus printing and preparation | Reach a defined cluster of homes near an installation |
| Country Club neighborhood publication | Quote required; current circulation and representative need confirmation | A possible neighborhood-specific placement or demonstration introduction |
| Nebraska Builders Home & Garden Show | Published standard 10 × 10 booth **$800 nonmember / $600 member**, plus other costs | A later live-demonstration option with substantial staffing time |
| KLIN / NRG Media | Quote required | A local broadcast comparison or a possible business-story pitch |

Sources and the limits of each finding follow. No conversion rates, reach-to-sale
ratios or Lincoln audience sizes are assumed.

## StarTran bus and shelter advertising

The [City of Lincoln's StarTran page](https://www.lincoln.ne.gov/City/Departments/LTU/StarTran)
directs prospective advertisers to Houck Transit Advertising. The
[Houck Lincoln market page](https://houckads.com/markets/lincoln-ne/) lists bus and
shelter placements and identifies **John Young, Market Manager**, at
**john@houckads.com**, **800-777-7290**. It gives no public Lincoln rate card.
Houck says buses generally rotate among routes in most markets; specific Lincoln
route assignment must be confirmed.

There is a public price reference from a separate seller:

| Blue Line Media's Lincoln listing | Published reference |
|---|---|
| Exterior display | $600–$6,000 per display per four weeks |
| Interior display | $45–$65 per display per four weeks |

These are [Blue Line Media's stated averages](https://www.bluelinemedia.com/bus-advertising/lincoln-ne),
**not a Houck quote or a total campaign cost**. The listing does not establish a
minimum purchase, production cost, installation/removal charges or current inventory.
Its other market statistics are not used here.

An exterior ad reaches people on the street as well as people using transit. An
interior card is principally a rider placement. That difference matters to an
installed kitchen appliance even when both formats carry the same message. Compare
an exterior tail or small side panel and a shelter near an appropriate residential
or shopping area; get an interior-card alternative as a separate option.

The useful quote is the smallest complete campaign Houck will actually sell:

- Format, number of faces/vehicles and the actual minimum term.
- Media, design, production, installation, removal and taxes, itemized.
- One-display availability and any short-term or remnant inventory.
- Route rotation; any location control for shelters; any route guarantee in writing.
- When the ad becomes visible, how display is documented and what happens if a bus
  is unavailable during the paid period.
- Cancellation, renewal, creative replacement and payment terms.

Use `display count × periods × rate + fixed charges` to compare quotations. The
shortest period and the cheapest individual panel may not form a purchasable package.
The [draft request](working-materials.md#transit-quote-request) asks for that package
without inventing a launch date.

## Digital discovery

[Nextdoor's small-business page](https://business.nextdoor.com/en-us/small-business)
offers free Business Pages and Business Posts alongside paid advertising. Its
[ZIP-targeting guidance](https://business.nextdoor.com/en-us/small-business/resources/blog/zip-code-targeting-on-nextdoor)
describes radius and ZIP selection; the exact controls and minimum spend available
to this account need checking when a campaign is prepared.

Use a business post to introduce the maker, the kitchen use and the local installation
idea. If a paid test follows, send it to a local offer with a real price and inquiry
path. The **$300 proposed test limit** in the plan is our planning allowance, not a
Nextdoor rate or an expected result.

[Meta offers video placements on Facebook and Instagram Reels](https://www.facebook.com/business/ads/facebook-instagram-reels-ads).
A short real pour followed by Derek explaining the installation offer is a suitable
creative hypothesis. Confirm local targeting and budget controls in Ads Manager;
there is no verified Lincoln cost-per-click or cost-per-lead in this research.

Choose one platform for the first paid test. Use the actual household location to
qualify inquiries rather than treating the platform's location targeting as a
service-area guarantee. Ask flavor and price questions before scheduling visits.

## Neighborhood mail

[USPS Every Door Direct Mail](https://www.usps.com/business/every-door-direct-mail.htm)
lists EDDM Retail postage at **$0.26 per piece**. It supports route selection using
neighborhood geography and aggregate Census information, with retail mailings of
200–5,000 pieces per day per ZIP code and no special mailing permit.

Illustration: 1,000 pieces at that rate means **$260 in postage**, before artwork,
printing and preparation. Actual quantity comes from the selected carrier routes;
it cannot be assumed to equal exactly 1,000 or a handpicked list of homes. Confirm
the residential route selection, required piece dimensions and final counts in the
USPS tool before requesting print prices.

This is a candidate for a neighborhood where an installation or demonstration already
provides context. The card can show a real faucet in a real kitchen, explain the local
installation offer and invite a demonstration. Use a unique source link and retain
the total mailed count. Route demographics help choose an area; they do not establish
that a particular household drinks soda or wants the machine.

## Country Club neighborhood publication

The [Country Club Neighborhood Association newsletter page](https://www.ccnalinc.org/news-events/newsletters.html)
identifies its neighborhood publication with N2 Publishing and lists
**kristin.holmes@n2pub.com** for publication inquiries. The
[association's general contact](https://www.ccnalinc.org/) is
**ccnalinc@gmail.com**. Confirm the current advertising representative through them.

Current advertising prices, household delivery counts and minimum terms are
unverified. The Chronicle PDFs linked on that page are archival material and are
not a current rate card. Obtain a present circulation map, issue frequency and
one-insertion price before comparing this with direct mail.

The [city's neighborhood association map](https://www.lincoln.ne.gov/City/Departments/Urban-Development/Neighborhoods)
provides a starting point for other neighborhood contacts. Ask about a small paid
placement or an invited demonstration. A neighborhood association's presence does
not establish that it sells advertising or endorses the product.

## Vehicle decals and roof topper

Two local service providers offer a concrete starting point:

| Provider | Verified capability | Contact |
|---|---|---|
| [Signs Now Lincoln](https://www.signsnow.com/lincoln/vehicle-graphics) | Vehicle lettering, magnets and wraps | 402-441-9383; 5555 South 48th St, Suite B |
| [Vivid Sign Co.](https://www.vividsignco.com/) | Vehicle branding and other printed signage | Quote request through its website |

Neither reviewed page establishes a price for this car or availability of a suitable
roof topper. Send the same brief and vehicle photographs to both for comparable
quotes. Ask for door/rear decals, a removable alternative, and a separately priced
topper if they can supply or letter one. Confirm artwork, application, removal,
material life, paint compatibility and any vehicle-specific limitations.

Use [the existing brand assets](../../brand/README.md). The roof topper's mounting and
driving rating must come from its supplier and fit the actual roof; the presence of
vehicle-graphics services is not evidence of roof-topper compatibility. The car and
topper dimensions remain inputs to collect.

## Home & Garden Show

The [HBAL exhibitor page](https://hbal.org/builders-home-garden-show/exhibitors/)
publishes **$800 for a standard 10 × 10 booth for nonmembers**, **$600 for members**,
and full-color program ads starting at **$250**. Utilities and other exhibitor services
are ordered separately. Membership has its own cost; the member booth figure is not
the total cost of joining and exhibiting. Contact: **info@hbal.org**.

The [organizer's event page](https://hbal.org/builders-home-garden-show/)
lists **12–14 February 2027**, at Sandhills Global Event Center. This is an external
event date, not a product deadline. Published opening hours total 23 hours, before
setup, teardown and follow-up.

A live pour could answer taste and credibility questions efficiently. The full cost
also needs the demo setup, water, power, signage, supplies, transport and staffing.
Confirm beverage-sampling and CO₂ requirements with the organizer when considering
a working demonstration. A non-dispensing display would test a different experience.

Consider this after the local installation process and demonstration are repeatable.
First visiting the show or arranging a small demonstration with a kitchen professional
can inform a later booth decision. Neither a partnership nor booth availability is
established by this research.

## Local radio and a business story

[KLIN's advertising page](https://klin.com/advertise/) offers NRG Media radio and
digital campaigns and lists **Jen Witherby, jen@broadcasthouse.com**, for sales.
[KLIN's contact page](https://klin.com/contact-us/) gives **402-475-4567** and
**growlincoln@klin.com** for Grow Lincoln. Rates are quote-only in the reviewed pages.

Request the smallest complete schedule, including production, station, dayparts,
spot count and any added digital placement. Determine how much of the audience is
inside the actual service area. A broad regional audience can generate inquiries
that the local offer cannot serve.

A maker solving a recurring household annoyance is also a possible local business
story. An editorial pitch is separate from purchasing a schedule; coverage is not
guaranteed. A working demonstration and a usable local inquiry page give either
approach something concrete to point to.

## Quote comparison

Record each complete offer in [the quote worksheet](working-materials.md#quote-worksheet).
Keep these distinctions visible:

| Cost | Record |
|---|---|
| Media | Purchased placement and minimum term |
| One-time work | Design, print, setup, installation and removal |
| Total cash commitment | All charges and applicable taxes, including any minimum renewal |
| Founder time | Creative, preparation, delivery, staffing and lead follow-up |
| What is delivered | Defined inventory, geography, dates/periods and evidence of display or mailing |
| Outcome | Qualified inquiries, actual installations and attributable contribution |

Check the contract's minimum commitment before translating any rate into a pilot
budget. A small initial test should have a clear endpoint and an offer that remains
usable for inquiries received after the media stops.
