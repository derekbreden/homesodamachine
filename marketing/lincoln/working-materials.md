# Lincoln working materials

Drafts and blank records for the [local plan](README.md). The offer, installation
scope, price and budget are unresolved. These are preparation materials; they do not
represent a published offer or requests already sent to vendors.

## Local inquiry page brief

Proposed destination: `homesodamachine.com/lincoln`. This route is **not implemented**.
The current [landing page](../../web/lib/landing.js) and
[subscription handler](../../web/server.js) collect an email for a one-time
availability notification. They do not collect local qualification or source
attribution. A local conversation needs its own explicit invitation and reply path.

The page should contain, in this order:

1. A real pour and a clear description of the machine shown. Identify prototype
   footage as a prototype while the integrated machine is under development.
2. Derek's introduction and the local delivery/installation offer under consideration.
3. The actual product price when selected, installation inclusions, required kitchen
   preparation, supplies and the agreed service area.
4. A concise explanation of flavors, concentrate, CO₂ and owner care.
5. One action: request a local conversation or demonstration. Collect name, reply
   email, ZIP code, preferred flavor and permission to reply about this inquiry.
6. A source question: “How did you first hear about this?” Preserve campaign source
   on submission too; a page visit alone is not an attributable inquiry.

Collect kitchen photographs, exact address and detailed fit information during the
follow-up, when needed. Keep customer contact details and home photographs in a
private customer record; repository reporting can use anonymous household IDs.

Use an explicit early-interest version while price or readiness is unresolved. Do
not treat those registrations as orders. The existing notification signup's stated
purpose remains the guide to contacting those subscribers.

## Pilot record

Use one anonymous household ID through inquiry, installation and support. A simple
private table is sufficient; no customer-management application is needed for the
first batch.

| Stage | Fields to retain |
|---|---|
| Inquiry | Household ID; first source; campaign; later influences; local eligibility; flavor; price shown; price response |
| Fit | Authority to install; cabinet survey; countertop; water; power; CO₂; required preparation; go/no-go reason |
| Offer | Written scope; price; included supplies/support; relationship ring; beta/public offer; acceptance or loss reason |
| Installation | Unit ID; actual receipt; travel time/miles; setup and commissioning hours; trade work; supplies; acceptance |
| Ownership | First follow-up; daily use; refill/cleaning experience; reported issues; owner questions |
| Service | Symptom; cause if known; remote help; travel; work time; parts; resolution; outstanding action |
| Referral | Introducing household; new inquiry ID; optional permission to use a review, photo or demonstration |

At each batch review report counts with their denominators: inquiries, qualified
households, offers, commitments and completed installations. List open inquiries,
cancellations and unresolved service issues so they remain visible. Compare cash
spent and founder hours with the actual contribution and learning from the batch.
