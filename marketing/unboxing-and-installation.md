# Unboxing and the install guide

The **install guide** is a bound 9 × 7 inch landscape booklet with 32 numbered interior pages.
It sits face up on the documentation face of the install kit, above the welcome letter.
Its illustrated kit page shows each supplied item, with the customer-supplied preparation
checklist beside it. Packing placement is in
[`finish-pack-ship.md`](/hardware/assembly/finish-pack-ship.md).

## Installation

The guide follows seven steps:

1. Mount the faucet.
2. Add the cold-water tee.
3. Match the rear connections.
4. Prepare the cylinder.
5. Water, then gas, then power.
6. Fill both flavors.
7. Chill. Choose. Pour.

Both the plastic-tube and braided-hose water connections have illustrated instructions.
The filtered run ships assembled; lay the filter flat and leave the long tubes in easy curves.
The booklet also covers the countertop opening, cabinet space, care and connection checks.
The water-connection supplies include a towel and the applicable wrenches.

The guide is published on [Drawings](https://homesodamachine.com/drawings), with its download at
[install-guide.pdf](https://homesodamachine.com/docs/install-guide/install-guide.pdf).
Its source and print instructions are in
[`hardware/install-guide/`](/hardware/install-guide/README.md).

The [On tap identity](/brand/README.md) supplies cobalt, navy, ice and orange.
Instructional scenes use slate contours, and tube and connector colors match the hardware.
Kit inventory uses consistent vector line drawings. Artwork, fonts and the finished PDF
are committed beside the guide.

The cold kit keeps its own guide in its own bag
([`bom.md`](/hardware/ledger/bom.md) §14). Its tube cutter and insulation are for
optional cold-line work after installation.
