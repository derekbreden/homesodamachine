# Unboxing and quick start

The current public artifact is a development installation-planning reference on `/drawings`. It is
not a customer carton instruction and does not authorize field work.

The single-sided landscape 11 x 17 in sheet identifies the complete factory faucet and umbilical,
shows the current cabinet-space reservations, and uses detached badges to match factory-tail words
with future rear labels. It visibly stops before countertop work, mounting hardware, cutting,
connection, signal termination, utilities, power, or commissioning.

The customer-documentation packet requires a literal accepted installer procedure and a verified
owner sequence. Safety, electrical, pressure-system, and refrigerant notices remain separate
regulatory content. No public first-pour sheet is released while the shipping product lacks a
verified first-pour path.

The five product-evidence gates are the installed mechanical/utility/electrical handoff, cold
carbonated-water substrate, one-bottle reservoir transfer, wet prime, and flow-triggered metering.
Their observable acceptance contract is defined in the
[installation planning evidence gates](/hardware/quickstart/README.md#public-sequence-evidence-gates).
No illustration may substitute a planned interface, serial-only result, or recreated screen for a
state the shipping product literally exposes.

## Print review

Print `quick-start.pdf` landscape, 11 x 17 in, color, actual size for in-house Epson iteration. Do
not place the development planning reference in the customer-documentation packet.

Object identity, space reservations, future label identities, and the no-action boundary must
remain legible in grayscale. Specifications, maintenance, warranty, regulatory copy, and validated
field procedures live outside this reference.
