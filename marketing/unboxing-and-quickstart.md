# Unboxing and quick start

The customer-documentation packet lies face-up at the top of the carton. It contains one
single-sided landscape 11 x 17 in installation reference built in
[`hardware/quickstart/`](/hardware/quickstart/README.md). Safety, electrical, pressure-system, and
refrigerant notices remain in a separate regulatory insert.

The installation reference uses three large visual acts:

1. Lower the complete faucet through the prepared opening, seat it rearward, and retain it from below.
2. Plan the appliance position and connected pull-forward service reach before cutting anything.
3. Fit, mark, square-cut, seat, and tug-check only the blue `SODA` tube and the two black `FLAVOR`
   tubes at the exact rear face.

The sheet stops there. It does not authorize `TAP` water, CO2, faucet-signal termination, AC power,
or commissioning. The documented carton state is dry, atmospheric, and capped, so no public
first-pour sheet accompanies the installation reference.

The remaining customer sequence is released only from observable product behavior. Its five
evidence gates—installed utility/electrical handoff, cold carbonated-water substrate, one-bottle
reservoir transfer, wet prime, and flow-triggered metering—are defined in the
[Quick Start evidence contract](/hardware/quickstart/README.md#next-public-sequence-evidence-gates).
No illustration may substitute a planned interface, serial-only result, or recreated screen for a
state the shipping product actually shows.

## Print and packing

Print `quick-start.pdf` landscape, 11 x 17 in, color, actual size. It is single-sided for fast
in-house Epson iteration. Put the installation reference face-up in the customer-documentation
packet.

The essential sequence and connection identities must remain legible in grayscale. Specifications,
maintenance, warranty, regulatory copy, and validated field procedures live outside this reference.
