# Unboxing and quick start

The public installation quick start on `/drawings` is one single-sided, borderless 19 x 13 inch
sheet for in-house print review. It is not carton-release documentation.

Fourteen wordless scenes run in four rows on a pale blue-grey field. The sequence keeps dry faucet
work first, closes the household cold-water valve immediately before releasing the existing
push-connect tube, adds one tee with only push-fit actions, and finishes at the appliance rear
panel. It follows the modern-home path where a 1/4-inch plastic cold-water line and push fitting
are already accessible under the sink. The new white branch passes through the supplied filter
before reaching `TAP`.

The first row uses four registered faucet models to show lowering, seating, sliding the open
under-counter plate around the attached tubes, and hand-tightening the retained nut. The second row
crops the valve pair to the handle, then shows the existing fitting's release ring pressed and the
original tube withdrawn. The third row adds the tee without drawing the white branch until the
scene where that branch is inserted. The final row preserves the paired rear views before and after
all five tubes and the RJ11 signal lead are connected. Motion arrows are the sheet's only added
marks; part labels that belong to the modeled hardware remain in the scenes.

## Print review

Print `hardware/quickstart/quick-start.pdf` landscape on one sheet of A-SUB satin RC photo paper,
ASIN `B0DSJ9X4CR`: Epson ET-8550 rear feed, **13x19 borderless**, premium semigloss, photo quality,
and 100 %. Check the page at actual size, at quarter scale, and in grayscale. Object identity, valve
state, open mating faces, collar words, both black flavor tubes, and the before/connected
distinction must remain legible without the instructional copy.

Specifications, maintenance, warranty, regulatory copy, powered commissioning, filter flushing,
and first-pour instructions live outside this visual installation guide until their released
procedures are complete.
