# Don't Name Names

Notes for a future video. Captured 2026-05-22 — the initial motivation for the iOS companion app, why I can't ship logos myself, and the wink-wink-nudge-nudge short I'd like to make about it. In my own words. Not a production plan.

## What I needed

The initial motivation for the companion app was entirely for being able to upload custom photos to the external display.

I knew that I wanted real logos in my kitchen when I toggled the button. That was absolutely a must have for the "show off to friends and family" moment. It just doesn't land nearly the same without them. It's not "real diet mtn dew" unless you're looking at the logo when you drink it. And frankly, I think it makes a difference for my own "taste" as well. I'm not sure I have a concrete evidential and well studied basis for my presumption on that, but I'm also not sure such evidence could ever exist, for something that can only ever be self reported (taste).

## What I knew I couldn't do, and what I could

So anyways, I know I needed real logos for many reasons. And I knew that was going to be a key selling point for this device. And I knew I couldn't actually ship the device with the logos. But I knew what I could do, and that is make it possible for users to upload whatever image they want, from their phone to their device with no cloud in between, no one liable but them.

## What the video is

I plan to do at least one short showing uploading an image. I'll upload a generic image sure, but it will very much be a "wink wink nudge nudge" about "any logo you want."

## The bit

> "any logo you want *ahem* *cough* pepsi, just grabbing a sip of my pepsi here, excuse me a moment *cough* so as I was saying, any logo you want — I won't name names because they wouldn't like that, but really, any logo you want"

## Why the comedy works

The fake terror at being noticed by the brand is half the comedy. The other half is that the audience knows the founder knows that — it's two layers of "we're all in on this together."
