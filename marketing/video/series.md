# The Capability Series

The video program has a narrative spine: six capability arcs, released in series, each ending in a photogenic moment that demonstrates one capability the finished appliance must have. This is the serialized-narrative axis; [`concepts.md`](concepts.md) is the menu of individual pieces that fill it.

## The six arcs

1. Carbonated water
2. Cold carbonated water
3. Cold carbonated water with flavor
4. Faucet — soda on tap
5. Hopper — refill
6. Clean cycle — self-maintenance

These track the real build roadmap over the coming months — the order the capabilities come online — minus the open-ended integration tail (every subsystem keeps getting tuned long after its arc closes). Arcs 1–3 prove the *drink*; arcs 4–6 make it a *livable appliance*. The end of arc 3 — cold flavored fizz in a glass — is the natural season break.

## Each arc is a capability, not a component

The unit of the series is a thing a viewer can watch *work* — fizz in a glass, frost-cold fizz, the color swirl of flavor, soda from a handle — not a part on a bench. Each arc is one atomic claim from the product's pitch, proven on camera. By the end of arc 6, every claim the sales page makes has been demonstrated, honestly, in the archive. Each arc can span many short parts; the arc is the unit, the part is the episode.

## The object accretes

The arcs build on one physical core (see the cold-core layers in [`/hardware/future.md`](/hardware/future.md)). The bare welded vessel from the welding videos gains the evaporator coil and foam (arc 2), the flavor reservoirs and injection (arc 3), then the faucet, hopper, and enclosure (arcs 4–6). The bench looks more like an appliance every arc — continuous on-camera transformation, the engine of any serialized build.

## Narrative decouples from roadmap

The roadmap is a tangle: parallel, non-linear, never "done." The narrative is a clean line. An arc closes the moment one honest, demonstrable instance of the capability is captured — not when the subsystem stops changing (it never does). The timeline is what gets edited; the demonstrated result is never staged or faked — the channel's whole credibility is the honest-novice voice.

Each arc closes on the same ritual: a glass, the current capability, a sip, a verdict. Same ritual every time, escalating payoff — plain seltzer at arc 1, cold Diet Mountain Dew from the machine at arc 3.

## Cadence

There is no cadence to defend (see [`principles.md`](principles.md), "Capture habit, not publishing cadence"). At current scale discovery is lumpy — growth comes when one video is picked up by the recommendation feed and viewers binge backward, not from steady weekly drops (see [`distribution.md`](distribution.md)). The series is a library being stocked for that moment; what matters is that the arcs exist and sit in order, not how often they post. The welding videos are already the opening parts of arc 1.
