- The updates feed now scrolls continuously
  - Before today the page rendered every post at once
  - After today, it loads ten at a time as you scroll, and a tap on a notification pages straight to that post
- The wiring plan settled on consistent connectors
  - Keyed JST plugs on the pin headers
  - Screw terminals on the relays
  - A real-time clock chip picked for timekeeping
- The whole machine packed into one enclosure
  - A six-walled printed shell about 32 cm on a side
  - Every subsystem inside it — carbonator, compressor, valves, and pumps
  - Sized to fit the big 3D printer's bed with no parts overlapping
