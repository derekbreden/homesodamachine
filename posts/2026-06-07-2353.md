- The updates feed now scrolls continuously
  - Before today the page rendered every post at once
  - After today, it loads ten at a time as you scroll, and a tap on a notification pages straight to that post
- The wiring plan settled on consistent connectors
  - Keyed JST plugs on the pin headers
  - Screw terminals on the relays and the controller hub
  - A real-time clock chip picked for timekeeping, on a clean inline 4-pin header
- The kitchen machine packed into its own printed enclosure
  - Before today the kitchen edition's subsystems were separate models, while only the Lite edition had been boxed into a shell
  - After today, it has:
    - A six-walled printed shell about 32 cm on a side, the same shell treatment the Lite edition already got
    - Every subsystem inside it — carbonator, compressor, valves, and pumps
    - Sized to fit the big 3D printer's bed with no parts overlapping
