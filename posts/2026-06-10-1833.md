  - Before today the replacement tee that splits the water line was in transit, but the fitting stepping its branch down to the flavor feed was still open
  - After today, that reducing branch adapter is ordered, closing the full path from the water line to the flow regulator
- The faucet's display got its driving firmware
  - Before today the touch screen sat mocked on the dispense head with no firmware driving it, no idle behavior, and nothing holding it
  - After today, it has:
    - Standalone firmware on the screen's own board — it shows the chosen flavor, a tap anywhere switches, and the choice survives a power cycle
    - A soft idle glow — the backlight fades to an ember after a minute, and the next touch wakes it without switching
    - A printed cradle on the dispense tip that captures the screen between two shell pieces as the joint closes, no fasteners
