- The controller board went out for fabrication
  - Before today the four-layer board was routed and named but had never been sent to a board house
  - After today ten copies are in production — a soda-glass brand logo added to the silkscreen and the bulk smoothing capacitor pulled onto the board just before it shipped ($122.17 for the run)
- A machine-assembled successor board started
  - Before today the controller board was a through-hole carrier — its modules and parts hand-soldered in-house after the bare board arrives
  - After today, it has a successor the board house builds entirely by machine:
    - Every plug-in module redrawn as bare surface-mount silicon, each part matched to a real in-stock catalog number
    - The off-board connectors left as through-hole parts the same house solders, so nothing comes back for hand-assembly
    - The factory's files — a bill of materials and a pick-and-place list — written on every build, the conversion proven part by part
