The sixth video is on the channel. Five minutes of laser welding stainless on the Xlaserlab X1 Pro — three clean pull-aways using a technique pulled from a Chinese welding short: keep the trigger held, let the safety clip's conductance break on the lift, and the welder treats it as a normal release with the laser stopping and the wire retracting, no stick. With conductance dropped entirely, the same trigger button feeds the wire — the wire-feed button the X1 Pro doesn't officially have.
