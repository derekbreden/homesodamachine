- The tank welds got a crack-inspection step
  - Before today the weld procedure went straight from welding to the hydro pressure test
  - After today, a dye-penetrant inspection slots in first — a visible dye wiped onto the closure welds that bleeds out of any surface crack before water pressure goes near them, with the three chemicals on order
- The front-face config display got its firmware
  - Before today the front-face touchscreen was picked but had nothing driving it
  - After today, it has:
    - Foundation firmware on the display's own board — the 800×480 panel boots to a centered logo
    - An animated loading logo, the glass-and-bubbles loop, driven tear-free
    - A backlight-off idle after a minute, woken by the first touch
- The controller board got a from-code layout route
  - Before today the board was a design package — a parts-and-connections list and a bill of materials, but no actual board drawn
  - After today, it has:
    - The schematic captured in code, about 40 parts and 94 connections in a script that emits a standard netlist
    - Placement generated with no hand-dragging — all 89 parts dropped onto a 100×100 mm four-layer board straight from that netlist
    - One explored route, not the committed path — the placement is a rough first pass, not ready to manufacture
