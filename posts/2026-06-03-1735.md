- The compressor shroud became a manufacturable part
  - A 5-sided open-bottom sheet-metal box with bend-relief notches at the four corners
  - An earth-bond hole and two enclosure mounting holes
  - A SendCutSend quote of $27.83 per part at qty 10
- The Lite Edition's innards got a shell
  - Before today its bag box and valve trays sat as a bare layout, no enclosure
  - After today, it has:
    - The four valve trays packed around the reservoir on its front face
    - Two flavor pumps and a printed hopper funnel filling the remaining corners
    - A six-walled transparent shell with a square lid hole for the funnel
