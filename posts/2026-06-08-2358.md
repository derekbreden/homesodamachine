- The printed faucet shell grew three mounting feet
  - Before today the shell had no way to bolt down
  - After today, it has three pill-shaped anchors, each with a screw hole and a pocket for a press-in threaded insert
- The 3D drawing viewer got three upgrades
  - An X-ray mode that ghosts surfaces so you can see inside nested parts
  - An edge picker — click any edge to copy its coordinates
  - Part previews now rendered on our server, so pages load faster
- The faucet got a touch screen
  - A 1.47-inch touch LCD on the dispense head, screen facing the user
  - A printed label for each flavor, shown on the screen
  - A touch toggle to switch which flavor pours
