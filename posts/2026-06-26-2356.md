- The funnel mold's spout pin seals the floor
  - Before today the spout pin sat in its floor register with a 0.15 mm slip
  - After today the pin press-fits at zero, the PETG-on-PETG joint sealing the seam so silicone can't bleed under it at the cast's lowest point
- The controller board became a via-free four-layer carrier
  - Before today the board was wired but routed with vias across two copper layers
  - After today, it has:
    - Every net maze-routed to zero vias on a four-layer stack, inner planes added for the 3.3- and 5-volt rails
    - The placement tightened to a uniform 2 mm grid, the outline pulled in to 131 × 102 mm
    - A silkscreen identity — four-character pin labels reading inward and a nameplate stamping the board's name and version
