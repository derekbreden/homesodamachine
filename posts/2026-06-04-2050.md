- 3D drawings now show per-part colors
  - Before today the viewer painted every solid one flat gray
  - After today, it reads the per-part color out of each STEP, and the generator writes a color per solid
- The cold core got a poured-foam plan
  - Before today the foam cap and lid screwed to the shell but were not set up to cast foam in place
  - After today, it has:
    - A mouth-down bottom cap that seats against the shell and clamps the foam pour
    - A doubled pour hole through the lid, Ø10 to Ø20
    - An assembly that verifies all six screws pass clear through the cap and lid
- The CO2 cylinder got a weighing platform
  - A grounded base on three feet
  - A floating well the cylinder drops into
  - A single-point load cell (a scale sensor) bridging the two, so the appliance reads grams remaining
