- The integrated machine's split joint got re-derived from function and made printable
  - Before today the enclosure split into two halves on a slip-fit lip with four corner bosses, but the overlap depth was a copied number and the front half wasn't proven to print
  - After today:
    - The joint is a back-half D-pin registering into a front-half socket pod, coaxial by construction — the overlap depth falls out of the boss-to-wall matings instead of being chosen
    - Each corner cross-pin takes an M3 screw driven from the ±X exterior face, sized down its length: head counterbore, pin body, heat-set, one-wall cap
    - Both halves' print-axis corners are rounded for anti-warp relief and the back corners braced; the front half orients face-down with no overhang past 45°
    - Reduced-size test coupons for both halves are exported — every mating feature at full size — to prove the fit before committing a full print
- The front-face display got let into its facet
  - Before today the 45° corner facet was a flat solid mounting surface with no pocket for the screen
  - After today it's an 18 mm housing — the display's full depth — with the screen let in:
    - A 1 mm counterbore on the user face recesses the bezel (re-measured to 113.5 × 77 mm, the facet re-derived to 119.5 × 83), registering it on the bottom and right edges
    - A 106 × 69 mm hole through the full thickness passes the PCB body, clearing the corner ribs behind it
    - The recessed panel sealed from the cavity at both lateral edges — the left by the exterior wall, the right by a one-wall gusset spanning the housing depth
- The internals repacked around the new corner ribs
  - Before today the contents were laid out before the ribs existed, with the SeaFlo and pump cases sitting where the facet now claims the top-front-left corner
  - After today the refrigeration shroud and condenser are inset off their side walls to clear the ribs, the SeaFlo turned flat and shelved on the compressor top beside the bib-gate valve tray, and the two pump cases shifted right of the display
