- A supplier discontinued our tap-point tee
  - Before today the tee that splits the water line to inject flavor was committed and on order, due to ship
  - After today, the supplier flagged it discontinued before shipping, so that part selection reopened with three in-stock-verified replacements
- The faucet's under-counter mount traced the shell foot
  - Before today the plate was a clean disc, held on by gravity and body friction
  - After today, it has:
    - A plate and rubber gasket re-cut to the shell's exact foot outline, flush underneath
    - Three screw bosses on the plate, set under the shell's three mounting feet
    - A round below-counter disc, re-centered on the shank, spreading the clamp load like a 2-inch washer
