- The faucet's under-counter mounting got worked out
  - A mounting plate and rubber gasket that trace the shell's exact foot outline
  - Three screw bosses, each with a recessed bolt head, tying the plate up into the shell
  - A round below-counter disc that spreads the clamp load like a 2-inch backing washer
