- The redesigned faucet head printed and fit
  - Before today the redesigned head printed once, but everything came out too tight to assemble
  - After today, a print with opened-up clearances came off the bed assembling cleanly — the plate's screw bosses seat, both spout sections slide together, and the screw heads sink clear of their pockets
- The front-face config display got picked
  - Before today the front-face config screen was a small round dial unit on a pull-out cord
  - After today, it's a fixed 4.3-inch (800×480) touchscreen set into the face and angled up at the standing user, powered off the machine's 12-volt bus and linked to the controller over RS485 (a two-wire serial line)
- The reservoir's level float got a stronger magnet
  - Before today the float was salvaged from a cheap switch, weak enough to need the wall
  - After today, it has:
    - A purpose-printed buoyant puck carrying a neodymium ring magnet, about four times the field
    - The magnet sealed inside mid-print — the printer pauses, the ring drops into an open pocket, and a roof bridges over to close it
    - Enough field to read through a gap, so the float rides free instead of dragging the tank wall
