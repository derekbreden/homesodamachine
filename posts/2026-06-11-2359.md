- The faucet display's harness got its solder kit
  - Before today the iron tips and solder on hand were too coarse for 28-gauge wiring
  - After today, it has:
    - Five parts in hand — 28-gauge ribbon cable, a USB-to-serial adapter, low-melt removal alloy, polyimide tape, and precision tweezers
    - A fine 0.8 mm iron tip, thin 0.020-inch solder, and a fine-gauge wire stripper on order
    - Enough to wire the screen through the shell to the under-counter base
- The shared gaskets plate went back on
  - Before today its first run batched thirteen seal copies on one bed
  - After today, a second run trimmed to nine — the wet bulkhead seals dropped as purchased silicone, and the under-counter seal re-cut to the plate's outline
- The plate-to-shell joint got its screw fits
  - Before today the plate carried bosses but no fitted screws or counterbores
  - After today, it has:
    - Counterbores cut to the measured M3×12 cap-screw head, each head sunk clear of the gasket face
    - Boss-to-socket slip clearances and a lead-in chamfer on every boss, so the plate seats into the shell's feet
    - An assembly sheet and bill of materials that now spec the joint — three screws into the shell's heat-set inserts, with the heat-set tooling listed
