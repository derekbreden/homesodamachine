- The level probes got their rod and a saw
  - Before today the float-and-reed level sensors had drilled rod seats but no rod stock on order
  - After today, 1/8-inch 316 stainless rod is ordered for the carbonator and reservoir probes, with a metal-cutting band saw and blade to cut each one square
- The front-face config display got its mounting facet
  - Before today the 4.3-inch touchscreen was picked and running its own firmware, but had no physical home in the machine
  - After today, its real envelope is measured off the panel with calipers and a 45° facet is cut into the enclosure's front corner, angled up toward a standing user
- The integrated machine's enclosure became printable
  - Before today the all-in-one kitchen machine sat in one printed shell, bigger than any print bed
  - After today, it splits into two halves that each fit the bed:
    - A front and back that telescope together on a slip-fit lip
    - Four corner bosses screwing the halves shut from the outside faces
    - The contents re-packed around the seam, the cold core seated behind the joint
