The seventh video is on the channel. Just under two minutes of picking up a vacuum desoldering gun for the first time — touch the tip to a through-hole joint and the solder gets sucked right out. Clearing a row of header joints off a Waveshare MCP23017 I/O-expander module (a part for the soda machine), working the freed pin header loose, missing two, then cleaning those up. Absolutely zero skill level, and way easier than solder wick.
