# Glossary

Derek's approved meanings from **Glossary** (Codex), with supporting passages
and examples from his conversations with agents.

## Obvious / reasonable, used correctively

Take the particular thing I’ve emphasized into account alongside everything
else you understand. Continue exercising ordinary practical judgment about
the whole situation, and proceed independently when that understanding
supplies a clear answer.

Derek, **Glossary** (Codex), on the corrective use of these words:

> It is only after I've said other things I'm afraid will overly narrow your
> focus that I'm using these "corrective" phrases to simply try to reset you
> to that natural behavior, a correction to prevent overcorrection.

## Simple, geometrically

A shape that provides its function with fewer edges and surface breaks,
broad continuous surfaces, and substantial connected material.
Simplification can mean adding material, filling a void, or moving one
larger boundary so several local steps, notches, or projections disappear.
A detailed fitting cavity can sit inside a simple exterior.

Derek, **Visualize pump cartridge redesign** (Codex), in the
[recorded instruction](https://github.com/derekbreden/homesodamachine/commit/7c311ff2203af2c17996603e441460c0e2e0b0a4):

> Please consider our preference for fewer edges and more material.

The example is a pump holder: its fitting cavity follows the pump; its
outside can remain a simpler solid shape.

Derek, **Fit** (Codex):

> If you're going to do a "stop" like that, then please make a large solid
> piece with fewer edges. Can you see how putting a thin block in spot does
> not achieve our goal of fewest edges?

## Replica detail

The [Kamoer adapter](../hardware/reference/kamoer-kphm400/scan-review.md#replica-detail-example)
is a canonical example. Its paired columns, recessed straight walls and layer
thicknesses preserve the exterior a mating printed part follows. The black plate
and white body are solid extrusions of the same outline. The deeper cylindrical
recess is filled. Photographs identify the manufactured features; the scan measures
their positions, planes and radii. The authored surfaces remain clean planes and
cylinders.

Derek, **Simplify scanned model surfaces** (Codex):

> We're not including that deeper cylindrical recess at all. You're going to make
> both layers the same block of material, the same shape.

## Simple, in part selection and arrangement

Parts chosen and placed so their connections, mounting, and assembly work
together straightforwardly. Their shapes, orientations, and relative
positions reduce the need for intermediate pieces, detours, special
retention features, and separate assembly operations. A good choice or
rearrangement can make other components or mechanisms unnecessary.
Simplicity belongs to the complete arrangement, including how it is put
together.

The [check-valve example](https://github.com/derekbreden/homesodamachine/commit/66afca468f088b1b049a6c00557e99e611ccbb09)
puts the required tube connections on the valve itself.

Derek, **Above cold core** (Codex), on regulator placement:

> I appreciate the simple place you found. It does seem awkward to route to
> the tee fore and then u turn the tube back to the regulator aft, and then
> u turn the tube again to go fore and into the valve.
>
> Also, it seems to be positioned at an awkward angle.
>
> Do you see any clean possibilities for getting the regulator more inline
> with where we want to go? Anything less awkward?

Derek, **Scan results** (Codex), on a separate keeper for a snap fit:

> I would add that half the value to me personally of moving to snap fit, is
> if it simplifies assembly, which a separate "keeper" sounds like it does
> not do.
