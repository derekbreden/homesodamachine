    About this experiment

    This page runs SolidCraft’s native engine and renderer. The feature history, sketches, dimensions and appearances come from the valve’s source design.

    Update times include the command and the following browser frames. They are observations on your device, separate from print readiness.

    The engine loads only on this page. Backgrounding or pausing the page saves the current design locally and releases the embedded runtime after pending work finishes.
