      What this evaluates

      The parent opens one saved exterior of the instrumented carbonator. Open it to see its direct components, then the fitted vessel, weldment, cap groups and float. Each level keeps the child histories closed.

      The weldment reference retains the tube rim, cap recesses, clearance and ports. Its captive float and guides load when you open the weldment. The instrumented exterior also retains the copper coil, fittings, reed bridge and probe envelopes.

      The coil lifts over the reed bridge. The controlled guide rod sits in conical blind registers, and the float has a limited native slider joint. Rigid joints retain the assembled positions of fixed components.

      Printed shapes follow their source interfaces. Bought parts retain their fit envelopes; thread helices, weld beads and electronic internals are omitted. The earth lead shows its attached terminal and conductor exit. Free conductor dressing and the insulation casing belong outside this assembly.

      The coil retains its specified tube wall. Open native leaf designs for authoring. These saved references supply display and layout geometry; manufacturing and aggregate mass use the owning detailed designs.
