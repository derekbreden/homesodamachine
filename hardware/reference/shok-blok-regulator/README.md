# Hydronix Shok Blok SB-FPV-40 water-pressure regulator

The Hydronix Shok Blok SB-FPV-40 is an inline water-pressure regulator with
1/4-inch quick-connect ports. It is not part of the appliance, and its
acquisition is not recorded. V-A's clean-water feed uses the
[YZMSZ fixed 0.3 MPa regulator](../yzmsz-water-regulator/README.md) on back-top.

The manufacturer's [data sheet](https://hydronixwater.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/10/SB_DS.pdf)
specifies nominal 40 psi output. Its table reports 40 psi at 100 psi inlet,
44 psi at 150 psi inlet and 50 psi at 200 psi inlet. Maximum temperature is
40 °C. NSF/ANSI 61 certification covers material requirements.

The [Prime listing](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B01CFOGH6O) read $16.05, shipped
and sold by Amazon, on 2026-10-21. The envelope uses the listing's 101.6 mm
length and photograph-scaled sections, including a 34.3 mm body ring. Those
sections, the assigned inlet/outlet ends and the 10 mm tube insertion are
estimates. Internal working parts are omitted.

[`shok_blok_regulator.py`](shok_blok_regulator.py) authors the reference with
flow along +X and the inlet face at the origin.
