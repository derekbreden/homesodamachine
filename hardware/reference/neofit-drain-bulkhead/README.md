# neoFit 4 mm DRAIN bulkhead

Black acetal **ABU44M-E** from [Fresh Water Systems](https://www.freshwatersystems.com/products/neofit-acetal-black-bulkhead-connector-4mm-5-32-tube). Both sockets take 4 mm OD tubing. The separate DRAIN tube is white, with its own white rectangular chip and black word.

The [manufacturer drawing](https://assets.freshwatersystems.com/image/upload/qnbgcy8djx3nj2weqz9v.pdf) supplies the M15 × 1.5 barrel, 32.4 mm overall length, 8.1 mm bare panel section, 6.5 mm nut length and 14.7 mm socket ends. The flange outline is not dimensioned; the CAD uses a conservative 22 mm envelope. Confirm the received fitting's flange and insertion marks before accepting printed mounting fit.

The rear mounting stack is 6 mm: chip, wall and retained pocket backing. The nut's maximum torque is 1.1 ft lb. The internal return, vent adapters and umbilical connection are described in [drain assembly](/hardware/assembly/asse-drain.md).
