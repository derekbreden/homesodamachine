# neoFit flavor stem reducer

[ARD4M4-E](https://www.freshwatersystems.com/products/neofit-acetal-black-stem-reducer-4mm-5-32-tube-x-1-4-stem) connects each dispense valve's 1/4-inch push-fit outlet to black 4 mm flavor tubing. Two are allocated per appliance from the acquired bag of ten.

The [supplier drawing](https://assets.freshwatersystems.com/image/upload/s--kDMqgZEg--/ogt8i8sqv3vd0r25jd0r.pdf), ARD4M4 row, supplies the reference dimensions:

| Dimension | mm |
|---|---:|
| Stem OD | 6.35 |
| Tube OD | 4.00 |
| Maximum body OD | 13.208 |
| Overall length | 36.703 |
| Stem length to shoulder | 21.006 |
| Shoulder to outlet face | 15.697 |

The analytic model runs along local +Y, with its shoulder at the origin. Its cylindrical body retains the drawing's maximum envelope; its through bore identifies the connection, without modeling the grab ring or seal. Valve insertion depth and the received part's installed shoulder gap are unmeasured. The assembly uses the nominal shoulder datum; cut internal tube blanks with the fully seated physical reducer and actual socket engagements.

The valve reference retains an occupied socket envelope. The inserted stems therefore appear as connected overlaps in the assembly’s general clash report; the nominal shoulder datum does not qualify physical insertion depth.

The complete path and qualification scope are in [flavor dispense lines](../../printed-parts/faucet/flavor-lines/README.md).
