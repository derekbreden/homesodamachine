# Pump exterior replica detail

`replica.rs` separates the head cover's flat top land from its round and
capsule-shaped crown features. The observed centers and feature identities come from
[reference-parameters.json](reference-parameters.json), with shape interpretation
supported by the received-part photographs in
[under-head-tube](under-head-tube/physical-observations.json).

The flat perimeter lands of the archived feet-down scan, outside the crown
features, establish the head's top plane. The plane and disjoint fit
observations are held in
[replica-shell-parameters.json](replica-shell-parameters.json). The casing and
head shell stop at that plane. The deeper depressed regions remain filled;
their complete pocket walls are outside this exterior reference's scope.

The crown profiles enclose their source vertices with straight cylindrical
walls and a broad tapered crown, continuing into the measured cover land.
Their fitted outer radii are not nominal fastener specifications. The exported
fit record separates this construction from a received-part fit.

The motor, measured barbs, rubber feet and their independent poses are imported
unchanged. The fitted motor cylinder supplies the barrel exterior. The generic
casing loft stops at the documented lower-cradle boundary over that cylinder's
axial span, retaining the measured lower rails, finite wire cover and rear cap.
The source's metadata-only lower convex hull is excluded from visible anatomy.
The head casing stops at its observed top land. The source
[integration model](integration-envelope/README.md)
remains available to conservative clearance callers: the replica intentionally
removes its convex bridges between distinct crown features. The switch shell,
side lugs and motor surface reliefs retain their exterior approximations and
have no new physical interface qualification.
