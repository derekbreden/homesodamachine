# Water-tightness test cup

A 4 fl oz (118.3 mL) open-top cup with 3 mm walls and a 3 mm floor — a
water-tightness coupon for the reservoir's 3 mm PETG wall spec. Print it in
PETG at the reservoir's wall settings, fill it with water, and watch the
walls and floor for weeping or seepage over time.

- Interior: ⌀50 × 60.25 mm (4 fl oz)
- Outer: ⌀56 × 63.25 mm
- Walls + floor: 3 mm
- Open top (no cap)

Generated by `water_test_cup.py` → `water-test-cup.step`.

This coupon is where the reservoir's watertight recipe comes from.
[`print-log.md`](print-log.md) records the print that held water; the
reservoir's own [`print-log.md`](/hardware/printed-parts/cold-core/reservoir/print-log.md)
carries that recipe onto the reservoir body, and the standing guidance drawn
from it is [`watertight-petg.md`](/hardware/printed-parts/cold-core/reservoir/watertight-petg.md).
