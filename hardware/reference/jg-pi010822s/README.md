# John Guest PI010822S

This reference shows the gray acetal male connector that joins the red tether to the
[Taprite regulator](../taprite-3741-regulator/README.md). The push-fit mouth is at the origin and the
thread runs along +X.

The body and thread lengths, wrench flats, maximum body envelope, tube insertion depth and
through-bore come from the PI010822S row of the [John Guest dimensional data sheet](https://www.johnguest.com/sites/jg/files/2023-06/JG%20Drinks%20Grey%20Acetal%20Straight%20Adaptor%20%28NPTF%20Thread%29%20Data%20Sheet.pdf).
The molded steps and release-ring profile are estimated from its elevation and the
[John Guest product photo](https://www.johnguest.com/us/en/od-tube-fittings/acetal-gray/threaded-connectors/male-connector-nptf-thread).
The thread has the pitch and taper of the [Dryseal thread standard](https://www.asme.org/codes-standards/find-codes-standards/b1-20-3-dryseal-pipe-threads-inch),
with an approximate groove for display. The collet internals are represented by a sleeve.

`jg_pi010822s.rs` exports the standalone model through the `jg-pi010822s` example in `hsm-sandbox`.
