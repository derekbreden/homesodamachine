# Soda-riser insulation sleeve

This reference is a 15 mm length of CARGEN closed-cell nitrile pipe foam,
nominal 6.35 mm inside diameter and 25.4 mm outside diameter, around a straight
soda tube at X50/Y354.3696 from Z264.5 to Z279.5, in the enclosure-world frame
with an identity placement. It is not part of the appliance. The continuous
soda inlet carries one approximately 63 mm square-cut CARGEN piece over the
tube and its R14 bend holder
([internal plumbing](../../assembly/internal-plumbing.md) §4;
[production soda inlet](../../manifold-layout/soda_inlet.py)).

[soda_rise_insulation.py](soda_rise_insulation.py) defines the nominal
uncompressed annulus, matching the
[study sleeve](../../manifold-layout/studies/funnel-gate/parts/soda-rise-insulation.json).

Stock: [purchases §9](../../ledger/purchases.md), CARGEN B0D2XFK337.
