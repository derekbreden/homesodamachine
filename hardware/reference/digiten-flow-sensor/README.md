# DIGITEN G3/8" Hall-effect flow sensor — reference solid

A keep-out envelope for the DIGITEN G3/8" Hall-effect water-flow sensor
(model FL-308 / FL-308ZJ, Amazon B07QQW4C7R) — the inline turbine flow meter
between the rear-panel pass-through ports in the lite edition. Carbonated
water spins the rotor; the Hall pulse train (≈450 pulses/L, F = 21·Q) triggers
the flavor pumps. A purchased part, not a printed one — the model is for
placement and tube routing, not a manufacturing drawing.

Three pieces sharing one center, reduced to coaxial/cylindrical keep-out:

| Piece | Size | Part |
|---|---|---|
| Disk | Ø[36.42](BODY_DIA) × [28.35](BODY_THICK) thick | body — round turbine housing carrying the rotor and Hall sensor |
| 2× barrel | Ø[21.95](PORT_DIA) collet, out to [60](OVERALL_LEN) tip-to-tip | quick-connect ports, coaxial along the flow axis |
| Stub | Ø[8](WIRE_BOSS_DIA) × [3](WIRE_BOSS_LEN) proud | wire-exit boss where the 3-wire pigtail leaves the rim |

The ports are 3/8" push-to-connect as sold — a 3/8" OD tube pushes straight in
behind the blue collet ring; there are no exposed threads. The pigtail carries
red VCC (DC 5–18 V), black GND, and yellow signal (square-wave, open-collector).

## Coordinate convention (repo world frame)

- **Y** = flow axis — the through-flow runs along Y; the two ports open at ±Y,
  their outer collet faces at Y = ±[30](PORT_FACE_Y)
- **Z** = rotor axis — normal to the flow plane, through the disk thickness
- **X** = lateral — completes the right-handed frame, in the flow plane

Origin is the body center: the disk is centered on it, the two ports are mirror
images along Y, and the wire-exit boss leaves toward −X.

Overall envelope: [60](OVERALL_LEN) (Y) × [36.42](BODY_DIA) (X) × [28.35](BODY_THICK) (Z) mm,
plus the wire-exit stub off −X.

## Measured vs estimated

- **Measured** (manufacturer dimension drawing, Amazon listing image 4):
  overall length 60 mm, disk diameter 36.42 mm, disk thickness 28.35 mm,
  quick-connect collet OD 21.95 mm.
- **Photo-estimated** (read off product photos, no drawing): the wire-exit
  boss diameter, stand-off, and height, and its −X exit direction. The flexible
  15 cm pigtail itself is not enveloped.
