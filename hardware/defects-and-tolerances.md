## Cold-core shell — small upper-wall ripples

**Accepted for shipping by Derek, November 1, 2026.**

The internal cold-core foam shell may have the very small, localized upper-wall
ripples shown in photos 1–3 of the
[October 29 Mark2 print result](printed-parts/cold-core/foam-shell/prints/2026-10-29-mark2-seam-channel/README.md#physical-result--accepted-for-shipping-2026-11-01).
The defect is near the upper rim and lines up with a hole in the shell. It is
invisible in some lighting and quite visible in others.

The accepted extent and severity are the photographed condition:
[close view](printed-parts/cold-core/foam-shell/prints/2026-10-29-mark2-seam-channel/physical-result/photos/02-upper-wall-ripples.jpg)
and [wider view](printed-parts/cold-core/foam-shell/prints/2026-10-29-mark2-seam-channel/physical-result/photos/03-wall-overview.jpg).
No numerical surface tolerance is specified.

Derek: "But this is a defect that we are okay shipping with."

The [physical-result record](printed-parts/cold-core/foam-shell/prints/2026-10-29-mark2-seam-channel/physical-result/physical-result.json)
preserves his full report and all six original photographs, bound to Mark2 task
**1386110458**. His suspected connection to high layers and the other geometry on
those layers is recorded with the print result.
