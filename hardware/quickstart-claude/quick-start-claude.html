Home Soda Machine

Quick Start

Six pushes and one nut to install it, then four things before the first glass. Nothing is cut, and nothing is threaded on the path drawn here. Prop this sheet against the cabinet door; the bound guide in the kit carries everything around it.

Before you start
The hole is cut. One 1-3/8 in hole through the countertop, the standard single-hole faucet opening. Cutting it is not part of today — guide, page 6.
Your kitchen has a 1/4-in line. A soft plastic cold-water tube on a push fitting under the sink, the kind a fridge runs on. A braided hose on a shut-off instead? Use the white tee and the guide's pages 10–11 in place of 3, 4 and 5.
The cylinder is here. A 5 lb CO2 cylinder, filled, with the two-gauge regulator from the kit fitted on its valve. The shop that fills it will fit the regulator if you have not.
1

Lower the faucet

Feed the three tubes and the cable down through the hole and lower the faucet after them. Then push it back, away from you, until it stops. That is the seat; nothing is measured.

The tubes are long on purpose. Leave them long.

2

Plate, then nut

Under the counter, hold the steel plate flat above the washer and nut on the shank. Slide it in sideways, wide slot to the shank and narrow slot to the two black tubes, until it stops. Run the nut up by hand.

Hand-tight is tight. No wrench.

3

Close the cold water

Follow the 1/4-in line back to its shut-off and turn the handle a quarter turn. Run the kitchen's cold tap: if it goes dry, you closed the right one. Leave the tap open a moment to let the pressure off.

4

Release the line

Press the fitting's collar in with the collet press from the kit, or a thumbnail, and pull the tube out. Keep a cup under it. A closed valve is not an empty line.

5

Tee in

Push the tee's short stub into the fitting you just emptied, then the line you pulled out into the tee's open end. All the way home each time: a little over half an inch, further than it feels like it should go.

The long white run, filter and all, is already in the tee's third port.

6

Six pushes at the back

Pull the two shipping caps out first. Each collar carries the word of its port; push every tube home, and the plug in until it clicks.

Tapthe white run, from the filter
CO2the red tether, from the cylinder
Sodathe blue tube, from the faucet
Flavorboth black tubes, either port
Plugthe faucet's cable, into the jack
Then, the first glass — water, then gas, then power, then flavor; about an hour
7

Connect the cylinder

Stand the cylinder upright beside the appliance, valve closed. Pull the red tether's brass nut down onto the regulator's outlet: metal cone on metal cone, hand-tight, no wrench.

8

Water, gas, power

Open the shut-off from 3 slowly and watch every joint for a minute. Open the cylinder valve and turn the big knob until the needle sits in the green. Plug the cord into the socket at the left of the top row. It chimes.

9

Fill each flavor

On the display choose FILL, pick a flavor, press Start. Upend a bottle of concentrate in the funnel and let it all go in; it drains itself and the appliance stops on its own. Then the other flavor.

10

Chill, then pour

Give it about an hour to get cold. Then pick a flavor on the faucet's screen, put a glass under the spout, and press the lever.

Every glass after the first is quick.
