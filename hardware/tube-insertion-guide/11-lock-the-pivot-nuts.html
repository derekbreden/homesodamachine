Build · operation

Retain a freely moving pivot

A large washer spreads the pivot load. Two square nuts hold the setting without clamping the printed lever tight.

  1. Place one Ø25 mm washer over the pivot screw and onto the lever.
  2. Thread on one M5 square nut. Set it so the lever is retained with slight endplay and swings freely.
  3. Thread on the second square nut. Hold the first nut at that setting while jamming the second nut against it with the two small wrenches or pliers.
  4. Swing the lever through the stroke by hand. The nose should drive the square ram without binding or lifting it.
Finished when

The two nuts remain jammed together, the lever stays retained and it swings without pinching at the pivot.
