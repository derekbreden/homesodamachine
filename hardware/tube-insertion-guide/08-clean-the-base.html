Build · operation

Clean the working surfaces

Base A carries the donor nest, thrust wall, ram guides, stop and pivot pedestal as one print.

  1. Remove the print remnants and any supports generated by the prepared project. Clear the underside pivot-head seat completely.
  2. Remove strings and raised edges from the nest, rail faces, stop face, ram feet and nut/washer bearing faces.
  3. Keep the thrust wall's annular metal-contact face and both rail faces flat. Preserve their printed bearing surfaces.
Finished when

The ram path is clear, the donor nest is clean and the pivot screw can enter its underside seat.
