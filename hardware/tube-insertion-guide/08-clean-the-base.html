Build · operation

Clean and mark base A

Base A combines the donor nest, reaction block, carrier guides and travel stops. Mark the two fixed-pad centres on the block's rear face.

  1. Remove print remnants and any supports generated by the prepared project. Clear the donor notch, guides and stop faces.
  2. Remove strings and raised edges from the two fixed-pad regions and the donor annulus contact face. Preserve their flat bearing surfaces.
  3. Clear the carrier path and guide faces. Keep the attached faucet lever and valve plunger clear of the fixture.
  4. Use fine ink to mark two dots on the block's rear face, both 30 mm above the base underside: the near dot is 36.75 mm from the front (-Y) edge; the far dot is 19.25 mm from the back (+Y) edge of the 120 mm-wide block.
Finished when

The bearing faces and carrier path are clean. Both fixed-pad centres are marked on the flat rear face.
