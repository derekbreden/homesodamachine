Prepare for pressing · operation

Seat both pressure points

The common carrier guides the grip. Both fixed pads and both screw pads must seat full-face before either screw advances under load.

  1. Set the sleeved cap at the port and align the tube with its bore. Keep B attached to D; lift the assembly clear of the guides for any initial hand advance.
  2. For D > 20 mm, press both T wings evenly to start the sleeve D - 20 mm into the port. Stop when B can enter the fully retracted guide position.
  3. For D ≤ 20 mm, keep the cap at the mouth and place B between its guides 20 - D mm ahead of the rear start. The loaded travel is D.
  4. Confirm the B-to-D joint remains seated. Fit C1/C2 at the marked pad regions, with their frames open outward in the same plane.
  5. Bring both screws into light contact. Check all four pad faces, grip position, donor seating and free faucet-lever clearance.
Finished when

All four factory pads seat fully, both screws contact the common grip, and the tube stays straight with the grip front at its mark.
