Insert · operation

Move the press lever slowly

The short nose moves the guided ram; the long handle provides approximately 4:1 leverage. The faucet lever remains unpressed.

  1. Hold the fixed palm area with the opposite hand. Keep the donor's annular base face against the thrust wall.
  2. Move the long press lever slowly through the stroke. Watch the thimble enter straight and the grip remain at its mark.
  3. Stop when the port-lip witness reaches the mouth and the ram meets its travel stop. Do not force the lever beyond the stop.
  4. If the mark and stop disagree, or the tube slips or the sleeve folds, release the load and check the depth, marks, grip position and alignment.
A stop limits travel

The stop is a geometric overtravel limit. It does not prove full seating or load capacity. If the witness and stop disagree, recheck the setup instead of increasing force.
