Insert · operation

Advance in small steps

Use both hands on C1/C2's compact wooden handles. Advance the common grip in small steps and watch that it stays square.

  1. Bring both screws into light contact with their rear grip faces. Confirm the fixed pads remain seated and the donor annulus stays against the donor reaction block.
  2. Advance both handles together when convenient. Otherwise make one very small advance, then immediately take up the opposite pad's gap before another advance. Watch that the grip stays square and at its tube mark.
  3. Stop at the first port-lip witness or travel-stop contact. If they disagree, unload both screws and recheck the depth, marks, sleeve, grip and alignment.
  4. Unload and inspect if a gap or skew grows, a pad tilts, a part flexes, the tube bows or slips, the sleeve folds, or an advance produces no tube movement.
Release when motion changes

Keep the clamps in one plane. Do not rock the tube or continue increasing effort against binding or a stop. The stop limits travel; it does not prove full seating or load capacity.
