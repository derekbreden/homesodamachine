After insertion · operation

Open the grip and lift away

The tool comes off without pulling the sleeved tube back out of the donor.

  1. Move the press lever back to release the ram load. Keep the donor and tube supported.
  2. Loosen the two jaw screws and open the upper jaw. Lift both tool halves off the tube without sliding a closed grip along it.
  3. Lift the donor and tube together out of the nest. Leave the faucet lever attached at unpressed rest.
  4. Check the seating witness and the released gripping area. The tube must remain round, without a persistent flattened section, gouge or folded sleeve.
Finished when

The tooling is removed, the tube retains its seating witness and the released gripping area remains round.
