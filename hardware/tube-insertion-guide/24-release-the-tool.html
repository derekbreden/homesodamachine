After insertion · operation

Unload both clamps first

Release the two screw loads before opening the grip. Keep the tube and donor supported together.

  1. Back off each screw a little in turn until both moving pads are unloaded and clear of the rear grip faces.
  2. Support the grip while taking the two metal clamps off their pad regions. Keep the tube and donor together in the nest.
  3. Loosen the two M5 × 50 grip screws and remove E. Leave the bolted B/D assembly in the base with its lower channel open.
  4. Lift the donor and seated tube together out of the nest and lower liner. Check the seating witness and released grip area: the tube must remain round, without a persistent flat, gouge or folded sleeve.
Finished when

Both metal clamps and the grip are removed, the tube retains its witness and the faucet lever remains attached down at unpressed rest.
