Build the common grip · operation

Bolt B to lower jaw D

Two countersunk screws join the carrier to the lower jaw. Side-loaded square nuts secure the joint before the carrier enters its guides.

  1. Load one M5 square nut into each lower-jaw side pocket, with its threaded hole aligned to the carrier screw axis.
  2. Align D's underside pockets with the two raised locating keys on B. Keep D seated fully on the carrier support face.
  3. Pass the two M5 × 20 countersunk screws upward through B and thread them into the square nuts.
  4. Close the joint evenly. Confirm both countersunk heads seat at or below B's underside and the jaw does not move sideways on the carrier.
Finished when

B and D form one detachable assembly. Both locating features are seated, both nuts are retained and the carrier underside is clear to slide.
