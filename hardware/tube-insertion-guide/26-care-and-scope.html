Care · operation

Keep the gripping surfaces clean

The tool is an unprinted prototype. The assembly order comes from the observed faucet build; the fixture's grip and insertion performance remain unmeasured.

  1. Remove strings or debris from the liners, rail faces, stop and donor nest before use. Keep the cap-support puck's hole clear.
  2. Replace a torn, rolled or permanently distorted liner from the same prepared liner project. Do not add teeth or extra jaw squeeze to cure slipping.
  3. Inspect the rigid jaws, ram, thrust wall and pivot for a crack or permanent distortion. Reprint a damaged part before using the press.
  4. For storage, remove the grip from the tube and leave the liners unloaded. The ram lifts out once the lever is moved clear.

Geometry and native slicing establish shape and print preparation. They do not establish insertion force, tube retention, complete seating, fixture strength or endurance. The port-depth setup is performed on the actual donor during ordinary assembly.
