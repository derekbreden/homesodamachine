Care · operation

Keep the contacts clean

The printed fixture is an unprinted prototype. The observed faucet sequence is established; grip, insertion force and fixture endurance remain unmeasured.

  1. Remove strings and debris from liners, guide faces, pad regions, stops and the donor nest. Keep the cap-support puck hole clear.
  2. Replace a torn, rolled or permanently distorted liner. Inspect the printed jaw contact faces, guides and donor reaction block for cracking or permanent deformation.
  3. Keep both metal clamp pads clean and able to seat flat. Inspect the bars, sliding jaws, screws and swivel pads before use.
  4. Store the grip and liners unloaded, with the metal clamp screws backed off.

CAD and native slicing establish nominal shape and print preparation. They do not establish grip retention, full seating, equal load sharing, fixture strength or lifetime. The supplier's clamp specifications describe the clamp, not a rating for this assembly.
