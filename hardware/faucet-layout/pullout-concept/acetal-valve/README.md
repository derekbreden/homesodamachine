# Machined acetal head valve

This study packages a garage-machined wet cartridge and a purchased dry pull solenoid in the Sculpted pull-out grip. The seat closes at the dispense face. The flavor lines and drain retain their separate outlets.

## What the shop makes

The acetal pressure body has an offset tube socket and a plugged cross-drilling. The soda tube passes beside the solenoid and feeds that socket. Water flows through windows in the removable acetal cage into the chamber behind the poppet. The cage clamps the rim of a punched, cured EPDM washer against the body's floor. The poppet's stainless lip seals against its exposed face.

The cage also carries the balance bore. A drilling in the stationary body connects the outlet collector to a slot behind the balance piston. Supply pressure acts on opposed areas of the poppet and piston. A seal in the rear acetal gland keeps the spring, link and actuator dry. Turning the captive seat, piston and rod as one solid poppet lets a turning shop supply the precision metal surfaces from one axis. The threaded dry eye and cross pin attach after the gland is in place.

[Closed seat](https://homesodamachine.com/parts#step:faucet-layout%2Fpullout-concept%2Facetal-valve%2Fseat-closed.step) · [Open seat](https://homesodamachine.com/parts#step:faucet-layout%2Fpullout-concept%2Facetal-valve%2Fseat-open.step)

The main body, cage, outlet collector, rod gland and actuator carrier are candidates for food-contact acetal copolymer stock. A lathe makes the bores, shoulders and glands. Indexed milling or drilling opens the side feed, cage windows, reference passage, nut pockets and mounting features. The inlet tube is formed from stock stainless tubing. The actuator, seals, spring and fasteners are purchased components. The stock declarations and actuator drawing are available from [Ensinger](https://www.ensingerplastics.com/en-us/shapes/acetal-tecaform-ah-natural) and [Ledex](https://www.digikey.com/en/products/detail/ledex-dormeyer-saia-a-division-of-johnson-electric/195200-234/9676183).

## What the views establish

The grip, display, flex, hose, crimp, flavor lines and drain come from the docked faucet's CAD. The grip has an internal counterbore for the outlet flange. The solenoid model represents the can, mounting bush and clevis. The hose transition and thread envelopes locate interfaces. Holding the cartridge against the tip shoulder, retaining the tube at the crimp and terminating the solenoid leads need physical development.

A bench cartridge can establish breakaway force cold, pull and holding current after warming, shutoff leakage, drip mass and the shape of the carbonated stream. A sustained pressure and sanitizer trial can establish whether the acetal gland and pressure body retain their dimensions and sealing force.

The sandbox example `faucet-acetal-valve` generates the models in this directory from the docked export and the balanced seat profile.
