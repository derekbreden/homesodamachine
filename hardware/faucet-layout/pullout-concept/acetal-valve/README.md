# Machined acetal head valve

This cartridge shuts off chilled carbonated water at the face of the Sculpted pull-out faucet. Its design life is a decade of household dispensing, including the time it stands closed under pressure. The flavor lines and fault drain have their own outlets. The cartridge is removable for repair. Its service-life target assumes the original poppet, seat and dynamic seals.

## Acetal parts

Use unfilled natural acetal copolymer rod and plate with a supplier food-contact declaration covering the stock and conditions of use. The shutoff bead, stem, balance piston and dry pull eye share one turning. The rounded bead bears on a stationary EPDM seat washer. The balance piston and sealing face share a lathe setup. The stem has radiused shoulders and an uninterrupted cylindrical surface through the rod seal. A mill cuts the dry flats and drills the eye. The washer and rings are mechanically retained. [Ensinger stock shapes](https://www.ensingerplastics.com/en-us/shapes/acetal-tecaform-ah-natural) · [John Guest beverage regulator material specification](https://www.johnguest.com/sites/jg/files/2025-11/JG%20Drinks%20Acetal%20Mini%20Plastic%20PRV%20Installation%20Guide.pdf)

The garage also machines the pressure body with its integral outlet, the seat cage with its balance bore, the rear rod gland and the hose barb with its angled tube socket. The body loads from the rear. The cage clamps the seat washer's rim against the body and admits supply water through its side windows. The body can be drilled from the inlet, outlet and rear face. A small passage connects the outlet to the back of the balance piston. Supply pressure acts on nearly equal opposing areas. The metal tie screws close against steel sleeves. The gland and cage shoulders set the compression of the static seals.

A collet lathe makes the sealing diameters and glands. An indexed fixture on a benchtop mill makes the windows, flats and intersecting drillings. The piston groove and running bore need polished cutting edges, clean lead-ins and finish inspection. Matched gauge pins, an optical check of the sealing bead and air or bore gauging on the finished lands distinguish cutting error from seal friction. [Parker O-Ring Handbook, dynamic sealing](https://www.parker.com/content/dam/Parker-com/Literature/O-Ring-Division-Literature/ORD-5700.pdf)

## Spring and actuator

When power is off, a stainless compression spring pushes a slotted thrust washer against the shoulders where the dry stem becomes flat. The seat can follow the rubber as it settles. The pin has axial clearance in the acetal eye. When powered, the solenoid takes up that clearance and pulls through both sides of its steel fork. The bushing threads and jam nut set the magnetic end position in the dry aluminum carrier. The magnetic circuit, return spring and retained pin are metal. The acetal eye sees the opening load during a pour.

The actuator uses a current-regulated pull pulse followed by reduced holding current. Select the hold setting and thermal limit for the assembled grip. The manufacturer's free-air heat sink differs from the carrier inside this grip. The valve closes on loss of power. Nominal seated spring force, installed length and rate are reported by the geometry producer. [Ledex actuator drawing and force table](https://peerlesselectronics.com/media/erp_files/files/195200-237.pdf)

## Pressure and service interfaces

The stainless bypass tube passes beside the actuator. Its ends carry beads. The aluminum carrier catches the valve-end bead and a swaged stainless sleeve traps the other bead against a shoulder on the acetal hose fitting. Static radial seals close the sockets. The intersecting holes in the hose fitting are drilled through its open ends. The grip face washer and stainless circlip capture the complete cartridge against the outlet shoulder. The grip carries the tether load through its strain relief. A tube-forming shop can supply the beaded tube and swaged joint as a subassembly. A spring maker can supply the return spring to its seated load.

The open and closed views use nominal geometry, with first contact shown at the shutoff bead. They establish packaging and travel. The release evidence still needed for the decade claim concerns housing creep with the specified water and cleaner, EPDM compression set and breakaway after long idle periods, and hot actuator force and temperature in the assembled grip. A rebuild can use fresh seals and a replacement clevis rivet.
