# Beduan Solenoid Valve (2-Way NC, 1/4" QC) — Geometry

Beduan 12V DC normally-closed solenoid valve, Amazon B07NWCQJK9. Two-way, with built-in push-to-connect (quick-connect) fittings for 1/4" OD tubing. A product label on the valve body reads "DC12V 0.02-0.8MPa".

## Overall Form

A **T-shaped assembly** of two sub-bodies:

1. **Valve body (white plastic, fluid section):** Contains the diaphragm/poppet mechanism. Two tube ports (inlet and outlet) with push-to-connect fittings for 1/4" OD tubing. The white body is **square in cross-section, 32.71mm x 32.71mm**, excluding the collets and push-connect port stubs. This is the horizontal bar of the T.
2. **Solenoid coil (metal/black-cased, electrical section):** Electromagnetic coil mounted perpendicular to the valve body, rising vertically from the **center** of the white body. This is the vertical stroke of the T. Two blade-style spade connector terminals protrude from the top.

The white body carries a **2x2 grid of mounting holes** on one face. The mounting hole grid outer edge-to-edge dimension is **31.41mm**.

## Axis and Orientation Convention

Oriented for typical rack mounting (solenoid coil on top, tube ports horizontal):

- **X (width):** Across the valve body, perpendicular to tube flow direction
- **Y (depth):** Along the tube flow direction (port-to-port axis)
- **Z (height):** Vertical — from mounting surface of white body up through solenoid coil to spade connector tips

### White Valve Body (Fluid Section)
- Cross-section: **32.71mm x 32.71mm square**
- Collet-to-collet distance (Y): **56.00mm**
- The top face has a **raised circular diaphragm boss** — the diaphragm chamber sits beneath it internally
- Two cylindrical QC port stubs with collets extend from opposite ends along the Y axis
- **2x2 mounting hole grid** on one face, outer edge-to-edge dimension **31.41mm**

### Metal/Black Solenoid Coil Housing (Electrical Section)
- Rectangular case containing the electromagnetic coil
- Mounted on top of the valve body at its **center**, forming the T-shape
- Distance from white body mounting surface to furthest edge of black body: **56.04mm**
- **Spade connector terminals:** Two blade-type electrical contacts protruding from the top of the housing. Spade protrusion from one edge of the black body to spade tips: **31.41mm**

### Overall Assembly Envelope
- Collet-to-collet (Y): **56.00mm**
- Mounting surface to black body edge (Z): **56.04mm**
- White body edge-to-edge (X and Y cross-section): **32.71mm square**

## Mounting Geometry

The metal solenoid coil is centered on the white body, giving a symmetric T-profile from the front (symmetric in X). The white valve body is the primary mounting surface: the 2x2 mounting hole grid (31.41mm edge-to-edge) provides a screw-mounting interface, and the 32.71mm square body suits saddle clamping.

- **White body (X):** 32.71mm square, excluding ports/collets.
- **Collet-to-collet span (Y):** 56.00mm; port stubs extend beyond the collets.
- **Solenoid coil (Z):** rises from the center of the white body, 56.04mm from mounting surface to top of black body.
- **Tube ports:** at both ends of the Y axis, for push-to-connect tubing insertion/removal.
- **Spade connectors:** at the top of the black body, protruding 31.41mm from one edge.
