# John Guest PP0408W 1/4" Union — Geometry Description

John Guest PP0408W push-to-connect union fitting. White acetal copolymer. A symmetric inline union: a single body with identical tube-accepting ports on each end, connecting two pieces of 1/4" OD (6.35 mm nominal / 6.30 mm) tubing end-to-end through a straight-through internal passage. Rotationally symmetric about its long axis (the axis of tube flow). Some units carry a blue collet in place of the white one; mechanism and dimensions are identical.

In side profile the fitting is a barbell: two wider collet-ring sections (15.10 mm OD) at each end, joined by a narrower central body (9.31 mm OD).

## Axis Convention

- **Long axis (L):** the axis of tube flow, running through the center of both ports. All length dimensions are along this axis.
- **Radial (R):** perpendicular to L. All cross-sections perpendicular to L are circles.

## Zones Along the Long Axis

Five zones along L; three unique, because the fitting is symmetric. Described from the left port inward:

```
  ┌──────┐              ┌──────┐
  │COLLET│  ┌────────┐  │COLLET│
  │15.10 │──│  9.31  │──│15.10 │
  │      │  │ center │  │      │
  │12.08 │  │ 12.16  │  │12.08 │
  └──────┘  └────────┘  └──────┘
  ◄─────── 36.32mm body ────────►
  ◄──── 39.13mm collets compressed ────►
  ◄───── 41.80mm collets extended ──────►
```

### Zone 1: Body End Section (left end)
- **OD: 15.10 mm** — the fixed housing containing the collet mechanism.
- **Length: 12.08 mm.**
- Houses the internal collet mechanism: spring-steel gripper teeth that grab the inserted tube, and the collet (release sleeve).
- Outer surface is smooth cylindrical white acetal.
- **End face:** open circular port accepting 1/4" OD (6.35 mm nominal / 6.30 mm) tubing.
- **Collet (release sleeve):** the visible moving ring at the end face that is pushed inward to release the tube. It protrudes from the body end face and translates axially inward when pushed.
  - **Collet OD: 9.57 mm.**
  - **Collet wall thickness: 1.44 mm.**
  - **Collet ID: 6.69 mm** (9.57 − 2 × 1.44). The tube (6.30 mm) passes through this opening with 0.39 mm clearance.

### Zone 2: Shoulder / Step-Down
- Sharp transition from the 15.10 mm body end OD to the 9.31 mm central body.
- A radial face — a flat annular ring perpendicular to the long axis.
- **Shoulder annular width:** (15.10 − 9.31) / 2 = **2.90 mm** per side.
- The shoulders provide axial location when the center body sits in a pocket bore.

### Zone 3: Central Body
- **OD: 9.31 mm** — the narrowest part of the fitting.
- **Length: 12.16 mm.**
- Smooth cylindrical section.

### Zone 4 & 5: Mirror of Zones 2 & 1
Identical shoulder and body end on the opposite end.

## How It Mounts

- The 9.31 mm central body is the section that seats into a pocket bore; the shoulders provide axial location. The 15.10 mm body ends protrude on both sides of the wall.
- The collet is the moving part (9.57 mm OD, 1.44 mm wall, 6.69 mm ID). A release plate pushes on the collet's annular end face. The plate's through-hole sits between 6.30 mm (tube OD) and 6.69 mm (collet ID) so it contacts that face across the full 1.44 mm wall; an inner lip just over 9.57 mm hugs the collet for lateral constraint; an outer bore just over 15.10 mm cradles the body end.
- **Tube insertion depth:** ~16 mm per side. The 6.30 mm tube protrudes ~16 mm into each end.
- **Total axial footprint:** 42 mm with collets extended, 39 mm compressed, plus tube insertion lengths on each side.
