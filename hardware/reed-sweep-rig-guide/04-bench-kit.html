Bench tools

Heat-set tip, soldering iron, fine pliers, cutters, hex keys, multimeter, calipers and smooth gauge pins or drill shanks. Set out a bank card for the striker and an insulated tool for the driver's trimmer.

Before assembly

Dry-fit the bought slide and switch against the printed cradle. Their modeled dimensions come from supplier drawings. Check the actual hole pattern and seating faces before setting inserts or forcing a fit.
