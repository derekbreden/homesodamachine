Calibration

Use the directional crossings

The dry rig measures the magnet's position relative to the reed. The float procedure supplies the waterline reading and the final liquid check.

  1. Capture NOMINAL, then NEAR and FAR, in separate directories. Home again after changing sleds.
  2. Read the directional closing and opening positions and their spread in sweep.json. Keep separate activation regions and retain raw.log so the run can be replayed.
  3. Use the inward closure for full and the return closure for empty. In the same height frame, add the measured magnet-to-liquid offset to the target liquid height, then subtract the signed magnet-to-reed offset at closure. Record the result in the reed calibration record.
Check in liquid before fixing the reeds

Confirm both control crossings are accessible and repeatable in the intended vessel. Apply the acceptance limits in the float calibration procedure before soldering fixed columns or printing the final bridge.
