Wire the controller

Match the driver to the carrier

Identify the enable and motor-supply pins on both boards before fitting the TMC2209.

  1. Disconnect USB and the motor adapter. Locate EN and VS on the driver and the matching socket positions on the carrier.
  2. Set the carrier's three configuration switches to OFF for standalone STEP/DIR. The TMC2209 uses eight microsteps when both MS inputs are low.
  3. With the driver still out, confirm both socket rows match the driver's pinout. Leave it out for the wiring and carrier meter checks.
Check the socket

EN, STEP and DIR go to their labeled signals. Logic supply goes to VCC_IO, motor supply to VS, and ground to GND. Every pin fits its own socket with both rows aligned.
