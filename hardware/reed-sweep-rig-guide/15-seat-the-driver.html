Wire the controller

Match the driver to the carrier

Match the driver's enable and motor-supply corners to the carrier before seating the TMC2209.

  1. Disconnect USB and the motor adapter. Locate EN and the motor supply pin marked VS or VM on the driver.
  2. Set all carrier configuration switches to OFF for standalone STEP/DIR. Confirm that both microstep inputs are low, the setting expected by the rig firmware.
  3. Compare both socket rows with the pinout. Set the driver aside until the wiring and carrier voltage checks are complete.
Check the socket

Match EN, STEP and DIR to their labeled signals. Match VCC_IO to logic supply, VS or VM to motor supply, and both GND pins to ground.
