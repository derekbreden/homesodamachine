Run the rig

Verify direction and home

Start with the loaded sled clear of both ends and the roller in its slot. Keep the motor adapter within reach.

  1. Connect USB and open the console. Repeat the lever check on page 19, then connect the motor adapter. To stop a move, send stop in the console. If it keeps moving, disconnect motor power and reset the controller before repeating the lever check.
  2. Send jog 5. If the sled moves toward the tab, stop. Use status to check the current direction, change it with dirinvert on or dirinvert off, then jog again.
  3. Enter the measured card thickness in millimeters with tab <mm>. Send home and watch the sled approach the switch, back off, creep onto it, then back off to release it.
  4. Home again to confirm that it stops at the same spot. Compare measured carriage travel with a short commanded jog.
Ready to sweep

The switch trips before the sled reaches the tab and the carriage moves the commanded distance without binding. Home again after an interrupted move.
