First motion

Prove direction and home

Start with the loaded sled partway down the tube, clear of both ends, with the roller engaged. Keep the motor adapter within reach.

  1. Connect USB and then the motor adapter. Repeat the free/pressed check if the ESP32 has restarted.
  2. Send jog 5. The sled should move into the tube. If it moves toward the tab, stop and flip the saved direction with dirinvert on or dirinvert off, then jog again.
  3. Send home. Watch it trip, back off, creep onto the switch and back off to release. Use the card's measured thickness at the trip for tab <mm>. The final stopped position includes the release travel.
  4. Home again and look for the same stop. Compare a short commanded move with measured carriage travel.
Ready to sweep

The switch trips before contact, the drive travels the commanded distance and nothing binds. Send a non-empty line to stop motion if the rig misbehaves. Home again before taking data after an interrupted move.
