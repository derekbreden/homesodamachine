How to read the pages

Coral marks the part in hand or the direction it moves. Brass marks a heat-set insert. Blue marks a datum or gauge. The tube is translucent where you need to see inside. The boxed check tells you when to continue.

Enter a page number in the website reader to jump to an operation. A PDF app can use the bookmarks. Print single-sided on Letter at actual size.
