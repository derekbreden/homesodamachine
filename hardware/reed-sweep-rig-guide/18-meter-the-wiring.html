Meter the wiring before power

Pull USB and the barrel adapter. Leave the driver and motor unplugged while you trace the carrier and harness.

Probe atCheck at the other end
Barrel connectorPlus reaches carrier VIN. Minus reaches carrier power GND and the logic return.
Breakout 3V3Reaches carrier V and endstop supply. On the empty carrier, V reaches the driver's VCC_IO socket.
Breakout GNDReaches carrier G, endstop ground and reed return.

Unplugged motorEach phase has finite resistance within its pair. A wire from one pair to the other reads open.
Check for bridges

VIN to GND, VIN to 3V3, and 3V3 to GND must show no persistent short. Check for strands bridging neighboring terminals and tug each connection lightly.

Prove the carrier's logic rail

Disconnect the whole logic header and leave the driver out. Apply the adapter to the carrier alone. Meter VIN to GND for the correct positive supply and V to G for an undriven logic rail. If the carrier sources voltage at V, stop and check its circuit before joining it to the ESP32's 3V3. Unplug the adapter and reconnect the logic header.
