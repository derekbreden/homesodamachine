Meter the wiring before power

Disconnect USB and the barrel adapter. Leave the driver out of its socket and the motor unplugged while you check continuity.

Check for bridges

Check VIN to GND, VIN to 3V3, and 3V3 to GND for shorts. Look for stray strands between terminals and tug each wire lightly.

Check the carrier with motor power alone

Unplug the carrier's logic header and leave the driver out. Connect the barrel adapter and verify positive supply voltage from VIN to power GND. Check V against G for voltage from the carrier. If V is powered, stop and identify its source before connecting the ESP32's 3V3. Unplug the adapter before reconnecting the logic header.
