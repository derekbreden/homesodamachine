Before assembly

How the rig works

The slide moves the Aero float through the dry tube. The controller logs where each reed switch closes and opens as the magnet passes.

The sled sets the gap

NEAR, NOMINAL and FAR place the same float progressively farther from the reeds. The sled's runners ride on the bore.

The switch sets home

The striker trips the switch as the sled moves toward the mouth. The trip sets the controller's zero. The clearance from the tab puts that zero in the tube's coordinate system.

Set up on wood or plastic

Steel can distort the float's magnetic field. Use the specified stainless fasteners near the tube mouth and keep loose steel tools and fasteners a hand's width from the tube while taking readings.
