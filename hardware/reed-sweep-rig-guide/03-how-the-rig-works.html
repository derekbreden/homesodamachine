Before the bench

How the rig works

The slide drives the float lengthwise. The reeds sit under the tube wall on the same line the float passes over.

The sled sets the gap

NEAR, NOMINAL and FAR place the same float progressively farther from the reeds. The sled's runners ride on the bore.

The switch sets home

The striker trips the switch as the sled moves toward the mouth. The distance from that trip to the tab ties the step count to the tube.

Set up on wood or plastic

Keep loose steel, rulers and tools a hand's width from the tube during readings. Use the specified stainless fasteners by the mouth. The slide stays out at the end of the long push rod.
