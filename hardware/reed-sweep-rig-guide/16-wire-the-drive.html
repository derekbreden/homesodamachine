Wire the drive and supplies

The Mac powers the ESP32 over USB-C. The barrel adapter supplies motor power at the driver carrier.

  1. With both supplies unplugged, match the breakout terminals to the carrier labels. Wire the barrel connector's marked plus to VIN and minus to the carrier's power GND.
  2. At the loose motor plug, use resistance to identify the two coil pairs. The expected pairs are black/green and blue/red. Each pair must land on its own phase, 1A/1B or 2A/2B.
Leave power disconnected

Check each wire against the diagram. Leave the motor plug out through the current adjustment.
