Wire the drive and supplies

USB-C powers the ESP32. The barrel adapter connects only to the driver carrier's motor power terminals.

  1. With both supplies unplugged, wire the ESP32 terminals to the carrier as shown. Connect the barrel connector's marked plus to VIN and minus to the carrier's power GND.
  2. Measure resistance at the loose motor plug to identify the coil pairs. Expect black with green and blue with red. One pair goes to 1A/1B and the other to 2A/2B.
Leave power disconnected

Check each wire against the diagram. Leave the motor plug out through the current adjustment.
