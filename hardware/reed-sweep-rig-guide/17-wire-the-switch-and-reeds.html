Wire the switch and reeds

The home switch takes power from the breakout. The reeds share a ground return and each has a dedicated input.

  1. Match the endstop lead colors against the board's markings. Run the red supply to 3V3, black return to GND and green signal to P33.
  2. Connect the comb signals in station order from P16 through P19. Connect the common return to GND.
  3. Route leads outside the cradle and away from the rod, carriage and coupling.
Check station order

Trace each pocket back to its terminal from the tab end. The home lead and comb wires stay clear throughout the hand sweep.
