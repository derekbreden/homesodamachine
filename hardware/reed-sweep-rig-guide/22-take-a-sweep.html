First motion

Take a complete sweep

A sweep travels into the tube and returns at the same speed. Keep the direction with every contact change.

hsm-peripheral reed-sweep --port <port> --sled nominal \
  --reeds 2=A,3=B --play <mm> --glass <mm> \
  --output <new-run-directory>
  1. Clear loose metal. In the console, try one slow sweep <from> <to> inside the range from status. Both distances locate the sled's leading face from the tab.
  2. Close the console. Run the host command with your port, sled and reed names. Pass the measured roller play as --play and the reed's glass length as --glass, both in millimetres.
  3. The host homes and repeats the sweep. Press Ctrl-C to stop a host run. Keep the float and tube identities and the measured wall gap with raw.log and sweep.json.
