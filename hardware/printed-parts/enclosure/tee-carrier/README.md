# Tee carrier

Two PET-GF halves carry Y-C, Y-D, Y-F and Y-G on one guided span. A broad fore
lap, captured centre rail and overlapping upper shelf transmit load between
the halves; an integral rear wall retains their seated X position. Eight ties
hold the tees in their stations. Both return springs bear in closed moving
cups and deeper fixed cups integral to enclosure-front-top.

The complete enclosure assembly-test print evaluates actual assembly effort,
spring capture, return tension, joint retention and overall rigidity. The
[current wall check](../tee-readiness/full-enclosure-print/current-front-top-check.json)
reads the complete production front-top, canonical halves and reusable pusher.
The [study evidence](simple-carrier-study/README.md) identifies its separate
frozen wall and placed hardware. These geometric readings do not establish
printed stiffness or make the real helical springs rigid.

## Bearing structure

The web spans [188 mm](WEB_WIDTH) in X, [55.251 mm](WEB_HEIGHT) in Z and
[6 mm](WEB_T) in Y. The upper shelf is [13.35 mm](FLANGE_HEIGHT) tall and
[18 mm](FLANGE_DEPTH) deep over the middle [113.64 mm](FLANGE_REACH). Broad
inner-web backing makes the available section [6.9 mm](SIDE_WEB_T) thick
between Z182.175 and Z209.075. Its underside clears the inner valve coils'
forward features during their rise from below. The production derivation
includes 0.25 mm running air around that complete entry path; the
[native backing check](entry-backing-check.json) records its placed coils.

Each tee sits in a vertical [17.5 mm](TROUGH_D) trough, with
[0.5 mm](STATION_AIR) radial air and [2.5 mm](STATION_T) backing at the bearing
line. The trough ends at Z[207.724 mm](TROUGH_TOP_Z). Above it, the shallower
bowed-tube relief retains [5.50417 mm](STUB_RELIEF_WEB) of web. The selftest
requires the complete tie backing and upper backing at all four stations.

Each tie crosses two 1.5 × [3.75 mm](TIE_SLOT_HEIGHT) slots. The outermost
slots stand at X±90.00 mm, 0.25 mm inboard of the full handhold backing. Clock
the inner tees' heads outward and the outer tees' heads inward, into the gaps
between tees. Their straps cross the aft web; their heads close on the fore
side. Flush-cut the tails. The valve sockets keep their accepted 7.2 mm bores
and use the existing ties for positive retention.

The left fore lap is 26.35 mm wide and [6 mm](LAP_T) thick over the full web
height. Its single captured rail is 15.75 mm wide, 2 mm thick and 45.251 mm
high. The receiver has a 2.20 mm fore lip, 1.65 mm back wall and 4.85 mm end
bridges. Its broad shelf entry interrupts the upper portion of the rail
bearing; the exact stock is recorded in [checks.json](simple-carrier-study/checks.json).

The shelf plies overlap [31.85 mm](SHELF_OVERLAP_X) across X and
[18 mm](FLANGE_DEPTH) in Y. Each ply is 6.625 mm thick, separated by 0.10 mm
nominal face air. A 2.5 mm upper cheek forms a second broad Z bearing over the
right shelf. Its aft edge lies at Y135.99. The shelf passes through its
matching receiver opening during the ordinary fore slide and outward seat.

The integral rear retaining wall is 51.75 × 9 × 3 mm. Its geometric deflection
witness moves the free tip 2.3 mm while its root remains fixed. The broad lap,
rail and shelf carry the structural overlap; the flexible wall is excluded
from section-stiffness readings. Snap force, print tolerance and endurance
are unmeasured.

The beam spans X. A transverse Y load bends about Z and uses geometric Iz;
a transverse Z load bends about Y and uses Iy. The observed bowing along the
span does not identify the applied force direction. The
[section readings](entry-backing-check.json) compare
native sections and identify their reference; they do not measure assembled
rigidity, interface slip or layer adhesion.

## Springs

The [measured spring record](spring-measurements.json) gives
[27 mm](SPRING_FREE) free length, [6 mm](SPRING_OD) outside diameter and about
[7 mm](SPRING_COMPRESSED_ESTIMATE) fully compressed length, possibly slightly
less. Wire diameter, ID, rate, force and manufacturing tolerances are unknown.

Each moving cup has a closed [6.57 mm](SPRING_BORE_D) teardrop bore,
[11.1 mm](SPRING_BORE_DEPTH) deep, with [4.9 mm](SPRING_BAR_WALL) behind its
floor. Axes are X±[97.535 mm](SPRING_X), Z[211.209 mm](SPRING_Z). Each fixed
cup has the same bore diameter, [8 mm](FIXED_SEAT_DEPTH) depth,
[10.57 mm](FIXED_CUP_OD) outside diameter and [2 mm](FIXED_CUP_WALL) radial
wall. The spring floors stay at Y90.040 and Y109.390 plus carrier travel.

| State | Floor separation | Gap between cup mouths |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Release | [19.35 mm](SPRING_LENGTH_RELEASE) | [0.25 mm](CUP_GAP_RELEASE) |
| Connected | [21.35 mm](SPRING_LENGTH_CONNECTED) | [2.25 mm](CUP_GAP_CONNECTED) |
| Aft stop | [23.85 mm](SPRING_LENGTH_LIMIT) | [4.75 mm](CUP_GAP_LIMIT) |

Both ends lie inside closed bores. The maximum exposed middle length is less
than the measured OD, and a rigid D6 lateral witness is blocked. A real coil
can deform; actual both-end retention, side bowing and unequal-hand operation
are observations for the complete enclosure trial.

During half installation, hold the spring axially at
[12.15 mm](SPRING_LOAD_LENGTH) with the
[temporary flat pusher](../../fixtures/carrier-spring-pusher/README.md). Its
6.3 mm tip and 10 × 3 mm inboard tongue are 2 mm thick. Withdraw it through
the outer tee well after the left half seats; the same tool then loads the
right half. No tool remains in the assembled carrier.
The assembled model displays springs as named clearance envelopes; solid
cylinder volume supplies no spring mass, force or stiffness estimate.

## Grasp and guidance

For insertion, each hand spans its cartridge pocket and carrier tab. Push the
cartridge aft while pulling the carrier fore, bottoming the four tubes at the
fore stop. Relax the grips and finish the cartridge's
[2 mm](CONNECTED_TRAVEL) seating stroke. At connected, the fully extended
branch noses have [0.5 mm](AFT_COLLET_GAP) air to the fixed plate.

For removal, pull the cartridge against the enclosure. The fixed plate carries
the reaction into the complete box. The carrier follows the tubes fore until
the plate continuously depresses the sleeves and releases the tubes.

The [16 mm](GRIP_BAR_T) handhold bars retain their full web roots, aft backing
and broad outer bearing faces. The broad inboard fore corner relief clears
the outer tee during lateral installation; its minimum spring-bore wall is
1.82 mm. The openly accessible fore-rim notch clears the fixed cup and retains
16 mm of rim length through the notch height. The
[grip stock report](simple-carrier-study/grip-stock-checks.json) records the
remaining stock and removed regions.

Flat guide faces retain the carrier in X and Z. Nominal X running air is
[0.25 mm](GUIDE_AIR); supported Z gaps are [0.5 mm](SUPPORTED_GUIDE_AIR).
The outer faces finish flush, giving [215 mm](GRIP_WIDTH) total width. The
finger spaces remain open above and below, backed by continuous 3 mm walls.
The fixed body connects the guide lands and tee wells to both enclosure
flanks. The fixed cup extensions merge into its fore spring-seat wall.

The fore stop depresses all four branch sleeves. Nominal connected travel is
[2 mm](CONNECTED_TRAVEL); the aft stop lies [4.5 mm](GUIDE_TRAVEL) from
release, leaving [2.5 mm](AFT_OVERTRAVEL) beyond connected. An empty carrier
returns to the aft stop under the springs. Tube projection is 11.50 mm beyond
an extended branch face at connected and 10.00 mm beyond a depressed face at
squeeze. Actual forces remain unmeasured.

## Assembly

Work with the front-top loose and the pump bay empty. Seat the four bare tees
at release. Both valve rows and flexible links remain absent.

For each half, left first then right, preload its spring in the closed cup:

1. Carry it through the open rear, 70 mm above its final height and 17.15 mm
   inboard.
2. Lower it 70 mm behind the tees at Y+33 mm.
3. Shift outward 13.90 mm, leaving 3.25 mm to its final lateral position.
4. Slide fore 28.50 mm to the aft stop. The right shelf passes through the
   receiver opening while the integral rear wall deflects.
5. Seat outward 3.25 mm. On the right half the retaining wall returns during
   the final portion of that motion, retaining the joint.
6. Withdraw the temporary pusher 15.435 mm inboard, then lift it 70 mm through
   the outer well. The spring expands into its fixed cup.

The shelf needs no separate lift or twist, and the joint needs no fastening
operation. These are geometric paths; actual hand effort is a full-enclosure
trial observation. The [assembly view](simple-carrier-study/assembly-sequence.png)
shows the right half's final motions against the parked left half.

Hold the joined carrier at release while the aft valves rise from underneath,
staged 5.45 mm fore of their seats, then slide them aft onto their posts.
Fit the tee ties after this valve entry. Complete the fore valves, bowed stubs
and hairpins using [internal-plumbing.md](/hardware/assembly/internal-plumbing.md).
The fixed Beduan sockets retain their accepted fit and existing tie retention.

## Print and verification

Each half is one valid native solid. The generator exports both primary STEP,
STL and viewer payloads. The STEP keeps native analytic geometry; the STL and
payload carry the enclosure-aligned flutes on the outside grip faces.

The upright orientation places the web and backing on the bed. Loose-half
support regions include the flat handhold undersides, upper shelf, rear wall
and short trough-top ledges. These have exposed removal access before hardware
installation. The captured rail's receiver has an undercut behind its fore
lip; its slice must show the support and extraction path through the fore
opening. The fixed cups open into the flank recesses. Functional bearing and
hand-contact faces remain flat. The enclosure
[support-removal policy](../enclosure/README.md#support-removal-strategy)
applies to the full trial's actual slice.

The source selftest requires complete tee and tie backing, open tie paths,
closed spring bores and floors, full handhold roots and backing, outer shelf
stock, valid solids and the native inter-half assembly path. The assembly
check uses complete bodies for the wall/tee route, spring preloading and
pusher removal, valve installation and working travel. Its rigid-body contact
readings do not prove elastic stiffness or spring retention.

The complete enclosure trial supplies spring capture through every stop,
empty return, unequal-hand behavior, coupled tee motion, global bowing,
retaining-wall fit, support removal and actual assembly effort. Functional
release/reconnection uses all actual flexible links. No separate carrier
fixture or pre-print physical spring acceptance gate is specified.
