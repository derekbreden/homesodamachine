# Tee carrier

The tee carrier is the pump-tee dock of the [Lillium build](/hardware/assembly/lillium-cutover.md),
fitted to the printed front-top and front-bottom that build uses; the flank windows, cover posts
and spring pockets below are features of those prints. The integrated machine has no carrier:
its four pump tees stand in front-top's tee wall ([pump dock](../pump-dock/README.md)).

One PET-GF plate, `enclosure-tee-carrier-plate`, carries Y-C, Y-D, Y-F and Y-G across the front
column. It is long, flank face to flank face, so neither end stands past the
enclosure. Two window covers close the flank openings aft of it.

## Low-force sliding clearance

The sliding fit requires **low-force clearance for rough overhang surfaces involved**.
The [accepted carrier](low-force-trial/physical-acceptance.json) slides well with less
tilt against the existing front-top opening. The [low-force geometry](low-force-trial/README.md) provides
1.00 mm nominal roof clearance: 0.25 mm sliding, 0.25 mm for the supported roof and
the [working 0.50 mm low-force addition](../clearance-audit.md). The floor has its
separate 0.25 mm sliding gap, giving 1.25 mm total vertical travel.
Support-removal roughness and the available actuation force are part of that fit; the
usual nominal clearance alone does not establish low-force motion.
This acceptance applies to the carrier’s own contact geometry and print orientation.
Spring return and simultaneous four-collet release remain separate assembly observations.

## Plate

The plate is thick and tall, the tee's
 run span with both sleeves pressed, so the plate ends where a
pressed run collet's face does. At each end, from the outer tee's trough edge out through the
flank, it is a column wide and tall that slides on the
opening's floor and roof, and deep: it reaches fore of the plate, so that with
every collet pressed home it stands off the tee wall's aft face.

Each tee's run axis lies on the plate's fore face, and its barrel sits half inset in a vertical
 trough: the collar envelope with
 radial air. of plate stands behind every trough; the troughs
and the tie slots are all that break the plate's section.

Two ties hold each tee, one at each tie band above and below its run axis,
round the run roots either side of the branch. Each band has a
 × slot through the plate at each edge of the trough, its
outboard wall on the trough's edge. The tie closes round the tee's front, drops past the run
root into the slot on each side and crosses the plate's back. Its
 strap across the back is inside the flank openings.
The fore mouths open into the trough across its full adjoining curve. Their inboard
edge stands one backing thickness inside the narrow passage; the 2 mm through passages
and the full aft backing keep their stated dimensions.

Each column's end face is flush with its flank and is show face. All four of its edges, and the
column's four edges running inboard to it, roll over on the enclosure's
shoulder, the radius of the enclosure's own side edges, so each corner closes as one blend the
way the enclosure's front corners do. The plate joins each column at the column's inboard face;
the plate's back is flush with the column's and its underside stands just over the column's, so
the plate's square bottom-aft corner stands out past the column's rounded one there. Each end
face is smooth within its rounded edges.

The plate prints lying on its back, troughs open upward; the columns stand out in the plane of
the bed, and their aft shoulders rise off it.

The [low-force trial](low-force-trial/README.md) prints without supports. Its wider
print-bottom foot has an additive chamfer tangent to the near-vertical taper, entirely
inside the carrier's overall dimensions. The first layer is 0.20 mm; the bottom
transition and body use normal 0.24 mm PET-GF layers. The complete print-top round
uses 0.08 mm layers.

Six walls apply only in print Z 0–6.1 mm, with two walls above and the original
order: inner walls, outer wall, then infill. Infill/wall overlap is 15%; speeds and
accelerations retain the saved profile values. The [surface record](physical-acceptance.json)
accepts the bottom transition; the [sliding-fit record](low-force-trial/physical-acceptance.json)
accepts the 1.00 mm roof-gap carrier with the existing front-top.

## Openings and travel

Each front flank carries a window in Y by in Z
through front-top's flank section and front-bottom's seam rail under it. Its
fore face is the tee wall's aft face; its aft face is the staged plate's strapped back plus
. Its floor stands under the tees' extended run span and the
columns. Its roof is the root of the fore valve tray's corbel on the tee wall's aft face; it
prints facing down over support in front-top, so it stands over the columns:
0.25 mm running, the supported face's, and the local 0.50 mm low-force addition.

Between the flanks the same cutter is the tees' sweep, tall across the
window's depth, and the +X column's crossing at the staged plate, floor to roof. Inside the
column it cuts only front-bottom's seam rail.

The assembly shows the tees connected: each branch collet extended, its nose
 off the tee wall's release face. Pressing every collet home takes the plate
 fore, that air and the stroke; the windows
leave the columns fore.

## Springs

Four return springs, two in each column, push the carrier aft off the tee wall. Each is a
uxcell 304 stainless spring, wire, measured at OD,
 free and about solid. Each runs in
 tunnels on both sides of the gap between the column
and the tee wall: a blind bore deep in the column's fore face, and a
pocket deep in the tee wall's aft face, teardropped over its crown
for front-top's mouth-down print.

The column's bore is as deep as keeps a free spring's tip out of
it, no further fore of the staged column than the branch noses; the pocket takes the rest of the
spring's connected length. Each bore keeps of
column inboard of it; outboard, a land of flat fore face stands between its mouth
and the end face's shoulder, and the round thickens the wall from there. The two in a column
stand apart, one either side of the tees' run axis, the lower's mouth the same
land clear of the column's rounded bottom edge.

Connected, each spring is long,
 short of free; with every collet pressed home it is
 long, short of free.

## Window covers

Aft of the seated carrier each window stands open to its aft face. A
window cover closes that from inside the flank: `enclosure-window-cover-west` and
`enclosure-window-cover-east`, mirror images, each a PET-GF slab thick with its
outboard face inboard of the flank's inner face.

Front-top carries a post for each on the flank's inner face at the window's aft face,
 across, aft and tall. It stands from where the flank's own face begins
over the seam channel up to the window's roof, and its underside is a 45° corbel on the same
plane as the flank's underside below it. Its top half is slotted against the flank,
 wide and deep. After its running relief, the inboard
finger retains a full 3 mm section.

The cover's tongue drops into the slot and hangs
 over its floor: 0.25 mm running and for the
floor's rounded turn up into the slot's walls. The slab bottom also stands 0.35 mm above
front-bottom's seam rail. The cover's post guides and its clearance behind the carrier
use. Fore of the post the slab reaches, and stands the
window's full height,. Aft of the post it reaches
 at the post's height, so post and cover stand aft of the
window. The finger stands between the two, so a cover lifts off
only straight up.

The fore window continues 0.35 mm below the cover and 0.50 mm above it. At the
inboard flank face, 45° entry run-outs join those floor and roof clearances to the
guide's receiving air. The guided faces keep 0.25 mm of normal room, and the post's
inboard finger has its complete 3 mm section.

Each cover prints lying on its outboard face.

## Assembly

The carrier goes in before both valve rows: its staged pass crosses the coils of V-C, V-D, V-G
and V-J, and the +X column's crossing the bodies of V-E, V-F, V-H and V-I.

1. Tie the four bare tees into the troughs, branches fore. No tube is in any tee.
2. Enter the plate through the −X flank opening aft of its seat, where
   every branch nose passes behind the tee wall's aft face.
3. With a spring in each of the four column bores, slide the plate fore
  . Each branch enters its journal in the tee wall, and each spring's tip
   enters its pocket and bottoms there.
4. After V-G and V-J and before V-F and V-I, lower each window cover onto its post, tongue into
   the slot.
