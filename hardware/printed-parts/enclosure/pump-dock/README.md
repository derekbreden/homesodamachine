# Permanent pump dock

The customer withdraws one complete two-pump cartridge, changes the prepared
pumps under its two-screw cap, and pushes the cartridge home. Four machined
pump stems enter four permanent TPU receivers together. The receivers,
flat interior keepers, rear support, commercial tees and magnetic mounts
remain in the machine. No additional customer replacement item is required.

## Interface and load paths

The closed PET-GF wall contains each TPU receiver in X/Z. Its small front
funnel flexes through the wall bore during factory assembly. One flat
3 mm keeper slides down guides on the interior wall face into the
receiver's rear groove and locks with an M3×8 screw. The keeper restrains Y;
its U opening has 0.35 mm radial running clearance around the relaxed TPU
neck. The rigid bore establishes the receiver's transverse position.
The tightened keeper bears on the interior wall face and the TPU collar's
rear plane, with 0.35 mm axial room to the groove's rear flange. A separate
two-screw frame supports all four rear cuffs and installs after the keepers.
Its screw holes retain 3 mm stock above and below the bridge. Four rings
connect through narrow uprights to a high bridge, clearing the keeper
heads and tee branches. Its two mounting posts meet the frame's fore face,
so tightening the screws does not drive the support into the TPU flange.
The locks lie on the two magnet-column axes, X±52.225 mm, between each
pump's fluid ports. The 10 mm square posts retain 3 mm stock around their
Ø4 mm insert pilots.

Each receiver has a 9 mm funnel mouth, a 6 mm long Ø6.10 mm front seal land,
a short relieved transition and a 6 mm rear seal land. The sealing surface
is continuous TPU. The front retaining collar has a 1.1 mm outer rim and a
45-degree ramp inside the wall; the 0.6 mm front lip remains flexible for
factory insertion. The front land receives the smooth Ø6.35 mm machined
stem; the permanent rear land receives an 18.175 mm LLDPE tail entering a
commercial PP0208E tee. The nominal customer tip has 0.50 mm of travel before
its actual Ø6.05 mm end first contacts the inward 45-degree relief taper.
The taper runs from
Y7.625 to Y8.525 in the receiver frame and reaches a Ø5.00 mm throat; it
contains no flat downward-facing internal shoulder. The factory-tail stop
retains 0.475 mm axial stock at the throat and a nominal 0.675 mm radial
bearing width on the Ø6.35 mm tail. The cartridge body stops establish
insertion; the four tube ends do not have to compress against a common bottom.

Four integral 4 × 12 mm PET-GF stop lands meet the lower cradle at two
heights on its outboard edges. Their 192 mm² total bearing and 3 mm wall
backing establish the closing plane, while the remaining broad cartridge
face retains 0.246 mm clearance from the wall. The magnets are match-set
after these rigid lands seat. The [body-stop review](body-stop-native-review.json)
checks contact, overtravel restraint and the cartridge/cap service paths.

The lower cartridge positively catches each stem's Ø16 × 2.5 mm flange
in both Y directions. Its cap blocks the vertical installation route.
The flexible pump tubing therefore does not carry the stem's insertion
reaction as an unsupported axial column. Split Ø22.30 mm guards retain
3.00 mm radial stock around their Ø16.30 mm flange pockets, back the front
of each receiver and enter Ø22.80 mm counterbores in the fixed wall.
The tip projects 7.746 mm beyond the cartridge's flat aft plane; the guard
extends only to Y82.25 mm. See the [machined stem definition](machined-stem.md)
for material, surface finish, factory preparation and load screens.

Six attracting SB443-IN pairs occupy two columns between the fluid ports.
Their bare pole faces meet when the cartridge reaches its body stops.
The magnets load after printing and are captured by thin groove rails and
keyed DP420 Off-White bedding. The [magnet process](magnet-retention.md)
defines polarity, dimensional tolerances, setting and cure. Published pull
to steel is not an installed retention rating. Electrical contact faces
are recessed 0.127 mm into each carrier, giving a nominal 0.500 mm face gap
independently of the 0.246 mm body gap.

## Fixed plumbing and enclosure

The four pump-side tees stand in integral open-aft saddles behind the wall.
Two owned six-inch, 18 lb ties secure each tee's fixed run collars. Open
central tie bands retain 3 mm outer rails and end stock; 3 mm side webs join
each saddle to the wall. The two run-end collets remain exposed. The fore
branch collet has about 0.733 mm clearance to the rear frame and needs the
tee clear of that frame for access.
The four short tails are straight and stationary. The pump-side valve
links are fixed S bends, with 14 mm centerline radius.

For factory branch repair with a qualified isolated, depressurized flavor
circuit, disconnect the two exposed run links and remove that tee's two
ties. Withdraw the tee and its LLDPE tail aft out of the permanent rear TPU
socket, then operate the branch collet with the tee clear of the frame.
The Ø6.35 mm tail passes through the frame's Ø12 mm ring; receivers,
keepers and frame stay installed. This defines the geometric removal
route without a qualified extraction-force or service-life result.

The tees stand at Y117.54 mm. The fore valve tray plane is Y106.24 mm with
seats at Z252.425 mm; the aft deck's tray plane is Y184.29 mm. The enclosure's rear
plane is Y475.3 mm. Pump mounting and front cartridge datums are independent
of these plumbing stations. The enclosure has closed side walls at the pump
bay and contains no tee carrier, release springs or side window covers. The
keeper access pocket has a 45-degree roof rising aft, preserving its full
20 mm lift space.

## Factory assembly

1. Remove supports from the wall bores, keeper guides, saddle windows,
   cartridge flange pockets and cap guard webs. Finish and inspect functional
   lands without widening the TPU seal bores. Fit inserts before the wet parts.
2. Flex each receiver's small front funnel through its wall bore from the
   interior. Seat its recessed collar without twisting the bore. Slide each
   flat keeper down from above the guides, capture the groove and secure its
   M3×8 lock. Fit the common rear support over the rear cuffs and secure its
   two M3×8 locks. These six screws use RX-M3S×4.0 short inserts and an owned
   Ø7 × 0.5 mm M3 washer under each head.
3. Thread the eight six-inch ties through the saddle windows before fitting
   tee bodies. Prepare square, undamaged 18.175 mm LLDPE tails and insert
   each into its receiver's rear socket to the internal shoulder, engaging
   6 mm. Bring the tee fore from the interior, engaging its branch to the
   11.5 mm insertion datum. Seat the tee in its fixed saddle and secure both
   run collars with the prethreaded ties.
   Complete the stationary valve links and hairpins.
4. Prepare complete pumps with the specified machined stems, BPT trim and
   ties. Drop them into the lower cradle with each flange in its upward-open
   pocket, connect the four pump terminals and close the cap. The assembled
   four-tip gauge registers the cartridge back and checks simultaneous tip
   position. Four open rectangular fore reliefs clear the moving guards; the
   aft tip backstop is 3 mm thick. Calibrate its actual 7.746 mm datum before
   using it as a factory measurement fixture; printed nominal dimensions
   alone do not establish it.
5. Assemble the electrical contacts on their recessed ear datums. Follow the
   [contact mounting audit](../../../reference/yyfkgcp-pogo-4p/mounting-audit.md)
   and measure actual pin compression with the frame on its own stops.
   Set, bed and cure the magnets using the matched installed frame as defined
   in the magnet process. Keep resin away from pole faces and wet parts.
6. Inspect all four receiver lips and all six magnet contacts with the
   cartridge fully seated. Verify the cartridge withdraws straight and that
   the receiver keepers, rear cuffs and tees remain stationary. Continue with
   the [assembly acceptance procedure](../../../assembly/acceptance-and-burn-in.md)
   and the [customer service sequence](../../../service/pump-replacement.md).

## Engineering evidence and remaining qualification

The [pressure and retention review](pressure-retention-review.json) identifies
the canonical single-channel pressure paths, compound fault comparisons and
a one-off qualification procedure using the documented bench tools.

The [fluid review](fluid-geometry-review.json) checks the fixed pack and
occupied receiver envelopes. The [stem review](stem-native-review.json)
checks positive axial capture, cap installation and actual purchased pump
clearance. The [magnet review](magnet-geometry.json) checks the supplier's
full size envelope, keyed fill, loading route and mating datums. These are
native geometry and analytical screens, not physical pressure or lifetime
results. Local source hashes identify their scope.

The [local manufacturing review](post-lint-correction-review.json) records
the collar and internal taper, rear-frame ligaments and metal clearances,
open jig reliefs, saddle tie bands and sloping keeper-pocket roof. Its local
native results have separate bindings from the complete appliance and
native TPU slice.

The [rear-frame support review](rear-frame-support-review.json) binds the
column-centered locks, 3 mm post ligaments, keeper lift envelopes and local
packing clearances. Its idealized outer-load beam comparison does not
establish the assembled frame's stiffness or a PET-GF allowable.

The retained pump-holder fit and manual TPU elbow fit support the fitted
pump geometry and initial receiver bore choice. They do not establish the
new dock's seal force, retention, compression set, cleaning performance or
service life. The TPU's technical data do not establish finished wet-part
food-contact suitability. The specified machined PP stock declaration also
requires finished-part application assessment. No permanent-lifetime or
system-pressure rating is claimed by these CAD checks.

Physical qualification must resolve four coupled questions on the complete
frame: whether the magnets fully seat all four seals, whether extraction is
comfortable, whether hydraulic separation can unseat the cartridge, and
whether repeated pump service plus the actual cleaning chemistry damages
the permanent receiver. The force screen in the stem definition uses 80 psi
as a conservative comparison case, not an operating setpoint. Use the
actual isolated flavor circuit's operating and fault pressure envelope to
set the wet test. Retain the measured force, pressure, temperature, fluid,
cleaning exposure, cycle count and article hashes with the result; a short
room-temperature water fit cannot establish unattended service life.
