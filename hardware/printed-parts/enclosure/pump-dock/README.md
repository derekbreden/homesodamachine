# Pump mount and tee wall

Front-top contains the upper fitted pump inverses in one broad body that continues into the
display housing and enclosure roof. The broad lower retainer, `enclosure-pump-retainer`,
carries the fitted lower head wells and skirt lands. Two integral wings engage front-top’s
flank receivers. Each wing is rooted at the top and carries its retaining hook at the bottom.
The two printed halves capture each pump’s molded skirt; the surrounding stock retains the
enclosure’s simple outer form.

The pumps and retainer enter motor-first from the underside while front-top is loose.
Four bottom-open gates in the tee wall admit their prepared 1/4-inch LLDPE stubs. Fit the
four purchased John Guest PP0208E tees from aft after the retainer is secured, pushing each
branch onto its fixed pump tube. The snap-fit mount retains the pumps; each tee's
branch collet grips and seals its own tube. Complete this work before front-top joins the
populated lower enclosure.

## Tees and tee wall

One PP0208E stands on each pump port: Y-C and Y-D on pump A, Y-F and Y-G on pump B, west to east Y-G, Y-F, Y-C, Y-D. Each tee's branch faces its pump on the pump tube's axis, [Z186.174](DOCK_ROW_Z); its run stands upright.

Front-top's bay bulkhead is the tee wall: one flat slab of PET-GF the bay's whole width and height, [15.821 mm](DOCK_WALL_T) thick from the bay face at [Y82.515](DOCK_BAY_FACE_Y) to its aft face at [Y98.336](DOCK_WALL_AFT_Y). Its fore band, the collet plate, [4.675 mm](DOCK_PLATE_T) thick, ends at the bore face, [Y87.19](DOCK_BORE_FACE_Y). Each tee has two teardropped cuts on its branch axis: a [Ø11.93 mm](DOCK_HOLE_D) collet opening through the fore band and a [Ø17 mm](DOCK_BORE) branch bore from the bore face through the wall's aft face, [11.15 mm](DOCK_BORE_DEPTH) long. The collar's [Ø16.5 mm](DOCK_COLLAR_ENVELOPE) reference envelope has [0.25 mm](DOCK_BORE_AIR) of running air. A bottom-open tube-loading gate joins each pair of openings to the underside. The remaining bore-face land stops the fixed branch barrel. The wall also roots the fore valve tray and joins the continuous pump/display body above it.

Each tee stands with its fixed branch barrel [0.6 mm](DOCK_BARREL_AIR) aft of the bore face, its run axis at [Y108.04](DOCK_TEE_Y). Its branch collet stands extended in the collet plate's hole, its whole [1.5 mm](DOCK_STROKE) stroke fore of the bore face, and grips the pump tube; the tube bottoms at the tee's own stop and seals on the collet's O-ring. The collet reaches [2.1 mm](DOCK_COLLET_PROUD) past the fixed barrel, and the hole runs on to the bay face, so with the tee pushed fore onto its barrel the collet still stands clear of the wall and keeps its grip.

## What holds each tee

The bore holds the tee's branch in X and Z. Its fixed branch barrel stops it fore, landing on the bore face's annulus, and its lower return, anchored in the rear valve, stops it aft. The wall's aft face stands [1.454 mm](DOCK_BODY_AIR) clear of the tee's run barrel.

## Fore valves and upper tubes

V-E, V-F, V-H and V-I stand fixed on their tray posts directly over the tees, bottom collets at [Z222.675](DOCK_FORE_VALVE_Z). Each fore valve's axis is [Y108.04](DOCK_FORE_VALVE_Y), over its tee's run axis, so the short upper tube between them, [15.25 mm](DOCK_UPPER_TUBE) exposed, stands straight.

## Lower returns

Each lower return joins a tee's lower collet ([Z164.924](DOCK_LOWER_PORT_Z)) to its rear valve's bottom collet with two NeoFit ATEU44W stem elbows and one straight tube of the machine's black 1/4" LLDPE. The tee's elbow has its stem cut to the run collet's seat, so its shoulder stands on the collet face and its axes cross [6.05 mm](DOCK_ELBOW_DROP) under it, at [Z158.879](DOCK_RETURN_Z). All four rear valves' bottom collets stand at [Z168.675](DOCK_RETURN_VALVE_Z), and each valve's elbow keeps [3.75 mm](DOCK_STEM_SHOWING) of stem below its collet, so it crosses at the same height. Each elbow's socket opens toward the other and the tube runs level between them: [20.1 mm](DOCK_RETURN_TUBE) exposed on the inner two, [20.69 mm](DOCK_OUTER_RETURN_TUBE) on the outer two, which lean inboard to V-G and V-J at [X±73.82](DOCK_OUTER_RETURN_X). The elbows' lowest surface is [Z151.132](DOCK_RETURN_FLOOR), [5.13 mm](DOCK_CONDENSER_AIR) over the condenser's top at the east-outer station. The tube bends nowhere.

## Pump tubes and lower retainer

Each pump carries two straight [33.2 mm](DOCK_PUMP_TUBE) tubes of the machine's black 1/4" LLDPE, pushed into its BPT tube ends in place of Kamoer's barbs. The pump head's own housing, tightened at the factory, clamps the tube around each one. The tubes pass the retainer's open passages and rise through the tee-wall loading gates; their tips stand at [Y97.19](DOCK_TIP_Y), [14.925 mm](DOCK_TIP_FROM_BACK) behind the retainer's flat back at [Y82.265](DOCK_CRADLE_BACK), bottomed at the tees' stops.

Set the tips with both pumps seated in the loose lower retainer on the bench. The existing
tube setting jig bears on that flat rear face. Remove it before raising the prepared unit
into front-top: the jig's broad body needs the space occupied by the fixed tee wall.
Installed, the retainer's back stands [0.25 mm](DOCK_FRAME_GAP) ahead of the wall's bay face.
The two lower hooks seat on the receiver floors and retain the molded-skirt capture. Pump
removal requires opening the front assembly and disconnecting its plumbing and motor leads.
Each receiver has a central screwdriver opening to flex its wing inward for release.

The pump and display leads share one rear passage through the continuous stock. Its
22 mm diamond section is centred at X0/Z280; the rear upper half opens as a plain U
over the bulkhead lip through Y99. Thread DC-5's
bare pump end through it before crimping and landing the Fastons on the loose pumps. Draw
slack aft during motor-first insertion. SIG-7 uses the same passage with the display facet
open; finish the display and loom before installing the funnel frame.

## Snap wings and release access

Each wing, hook and carrier occupies the same Y14.25–82.265 span, 68.015 mm long.
Its receiver spans Y14–82.515, leaving 0.25 mm at each end. The upper section joins the
wing to the retainer continuously; the lower [21 mm](PUMP_SNAP_LENGTH) is free to flex,
with a 16 mm straight spring above its 5 mm hook and a [2.4 mm](PUMP_SNAP_WING_THICK) wall.
The upper root is at Z = [186.195 mm](PUMP_SNAP_ROOT_Z).
The hooks flex inward during upward insertion and spring outward into the receivers.
The receiver floors at Z = [165.045 mm](PUMP_SNAP_RECEIVER_FLOOR_Z) leave the specified 0.15 mm static allowance below the
nominal hook-bearing faces at Z165.195.

One [4.5 mm](PUMP_SNAP_PORT_WIDTH)-wide opening at each receiver’s Y centre admits a small flathead screwdriver
from below. Its roof is one continuous 45° face, from X99.6/Z174.8 to X104.5/Z169.9 on the
right, mirrored on the left, leaving 3 mm of outer wall. This solid corbel supports the
roof while preserving the blade path. Use the receiver’s lower edge as the fulcrum to
push the wing inward until its hook clears. Support the pump/retainer unit while releasing
both sides and lowering it.

## Factory assembly

The [internal plumbing procedure](../../../assembly/internal-plumbing.md) owns the complete
sequence. Keep front-top loose, with both valve rows, pump tees, display and funnel frame
absent while loading and securing the pumps.

1. Seat both prepared pumps in the loose retainer. Set the four tube tips with the jig,
   tighten the pump-head housings and remove the jig.
2. Thread the bare DC-5 pump end from the shared rear passage to the open underside. Crimp,
   ring out and land its Fastons on the loose pumps, with the +X pair serving pump A.
3. Raise the prepared unit motor-first. Guide the four tubes up their loading gates while
   drawing lead slack aft. Press upward until both wings spring outward and their lower
   hooks seat on the receiver floors. Inspect both engagements before fitting plumbing.
4. With the rear valve row absent, make up each tee, upper tube and fore valve, then bring
   the group in from aft. Push the branch over its fixed pump tube and into its wall bore
   as the fore valve seats on its posts. Check the actual insertion marks. Seat all four
   fore groups before pressing the rear valves onto their tray posts.
5. Cut and fit the lower-return elbows and straight tubes from the open underside. Use
   actual insertion marks and tug-check every push-fit joint. Finish the remaining front
   manifold connections and wiring.
6. Thread the display loom through the shared passage and leave its end accessible at the
   facet. Join front-top to the populated lower enclosure with pumps, retainer and plumbing
   secured. Fit and wire the display with the facet open. Complete central connections
   through the open funnel aperture and plug the XHP-4 into J13 before fitting the frame.
7. Follow [assembly acceptance](../../../assembly/acceptance-and-burn-in.md).

## Engineering evidence

The [accepted pump-holder fit](../../../reference/kamoer-kphm400/physical-fit.json) records
firm holding without vertical play in its identified cradle and cap. The integral upper
half and snap-fit underside retainer use the fitted profiles with a direct two-face skirt
capture. This assembly has no physical fit, retention, release-force, vibration, load-capacity
or lifetime result. The [pump-snap review](../enclosure/pump-snap-review/README.md) records
its geometry evidence. The [three-screw mount receipts](../enclosure/pump-mount-review/README.md)
retain their identified inputs and scope; they do not qualify the snap interface.

The assembled fluid path has no physical seal or cleaning acceptance. Each tee's seating
force on its fixed pump tube, the tee's aft stop through its lower return, the pump head
clamp's hold on each tube, the tees' running fit in their gated bores and the sealing of
the complete assembly remain unmeasured. The valve's seat depth, which sets its elbow's
stem cut, is a layout estimate, and the stem elbows' seal with a cut stem is unqualified.
Material data alone do not establish finished wet-part food-contact suitability.
