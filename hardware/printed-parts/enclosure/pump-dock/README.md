# Permanent pump dock

The customer withdraws the complete two-pump cartridge, changes the prepared pumps under its two-screw cap, and pushes the cartridge home. Four short machined stems enter permanent TPU junctions together. The junctions, returns, straight links, dry fixtures and magnets remain in the machine. Customer service requires only complete prepared replacement pumps.

## Fluid path and support

Each asymmetric junction has a pump-facing funnel, an upward outlet and a lower outlet angled 45 degrees aft. Its front seal is a continuous 6 mm long Ø6.10 mm TPU land around the smooth Ø6.35 mm pump stem. The upper outlet connects to its fore valve with a straight LLDPE link. The lower outlet connects through a straight diagonal link, a fixed compound TPU return and a short straight valve tail. The compound return turns 45 degrees into its cross-run and 90 degrees upward; the LLDPE supplies straight connections. Two purchased PP0208E tees remain in the source crossbar.

The closed PET-GF wall locates each junction in X/Z. The front funnel folds through its wall bore during factory assembly. A flat 3 mm keeper slides down the interior guides and captures Y. Its fore face is 0.15 mm behind the wall plane; a broad raised land supports that face when tightened. The U slot has 0.35 mm radial clearance around the relaxed TPU neck, and the groove has 0.20 mm room behind the keeper. The keeper has 0.25 mm fore room in its temporary insertion pose. One front-driven M3×8 screw and Ø7 × 0.5 mm washer lock each keeper. The RX-M3S×4.0 insert sits in its 5 mm thick rear boss, with 3 mm radial PET-GF stock around the insert knurl. A 3.75 mm deep crowned wall recess puts the screw head 0.25 mm behind the pump-bay face and leaves 3.25 mm of washer backing. The nominal screw reaches 4.10 mm past the insert entry, fully engaging its 4 mm body, and ends 0.90 mm before the boss back. Fit the insert before sliding the keeper into place. The keeper retains its 20 mm upward removal route.

Each station also has one rigid PET-GF cradle and one closing cover. Four broad TPU keys sit beside the two junction socket stops and two return socket stops. Their fitted necks sit in the closing cover's rigid throats, and their heads catch its nominal 3 mm outside plate. Complementary 45-degree ledges follow the actual printed key undersides, giving each socket a local reaction in both axial directions. The ledges have 0.15 mm of geometric take-up along their socket axes; this is not a loaded-deflection rating. Each head retains a broad complete-thickness bearing patch; the named valve-port clearance corner has 2–3 mm backing. Two shared M3×12 bolts pass through the cover and cradle into the wall and aft-tray host lands, closing the escape path of the cover and wet bodies together. Only TPU flexes during local head pull-through. The four keeper M3×8 screws and eight shared fixture M3×12 screws are factory locks. Each fixture lock includes a 0.5 mm washer, two 3 mm tabs, a supported 0.15 mm spacer and a 0.25 mm host land. Its tip projects 5.10 mm behind the nominal host plane, 5.35 mm from the raised insert entry, leaving 0.65 mm before the blind pilot end. The broad aft backstrip retains a named 2.90 mm section beside the sliding arm pocket. The hard lock seats retain 3 mm beneath their actual bearing patches. Outer aft washer seats have 19.186 mm² of balanced contact, with the screw axis inside the support hull; their partial annular footprint is recorded in the integration review.

The bolted cradles and closing covers support the four socket-stop locations and close the diagonal tube guide. Each guide has a 4 mm axial band with 3 mm upper, lower and inboard walls; the cover closes its outward escape route. The inner guide's unused upper continuation is open beside the coil-terminal clearance. The bases have open factory loading paths; the closing covers carry the complementary stop-support fingers. A straight tube cannot withdraw from one socket without meeting the opposite fixed stop. Each socket contains 6 mm of circular seal land and a 0.5 mm entry. The nominal tube-to-stop clearance is 0.15 mm. Along a diagonal link, separating motion meets the junction key's opposite roof after 0.010 mm and the return key's ledge after 0.150 mm. Together with the tube clearance, the geometric allowance is 0.310 mm, leaving 0.190 mm for manufacturing variation, temperature and loaded deformation within the 0.50 mm total budget. At that limit, at least 5.50 mm of seal engagement remains. The local axial ledges carry the socket reactions through the bolted cover; the budget does not assume a free TPU branch holds its stop in place. TPU relaxation and the assembled permanent seal have no lifetime result.

The nominal diagonal cuts are 46.132 mm at the inner stations and 33.019 mm at the outer stations. Actual mounted stop spacing governs factory preparation. For a short valve tail, seat the tube fully in the actual Beduan valve, mark its mouth and cut 8.5 mm beyond that mark: 2 mm exposed plus 6.5 mm in the TPU socket. The CAD insertion depth is not a measurement of the received collet.

The enclosure's rear inner boundary is Y468.3 mm. The four new upper links and eight links around the returns are straight. The two retained formed source links, fluid-3 and fluid-5, have their own R14 geometry; this pump-link design does not qualify those source bends.

## Factory assembly

Keep the front-top loose, with access from underneath. Fit and finish its inserts and rigid seats before introducing wet parts. The [internal plumbing procedure](../../../assembly/internal-plumbing.md) owns the complete sequence.

1. Fit the aft valves and their coils to the loose front-top, with its underside open. Prepare the short valve tails from their actual seated mouth marks.
2. On the bench, engage each empty cradle and closing cover through its proved side-entry route. Leave the cover parked 6.25 mm outward from its closed position. Raise these empty pairs into the front-top from underneath; keep all wet groups out of the loading windows.
3. Prepare each fore valve, straight upper link and junction as a connected group. Move that group toward the pump side in −Y. The fore valve enters its mounting posts while the junction funnel folds through the closed wall. Seat the junction collar without twisting the seal bore.
4. Slide each keeper down its guides and fit its washer-backed M3×8 screw from the pump bay. Tighten against the raised wall land. This front access remains available with both valve rows seated and the cartridge absent.
5. Seat each diagonal link in its junction. Engage the free return on that link below its final pose, then raise it 6.5 mm onto the short vertical valve tail through the open cradle. Keep the bare sockets and links out of the parked cover; only the local TPU heads flex through their intended key openings.
6. Close each cover through its final closure travel of 6.25 mm in the local outward coordinate (6.291 mm along world X at the clocked outer stations). Its stop fingers enter the open cradle, the TPU heads catch the outside plate and the paired guide surfaces close around the diagonal link. Fit both shared M3×12 screws with their washers and seat the dry pieces on their wall and tray hard lands. Check all four key heads, the socket supports and both lock seats before joining the enclosure.
7. Prepare the complete pumps and stems, close the cartridge cap and check all four tips against the calibrated cartridge-back gauge. Assemble the pogo contacts, set the magnets and complete both cures before loading the dock.
8. Join the front-top to the lower enclosure after its plumbing and fixtures are fitted. Check simultaneous cartridge seating, straight withdrawal and fixed-body retention, then follow [assembly acceptance](../../../assembly/acceptance-and-burn-in.md) and the [customer pump procedure](../../../service/pump-replacement.md).

The eight shared fixture locks use the [Wiha 26305 geometry](https://wiha.com/tools/screwdrivers/mechanic-s-screwdrivers/softfinish/hexagon-socket/screwdriver-softfinish/26305): a 2.5 mm ball-end blade, 100 mm visible length and Ø23 mm handle, 204 mm overall. Fore locks approach from aft at 15 degrees downward. Aft locks approach from fore at 15 degrees upward, with 4.5 degrees outboard yaw on the outer stations and 5 degrees inboard yaw on the inner stations. The declared angles stay within the manufacturer's 25-degree ball-end allowance. The access record bounds the complete blade, handle and axial approach with the cartridge absent and the front-top loose. The four keeper screws use straight front access. Physical hand clearance and tightening torque remain separate from this geometry reading.

The return's temporary assembly stroke takes place before the front-top joins the compressor/condenser half. Removing a complete wet branch for factory work requires an isolated, demonstrated depressurized flavor circuit; it is not part of customer pump replacement.
