# Pump dock

The two pumps ride the pump cradle in front-top's bay, installed once, at the factory. Each pump's two straight LLDPE tubes pass front-top's tee wall into four purchased John Guest PP0208E tees. Each tee's branch collet stands extended and grips its tube like any push-fit, and those four collets hold the cradle home. The tees, the valves and the tubing stand fixed in front-top.

## Tees and tee wall

One PP0208E stands on each pump port: Y-C and Y-D on pump A, Y-F and Y-G on pump B, west to east Y-G, Y-F, Y-C, Y-D. Each tee's branch faces its pump on the pump tube's axis, [Z186.174](DOCK_ROW_Z); its run stands upright.

Front-top's bay bulkhead is the tee wall: one flat slab of PET-GF the bay's whole width and height, [15.821 mm](DOCK_WALL_T) thick from the bay face at [Y82.515](DOCK_BAY_FACE_Y) to its aft face at [Y98.336](DOCK_WALL_AFT_Y). Its fore band, the collet plate, [4.675 mm](DOCK_PLATE_T) thick, ends at the bore face, [Y87.19](DOCK_BORE_FACE_Y). Each tee has two teardropped cuts on its branch axis: a [Ø11.93 mm](DOCK_HOLE_D) hole through the collet plate from the bay face to the bore face, the extended branch collet's clearance and one running fit; and a [Ø17 mm](DOCK_BORE) bore from the bore face out through the wall's aft face, [11.15 mm](DOCK_BORE_DEPTH) long: the collar's [Ø16.5 mm](DOCK_COLLAR_ENVELOPE) envelope from the scan and [0.25 mm](DOCK_BORE_AIR) of running air. Between the two, the bore face is a flat annulus. The wall also carries the pump lead's bore and the display backing, and roots the fore valve tray.

Each tee stands with its fixed branch barrel [0.6 mm](DOCK_BARREL_AIR) aft of the bore face, its run axis at [Y108.04](DOCK_TEE_Y). Its branch collet stands extended in the collet plate's hole, its whole [1.5 mm](DOCK_STROKE) stroke fore of the bore face, and grips the pump tube; the tube bottoms at the tee's own stop and seals on the collet's O-ring. The collet reaches [2.1 mm](DOCK_COLLET_PROUD) past the fixed barrel, and the hole runs on to the bay face, so with the tee pushed fore onto its barrel the collet still stands clear of the wall and keeps its grip.

## What holds each tee

The bore holds the tee's branch in X and Z. Its fixed branch barrel stops it fore, landing on the bore face's annulus, and its lower return, anchored in the rear valve, stops it aft. The wall's aft face stands [1.454 mm](DOCK_BODY_AIR) clear of the tee's run barrel.

## Fore valves and upper tubes

V-E, V-F, V-H and V-I stand fixed on their tray posts directly over the tees, bottom collets at [Z222.675](DOCK_FORE_VALVE_Z). Each fore valve's axis is [Y108.04](DOCK_FORE_VALVE_Y), over its tee's run axis, so the short upper tube between them, [15.25 mm](DOCK_UPPER_TUBE) exposed, stands straight.

## Lower returns

Each lower return joins a tee's lower collet ([Z164.924](DOCK_LOWER_PORT_Z)) to its rear valve's bottom collet with two NeoFit ATEU44W stem elbows and one straight tube of the machine's black 1/4" LLDPE. The tee's elbow has its stem cut to the run collet's seat, so its shoulder stands on the collet face and its axes cross [6.05 mm](DOCK_ELBOW_DROP) under it, at [Z158.879](DOCK_RETURN_Z). All four rear valves' bottom collets stand at [Z168.675](DOCK_RETURN_VALVE_Z), and each valve's elbow keeps [3.75 mm](DOCK_STEM_SHOWING) of stem below its collet, so it crosses at the same height. Each elbow's socket opens toward the other and the tube runs level between them: [20.1 mm](DOCK_RETURN_TUBE) exposed on the inner two, [20.69 mm](DOCK_OUTER_RETURN_TUBE) on the outer two, which lean inboard to V-G and V-J at [X±73.82](DOCK_OUTER_RETURN_X). The elbows' lowest surface is [Z151.132](DOCK_RETURN_FLOOR), [5.13 mm](DOCK_CONDENSER_AIR) over the condenser's top at the east-outer station. The tube bends nowhere.

## The cradle and its tubes

Each pump carries two straight [33.2 mm](DOCK_PUMP_TUBE) tubes of the machine's black 1/4" LLDPE, pushed into its BPT tube ends in place of Kamoer's barbs. The pump head's own housing, tightened at the factory, clamps the tube around each one, and the cradle and its clamp hold the rigid head. The tubes pass the cradle's plain open tube passages and the collet plate's holes; their tips stand at [Y97.19](DOCK_TIP_Y), [14.925 mm](DOCK_TIP_FROM_BACK) behind the cradle's flat back at [Y82.265](DOCK_CRADLE_BACK), bottomed at the tees' stops. The tube setting jig sets each tip.

Home, the cradle's flat back stands [0.25 mm](DOCK_FRAME_GAP) off the bay face. The four gripping collets hold it there through the tubes and the head clamps; nothing else retains it. The collets cannot be released from the bay, so the cradle does not come back out: reaching a pump is a factory teardown.

## Factory assembly

The [internal plumbing procedure](../../../assembly/internal-plumbing.md) owns the complete sequence. Steps 1–3 run on loose front-top, with access from its interior and underside.

1. Seat the rear valves on their tray posts.
2. For each tee: push the upper tube into the tee's upper collet and its fore valve's bottom collet, with both fully seated. Bring the tee, tube and valve in together, the branch into its bore from the wall's aft face, and press the fore valve onto its tray posts.
3. Cut each elbow's stem square and chamfer its edge: the tee's elbow to the run collet's seat, the valve's elbow to the valve's seat and the stem it shows. From front-top's underside, push each tee's elbow up into its lower collet and each valve's elbow up into its rear valve's bottom collet, turn the two sockets to face each other, and push the return tube into both. Use actual insertion marks and tug-check every push-fit joint.
4. Join front-top to the lower enclosure after its plumbing and fixtures are fitted, and close the box with the funnel frame out.
5. Prepare the complete pumps and their tubes in the cradle, close the clamp, set the four tips against the tube setting jig and take the jig off.
6. Feed the J13 ribbon's free end through the tee wall's lead bore into the bay, crimp its four Faston receptacles and land them on the motor tabs, the +X leads on pump A and the −X leads on pump B. Dress the leads into the clamp's lead slot and crown grooves. Push the cradle straight home while drawing the slack back from behind through the funnel-frame opening: the four tips enter the collet plate's holes and bottom in the gripping collets. Plug the XHP-4 into J13.
7. Follow [assembly acceptance](../../../assembly/acceptance-and-burn-in.md).

| Tube setting jig, factory tool | PET-GF; `enclosure-pump-tube-setting-jig` |

The tee wall, its holes and its bores are front-top's own material, printed with front-top standing on its seam plane; every cut in the wall is teardropped, so its roof prints without support. The [PET-GF handoff](petgf-production-parts.md) specifies the jig's process.

The one factory tool is separate from the appliance's installed dock pieces: the [tube setting jig](../enclosure/enclosure-pump-tube-setting-jig.stl), which seats on the cradle's flat back and stops all four tube tips at their datum.

## Engineering evidence

The dock has no physical seal, retention, lifetime or cleaning acceptance. The force to push all four tubes home at once through their gripping collets is unmeasured, as are the tee's aft stop through its lower return, the pump head clamp's hold on each tube, the tees' running fit in their bores and the sealing of the assembled dock. The valve's seat depth, which sets its elbow's stem cut, is a layout estimate, and the stem elbows' seal with a cut stem is unqualified. Material data alone do not establish finished wet-part food-contact suitability.
