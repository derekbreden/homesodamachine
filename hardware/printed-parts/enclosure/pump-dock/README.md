# Pump dock

The customer withdraws the complete two-pump cartridge, changes the prepared pumps under its two-screw cap, and pushes the cartridge home. Each pump's two straight LLDPE tubes pass front-top's collet plate into four purchased John Guest PP0208E tees, whose branch collets the plate holds pressed, so the tubes slide in on the collets' O-rings and draw straight back out. No tee moves. The tees, the plate, the valves and the tubing stay in the machine; customer service requires only complete prepared replacement pumps.

## Tees and tee wall

One PP0208E stands on each pump port: Y-C (X22.35) and Y-D (X82.1) on pump A, Y-F (X-22.35) and Y-G (X-82.1) on pump B. Each tee's branch faces its pump on the pump tube's axis, [Z186.174](DOCK_ROW_Z); its run stands upright.

Front-top's bay bulkhead is the tee wall: one flat slab of PET-GF the bay's whole width and height, [15.821 mm](DOCK_WALL_T) thick from the bay face at [Y82.515](DOCK_BAY_FACE_Y), [0.25 mm](DOCK_FRAME_GAP) behind the cartridge's flat back, to its aft face at [Y98.336](DOCK_WALL_AFT_Y). Its fore [4.675 mm](DOCK_PLATE_T) is the collet plate, which ends at the release face, [Y87.19](DOCK_RELEASE_Y). Each tee has one teardropped [Ø17 mm](DOCK_BORE) bore from the release face out through the wall's aft face, [11.15 mm](DOCK_BORE_DEPTH) long: the collar's [Ø16.5 mm](DOCK_COLLAR_ENVELOPE) envelope from the scan and [0.25 mm](DOCK_BORE_AIR) of running air. Ahead of each bore a teardropped [Ø8.5 mm](DOCK_HOLE_D) hole through the plate passes the tube and leaves the face the branch collet presses on. The wall carries the contact pair's seat and the display backing and roots the fore valve tray.

Each tee stands with its branch collet pushed home its whole [1.5 mm](DOCK_STROKE) stroke against the release face, its run axis at [Y108.04](DOCK_TEE_Y). A pressed collet's teeth do not bite, so the pump tube slides in through it to the tee's own stop, sealed by the collet's O-ring, and draws straight back out when the cartridge is pulled.

## What holds each tee

The bore holds the tee's branch in X and Z. The plate stops it fore, through its pressed collet, and its lower return, anchored in the rear valve, stops it aft. The wall's aft face stands [1.454 mm](DOCK_BODY_AIR) clear of the tee's run barrel.

## Fore valves and upper tubes

V-E, V-F, V-H and V-I stand fixed on their tray posts directly over the tees, bottom collets at [Z222.675](DOCK_FORE_VALVE_Z). Each fore valve's axis is [Y108.04](DOCK_FORE_VALVE_Y), over its tee's run axis, so the short upper tube between them, [15.25 mm](DOCK_UPPER_TUBE) exposed, stands straight.

## Lower returns

Each lower return joins a tee's lower collet ([Z164.924](DOCK_LOWER_PORT_Z)) to its rear valve's bottom collet with two NeoFit ATEU44W stem elbows and one straight tube of the machine's black 1/4" LLDPE. The tee's elbow has its stem cut to the run collet's seat, so its shoulder stands on the collet face and its axes cross [6.05 mm](DOCK_ELBOW_DROP) under it, at [Z158.879](DOCK_RETURN_Z). All four rear valves' bottom collets stand at [Z168.675](DOCK_RETURN_VALVE_Z), and each valve's elbow keeps [3.75 mm](DOCK_STEM_SHOWING) of stem below its collet, so it crosses at the same height. Each elbow's socket opens toward the other and the tube runs level between them: [20.1 mm](DOCK_RETURN_TUBE) exposed on the inner two, [20.69 mm](DOCK_OUTER_RETURN_TUBE) on the outer two, which lean inboard to V-G and V-J at [X±73.82](DOCK_OUTER_RETURN_X). The elbows' lowest surface is [Z151.132](DOCK_RETURN_FLOOR), [5.13 mm](DOCK_CONDENSER_AIR) over the condenser's top at the east-outer station. The tube bends nowhere.

## Cartridge and magnets

Each prepared pump carries two straight [33.2 mm](DOCK_PUMP_TUBE) tubes of the machine's black 1/4" LLDPE, pushed into its BPT tube ends in place of Kamoer's barbs. The pump head's own housing, tightened at the factory, clamps the tube around each one. Docking and withdrawal friction pass from each tube through that clamp into the rigid head, which the cradle and cap hold. The tubes pass the cradle's plain open tube passages and the collet plate; their tips stand at [Y97.19](DOCK_TIP_Y), [14.925 mm](DOCK_TIP_FROM_BACK) behind the cartridge's [Y82.265](DOCK_CARTRIDGE_BACK) back datum, bottomed in the tees. The [pump procedure](../../../service/pump-replacement.md#factory-preparation-of-replacement-pumps) sets each tip against the tube setting jig.

[4](DOCK_MAGNET_PAIRS) SB443-IN pairs seat the cartridge: one column of [2](DOCK_MAGNET_ROWS) attracting pairs on each pump's own load line, at [X±52.225](DOCK_MAGNET_X) centred on the tube row. Front-top's two slots are cut into the tee wall from the bay face; the cartridge's are in its flat back. The meeting poles set the cartridge's final position with [0.25 mm](DOCK_FRAME_GAP) of air between the broad faces. No collet grips a pump tube, so the magnets hold the cartridge, and its four tubes, in against line pressure. The [magnet design](magnet-retention.md) defines polarity, slots and loading. Follow the [contact mounting audit](../../../reference/yyfkgcp-pogo-4p/mounting-audit.md) for the contact pair's assembly and compression.

## Factory assembly

Keep front-top loose, with access from its interior and underside. The [internal plumbing procedure](../../../assembly/internal-plumbing.md) owns the complete sequence.

1. Seat the rear valves on their tray posts.
2. For each tee: push the upper tube into the tee's upper collet and its fore valve's bottom collet, with both fully seated. Bring the tee, tube and valve in together, the branch into its bore from the wall's aft face until the collet bottoms against the plate, and press the fore valve onto its tray posts.
3. Cut each elbow's stem square and chamfer its edge: the tee's elbow to the run collet's seat, the valve's elbow to the valve's seat and the stem it shows. From front-top's underside, push each tee's elbow up into its lower collet and each valve's elbow up into its rear valve's bottom collet, turn the two sockets to face each other, and push the return tube into both. Use actual insertion marks and tug-check every push-fit joint.
4. Prepare the complete pumps and their tubes, close the cartridge cap and set the four tips against the tube setting jig. Assemble the pogo contacts before loading the dock; load the SB443-IN blocks at each printed part's pause.
5. Join front-top to the lower enclosure after its plumbing and fixtures are fitted. Follow [assembly acceptance](../../../assembly/acceptance-and-burn-in.md) and the [customer pump procedure](../../../service/pump-replacement.md).

The tee wall, its collet plate and the magnet slots are front-top's own material, printed with front-top standing on its seam plane; every bore and hole through the wall is teardropped, so its roof prints without support. The [PET-GF handoff](petgf-production-parts.md) specifies the jig's process.

The one factory tool is separate from the appliance's installed dock pieces: the [tube setting jig](../enclosure/enclosure-pump-tube-setting-jig.stl), which seats on the cartridge back and stops all four tube tips at their datum.

## Engineering evidence

The dock has no physical seal, retention, lifetime or cleaning acceptance. With the collets held pressed, nothing but the cartridge's magnets holds the pump tubes in against pressure; the magnets' margin against the line pressure on four tubes is unmeasured. The O-ring friction of four tubes through pressed collets, the complete withdrawal and insertion force, the tees' running fit in their bores and the prepared pump's tube clamp under docking load are unmeasured. The valve's seat depth, which sets its elbow's stem cut, is a layout estimate, and the stem elbows' seal with a cut stem is unqualified. Material data alone do not establish finished wet-part food-contact suitability.

Customer release requires a demonstrated depressurization route for a failed or stalled pump. A timed command or accepted PWM does not establish rotation, flow or that both ports have vented. The customer procedure remains conditional on the qualified depressurized service state.
