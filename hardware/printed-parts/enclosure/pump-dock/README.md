# Permanent pump dock

The customer withdraws the complete two-pump cartridge, changes the prepared pumps under its two-screw cap, and pushes the cartridge home. Four short straight LLDPE stubs, clamped in the pump heads, enter permanent TPU junctions together. The junctions, returns, straight links, keepers, return block and magnets remain in the machine. Customer service requires only complete prepared replacement pumps.

## Fluid path and support

Each asymmetric junction has a pump-facing funnel, an upward outlet and a lower outlet angled 45 degrees aft. Its front seal is a continuous 6 mm long Ø6.10 mm TPU land around the smooth Ø6.35 mm pump stub. The upper outlet connects to its fore valve with a straight LLDPE link. The lower outlet connects through a straight diagonal link, a fixed compound TPU return and a short straight valve tail. The compound return turns 45 degrees into its cross-run and 90 degrees upward; the LLDPE supplies straight connections. Two purchased PP0208E tees remain in the source crossbar.

The closed PET-GF wall locates each junction in X/Z. The front funnel folds through its wall bore during factory assembly. A flat 3 mm keeper slides down the interior guides and captures Y. Its fore face is 0.15 mm behind the wall plane; a broad raised land supports that face when tightened. The U slot has 0.35 mm radial clearance around the relaxed TPU neck, and the groove has 0.20 mm room behind the keeper. The keeper has 0.25 mm fore room in its temporary insertion pose. One front-driven M3×8 screw and Ø7 × 0.5 mm washer lock each keeper. The RX-M3S×4.0 insert sits in its 5 mm thick rear boss, with 3 mm radial PET-GF stock around the insert knurl. A 3.75 mm deep crowned wall recess puts the screw head 0.25 mm behind the pump-bay face and leaves 3.25 mm of washer backing. The nominal screw reaches 4.10 mm past the insert entry, fully engaging its 4 mm body, and ends 0.90 mm before the boss back. Fit the insert before sliding the keeper into place. The keeper retains its 20 mm upward removal route.

Each junction is one solid TPU body behind its rear collar: flat-sided TPU fills the space between the two branches, from the collar to the upper socket's stop height and back to the upper cuff's aft tangent, down to the lower mouth plane. The upper socket's separating load runs through that fill into the body, the wall and the keeper. The lower socket's load line passes within a few millimetres of the branch root. Both sealing cuffs stay round beyond their stops.

The four compound returns snap into one PET-GF return block under the front-top's seam. Each return's channel is a straight slot through the block, 0.20 mm per side narrower than the relaxed TPU body, and a round seat along the return's own path with 0.15 mm of air round its occupied body. The seat cuts about 0.4 mm into each slot wall. The TPU squeezes through the slot as the block comes up under the returns and springs out into its seat, which then holds it from above and below. A tube's pressure pushes each return away from that tube, down off the valve tail and down-aft off the diagonal link, into the seat's lower half. The diagonal link is captive between the junction's socket stop and the return's: a straight tube cannot withdraw from one socket without meeting the opposite fixed stop. Each socket contains 6 mm of circular seal land and a 0.5 mm entry. TPU relaxation and the assembled permanent seal have no lifetime result.

The block's top face is the front-top's own print bed plane. It bears on the aft valve tray's foot, where three M3×12 screws drive straight up through counterbores into short inserts in bosses on that foot, at the centreline and 48 mm either side. Its front face stands aft of the aft coils' spade receptacles. Its underside is a millimetre above the bottom of the lowest seat in each region: deep under the inner pair over the compressor, raised at the outer stations over the condenser bay. Each slot runs out through the front face and the underside and ends behind its outlet, so the block's back carries all four channels' walls and the three screws. Round each inner outlet a 45-degree countersink keeps the valve collet's air.

The nominal diagonal cuts are 46.132 mm at the inner stations and 33.019 mm at the outer stations. Actual mounted stop spacing governs factory preparation. For a short valve tail, seat the tube fully in the actual Beduan valve, mark its mouth and cut 8.5 mm beyond that mark: 2 mm exposed plus 6.5 mm in the TPU socket. The CAD insertion depth is not a measurement of the received collet.

The enclosure's rear inner boundary is Y468.3 mm. The four new upper links and eight links around the returns are straight. The two retained formed source links, fluid-3 and fluid-5, have their own R14 geometry; this pump-link design does not qualify those source bends.

## Cartridge and magnets

Each prepared pump carries two straight 26 mm stubs of the machine's black 1/4" LLDPE, pushed into its BPT tube ends in place of Kamoer's barbs. The pump head's own housing, tightened at the factory, clamps the tube around each stub. Docking and withdrawal friction pass from each stub through that clamp into the rigid head, which the cradle and cap hold; the flexible tube outside the clamp carries no axial load as a free column. Each stub seals on its own 6.35 mm OD at both ends, so pressure inside it nearly balances along its length. The stubs pass the cradle's plain open tube passages. The four tips end at Y90.015 mm, 7.750 mm behind the cartridge's Y82.265 mm back datum: across the 0.250 mm body air, the junction's 1 mm entry and 6 mm seal land, and 0.5 mm beyond it. The [pump procedure](../../../service/pump-replacement.md#factory-preparation-of-replacement-pumps) sets each tip against the tube setting jig.

Four 4 × 12 mm rigid lands establish the cartridge's closed pose. The broad body faces retain 0.250 mm clearance. Each pump's own load line carries one column of four SB443-IN attracting pairs, bonded in one plain pocket in each part. Each fixed block bottoms in a pocket as deep as the thinnest block K&J's drawing allows, so it stands flush with the wall face or up to 0.20 mm proud of it. The moving blocks are bonded against those poles with the cartridge seated on its lands, and DP420 Off-White fills each pocket behind the front bands. At K&J's 3.42 lbf steel rating each column carries 60.9 N against the 52.4 N screen for its pump's 80 psi blocked fill. The [magnet process](magnet-retention.md) defines polarity, bonding and the two sequential 24-hour cures. Pull force to steel is not an installed retention rating. The electrical faces are recessed 0.125 mm each, giving 0.500 mm nominal face separation independently of the body gap. Follow the [contact mounting audit](../../../reference/yyfkgcp-pogo-4p/mounting-audit.md) for actual assembly and compression.

## Factory assembly

Keep the front-top loose and inverted, with access from underneath. Fit and finish its inserts and rigid seats before introducing wet parts. The [internal plumbing procedure](../../../assembly/internal-plumbing.md) owns the complete sequence.

1. Fit the aft valves and their coils to the loose front-top. Prepare the short valve tails from their actual seated mouth marks.
2. Prepare each fore valve, straight upper link and junction as a connected group. Move that group toward the pump side in −Y. The fore valve enters its mounting posts while the junction funnel folds through the closed wall. Seat the junction collar without twisting the seal bore.
3. Slide each keeper down its guides and fit its washer-backed M3×8 screw from the pump bay. Tighten against the raised wall land. This front access remains available with both valve rows seated and the cartridge absent.
4. Seat each diagonal link in its junction. Push each return onto its link and then onto its valve tail until both tubes stop. The four returns now hang on their tubes below the seam plane.
5. Bring the return block onto all four returns at once, top face first, until it bears on the tray foot: each return squeezes through its slot and snaps into its seat. Drive the three M3×12 screws up through the block into the tray-foot inserts.
6. Prepare the complete pumps and their stubs, close the cartridge cap and set all four tips against the tube setting jig. Assemble the pogo contacts, bond the magnets and complete both cures before loading the dock.
7. Join the front-top to the lower enclosure after its plumbing and fixtures are fitted. Check simultaneous cartridge seating, straight withdrawal and fixed-body retention, then follow [assembly acceptance](../../../assembly/acceptance-and-burn-in.md) and the [customer pump procedure](../../../service/pump-replacement.md).

Removing a complete wet branch for factory work requires an isolated, demonstrated depressurized flavor circuit; it is not part of customer pump replacement.

Every stem has STEP and STL outputs. These manufacturing outputs already contain the print pose. Junctions stand on their lower-mouth plane with the corresponding yaw and tilt; the fill between their branches has its aft face at 45 degrees in that pose and its foot on the bed. Returns stand on the flat foot of a teardrop heel on the outside of their last turn; in place, that heel stops level with the return's own underside. Each part's one turn that runs flat in its pose carries a teardrop passage roof, swept with its point up, while the seal lands stay circular; a return's roof ends 3 mm below its outlet's tube stop. Keepers print flat. The return block prints on its top face without support: its slot walls stand vertical and each seat's arcs stay within 22 degrees of vertical.

The PET-GF preparation uses the shared [petgf.3mf](../../petgf.3mf) and [localized cooling policy](../../cooling/README.md): ordinary and auxiliary cooling 0%, qualifying bridge/overhang cooling 70% at the 50% unsupported-width threshold, fan-always-on off and pre-start 0. Keep the 0.20 mm first layer / 0.24 mm body recipe. The keepers and the block select six walls and 100% fill as object overrides. The [PET-GF handoff](petgf-production-parts.md) prepares all five parts on one plate.

Prepare each wet part as one object per plate and review the actual emitted walls, bore crossings and attachment paths. The [archived four-straight-receiver preparation](print-preparation/2026-10-18-receivers-siraya85a-mark1/README.md) is scoped to its saved meshes and vertical-bore four-object plate. The [eight one-object native projects](print-preparation/2026-10-18-asymmetric-wet-siraya85a-mark1/README.md) retain the Siraya recipe in the actual handed print poses; these estimates and geometric readings do not establish physical yield, clean internal surfaces or service life.

## Engineering evidence

The [fixed contact-stock review](pogo-fixed-stock-review.json) covers the high contact backing, its valve clearance and pilot stock. The [pressure and retention review](pressure-retention-review.json) preserves the actual single-channel paths, reachable blocked-fill case, compound comparisons and isolated qualification procedure: at its 80 psi blocked-fill screen each port separates with 17.5 N. These are engineering evidence, not a system pressure rating.

The current dock has no physical seal, lifetime, cleaning or snap acceptance. The complete frame still needs measured simultaneous seal seating, extraction effort, hydraulic retention and permanent-part aging under the actual fluid, temperature and cleaning exposure. The short factory assembly and geometry readings establish none of those properties. The prepared pump's stub clamp also requires an actual free-rotation, pumping and stub pull-out result. Material data alone do not establish finished wet-part food-contact suitability.

Customer release requires a demonstrated depressurization route for a failed or stalled pump. A timed command or accepted PWM does not establish rotation, flow or that both ports have vented. The customer procedure remains conditional on the qualified depressurized service state.
