# Purchased elbows beneath the rear valves

Four purchased [John Guest PP0308E elbows](../../../../reference/jg-pp0308e-elbow/README.md) stand upright directly beneath V-C, V-D, V-G and V-J. A short straight tube joins each elbow to its valve, with a nominal 1 mm exposed gap. The forward leg faces the permanent wall junction. One shallow R28 bend in black 1/4-inch LLDPE connects the junction's diagonal outlet to that leg. It turns 50 degrees at the inner stations and about 49.7 degrees at the outer stations; the purchased elbow supplies the final 90-degree turn into the valve.

![Upright purchased elbow beneath the rear valve](elbow-layout.png)

The rear valve row and source crossbar stand 8 mm above their deck reference. This clears the west inner elbow from the compressor and the east outer elbow from the condenser; the latter has about 2.45 mm geometric clearance. Round Ø17.4 mm openings through the rear tray foot taper into the valve channels. The tray crown meets the adjacent connector pocket's existing floor on one plane. The [surface-join reading](surface-joins.json) records the reconciled faces. V-A and V-B retain their cold-core seats. The reservoir-B draw tube follows a western approach clear of the raised source bends, retaining R14 bends.

Each TPU junction has one rounded Ø4.3 mm common gallery and two short coaxial outlet throats. Its lower outlet aims 50 degrees down and aft, with a small inward clock at the outer stations. Six-millimetre circular sealing lands and their annular tube stops remain. The wall and three gravity-seated keepers locate the junctions; the straight upper links clock them.
