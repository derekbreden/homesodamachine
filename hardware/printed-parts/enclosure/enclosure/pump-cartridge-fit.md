# Pump cradle and cap

The pump cradle and raised cap are parts of the complete enclosure trial. The complete
assembly and its physical observations are indexed in
[print readiness](../print-readiness.md). The J13 ribbon reaches the motor tabs through the
cap's lead slot and crown grooves ([the pump lead](README.md#the-pump-lead)).

The cap has a broad underside, fitted octagonal boss openings and Ø37 motor bores.
Derek confirms that the assembled holder retains both pumps firmly with no vertical
play. The [physical-fit record](/hardware/reference/kamoer-kphm400/physical-fit.json)
and [fitted-well comparison](/hardware/reference/kamoer-kphm400/fitted-well-audit/README.md)
identify that accepted fit. The surrounding crown reaches the cradle top at
Z284.174 mm. Above each motor bore a 45° cone opens through the crown and leaves the
motor end open. Two M3×60 screws seat in counterbores accessible from above.

The cradle uses the relieved enclosure floor. The cap and cradle share an
aft face at Y82.265 mm, with a complete 3 mm skirt band and 0.250 mm body clearance
to the tee wall. The four prepared pump tubes are clamped in the pump
heads and pass the lower cradle's open tube passages and the collet plate's holes. Their
tips stand at Y97.19 mm, 14.925 mm behind the cradle's back, bottomed in the
PP0208E tees, whose extended branch collets grip them and hold the cradle home
([pump dock](../pump-dock/README.md)).

The [fitted-well audit](/hardware/reference/kamoer-kphm400/fitted-well-audit/README.md)
records its sampled 0.004 mm match to the retained printed input and its cap,
screw-access, front-rim and outlet-axis scope. It does not extend the accepted
holder fit to the complete fluid dock.

## Print and assembly

Each reviewed two-part archive, with the printer, settings and geometry hashes it binds,
is listed in the [slice reviews](../tee-readiness/full-enclosure-print/native-slice-reviews/README.md).
These archives qualify their identified meshes, not the current cradle and cap.
Both parts use black PET-GF on the left 0.4 mm nozzle. The cradle stands on its flat
underside; the cap prints crown-down.

The [support audit](pump-support-audit.md) names the support bodies of its printed pair and
their removal lanes. Remove supports before installing pumps or screws. The complete enclosure
trial checks the raised crown with actual terminal connectors, pump seating, marked
tube tip positions, tube clamping and simultaneous insertion into the four tees' gripping
collets. Physical support-removal effort and operation remain observations of that trial.
