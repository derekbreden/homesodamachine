# Integral pump-mount fit

Front-top contains the fitted upper pump pockets in broad stock continuous into the
display housing and roof. The rectangular lower retainer carries the fitted head wells
and skirt lands. Two integral, full-depth wings engage front-top’s flank receivers and
retain the molded-skirt capture between these parts. The [pump dock](../pump-dock/README.md)
defines their dimensions, screwdriver release and factory assembly order.

The prepared pumps enter motor-first from underneath loose front-top. The four tube
stubs rise through bottom-open gates. After the retainer is secured, each prepared tee,
upper tube and fore valve approaches its fixed pump tube individually from aft. The
tee's branch collet grips and seals that tube. Pump and display leads use the shared
rear passage.

## Current geometry and print evidence

The [pump-snap review](pump-snap-review/README.md) identifies the snap-wing retainer and
receiver inputs and its geometric scope. Both wings retain their upper roots and lower
hooks. The carrier, wing and hook use the retainer’s full Y span, with 0.25 mm running
clearance at either receiver end. One central opening per side admits a small flathead
screwdriver; its roof continues as one solid 45° corbel.

The [three-screw mount receipts](pump-mount-review/README.md) bind their listed front-top,
retainer, rigid paths and native slices. Their screw-access and dense-host results do not
qualify the snap wings, receiver floors or release openings. Production orientation places
front-top on its seam plane and the lower retainer on its underside, both in black PET-GF
on the left 0.4 mm nozzle.

The nominal face allowances and spring displacements are manufacturing dimensions.
Physical support removal, complete seating, axial play, insertion and release forces,
retention, vibration and lifetime of the snap-fit mount remain unqualified. The individual
tube joints also require their own insertion, sealing and retention evidence.
