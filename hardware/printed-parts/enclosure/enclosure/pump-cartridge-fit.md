# Integral pump-mount fit

Front-top contains the fitted upper pump pockets in broad stock continuous into the
display housing and roof. The rectangular lower retainer carries the fitted head wells
and skirt lands. Three upward M3 × 60 screws capture the molded pump skirts between
these parts. The [pump dock](../pump-dock/README.md) defines their dimensions and factory
assembly order.

The prepared pumps enter motor-first from underneath loose front-top. The four tube
stubs rise through bottom-open gates. After the retainer is secured, each prepared tee,
upper tube and fore valve approaches its fixed pump tube individually from aft. The
tee's branch collet grips and seals that tube. Pump and display leads use the shared
rear passage.

## Current geometry and print evidence

The [native pump-mount reading](pump-mount-review/README.md) identifies the current
front-top and retainer inputs, complete rigid loading and closing paths, screw access,
skirt-bearing geometry and nominal closing travel. Its
[print review](pump-mount-review/print-review.json) binds the matching native slices,
local dense screw hosts and support-body access. Front-top prints on its seam plane;
the lower retainer prints on its underside, both in black PET-GF on the left 0.4 mm nozzle.

The nominal supported-face allowance and closing travel are manufacturing dimensions.
Physical support removal, complete seating, axial play, retention, vibration and lifetime
of this integral mount remain unqualified. The individual tube joints also require
their own insertion, sealing and retention evidence.
