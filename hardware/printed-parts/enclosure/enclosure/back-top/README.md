# Back-top receivers and print supports

Back-top prints ceiling-down, with the rear flutes clear of the bed. Keep that layer
direction for receiver coupons. Use the production layer schedule and the specified
fine bands on [show rounds](../README.md#print-down-show-rounds).

## Ceiling and catches

The common ceiling recess over the ASSE and Pisco reaches both flanks and the rear
wall. The panel relief runs from the rim to the recess at the face of the core's
pocket. The bulkhead nuts and the Pisco shoulder seat on that relief. The recess
goes around the row of connector wells. Root the wells, rear receivers, corner
columns, power-supply bosses and inlet tunnel on their actual ceiling face. The
[rear wall](rear.rs) and [receiver seats](supports.rs) take those planes from the shell.

Each rear catch has a solid 45° face. As the hooks return, the two flat crests
on each cover meet their pocket roof or the meter’s east wall. The ASSE and
water roofs have local recesses for their measured seating travel. Their
outboard clearance lets the crests sweep past the roof skin while releasing
the hooks. The Pisco wings stop ahead of the rear corner column.
[Measured rear cap fits](../../rear-equipment-caps/production-fit.json)

The meter’s upper catch ends at the Pisco receiver’s fore wall. Both
sideways channels return to the flank on bevels. The aft boss-channel crown
grows from a permanent 45° slope with a taper at the upper spring’s end.
The Pisco clears the shoulder as it tips in. Support the fore shoulder, upper
lip, boss channels, screw-head channels and wire chamber through the open
west side. Leave the two catch pockets clear. [Backed shoulder specimen](../../../../print-tests/rear-receiver-quick/README.md)

## Removable supports and open passages

- Use manual Tree Slim on exposed joint ends, funnel receivers, the valve seat
  and initial boss curves. Derive the contact bands from the full stock and its
  exposed undersides. Raked joint ends and counterbore roofs take support above the
  self-supporting bed chamfer. The fill tube seat's upper wing prints on breakaway
  strips rooted on its corbelled lower wing. The cable hooks' beak lands print
  without support, as on front-bottom. [Removable stock](print_supports.rs)
- The nameplate receiver's crown and lower mouth edge grow from the rear wall on
  corbels. The lower rear edge of the nameplate follows the mouth slope. Each rail cap
  covers the bearing from its stop to its rear end, splits at the vent passage and
  starts on narrow roots on its own cavity floor. Sever the necks and pull the cap
  inward through the open sliding lane.
- Support the C14 flange pocket and cord passage. The inlet's blind pilots and the
  funnel insert pilots bridge between their own walls inside the supported stock.
  The connector insertion pockets bridge between their well walls. Keep all these
  bridges clear of support. Covered bulkhead bores and horizontal valve sockets use
  teardrops pointing in the build direction. Build-axis valve sockets stay circular
  and open. The PRV cap has a rooted chamfer and end-wall backing. Its short mouth lip
  bridges between the side walls, leaving the vent passage clear.

## Reading the slice

Native construction exports support exclusions bound to the STL. Preparation carries
them through orientation and placement as blockers and painted surfaces. Read the
first courses on each exposed underside, the support or stock they land on, and both
landings of each bridge. Count ordinary support beads as well as labeled interfaces
when checking that a passage stays clear. Back-top allows roots on both the part and
the bed. Check bead margins and the actual removal openings to the roots and contacts.
