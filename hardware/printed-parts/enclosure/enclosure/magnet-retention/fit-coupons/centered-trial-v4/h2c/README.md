# H2C centered fit samples

This plate contains nine open RC62 friction-fit pockets (C1, C2, C3, C4, D1, D2, D3, D4, C0) and three valve-socket panels (V70, V69, V68). The upright source orientations and printed identifiers are retained. C0 and V70 appear on both printers as comparison references.

The minimum emitted model-bead clearance from every bed edge is 86.70 mm. The twelve objects occupy a centered footprint of approximately 152 × 98 mm. Every second-layer bead overlaps the first layer by at least 65.77%; outer-wall overlap is at least 99.31%. These native checks do not establish visual bed adhesion.

The plate uses the fixed left hardened standard-flow 0.4 mm nozzle, black PET-GF labelled PET-CF/GFT01 on external left slot 254, Textured PEI, and 0.18 mm user trim (0.16 mm emitted). Layers are 0.20 mm first and 0.24 mm above, with two walls, 15% infill, 0.9555 flow and zero XY compensation. The objects have no supports, pauses, modifiers or other full parts. Native estimate: 1 h 38 min 32 s, 51.19 g.
