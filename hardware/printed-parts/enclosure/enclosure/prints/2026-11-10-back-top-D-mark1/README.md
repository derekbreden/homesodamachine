# Back-top with Support D on Mark1

This package contains the current full enclosure back-top with the shared PET-GF
contact and bridge recipe. The ceiling-down pose, manual Tree Slim targets,
authored meter shoulder supports and inaccessible-interior exclusions retain
their production treatment. Solid rail and fastener regions remain local.

The [print record](print-job.json) binds the geometry, recipe, native estimate,
archive and launch. The [native review](review.json) and
[deposited C14 bead review](c14-contact-review.json) establish the commanded
paths; [print-review.json](print-review.json) records their resolved result.

The [active launch](restart-launch.json) binds the running Mark1 job to the
reviewed archive. [Resume readiness](resume-readiness.json) records the completed
peer conversations and unchanged print inputs. The initial cancellation is
retained in [the stop record](stopped-printer.json).

The editable native project and reviewed machine archive are retained here.
The print-only archive preserves the same G-code and is the submitted package.
Physical full-part finish, support removal and fit remain untested.
