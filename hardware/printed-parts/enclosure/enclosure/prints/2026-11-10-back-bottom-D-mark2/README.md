# Back-bottom with Support D on Mark2

This package binds the full enclosure back-bottom to its reviewed native slice
and [accepted launch](launch.json). The shared PET-GF contact recipe applies to
the native trees. Local targets cover the handhold mouths and exposed seam lips;
the inclined gripping interiors remain outside the forced regions.

The [native review](review.json) checks the deposited paths and bed clearance.
The [support audit](support-audit-1901.json) locates the rail trees on the cavity
floors. Their Default style and constrained branches retain that local rooting
and the open sliding lane. The [print record](print-job.json) owns the estimate,
calibration, archive hashes and physical qualification status.

The editable project and native machine archive are retained here.
The [submitted archive copy](archive-copy.json) preserves the reviewed G-code.
Full-part finish, support removal and fit await physical results. D's successful
flat rail result does not qualify inclined faucet contacts; those have a
[separate comparison study](../../../../../print-tests/petgf-dense-roofs/inclines/).
