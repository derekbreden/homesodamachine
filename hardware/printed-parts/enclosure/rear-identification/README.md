# Rear identification

Six 42 × 38 × 1.8 mm colour tiles identify the selected two-column, three-row
rear grid. TAP and OVER use white, SODA blue, CO2 red and both FLAVOR ports
black, with contrasting raised lettering. The tiles' centres follow rows
Z331.2, Z294.2 and Z264; their bores follow the actual fitting axes. TAP's bore
therefore includes the scanned adapter's offset from the nominal grid.

Each tile slides in a continuous side-rail channel at Y469.3–471.1, retaining
4 mm of wall before the Y465.3 nut plane and 0.2 mm below the Y471.3 flange.
Each side rail overlaps 2 mm of the tile, exposing a 38 × 38 mm color face.
The channel floor is Z240.6. The upper tiles finish at Z355. Each lower tile
ends 0.2 mm below the next tile, with the words above the upper two fitting rows and below the bottom row. The rails retain 3 mm of fore–aft stock at
Y471.3–474.3, with 3 mm outer roots beside the tile channels. Their complete
sections remain plain through exterior fluting. Print face up with the native Y0 back on
the bed. The raised word uses the shared two-colour label process and two
0.24 mm layers above the face. The accepted TAP/FLAVOR
[appearance record](../tee-readiness/full-enclosure-print/native-slice-reviews/2026-09-30-bulkhead-raised-mark2-v2/physical-result.json)
retains its recorded material and label-interface scope.

The [accepted flat-wing nameplate](../nameplate/README.md) sits at
world X0/Z200 with its face at Y473.5. Its broad plain field stands 2.2 mm
above the rear wall. The full 3.6 mm receiver backing begins at Y466.39,
clear of the cold-core shell. Both accepted retaining lips and
their print smooth lands remain complete. The plate takes no screws.

[`rear_identification.py`](rear_identification.py) provides installed parts,
shell recesses/receiver and print smooth lands from one source. Its main
emits the six compact two-colour chip STEP/STL pairs. The nameplate uses the
existing unit-0001 part. The [native review](native-review.json) records zero
contact with C14, RJ11, all six bulkheads and their nuts. It also records
the cap profiles, roof stock, meter loading clearance and rear tube routes.
These are CAD clearances. The installed wire review checks the current conductors against the receiver region. The accepted receiver-coupon fit retains its own scope, and
physical fit in this enclosure is unmeasured.
