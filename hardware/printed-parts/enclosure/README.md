# Enclosure

The appliance enclosure — outer dimensions, internal zoning, and what lives where. This README is the architectural orientation for the printed enclosure as a whole; panel-specific design lives in the sub-folders. The cabinet it installs into, and what is already in there, is [`/marketing/install-envelope.md`](/marketing/install-envelope.md).

This is the **thin** machine: tall and narrow, [215 mm](APPLIANCE_WIDTH) wide × [481 mm](APPLIANCE_DEPTH) deep × [400 mm](APPLIANCE_HEIGHT) tall. Two of those three are bounds rather than consequences, and the first one is the whole edition:

- **Width** is the cold core's SHORT axis. The foam shell is ~[283](FOAM_SHELL_X) × [181](FOAM_SHELL_Y) × [213.4](FOAM_SHELL_Z) mm, and the foam assembly is **yawed a quarter turn** so the 181 runs across the machine and the 283 runs front to back. One boss chain either side of the 181 is the whole interior width. Nothing else in the machine is as wide, so nothing else sets it.
- **Height** is stated at 400 mm — the silhouette the edition exists for. The contents live under it; the build fails if they cannot.
- **Depth** follows the pack: the core's long axis, plus whatever is packed in front of it.

What the yaw buys is vertical: a 400 mm column above and ahead of the core, where the width used to be spent sideways.

## Where the pack stands

**Zone A (back-bottom) is packed.** The cold core — foam shell plus its foam-cap stacks top and bottom — is yawed and seated in the back-bottom corner, flat on the floor slab (its bottom cap's lid is a plane and every cap screw is down in a counterbore, so nothing goes under it). The ±X walls stand one boss chain off it, which is what makes it flush at the sides. It no longer sets the BACK wall: the SeaFlo runs front to back over it and reaches past its rear face, so the pump is what the back wall stands off and the core sits a few millimetres forward of the rear lip. Geometry source: [`/hardware/printed-parts/cold-core/foam-shell/README.md`](/hardware/printed-parts/cold-core/foam-shell/README.md).

**The top of the box is packed.** A flat 45° facet chamfers the whole top-front arris, wall to wall, with the ESP32-S3 config display centred on it and flat 45° face either side. Immediately behind it, the flavor funnel takes the top wall's full width and reaches aft for the plan area its capacity needs — which puts it across the front↔back seam, both halves taking their share of the opening. Detail: [`enclosure/README.md`](/hardware/printed-parts/enclosure/enclosure/README.md), [`/hardware/printed-parts/zone-c/README.md`](/hardware/printed-parts/zone-c/README.md).

**Zone D (front, floor to z [311.5](ZONE_D_TOP)) is packed, stacked.** The compressor stands **upright** on the floor in its sheet-metal shroud — its own feet under it, the shroud's open face down — yawed a quarter so its single copper-bearing face reads +Y across the machine corridor at the core and its AC gland reads +X. The condenser block stands over it on its [151](CONDENSER_STANDING), airflow **across the machine**, exhaust +X — the block stands against the +X wall and vents through it, so the hot end leaves by the nearest face and what crosses the cabinet is the cool intake. Only its [56](CONDENSER_ACROSS) runs across, at the +X end, so a [125](CONDENSER_LANE) mm lane stays open at the −X end of the band. The [6](STACK_GAP) mm between the shroud's roof and the block is the whole gap and it is the minimum: nothing leaves that roof, so what holds the two apart is the front Z seam, which has nowhere else in this column to run. Behind the shroud is the **machine corridor**: the band the refrigerant loop turns in on its way to the core's front face, and the one the manifold's cross-machine lines will run along.

**The cold core's port column stands over Zone D.** Both bodies in the stratum are packed flat against the core's whole front face up to the shroud's roof at [154.5](SHROUD_ROOF), and both span both of the shell's lanes — so what keeps a port reachable is height, not lane. Five of the core's wall penetrations climb clear of that roof and cross on one x, [168](CORE_PORT_X), topping out at [190.2](CORE_PORT_TOP): each opens into the machine corridor with [47.5](CORE_PORT_FREE) mm ahead of it, cast at its own bore's Ø from its own face. That is the shell's doing rather than the pack's: a station's Z is where its line crosses the wall and not the height of the fitting it serves, so a line climbs its lane to a station the stratum leaves open instead of crossing where its own tank sits (`../cold-core/copper-plugs/copper_plugs.py`).

**The two refrigerant stations are the exception, and they are inside the stratum on purpose.** Each crosses a plane the core already shares with the body packed against it — the condenser's outlet on the east lane, the compressor shroud's suction on the west — so the pair is one point read twice and the copper between them is the length of the union rather than a run through the corridor. The core's other three leave by the TOP, up conduits through the cap onto Zone B's floor, and stand in that zone's reckoning rather than this one. One gate reaches part of the way into the corridor: `port-leads` casts each port's own bore forward for the stub and the tangent a turn off it takes, which is short of the corridor's full depth — so a body that moved back over the column but stayed clear of those leads would not be caught.

**Zone B (the service bay, foam-cap top at z [253.4](DECK_TOP) to the ceiling) is packed.** [140.6](DECK_HEIGHT) mm of column over the core's lid, in strata:

- **The electronics shelf is not posed by this tree.** The foam cap owns its five deck-mount stations and the yaw carries them — PSU, PCBA, AC hub, relay #1 and the ground stud each land on their own rectangle of boss columns, each module's yaw its own plus the cap's. Every station sits in the bay's **front third**.
- **The water deck takes the rear band, in two lanes.** The SeaFlo runs front to back in the east lane, over the column the core's seven wall ports climb: its motor axis is longer than the cap is wide, and longer than the clear cap the shelf leaves behind it, so it reaches past the core's rear face. Its base foot is a fraction of its footprint and the motor can overhangs it. West of it the fittings lane runs front to back too, the ASSE 1022 chain being longer than that lane is wide — inlet aft under its bulkhead, outlet forward at the split, vent weeping down its own column onto the drip basin, with V-K on its cradle, the split beneath V-K, and the flow regulator in the open air the three leave.
- **The rear port field is one row at z [358.2](PORT_ROW_Z)**, above the deck: tap-water bulkhead on the ASSE chain's own inlet column, C14 beside it, the umbilical's three clustered at the nut pitch at the east end. The row's floor clears the back column's Z-seam lip band, so every hole is cut in one piece; `_panel_bodies` measures the seam the box actually got and refuses a field inside it.

**The back column carries no open Z band under its content, so its seam runs through it.** The core's cap stack runs from the floor slab and the whole bay stands on its lid — the pump's crown alone at z [325.4](PUMP_CROWN) — so the column is solid to the bay's crown, and every band it leaves open stands higher than a [320](BED_Z) mm bed carries the piece beneath. A Z seam's lip is a one-wall ring inset from the cavity, and the pack stands one wall off the front and back walls and one boss chain off the sides, so that ring runs free at every height; the seam takes the bed's own band, nearest the box's half-height — [205.2](BACK_Z_SEAM), leaving [221.3](BACK_BOTTOM_H) mm under it. The cold core spans it, as it spans the front column's seam on the other side of the Y joint.

**Zone C is the valve manifold, and it stands in TWO places.** Its head column is in the front column, three trays stacked under the basin. The valve manifold is seven trays — four identical [two-valve](/hardware/printed-parts/valve-manifold/two-valve-tray/README.md) and three of the [one-seat plate](/hardware/printed-parts/valve-manifold/single-valve-tray/README.md) — and the **source pair** — V-A on tap water, V-B on the hopper — heads it. The column stands on the refrigeration stratum's roof and this pair rides at the top of it, coils crowning at [357.4](SRC_CROWN_Z). The basin overhead is a **ceiling**: [3](SRC_HEADROOM) mm is the minimum these coils hold off it, the allowance a compliant silicone part loaded with syrup wants rather than the pack's rigid-body clearance floor, and the column leaves [3.372](SRC_HEADROOM_LEFT) mm. That gap is measured against the funnel's **real surface**, not its bounding box, and the two are far apart — the basin's floor slopes up toward the front, so over these coils it stands well above the spout tip that sets the funnel's own zmin, and the spout is aft of the tray besides.

Its plan pose is derived rather than chosen: V-B gates a **gravity** drain, the one line in this machine that cannot be routed around an obstacle, so the tray's east seat hangs on the hopper spout's own column and the fall is [34.62](HOPPER_DROP) mm straight down to the collet's plane with nothing in it. The tray lies flat, both inlets aft and both outlets forward. Y-A stands ahead of the outlet collets on the pair's own port plane at [312.1](SRC_PORT_PLANE), as far forward as the front chain lets a body stand — the standoff its two legs turn their corners in. Ahead of the core's front face the tray leaves a [24](SRC_AFT_BAND) mm band, and both feeds turn in it — a depth set not by this pair but by the pump row two storeys down, where three lines run abreast in it: the suction leg turning west, the discharge leg turning east, and reservoir B's climb crossing on its way to the loft.

**The selects pair — V-C and V-D — stands in that tray's own column, packed up under its plate.** One stack pitch of [65.6](STACK_PITCH) mm: X and Y off the tray above, and in Z its coils stand [6](STACK_GAP_TRAY) mm under that tray's floor, which is the depth a valve's four corner posts stand in the sockets that hold it — so a valve lifts straight out of its seat with the stack made up. Its clocking is the source pair's turned round, both inlets forward and both outlets aft, and the band behind those outlet collets is the same [24](SRC_AFT_BAND) mm one. So Y-B is Y-A's twin one column east: each tee's run stands UP its own column through the stack pitch, their two branches face each other across the gap between them, and the crossbar on that line is where the merge meets the split — down to the selects' own port plane at [246.5](SEL_PORT_PLANE).

**The bag-A pair — V-E and V-F — takes the column's bottom seat, one more pitch down.** Its two valves are seated opposite ways round, because the bag is V-E's inlet and V-F's outlet: those two face forward at Y-E on the pair's own port plane at [180.9](BAG_PORT_PLANE), and V-E-O and V-F-I face aft at the pump row. **Y-E is the one junction that joins a pair on its own tray**, and what decides its pose is the room: ahead of this pair stands the pump row, and between the two is a strip [20.59](BAG_TEE_STRIP) mm deep. A tee stands ACROSS one on its own 13.72 mm diameter, both collet axes square to the strip and all three collets in one vertical plane, forward face at [80.35](BAG_TEE_FRONT_Y). Its RUN lies along the strip, and its two collets face outboard 40.13 mm apart against a seat pitch of 34.25 — so the run can never be fed from both seats' own columns, and one of the two valves joins on the BRANCH. That branch faces down, a down-facing collet is entered by a rising leg, and so the fitting stands over the plane it serves and both valve legs climb into it. Reservoir A rides the run's east end, so one line carries both the fill and the draw — [303.9](BAG_LINE_LEN) mm of it. It leaves the core's face low, at the east end of the port column, crosses the machine in the corridor under Zone D only as far as the lane east of the trays, and climbs there — the [15.38](TRAY_EAST_LANE) mm between the column's own plate edge and the condenser's intake face, which is the one corridor at this height running the machine's full depth. So it passes the column on the outside, comes up that lane to the fitting's own run plane and forward down it into the strip, and the [12.08](COLUMN_FLOOR) mm left under the bottom plate carries no line at all. Under that plate the column is out of floor: the next seat's plate would be in the roof.

**Zone C's second stand is the LOFT — the band between the water deck's crown and the ceiling, over the whole of Zone B.** The front column takes exactly one more body after its head column: a `fit.py search` over the whole of it, four orientations on one grid, returns 23 free poses of 20160, and only one of those places takes a Kamoer standing on end. **Both pumps** take it, standing side by side with all four barbs on the bag-A pair's own port plane, and the manifold's second stand goes aloft. The lane behind them is the **pump row**: both of pump B's lines run down it and both of channel A's tees stand in it, **Y-C** on the suction barb's column and **Y-D** on the discharge barb's, each pushed off the rim it would otherwise overrun — the front column's own lip on one side, the tray column's west face on the other, [32.41](PUMP_LANE_W) mm apart. A tee's run is a lane and its branch is the leg that leaves one, so both runs lie along that lane and both branches stand up: the whole row holds one level, and the only legs that climb are Y-C's fall from the selects pair and Y-D's storey-high reach to the nozzle gate. Which barb is the inlet is an assignment and not a property of a peristaltic head, and it is made here so the two band legs cross once rather than twice: the suction tee stands east because the bag's draw is on the tray's west seat.

Up there, the **bag-B pair** takes the loft's west lane, its four collets on the loft's own port plane at [267.7](LOFT_TRAY_PLANE), standing at the front of that lane clear of the funnel's aft skirt, its two reservoir mouths facing west; the **nozzle gates** stand [16](LOFT_TRAY_BAY_MM) mm behind it. The two pairs face each other collet for collet across that gap, so it is a **junction bay** — but no fitting stands in it: **Y-G** lies in the lane EAST of V-K's plate, on the corridor `fluid-27` already runs down to reach it, its run along that lane with one valve on each end and its branch standing up at the pump's high barb. What the bay carries is the runs that cross it. The nozzle gates are the only pair whose outlets leave the machine, which is why they are up here beside the rear panel's own flavor bulkheads: [350](NOZZLE_A_LEN) mm and [223.6](NOZZLE_B_LEN) mm of tube, each turning east on its own lane so neither stands where the other crosses. **Channel B's pump is not up here.** Both pumps stand upright in the front column, side by side on one lane and on one plane — same part, same native turn, motor down and barbs out the back, the second one's Y and Z its twin's faces and its X stepped so the two inner barbs of that row stand a tube's width and a floor apart — because that column is the only place in this machine with the height a KPHM400 on end wants, and what the second one needs across is its **motor's** 62.61 mm square rather than its box: the part is three stacked solids and only its bottom third is that wide. So **Y-F** stands alone on its own column [33.98](LOFT_TEE_INSET) mm inside the trays' east face — aloft of the stand rather than off its flank — branch reaching west at the bag pair's draw, and the two legs between it and the pump — fluid-21 in, fluid-22 out — are the runs that cross a storey and a half. They pass the head column on OPPOSITE sides, so neither owes the other any separation. The inlet takes the **collet band**, which is the column's own aft margin: `two_valve_tray` sizes each plate to the valve's saddle and the ports reach [9.5](COLLET_PROUD) mm past it, so all three storeys leave the same empty band over their plates and it runs the whole height. Nothing crosses it — everything turning off an aft collet does so in the aft band behind it — so fluid-21 falls the storey and a half in ONE leg, on the east plate edge's own column and clear of the east seat's valve, and only steps out of the band at the bottom to reach the barb. The outlet takes the **tray-east lane**, hugging the condenser's intake face, and CLIMBS the strip before it crosses, because the height it would otherwise cross east on is the height the refrigerant line leaves the condenser across this lane on. That leaves the lane carrying one 1/4" line at any height, which is what it is wide enough for. Five of the twenty-eight segments now cross between the manifold's two stands.

Reservoir B's line is reservoir A's opposite. A's leaves the core's front face low and spends its whole length getting up here. B's never leaves the core's crown: it climbs the +Y pour band **inside** the shell, potted, and comes out a conduit through the cap onto the lid in the patch of deck the pair's own west face leaves. So the run this machine carries for it is the fall off that conduit into the pair's inlet — [38.55](BAG_B_LINE_LEN) mm, one of the two shortest in the loft, the other being the fill falling the other way into the reservoir's own cap.

**What is still unpacked:** the CO2 chain that lands on the front panel. The panels carry no other through-holes either, including the two the crossing airflow needs: an intake grille on the −X side face and an exhaust grille on the +X one. The front column IS that intake, and what the pack costs the block is [71](INTAKE_SHADOW_PCT)% of its [178](INTAKE_FACE_W) × [151](INTAKE_FACE_H) mm intake face shadowed, [29](INTAKE_OPEN_PCT)% open, which is what the grille and whatever ducts the crossing have to work with.

That is what the assembly scorecard reports: the `routed` axis at [59](ROUTED_PCT)% — [41](ROUTED_N) of the [70](CONNECTIONS_N) connections the machine owes are built, which is **every fluid segment in the manifold's topology** — the shared section from source to channel split, both bag circuits, both pump rows, and both nozzle gates' runs out to the rear panel — and with them the tap-water path, the CO2 chain, the refrigerant loop and the carb riser. Nothing is blocked by the pack. What is left is the wiring, counted rather than lost, so nothing disappears by being absent. Ten of the eleven gates are green and the red one is bend-radius. A green gate is not a claim that every connection can be made, only that no two placed solids clash, no built tube drives through one, and **every tube port has the straight a run off it needs**: the port-leads gate casts each collet's own bore along its own axis for the stub and tangent a turn off it takes, which is the check that says a located port can actually be used. It is the one that would have caught the loft's four facing collets, and it now stands over all 89.

## Constraints the layout respects

The arrangement above is the current pack — a working layout. It is free to rearrange so long as it respects these physical and functional realities:

- The foam shell is the largest single solid; it cannot move or compress. Its short axis across the machine is what makes the machine narrow, so the yaw is not negotiable while the width is.
- The core spans the box's full depth, so it goes in before the front and back halves telescope together around it. The seam's furniture all lives in the bands the standoffs open, so none of it meets the core.
- The cold core stands flat on the floor, on its bottom foam-cap lid: the six M3 cap screws driven up from below sit in counterbores in the lid's own head pads.
- Appliance width follows the foam shell's short face; nothing else may become the widest object without giving the edition up.
- The condenser's airflow axis is its own short dimension, and it lies ACROSS the machine: the block draws through its finstack from the −X side face and exhausts out the +X one it stands against, so the air crosses the cabinet rather than turning inside it and the hot end leaves by the nearest wall.
- The compressor stands upright, on its own feet, and cannot be laid on its side or inverted: the oil charge sits in the bottom of the hermetic can and the pickup is gravity-fed. That fixes the shroud's open face downward, which leaves a yaw as the only turn it has — and a yaw keeps its copper-bearing face horizontal, so the copper always leaves sideways.
- The compressor shroud is a fixed-size sheet-metal part enclosing the terminal block + PTC relay/overload (UL 60335-2-89, 130 × 175 × 150 mm interior, the only metal part in the enclosure).
- The flavor funnel feeds the pumps from above and stays top-removable for cleaning.
- The Multiplex drip pan sits directly under the Multiplex's atmospheric vent; the backflow preventer + drip pan + moisture sensor co-locate on the water-inlet path.
- The CO2 line enters the cold core low, on the vessel's bottom-plate port line.
- The carbonated-water dispense run from the core's outlet to the front spout stays short.

**The loft's west lane is packed end to end, and every one of its ends is a measurement.** What it holds runs [253.9](LOFT_LANE_LEN) mm — the bag-B pair's forward face to the nozzle-B gate's aft one. Three stands are in it now, the bag-B pair and the nozzle-B gate with the fill valve's one-seat plate between them: [190.5](LOFT_TRAY_LEN) mm of plate and [63.42](LOFT_LANE_BAYS) mm across the two bays they leave, one of which is the junction bay ([16](LOFT_TRAY_BAY_MM) mm by construction). Four things bound it, and each was bought:

- **The funnel's aft reach.** The bag-B tray's box now stands *inside* the funnel's in plan, and its solid still stands [34.02](BAG_B_FUNNEL) mm clear: the basin slopes up toward the front, so packing to the box wastes the difference the way the source pair's seat once did. The pair is placed against the real surface, and `clear hopper-funnel` on the scorecard is what holds it there.
- **The lid's own front edge.** The pair packs FORWARD onto it (`bag_b_tray_y`): the deck it stands on runs out there, and forward of that plane a plate has nothing under it. Its two mount ears stand on the turned pattern, so the plane a cap deck column may reach (`fore_deck_column_face`) is read as a second floor and the aft of the two wins.
- **The ASSE chain's crown.** The chain is the one body on the water deck that reaches the loft's own tray floor: its vent bonnet stood 1.9 mm proud of it over a 30 mm sill, and fouled the aft tray's bottom plate. It is dropped out of the loft, and the drip basin rides it down on the rails the −X wall stands under its rim, so the vent's own column is unchanged.
- **The rear panel's port row.** The two runs that leave the machine turn on their own lanes behind the nozzle pair and have to reach the row at [358.2](PORT_ROW_Z), which is what fixes the aft end.

**The head column's forward face is its JUNCTIONS'.** Each of the three stands a fitting's own reach off the collet plane it serves and nothing stands ahead of them: Y-A and Y-B a `JUNCTION_LEG_LEAD` off the two columns their four ports make, forward face [77.91](JUNCTION_FRONT_Y), and Y-E its own half-diameter into the strip ahead of the bag pair, forward face [80.35](BAG_TEE_FRONT_Y). The pump row stands forward of all three, so what the head column spends in Y is a fitting's body and not a leaning leg.

A soft-LLDPE leg leaves or enters a collet as one straight length through [22](DIVIDER_LEAN)° — the whole of what a push-to-connect collet grips through — and owes [3](DIVIDER_LEG_STRAIGHT) mm of tube still running straight into the collet once an arc seats. `fluid-23` is the run that spends both, leaning into the leg between the bag pair's fill collet and Y-G's forward run port.

**The pocket between the front wall and the manifold is the junctions' to spend.** What reaches furthest forward in this column is a junction's own forward face, and the pocket left in front of it is [80.91](FRONT_POCKET_D) mm — the pump row stands in it, on the band the front corner's seam furniture leaves.

Everything else is open: where the two side grilles sit and what ducts the block's faces to them, the funnel/pump access, the exact zone boundaries and the appliance depth, and all intra-zone placement. The holders the bay, the front column and the loft still owe — both flavor pumps' isolation mounts, the SeaFlo's, the ASSE chain's bracket, V-K's cradle, the discharge chain's, all four pump-row tees', the standoff the manifold's stack pitch implies and the floor the loft's two trays stand on — are the `held` axis, at 37%.

## What is on the front face

- ESP32-S3 config display — a 4.3" touchscreen on the 45° facet, centred, angled up toward the standing user. Default state shows the selected flavor; touch reaches flavor-image/ratio tuning, clean cycles, priming, and advanced settings.
- Front-dispense spout (the drill-trigger moment).
- CO2 inlet — possibly migrated to the furthest-forward edge of a side face. Disconnection-under-pressure cable-whip and asphyxiation risk make this the most physically dangerous connection.
