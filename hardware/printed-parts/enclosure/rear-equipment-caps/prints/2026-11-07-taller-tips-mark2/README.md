# Regulator lead-in comparison

Mark2 prints the standard pointed cover and a taller pointed cover, each with
its complete matching receiver. The taller lead-in keeps the same lateral
position, retaining nose, spring section and fitted bearings. Its receiver
extends the flank pockets into the enclosure roof stock.

The receivers retain the production back-top layer direction and have no
supports inside their pockets. The covers stand on their production axial
ends with ordinary layers; fine layers belong only to the tilted receivers.
The other articles provide the covers' time away between layers. The plate
uses traditional timelapse without a priming tower.
