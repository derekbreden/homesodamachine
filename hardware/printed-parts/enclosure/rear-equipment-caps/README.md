# Rear equipment caps

Four PET-GF caps close the integral seats on back-top. Each cap is drawn from
the station of the receiver it snaps into: [stations.rs](stations.rs) figures
the stations as back-top's [supports.rs](../enclosure/back-top/supports.rs)
figures its receivers, and [rear_equipment_caps.rs](rear_equipment_caps.rs)
cuts the caps.

The ASSE cap holds the lower halves of both ASSE hexes and captures both
axial shoulders of the inlet adapter's hex. Its solid lower body extends to
Y461.078, while its springs retain Y396.453–438.114. The adapter pocket follows
the captured profile and all six wrench-flat directions at the measured clock.
Its complete fitted cavity and installed adapter sit 0.18 mm higher in Z than
the captured chain pose, matching the wrench-band center height to the ASSE body axis.
The three lower-facing flats have 0.25 mm additional vertical seating allowance;
the upper flats, 0.15 mm cylindrical neck seats and both axial shoulder
stations retain their fits. The hex's rounded corner room has 12.874 mm radius
through the 10.25 mm wrench band, clearing the lowered flats. The ASSE body's wrench flats
locate the body separately.
The downward vent cup passes through the bottom flat. The Pisco
cap has a raised lip around the short outlet fitting's neck and a broad body
bearing between its independent leaves. It spans 41.704 mm in Y, ending at
Y462.05 before a full 3 mm receiver end wall and the PISCO's rear-wall bore.
The meter cap enters from the west
and has a solid 9 mm spine, shaped bearing stock extending toward the housing,
and two complete 38 mm-long leaves. A 6 mm lower outside corner bevel clears
the A-fill tube while retaining the 9 mm central spine, full fitted bearing
and spring-root geometry. Its flexible
leads turn west against the housing above the lower leaf. The water regulator
cap spans 36 mm of the housing and smaller outlet neck, with its lowest face
at Z315.924 mm. Each cap is one solid, with two integral spring walls and hooks.

The ASSE seat uses 0.25 mm running clearance. Its cap has one continuous
hex cavity with a 14.6 mm nominal upper apothem at the two wrench flats' mean
clocking. Its three lower-facing planes have 0.25 mm additional vertical seating
allowance over the main body and bonnet; all six flat directions are retained.
The common envelope includes at least 0.15 mm slip clearance around both hexes. The ASSE seat allows 1.5 mm of threaded-joint position variation.
The drain-sleeve window carries that full fore/aft adjustment and clears the
sleeve's measured upper exterior.
The Pisco cap follows the scanned body and exposed neck with 0.15 mm radial
and 0.25 mm axial air. The receiver has a 3 mm-wide upper ring bearing at
Y438.125–441.125 with 0.15 mm radial air; its loading corridor retains 0.5 mm.
The neck lip excludes the release collet. The meter's cap and receiver follow
the housing taper, flange and label-side bevel with 0.15 mm radial and axial
air. Bosses and screw heads have separate fitted passages; the receiver's
fore corridor admits the complete 16 mm insertion slide. Its spring station,
wire exit and lower outside bevel retain their positions. The water
regulator uses its measured linear radius profile and true installed axis,

Its body-to-outlet shoulder retains the scanned position. The central cap
bearing ends 1 mm short of the spring ends beside the outlet release collet.
The PISCO, water-regulator and flow-meter hardware fits are physically approved.
The [latest pointed-set result](../../../print-tests/asse-body-seating/physical-result/physical-result.json)
reports tight hardware fits, with the ASSE body seating as the exception. The
[ASSE body cap](../../../print-tests/asse-body-seating/README.md) carries its local seating allowance.
All leaves are 2.0 mm thick, with a 1.5 mm inside root radius and 22.25 mm
root-to-catch length; the water regulator uses 21.25 mm within its retained
height. The straight wall above the root blend is 20.75 mm, or 19.75 mm on
the water regulator. Each rear cap has the same single planar lead-in ending at a
pointed free edge above a solid nose land. Its matching receiver ceiling clears
the complete point. All retaining noses are solid. The rear receiver bases are continuous,
with no enclosed screwdriver tunnels. Release the leaves from their exposed
edges with the surrounding service room open. The front paired-pump cartridge
uses its separate open flank pry windows. The nominal catch
clearance is 0.4 mm. One three-mm-high planar bevel on the ASSE receiver's lower
east fore corner clears the neighboring PISCO cap's service path; the neck
retains 3 mm of stock and the upper end stop retains its fitted planes.
ASSE, Pisco and meter enter their seats 16 mm forward and slide aft. The
regulator enters directly upward. The caps close after their bodies seat.

Work with back-top ceiling-down on the bench, separated from the cold core.
Disconnect and dress the flexible plumbing and leads aside. Reverse the
following service sequence for assembly; fit the ASSE chain after the meter
and PISCO. Fit the vent sleeve and its return tube after all three caps are
seated, with the sleeve clocked 15° from fore toward the meter. For meter-cap
service, disconnect DRAIN and remove the sleeve from the ASSE barb first.

For ASSE, remove the inlet header, disconnect the drain connection and remove the drain sleeve and
rear drain bulkhead. Spread the exposed leaves and
lower the cap 18 mm. Support the ASSE chain and cap together, slide both
32 mm fore, and withdraw them from the open side. The cap stays 18 mm below
the chain during this move.

After removing the ASSE chain, release the meter leaves, withdraw the cap
20 mm west and let the leaves relax. Move it another 25 mm west, then 40 mm
fore and out through the open side. Remove the meter, its disconnected soda
line/insulation and bend holder before withdrawing the PISCO cap.

Remove VK to expose the PISCO receiver's service room. Spread the PISCO
leaves, lower the cap 3.6 mm, depress the exposed outlet collet and withdraw
the stem check. Slide the cap 45 mm fore with the leaves held open. With the
cap clear of the hardware, squeeze its free wings slightly inward, move it
west and fore, then withdraw diagonally west and down through the freed bay.

The regulator releases directly downward with its exposed leaves spread.

Remove the small cavity supports with a tiny flathead screwdriver that fits
inside the opening. Derek reports that the original trial's small automatic
cavity supports removed easily with that tool. Preserve the flat catch
undersides while clearing them. The [physical record](../../../print-tests/rear-snap-retention/physical-result/physical-result.json)
records this removal result and the hardware fit observations.

For release, put a small flat blade into the receiver channel beside the
solid hook and ease the leaf outward until its retaining face clears. Support
the cap while releasing both sides. The checked blade is 3 mm wide and
0.6 mm thick with a 2.5 mm shaft. PISCO uses a long slim precision driver;
its recorded envelope has 65 mm of reach and a short handle. These are
modeled tool envelopes; removal effort remains a physical print check.
