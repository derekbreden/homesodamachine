# H2C grip insert print record

The [launch receipt](launch.json) and [completion reading](completion.json)
identify the cover article and the printer-reported completion. The cover matches
the insert identified in the [physical acceptance](../physical-acceptance.json).
Physical fit and print quality for this particular insert are not recorded.

The [preflight](preflight.json) binds the source and slice hashes, print profile,
layer schedule, wing bead overlap and trim commands. The [preview](preview.png)
shows the plate. The [prestart reading](prestart-status.json) records the printer
observations, and the [print-only package record](print-only-package.json)
identifies the submitted archive.
