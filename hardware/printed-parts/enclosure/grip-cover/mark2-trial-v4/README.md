# Mark2 grip receiver and insert trial records

The [launch receipt](launch.json) identifies the submitted cover and receiver
coupon plate and its native archive. The cover rests with its wings on the bed;
the receiver samples stand in the tested enclosure orientation.

![Coupon plate](plate.png)

The [preflight](preflight.json), [geometry check](geometry-check.json),
[print check](print-check.json), [preparation](preparation.json) and
[support audit](support-audit.json) bind the source geometry and reviewed slice.
The [prestart reading](prestart-status.json), [startup reading](startup-status.json)
and [acceptance reading](acceptance-status.json) identify the printer observations.

The [physical acceptance](../physical-acceptance.json) retains the support-removal
and assembled-fit observations for the specific articles it identifies.
