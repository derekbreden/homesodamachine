# Nameplate

A [104.53 mm](PLATE_W) × [38 mm](PLATE_H) horizontal plate, flush with the rear wall of
`enclosure-back-top`. The face carries the faucet mark, `HOME / SODA / MACHINE` in equal-size
Helvetica Bold, and a unit-specific QR. The three groups have equal 5.1 mm visible gaps.
There is no printed domain, serial, dividing rule or rating footer.

[`nameplate.py`](nameplate.py) generates the two-colour part. [`wordmark.svg`](wordmark.svg)
contains the exact outlined lettering; the faucet comes from [`brand/mark.svg`](/brand/mark.svg).
The flat face is [3.36 mm](NAMEPLATE_T) thick with R3 corners and no raised edge.
All white artwork has a [0.72 mm](INK_DEPTH) embedded base and rises 0.48 mm above the black face.
The STEP and 3MF preserve both colour volumes; the STL represents their combined exterior.

## QR

The unit-0001 payload is `HTTPS://HOSM.US/0001`. Uppercase scheme and host allow the complete
URL to use QR alphanumeric mode: version 1, error correction M, **21 × 21 active modules**.
Each module is 1.1 mm; the active square is 23.1 mm and its four-module quiet zone makes a
31.9 mm square. The quiet zone is part of the black face and contains no other artwork.
Diagonal-only white contacts have 0.02 mm corner joins to keep the CAD interface manifold;
the 1.1 mm module pitch is unchanged. The unit identifier is encoded in the QR. The full-domain address in written instructions is
`homesodamachine.com/0001`; neither address is lettered on this plate.

The separate [appliance marking specification](/hardware/markings/README.md) covers ratings and
refrigerant warnings. The exterior disposal warning is raised into a plain field low on the
rear enclosure.

## Retention

Two coplanar horizontal wings enter pockets in back-top. Each projects 2.40 mm,
is 1.68 mm thick and spans 30 mm, with R0.6 ends. The plate and wings share a
flat back. Flex the plate outward to insert the wings and seat it against the
pocket floor. A rear diamond opening permits pushing the centre outward for removal.

Body X clearance is 0.15 mm per side. Wing tips have 0.25 mm centered clearance,
0.10 mm at full body float. The wing-thickness gap is 0.45 mm; the 2.13 mm slot
leaves a 1.23 mm retaining lip. Z uses 0.15 mm static clearance plus 0.25 mm once
at the print-down mouth and slot end. The entry bevel is 1.10 mm wide × 0.40 mm
deep. Minimum capture is 2.10 mm, including at least 1.00 mm of flat bearing.
These are the [accepted physical fit](horizontal-wing-trial/physical-acceptance.json).
There is no low-force or sliding allowance in this joint.

In the prepared full back-top, a local relief behind the east wing leaves 2.14 mm
of backing and 1.00 mm clearance to the PSU terminal corner. The remaining backing
is 3.60 mm. Smooth lands preserve the complete 1.23 mm retaining lips through the
exterior fluting. The tested nameplate geometry and its pocket clearances are unchanged, and the
current back-top's slice review and launch state are in
[print readiness](../print-readiness.md).

The plate is centred on the flavour chips' height and between the flavour A pocket
and rear tangent. The production pocket is generated by
[`_nameplate_wing_interface.py`](../enclosure/_nameplate_wing_interface.py).
The [full-enclosure checks](../enclosure/accepted-fit-integration/geometry-check.json)
compare this cutter with the accepted receiver and check surrounding hardware.

## Printing and assembly

Print face up with the plate back and both wings on the bed, without supports.
All white lettering, the faucet/drop mark and QR rise 0.48 mm. Preserve the accepted
Mark2 black/white alignment compensation in the print package; it is not a CAD
translation. Use the shared PET-GF material settings and a 0.20 mm first layer.

The receiver prints as part of back-top in its roof-down orientation, with shared
PET-GF tree supports and clearance. Remove its supports through the front opening
before installing the nameplate. Printed full-enclosure fit remains a physical
assembly check; the accepted evidence is from the matching receiver coupon.

## Generation

```sh
tools/cad-venv/bin/python hardware/printed-parts/enclosure/nameplate/nameplate.py 27
tools/cad-venv/bin/python hardware/printed-parts/enclosure/nameplate/nameplate.py selftest
```

The first command emits `nameplate-027.step`; the assembly uses `nameplate-001.step`.
Units are restricted to 0001–9999 so every QR stays version 1. The self-test checks solid
validity and QR quiet-zone geometry.

## Print records

The [horizontal-wing print records](horizontal-wing-trial/README.md) identify the
accepted flat plate, Mark2's black/white alignment compensation and the matching
receiver. Black PET-GF maps to the left external spool and white PET-GF to the
right external spool, both using hardened 0.4 mm nozzles and the device's PET-CF
label. Keep the standard nozzle-offset startup option at Auto.

`horizontal-wing-trial/prepare_pair.py` prepares the face-up plate and a receiver
coupon. `horizontal-wing-trial/prepare_receiver.py` prepares a receiver alone in
its production print orientation. The nameplate has no supports; the receiver
uses the shared PET-GF tree-support settings.

The [full back-top integration](../enclosure/accepted-fit-integration/README.md)
contains this receiver, with measured hardware clearance and full-thickness
retaining lips in its fluted exterior.
