# Electronics access panel

The empty PET-GF roof panel closes the dry electronics bay after the C14,
Mean Well and nearby harness connections are complete. No equipment, wires,
tubes or mounts attach to the panel. Remove its four screws and lift it
vertically to reopen the bay.

The panel is 100 × 126.55 × 9 mm with R3 corners and 0.25 mm perimeter
clearance. Four M3 × 12 socket screws sit in Ø6.2 × 3.2 mm counterbores;
their heads are 0.2 mm below the roof. The matching RX-M3×5.7 inserts remain
in four integral bosses on back-top. Each boss has a 9 mm fore/aft roof root;
the rear pair also join the rear wall. Both east bosses also have full-depth
9 mm roots into the fixed roof on their +X sides. There is no perimeter bearing rim.

A 6 × 2 mm front-edge notch admits a flat screwdriver to lift the panel.
Its flat underside is the print bed face. Use the shared PET-GF recipe and
support policy. The native placement is defined in
[above-core.json](../../../manifold-layout/above-core.json); the shell and
this part share the same geometry functions. Nominal fit and removal geometry
do not establish physical cleanup, installed fit or retention strength.
