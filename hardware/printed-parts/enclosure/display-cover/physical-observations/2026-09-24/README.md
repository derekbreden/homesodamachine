# Display-cover fit observations

Derek reports an approximately 4 mm center lift with the snaps engaged. The rim presses flat easily, the removed cover lies flat, and the upside-down cover slips into the recess easily with its snaps unengaged. These observations point to snap-induced frame compression. The 4 mm figure is an estimate.

The prepared trial preserves the perimeter and shifts each cover snap inward 0.3 mm. Its installed flatness and retention remain untested. [Observations and photo hashes](observations.json).
