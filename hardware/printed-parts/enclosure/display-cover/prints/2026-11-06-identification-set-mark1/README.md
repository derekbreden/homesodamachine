# Identification set on Mark One

The rear identification chips, serial nameplate and status plate share one face-up print.
Their flat backs lie on the glued Engineering Plate; the screw seats and raised artwork face
upward. The status light's translucent PETG occupies the plate's full thickness.

Black Bambu PETG Basic feeds the fixed left hotend from the AMS HT. White, blue and red PETG
Basic and clear PETG Translucent feed the right hotend rack from the AMS Pro. The preparation
record names each slot, source mesh, filament volume and print setting. The native sliced
archive retains the models and toolpaths for the complete plate.

The launch record identifies the printer's accepted job and the dryer-stop result.

The [physical result](physical-result.json) records the stopped print and its
first-layer photograph. The [retry](../2026-11-06-identification-set-mark1-z000/README.md)
uses no Z offset on the glued Engineering Plate.
