# Nameplate with all white artwork raised

One face-up nameplate for Mark2 with HOME / SODA / MACHINE, the faucet logo/drop
and the unit-0001 QR all raised 0.48 mm above the black face. The 32 mm leaves,
3.6 mm hooks and 1.23 mm bearing clearance use the existing broad-leaf receiver.
The geometry below the show face is unchanged, including the embedded white inlay.

The native slice contains all three white artwork groups at Z 16.29 and 16.53 mm,
each a complete 0.24 mm layer. The black show face ends at Z 16.05 mm. The final
face toolpaths decode to HTTPS://HOSM.US/0001. Physical scanning is pending.

Normal Snug supports are entirely black. Three interface layers at 0.20 mm spacing
end at Z 13.41 mm, leaving 0.24 mm below the plate back. XY separation is 0.40 mm;
first-layer support gap is 0.50 mm. Flushing into supports is disabled. The support
mat is accessible between the leaves and from the plate's open ends before fitting.

Print estimate: 64 minutes, 70 layers. The first layer is 0.20 mm; ordinary model
layers are 0.24 mm, with the two alignment layers recorded in verification.json.
Black PET-GF uses the left 0.4 mm nozzle and white PET-GF the right 0.4 mm nozzle.
