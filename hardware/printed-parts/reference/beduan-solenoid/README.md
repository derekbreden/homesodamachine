# Beduan solenoid valve — reference solid

A coarse keep-out envelope for the Beduan 12V DC normally-closed solenoid
valve (2-way, 1/4" quick-connect — Amazon B07NWCQJK9), the off-the-shelf
valve used 12× per unit on the fluid-topology manifold (`hardware/bom.md`).
This is a purchased part, not a printed one — the model is for rack and
manifold layout, not a manufacturing drawing.

Built from four primitives, fused into one solid:

| Primitive | Size (X × Y × Z) | Part |
|---|---|---|
| Box | 32.25 × 32.25 × 30.6 | white valve body (fluid section) |
| Box | 32.25 × 24 × 26 | solenoid coil (electrical section) |
| Cylinder | ⌀15 × 59, axis ∥ Y | port / tube-flow axis (two QC collets) |
| 2× box | 6.3 × 0.8 × 15 each | spade terminals (on the coil top) |

## Coordinate convention

- **X** = width — across the body, perpendicular to flow
- **Y** = depth — along the tube-flow axis, port-to-port
- **Z** = height — up from the mounting surface, through the coil

Origin is the center of the white-body footprint in X–Y; the mounting
surface (bottom of the white body) sits at Z = 0.

## Arrangement

- The white body sits on the Z = 0 mounting plane, rising to Z = 30.6.
- The solenoid coil is centered on top of the body in X and Y, from
  Z = 30.6 to Z = 56.6 — a T-profile, symmetric in X. The coil is
  shallower in Y than the body (24 vs. 32.25), so the body steps out as
  a ledge below it.
- The port cylinder runs along Y through the body, centered in X, 4 mm
  below the body's mid-height, overhanging the body by ~13.4 mm each
  side as the two quick-connect collet stubs.
- Two spade terminals stand on the coil top (Z = 56.6 → 71.6), side by
  side 10 mm apart in X and offset to Y = +6 — toward the connector-block
  edge in the photos rather than dead-center on the coil.

Overall envelope: 32.25 (X) × 59 (Y) × 71.6 (Z) mm.
