# Post-publication geometry review

The live Sculpted, Industrial and enclosure payloads were verified against their local SHA-256 digests after publication on October 21, 2026. The faucet guide and finish-control module also matched their current files. This is a geometry review, not physical fit, support-removal, capacity or lifetime acceptance.

[Native faucet evidence](native-check.json), [internal-path evidence](interior-native-check.json) and the [organizer reading](../umbilical-organizer/integration-check.json) bind the inspected geometry. Both internal flavor paths hold at least R25 and have no overlap with fixed plumbing. Four flexible wiring envelopes cross the paths: J1-positive-C/H, J2-OUT1 and J7-GND. The assembly procedure requires dressing those leads clear; their independent wiring model is not qualified by this review.

Both nominal reducer stems overlap their connected valve's occupied reference envelope. The complete overlap lies within the inserted stem cylinder; none extends beyond it. The valve's internal socket, insertion stop and actual installed shoulder gap remain unmeasured. These connected-envelope findings remain visible in the general assembly clash report.

## Lint scope and dispositions

[The retained lint output](post-publish-lint.txt) records the findings, coordinates and existing answers. Top-six readings were taken on the larger parts; the cap and small identification bodies were read in full. The two FLAVOR chips and two FLAVOR collars share their respective geometry.

| Part | Reading and disposition |
|---|---|
| Sculpted and Industrial bases | Retained 0.325 mm donor-to-mounting steps, lever-window strips and thin tapered window rims remain open outside the changed flavor passages. Broadening the donor cavity or growing the shoulder would be a separate donor/lever geometry correction. This change does not accept those surfaces or claim their physical finish. |
| Shared tip | The retained display-pocket edges and 2 mm neck/socket surfaces remain visible in the thickness reading. The 4 mm passage change does not qualify those existing thin-section findings. Growing the exterior or relocating the display/joint requires a separate interface revision. |
| Organizer | Native face material is at least 1.5625 mm. Soda-to-rim face material is 1.7133 mm; flavor-to-rim is 2.8383 mm. The current lint answers use these dimensions. Enlarging the puck consumes its nominal 0.465 mm radial braid clearance in the countertop hole. Black-tube fit remains unmeasured. |
| 4 mm anchor cap | The eight strips are the intentional mouth eases, lip tips and latch faces; the two thin readings are the nominal 1.5 mm spring wings. [The cap answers](../../lint-answers/enclosure/anchor-cap/anchor-cap-4mm.lint-answers) identify each face and the larger/different constructions. No retention force or endurance result is inferred. |
| Back-top | The new flavor-B tie saddle has a supported flat ceiling; retain Tree(auto)/Default in the production orientation. Removing that ceiling removes the tie-routing space. The retained signal-jack roof, teardrop slopes and separately answered thin-region findings remain outside the tubing revision. Their finish and cleanup are not accepted here. |
| Cap lid | The new A bearing clears the lid openings and discharge chain. The reported step/strip/ceiling picks are at the retained discharge-chain, valve-cradle and side-anchor features. Reworking those bearing and access profiles is outside this change. |
| FLAVOR chip and collar | The temporary single-material body readings omit the separately printed word bodies. Their small strips, pockets and short-ray minima concern the unchanged lettering and are not a whole-article wall measurement. The current two-color STEP assemblies remain the print sources; a whole-article wall reading must include both materials. The M15 chip opening and 4.28 mm collar bore are native dimensions, not physical fit results. |
