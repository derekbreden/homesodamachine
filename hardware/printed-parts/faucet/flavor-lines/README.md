# Flavor dispense lines

Each flavor has this path:

**V-G-O / V-J-O → ARD4M4-E → black 4 mm LLDPE → ABU44M-E FLAVOR bulkhead → continuous black 4 mm LLDPE → dispense face.**

Both faucet styles and finishes use black flavor tubing. The separate soda supply is 1/4-inch OD through the umbilical, with a 3/8-inch OD soda tube between the Westbrass outlet and the tip. OVER is a separate white 4 mm tube and separate rear fitting.

## Flow and qualification scope

The customer outcome is a smaller continuous flavor line with less concentrate held between the outlet valve and the glass. A 2.5 mm bore holds **4.91 mL per metre**. A straight 2.5 m run at 100 mL/min has a calculated laminar pressure drop of **0.63 psi per mPa·s of viscosity**, before fittings. This follows the circular-tube Poiseuille relation; concentrate viscosity, the pump's delivered flow and actual fitting losses are not measured by that calculation.

Use the existing [installed-path ratio acceptance](../../../assembly/acceptance-and-burn-in.md): prime each complete channel, collect through its actual tip, and measure the concentrate/water ratio with the bench's existing graduated vessels or scale. The specified 1:20 ratio and ±5% acceptance belong to that procedure. Pump output and tubing resistance cannot be established by tube OD or an unloaded CAD route. Firmware's volume calibration must reflect the assembled path; no tube diameter constant changes the delivered dose by itself.

CAD evidence covers native validity, passage and support fit, bend radii and clearance to fixed plumbing. The installed wiring is dressed clear of the new route at assembly; the current wire drawings are a separate routing workstream. Prior faucet finish/lever/display acceptance records retain their identified articles and scope. Black-tube push-fit engagement, printed threading/sliding fit, completed dispensing, drip behavior and service life have no invented physical result.
