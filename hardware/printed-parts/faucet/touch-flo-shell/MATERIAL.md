# Touch-Flo shell — material

PET-CF. Sourced as Bambu PET-CF, with Polymaker Fiberon PET-CF17 as a Prime-available alternative at the same material class.

**Finish differs between the two — open tradeoff, not yet decided.** The two are interchangeable on the *mechanical* spec envelope, but observed on attempt 18 (2026-05-27): Bambu PET-CF prints a duller **matte**, Polymaker Fiberon PET-CF17 prints noticeably **glossier**. This is a filament-formulation property, not a slicer setting — temperature is a weak lever (gloss rises slightly with nozzle temp) but cannot close the gap. Since the touch-flo-shell is the visible, above-counter faucet exterior, surface finish is a genuine application requirement the mechanical envelope doesn't capture. Weighs toward reserving Bambu PET-CF for finished/visible parts once restocked; counterweight is that Polymaker is the Prime-available, cheaper fallback that exists for exactly the "Bambu ran out" case. Decision deferred.

Calibration history at [`print-log.md`](/hardware/printed-parts/faucet/touch-flo-shell/print-log.md).
