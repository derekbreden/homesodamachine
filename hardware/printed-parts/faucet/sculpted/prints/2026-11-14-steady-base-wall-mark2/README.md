# Sculpted faucet steady base wall on Mark2

The [print archive](sculpted-faucet-steady-base-wall-black-mark2.gcode.3mf)
uses the complete black PET-GF matched-shoulder plate. The Sculpted base's
ordinary outer-wall laps take a common speed ceiling from the inclined courses
through the upper shaft. Slower moves keep their existing feeds, including
moves limited by Z speed. The underside courses, the support paths and the
matched-shoulder tip carry through from the
[source plate](../2026-11-14-matched-shoulder-mark2/README.md).

The [wall-speed reading](wall-speed.json) ties the rear surface sample,
command comparison and motion estimate to the packaged G-code. The
[photographs of the oval](https://github.com/derekbreden/homesodamachine/tree/771d5a7e7775cc1106dbe0d9c7a3cc16b49e2b26/hardware/printed-parts/faucet/sculpted/prints/2026-11-14-rear-base-oval)
record the surface to compare after printing. Physical finish is pending.
