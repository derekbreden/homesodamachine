# Sculpted faucet with short inclined foundations on Mark2

The [print plate](sculpted-faucet-seven-courses-black-mark2.gcode.3mf) and
[editable project](sculpted-faucet-seven-courses-black-mark2.3mf) hold the
complete black PET-GF faucet with the extended display cover. The
[launch receipt](launch.json) identifies the submitted job.

On the base, a level scaffold carries the inclined roof and the part courses
parallel to the mounting face. On the tip, the anchor, plug and support roots
start level. The interface and the visible shoulder follow the bearing plane.
The [course record](course-scope.json) gives the scope of each stack and the
archived Mark2 recipe for the level walls. The level roads start where the
nozzle clears the top of the stack, with extrusion adjusted to the space left
under each cut road.

The [motion replay](head-replay.json) and its [approach readings](approaches.json)
measure clearance against the nominal deposited paths. The [estimate](chronology.json)
uses the planner's motion time. The first visible shoulder, the joins between
course directions, the support release and the overall finish are physical
checks for this trial.
