# Reservoir floor + bulkhead port

The cavity floor's **interior** (wet) surface is a Y-symmetric V swept across the full cavity X width: from each ±Y wall the floor slopes inward and down to a flat rectangular trough at y=0 that spans the full interior X width and hosts the bulkhead port. The wet surface is a single Y–Z section — slope down, flat, slope up — extruded straight across X; the only curved floor boundary is the cavity's existing centerward arc. The floor's **exterior** (dry) underside is a single flat horizontal plane across the whole footprint, with the floor filled solid between the wet V and that plane — so the reservoir prints mouth-up with no support under the floor. Below that flat bottom is open bag-pocket space for the bulkhead's below-floor hardware. The bulkhead — PureSec 1/4" RO push-to-connect 90° elbow bulkhead ([B0968K4JRN](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0968K4JRN)), white PP, water/RO-rated — clamps vertically through the trough and mounts **elbow-down**: its threaded barrel passes **up** through the trough floor (a ⌀[16 mm](BULKHEAD_PANEL_HOLE_D) hole), the ⌀[18.7 mm](BULKHEAD_DRY_FLANGE_OD) elbow-side flange + the integral 90° elbow hang **below** the floor in the bag-pocket cavity, and the hex nut threads on from **above**, in the cavity. The elbow turns the dry line laterally below the floor toward the bag-pocket +Y pass-through. The PureSec ships without a panel o-ring, so a printed TPU face washer in a counterbore on **each** trough-floor face supplies the seal — sized to its clamping face: a generous ⌀[21 mm](BULKHEAD_SEAL_WET_OD) wet washer clamped by the ⌀[21.9 mm](BULKHEAD_WET_NUT_OD) nut-side face (`reservoir-bulkhead-seal-wet.step`), and a narrower ⌀[18.5 mm](BULKHEAD_SEAL_DRY_OD) dry washer clamped by the ⌀[18.7 mm](BULKHEAD_DRY_FLANGE_OD) flange (`reservoir-bulkhead-seal-dry.step`).

The nut sits in the open cavity (wet side), threaded onto the barrel from above and hand-tightened — there is open cavity space all around it, so it needs no anti-rotation pocket.

Syrup drains by gravity from anywhere in the cavity down the V to the central trough and into the bulkhead port. The lowest drainable line is the bulkhead's port axis; residual below that line stays in the bulkhead body itself.
