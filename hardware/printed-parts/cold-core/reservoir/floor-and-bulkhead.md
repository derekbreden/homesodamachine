# Reservoir floor + bulkhead port

The cavity floor is a Y-symmetric V swept across the full cavity X width: from each ±Y wall the floor slopes inward and down to a flat rectangular trough at y=0 that spans the full interior X width and hosts the bulkhead port. The floor is a single Y–Z section — slope down, flat, slope up — extruded straight across X; the only curved floor boundary is the cavity's existing centerward arc. The bulkhead — PureSec 1/4" RO push-to-connect 90° elbow bulkhead ([B0968K4JRN](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0968K4JRN)), white PP, water/RO-rated — clamps vertically through the trough and mounts **elbow-down**: its threaded barrel passes **up** through the trough floor (a ⌀16 mm hole), the ⌀18.7 mm elbow-side flange + the integral 90° elbow hang **below** the floor in the bag-pocket cavity, and the hex nut threads on from **above**, in the cavity. The elbow turns the dry line laterally below the floor toward the bag-pocket +Y pass-through. The PureSec ships without a panel o-ring, so a printed TPU face washer in a counterbore on **each** trough-floor face supplies the seal — the wet/top washer clamped by the ⌀21.9 mm nut-side face, the dry/under washer by the ⌀18.7 mm flange.

The nut sits in the open cavity (wet side), threaded onto the barrel from above and hand-tightened — there is open cavity space all around it, so it needs no anti-rotation pocket.

Syrup drains by gravity from anywhere in the cavity down the V to the central trough and into the bulkhead port. The lowest drainable line is the bulkhead's port axis; residual below that line stays in the bulkhead body itself.
