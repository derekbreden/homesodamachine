# J1/J2 cable bank

The lid carries one shared bank at world X70–83.4, Y184.6–200.6,
Z253.3–273.45 mm. The original PCB, its ten plugs, WAGOs, valves and fluid
routes retain their positions. The bank is east of the PCB, beside the
front-going cable paths.

Its two seats share a filled 3 mm base and 3 mm centre wall. Both outer
walls are 3 mm thick. Each seat has 2.2 mm clear depth, 16 mm cable span,
a 0.75 mm retaining beak and a 45° entry. The first cavity is 13.3 mm high;
the second is 7.75 mm high. The catches retain the accepted four-lead
cable-hook section. The lid prints on its flat underside, with both cable
mouths opening in its +Z growth direction.

Seven individually dressed J1 leads use the first seat: COM and OUT3–OUT8.
The second seat carries J2 OUT1, OUT2, FAN and COM. The nominal insulation
OD is 1.70 mm, with 1.85 mm pitch through each seat. J1 OUT1/OUT2 and
J2 OUT3 branch before the bank. Dress these flexible leads after the cap
pour, with the funnel frame removed.

[Native fit](native-fit.json) records the exact bank, current lid root,
seated conductor sections and local vertical loading bands. [Routing](routing.json) records the twelve updated conductors, retained
terminals and guide spans. The combined cap publication supplies the full
source lid and printable-surface receipt.

Physical acceptance covers the tested four-lead cable section. The
seven-lead grouping, complete installed harness dressing, insertion force,
service access and long-term retention retain their physical-fit scope.
Between the defined guide lands, dress the loose leads past the flexible
flavor tubes and compressor jacket.
