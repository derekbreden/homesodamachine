# V-A feed lid snap

One integral C2 clip holds the straight white V-A feed at enclosure-world
(11, 254.5, 261.6) mm. The logical run is `fluid-3`; its native continuation
is `above-core-va-fore-feed`. The existing route and fitting datums supply
the tube position.

The guide has a 6.35 mm CAD bore, 2.40 mm spring walls and an 8 mm span.
Its broad filled root reaches from the Z253.4 lid face to the tube axis,
8.2 mm above it. The minimum ordinary stock below the bore is 5.025 mm.
The 2.40 mm section belongs to the functional jaws.

Press the tube downward through the +Z mouth before final dressing of
`PE-carbonator` and `J4-V5`. Keep those leads clear of the loading approach.
Release the exposed jaws and lift the tube before separating back-top from
the core. The clip is part of the lid and adds no loose part or fastener;
existing ties retain their assigned quantities.

The [accepted C2 coupon](../../../../../future/snap-fit-tests/2026-10-26-mark2-combined/physical-result.json)
supplies this quarter-inch section's fit basis. The integrated lid's print
and installed service remain untested. Native receipts in this directory
apply to the geometry and artifacts named by their hashes.
