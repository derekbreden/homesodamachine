# Flavor-fill funnel and sliding frame

The removable silicone funnel holds nominally [531 mL](FUNNEL_CAP). Its PET-GF
frame slides into front-top on one pair of front rails. Closing the enclosure
captures the frame against withdrawal. Its rear passes between the upper Y-seam
screw blocks. The frame uses the enclosure's production rail section and its
running clearances. Both flavors share this filling interface.

The [lift-off cover](../funnel-cover/README.md) closes the mouth between fills.
Remove it before pouring or lifting the silicone out, and reseat it after filling.

## Silicone

The collar center is world X0, Y161.70. Its frame-bearing brim underside is
Z349. The mouth and cover seat are Z358, 3 mm above the rigid enclosure roof;
the fitted cover reaches Z361. The collar is
165 × 123.392 mm with R20 corners; the mouth is 153 × 111.392 mm with R14 corners;
the bearing brim is 179 × 137.392 mm with R27 aft corners and square front corners. The collar
wall and ramp's normal wall are 6 mm. The ramp falls to the outlet at the collar's centre,
X0, Y161.70.

The native cavity holds 530.54 mL to the mouth rim. The bearing brim's flat front edge, Y93.0, stands
one 0.25 mm running fit behind the display wall across its full width; its square front
corners meet the frame's two side strips. The drain exits at X0, Y161.70, Z306.05.

An integral **215 × 155.392 mm silicone apron** covers the exposed frame/roof
joints and upper seam screw wells. It reaches both exterior sides and runs
from Y87.0 to Y242.392. Overpour crosses this continuous silicone surface to
the exterior. A blunt underside rib contacts the rigid roof outside the frame
opening, opposing roof runoff beneath the apron. Its 1.5 mm contact band has
0.3 mm nominal seating interference; the 2.5 mm apron sheet above it is free
to bend. This is a local cast-silicone sealing section.

The [overflow review](overflow-review/README.md) records native coverage,
intended roof contact, mold fit and lift-out clearance. Water exclusion and
seating force have not been measured on a cast part.

The integral silicone plug is a [36 × 41.0 mm](FUNNEL_PLUG) rectangle with square corners,
centred on the outlet, 12.85 mm tall with its flat bottom at Z306.05 resting on the cradle's
hook tops.
Its walls run up into the bowl's underside, so none of its top shows. Its straight 6 mm outlet grips the 6.35 mm drain tube along the complete
5.015 mm insertion depth. The tube stays in the elbow when the silicone
funnel lifts out for cleaning.

The intended cleaning motion is lifting the whole silicone funnel out by hand.
Its straight bore slides off the raw 1/4-inch LLDPE drain stub, which stays standing in the drain
elbow's collet under the frame. To refit, press the plug into the frame's socket
until its flat bottom bears on the cradle's hook tops and the brim seats in the frame's roof recess.
Fit it after the enclosure halves and upper seam screws are closed. Smooth
the apron onto the roof with its underside rib seated, and lift it to wipe
both faces during cleaning.
The [two mold shells and straight steel rod](../funnel-mold/README.md) form the
complete block and cylindrical outlet.

## Elbow cradle

The PET-GF cradle holds the scanned [PP0308E elbow](/hardware/reference/jg-pp0308e-elbow/README.md)
under the frame's drain hole, its vertical leg up the hole and its horizontal leg aft toward V-B.
The pocket adds [0.15 mm](CRADLE_SLIP) radially to the scanned profile. Its forward lower
following surface is 302.779 mm². Beyond the largest horizontal section at local Y15.5,
the lower mouth holds that radius through the aft exit, leaving a full 3 mm floor.
The trailing 3.337 mm of the fixed-body span opens into this exit rather than following
the body's narrowing profile. The upward mouth carries the widest horizontal clearance
through the aft opening and joins the bend-core circle with a tangent boundary.
The elbow drops straight in. At the body's top, 1 mm under the top of the vertical leg's
root band, the circular pocket wraps 249.7° (69.4%) around the leg. The collar, rung and nose
stand free above it. The [drain stub](/hardware/reference/funnel-drain-stub/funnel_drain_stub.py)
stands in the elbow's upper collet and up into the plug's bore, and `fluid-4` leaves the elbow's
aft collet for V-B.

The body is [30.1 mm](CRADLE_WIDTH) wide. Both X sides carry
[1.3 mm](CRADLE_WING_T) wings along its full [31.8 mm](CRADLE_LENGTH) length,
[15.64 mm](CRADLE_WING_H) above the body, through the frame's slots. Each flat hook
reaches [3.1 mm](CRADLE_HOOK) outward, [2.6 mm](CRADLE_OVERLAP) past its slot's
outer edge. The hook tip has [0.15 mm](CRADLE_TIP_GAP) X air to the socket wall;
the receiver's R0.25 corners preserve at least 0.15 mm normal static air at the
hook ends. The slot keeps its 0.25 mm allowance beyond each wing end.

The installed cradle stands [0.65 mm](CRADLE_CATCH) below its insertion datum.
The hook undersides are at Z303.3, [0.40 mm](CRADLE_WEB_AIR) above the frame's
Z302.9 web top: 0.15 mm static allowance plus 0.25 mm for the supported hook
underside. Each hook is [2.75 mm](CRADLE_HOOK_T) thick, with its top at Z306.05
under the silicone plug's flat lower face. This finite rectangular snap hook
is a named local thickness feature; the cradle's body wall and frame web are
3 mm. The silicone plug, drain seal, elbow and installed joint poses retain
their specified dimensions.

Each hook passes its slot's outer edge with 0.25 mm air while the wing is pinched
inward. The source's tip-loaded cantilever model sizes a [3.74 mm](CRADLE_LANE)
inboard lane and estimates [2.67 mm](CRADLE_BEND) deflection over a 12.89 mm free
length, about [3.1%](CRADLE_STRAIN) nominal root strain. The remaining web from
the slot to the drain-hole edge is [4.39 mm](CRADLE_WEB). This model describes
nominal passage geometry; it does not establish insertion force or strain capacity.
The [cradle trial](cradle-trial/README.md) records accepted insertion for its
identified meshes; it does not establish this joint's loaded retention or lifetime.

The cradle prints on its bottom. The hooks' flat undersides take accessible
supports.
