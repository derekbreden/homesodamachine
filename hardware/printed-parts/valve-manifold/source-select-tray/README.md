# Source-select tray (4 valves + 2 dividers)

The [fluid-topology](../../../topology/fluid-topology.md) front end as a tray:
V-A and V-B merge at Y-A, Y-A bridges to Y-B, Y-B splits to V-C and V-D.

```
   V-A ┐                       ┌ V-C
       ├─ Y-A ==(bridge)== Y-B ─┤
   V-B ┘                       └ V-D
```

## Arrangement

The Y-divider ([reference](../../reference/y-divider/README.md), a McMaster
stand-in for the JG PP2308E) is a trident: one stem, two parallel outlets
14.7 mm apart. Origin = cell center; Z = 0 the valve mounting plane; ports at
Z = 11.3.

- The two dividers sit close, stems **2 mm** apart at the center bridge: **Y-A**
  flat at (−20.25, 0, 11.3), stem → +X, two outlets → −X at Y = ±7.35; **Y-B**
  mirrored.
- Each valve is rotated about Z so its **port axis points straight at the
  divider outlet** it feeds — a **straight 15 mm tube** spans the gap. The
  valves sit at **(±82.03, ±20.45)**, tilted ~17° off X (the minimum Y
  separation that keeps the four bodies clear).

The 6 bodies are clash-free.

## The tray

A frame plate (Z −3 → 6), **209 × 89 × 63 mm**, with four valve cradles —
single-cell sockets at each valve's rotated corners plus a port saddle along
each aim line — around a **central open gap** (X ±56, Y ±20) holding both
dividers (which dip to Z 3.2, below the Z=6 top) and the tubes. The valves seat
with 0 mm³ tray overlap; the dividers sit free in the gap.

Two **side walls** (±Y, full length) rise to Z = 60, clear of the 56.6 mm
valve-coil tops; a second tray's floor lands on the wall tops at a **63 mm
stack pitch**. The X-ends stay open for the ports.
