# Source-select tray (4 valves + 2 dividers)

The [fluid-topology](/hardware/topology/fluid-topology.md) front end as a tray:
V-A and V-B merge at Y-A, Y-A bridges to Y-B, Y-B splits to V-C and V-D.

```
   V-A ┐                       ┌ V-C
       ├─ Y-A ==(bridge)== Y-B ─┤
   V-B ┘                       └ V-D
```

## Arrangement

The Y-divider ([reference](/hardware/reference/y-divider/README.md), a McMaster
stand-in for the JG PP2308E) is a trident: one stem, two parallel outlets
[14.7](OUTLET_GAP) mm apart. Origin = cell center; Z = 0 the valve mounting plane; ports at
Z = [11.3](PORT_Z).

- The two dividers sit close, stems **[2](BRIDGE_GAP) mm** apart at the center bridge: **Y-A**
  flat at (−[20.25](DIV_X), 0, [11.3](PORT_Z)), stem → +X, two outlets → −X at Y = ±[7.35](OUTLET_Y); **Y-B**
  mirrored.
- Each valve is rotated about Z so its **port axis points straight at the
  divider outlet** it feeds — a **straight [15](TUBE) mm tube** spans the gap. The
  valves sit at **(±[82.03](SRC_VALVE_X), ±[20.45](SRC_VALVE_Y))**, tilted ~17° off X (the minimum Y
  separation that keeps the four bodies clear).

The 6 bodies are clash-free.

## The tray

A frame plate (Z [-3](TRAY_BOT_Z) → [6](TRAY_TOP_Z)), **[207](SRC_PLATE_W) × [89](SRC_PLATE_D) × [63](STACK_PITCH) mm**, with a **solid floor**. Each
valve drops into rotated corner sockets and a port saddle along its aim line;
each Y-divider sets into **three grooves** cut into the floor (the stem axis at
Y = 0 and the two outlets at Y = ±[7.35](OUTLET_Y)).

Two **side walls** (±Y, full length) rise to Z = [60](WALL_TOP_Z), clear of the [56.6](COIL_TOP) mm
valve-coil tops; a second tray's floor lands on the wall tops at a **[63](STACK_PITCH) mm
stack pitch**. The X-ends stay open for the ports.
