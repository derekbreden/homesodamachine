# Quad tray — source-selection cell (4 valves + 2 dividers)

The front end of the [fluid topology](../../../topology/fluid-topology.md),
as a real tray: **V-A** and **V-B** merge at **Y-A**, Y-A bridges to **Y-B**,
and Y-B splits to **V-C** and **V-D**.

```
   V-A ┐                         ┌ V-C
       ├─ Y-A ===(bridge)=== Y-B ─┤
   V-B ┘                         └ V-D
```

## How it's arranged

The Y-divider ([reference](../../reference/y-divider/README.md), a McMaster
stand-in for the JG PP2308E) is a **trident**: one stem, two **parallel**
outlets 14.7 mm apart. Because the outlets are parallel, the valves stay
**axis-aligned** — no fanning — and reach their divider with a short tube.

Origin = cell center; Z = 0 the valve mounting plane; ports at Z = 11.3.

- Four valves rotated so their ports run along **X**, at **(±75.5, ±18)**.
  The left pair's inner ports face +X toward Y-A; the right pair's face −X
  toward Y-B. Their outer ports (V-A/V-B inlets, V-C/V-D outlets) stick out
  the ends for tubing, as on the single tray.
- **Y-A** laid flat at (−21.75, 0, 11.3): stem → +X to the bridge, two
  outlets → −X at Y = ±7.35 to V-A / V-B. **Y-B** is the mirror.

## The tray

A frame plate (Z −3 → 6) with four valve cradles — the verified single-cell
sockets, plus one shared port saddle per Y-row (V-A/V-C at Y=+18, V-B/V-D at
Y=−18) — around a **central open gap** (X ±44, Y ±20) that clears both
dividers (they dip to Z 3.2, below the Z=6 top) and the bridge/branch tubes.
