# Quad tray — source-selection cell (4 valves + 2 dividers)

The [fluid-topology](../../../topology/fluid-topology.md) front end as a tray:
V-A and V-B merge at Y-A, Y-A bridges to Y-B, Y-B splits to V-C and V-D.

```
   V-A ┐                         ┌ V-C
       ├─ Y-A ===(bridge)=== Y-B ─┤
   V-B ┘                         └ V-D
```

## Arrangement

The Y-divider ([reference](../../reference/y-divider/README.md), a McMaster
stand-in for the JG PP2308E) is a trident: one stem, two parallel outlets
14.7 mm apart. Origin = cell center; Z = 0 the valve mounting plane; ports at
Z = 11.3.

- Four valves at **(±75.5, ±18)**, ports along X. The left pair's inner ports
  face +X to Y-A, the right pair's face −X to Y-B; the outer ports (V-A/V-B
  inlets, V-C/V-D outlets) stick out the ends for tubing.
- **Y-A** flat at (−21.75, 0, 11.3): stem → +X to the bridge, two outlets →
  −X at Y = ±7.35 to V-A / V-B. **Y-B** mirrored.

The 6 bodies are clash-free; each valve→divider tube run is ≈ 11.8 mm; the
Y-A↔Y-B bridge gap is 5 mm.

## The tray

A frame plate (Z −3 → 6), **189.6 × 80.2 × 63 mm**, with four valve cradles —
single-cell sockets plus one shared port saddle per Y-row (V-A/V-C at Y=+18,
V-B/V-D at Y=−18) — around a **central open gap** (X ±44, Y ±20) holding both
dividers (which dip to Z 3.2, below the Z=6 top) and the bridge/branch tubes.
The valves seat with 0 mm³ tray overlap; the dividers sit free in the gap.

Two **side walls** (±Y, full length) rise to Z = 60, clear of the 56.6 mm
valve-coil tops; a second tray's floor lands on the wall tops, 3.4 mm over the
coils, at a **63 mm stack pitch**. The X-ends stay open for the ports. Three
stacked: `../quad-stack/`.
