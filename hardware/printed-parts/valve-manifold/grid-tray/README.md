# Grid tray — naive 12-valve layout (first waggle)

The simplest thing that holds all 12 Beduan valves: a flat **2×6 grid**,
tiling the verified [single cell](../single-tray/README.md). This is a
**measurement, not a design** — it exists to expose the packing problem with
a real footprint, not to solve it. It is not topology-aware.

## What it reveals

The Beduan port is an **in-line through-line** sticking out ~29.5 mm from
each end of the body. So valves pack tightly across **X** (perpendicular to
the ports) but need a **Y pitch greater than the 59 mm port length** — the
ports of one row would otherwise run into the next. That asymmetry sets the
shape:

- **6 columns × 36 mm pitch** (bodies ~3.75 mm apart) → wide.
- **2 rows × 62 mm pitch** (ports of the two rows clear by ~3 mm) → deep.

Verified result:

- Tray slab: **219 (X) × 101 (Y) × 9 (Z)** mm.
- Valves + ports + coils envelope: **212 (X) × 121 (Y)** mm, top at **Z 56.6**.
- All 12 valves seat with **0 mm³** overlap with the tray.
- **0 mm³** collision across all 66 valve-pairs.

So a naive flat 12-pack is roughly **21 × 12 cm** of plan area. That is the
number to push against: it's almost certainly too wide for the enclosure,
which is the argument for folding/tiering the bank rather than laying it flat.

## What this is NOT (yet)

- Not mapped to the [topology](../../../topology/fluid-topology-manifold.mmd):
  the two rows are not the two flavor banks, and nothing here places the
  Y-junctions or routes between valves. Columns/rows are just a grid.
- No tiering (everything is one Z layer), no shared walls or lightening, no
  wiring/tube routing.
- Pitches (36, 62), wall (3), and the Z=6 rest plane are first-pass.

## Geometry

Each column has one continuous port saddle (both rows' colinear ports share
it); each valve has four corner-boss sockets blind to Z = 0. Cell radii and
the Z=6 top plane are imported from `single_tray` → the valve model, so the
whole stack tracks the Beduan.

Generated by `grid_tray.py` → `grid-tray.step`. Regenerate with
`tools/cad-venv/bin/python grid_tray.py`.
