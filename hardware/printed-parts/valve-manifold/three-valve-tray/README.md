# Three-valve tray

The manifold's wide cradle, **one off** — the
[two-valve tray](/hardware/printed-parts/valve-manifold/two-valve-tray/README.md)'s own
cell packing, one seat wider.

| Seat | Valve | What it gates |
|---|---|---|
| −X | V-J | nozzle B |
| centre | V-G | nozzle A |
| +X | V-K | tap-water fill, into the SeaFlo's suction |

The nozzle gates are the pair that meets at no junction, and their two outlets are the
only lines the manifold sends OUT of the machine. **V-K** rides the third seat, one more
of the same Beduan solenoid. The band the plate stands in reaches x 109 — the SeaFlo's
aft end is its pressure switch, 44 mm across — against this plate's [106.8](PLATE_X).

## Geometry

Three of [`single-tray`](/hardware/printed-parts/valve-manifold/single-tray/README.md)'s
cells on one floor plate, cut by that module's own `cut_cell`, at the two-valve tray's
own seat pitch — both read rather than restated, so a seat here cannot drift from a seat
there.

- Plate [106.8](PLATE_X) (X) × [40](PLATE_Y) (Y) × [9](PLATE_Z) (Z), spanning **Z [-3](TRAY_BOT_Z) → [6](TRAY_TOP_Z)**.
- Seats at X = 0 and ±[34.25](SEAT_X), so neighbouring centers stand [34.25](PITCH) apart —
  the valve's own declared X keep-out, its footprint plus a pad per side.
- Ports run along Y at Z = [11.3](PORT_Z). The port reaches ±[29.5](PORT_HALF) from its valve
  center, so [9.5](COLLET_PROUD) mm of quick-connect collet stands proud of the plate at each
  end, clear for the tube.
- The valve's round boss bottoms on the plate's top face at Z = [6](TRAY_TOP_Z) and carries
  the vertical load; its coil tops out at [56.6](COIL_TOP), and nothing on the tray reaches
  that high.
- FOUR MOUNT EARS — the two-valve tray's own tongue and ⌀[3.4](MOUNT_HOLE_D) M3 hole at its own
  Y = ±[24.75](EAR_Y), at this plate's stations: X = ±[17.12](EAR_X), midway BETWEEN the cells,
  because a three-seat plate has no open centreline — every cell's port tube runs its
  full Y. Tube, posts and spades stand half a cell away by construction. The matching
  bosses live in the foam cap's deck-mount table.

Each cell is symmetric under a half turn about Z, so a valve seats either way round: the
tray locates a valve and never fixes which end of its port is the inlet.

## Boundary

`port_collets()` is the whole boundary — six bare collet tips, keyed seat-then-end by
sign (`xn-yp` is the −X seat's +Y collet, `xc-yn` the middle seat's −Y one). Nothing is
turned onto any of them.

No fitting seats on this tray: no groove, no wall, no boss reaching a divider or a tee.

## Open

- **Nothing holds the valve down.** Sockets, saddle and boss locate it and carry it;
  lifting it out takes no tool. What stands over this plate is the drip basin's rails,
  `_contents.aft_tray_headroom` clear of the coils.
- **This tray stands on the water deck**, aft in the strip west of the SeaFlo, plate
  underside on the foam cap, outlets facing the rear panel's own flavor bulkheads —
  bolted down through its four ears to the cap's own deck-mount columns.
  [`enclosure/README.md`](/hardware/printed-parts/enclosure/README.md) has the pack.
- **V-K's own line is not routed.** The pump's suction barb faces WEST at this plate, and
  the slot between the barb tip and V-K's valve keep-out is 5.25 mm against the 8.35 a 1/4"
  line stands in. The stand is on the −X wall and the plate is [106.8](PLATE_X) long, so
  that slot is what the two leave each other. `_lines.BLOCKED` carries the measurement.
