# Bag-circuit tray (4 valves + 2 dividers)

The [fluid-topology](../../../topology/fluid-topology.md) bag circuit as a
tray: V-F and V-E share Y-E (with Bag A), V-I and V-H share Y-H (with Bag B).

```
   V-F ┐        ┌ V-E
       ├  Y-E  ─┤
       ┊        ┊
       ├  Y-H  ─┤
   V-I ┘        └ V-H
```

## Arrangement

The valves sit **ports-along-X with no aiming tilt**, butted in two columns —
V-F over V-I on the −X side, V-E over V-H on the +X side. The two Y-dividers
([reference](../../reference/y-divider/README.md)) sit **side by side in the
center**, long axis along X, stems toward −X, outlets toward +X. Because the
dividers are parallel rather than in series, the gap between the two valve
columns spans a **single divider length**, packing the valves to X = ±48.75.

Each −X valve's inner port meets its divider's stem; the +X valve and the bag
take the divider's two +X outlets. The pump-side and bag runs leave the tray.

Origin = cell center, Z = 0 the valve mounting plane, ports at Z = 11.3. The
six bodies are clash-free.

## The tray

A frame plate (Z −3 → 6), **136 × 72 × 63 mm**, with four valve cradles (four
corner sockets + a shared per-row port saddle along X) around a **central open
gap** (X ±24, Y ±32) holding both dividers. Two **side walls** (±Y) rise to
Z = 60 for a **63 mm stack pitch**; the X-ends stay open for the ports.
