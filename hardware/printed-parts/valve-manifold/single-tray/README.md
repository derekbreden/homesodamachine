# Single-Beduan cradle tray

A cradle for **one** Beduan solenoid valve (the part modeled in
[`reference/beduan-solenoid`](/hardware/reference/beduan-solenoid/README.md)).
This is one manifold cell, and `cut_cell` is the cut that makes it.

**The cut is what ships, not the plate.** `cut_cell` is imported by
[`foam_cap.py`](/hardware/printed-parts/cold-core/foam-cap/foam_cap.py), which
prints this cell straight into the cold core's top lid at each valve that stands
on that face (`_cold_core_interface.cap_cradles`) — a pad off the lid's outer
face with this cell's saddle and four sockets in it. So the valve presses into
the core itself and no plate stands between them. The two plates in this family
— [single-valve](/hardware/printed-parts/valve-manifold/single-valve-tray/README.md)
and [two-valve](/hardware/printed-parts/valve-manifold/two-valve-tray/README.md)
— are the same cell on a floor that bolts down instead; neither is placed in the
machine, and none of the three is in the BOM.

The valve seats three ways at once:

- **Four corner posts → four sockets.** ⌀[6.8](POST_DIA) posts seat in ⌀[7.2](SOCKET_DIA) blind
  sockets that bottom at Z = [-1](SOCKET_FLOOR_Z), the posts hanging free. Locates X-Y.
- **Port → lengthwise saddle.** The ⌀[15](SOLENOID_PORT_DIA) port nestles into a ⌀[15.4](SADDLE_DIA) trough
  along Y ([0.2 mm](SADDLE_CLEAR) clearance), cut from the port's underside up through the
  tray top.
- **Round boss → tray top.** The body's round boss bottoms on the tray's
  top face at Z = [6](TRAY_TOP_Z) (the plane where the boss begins, just above the
  posts-only band). Carries the vertical load.

The socket positions, saddle radius, and the Z = [6](TRAY_TOP_Z) top plane are all
**imported from the valve model** (`beduan_solenoid.py`), so they track the
valve as it changes rather than drifting.

## Geometry

- Tray block: [38.25](BLOCK_X) (X) × [40](BLOCK_Y) (Y) × [9](BLOCK_Z) (Z), spanning **Z [-3](TRAY_BOT_Z) → [6](TRAY_TOP_Z)**.
- Top face at Z = [6](TRAY_TOP_Z); sockets bottom at Z = [-1](SOCKET_FLOOR_Z) over a [2 mm](FLOOR_UNDER_SOCKET) floor.
- Saddle runs Y ±[20](SADDLE_REACH) — short of the ⌀[15](SOLENOID_PORT_DIA) port's ±[29.5](PORT_HALF) ends, so the
  quick-connect collets stay clear for tubing.

## Open

- Saddle clearance ([0.2 mm](SADDLE_CLEAR) radial), wall ([3 mm](WALL)), and floor ([3 mm](FLOOR)) are guesses.
- Saddle Y-reach (±[20](SADDLE_REACH)) trades port support against collet access.
- Whether the round boss should bear on the tray top, or the posts +
  saddle alone should locate and support the valve.
