Controller · step 27

Store the settings

Same cable, same socket. Open the serial console at 115200 baud and type these. They persist in NVS, so you do this once.

# the console is serviced only while the table is stopped
status       read back everything it currently holds
mode jog     the pedal follows your foot, for setup
direction cw tube direction, viewed from above
speed 8.0    bead travel, mm/s. 5 to 15 accepted
overlap 20   degrees past 360 the lap runs
status       read it back again and check every line

The rest of the vocabulary

mode lapThe weld mode. Hold for a counted 380°.
direction ccwThe other way.
dirinvert onFlips which way cw means. Issued once, on page 40, if the table turns the wrong way.
defaultsBack to 8.0 mm/s, 20°, lap, cw.
Commands are taken only while stopped

Deliberately. Serial formatting and a flash write both take time, and neither may stretch the interval between two weld-step pulses. The pedal is the live stop control, not the console.

Why 8.0

It sits in the middle of the useful window, runs the table at 1.235 rpm, and completes a 380° lap in 51.3 s. Page 47 has the whole table.

Gate — status reads back what you typed

Mode jog, speed 8.0, overlap 20, direction cw. If a line did not take, the table was not stopped when you sent it — send it again.
