What the controller is

Two supplies that never meet, one dry-contact pedal, and an ESP32 that counts pulses. Read this page before you strip a single wire.

supply signal motor phase
The two supplies never meet

The 5 V adapter powers the ESP32 and both 5 V signal loops. The 24 V brick powers only the DM542T's power stage and the motor. They stay separate across the driver's own opto-isolators. Never connect 24 V to the ESP32.

The pedal is not a power switch

It carries 3.3 V through a 4.7 kΩ pull-up and nothing else. Opening the contact — releasing it, or pulling a lead out — always stops new pulses. The controller also refuses to move at boot until it has seen the pedal released once.
