Coral is the lever pushed to the ON end of its travel. Check the silkscreen on your own driver for which end that is — it is marked.

What those eight mean

SW1–SW4Peak current, 3.76 A
SW5Standstill current, 50%
SW6–SW8Microstepping, 3,200 pulses/rev

3.76 A is the DM542T table's closest setting to the manual's phase current × 1.4 peak rule for a 2.8 A/phase motor. The standstill reduction limits motor heating while you are setting the welder up.

Also on the driver

  • Find the signal-voltage selector and set it to 5 V. It is separate from the eight DIP switches. Get this wrong and the ULN2803A's sunk outputs will not register as pulses.
  • Leave ENA+ and ENA- unconnected. The driver is enabled by default.
3,200 is load-bearing

The firmware's pulse counts assume it: 3,200 × 4.5 = 14,400 pulses per table revolution, and a 380° lap is 15,200 of them. Change SW6–SW8 and every lap is the wrong length.
