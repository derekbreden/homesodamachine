Bearing · step 10

The spool, up from below

The spool does not carry the running load. It catches lift, and it keeps the Ø90 mm passage continuous through the base.

  1. Turn the fixture on its side, or get under it — the feet leave 24 mm, which is enough for a driver but not for a hand and a driver.
  2. Pass the spool hub up through the base's central bore from below, flange down.
  3. Turn it until its three flange holes find the platter's three inner-circle inserts at 60°, 180° and 300°. Turning the platter is easier than turning the spool.
  4. Start all three M3 × 25 before tightening any, then bring them up evenly at 0.5 N·m.
  5. Set the fixture back on its feet and look at the flange rim.
Gate — the flange floats

The flange rim sits inside the base's recess, about 1 mm clear of the base shoulder, and nothing projects below the base. Turn the platter a revolution: it must still be free. If the spool has taken up the running load, the balls are now unloaded and the axis is a flexible hub — back the three screws off and re-seat.
