Every row in the first table beeps, or reads its 3.3 kΩ, and every row in the second does not. The 24 V rail must have no path to the ESP32 at all. The isolation lives in the DM542T's input opto-couplers, and the only way it can be defeated is a wire in the wrong terminal.

Also check with your eyes

The DM542T's logic selector is on 5 V, its eight DIP switches read ON OFF OFF OFF ON ON OFF ON, and the ULN's COM is empty.
