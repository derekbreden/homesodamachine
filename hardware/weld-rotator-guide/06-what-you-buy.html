Two rows worth checking twice

The 20-tooth pulley went missing once and was reordered; make sure the one in your hand is the 15 mm-belt, 6.35 mm-bore part. And the copper bar is 1/4 × 2 inch, not 1/8 — the shoe's thickness is what sets its preload.
