Controller · step 28

Move to bench power

Settings stored. Now the board comes off the laptop and onto the 5 V adapter, and the 24 V brick goes in for the first time.

  1. Close the serial console.
  2. Unplug the Micro-USB from the laptop end.
  3. Fit the Micro-USB tip to the universal 5 V / 3 A adapter and plug it into the ESP32's same Micro-USB socket.
  4. Plug the 5 V adapter into the wall. The board's power LED comes up.
  5. Confirm the pedal is released and that nothing — hand, cloth, cable — is inside the belt path or on the turntable.
  6. Plug the 24 V brick into the wall. The DM542T's LED comes up and the motor becomes stiff to turn by hand: that is the standstill current, and it is correct.
Gate — nothing moves

With both supplies live and the pedal released, the table stands still and stays still. Any motion at all here means a pulse is being generated with no pedal — pull the 24 V and go back to page 36.

Order matters, both ways

5 V first, then 24 V. Coming down, 24 V first, then 5 V. The driver's inputs should never see a floating logic side with its power stage live.

The console is still there

The onboard CP2102 drives RX0/TX0 whenever the board has USB power, so nothing external is ever wired to those pins. To change a setting later, swap the tip back for the laptop cable.
