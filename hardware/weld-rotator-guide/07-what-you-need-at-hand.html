Before the bench · tools

What you need at hand

Three hex sizes cover the whole fixture. Everything else on this page is either for the inserts, the copper, or the metering.

ToolFor
2.5 mm hexEvery M3 in the fixture — 30 of them.
4 mm hexThe six M5 × 10 socket-head screws, tower and ground tower.
3 mm hexThe two M5 × 20 countersunk motor screws. DIN 7991 does not share the cap-screw socket.
A slim-shank 2.5 mm bit or a plain L-keyThe three nest retainers. Their Ø6.2 mm access wells are narrower than a 1/4 inch bit body.
HOTO PixelDrive + M3 and M5 insert tipsAll 37 heat-set inserts. Its 0.5 N·m floor is the point — see page 12.
Soldering iron with M3 / M5 insert tipsPressing the inserts themselves.
0.25 mm / 0.010 inch feeler bladeGauging the motor pulley off the face pilot.
Digital calliperSorting the 36 balls, and measuring the pulley's actual overall length.
Multimeter with continuity beepMetering the harness before power, and the shoe-to-tube contact.
Stopwatch or a phoneTiming the dry revolutions at 5, 8 and 15 mm/s.
BA4555 bandsaw, 24 TPI M42 bladeThe single 25 mm crosscut in the copper bar.
Plastic scraperDeburring both bearing races. Nothing abrasive.
A 25.4 mm round bar or socketThe service-envelope probe on page 18.
Two blocks about 25 mm tallStanding the carriage skin-up while the motor goes on.
Laptop with PlatformIO, and a Micro-USB data cableFlashing and setting the controller, pages 37–39.
Paper index tape, markerThe direction check and the lap check.
The one thing you will want and not have

The nest retainers. A normal 1/4 inch hex bit will not enter their access well. Find the slim bit or the L-key before the nest is on the turntable, not after.
