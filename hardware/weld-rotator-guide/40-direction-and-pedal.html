Commissioning · gate 1

Direction, and the pedal

No tube. Two readings: that cw means clockwise from above, and that the pedal is a real deadman.

  1. Stick a strip of paper tape on the turntable rim as an index mark.
  2. In mode jog and direction cw, tap the pedal and watch the mark from above.
  3. If it moved counterclockwise, send dirinvert on once, then tap again and confirm it is now clockwise.
  4. Power the controller down and back up (5 V last off, 5 V first on).
  5. Hold the pedal down while it boots. The table must not move.
  6. Release the pedal. Press it. The table turns.
  7. While it is turning, unplug one pedal lead. The table must stop immediately.
  8. Plug the lead back in and confirm it jogs again.
Gate — four things, all of them

Clockwise from above means clockwise. Held at boot, the table stays still. Released then pressed, it turns. A pulled lead stops it. Do not set the wire's lead/trail orientation, or go near a tube, until the direction convention is true.

Once only

dirinvert is issued once and stored. If you find yourself sending it a second time, something else changed — check the motor's A and B pairs before you invert again.
