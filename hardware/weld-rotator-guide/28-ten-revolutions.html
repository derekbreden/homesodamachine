Drive · step 17

Ten revolutions, by hand

The last thing before the nest goes on. Nothing here needs power. You are running the bearing in and looking for the one fault that is cheap now and expensive later.

  1. Turn the turntable slowly through ten full revolutions clockwise, watching the belt at the small pulley.
  2. Turn it ten more counterclockwise.
  3. Lift the platter off, wipe the lower race and the cage with a dry cloth, and look at what came out. A little PET-GF dust is expected on a first run-in.
  4. Put it back on, level, and turn it another two revolutions.
  5. Look at the spool flange from underneath as it turns.
Gate — three readings at once

The belt stays between both flanges for all twenty revolutions. The race does not bind at any angle. The spool flange stays unloaded — it should never touch the base shoulder while turning. Any one of the three failing stops the build here.
