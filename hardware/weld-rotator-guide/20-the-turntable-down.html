Bearing · step 9

The turntable down

Straight down onto the loaded race, level, with the ring's groove finding the balls all the way round at once.

  1. Pick the turntable up by its rim with both hands, ring downward.
  2. Bring it over the base and align the ring's circle with the ball circle by eye.
  3. Lower it level and let it settle on its own weight. Do not press.
  4. Look round the joint. The ring's groove should sit on the balls with an even gap to the base all the way round.
  5. Turn the platter slowly by hand, one complete revolution, then back.
Gate — it turns free and quiet

One revolution each way with a fingertip's push and no binding, no grinding, no catching at any one angle. A regular catch once per revolution is a ball, a proud screw, or a burr in a groove — lift the platter and find it.
