The wiring

Every conductor in the controller, once. Ferrule the ends, sleeve the pedal lead in 1/4 inch braid, and leave both supplies unplugged until page 36 passes.

Making each joint

  1. Strip 22 AWG to the ferrule's barrel length, crimp a ferrule on every end that lands in a screw terminal, and heat-shrink the transition.
  2. Twist the pedal's COM and NO conductors together along their whole run and sleeve them in 1/4 inch braid.
  3. Fit the 3.3 kΩ resistor at the controller end, not the pedal end.
  4. Leave the 5 V adapter and the 24 V brick unplugged. They do not go in until page 36 has passed and page 39 says so.

That is the point of the external pull-up. It also drops the input impedance enough to reject the noise a laser welder puts on a long bench lead.

The pull-up is a banded resistor

One from the Chanzon 3.3 kΩ pack: a small blue cylinder banded orange, orange, black, brown, brown. The pedal's WAGO holds GPIO27, one of its leads and NO, and nothing else; the other lead meets 3V3 in its own WAGO.
