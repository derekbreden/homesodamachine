Bearing · step 7

The race ring, groove down

The ring printed groove-up, because a ball's running surface should be a first-layer flat. It bolts on groove-down.

  1. Turn the turntable underside up.
  2. Sit the race ring on it with the grooved face upward in this position — which means downward, toward the balls, when the fixture is the right way up.
  3. Line the three screw holes up with the three outer-circle inserts at 30°, 150° and 270°.
  4. Drive three flush M3 × 8 by hand or at 0.5 N·m.
  5. Run a fingertip round the groove over each screw.
Gate — nothing proud in the groove

No screw head stands above the ring's groove face. A proud head is a ball riding over a bump once per revolution, and that shows up as a periodic wobble in the indicator later that no adjuster screw will remove.

Plastic only

If the groove needs deburring, a plastic scraper. Abrasive changes the groove radius, and the groove radius is the bearing.
