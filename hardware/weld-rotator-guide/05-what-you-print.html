Before the bench · parts

What you print

Fourteen shapes, all Polymaker Fiberon PET-GF15 black through the 0.4 mm nozzle. Their STEP files are in hardware/printed-parts/fixtures/weld-rotator/.

FileQtyWhat it is
pulley-coupon1Twelve grooves and both flanges. Printed and checked first, before the turntable.
base1The 300 × 250 × 12 mm bed. Lower ball race, bench-clamp holes, tower stations.
base-foot4Holds the base 24 mm off the bench so the lower ports stay reachable.
turntable-90t1The moving disk, with the 90-tooth pulley and the nest register on it.
race-ring1Upper bearing race. Printed groove-up, screwed on groove-down.
spool1Hub and lift-catch flange, through the base from below.
ball-cage1Spaces the 36 balls. Carries no load.
tube-nest1The replaceable register the tube end lands in. The only tube-size-specific part.
motor-tower1Two rails and a rear wall, all outside the belt's swept path.
motor-carriage1The sliding motor mount. Belt tension is its four slotted screws.
motor-clamp-pad2Load spreaders under the carriage's four side screws.
ground-tower1Stationary support for the copper contact.
ground-arm1The 5.0 mm leaf spring that holds the shoe on the tube. Replaceable.

In this order

  1. The coupon, and check the delivered belt against it. Page 8.
  2. The base. Nine hours; watch its first two layers. Page 9.
  3. The turntable and the race ring, once the coupon has passed. Page 10.
  4. Everything else, in any order. Page 11.
The fourteenth is not printed

ground-shoe is one 25 mm crosscut from the acquired 1/4 × 2 inch C110 copper bar. Its STEP is there so the reference assembly closes; nothing prints it and nothing drills it.

One part is tube-specific

Only the tube-nest. A fit correction, or a later tube size, costs that one 150 mm print and leaves the base, the bearing, the pulley and the motor mount alone.
