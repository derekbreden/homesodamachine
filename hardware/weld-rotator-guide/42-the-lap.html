Commissioning · gate 3

The lap

Nothing counts a lap. The table turns while the pedal is held, and a lap — one full turn and about 20° past the first tack — is judged at the index mark. The console's degrees are a readout, never a stop.

  1. Send status and check the speed.
  2. Tap the pedal until the paper index mark lines up with a fixed reference. Mark that reference too, so you have a pair.
  3. Press and hold the pedal. Keep holding through more than one full turn.
  4. Watch the mark come round past the reference and keep going.
  5. Release about 20° past the reference, which is about 33 mm round a 190 mm-diameter rim.
  6. Read the degrees the console reports at the release and compare them with where the mark stopped.
Gate — one steady pass

The table does not stop, pause or change speed as the mark comes round, and the console's degrees agree with the mark within one pass. A table that stops on its own mid-turn fails the drive.

Your foot ends the bead

The controller never takes the table away mid-bead. Release the pedal to stop; the console's degrees check the judgement at the index mark and do not make it.
