Commissioning · gate 3

The lap

The counted move. 15,200 pulses, which is 380 degrees, which is one full turn plus a 20-degree overlap onto the start of the bead.

  1. Send mode lap. Confirm with status.
  2. Turn the table by hand so the paper index mark lines up with a fixed reference. Mark that reference too, so you have a pair.
  3. Press and hold the pedal. Keep holding.
  4. Watch the mark go round once and keep going.
  5. When motion stops, keep holding the pedal for another few seconds.
  6. Look at where the index mark finished.
  7. Release the pedal. Press it again.
  8. Reset the mark, start a second lap, and this time release the pedal halfway round.
Gate — three behaviours

The mark finishes 20° past its start, which is about 33 mm round a 190 mm-diameter rim. Motion stops on the count even with the pedal still down, and does not restart until the pedal has been released and pressed again. An early release aborts immediately. A lap that repeats while the pedal is held is the one failure that would put a second bead on top of a finished weld.

The count, not the clock

The lap ends at 15,200 pulses. That figure is independent of any elapsed-time error, which is why the overlap is exactly 20° every time and not approximately 20°.
