The whole path

Six phases. Nearly three days of them are the printer running unattended, and the two long plates are only started after a two-hour plate has proved the fits they depend on.

  1. Print the witnesses and check the fits. Two hours buys you the right to start a 37-hour plate.
  2. Print the cavity, then the core. Clear every air passage as each comes off.
  3. Dry-assemble. Full stroke, real rod, before any coating.
  4. Prove the finishing process on the coupon. Then apply it to the two forming faces.
  5. Fit the steel. Four nuts, four washers, four screws.
  6. Dry-run the empty mold in the chamber.
