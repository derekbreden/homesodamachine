You have a tool

Two coated halves, four steel stations, one ground rod, and a coupon with numbers written on it. The mold is reusable; from here on the work is casting, and that is the other document.

What this guide does not decide

These are open in the source, not answered here. Each one is a thing you will meet at the bench, and each one is worth writing down once you have settled it.

  • Which coating. Two candidates in the shop, one purpose-made alternative not owned. The coupon trial decides it; nothing else can.
  • How many coats, and what grit. "Lightly abrade" and "sand and polish" are the whole specification. Your coupon numbers are the answer.
  • The holding arrangement. Named four times in the source, specified nowhere.
  • The catch tray. Required under the mold, no object named.
  • How the mask is made — tape, liquid mask, plugs in the bores.
Keep the coupon Do not throw the finish witness away when the mold is built. It is the record of what your process produced, and the reference when you re-coat a worn face later.
