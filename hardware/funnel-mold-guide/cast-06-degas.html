Degas in the cup

Degas the mixed silicone in its own container, before it goes anywhere near the mold. It needs room to climb that the mold does not have.

  1. Cup into the chamber, lid on, pump on. Watch it through the glass.
  2. If it climbs toward the rim, bleed a little air to knock it back, then pull again. Silicone over the rim is silicone in your chamber.
  3. Hold until the surface is flat and stays flat, then admit air gently and take it out.
Done when The mix has risen, collapsed, and stopped moving under vacuum.
