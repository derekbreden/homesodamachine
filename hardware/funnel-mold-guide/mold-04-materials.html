What the mold is made of

Everything here is in the shop except the jack screws, which are on order. Check them off before you dry filament — the finishing coat is the only line with an open decision on it, and page 15 is where you close it.

ItemSpecQtyWhere
PETG filament Bambu PETG Translucent, 1.75 mm. Dry it: 65 °C for 8 h minimum. ~3 kg owned
Jack screws M5 × 0.8 × 50 mm fully threaded socket head, 12.9 alloy. The 4 mm hex key in the pack is the tool you demold with. 4 on order
Square nuts M5 × 0.8, 8 mm square × 4 mm thick, stainless 4 owned
Fender washers M5, 25 mm OD steel. The pocket takes 0.8–2.0 mm thickness — measure your stock. 4 owned
Spout rod Ground steel dowel, 6.35 × 50.8 mm (¼″ × 2″), ground to 0.0001″–0.0005″. This is the funnel's whole bore. 1 owned
Finishing coat Candidates in the shop: Krylon Crystal Clear acrylic (gloss) and Craft Resin crystal-clear epoxy. XTC-3D is the purpose-made alternative and is not owned. 1 choose
Release agent Mann Ease Release 200, addition-cure compatible. A release film, not a silicone fluid. 1 owned
⛔ Both large plates outrun a spool The cavity is 1,271 g and the core 1,016 g, on 1 kg spools. Arrange automatic spool backup, or plan a supervised runout change, before you start either plate. The saved project does not configure the printer's backup spools for you.
