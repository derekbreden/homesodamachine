Before the next one

  • Both forming faces washed clean of cured silicone and re-released.
  • All six ports and every backing-air channel clear again.
  • Washers checked for a scored bearing track; replace a deeply scored one.
  • Screw tips still flat and burr-free.
  • Rod's ground surface unmarked.
  • Coating inspected on both faces — re-sand, re-seal and re-release as the film wears.
