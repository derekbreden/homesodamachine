Find zero

Before anything lifts, all four screws have to start from the same place. That place is the moment each tip just touches its washer.

  1. Take the holding weight and board off.
  2. Turn each screw down by hand until its tip just contacts its washer. By feel, with the hex key — you are looking for the first change in resistance, not for tightness.
  3. That is zero-lift. All four now, before any of them go further.
⛔ The hand key, only A powered driver will overload a printed ear long before a stuck casting lets go. The hex key that came with the screws is the tool, because resistance is the instrument you are reading.
Done when All four screw tips are in contact with their washers and none has lifted the core.
