Release, then the rod

Two faces get release, every single pour. It is a film, not a lubricant, and it wears — a thin patch is where a spout wall tears.

  1. Release the cavity's bowl. Even coverage right into the spout pocket and up the corners. Let it flash off.
  2. Release the core's plug and the brim recess in the plate's underside — the whole of the coated face.
  3. Keep it off everything else. Nuts, guides, washer seats, parting lands, and the inside of the rod socket. Overspray in a guide is a bound guide.
  4. Seat the rod in the core last, by hand, until it bottoms. 22.0 mm stands out below the plug.
Where the rod ends up With the core seated, the rod's tip stops 2 mm above the pocket floor. That 2 mm of silicone closes over the end — it is why the demolded spout looks blind, and why you cannot judge the bore before you trim.
Done when Both forming faces carry an even release film, nothing else does, and the rod is bottomed in a dry socket.
