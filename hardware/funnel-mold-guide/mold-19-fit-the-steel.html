Fit the four jacks

One station repeats at each side of the mold. A screw threads through a square nut captured in the core's arm and rests its tip on a steel washer sitting in the cavity. Turning it down cannot push the washer anywhere, so the core goes up instead.

  1. Slide one square nut into each core arm from its outboard edge, into the side-entry slot.
  2. Pass a screw down through the arm and into the nut to retain it. The nut is captured by the screw, not pressed in — it is meant to be a little loose until then.
  3. Lay one washer flat in each cavity pocket. The screw tip lands on the solid ring, not the hole; the axis is deliberately off-centre.
  4. Back all four screws out until their tips clear the washers with the core seated.
Keep the screw ends flat A burr on a screw tip ploughs the washer, and a deeply scored washer gets replaced rather than reused. Check the four tips before they ever touch steel.
Done when Four nuts captured, four washers flat in their pockets, four screws backed clear, and the core still seats fully with all four fitted.
