Before the clock3

Set the bench

Everything below is in reach and ready before either tin is opened. Once the two parts are together you cannot leave the bench.

On the bench

  • The mold, coated and dry, cavity open and level, core beside it.
  • The ground rod, clean, checked to bottom at 28.8 mm.
  • Four square nuts in the core arms, retained by their screws, screws backed out so the tips clear the washers.
  • Four washers flat in the cavity pockets.
  • The holding arrangement for the core — the board and weight you settled on the dry run.
  • A catch tray under the mold, wider than 270 mm, for overflow and foam.
  • Release agent — Ease Release 200.
  • Scale reading to 0.1 g, one 1000 mL mixing cup, stir stick, nitrile gloves.
  • Silicone A and B, and the black pigment.

In the chamber

  • Chamber clear, gauge readable, shutoff valve to hand, pump warm and connected.
  • A flat shelf the cavity can stand on with its backing-air exits still open.
  • Room, with the lid closed, for the mold and the tray and the holding board — you proved this on the dry run.
⚠ Gloves, and one pump at a time Nitrile gloves for both the release and the silicone. The vacuum pump is shared with the refrigerant work — make sure it is not mid-evacuation somewhere else before you mix.
Done when Every box above is ticked and you could complete the pour without walking away from the bench.
