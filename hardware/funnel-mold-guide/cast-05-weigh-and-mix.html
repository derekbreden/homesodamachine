Weigh and mix

The clock starts when A and B meet, and it runs for 30 minutes at 23 °C. From here to the closed, degassed, topped-up mold is one continuous run inside that.

WhatHow muchWhy that
Part A76.5 g1 : 1 by weight, not by volume. 153 g is the cast including its sacrificial tip.
Part B76.5 g
Black pigment≤ 2 %, about 1.5 g Platinum-cure pigment only. Black hides concentrate staining in service.
Mixing cup1000 mL Sized for the rise in the chamber, not the pour — expect 3–4×.
  1. Tare, weigh A, tare, weigh B. To 0.1 g. A platinum system that is off ratio does not cure soft — it cures wrong, or not at all, in patches.
  2. Pigment into one part first and disperse it there before the two meet. Chasing pigment streaks in the mixed batch spends working time you need later.
  3. Mix slowly and completely. Scrape the cup wall and the bottom corner twice. Unmixed material on the wall is the tacky patch you find on the demolded funnel.
  4. Do not hurry this to save air. The chamber removes the air. It cannot remove an unmixed streak.
⚠ Keep the cure clean Platinum silicone is fussy about what it touches before it gels. Fresh cup, fresh stick, clean gloves, and keep sulphur-bearing rubber, tin-cure silicone and solvent rags away from the bench.
Done when One uniform, single-coloured batch of about 153 g, with no streaks on the cup wall.
