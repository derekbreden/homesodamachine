What 0.20 mm buys

Both forming faces are printed away from the funnel's real size. The cavity is grown 0.20 mm everywhere; the core's plug is shrunk by the same. Finishing puts that thickness back as coating. The mold is only the right size once it is coated.

The arithmetic that catches people A 0.20 mm film on both walls of a channel closes it by 0.40 mm; on both sides of a boss it opens it by 0.40 mm. Record the cavity's collar width and the core's width at matching stations before you coat, and you can read the film directly afterwards.
