Ratings and safety

What this
machine is

Electrical
Supply120 V, 60 Hz only. Not for 240 V.
Draw5 A, 600 W
ConnectionGrounded cord to a standard household outlet. The carbonator, the compressor and the faucet's under-counter plate are bonded to that ground.
Refrigerant
TypeR-600a (isobutane) — a flammable refrigerant
ChargeUnder 40 g, sealed at the factory. The limit for appliances of this class is 150 g.
ServiceThe refrigeration circuit is sealed. It is not a home-service item and it must not be opened, punctured or heated.
Water
SupplyCold potable mains, through the supplied filter
BackflowA backflow preventer inside the appliance keeps carbonated water out of your household supply. It is sealed into the plumbing, with nothing on it to set or adjust.

Sold direct rather than through retail, this appliance carries no UL or ETL listing mark and none is claimed. It is designed to UL 60335-2-89 for hydrocarbon refrigerant handling, and its refrigerant is approved under EPA SNAP for this class of appliance.

Mind this

Do not run the appliance in a sealed cabinet with no gap at its sides. It moves air across itself to stay cold, and it needs somewhere to take it from and somewhere to put it.
