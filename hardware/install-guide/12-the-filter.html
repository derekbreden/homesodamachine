In the cabinet

The filter

A 0.01 micron cartridge, already in the middle of the white run. It arrives connected at both ends and the right way round — you never choose its direction.

It is about 2½ inches across and a foot long, and it lives on your side of the appliance's wall rather than inside it, the way a fridge line's filter does. That is deliberate: it is longer than the appliance is wide, and once a year you will want to change it without opening anything.

Where to put it

Lay it wherever the run reaches comfortably — along the back of the cabinet or down one side — clear of the disposal and clear of anything that gets pulled in and out. The tube is stiff enough to hold it wherever you set it down. Two rules only:

  • No tight bends in the white tube on either side of it. A kinked tube is a closed tube.
  • Leave it where a hand can reach both of its ends, because in a year one hand is all it takes to swap.

Changing it

Once a year. Close the cold-water valve, press each collet with the collet press, pull the cartridge out, push the new one in. No tools beyond the little printed one in your kit.
