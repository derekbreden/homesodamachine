In the cabinet

The cylinder

Your 5 lb cylinder stands on the cabinet floor beside the appliance, where you can see the connection and reach the valve without crawling.

The regulator

The two-gauge regulator in your kit threads onto the cylinder's valve. If you have not fitted one before, hand the regulator to the shop that fills the cylinder and ask them to fit it — they do it every day, and they will check it for leaks before you carry it home.

It carries two gauges: one reads what is left in the cylinder, the other reads what the regulator is sending on to the appliance.

Set it anywhere between 70 and 100 PSI

The number does not have to be exact. There is a second regulator inside the appliance that holds the working pressure steady wherever you leave the first one, as long as it is in that band. Set it, and forget it.

The red tether

The brass nut on one end of the red tube pulls down onto the regulator's outlet. It is a metal cone seating on a metal cone — hand-tight, no wrench. The other end of the tether pushes into the red-ringed port on the back of the appliance, like every other tube in this install.

Mind this

Stand the cylinder upright and put it where it cannot be knocked over — behind the appliance's own footprint, or against the side of the cabinet. Leave its valve closed until every connection on page 16 is made.
