In the cabinet

The cylinder

Your 5 lb cylinder stands on the cabinet floor beside the appliance, where you can see the connection and reach the valve without crawling.

The regulator

The two-gauge regulator in your kit threads onto the cylinder's valve. If you have not fitted one before, the shop that fills the cylinder will fit it and leak-check it.

The upper gauge reads what goes to the appliance, the left one what is left in the cylinder. Big knob sets the pressure, small knob opens the gas.

Set the needle in the green

The upper gauge carries a green band across its scale. Turn the big knob until the needle sits in it, and stop. Lower in the band is a softer drink and higher a fizzier one, up to the 90 PSI a second regulator inside the appliance holds it to. Set it and leave it.

The red tether

The brass nut on one end of the red tube pulls down onto the regulator's outlet. It is a metal cone seating on a metal cone — hand-tight, no wrench.

Mind this

Stand it upright in its own lane beside the appliance, against the side of the cabinet — not behind it, where the 60 mm is what the tubes turn in. Leave its valve closed until pages 14 and 15 are done; page 16 is where you open it.
