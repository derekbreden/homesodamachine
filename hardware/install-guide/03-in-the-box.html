Before you start

What is in the box

Take the lid off and the Quick Start is lying face up on top of everything. Under it: the appliance, the faucet in its own bag, and the install kit.

What comes with it
Faucet and umbilicalIn its own bag beside the appliance, not in the kit. One piece: the faucet, three tubes and the display cable are joined at the factory and never come apart.
Tee subassemblyIn the kit. A black tee, a short jumper in one end, and the long white run — with the water filter already inline — in the branch.
Second tee, baggedBrass, for an older kitchen. You will use one tee or the other, never both. Page 9 says which.
Red tetherAbout a foot of red tube with a brass nut on one end. It reaches your CO2 cylinder.
CO2 regulatorTwo gauges, in its own box. It mounts on the cylinder you supply.
Collet pressA small printed tool with a U-shaped jaw. It is what makes 4 easy.
Power cordSix feet, grounded.
Cold kit, baggedA tube cutter and foam sleeves. A bonus, not a step — open it after the install is done.
Mind this

Every white end you will push is already cut square. Nothing in this install needs cutting, and a tube trimmed short cannot be lengthened again.
