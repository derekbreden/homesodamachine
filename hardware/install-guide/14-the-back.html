The back of
the appliance

Six connections, and every one of them is a push. Each tube wears a printed collar carrying the same word as the port it belongs in.

Ports, jack and nameplate. The power inlet is at the left.

TAPthe white run, out of the filter
CO2the red tether, from the cylinder
SODAthe blue tube from the faucet — blue tube, blue ring
FLAVORthe two black tubes from the faucet. Either one in either port
the small rectanglethe faucet's plug, in until it clicks. The one station with no word on it: it takes a plug, not a tube
Mind this

Two ports travel with caps in them. Pull them out; they do not go back on. And push every tube properly home — a little over half an inch, further than it feels like it should go. A tube stopped short is the one thing on this face that leaks.
