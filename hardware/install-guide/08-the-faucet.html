Quick Start 12

The faucet

The faucet, its three tubes and its display cable are one piece, joined at the factory. Nothing on it comes apart, and nothing on it gets cut.

It seats itself

Feed the tubes down through the hole and lower the faucet after them. Then push the faucet back, away from you, until the two black tubes under it rest against the back of the hole. That is the seat. Nothing is measured and nothing is aimed at — back until it stops is the whole of it.

The plate goes on sideways

The under-counter plate — the steel disc in the faucet's bag — has two slots open at the edge. Hold it against the underside of the counter, above the washer and nut already on the shank. Line up the wide slot with the shank and the narrow slot with the two black tubes. Slide it sideways until they reach their pockets, then run the nut up by hand.

From under the counter: the plate approaches above the washer and nut.

Mind this

The tubes are long on purpose: the slack you coil in the cabinet is what lets you pull the appliance forward later. Leave it long. Shortening it is what the cold kit is for, afterwards.
