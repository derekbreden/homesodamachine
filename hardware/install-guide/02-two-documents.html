How to read these two

The sheet and this

The Quick Start draws six actions. Nothing on it is cut and nothing is threaded, and it is drawn without words so you can read it at arm's length with both hands busy.

This guide is that sheet's margins. It carries what comes before the first action, what to do if your kitchen is not the one the sheet draws, and what happens after the last connection is made.

Wherever the two meet, this guide sends you to the sheet rather than drawing the same thing twice. A stone disc in this text is a step on that sheet:
