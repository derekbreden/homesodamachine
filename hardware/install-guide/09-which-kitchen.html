Quick Start 345

Which kitchen
do you have?

Two tees came in the kit. You will use one of them. Which one depends on what is already under your sink on the cold side.

Open the cabinet and look at the cold side

Is there a soft 1/4-inch plastic tube on a push fitting — the kind a fridge or an under-sink filter runs on?

Then use the black tee. The Quick Start draws the whole thing at 345, and there is nothing to turn. Come back here when it is in.

Or is the cold supply a braided hose on a shut-off valve, with no 1/4-inch line anywhere?

Then use the white tee, and turn the page. Two joints with a wrench; the appliance's own line still just pushes in. Sheet steps 12 and 6 are unchanged.

Your long white run arrives in the black tee's branch. Press that collet with the printed tool and pull the run out; that tee and its jumper are not used on this path.

Close the right valve

Whichever path you are on, close the valve feeding the fitting you are about to open. Follow the line back by hand to the first valve you meet and turn it a quarter turn. Run the cold tap: if it goes dry, you closed the right one. Leave it open a moment to drop the pressure.

Mind this

A closed valve is not an empty line. Put a towel and a cup under the work before you open the joint.
