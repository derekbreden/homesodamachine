Before you start

The cabinet

The appliance is 215 mm wide, 462 deep and 361 tall — about 8½ by 18 by 14 inches. It stands on the floor of the sink cabinet.

The slot, from above. Coral is floor you leave clear; the cylinder stands in its own lane beside the appliance.

A sink cabinet is never empty. The disposal hangs down the middle of it, the trap sits behind, and the supply lines run up one side. What is left is a slot beside all of that — as deep and as tall as the cabinet, and narrow. The appliance is built for that slot.

Your cylinder stands in the same slot, beside the appliance. Allow about 5¼ inches of width for it on top of the appliance's own.
