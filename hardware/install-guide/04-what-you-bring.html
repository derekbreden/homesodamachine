Before you start

What you bring

Four things are not in the box, and two of them want deciding before install day rather than during it.

A hole in the counter

One 1-3/8 inch hole, cut before anything else begins. It is the only cutting this installation asks for, and it is not yours to do on the day — see page 6.

A CO2 cylinder

A 5 lb cylinder, the size a homebrew or welding-gas shop refills for about $25. It stands on the cabinet floor beside the appliance, about 5 inches across and 18 inches tall. The regulator that goes on top of it is in your kit.

Flavor concentrate

Any SodaStream-compatible syrup, in the 440 mL bottle it is sold in. Two flavors, one each. The bottle is inverted straight over the funnel on top of the appliance — no measuring, no decanting.

And a wrench, if your kitchen is the older kind

An adjustable wrench, only if page 9 sends you to the brass tee. On the path the Quick Start draws, there is nothing to turn but your hands.

Mind this

Order the cylinder before install day. Everything else in this guide can be finished in an afternoon; a cylinder cannot be bought at nine in the evening.
