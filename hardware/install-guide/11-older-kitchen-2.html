The brass tee

The tee goes in between the shut-off and the hose you just took off, and offers a third port sideways for the appliance's white run.

Before
After
  1. Screw the tee's lower nut down onto the shut-off's outlet, where the hose was. Hand it on first — a nut that starts crooked will not seal however hard it is pulled up.
  2. Screw the braided hose back on, now onto the top of the tee.
  3. Land the white run on the tee's side port and tighten its nut.
  4. Nip all three up with the wrench, holding the tee's own flats while you do. Snug, then a little more. These are metal seats, not rubber ones — they do not want force.
  5. Open the shut-off slowly and watch all three joints for a full minute before you go back to the sheet.
Mind this

If your shut-off is not 3/8 inch, or anything here is unfamiliar, stop. Your Founder Edition includes a personal install consultation, and this joint is exactly what it is for. A plumber will also do it in ten minutes.
