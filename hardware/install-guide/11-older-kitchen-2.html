The white tee

It goes in between the shut-off and the hose you just took off. Two of its three joints are threaded; the third, the appliance's own, is a push.

Before
After
  1. Screw the tee's lower nut down onto the shut-off's outlet, where the hose was. Hand it on first — a nut that starts crooked will not seal however hard it is pulled up.
  2. Screw the braided hose back on, now onto the top of the tee.
  3. Nip both of those up with the wrench, holding the tee's body while you do. Snug, then a little more; force past snug does not help a compression nut.
  4. Push the white run into the side port, all the way home — further than it feels like it should go. No nut, no wrench. Turn that port's little lever to lie along it: open.
  5. Open the shut-off slowly and watch all three joints for a minute.
Mind this

This tee fits a 3/8 inch shut-off, which is what almost every kitchen has. If yours is a different size, or anything here is unfamiliar, stop and call — your Founder Edition includes a personal install consultation, and these two joints are what it is for.
