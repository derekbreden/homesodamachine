The white tee

It goes in between the shut-off and the hose you just took off. Two of its three joints are threaded; the third, the appliance's own, is a push.

Before
After
  1. Screw the tee's lower nut onto the shut-off's outlet, where the hose was. Start it by hand — a nut that starts crooked will not seal however hard it is pulled up.
  2. Screw the braided hose back on, now onto the top of the tee.
  3. Nip both up with the wrench, holding the tee's body. Snug, then a little more; force past snug does not help a compression nut.
  4. Push the white run into the side port, all the way home — further than it feels like it should go. No nut, no wrench.
  5. Leave that port's own small lever open. It is how you shut the appliance off one day without touching the rest of the sink.
  6. Open the shut-off slowly and watch all three joints for a minute.
Mind this

This tee fits a 3/8 inch shut-off, which is what almost every kitchen has. If yours is a different size, or anything here is unfamiliar, stop and call — these two joints are what your Founder Edition's install consultation is for.
