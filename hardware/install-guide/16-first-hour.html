After the last connection

The first hour

The appliance arrives dry — no water in any line, nothing in either reservoir. Everything from here is it filling itself and getting cold.

In this order

  1. Open the cold-water valve you closed on page 9, slowly, and watch every joint you made for a full minute.
  2. Open the cylinder valve. Under pressure, leak-check every joint in the gray connector assembly and at the red port.
  3. Plug it in. It answers with a chime — three rising notes, a breath, then the same shape higher — and the display reads Powering on · Getting everything ready. Nothing moves at power-up: it comes up listening, not running.

Then pour a flavor in

On the display, choose FILL, pick a flavor, and press start. Turn a bottle of concentrate upside down over the funnel on top and let the whole thing go in at once — it is sized for exactly that, and it drains itself dry. The appliance draws it down into its chilled reservoir and stops on its own. Do the second flavor the same way.

Neck down in the funnel's throat, and let it drain. Nothing is measured and nothing is decanted.

Syrup goes in at one part to twenty, so a bottle is a great many glasses.

Mind this

Allow about an hour for the first glass. The initial fill takes tens of minutes to chill from room temperature; later pours are quick.
