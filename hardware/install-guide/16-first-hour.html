After the last connection

The first hour

The appliance arrives dry — no water in any line, nothing in either reservoir. Everything from here is it filling itself and getting cold.

In this order

  1. Open the cold-water valve you closed on page 9, slowly, and watch every joint you made for a full minute.
  2. Open the cylinder valve, all the way. You will hear gas move and then stop. Check the tether's brass nut and the red port while it is under pressure.
  3. Plug it in. It answers with a rising four-note chime and the display reads Powering on · Getting everything ready, then opens onto flavour selection. Nothing moves at power-up: it comes up listening, not running.

Then pour a flavour in

On the display, choose FILL, pick a side, and press start. Turn a 440 mL bottle of concentrate upside down over the funnel on top and let the whole thing go in at once — it is sized for exactly that, and it drains itself dry. The appliance draws it down into its chilled reservoir and stops on its own. Do the second flavour the same way.

Syrup goes in at one part to twenty, so a bottle is a great many glasses.

Mind this

Give it about an hour before you expect the first glass. A tap-water fill starting at room temperature takes tens of minutes to come down to serving cold, and everything after that first chill is quick.
