Your cold shut-off, and the braided hose running up to the kitchen faucet. Same valve, one quarter turn apart.

  1. Close the shut-off and check it, the way page 9 says — the two pictures above are the same valve, a quarter turn apart.
  2. Put a towel and a cup under the valve.
  3. Unscrew the braided hose's nut off the top of the shut-off and lift the hose clear. Water will come out of it. The nut starts with a wrench and finishes by hand.
Mind this

Hold the shut-off's body with a second wrench while you turn the nut. A shut-off twisted on its own pipe is a far bigger job than this one.
