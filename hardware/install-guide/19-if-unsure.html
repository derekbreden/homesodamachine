If something is not right

Three things,
then call

Almost everything that goes wrong on install day is one of these.

A push fitting weeps

The tube is not all the way in. A 1/4-inch push fitting takes a little over half an inch of tube, which is further than it feels like it should go, and it will hold a tube that is short of that firmly enough to fool you. Close the valve, press the collet, pull the tube out, look at the wet mark on it, and push it back in past that mark.

Nothing comes out of the faucet

Either the cold-water valve is still closed, or the machine has not finished getting cold. It will not pour warm.

It did not chime when you plugged it in

It always chimes. Silence at power-up is worth a call rather than a second try.

Stop and call

Your Founder Edition includes a personal install consultation, and it exists for exactly the moments this guide cannot reach: a shut-off that is not the size on page 11, a joint you would rather not make yourself, a leak that does not stop when you remake it, or anything at all that does not look like these pages. Close the cold-water valve, close the cylinder, and get in touch before you carry on.
