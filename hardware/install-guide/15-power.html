Quick Start 6, and one more

The cord

The seventh connection on that same face, and the last one you make. The six-foot cord is in your kit.

The socket is at the left-hand end of the back face, below the row of tubes, recessed into the wall so that nothing stands proud of it. Push the cord in until its housing sits down in the recess — that housing is the strain relief, so there is no clip or clamp to fasten and none to look for.

The socket, recessed into the back face at the left of the tube row. Nothing stands proud of it.

What it wants from the wall

Supply120 V, 60 Hz, a standard grounded household outlet
Draw5 A, 600 W

The ground pin is doing real work. The metal inside the appliance is bonded to it — the carbonator and the compressor — and if either of them ever went live, that pin is what carries the fault back and trips your breaker before you could feel it. Do not run this machine through an adapter that lifts the ground.

Mind this

The socket itself carries a moulded 250V 10A stamp. That is the rating of the connector, not of this machine. The machine is 120 V 60 Hz only, as its nameplate below the socket says, and it must not be run on 240 V.
