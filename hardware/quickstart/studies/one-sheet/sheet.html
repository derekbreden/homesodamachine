Home Soda Machine

Installation in five steps. No threaded fitting is opened.

1Mount the faucet Keep the washer and nut on the assembly.

2Shut off the cold water Open the cold tap. Water must stop.

3Disconnect the tap-water line Water must be completely off.

4Add the water tee Push every tube in until it stops. Tug once on each to confirm it is locked.

5Connect the appliance Push each tube into the port that wears its colour and word.

  • TAPWhite, from the filter
  • CO2Red
  • SODABlue
  • FLAVORBoth black tubes, either port
  • RJ11Into the jack
