# Above-core enclosure integration

The production arrangement is defined by [above-core.json](../../manifold-layout/above-core.json),
[rear_plumbing.py](../../manifold-layout/rear_plumbing.py) and
[the assembly procedure](../../manifold-layout/above-core.md).
Back-top carries the threaded ASSE header, Pisco/check group, meter,
regulator, V-A/V-K, ceiling PSU and west-wall relays. The pump, PCB and V-B
remain on the cold core. Integral seats and removable snap caps share those
native placements; the rear nut-seat relief ends at the continuous ceiling.

## Current native records

The [vent-return review](vent-return-review.json) binds the printed TPU
elbow, direct white OVER return and both 4 mm flavor paths to the exported
appliance. It checks native material inside complete clearance windows,
with receiving interfaces identified separately. The elbow has no
unconnected-body overlap; the white return has at least 1 mm clearance.
Actual curve extrema give R55.674 for the vent return and R25 for both
flavor lines. The flavor-B mesh follow-up resolves its six ambiguous mesh
neighbors as native-zero intersections.

The [connector-service review](connector-service-review.json) records complete
continuous conservative nominal housing sweeps for J1, J2 and J11 against
the installed native larger bodies. Remove the funnel, frame and cradled
drain first. Release the three free final Wagos and move the flexible loom
aside. J1/J2 lift 17 mm and then move 8 mm fore. For J11, disconnect the
V-B-to-Y-B return; lift 6 mm, move 10 mm east, then lift another 20 mm.
Reservoir B's draw fitting stays installed. The nominal housing sweeps are
clear with those prerequisites. [connector_service.py](connector_service.py)
reproduces the recorded continuous stage bounds without generating a review.

The [installed wiring binding](../../wiring/installed/above-core-review/installed-wiring-binding.json)
reads all 120 native wiring bodies and compares all 101 conductor curves
with their current source. The [routing review](../../wiring/installed/routing-review.json)
checks larger bodies, excluding wires and tubes as wire obstacles. Its five
mesh witnesses are resolved by exact native readings: either zero material
intersection or contact wholly inside the named electrical attachment.
There are no unresolved larger-body contacts. Every preparation cut covers
its installed route.

Each current record names its artifact hashes. The final root-stock update
also records the added material and proves that it is outside the recorded
vent windows and connector sweeps, with no native intersection against any
other installed body. Earlier diagnostic records in this directory apply
only to the geometry and source hashes they identify.

## Assembly interfaces

Populate back-top ceiling-down on the bench. Fit the electronics and
threaded equipment before closing their snap caps. Prepare the meter with
its continuous R14 inlet, accepted holder, one square-cut 61 mm foam sleeve
and concealed outlet tube. Tighten the pump screws before threading this
inlet or loading the lid's tube snaps.

The rear label tiles are 1.8 mm thick in 2.0 mm continuous channels. Their
full roots join the rear wall outside the sliding paths. The flange/nut
planes remain Y471.3/Y465.3. The accepted nameplate receiver and keystone
catch profiles retain their fitted interfaces.

Keep the cross-quadrant tube ends free while positioning back-top. Turn the
round pump-side joints as needed during approach; connect and dress the
water, gas, soda and V-A/Y-A handoffs through the uncovered funnel opening.
The core guides carry continuous tubes and add no socket or tube cut.
Complete the V-B return and frame-carried drain connection before fitting
the removable frame, then its four screws and the silicone funnel.

The [internal plumbing procedure](../../assembly/internal-plumbing.md) gives
the component sequence and actual insertion-mark method. The
[core review](../../printed-parts/cold-core/foam-cap/above-core-native-review.json)
and [funnel record](../../printed-parts/zone-c/funnel/drop-in-review/native-review.json)
retain their specifically bound equipment, stock and tool-access scopes.
Accepted physical results retain their articles and materials in the
[qualification index](../README.md).

## Manual review

These readers are manual tools, outside builds and commits. Native CAD
establishes nominal shape, placement, material intersection and the named
rigid service paths. Physical fit, seal, force, support cleanup, printed
spring life and flexible handling retain their own qualification scope.
