# Cartridge printed-surface inspection

The production spring changes occupy the two flank bands recorded in
[geometry review](geometry-review.json). The seated retainer has zero
intersection with front-top. Both pump heads, bosses and motors have zero
intersection with the retainer and front-top. A 0.5 mm downward pull meets
the retaining floors with 32.76 mm³ of intersection. These rigid-mesh
readings do not establish holding force, friction or life.

The receiver pocket keeps 6.4 mm of outer backing, and the centered release
opening keeps 3 mm. The relaxed tip needs 2.85 mm inward retraction to pass
the entry edge; the nominal inward corridor is 3.35 mm. Both complete wing
and root profiles, their Y end shoulders and outer wall are in the compact
trial. Actual deformation and center-tool release across the full Y span
remain physical checks.

Outside the edited flank bands, the printed surfaces' sampled before/after
deviation is at most 0.002 mm on the retainer and 0.070 mm on front-top.
The exact manifold differences and sample counts are retained in the review;
these are not an assertion that the tessellated surfaces are byte-identical.
The fitted inverse construction is retained.

[Retainer lint](retainer-lint.txt) reports the required thin spring wall,
the corridor's source-Z surface and three 0.002 mm-wide lower-well ledges.
The [anchored answers](../../printed-parts/lint-answers/enclosure/enclosure/enclosure-pump-retainer.lint-answers)
identify the Y-end print direction, required flexure and existing well-profile
ledges outside the spring change. Those ledges were reported to Derek and
are not a physical surface acceptance.

[Whole front-top lint](front-top-lint.txt) includes outer flute strips near
X±106.866/Z351.683, a tee-bore profile strip near X−87.095/Y92.763/Z203.051,
curved show-surface samples above Z300 and a seam ledge near Y147.333/Z195.025.
Those locations are outside the two changed receiver bands. The change
pictures show the actual root, hook and receiver cross-section from one
camera; they were inspected together.

The [normal-build reconciliation](normal-integration.json) binds the active
published artifacts and identical source definitions. Its finer tessellation
has at most 0.0038 mm sampled deviation in the snap bands from the retained
trial surfaces. The seated retainer has zero overlap with the current receiver.
The prepared trial STLs, native projects and emitted G-code remain unchanged.
