# Retained cartridge preparation records

The active complete pointed set is in [pointed-snaps](../pointed-snaps/README.md).
Its six matched pairs include the connected cartridge mechanism and the
conforming ASSE adapter pocket. The comparison and full-fixture archives in
this directory are retained preparation records and are not ready jobs.

[Print jobs](print-jobs.json) and [handoff](handoff.json) identify those retained
archive hashes and their inactive queue status. [Geometry](geometry.json)
describes the surfaces in those files. [Normal integration](normal-integration.json),
[assembly integration](assembly-integration.json) and [installation scenes](installation-scenes.json)
bind their recorded production cohort. Current production geometry and bench
instructions belong to the complete pointed set.
