# Longer insertion tips

A single straight lead-in can run from a pointed outside corner to the top
of the existing 1.6 mm nose land. A 3 mm rise gives the hook another 2 mm
of insertion length. The candidate retains the 2.0 mm leaf, 22.25 mm free
length, 1.5 mm root blend, 2.6 mm flat catch overlap and centered blade entry.
The point replaces the current 2 mm-wide flat top with one sloping face.
This changes the insertion wedge without thinning the pry-entry ceiling.

| Rear cap | Candidate tip, installed frame | Clearance beyond a 0.5 mm pocket ceiling |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ASSE | Z344.850 | 0.650 mm to the Z346 interior roof |
| PISCO | Z344.850 | 0.650 mm to the Z346 interior roof |
| Meter | X93.200 | 4.800 mm to the X98.5 interior side |
| Water regulator | Z344.815 | 0.685 mm to the Z346 interior roof |

Each receiving pocket needs the same 2 mm ceiling extension. The values
above compare envelope planes; they do not establish clearance through every
local roof feature. The pump needs its own retained lid/motor clearance
reading before transferring this profile.

The v5 print trial keeps the existing tips so its result measures the ASSE,
PISCO and meter hardware bearings. This longer tip is a separate next
physical comparison; force, elastic opening and repeated release need that
trial. The accepted pump and water-regulator fit surfaces remain unchanged.
