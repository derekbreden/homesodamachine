# PISCO body and outlet-neck fit

The cap follows the registered PISCO exterior with 0.15 mm radial and
0.25 mm axial air. Its body and neck cutters meet at the retained Y423.875
stop plane. The receiver has one 3 mm-wide upper bearing at world
Y438.125–441.125, with a 10.9774 mm bore radius around the measured
10.8274 mm ring. The bearing lies within the complete 16 mm hardware
insertion sweep. The rear bore, axis X76.5/Z328, cap Y420.3457–462.05,
spring datums and body-stock upper and lower planes stay fixed.

[Fit data](pisco-fit-recommendation.json) supplies every body/neck profile
point in native axial Z and world Y, receiver runs, retained datums, and
SHA-256 digests of the measured candidate outputs. Native +Z points toward
world −Y; world Y is 480.8 minus native Z. The emitted installed cap NPZ
identifies the initial v5 service-query geometry. Its standalone print-frame
PISCO NPZ and STL are identified separately and retain that measured shape;
the installed archive also carries the independently revised ASSE cap.

The [physical photos](../../v4/physical-result/physical-result.json),
particularly the low/high outlet and neck views, establish the movement and
noncontact lip qualitatively. They supply no calibrated gap measurement.
The [registered hardware profile](../../../../reference/compact-inline-candidates/dimensions.json)
supplies the dimensions. Its capture uses a rigid native-scale registration;
paint thickness and absolute optical scale have no independent calibration.
The fixture's maximum-radius cylinders provided approximately 1.35 mm radial
neck air and 1.2 mm air at the plain main body. The fit profile removes those
local envelope gaps. The profile's outward chord reduction can add at most
0.06 mm to its stated air.

## Installed service geometry

Remove the ASSE and meter caps and dress disconnected tubes aside. Hold the
PISCO leaves open and lower the cap 3.6 mm. Depress the exposed outlet
collet and withdraw the stem check, then slide the cap 45 mm fore with its
leaves held open. Installation reverses this sequence.

- [Disconnected-check readings](pisco-disconnected-service-readings.json)
  measure the complete emitted cap at 12 positions through the fore slide.
  All reported back-top intersections belong to relaxed hooks, at
  Z336.65–339.089; the rigid lower cap clears the fixed meter receiver.
- [Released-cap readings](pisco-released-service-readings.json) retain the
  actual emitted cap below Z328. The isolated upper leaves move 3.2 mm
  outward as clearance proxies. None of 25 sampled drop/slide poses
  intersects the PISCO reference, the two relevant fixed tube meshes or
  the emitted back-top. The fixed meter receiver remains present. The
  proxies establish space for held-open leaves, without predicting their
  connected elastic shape, force or fatigue.
- [Check-disconnection readings](pisco-check-disconnection-readings.json)
  find no cap/check intersection at eight drop poses with the check
  attached, nine check-withdrawal poses after lowering the cap, or six
  collet poses up to 2 mm toward the body after lowering it. Exterior
  geometry does not establish the mechanism's required release travel or
  operating force; those need the physical trial.

The outlet collet is fully exposed at Y417.986–420.086; the seated cap
starts 0.26 mm behind it. Lowering the cap before operating that collet
provides its axial access. At the dropped pose, the collet's 6.124 mm
maximum radius needs 4.9541 mm horizontal half-width at the lip top. The
neck envelope's minimum radius is 5.009 mm, a 0.0549 mm modeled margin.
The cap's rigid bottom passes 0.923 mm above the fixed meter receiver.

The [8.25 mm drop readings](pisco-service-readings.json) and
[intersection locations](pisco-drop-intersection-locations.json) identify
the fixed meter receiver obstruction in the retained trial geometry. They
are evidence for the service constraint, separately from the candidate
fit queries. A straight 8.25 mm drop reaches that land at X83.05 and
Z309.25. Clearing it with an outer cap cut would leave only 1.398 mm
normal ring backing and a 45° bevel would enter the full spring root.
The service sequence uses the 3.6 mm drop and stem-check removal.
