# ASSE body seating

The complete production ASSE cap fits the existing pointed receiver. Its body
cavity retains all six wrench-flat directions and the 14.6 mm nominal upper
apothem. The three lower-facing planes carry 0.25 mm of additional vertical
seating allowance. The adapter has its own conforming hex pocket, neck bearings
and both axial shoulders. Its fit, the pointed catches, spring roots, centered
screwdriver access and outer cap dimensions are retained.

[The physical result](physical-result/physical-result.json) records the latest
pointed set and the slight angular lift over the ASSE body. The scan comparison
in [geometry-review.json](geometry-review.json) reads the observed body and
bonnet flats directly against their bearing planes. The cap is one closed solid.

[The plate specification](plate-spec.json) contains one full cap. Use the
ASSE receiver from the pointed set and seat the complete ASSE and inlet adapter.
Inspect that the cap closes level over both wrench sections and holds both
adapter shoulders. Remove the automatic cavity supports with a tiny flathead
screwdriver that fits inside the cavity. The cap prints on its production
axial end in black PET-GF, with three Classic walls and solid infill.
