# V-G funnel receiver and insert

This Mark1 fit coupon pairs a 25 × 20 mm crop of the production funnel frame with the complete flavor A dispense clip. The crop includes both 45° catches, the pocket roof and the original bowl-side wall. The frame sample stands on its production underside. The insert prints on the same flat end as the production part.

Press the insert into the pocket until both hooks seat. Press a 4 mm dispense tube into the lower opening. The two fits can be tried separately by releasing the tube while the insert stays seated.

The 82-layer black PET-GF plate sent as task 1483465690 uses the left 0.4 mm hardened nozzle and textured PEI. Its reviewed toolpath has no support.
