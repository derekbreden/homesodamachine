# Full-length Pisco cover

Derek accepted the full-length two-bar cover from Mark1. Its wing tips carry the +0.20 mm raised flats from the fitted front collar. Use `single-pisco-cap` for this production cover. [Full-length fit result](https://github.com/derekbreden/homesodamachine/blob/f1e8c6ba98e640ec28c19d8b89ce5f78908c782e/hardware/print-tests/pisco-raised-tip-cover/prints/2026-11-23-mark1/physical-result.json) · [Mark1 print job](https://github.com/derekbreden/homesodamachine/blob/28db775ffbc3650516a81910b1d5a603cd326bb7/hardware/print-tests/pisco-raised-tip-cover/prints/2026-11-23-mark1/print-job.json) · [Fitted collar](https://github.com/derekbreden/homesodamachine/blob/e62039246607676c41560a80c5378a1b932ebf89/hardware/print-tests/rear-receiver-quick/prints/2026-11-23-roof-mark1/physical-result.json)

In the original rear quadrant, Derek pressed the four-bar cover from Mark2 beside the regulator outlet. It stayed engaged, and he attributed the movement to the wing bending under load. He put the two-bar cover back on the same regulator and felt a tiny amount more play. He preferred the four-bar cover for this fit. [Four-bar video and observations](prints/2026-11-23-mark2/physical-result/physical-result.json) · [Two-bar video and observations](prints/2026-11-23-mark1/physical-result/physical-result.json)

The four-bar cover raises both flats by +0.40 mm. Mark2 printed the springs end-on in black PET-GF. The archived generator uses the production mesh from the time of the trial.
