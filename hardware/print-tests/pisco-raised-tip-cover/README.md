# Full-length Pisco cover

The paired covers compare raised flats along both wings in the original rear enclosure quadrant. Both carry the spring and retaining profile of the three-diamond cover. The two-bar cover uses the crest from the front collar that stayed put when Derek rocked it by hand. The four-bar cover raises the same flat closer to the receiver roof. [Fitted collar](https://github.com/derekbreden/homesodamachine/blob/e62039246607676c41560a80c5378a1b932ebf89/hardware/print-tests/rear-receiver-quick/prints/2026-11-23-roof-mark1/physical-result.json)

Fit each cover over the real regulator in the original quadrant. Compare the snap on insertion, the movement when pressing the exposed outlet and the release. The taller crests reach the roof earlier in the modeled seating path. [Two-bar fit](parts/fit-check.json) · [Four-bar fit](parts/fit-check-040.json)

Both plates print the springs end-on in black PET-GF. The tower gives the nozzle time away from the cover between courses. [Mark1 plate](prints/2026-11-23-mark1/print-job.json) · [Mark2 plate](prints/2026-11-23-mark2/print-job.json)

The Rust producer is `pisco-raised-tip-cover` in `hsm-rear-equipment-caps`. The native plate recipes are `pisco-raised-tip-cover` and `pisco-raised-tip-cover-040`.
