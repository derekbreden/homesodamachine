# East rear rail fit

The paired crops retain the complete east rail from its aft lead-in to its
seated stops, including the flush alignment rib, groove and rail backing.
[The crop inputs](inputs.json) and [geometry record](parts/geometry.json) bind
the coupons to their production solids in seated coordinates.
[The slide review](slide-review.json) records overlap through the approach.

Mark1 prints the upper coupon ceiling-down and the lower coupon floor-up
in black PET-GF. Both preserve the production layer phase at the bearings.
[The plate specification](plate-spec.json) and
[native review](prints/2026-11-18-mark1/review.json) record their print settings
and the cavity-floor supports. The upper groove stays clear.
[The job record](prints/2026-11-18-mark1/print-job.json) binds the slice to Mark1.

Remove the upper breakaway strips and the lower rail tree through the open
side of their cavities. Start the upper coupon aft of the lower one and slide
it toward the fore faces. Check the lead-in, drag along the full rail, and
alignment at the fore faces when seated.
