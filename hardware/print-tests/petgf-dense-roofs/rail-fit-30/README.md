# Rail cavity and mating wall pair

This plate places the [longer complete rail cavity coupon](../rail-cavity-30/)
beside its actual mating front-top enclosure section. The mating section retains
the foot, the peaked channel around the head, the exterior wall, and stock beyond
the channel's far wall and roof. Both parts retain their production layer
directions and native clearances.

The open cut ends permit a sliding fit test after the rail-cavity supports are
removed. The crop represents the engaged cross-section and its surrounding wall;
the enclosure's distant end stops and overall wall stiffness are outside its scope.
Facing the printer, RD is on the left and MD is on the right.

The rail cavity retains D's contact and bridge treatment with the locally rooted
production tree recipe. The matching section uses the same plate process and
solid stock setting. The Rust crop records their overlap in the source assembly
coordinates before labels and print placement are applied.

[`samples.json`](samples.json) owns the crop bounds and process settings.
[`preparation.json`](preparation.json) binds both source meshes to the plate.
[`verification.json`](verification.json) records the fit scope, surrounding stock,
support roots, contact planes, archive hashes and native estimate. Physical
sliding performance and support removal remain untested.

The editable native project is beside this file. The sliced machine archive and
estimate are in [`ready/`](ready/). Mark1's accepted job, selected send options
and startup reading are in [`mark1/`](mark1/). The physical rail removal and
sliding fit result remain pending.
