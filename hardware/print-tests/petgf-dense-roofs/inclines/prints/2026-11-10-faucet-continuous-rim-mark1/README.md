# Faucet first rim perimeter

The complete lower-curve coupon has a dedicated first outer perimeter along
its inclined seat. Short ascending arcs meet at shared endpoints and join the
native bed-backed tangent. The neighbouring courses meet the new perimeter
at the side and above. The rim fins and trees carry the same dense roof, and
the insert recesses use bridge roads over their shoulders and blind roofs.
