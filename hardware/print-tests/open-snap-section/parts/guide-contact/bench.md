# Compare the three guide-contact pairs

Keep the newer two-dot pair from C035 beside this plate. Derek tested it twice on camera. Use it to judge grip and release. [C035 comparison frame](../../prints/2026-11-25-mark1-refinements/physical-result/four-coupon-guides.jpg)

Match each cover and receiver by their recessed dots. Press straight onto the receiver, release your fingers, then sight through the open end against the same light. Check **both** long faces between the broad foot and the hook nose. Press over one wing and then the other, release, and see whether the cover rocks or springs up. Spread the wings to remove it.

| Dots | Where to look | What to compare with the newer two-dot pair |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One | Along the entire diagonal guide | A narrower line of light with some clearance throughout. Does the cover still seat and release with the same effort? |
| Two | Along the same diagonal, from the foot to the nose | The opposing faces approach each other at each printed layer. Does the line close all the way along while both feet stay down? |
| Three | Across the straight middle portion of each guide | Look for a roughly 4 mm strip of close contact. The entrance has room to start. Does the cover need an earlier push, and does it still settle square? |

Compare again after a few removals. Record the dot count, which end or side shows light, and whether either wing rises under alternating pressure. The closest fit that still seats square and releases comfortably is the candidate for a full-size trial.
