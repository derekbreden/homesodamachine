# Compare the three Mark1 refinements

Keep the previous two-dot pair beside this plate. It held steady on film. The previous three-dot pair showed a smaller gap along the long entry face and moved a little more under Derek’s fingers. [Filmed comparison](../../prints/2026-11-25-mark1-solid-pairs/physical-result.json)

Match each new cover to the receiver with the same number of recessed dots. Stand the receiver on its flat back and press the cover on square. Look through the open end along the long slope between the hook nose and its flat toe. Press alternately over each wing, let go, then spread the wings to remove the cover. Compare the line of light, the movement that remains after releasing pressure and the effort to remove it.
