# Pointed snap set

One complete cap and matching receiver for each of the six snap families. These
are the production pointed profiles and their final enclosing geometry. The rear
caps and the G-Ganen cap have one 3 mm-rise planar lead-in above the 1.6 mm
tool-backed nose. The cartridge's trailing barb has one 4.6 mm-high planar
lead-in running to its retaining corner. Each profile has a pointed edge.

The matching pockets clear the complete points. The cartridge pocket's back
roof is 0.5 mm above the lead-in's highest level, with a continuous 45° return;
the centered 5 mm screwdriver ports and nominal 3 mm outer backing are included. All caps
retain the 2 mm leaves, broad root transitions and full catch overlap. The
water-regulator leaves retain their 21.25 mm working length; the others retain
22.25 mm.

[Physical fit approval](physical-result/physical-result.json) records Derek's
approval of PISCO, water-regulator and flow-meter hardware fits. Their bearing
definitions are unchanged. Wing-tip insertion and release are the remaining
checks on those parts. The G-Ganen pump keeps its accepted motor rails, stops
and cradle fit.

The ASSE cap captures both axial shoulders of the adapter hex and locates its
two cylindrical necks with the measured profile and 0.15 mm air. All six adapter
hex-flat directions retain the measured clock; its lower flats have 0.25 mm
additional seating depth, with rounded corner clearance through the same hex
band. The upper flats retain their positions. The separate ASSE-body
flats, drain-sleeve window backing and threaded-joint adjustment remain part of
the complete cap.

compact pair verifies the complete paired snap mechanism; the two-pump

## Prepared prints

[Print jobs](print-jobs.json) binds the two current native projects and sliced
archives to their source hashes, printer profiles, Z trim, bead margins and
support readings. Mark1 carries ASSE, PISCO and the water regulator. Mark2
carries the G-Ganen pump, flow meter and connected cartridge pair. There is
one cap and one receiver per family, with no comparison duplicates.

Caps print on their production axial ends, with bending within the layer plane.
Receivers keep their production print poses. Black Fiberon PET-GF15 uses the
left hardened 0.4 mm Standard nozzle, 0.20 mm first layer, 0.24 mm ordinary
layers, three Classic walls, solid caps and 15% gyroid receiver bulk. Both
cartridge snap hosts have local solid modifiers. Tree(auto)/Default retains
the slicer's automatic support sections and localized cooling.

Remove cavity supports before seating hardware. The required post-processing
tool is a tiny flathead screwdriver that fits inside the cavity. Derek's
observed easy removal of the rear cavity supports is retained in the print
instructions; the connected cartridge receiver's bridge support is inspected
and removed through its open lower/axial faces. Its removal is part of the
physical check on that new sample.

Seat the actual hardware, close both wings and inspect the full retaining
contacts in X, Y and Z. Watch the whole leaf during insertion and check that
the point enters without striking its pocket roof. Support the assembly while
releasing both centered screwdriver openings. On the cartridge sample, check
that a center push releases the complete broad wing, including both ends.
Record seating, removal, corner marking and permanent set over repeated uses.
