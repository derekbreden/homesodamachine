  [ceiling · answered] Wrong print-direction classification for the full-Y spring corridor. The retainer prints on its Y-minus end, with source +Y as the build axis. The named Z197.445 surface is vertical in that print frame; it is not a horizontal print-down ceiling. The source corridor is 3.35 mm wide below the tangent 1.5 mm root blend. The native projects in print-tests/pointed-snaps bind the actual 90 degree X pose.
    alternatives: Filling this corridor joins the spring to its carrier and removes the required inward travel. Returning to a Z-up print changes the layer direction through the flexure. The ceiling classifier uses source Z for this class even when the recorded tilt is used for ledge findings.
