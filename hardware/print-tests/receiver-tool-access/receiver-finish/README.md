# Production receiver bases

The ASSE, PISCO, water-regulator, flow-meter and G-Ganen pump receivers retain
continuous bases and complete fitted hook bearings. Their rear-style stations
have no enclosed screwdriver tunnels. The front paired-pump cartridge keeps
its separate centered open flank pry windows.

The [first completed physical result](../physical-result/physical-result.json)
accepts the PISCO and regulator fits and identifies the ASSE assembled-axis
mismatch. The [ASSE alignment](../asse-alignment/geometry-review.json) raises
the installed adapter and its conforming cap cavity by 0.18 mm. The ASSE body
pocket, hex directions, shoulders and wings retain their dimensions. The other
cap meshes remain byte-identical to the accepted designs.

Follow the [assembly and service order](../README.md#assembly-and-service).
Work on the loose, unloaded units and expose the leaves before spreading them.
The current full enclosure, cold-core assemblies and installation views carry
these receiver bases. No additional ASSE fit coupon is requested.
