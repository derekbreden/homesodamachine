# Finished receiver openings

Complete ASSE, PISCO, water-regulator, flow-meter and G-Ganen pump receivers
with centered 3.5 mm by 3 mm screwdriver channels. Each passage starts at its
snap neck plane. Flat mouths meet the catch pocket and lower stock face with
3 mm shoulders. PISCO and water-regulator openings have a planar inner flare
into the curved cavities. All
hardware bearing profiles, axial end stops and cap geometry retain their fits.

Use the solid pointed caps from the [running complete set](../print-jobs.json).
The [geometry review](geometry-review.json) records identical cap STL bytes and
the full assembly's checked insertion and tool paths. [Fit readings](parts/fit.json)
show seated clearance and positive geometric pullout capture. Release force,
repeated bending and practical support removal remain physical checks.

The [prepared five-receiver plate](print-job.json) uses Mark1's left hardened
0.4 mm Standard nozzle, black PET-GF, three Classic walls, 15% gyroid receiver
bulk and Tree(auto)/Default. Every receiver keeps its production orientation.
The native full-bead minimum bed margin is 46.61 mm. Estimated duration is
6 h 16 min and filament use is 195.14 g. It is ready for the next clear Mark1
bed; the complete-set jobs continue running. The paired cartridge and all
caps are omitted because the complete-set plates already contain them.

Follow the [assembly and service order](../README.md#assembly-and-service),
including removal of the inlet header, drain connection, ASSE chain and meter
before PISCO, and removal of VK for PISCO tool access. The pump receiver is
tested with the equipment lid loose. The full enclosure, cold-core casing,
appliance scene and installation exports contain these receiver openings.
