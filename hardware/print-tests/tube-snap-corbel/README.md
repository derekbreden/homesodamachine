# East-wall tube snap fit print

The coupon is a crop of the production back-top solid. It retains the complete
wall behind the fill-tube seat, the corbel and the lifted free wing. The source
[`inputs.json`](inputs.json) sets the crop and [`coupon.rs`](coupon.rs) exports
the closed print mesh; [`parts/geometry.json`](parts/geometry.json) binds it to
the production solid. The coupon also carries the production breakaway stock:
contoured strips under the free wing, rooted on the corbelled wing below it.

The [Mark1 fit print](prints/2026-11-20-mark1-upper-wing-support/print-job.json)
uses the back-top's ceiling-down direction, PET-GF recipe and textured plate.
The support strips leave through the open tube mouth. A cooling tower gives the
small spring time between layers.

Seat the quarter-inch reservoir fill tube through the mouth. Observe the free
wing during insertion and release, how securely the tube stays seated, and
whether the corbel and wing print cleanly. Pry each support from the mouth with
a screwdriver, then observe the upper contact face and lower root marks. The
physical fit and cleanup await that observation.
