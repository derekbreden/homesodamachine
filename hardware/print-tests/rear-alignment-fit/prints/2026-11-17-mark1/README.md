# East rear alignment sliding trial

This plate carries the paired production rear halves through their real fore faces,
with the approved rounded key and corbelled receiver. The source coordinates in
[geometry.json](geometry.json) describe their seated relationship and retained layer
phase. The exact sliced package and its native review are bound by
[print-plan.json](print-plan.json).

[contacts.json](contacts.json) records the new standing-wall starts, key rooting,
rail contacts, support footing and removal access. The upper rail caps retain their
authored strip breaks; the bearing bridges across those narrow bands.

Remove the rail caps and cavity-floor tree before sliding the upper coupon fore
over the lower coupon. Compare both real fore faces and the lateral alignment at
the closed end, then record witness marks and sliding force with the physical result.
