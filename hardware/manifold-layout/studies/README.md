# Above-core arrangement

The selected [funnel-gate layout](funnel-gate/README.md) has a 35 mm aft-expanded
funnel with its outlet centered on the mouth and its drain snapped into the
removable frame, a compact B inlet seated directly in V-B, and a high aft-side return
above the PCB into Y-B. V-B lies square on the core roof east of the west
fore–aft PCB. V-A feeds Y-A through the flat under-head elbow and one low tube.
The measured DIGITEN meter stands on edge between the pump and CO₂ path,
fed by one continuous inlet tube from the core guide through an R14 bend
holder, with no exposed outlet tube to the rear fitting.
The inline YZMSZ native push-connect regulator estimate remains the water choice.
Native sidewalls, valve seats, relay bosses, the empty rear openings and
expanded upper-shell opening are included in the study. Ground and WAGOs
follow the primary components and full wire routes.

The study's whole-arrangement viewer has three highlight modes: assembly
quadrant, latest changes and normal. Its explicit mounting-parent map carries
cold-core-mounted equipment with back-bottom and the funnel/frame group with
front-top. Part details distinguish proposed attachments from located mounts.

This is the approved arrangement record. The production enclosure assembly,
complete harnesses and tube routes, and remaining equipment mounts retain
their separate scope. Rebuild commands, geometry results and source evidence
are beside the selected study.
