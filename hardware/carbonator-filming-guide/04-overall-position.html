SETUP 02 - CAMERA A

Set the overall view

Put the CT-10 on the floor, off one front corner of the bench. Keep the camera fixed.

  1. Mount the stock-lens GoPro on the tripod using its built-in 1/4-20 thread. Start around 1-1.5 m from the rig, then adjust to include the whole tube, nest and your working hands.
  2. Set the top edge below shoulder level, at upper chest or lower. Rehearse entering, reaching and leaving. Keep your head and shoulders out of every part of that movement.
  3. Keep enough downward angle to show the cap, while preserving the tube's rotation in the picture. Check shiny metal, windows and screens for face reflections.
Finished when

The viewer can see the hands, head of the tool and moving work. The picture stays below shoulder level throughout your movement.
