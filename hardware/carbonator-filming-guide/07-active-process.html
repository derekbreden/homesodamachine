SETUP 05 - BEFORE EMISSION

Set the active-process view

The detail pages are filmed with the laser disabled. Active welding uses a separate suitable camera arrangement.

  1. Use A only from a camera position outside exposure or through a viewing arrangement specified for this laser and use. Get that arrangement from XLaserlab or a qualified laser-safety provider before exposing a camera to the process.
  2. With emission disabled, rehearse the whole working movement and check the picture. The top stays below shoulder level; your hands, the tool head and moving tube remain visible.
  3. Clear temporary filming props and restore normal guarding before emission. Start recording before taking your working position. Make camera adjustments only after emission is disabled.
Picture exposure is not protection

A dark picture, photographic ND filter or ordinary welding glass does not establish protection. Keep an unprotected camera covered and outside exposure during emission, even when it is not recording.

XLaserlab: Class 4 and laser-specific protection. Sony: direct and reflected laser exposure can damage sensors. If a suitable active view is unavailable, use the dry demonstrations, actual observations and result footage to tell the story.
