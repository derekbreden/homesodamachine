SETUP 04

Set recording and focus

Make the image predictable before the real work starts.

A - stock lens4K / 30 fps / 16:9 / Linear
B - macro lensUse the detected Macro profile; select 4K / 30 fps / 16:9 where offered. Use a digital lens available in that profile.
Both positionsStandard SDR, HDR off, Natural color, High bitrate, HyperSmooth off on the rigid tripod.
Focus at BTurn the lens ring; use focus peaking and a recorded sample. Refocus after changing camera distance.
SpeechDJI receiver into iPhone. Voice Memos records the mic; GoPro audio supplies sync and shop sounds.
  1. Record a ten-second disabled sample with a representative hand movement, then a clear hold. Play it back at full size.
  2. Adjust camera or lighting if the joint is soft, a hand blocks it, reflections erase the edge, or a display flickers. Use shutter Auto as the first ordinary bench-light setting.
  3. For a static shot, start five seconds early and keep recording five seconds after the useful action. Name a clip aloud, then pause before the clean take.
Focus and exposure are different

The macro ring sets focus. If a bright reflection is white and featureless, move the light or camera and reduce exposure as needed. Brightness settings do not establish laser protection.

GoPro documents macro recognition, manual focus and peaking in its Macro Lens Mod guide and capture controls in Protune support.
