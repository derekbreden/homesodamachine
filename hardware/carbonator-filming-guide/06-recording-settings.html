SETUP 04

Set recording and focus

Make the image predictable before the real work starts.

A - GoPro4K / 30 fps / 16:9 / Linear. Standard SDR, HDR off, Natural color, High bitrate, HyperSmooth off on the rigid tripod.
B - R50 VMovie rec. size 3840x2160 / 29.97 fps / Standard (Long GOP). Movie rec. format XF-AVC S YCC420 8bit. Color mode: Picture Style Standard, HDR shooting (PQ) off. Movie digital IS off on the tripod.
Exposure at BMovie manual exposure: 1/60 s, f/8 to start, the lowest ISO that exposes well, zebras on. White balance 5600 K to match the lights.
Focus at BFocus mode MF. Turn the lens ring with MF peaking on and check the magnified view. Refocus after changing distance or zoom.
SpeechDJI receiver into iPhone. Voice Memos records the mic; camera audio supplies sync and shop sounds.
  1. Record a ten-second disabled sample with a representative hand movement, then a clear hold. Play it back at full size.
  2. Adjust camera or lighting if the joint is soft, a hand blocks it, reflections erase the edge, or a display flickers. If only a sliver of the joint is sharp, close B toward f/11 or move it back. On the GoPro, use shutter Auto as the first ordinary bench-light setting.
  3. For a static shot, start five seconds early and keep recording five seconds after the useful action. Name a clip aloud, then pause before the clean take.
Focus and exposure are different

The lens ring sets focus. If a bright reflection is white and featureless, move the light or camera and reduce exposure as needed. Brightness settings do not establish laser protection.

Canon documents the R50 V's movie size, format, manual focus and peaking in its online manual; GoPro documents capture controls in Protune support.
