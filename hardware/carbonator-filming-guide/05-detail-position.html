SETUP 03 - CAMERA B

Set the detail view

Use the CT25 to look into the joint without the near rim blocking it. Do this with emission disabled.

  1. Mount the R50 V on the CT25, above and to one side. Zoom to 30 mm and start about 20-25 cm from the selected joint arc; the lens focuses as close as 15 cm. Set focus mode to MF and turn the lens ring until peaking outlines the edge.
  2. Use the overhead arm only as far as needed. Follow the tripod instructions for leg position and counterweight. Keep support and cables outside the work and drive areas.
  3. Start both lights at 5600 K, about 50 cm from the subject: main at 50%, fill at 20%. Move the main light until wall and cap separate clearly. Reduce fill if the corner loses definition.
Finished when

Wall, cap and joint are sharp together. The metal has visible detail, the near rim does not hide the line, and there is space for labels.
