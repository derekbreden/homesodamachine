The two cameras can record at once: let A run through dry demonstrations and the attempt while B holds its detail. Keep the iPhone available for the DJI receiver and Voice Memos.

Prepare before work starts

Charge both cameras, phone, mic and lights. Format the R50 V's card in the camera, clear the GoPro's card, clean lenses, silence notifications and keep lens covers nearby. The light kit includes stands and power adapters.

These cameras serve ordinary setup, detail and result footage; page 7 covers the separate active-process camera arrangement.
