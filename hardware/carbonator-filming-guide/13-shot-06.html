Hold the unwelded detail

This is the key image for arrows and technical explanation.

CAMERAB - R50 V
RECORD40 s
  1. Move B to one short arc of the joint. Zoom to 30 mm and start about 20-25 cm from the surface. Focus with the lens ring and peaking, then refine position and aperture until wall, cap and joint line are sharp.
  2. Let the joint occupy about half to two-thirds of the picture width. Leave a plain area beside it for labels and keep the bottom strip available for subtitles.
  3. Withdraw your hands. Record a completely still thirty-second hold, then take a full-resolution still. Note the tripod position for the matching result shot.
Keep the take when

The joint line is sharp, reflections reveal both surfaces, and nothing moves through the clean hold.
