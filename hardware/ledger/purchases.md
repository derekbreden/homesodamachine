# Purchases

Project purchase ledger for the Home Soda Machine. Current scope: **2026**; IDs and the analytical schema support additional calendar years. Rows record project purchase values, quantities and fulfillment independently of payment evidence.

- [bom.md](/hardware/ledger/bom.md) allocates parts to one appliance.
- [tools.md](/hardware/ledger/tools.md) describes reusable tooling.
- [inventory.md](/hardware/ledger/inventory.md) describes current inventory and received items.

These views overlap the purchase ledger and are not additional expenditures. Each purchase row carries a persistent `purchase_id` in its inline comment. Keep that comment when moving or editing a row. [purchases.evidence.json](/hardware/ledger/purchases.evidence.json) owns source classifications, order relationships and distinct merchant charge/refund events; row costs remain here. See [the schema and private reconciliation contract](/hardware/ledger/purchases.schema.md) and [audit guidance](/hardware/ledger/purchases.audit.md).

Amounts include recorded project tax, shipping and item-specific tariffs. They are explicitly classified as a final vendor amount, an estimate or legacy/unverified in the analytical exports. A bundle, shipment or order price is a row total; only a price explicitly marked `ea` is multiplied by quantity. Fulfillment status does not establish whether a charge or refund occurred.

The generated procurement valuation includes ACQUIRED, ON-ORDER, MISSING and REPLACED purchases plus engineering services. It includes estimates and amounts whose payment evidence is unknown. Merchant-evidenced payments are a separate view and must not be added to the procurement valuation.
