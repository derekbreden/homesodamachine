# Bill of Materials — One Consumer Unit

Per-unit parts for a single finished appliance built on the **custom-carbonator** path specified in [hardware/README.md](/hardware/README.md). Carbonator: vertical 5" OD × 0.065" wall 316 welded SS round tube (OnlineMetals #12498, MTRs required) capped with 1/4"-thick laser-cut 316 SS circular plates from SendCutSend (`endcap-circular-2hole.dxf`), joined with the XLaserlab X1 Pro handheld laser welder. 1/4" NPT is direct-tapped into the plates (no weld-in bungs). Compressor is harvested from a countertop ice-maker; cold core is a 3D-printed foam shell with pour-in-place foam. Flavor reservoirs are custom printed translucent-PETG hard reservoirs ([printed-parts/cold-core/reservoir/reservoir.py](/hardware/printed-parts/cold-core/reservoir/reservoir.py)), two per build.

Tools, fabrication equipment (welder, slip roll, shop press, dishing dies), and duplicate SKUs live in [purchases.md](/hardware/ledger/purchases.md) only. Per-build consumables — anything used up making one unit, regardless of whether it ships in the product (mixing cups, gloves, citric acid, PTFE tape, cutting fluid, etc.) — live in this file with the rest of the per-unit parts.

First-pass draft. **Pricing convention: delivered cost** (product + shipping + tax) drawn from resolved order history in [purchases.md](/hardware/ledger/purchases.md) wherever the SKU has been ordered or acquired; list price for forward-plan SKUs not yet purchased. Pack-amortized lines show the math in the description (e.g., `$31.08/2`). Expect revisions.

## 7. Printed mechanical parts

Per-unit filament for every printed part shipped with one finished appliance, one row per part. **The enclosure and faucet exterior parts are PET-GF** (Polymaker Fiberon PET-GF15): the four enclosure quadrants, the integral pump mount and its lower retainer, the display cover plate, the funnel frame, the two-piece faucet shell, its lever and the above-counter plate. The loose collet press in the install kit is PET-GF too, and so is the drain-elbow cradle under the funnel frame's web, which prints on the exterior's 0.4 mm settings. **So is the cold core's own stack** — the smooth 2.38 mm shell, caps and lids, using the left 0.4 mm Standard nozzle with three Classic loops and 15% gyroid ([foam-shell/README.md](/hardware/printed-parts/cold-core/foam-shell/README.md#print-settings)). The [funnel cover](/hardware/printed-parts/zone-c/funnel-cover/README.md) is plain black PETG with a solid plate and mouth-contacting skirt. The small parts inside are PETG: the PRV shroud, the reed bridge and the fuse clamp. The four syrup-wetted reservoir parts print in **PETG Translucent Clear** so the customer reads fill state through the wall; the rest print black. The nameplate prints in black and white PET-GF, and so do the six bulkhead chips and their tube collars, each in the colour its station names — blue for carbonated water, white for tap water and the separate OVER return, red for CO2, and the two flavour rings in the same black as the wall, naming nothing ([`y-wall-of-back-top/_y_wall_dimensions.py`](/hardware/printed-parts/enclosure/y-wall-of-back-top/_y_wall_dimensions.py) `chip_filaments`). All PETG here is Bambu PETG Basic at one price, so colour changes what a part looks like, not what it costs. `Mass (kg)` and `$` are per-line totals (quantity included). **Mass is filament, not geometry** — a print is wall loops around a sparse grid, so the front-top's 1379 cm³ of solid comes off the plate as 456. The full shell uses its production native slice's feed mass. Other rows estimate the shell plus infill their own plate lays, taken off that part's CAD volume and surface area at the settings its print record states ([`_bom_masses.py`](/hardware/scripts/_bom_masses.py) `PROFILES`; measured plates name their committed `.3mf`, while unsliced groups remain explicit estimates), times density (PETG 1.27 g/cm³, PET-GF 1.43 g/cm³, Polymaker's own `filament_density` in `Fiberon PET-GF15 @BBL H2C`). The four reservoir parts, the collet press and the funnel cover use 100 % infill, so those rows are their geometry. The faucet's four PET-GF pieces share the saved H2C 0.4 mm profile: two wall loops, 0.24 mm layers and 15 % grid infill ([faucet-petgf.md](/hardware/printed-parts/faucet/faucet-petgf.md)). Against the two plates that have been sliced and measured the model reads −1.5 % (cold-core shell) and −11 % (front-top); the second gap is the tree supports, which are filament off the same spool and are not billed here. `hardware/scripts/_bom_masses.py` is what derives it all: it reads the shell slice and the other rows' STEP solids, writes this Mass column and the `$` that follows from it, and the mass reader runs when this allocation is updated. PETG $11.20/kg (Bambu PETG Basic Black + Translucent Clear, $224.04 ÷ 20 kg); PET-GF $25.02/kg (Polymaker Fiberon PET-GF15, $225.18 ÷ 9 kg).

The wetted surface is the print itself, qualified by [`wetted-surface-test.md`](/hardware/printed-parts/cold-core/reservoir/wetted-surface-test.md)'s screen against the actual syrup — no spool's resin compliance stands in for it.

The soda inlet bend holder is PET-GF with the accepted 2 mm wall, 3 mm legs and R14 tube centreline. Its mass and machine-time allocation use the small-part recipe as an unsliced estimate; the physical fit record does not measure retention lifetime.

## 13. Mechanical attach hardware

**Two M3 bodies, one hole.** ruthex's
RX-M3x5.7 and RX-M3Sx4.0 are the same insert but for length — same ⌀4.6 knurl over the same
⌀4.0 recommended hole — so which one a station takes is a question about the depth of the bore
it already cuts and never about its section. [49](M3_LONG_INSERTS) stations have that depth and
take the full-length body; the [30](M3_SHORT_INSERTS) that do not are each paying for something
else, named at the SKU rows below. Both bodies press with the same T18 tip into the same pocket.

The top cap adds [8](DECK_INSERTS) full-length inserts for the PCB and water pump, plus [3](B_FILL_CLAMP_INSERTS) full-length inserts and [3](B_FILL_CLAMP_SCREWS) recessed M3 × 10 screws for the B-fill passage clamps. ## External / user-supplied (not shipped)

- **5 lb CO2 tank** + refills (~$25/refill at welding/homebrew shops) — the tank only; the appliance ships with the CGA-320 primary regulator (§4) that mounts on it
- **Flavor concentrate** — SodaStream or BIB syrup
- **Tap-water source under the cabinet** — a cold-water line the kit's tee lands on. Scenario A, drawn in the [install guide](/hardware/install-guide/README.md), is an existing 1/4" plastic line on a push fitting and takes the John Guest union tee with no tool. Scenario B is an existing 3/8" chrome angle stop with a braided riser, and takes the John Guest ASVPP1LF between the two — one wrench at its two 3/8" joints, and the appliance's own branch pushes in. It is written out in the [install guide](/hardware/install-guide/README.md) (both tees are in §3). The kit includes the tee, the 1/4" LLDPE and the water filter; the customer brings the line.
- **The countertop opening** — a 1-3/8" hole through the counter for the faucet. Cut before installation begins ([`assembly/faucet-and-umbilical.md`](/hardware/assembly/faucet-and-umbilical.md) "Not in scope"); on stone it is a diamond core bit and usually a countertop pro. The [install guide](/hardware/install-guide/README.md) states the hole and who cuts it.
