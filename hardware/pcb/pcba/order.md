# Ordering this board from JLCPCB

The fab/assembly parameters that are **not** encoded in the gerber or BOM files and must be selected on
the JLCPCB order form. This is the project's first **assembled** JLCPCB order (the earlier order was a
bare through-hole carrier, `mini.tsx`), so the assembly-service selections matter as much as the
fab ones.

Upload set (regenerated by `bun render-board.ts pcba.tsx`): `out/pcba.gerbers.zip` (gerbers + drill),
`out/pcba.bom.csv`, `out/pcba.cpl.csv`.

## ⚠️ Must-select — the board is wrong without it

- **Via covering: Epoxy Filled & Capped (POFV).** 144 of the board's 152 vias sit **inside SMD pads**
  (via-in-pad — the plane-stitch geometry, see [`plane-stitching.md`](plane-stitching.md)). If vias are
  left tented/open, molten solder wicks down those 144 holes during reflow → starved/open joints on the
  exact pads that sit on vias. This is a paid add-on and a checkbox on the order form; nothing in the
  gerbers can encode it. **Non-negotiable for this board.**

## Fab parameters

| Parameter | Value | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Layers | **4** | L1 top signals + V12 island · L2 3V3 plane · L3 5V plane · L4 GND plane + signals (`pcba.tsx` header) |
| Size | **85.05 × 72.85 mm** | outline x[−68, 17] y[−36.3, 36.5] |
| Thickness | **1.6 mm** | standard |
| Outer copper | **1 oz** | the ampacity rules (`ampacity-audit.ts`) size the ~3.3 A V12 traces for 1 oz external |
| Inner copper | 0.5 oz | JLC 4-layer standard |
| Surface finish | **ENIG** | required, not HASL — via-in-pad + fine pitch (WROOM 1.27 mm castellations, USB-C 0.5 mm, SOD-923/SOT-353). HASL is bumpy/incompatible with POFV coplanarity |
| Min trace/space | 0.14 mm realized | clears JLC standard (0.127 mm) — standard process, no advanced-node tier |
| Min drill / via | 0.3 mm drill, 0.5 mm via pad (0.1 mm ring) | JLC standard |
| Controlled impedance | **No** | USB 2.0 full-speed + RS-485 at these lengths are tolerant; no impedance callout |

## Assembly parameters

- **SMT assembly, top side only** (single-side — all 94 placements + the paste layer are top; bottom
  paste is empty by design).
- **Through-hole (THT) assembly — also required.** The field connectors and a few parts are wave/hand
  THT: all XH wafers (J1–J9, J11, J13), the KF301 screw terminal (J10), the K7805 SIP module (U10),
  the CR2032 coin base (BT1), and the USB-C shield through-tabs (J14). Confirm JLC's THT-assembly service
  is selected — these are common, non-flagged THT parts, but it is a separate service tier from SMT.
- **Parts / stock:** 30 unique **Extended** parts (feeder fees ~$80–90 one-time + stock dependencies).
  Re-verify stock and lock the shallow-stock fallbacks **before** ordering — see the *Stock risk &
  second-sources* section in [`jlcpcb-parts.md`](jlcpcb-parts.md). 2026-08-03 sweep: the board
  carries CH340B `C81010` for U13 (~3,871 in stock; both CH340C codes sat Parts-Pre-order-only that
  day). Shallow but sufficient: `C5359637` (J1 XH-9P, ~310) and `C47023` (MCP23017, ~765 and
  falling).

## Two 30-second checks in JLC's gerber preview

Every render hard-asserts the fab set before writing it: each via and plated hole claims its own
`drill.drl` hit one-to-one on position and drilled diameter (non-plated likewise in `drill_npth.drl`),
no hit exists that the board didn't ask for, and every inner-layer termination carries its ring — the
*Drill coverage* section of [`requirements.md`](requirements.md). Two things are still worth an eyeball
in JLC's own viewer:

1. The **four USB-C shield cutouts** render as *slots*, not 8 round holes (they use the Excellon `G85`
   slot command).
2. The **four corner mounting holes come through plated** (they are in the PTH drill file, not the NPTH
   one) — this is intended (bonds the M3 standoffs to GND); confirm it's what you want.

## Edge rails — the assembly tier decides

Whether JLCPCB adds assembly edge rails is a function of the **PCBA tier**, not a standalone checkbox
(capabilities table + the "How to Add Edge Rails and Fiducials" help article, 2026-05-20):

- **Economic PCBA — rails & fiducials "not necessary."** JLC mounts the board on their own shared
  production panel; its rails and fiducials are theirs, routed away before delivery. The checkout shows
  **no edge-rail prompt on an Economic order — that is correct, nothing was missed** and the delivered
  boards carry no rails.
- **Standard PCBA — rails & fiducials "necessary."** The flow does not ask at checkout; the rails are
  settled at the post-order engineering/DFM review. Min width 5 mm, and **delivered attached by
  default** — ask at order time (remark field / support ticket with the order number) if they should be
  removed after assembly (paid post-processing).

This board cannot dodge the requirement on the Standard path: JLC's clamp rule wants component bodies
≥ 2.5 mm off the board edge, and **all four edges are inside it** — SW1/SW2 north faces 1.38 mm off the
north edge, the WROOM body 1.23 mm off the west edge (with the antenna silk overhanging it), the south
wafer bodies ~1.8 mm, J10/J1/J2 + FID1/FID2 near the east edge. FID1–FID3 satisfy the fiducial half
on-board; on the Standard path JLC adds rail fiducials regardless.

**What the checkout actually does (observed on the first assembled order):** it auto-adds the rails and
states the delivered size — *"The board size is modified to be 85mm\*82.8mm due to adding two 5mm edge
rails on the shorter sides."* Read the **numbers**, not the phrase: 85 × 82.8 means the 72.85 mm
dimension grew by 2 × 5 mm, i.e. **the rails ride the NORTH/SOUTH (85.05 mm long) edges** and the west
edge stays rail-free — the wanted placement (JLC puts rails along the longer sides so the board doesn't
bounce during placement). Their "shorter sides" means the shorter *dimension* being extended, not the
shorter edges; rails on the E/W edges would have read 95 × 72.8 instead. Rails-attached is JLC's
default; the first order selected **"Depanel boards & edge rail before delivery: Yes"**, so boards
arrive de-railed at the true 85.05 × 72.85 outline (the PCBA tray fits that).

## Polarity confirmation — the LED engineering query

Standard-PCBA file review emails a polarity-confirmation query before production: an annotated top
view marking anode/cathode on the five indicator LEDs **D2–D6**, asking the customer to confirm. Only
the LEDs are queried — the `KT_0603R` / `KT_0603G` / `Blue_light_0603` parts are the ones whose
library-vs-CPL orientation convention JLC will not assume. The 1N4148W (D7, unambiguous cathode band)
and the other polarized parts are not asked about.

The answer differs between D2 and D3–D6. Confirm both and proceed.

| LED | Rotation | Anode/+ | Cathode |
|---|---|---|---|
| D3–D6 | `pcbRotation={180}` | WEST, to its series R (R11–R14) | EAST, to GND |
| D2 (ERR) | `pcbRotation={0}` | EAST, to 3V3 | WEST, to R10 → IO15 |

D2 sits on IO15 (MTDO), whose level at reset decides whether the ROM prints its boot log on U0TXD. A
~45 kΩ internal pull-up cannot lift a node an LED to GND clamps to its forward drop (~1.6 V, under
the ~2.5 V V_IH), so ERR hangs off the rail instead: the pin idles at 3V3 through the dark LED, and
firmware lights it by driving IO15 LOW. D2 also stands 0.3 mm east of the D3–D6 column — the KT-0603
courtyard reaches 1.947 one way and 1.728 the other, and at rot 0 its long side faces R10.

`pcba.cpl.csv` exports each LED at its own placement rotation, so the annotated view JLC returns is
the board as drawn. The query arrives on the standard flow with no paid Confirm-Parts-Placement
checkpoint bought, and is the LED polarity's only pre-arrival verification — board #1 gets a
diode-tester check on all five pads before the batch is powered.

## Panelization

Single-board assembly is fine at D2C hand-build scale. If ordering as a panel with breakaway rails, put
rails on the **N / E / S** edges only — the **west edge is the no-rail edge** (flush USB-C receptacle +
the WROOM antenna silk overhangs it). The perimeter placement already reserves rail clearance on the
other three edges.
