Lower the prepared core

Hold it upright with the magnet at the top. The centre hole slides over the PETG bore sleeve.

  1. Centre the core over the sleeve. Lower it vertically, chamfered end first.
  2. Press evenly around its outer top face to start it into the shell. Keep it square as it descends.
  3. Keep the magnet seated in its pocket. Use the short insert for the final push on the next page.
