Change to PETG

Let the warm Aero chamber cool, then fit the clean Textured PEI plate.

Before starting

≤35 °C

Chamber temperature after the Aero jobs.

During PETG

70 °C

Textured PEI bed. Chamber heater off.

  1. Let the chamber cool to 35 °C or below. Wash the Textured PEI sheet with dish detergent and warm water, rinse and dry.
  2. Install the Textured PEI sheet, holding it by the edges. Use its clean texture directly for this PETG job.
  3. Open magnetic-float.3mf as a project. Keep the project's supplied PETG Translucent settings and map its material to the dried PETG Translucent in the left AMS feed.
  4. Leave the prepared core, short insert and plastic scraper beside the printer. Read the insertion pages before starting.
