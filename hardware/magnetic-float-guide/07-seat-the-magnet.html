At the pause · build guide

Seat one RC62

The printed body stays on the Engineering plate. Put only the ring magnet into the open pocket.

  1. Wait for the programmed pause and toolhead parking before reaching into the printer.
  2. Leave the sheet mounted and the float attached. Separate one RC62 from the stack and lower it squarely into the pocket.
  3. Seat it fully on the pocket floor with its hole coaxial with the guide bore. Its flat faces lie horizontal, below both pocket rims.
  4. Clear loose strings without shifting the body. Keep the central guide bore open.
Resume condition

The ring sits flat and fully down. Nothing projects into the covering path, and no hand or tool remains inside the printing area.
