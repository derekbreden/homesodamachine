Prepare the inserts · build guide

Clean the entry edges

Both pieces have a chamfer on the lower outside edge and the lower bore edge. These ends enter the shell first.

  1. Clip the thin brim from each cooled foam piece. Work around the edge with flush cutters.
  2. Pare away the remaining brim lip with a sharp craft knife, following the printed edge. Leave the cylindrical fitting surfaces and the chamfers intact.
  3. Clip loose strings from the central bores and the core's magnet pocket. Brush away all loose crumbs.
  4. Keep the pieces dry and covered on the bench until the shell is ready.
Orientation

Tall core: magnet pocket up, chamfered end down.
Short insert: chamfered end down, broad flat face up.
