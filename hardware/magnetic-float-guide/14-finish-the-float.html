Finish · build guide

Release and finish

The cooled print is the completed float. Keep the roof uppermost when fitting it to its guide.

  1. Lift the cooled Textured PEI sheet out of the printer. Flex it gently to release the float.
  2. Clip off the outer brim and pare its remaining lip flush with the bottom edge.
  3. Clip loose strings at the bore entrances and brush the exterior clean. Keep the printed PETG bore and shell walls intact.
  4. Slide the 3.175 mm guide rod through the 4.8 mm central bore, with the printed roof facing upward.
Top and bottom

The roof is the smooth, printed top surface above the magnet. The bottom carries the Textured PEI imprint. Keep that orientation when installing the float.
