# Lillium-fed prototype

The dispense-side prototype uses an external Lillium carbonator. Its reported
assembly and cutover procedure are kept in the
[prototype record](https://github.com/derekbreden/homesodamachine/blob/78c7aa7b01f90052b7ae4ae4bd7d0c9da992c17f/hardware/assembly/lillium-cutover.md).
The linked procedure belongs to that recorded article.

For parts printed from the current checkout, use the current
[pump and plumbing assembly](internal-plumbing.md),
[electronics assembly](electronics-bay.md),
[enclosure sequence](enclosure-mechanical.md) and
[faucet assembly](../printed-parts/faucet/faucet-shell/ASSEMBLY.md).
Their fasteners are allocated in the [mechanical BOM](../ledger/bom.md#13-mechanical-attach-hardware).

The integrated machine's tap-water inlet, carbonator, refrigeration and funnel
are specified in the [machine assembly sequence](README.md).
