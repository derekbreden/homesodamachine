CC-09

Close the reservoirs
— gasket, cap, vent

Cold core · 09/15Kitchen edition

The reservoir closes once: membrane and ring into the cap, gasket on the wall top, cap down over the rod, six screws into the body inserts.

  1. Press six ruthex M3 into the body bosses — the ⌀4 × 7 mm pockets at the wall top (4 insert + 3 relief).
  2. Drop the PTFE membrane into the cap pocket (⌀13 × 0.5 into ⌀13.2 × 2.5), then press the TPU 90A retaining ring — ⌀13.4, a light interference fit.
  3. Lay the gasket on the wall top — 5 mm perimeter ring with a ⌀10 pad at each screw position.
  4. Lower the cap — its register boss swallows the rod top (CC-08); vent boss up.
  5. Six M3 × 12 SHCS into the counterbores — each seats 4 mm into its insert with 2 mm spare at the tip.
