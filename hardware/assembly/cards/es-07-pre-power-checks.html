  1. AC continuity: each AC-1 pigtail through its Wago to every named out-leg, long coils included.
  2. AC isolation: H↔G, N↔G, H↔N — no continuity between buses.
  3. DC: DC-1 trunk pair through the block to every named branch; J10 V12 must trace to PSU +.
  4. Ground bus: every ring-terminal pigtail back to the AC-1 G stub.
  5. J5 loom: seated square, screw terminals tugged.
