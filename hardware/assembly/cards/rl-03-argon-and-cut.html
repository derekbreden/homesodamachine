RL-03

Start argon, cut the loop

Refrigerant loop · 03/8Kitchen edition

Argon first, cuts second. One continuous low-pressure flow guards the entire loop-open period.

⚠
Vented ≠ empty — R-600a lingers in the oil and low spots. Flowing argon sweeps fuel out ahead of the heat.
  1. Rig argon to the BPV31 flare port. Open the valve: a few PSI — flowing — before anything is cut.
  2. Cut the suction line close to the evaporator — between evap outlet and compressor inlet.
  3. Cut the cap tube at its evap-inlet end — drier, cap tube, helix stay untouched, at factory length.
  4. Strip the finger-plate evap + hot-gas bypass (solenoid, line, tee). Argon spills from both ends.
Tools Uniweld RHP400 (welder argon cylinder) · flared 1/4″ stub + Joywayus flare nut · charging hose · RIDGID 150 · Mastercool 70025
⛔
Never break the argon flow — first cut to vacuum-on (RL-06): it is the fuel sweep and the drier's dry blanket. A lapse = saturated desiccant, capillary icing.
Ink stays · dashed gray comes out. Argon (blue) enters at the BPV31 and exits both cuts.
Done whenBoth cuts open with argon flowing out of each; drier + cap tube + helix + bonded pair intact at factory length.
