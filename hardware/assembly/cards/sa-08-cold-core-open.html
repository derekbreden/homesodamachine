Sub-assembly · 09/9Kitchen edition

Everything that goes inside is in, the floor is closed, and the top is not on. This is what the pour goes into — 8 lines in, each cut and bent its whole length, standing out of the top.

Standing in the shell

The vessel with its coil, both reservoirs, the copper plugs. The bag pockets and reed channels take no foam. The long traverse is the arc around the coil at the reservoir sides, through the embedded wraps.

Out of the mouth

9 up their own conduits’ columns, one to a wall slot. Filled high, drawn low, all three. The PRV vent leaves sideways, under the copper in the west lane. Each of the other seven is one length from its own fitting to whatever takes its far end.

Not on yet

The top cap, its lid, and everything SA-04 stands on them. The cap comes down over these lines, onto the band the pour leaves clean. Its crown is populated on a bench of its own.

Mouth-up, no down-channels. One shot finds the outer gap, the corner pockets and the tank cavity in parallel.

Done when the floor is sealed and every line is up on its own conduit’s column, in one pour’s reach.
