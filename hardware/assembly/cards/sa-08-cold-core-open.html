Sub-assembly · 08/8Kitchen edition

Everything that goes inside is in, the floor is closed, and the top is not on. This is what the pour goes into — 8 lines standing out of the top, where the cap comes down over them.

Standing in the shell

The vessel with its coil, both reservoirs, the copper plugs. The bag pockets and reed channels take no foam. The long traverse is the arc around the coil at the reservoir sides, through the embedded wraps.

Out of the mouth

7 lines standing 20 mm proud, one to a wall slot. Filled high, drawn low, all three. The PRV vent leaves sideways instead, under the copper in the west lane. What the other seven stand proud is the cap’s own thickness.

Not on yet

The top cap, its lid, and everything SA-04 stands on them. The cap comes down over these lines, onto the band the pour leaves clean. Its crown is populated on a bench of its own.

Mouth-up, no down-channels. One shot finds the outer gap, the corner pockets and the tank cavity in parallel — the floor is the one way out it must not have.

Done when the floor is sealed and every line stands its own 20 mm proud on its conduit’s column, in one pour’s reach.
