Sub-assembly · 02/4Kitchen edition

The core closed and poured, and nothing standing on its lid yet. This is the state SA-03 starts from.

Clamped

Lid onto cap, cap onto shell. The 12 M3 × 25 SHCS are the pour clamp and they stay: six per face, through lid and cap into the shell-face inserts. The lid does not come off again.

Poured

Two-part 2 lb closed-cell foam, 1:1. In through the pour hole, out through the vents. The lid is what holds the shape while it rises.

Cleaned off the exits

All 7 cap conduits, both vents, the pour hole. Trim cured foam back to the plate and no further — into a cradle boss is into a mount. Every conduit mouth open, every countersunk lip clean.

Nothing on this card mounts anything. The valve cradles, the pump’s four columns and the anchor ribs are printed into that face and stand empty until SA-03.

Done when the stack is clamped, the pour has risen and cured, and every mouth in the lid’s outer face is open and clean with the face itself bare.
