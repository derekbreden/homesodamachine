SA-02

Foam shell top
cap lid — fill

Sub-assembly · 02/4Kitchen edition

The top cap and its lid, poured and free of the shell, with nothing standing on that face yet. This is the state SA-03 starts from.

Clamped

6 M3 × 25 SHCS, through lid and cap into the shell’s own inserts. The shell’s top face is the fixture and the screws are the pour clamp — expanding foam lifts an unclamped lid. Back them out after cure: the cap and lid come away as their own unit.

Poured

Two-part 2 lb closed-cell foam, 1:1. Mouth-up, in through the lid’s pour hole, air out through its two vents. The foam rises around the deck-mount columns and cures.

Cleaned off the exits

All 7 cap conduits, both vents, the pour hole. Trim cured foam back to the plate and no further — into a cradle boss is into a mount. Every conduit mouth open, every countersunk lip clean.

Nothing here mounts anything. The valve cradles, the deck mounts and the anchor ribs are printed into that face and stand empty until SA-03.

Done when the pour has cured, the six screws are out, and every mouth in the lid’s outer face is open and clean with the face itself bare.
