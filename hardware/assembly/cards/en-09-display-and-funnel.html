Enclosure · 09/9Kitchen edition

Two features in the box’s top — one takes parts, the other is an opening and takes nothing at all.

  1. Seat the display glass in its bezel counterbore — the glass is the datum. ESD-safe, leads loose.
  2. Lay the TPU ring on the glass — on the border, off the screen. No ring, no grip on the display.
  3. Lay the cover plate onto the ring, two M3×8 into the facet’s heat-sets — nothing proud of the face.
  4. Find the funnel opening behind the facet — nothing to fasten. Deburr the collar seat: residue under the brim holds the funnel crooked.
  5. Sight straight down: the fall corridor to V-B clear. Do not fit the funnel — it goes in at FS.
Parts + tools Waveshare 4.3B B0D925SBYF · display gasket · cover plate · 2 × M3×8 · 2.5 mm hex · ESD mat · deburr tool. No part for the opening.
