Three risers climb out the top face to the back-panel triangle. Blue tube to the blue ring — the top vertex.

Carbonated water — two lengths, meter between: blue LLDPE. carb-1: cold-core bottom-plate outlet, west immediately (refrig-2 stands ~9.5 mm off it — no head-on exit), up the front face west of the bag fall, east into the DIGITEN. carb-2: meter east into the lane under the SeaFlo, aft to the column east of the ASSE, up into the blue-ringed PP1208E. Square-cut, push, tug — four PTC ports.

  3. Flavors run bare: black LLDPE, V-G → left vertex, V-J → right — warm-in, warm-out, low duty.
