umbilical bulkheads

Internal plumbing · 05/6Kitchen edition

Three risers climb out the top face to the back-panel triangle. Blue tube to the blue ring — the top vertex; black lands on the flavor vertices.

  1. Carbonated water first: blue LLDPE, cold-core bottom-plate outlet → up the +Z channel → the blue-ringed PP1208E. Square-cut, push, tug.
  2. Insulate that run: CARGEN nitrile segments (1/4″ ID × 3/8″ wall), butted — foam-shell exit to bulkhead face.
  3. Flavor A: black LLDPE, V-G outlet → the left-vertex PP1208E.
  4. Flavor B: black, V-J outlet → the right vertex. Flavor runs bare — warm-in, warm-out, low duty.
The foam-shell exit is where the thermal mass ends and the chilled multi-meter run to the faucet begins — insulation picks up exactly there.
Stock + tools blue LLDPE · black LLDPE · CARGEN foam · Mudder cutter
Black-into-either-black is unambiguous — both flavor tubes share the panel-side bundle; only the blue ring must match.
Done whenThree risers landed and tug-tested — blue on the blue ring, carbonated run insulated end-to-end, flavor risers bare.
