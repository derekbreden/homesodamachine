The volumetric proof behind PT's surface screen — bare vessel, on a bench, before passivation, before any fitting.

  1. Bare vessel only — welded + tapped, nothing else. No PRV, no fittings, no cold core.
  2. PTFE tape on every 1/4″ NPT: plugs in the unused ports, the SENCTRL gauge on one.
  3. Pump on via the KOOTANS nipple — 1/2″ end on the hose's swivel gasket, 1/4″ end into a port.
  4. Pump to 180 PSI, hold 30 min. Working pass position: no visible drop on the gauge, no weep at welds or threads.
  5. Hunting slow weep? The glycerin gauge supports hour-scale soaks before passivation.
