GT-02

Push-to-connect —
cut, click, tug

Technique · 02/5Kitchen edition

Every John Guest-style collet in the machine takes the same three-beat: square cut, push to the stop, tug back. The tug is the connection.

  1. Cut square — no chamfer, no angle (Mudder cutter, one squeeze). The O-ring seals on the tube's side wall; a scarred or ovaled end weeps.
  2. Push past the collet to the tube stop. Two resistances: the collet's grip, then the O-ring — the seat is the hard stop behind both.
  3. Tug back. The collet teeth set on the pull — an un-tugged tube is resting, not held. Every PTC in the build gets this (IP-06 walks them all again).
  4. Release only square: press the collet ring flat against the body while pulling the tube — a tilted collet chews the tube wall; re-cut before re-using the end.
Materials 1/4″ OD LLDPE · Mudder cutter · the fitting's own collet
The tug is load-bearing — carbonated water at 90 PSI sits behind several of these joints, and both of the machine's inlets are one of them. Cut square, click, tug, every time.
Done whenEvery PTC end square-cut, seated to the stop, and tug-tested — no joint left "resting".
