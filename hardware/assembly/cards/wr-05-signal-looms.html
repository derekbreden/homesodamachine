WR-05

Signal looms

Wiring · 05/6Kitchen edition

One pre-fabricated loom per connector, seated on its labeled wafer. The looms are plain wire — pull-ups, dividers, damping, and ESD clamps all live on the board.

J4 + J7 share the same 7P housing — land each by its loom label; a swap puts J4's 3V3/V5 on J7's reed inputs.
  1. Cold-core set — J4 (SIG-1 · SIG-9) + the J6/J7 reed looms, ~600 mm; GND explodes in the far-end Wago.
  2. Shelf zone — SIG-4 off J4: meter inline on the riser 24 mm ahead of its union, boss east.
  3. Displays — SIG-6 UART ribbon up the umbilical (J3); SIG-7 RS485 + V12 to the front face (J9), ~1 m.
  4. Floor — J11 to the MQ-6, mounted low — R-600a sinks.
  5. Continuity-test each run before anything zip-ties down.
