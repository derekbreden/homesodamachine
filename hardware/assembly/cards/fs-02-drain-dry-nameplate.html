Drain dry + nameplate

Finish + ship · 02/5Kitchen edition

The appliance ships DRY, the plate ships signed — this builder's hand, applied before mounting, never after.

  1. Prove it dry three ways — tilt-listen ~15° (no splash, no thunk); sumps zero flavor; faucet cracked de-pressurized: no discharge, no vent. Wet = back to burn-in for a re-purge.
  2. Match plate to serial — SERIAL = the link under it = logs/<serial>/ archive. Two that disagree is scrap; another unit's number never mounts.
  3. Sign, then mount — pen first (touch-dry ~30 s), then the two M3×8. A plate in its pocket cannot be signed.
Laser-engrave path (pending test-print review): register to the datum, run the pen-trace vector, vacuum before mounting.
