Finish + ship · 02/5Kitchen edition

The appliance ships DRY, the plaque ships signed — this builder's hand, applied before mounting, never after.

  1. Prove it dry three ways — tilt-listen ~15° (no splash, no thunk); sumps zero flavor; faucet cracked de-pressurized: no discharge, no vent. Wet = back to burn-in for a re-purge.
  2. Match plaque to serial — plaque text = logs/<serial>/ archive = QR target. A QR that scans to another unit never mounts: escalate.
  3. Sign, then mount — archival pigment-ink pen inside the recess (working default; touch-dry ~30 s). Mounted is permanent — never mount first and sign in place.
Laser-engrave path (pending test-print review): register to the datum, run the pen-trace vector, vacuum before mounting.
