Stage DC distribution
+ 12 V branches

Electronics shelf · 05/7Kitchen edition

The 12 V spine: PSU secondary into the DC block, branches out to relay #2 and the board. Everything the board feeds draws through one run — DC-4.

⛔
Reversed J10 polarity cooks the board — the polarized bulk cap, the buck, and the drivers. V12 east screw, GND west, both silked.
  1. Mount the DC block beside the power-tray.
  2. DC-1 trunk: PSU +V and −V into the block's rails — 16 AWG, forks at the PSU, ferrules at the block.
  3. DC-2: block + → relay #2 COM. NC stays empty.
  4. DC-3 staged: + off NO, GND off the block — coiled, flagged, SeaFlo disconnects (WR-04).
  5. DC-4: + and GND to J10, ferrules under the screw clamps — verify V12 traces to PSU +.
Tools ferrule + fork crimpers · 16 AWG · multimeter
The relay switches the + leg only. COM and NO are both 12 V positive; the contact either bridges them or does not. NC stays empty. The pump's GND never touches the relay — it comes off the block's rail and rides beside DC-3's + conductor.
~3.3 A peak against a 17 A-rated block. 10 valves, both peristaltic pumps, the condenser fan, display 12 V, and both logic rails all draw through DC-4.
Done whenDC-1 landed, DC-2 terminated, DC-3 coiled + flagged, DC-4 under the J10 clamps — V12 screw traces to PSU +.
