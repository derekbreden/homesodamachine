PV-03

Drill the rod register
— both plates

Pressure vessel · 03/14Kitchen edition

A blind 9/64″ seat for the level rod, in the inside face of both plates — bottom seats the rod for its tack weld (PV-06), top captures the tip at closure (PV-09).

⛔
Must not break through. 0.100″ to the drill-point tip leaves 0.150″ of plate as the 90 PSI pressure boundary.
  1. Inside face up. Register at (0, −2.007″) — the −Y cap axis, clear of both ports.
  2. Clamp the disc. Slowest press speed (~740 RPM), Tap Magic on the point.
  3. Depth stop at 0.100″ to the tip — prove it on a scrap disc before any real plate.
  4. Drill both plates identically. The register is what tells the faces apart from here on.
Tools WEN 4208T press · 9/64″ M35 cobalt, 135° split-point · Tap Magic EP-Xtra · depth stop · scrap disc
Drill before weld + passivation — the fresh 316L passivates with the vessel (PV-12).
Done whenIdentical blind register in both plates — no light through either. Binds at closure? Open the top plate to 5/32″, never force.
