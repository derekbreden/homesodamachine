GT-04

JST-XH crimping —
wings, lance, housing

Technique · 04/5Kitchen edition

Open-barrel double-wing pins: conductor wings on bare strands, insulation wings on jacket — then the housing's lance does the holding. Every board loom ends in these (CA-01).

  1. Strip ~2–3 mm — strands to the conductor wings, jacket to the insulation wings, neither crossing zones.
  2. Pin in the die by its wings, wire in from the back; one squeeze forms both wing pairs in sequence.
  3. Inspect end-on: conductor wings curled into the strands (a "B" profile), insulation wings on jacket, a strand brush at the front. Tug.
  4. Insert until the lance clicks — contact face to the lance window; a light pull holds.
  5. Pin 1 on the silk, label at the housing — J7/J4 share the shell; the label is the guard (CA-02).
Tools open-barrel crimper (CA-01) · Klein stripper · XH pins + housings
The housing label is the misplug guard — J7 and J4 share the same 7P shell; a swap puts 3V3/5V on the MCP reed inputs (CA-02's one critical).
Done whenEvery pin "B"-profiled, tugged, lance-clicked, pin 1 on the silk — and the housing wears its loom name.
