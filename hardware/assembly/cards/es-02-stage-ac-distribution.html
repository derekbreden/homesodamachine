One Wago 221-413 lever per conductor — H / N / G — and the ring-terminal stack that is the ground bus. Every mains pigtail is cut and flagged here.

  1. Press the three mains Wagos into the fore wells, butt-end first, ports to the room; label the bays H / N / G.
  2. Cut + prep the AC-1…AC-3 pigtails per the table — 16 AWG, ferrules at every Wago end.
  3. AC-1: inlet ends stay long (~150 mm slack) for the C14 solder tabs (WR-02); flags "AC-1 H / N / G".
  4. AC-4/5/6 are not cut here — they are one 18 AWG SJOOW lead, built + landed at WR-02.
  5. Land the ring-stack on its boss; stage green pigtails — PSU chassis, pressure vessel, faucet SS plate, compressor body — load ends bare, labeled.
