ES-01

Prepare the shelf trays

Electronics shelf · 01/7Kitchen edition

Two printed trays are the shelf: the pcba-tray under the controller board, the power-tray under the mains. Inserts go in hot, before anything mounts.

  1. Heat-set M3 inserts into every mounting boss — 4 on the pcba-tray (one per board corner hole), plus the PSU, relay, and ground-stud bosses on the power-tray.
  2. Verify each insert is flush with its boss face.
  3. Placement is the tray CAD: the controller PCBA alone on the pcba-tray; PSU + relay #1 + Wago AC distribution + ground ring-stack on the power-tray.
  4. Relay #2 + the DC distribution block have no committed bay — stage them beside the power-tray.
pcba-tray bosses are standoffs: ⌀7, ruthex insert, 5 mm tall — the standoff clears the THT tails under the board.
Tools Hakko FX-888D + T18 tips · ruthex M3 inserts + M3 × 8 SHCS (bom.md §13) · ESD mat
power-tray — boss patterns for the PSU (turned 90°), relay #1, and the ground stud; three 45° press-fit slots take the Wagos butt-end-first.
