PV-01

Chamfer the end-plate
port holes

Pressure vessel · 01/14Kitchen edition

The 7/16″ tap-drill holes arrive laser-cut — with a recast lip at every edge. Chamfer before any tap touches them: the taper tap needs a square seat to start concentric.

  1. Chuck the 82° countersink — 5/8″ or 3/4″ body. The 1/2″ body spans only 0.031″ over the 0.438″ hole: no room to set chamfer width deliberately.
  2. Press slow, Tap Magic on the cut — the 5-flute grind chatters if crowded.
  3. Break BOTH faces of every hole — flip each plate: 2 holes × 2 faces × both plates.
  4. What it buys: the recast lip is gone and the taper tap starts in a square seat — first threads concentric with the hole (PV-02).
Tools JNB Pro 82° M35 countersink · WEN 4208T press · Tap Magic EP-Xtra
