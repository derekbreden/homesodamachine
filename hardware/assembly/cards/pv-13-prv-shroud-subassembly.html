⛔
Step 2 is the last manual access to the pull-ring — the shroud encloses it for good. Free + snappy, or replace.
  1. SV-125 into the elbow's lateral F outlet — PTFE tape, snug hand-tight + a turn or two on the hex.
  2. Pull-test. Vise the elbow by the M-end; ring straight up, light force — lifts clean, snaps back.
  3. Shroud on from the pull-ring end — past hex + discharge port, to the ⌀18.8 mm seat.
  4. 100% RTV bead around the joint, wet-fingertip fillet. Foam-tight is the bar, not airtight. Cure 20-30 min to handle, ≥24 h before install.
