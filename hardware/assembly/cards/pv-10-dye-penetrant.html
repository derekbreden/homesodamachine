PV-10

Dye-penetrant inspection
of the closure welds

Pressure vessel · 10/14Kitchen edition

Dye wicks into whatever breaks the surface — developer pulls it back out red on white, localized, which hydro can't.

  1. Clean + dry both closure welds — IPA on a lint-free wipe, flash off. PT runs before any water touches them.
  2. Penetrant on both rim fillets + the port-weld areas. Dwell ~10 min to wick in.
  3. Wipe the excess: dry wipe first, then one lightly IPA-damp. Never spray solvent at the part — flooding flushes dye out of fine defects.
  4. Mist a thin coat of developer. Read within ~10 min — key at right. Any indication: clean, re-weld the spot, re-PT.
Accept/reject limits for an indication are an open item.
Materials Cantesco P101S-A penetrant · D101-A developer · IPA · lint-free wipes
Solvent-removable visible dye — standard grade is fine; the water rinse + citric soak (PV-12) strips any sulfur/chloride residue.
Done whenClean white on both closure welds — no lines, no dots — and all chemicals wiped off before hydro.
