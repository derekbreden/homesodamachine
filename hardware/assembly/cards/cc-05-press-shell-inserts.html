CC-05

Press the shell inserts

Cold core · 05/15Kitchen edition

12 ruthex M3 shorts anchor the two cap stacks — 6 in the shell's top face, the same in the bottom. The printed pocket aims; the iron supplies heat and a straight push.

  1. Find the 6 positions on the top face — a boss at each corner, plus the mid-side pair offset ±18 mm, opposite signs.
  2. Soldering iron tip on the insert; press straight down until flush. The pocket is ⌀4 mm × 4 mm for the insert, with another 4 mm of relief below.
  3. Flip the shell; the same pattern into the bottom face — the bottom cap lands on it with no rotation.
  4. Sight every pocket: insert flush, bore open. These are the only thread in the cap stack — cap and lid are clearance — so they clamp the lid for the CC-06 pour, then take the cap + gasket at CC-15.
Stock + tools ruthex M3 short heat-set inserts B0D39W228K · soldering iron
Heat, not force — let the iron carry the insert down; the relief below keeps the screw tip off PETG.
Done whenTwelve inserts seated flush — six per face, every bore open — screw pattern identical top and bottom.
