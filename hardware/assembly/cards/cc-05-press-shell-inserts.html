CC-05

Press the shell inserts

Cold core · 05/15The appliance

20 ruthex M3 inserts anchor the two cap stacks — 10 in the shell's top face, the same in the bottom. The printed pocket aims; the iron supplies heat and a straight push.

  1. Find the 10 positions on the top face — one per corner, two per long wall at ±40 mm, one centred on each short wall.
  2. Soldering iron tip on the insert; press straight down until flush. The pocket is ⌀4 mm × 5.7 mm for the insert, with another 2.3 mm of relief below.
  3. Flip the shell; the same pattern into the bottom face — the bottom cap lands on it with no rotation.
  4. Sight every pocket: insert flush, bore open. These are the only thread in the cap stack — they clamp the lid for the CC-06 pour, then take the cap at CC-15.
Stock + tools ruthex M3 heat-set inserts B08BCRZZS3 (RX-M3x5.7, NOT the short bag) · soldering iron
Heat, not force — let the iron carry the insert down; the relief below keeps the screw tip off PET-GF.
Done when20 inserts seated flush — 10 per face, every bore open — screw pattern identical top and bottom.
