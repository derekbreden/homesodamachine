WR-02

AC mains — C14 to
compressor + PSU

Wiring · 02/6Kitchen edition

Six runs in schedule order — and one jacketed lead, the only mains that reaches the compressor.

⚠
Dead bench — the C14 stays off line until commissioning. Mains colors: black hot · white neutral · green ground.
  1. AC-1 first — land the long shelf pigtails on the C14's solder tabs; the inlet lands direct on the block.
  2. Pre-build the compressor lead — 18 AWG SJOOW ×3, ~400 mm: H_switched + N + G in one jacket.
  3. Run it whole to the compressor — one jacket the length of the run; the donor PTC module is already on the block, so nothing is opened up to lay it in.
  4. Fan out the last ~50 mm — H_switched + N disconnects onto the block's spades; G rings under an M3 foot screw, on the bushing (WR-01).
  5. Shelf end: H_switched on relay NO (fork), N at the N pole, G on the bus — ferrules at the block.
Tools ratcheting crimper (Faston die) · 18 AWG SJOOW · multimeter
One jacket, three conductors, one body — the block, the PTC and the foot bond are all on the compressor itself; the fan-out is the last ~50 mm.
Done whenAC-1 through AC-6 landed, the jacket unbroken from shelf to fan-out — the AC web is ready for its WR-03 cold check.
