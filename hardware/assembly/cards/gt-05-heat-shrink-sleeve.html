GT-05

Heat-shrink + sleeve —
collars, lacing, braid

Technique · 05/5Kitchen edition

Three jobs, one material: collars that cap a braid's cut ends, lacing that turns loose wires into one stiff piece, and plain strain-relief over a joint.

  1. Size 2:1 over the bundle — recovered diameter under the bundle's, supplied diameter over it. Cut square, slide on before terminating.
  2. Comb the conductors parallel before any sleeve or lace goes on — a crossed pair locks in forever.
  3. Collars: braid frays at the first cut — a collar on every cut end, half on braid, half on bundle (CA-01).
  4. Lacing: a band of shrink between parts — one between every reed — makes a drop-in-stiff column (CC-06).
  5. Heat evenly, rotate, stop at full recovery — snug, shiny, no scorch. The heat gun, never a flame.
Materials 2:1 heat-shrink · PET braid (CA-02 sleeve column) · heat gun
Slide it on first — shrink that can't pass the terminal goes on before the crimp; the order is the technique.
Done whenEvery braid end collared, every column laced stiff, every band fully recovered — nothing loose, nothing scorched.
