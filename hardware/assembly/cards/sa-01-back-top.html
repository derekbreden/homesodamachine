Enclosure back top

Sub-assembly · 01/8Kitchen edition

The piece on the bench, open faces up. All of this goes on before it comes down — afterwards the volume is the service bay and there is no way in.

Screwed to printed bosses

6 bodies — PSU, controller PCBA, both relays, ground stack, C14 inlet. M3 in from the room into a ruthex short, one boss per hole off the +X wall — 17. The lever nuts beside them press into wall wells and bolt to nothing.

Clamped in their own holes

6 crossings — four PP1208E unions, the ABU44 CO2 inlet, the GASHER check a hop inboard. Each crosses the rear wall and is drawn up by its own nut. fluid-18 goes into the lower nozzle union here — 472 mm, the longest run on the machine, its far end left loose.

Strapped into printed ribs

ASSE 1022 chain, DIGITEN meter, WR1110 regulator, 3 tube crossings. Tie through the cavity first, body second. 8″ on the chain, 6″ on the regulator, 4″ elsewhere.

No drip tray. Its channels are printed on the −X wall and stand empty here — the tray goes in at EN-08, east through that same wall, with the box standing.

Done when every body is fastened by its own method and nothing in this volume still wants a hand. AC pigtails hang free, unpowered.
