AB-04

Clean cycle + air purge

Acceptance · 04/7Kitchen edition

Water rides the syrup route to the nozzle, then air chases the rinse — both reservoirs end rinsed and dry, hands-off.

  1. Clean Channel A — tap water fills reservoir A through the manifold, then flushes out the nozzle. First pass pours faintly tinted.
  2. Chase it clear — command a follow-up pass. Rinse that never clears = reservoir cleanability problem, escalate.
  3. Clean Channel B — same cycle. A valve that fails to open or close flags on the config display.
  4. Purge both dry — funnel dry and open to air: pump pulls air in, pushes the slug out; the nozzle sputters dry and the reeds walk down to empty. A stalling pump or a reservoir that won't drain: escalate.
