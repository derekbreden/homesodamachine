Enclosure front top

Sub-assembly · 02/8Kitchen edition

The other top piece, worked the same way — over on its ceiling, both open faces up. Every seat under this manifold is the piece’s own material: no plate, no bracket, no part.

Pressed into printed seats

9 valves — V-C through V-J, on two panels. Four corner posts into four blind sockets and the body boss down on the boss tops, in a plate fused wall to wall on each deck. Nothing bonded, no screw.

Strapped under printed trays

2 Kamoer pumps. Head crown up to the plate, boss in its bore, then two 8″ ties apiece under the part’s own stamped bracket. The straps carry the pump; the tray it hangs under carries nothing.

Set into the walls

3 lever nuts, the display glass. The nuts stand butt-first in printed wells and bolt to nothing. The glass drops into the bezel counterbore on the 45° facet — ESD-safe, leads loose.

No funnel. The hopper opening is an opening — the funnel drops in at FS on its own brim, and nothing fastens it.

Done when every valve is down on its four posts, both pumps are strapped under their trays — and the hopper opening is empty.
