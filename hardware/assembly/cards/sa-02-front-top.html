Enclosure front top

Sub-assembly · 02/9The appliance

The piece as it leaves the bench: everything on it, pump bay and funnel opening still empty. The carrier is built into it at IP-04 and comes with it from here on.

Riding its own journals

4 tees on 2 joined halves. Held in X and Z by journals printed in the fixed body, free in Y. 8 ties hold the tees to the carrier and 2 springs return it to park. No screw reaches it from outside.

Down on printed bosses

9 bodies — V-C through V-J on two panels, and the display’s cover plate. Valve posts press into blind sockets. The display plate takes two M3 into heat-sets in the facet’s inset.

Closed under that plate

The display glass and its gasket. Glass into the facet’s bezel, gasket over it, plate lapping all four sides. ESD-safe, leads loose.

Set into the walls

2 lever nuts. Butt-first into their printed wall pockets.

Done whenEvery valve is on its four posts, the carrier is tied and returning to park on its own, the display is under its plate, and both the funnel opening and the pump bay are empty.
