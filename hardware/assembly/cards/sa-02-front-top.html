Sub-assembly · 02/9The appliance

The other top piece, worked the same way — over on its ceiling, both open faces up. Every seat under this manifold is the piece’s own material: the panels wall to wall, the wells pockets in its walls.

Down on printed bosses

9 bodies — V-C through V-J on two panels, and the display’s cover plate. Four corner posts into four blind sockets and the body boss down on the boss tops — nothing bonded, no screw. The plate takes two M3 into heat-sets in the facet’s inset.

Closed under that plate

The display glass and its gasket. Glass into the bezel counterbore on the 45° facet, soft ring over it, the plate’s border lapping all four sides. ESD-safe, leads loose.

Set into the walls

2 lever nuts. Butt-first into wells printed in the wall itself. They bolt to nothing.

The steel is aboard; no pump, no funnel. The collet plate goes in through the Z− face looking up here, down the slot in the bay floor until its two shoulders land on that floor’s top. Both Kamoers ride the pump cartridge into the front wall’s bay and the funnel drops into its opening — both with the box standing.

Done when every valve on its four posts, display under its plate, collet plate on its shoulders, funnel opening and pump bay empty.
