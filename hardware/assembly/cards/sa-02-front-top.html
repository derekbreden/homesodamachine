Enclosure front top

Sub-assembly · 02/9The appliance

Work with the piece loose and its pump bay empty. Load the filled tee body in the order given by internal-plumbing.md §3.

Load the moving tee assembly

Bare tees, then carrier halves, then aft valves. Enter through the open rear, lower each cup through its outer tee well, then seat it outward into the side recess. Left at release; right at park. Close the lap with two M3 screws from the empty pump bay. Fit the aft valves, tie each tee twice and load springs through the inner wells. Fore valves and flexible links go last.

Down on printed bosses

9 bodies — V-C through V-J on two panels, and the display’s cover plate. Valve posts press into blind sockets. The display plate takes two M3 into heat-sets in the facet’s inset.

Closed under that plate

The display glass and its gasket. Glass into the facet’s bezel, gasket over it, plate lapping all four sides. ESD-safe, leads loose.

Set into the walls

2 lever nuts. Butt-first into their printed wall pockets.

Done when every valve on its four posts, carrier tied and returning, display under its plate, funnel opening and pump bay empty.
