CC-07

Seat the reservoir
rods + floats

Cold core · 07/15Kitchen edition

Two rods, two floats — never welded. Printed bosses capture each rod at both ends, and the float donut must ride the cavity far wall or the reeds see nothing.

  1. Take the two 174 mm rods cut at PV-05 — square, deburred; a burr scores the float bore.
  2. Harvest each YXQ float: keep the SS capsule + its ferrite ring; discard the switch body and cable.
  3. Seat the rod in the body boss — the blind bore on the wet slope; the tip bottoms on solid PETG, the slope stays unbroken.
  4. Slip the float on — it must ride the cavity far wall. The rod parks it there: ~4 mm magnet-to-reed is comfortable; by ~10 mm there is no pull-in. Top stays free — the cap register captures it at CC-08.
