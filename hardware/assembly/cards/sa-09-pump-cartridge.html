SA-09

Pump cartridge

Sub-assembly · 09/9The appliance

Build the cartridge upright on its lower cradle. The brackets carry the pumps; the top cap holds them down.

Seat both pumps

Lower 2 Kamoer KPHM400s into the cradle. Each 68.6 mm bracket lies flat on its lands around the 62.61 mm head. Check both are seated before fitting the cap.

Clamp from above

Lower the cap over both motors. Its 53 mm octagons locate the bosses. Tighten the 2 M3 × 10 screws evenly from above; the cap must sit flat.

Set the tube projection

Fit 4 tube ends. Each tip stands 19.03 mm beyond its pump outlet tip. Join the LLDPE to the pump tubing and secure it before marking. The tubes bottom with the carrier aft and cartridge fully seated. Squeezed fore, they bottom 2.15 mm short of seating.

No screw crosses into the box. The collet plate is part of front-top — pull the face against a braced box and the plate stops each collet’s nose while the tube draws back out.

Done when both pumps are down all round in their bores, the cap drawn flat on both brackets, and four fresh stubs aft of the face.
