SA-09

Pump cartridge

Sub-assembly · 09/9The appliance

Two printed pieces closed on the pumps between them. The unit leaves the bench out of the bay — it goes in at IP-06, and only after the chassis is shut.

Closed in the case

2 Kamoer KPHM600-SW3B17 pumps. Each 68.6 mm stamped bracket lies on the cradle lands, standing proud of its 62.61 mm head all round, and the cap presses on that lip through 53 mm octagons. The bracket is the whole load path.

Screwed down from above

2 M3 × 10 into the cradle’s centre heat-sets. Top-access, drawn evenly. A cap that will not sit flat is a pump that is not seated — lift it rather than pull it down with the screws.

Standing in the air

4 LLDPE stubs aft of the face, each 19.03 mm beyond its pump outlet tip. Landed into the BPT tube itself, never onto the moulded barb, and zip-tied down onto it. Fit that joint before marking the projection — its engagement is extra stock.

No screw crosses into the box, and nothing on this unit is fitted in place. Its four faston receptacles stay on the motor tabs through every service; the cord follows the cartridge out.

Done whenBoth pumps sit down all round in their bores, the cap is drawn flat on both brackets, and four stubs stand aft of the face at one length.
