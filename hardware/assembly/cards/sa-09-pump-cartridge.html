SA-09

Pump cartridge

Sub-assembly · 09/9The appliance

The front of the machine with both pumps inside it. One block, parting on the pump’s own bracket plane — pump cartridge over, cap under, worked face-down.

Down on their own axis

2 Kamoer KPHM400s, one per tray in the deck. Each boss into a 53 mm octagon until the head’s crown lands all round. The bore takes a pump in plan and in clock.

Screwed up from under

The cap, on 2 M3 × 10 into heat-sets on the lane between the pumps. A pump is carried by its own stamped bracket — 68.6 mm across a head of 62.61 mm — lapping the cap’s top face all round. A cap that will not sit flat is a pump that is not seated.

Pushed over the barbs

4 barb tubes, each 8.729 mm proud. Fully over the barb, standing aft off the face. They thread the collet plate’s holes into the anchor tees as the deck goes in.

No screw crosses into the box. The steel stays in its seat in the bay floor — pull the face against a braced box and the plate stops each collet’s nose while the tube draws back out.

Done when both pumps are down all round in their bores, the cap drawn flat on both brackets, and four fresh stubs aft of the face.
