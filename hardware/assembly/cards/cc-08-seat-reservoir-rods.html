CC-08

Seat the reservoir
rods + floats

Cold core · 08/15The appliance

Two rods, two floats — never welded. Printed bosses capture each rod at both ends, and the ASA Aero float runs clear of the cavity far wall beside its reed column.

  1. Take the two 176.5 mm rods cut at PV-05 — square, deburred; a burr scores the float bore.
  2. Use two one-piece ASA Aero floats: 36 × 28 mm, RC62 centred at 14 mm. The running bore is 4.8 mm.
  3. Seat the rod in the body boss — the blind bore on the wet slope; the tip bottoms on solid PETG, the slope stays unbroken.
  4. Slip the float on and sweep its full travel. The rod axis is 20 mm from the wet far wall, at x=±106.25, y=32.5 mm. The 2 mm nominal body gap lets it slide freely. Calibrate reed closures in liquid before fixing the column heights.
