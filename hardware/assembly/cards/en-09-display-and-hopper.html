Display into the facet,
hopper opening clear

Enclosure · 09/9Kitchen edition

Two features in the closed box’s top — one takes a part, the other takes nothing at all. The hopper is an opening, not a component.

  1. Seat the display glass in its bezel counterbore on the 45° facet. The glass is the datum and today its only retention — an open item. ESD-safe, leads loose for WR.
  2. Find the hopper opening behind the facet, cut at the placed funnel’s own collar. Nothing to fasten, no pattern to land.
  3. Deburr and wipe the collar seat — residue under the funnel’s brim holds the basin proud and crooked.
  4. Sight straight down. The fall corridor to the pump row must be clear. Do not fit the funnel — it goes in at FS.
Parts + tools Waveshare 4.3B B0D925SBYF · ESD mat · deburr tool · shop light. No part for the hopper.
