  1. Sweep back-bottom’s slab clear before its column closes — it is the core’s datum, and a bead of brim on it is a high spot the whole ride bears on.
  2. Square the core on its cart — bottom lid flat, front face against the stratum’s aft plane, 0 by intent. Nothing goes under it.
  3. Confirm zero tension, and re-check the BPV31 cap — the mouth passes over it once the ride starts.
  4. Sight all 9 cap conduits — mouths open, lips clean — before the lid’s tenants go on (SA-04, IP), and they go on BEFORE the ride.
