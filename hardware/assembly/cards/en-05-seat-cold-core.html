⚠
The loop is charged. The stratum went in first precisely so the core drops straight down without dragging a brazed joint.
  1. Sweep the slab clear — it is the core’s datum, and a bead of brim under it is a high spot, not a blemish.
  2. Lower the core straight down — its bottom lid is the whole bearing surface. Nothing goes under it.
  3. Mate its front face flush to the stratum’s aft plane — 0 by intent. Confirm zero tension, and re-check the BPV31 cap.
  4. Sight all 7 cap conduits — mouths open, lips clean — before anything stands on the lid.
