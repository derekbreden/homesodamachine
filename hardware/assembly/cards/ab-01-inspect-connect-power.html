AB-01

Inspect, connect, power on

Acceptance · 01/7Kitchen edition

The commissioned chassis meets the bench rig. CO2 goes live and must sit silent; water stays closed; boot is idle — sensors live, actuators dark.

  1. Walk the exterior — FC sign-offs complete: no displaced fittings, no cracks, C14 recess clean, all 3 umbilical unions seated.
  2. Prove the pumps — Zone C door open, funnel out: both Kamoer heads seated, leads spaded to DC-5. Funnel back, door closed.
  3. Connect the rig — all of it on the back wall (right): water, CO2, mains. Primary to 90 PSI; the water-side valve stays closed.
  4. Power on — boot is idle: sensors live (table), actuators dark. Any CO2 hiss at a joint: reseat, re-test before continuing.
