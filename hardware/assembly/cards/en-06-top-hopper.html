EN-06

Clear the
Zone C opening

Enclosure · 06/8Kitchen edition

Nothing installs here. The hopper is an opening cut in the enclosure-front-top print, not a part — the silicone funnel drops into it, and that happens at FS.

  1. Find the opening in the top wall right of the display facet — cut at the funnel's collar, so it is already the right size. Nothing to fasten, no pattern to land.
  2. Deburr and wipe the collar seat. The funnel's brim underside rests on this face; brim residue or a stray support nib holds it proud and the basin reads crooked from above.
  3. Sight straight down the opening. The fall corridor to the pump row below must be clear and stay clear — the funnel spout's drop feeds the V-B hopper gate through open air.
  4. Do not fit the silicone funnel. It drops in at finish-and-pack (FS), after the wipe-down, not now.
