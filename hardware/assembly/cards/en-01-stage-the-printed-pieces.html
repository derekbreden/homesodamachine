Every insert goes in while the box is still open bench — once the pack is in, half of them are unreachable.

  1. Print-inspect all 7 — and wipe every interior face, above all the floor slab the core lands on.
  2. Heat-set every ruthex M3: the Y seam’s 4 sockets (Z seams take none), the C14’s 2 in its tunnel, 17 +X wall bosses (EN-06), the panel’s 2 up.
  3. Dry-fit every seam empty. Slide each top home — both aft, entering fore of home, onto their stops — and telescope the halves; every Y plug drops home.
  4. Then take it all apart again — the front pack goes in on open floor; the bay rides in with back-top.
  5. Nameplate blank and collet plate to the build folder; the nameplate goes on at FS, not here.
