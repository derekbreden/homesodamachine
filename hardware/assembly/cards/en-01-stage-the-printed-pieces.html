Heat-set every insert on the open bench, before the pack blocks access.

  1. Print-inspect all 6 — and wipe every interior face, above all the floor slab the core lands on.
  2. Heat-set by labelled size: 25 short M3 — 6 Y-seam, 2 C14 and 17 +X wall bosses; 2 full-length M3 condenser fingers; 2 M1.4 at the fixed male-contact seat; 4 M5 floor posts. The display cover takes no screws or inserts.
  3. Dry-fit every seam empty. Slide front-top aft from the fore end and back-top fore from the aft end, each onto its stops. Telescope the halves; every Y plug drops home.
  4. Take it apart again. Front pack installs on open floor; bay rides in with back-top.
  5. File the display cover, TPU gasket ring and nameplate in the unit's build folder (EN-10 / FS).
