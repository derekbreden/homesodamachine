Enclosure · 01/9Kitchen edition

Every insert goes in while the box is still open bench — once the pack is in, half of them are unreachable.

  1. Print-inspect all 5 — four quadrants and the cartridge — and wipe every interior face, above all the floor slab the core lands on.
  2. Heat-set every ruthex M3 insert: seam pods and sockets, the C14’s 2 bosses, the 17 +X wall bosses (EN-06). Press to flush.
  3. Dry-fit every seam empty. Every lip telescopes without shaving; every cross-pin plug drops home.
  4. Then take it apart again — the pack goes in on open floor, not through a mouth.
  5. Nameplate blank and collet plate to the build folder; the nameplate goes on at FS, not here.
Parts + tools five PETG pieces + collet plate + nameplate (bom §7) · ruthex M3 B0D39W228K · iron + tip · deburr tool
Four quadrants and a cartridge — front-bottom, front-top, back-bottom, back-top, and the pump cartridge that rides out of the front pair’s bay. Every face is a wall of one of them. The quadrants print standing on a Z face, the cartridge face-down on its own outer skin, and bed-fit holds all five to the H2C bed.
