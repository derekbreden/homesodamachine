PV-12

Citric-acid passivation

Pressure vessel · 12/14Kitchen edition

One soak restores the chromium-oxide layer at the weld zones — what lets 316L shrug off carbonic acid for the life of the vessel. Same treatment commercial bright tanks get.

  1. Mix ~1 qt of ~4 % solution — Viva Doria food-grade citric, about 1/20 of the 2 lb bag per vessel.
  2. Soak 30-60 min in the Cambro tub. A mild citric soak doesn't consume it — same tub, build after build.
  3. Rinse thoroughly with water, then dry.
  4. The sequence is the point: after hydro (PV-11) — failures get re-welded, re-introducing oxide — and before the sparge goes in (PV-14): silicone + sintered stone don't belong in the soak.
Materials Viva Doria citric acid · Cambro 6 QT polycarbonate tub · water
Order: after hydro, before the sparge. Re-welds put oxide back; the stone + silicone never see the acid.
