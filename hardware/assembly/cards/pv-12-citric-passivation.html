PV-12

Citric-acid passivation

Pressure vessel · 12/14The soda machine

Citric treatment removes free-iron contamination and supports passivation. Start with clean, oxide-free surfaces; cleaning and passivation acceptance still require qualification.

  1. Clean before the bath. Remove cutting fluid, penetrant residues, scale and unacceptable heat tint by the qualified method. Citric is not the weld-oxide removal step.
  2. Mix ~4 % solution with Viva Doria food-grade citric; use enough to contact every surface.
  3. Soak 30-60 min in the Cambro tub. Establish bath condition and reuse limits in qualification.
  4. Rinse through all four open ports, drain and dry. Treat the clean, qualified jet elbow separately; rinse through its female port and drilled passage.
Materials Viva Doria citric acid · Cambro 6 QT polycarbonate tub · water
Order: after hydro, before the fittings. Re-welds require renewed cleaning. The jet elbow is treated separately while its bore remains accessible.
