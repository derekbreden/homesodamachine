EN-03

Shroud the compressor,
then stand it on the floor

Enclosure · 03/9Kitchen edition

Shroud first, on the cart — once the core lands behind the stratum, this cover barely fits.

⚠
120 V terminals + a PTC at 140–150 °C in normal use live under this cover.
  1. Gland into the 7/8″ hole, locknut snug, facing the power column.
  2. Lower the shroud over the terminal block + PTC — ≥10 mm clear every side. The open bottom clears the feet and stubs.
  3. Ring-terminate the green bond lead at the ⌀6 mm hole, routed toward the ground stack.
  4. Shroud + condenser into front-bottom as one mated pair, on the slab, packed forward, shroud west.

5-sided open-bottom box — drops over the terminal end; the bond lead waits for the ground bus (AC-6, WR).
Nothing in the box fastens the stratum today. The shroud’s two ⌀4.5 holes, the compressor’s four grommeted feet and the condenser’s ears all want a printed station and none is cut — floor and mating hold it. When the schedule lands, the screw bears on each grommet’s steel bushing: the grommet is the isolation element and is never crushed.
