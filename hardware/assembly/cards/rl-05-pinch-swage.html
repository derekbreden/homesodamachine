RL-05

Pinch-swage
the capillary tie-in

Refrigerant loop · 05/8Kitchen edition

A 1/4″ stub meets a 0.031″ hair-bore. No reducer — the stub itself collapses onto the cap tube, then braze.

⚠
Braze under the flowing argon (RL-03) — residual fuel sweeps out ahead of the torch. Fresh wet rag on the stub, hard against the plug face — the one from RL-04 is dry, and the printed PETG plug (Tg ~80 °C) is about 68 mm down the copper.
  1. Slide the cap tube (from the drier, cut at RL-03) into the coil-inlet stub, out of the front wall on the port lane — the flank the condenser stands against.
  2. Squeeze with the Knipex Pliers Wrench — smooth parallel jaws: flats, not bites.
  3. Rotate 60°, squeeze again — repeat until the bore closes down evenly onto the cap tube.
  4. Wrap a freshly soaked rag on the stub, tight to the plug face, and keep it wet through the braze.
  5. Braze the swaged mouth dry — no flux, BCuP-5 — under the flowing argon, flame aimed away from the core. Check the plug face after: no gloss, no sag, no scorch smell.
Tools Knipex 86 01 180 Pliers Wrench (7.25″, smooth parallel-jaw) · BCuP-5 15 % Ag · TS8000 + MAP-Pro · Scotch-Brite maroon · wet cotton rag
Knipex 86 01 180 — smooth parallel jaws collapse the bore in flats; the progressive 60° turns bring it down evenly, no reducer fitting.
Cap-tube length is factory length (cut of RL-03). If it ever changes substantially, a refrigeration tech recalculates it for the new load.
