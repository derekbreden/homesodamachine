CC-13

Stack the copper plugs

Cold core · 13/15Kitchen edition

Four printed plugs tile the slot from the lowest copper to the wall top. Each grips the wall like a binder clip, and each end arch closes around half a tube — the tube is the gap.

  1. Order the stack: lower (the tall one) → middle → upper → top. Only the top plug has a flat top.
  2. Slide each down the open slot from above — web in the slot, wall seated in the gap between the flanges: the binder-clip grip.
  3. Arches seat the tubes — never crushing. Plug above takes the upper half, plug below the lower; faces meet at the tube centers.
  4. Top plug last — flat side up, flush with the wall top.
  5. Leave the slivers to the pour — the strip at the slot bottom and the bands around each tube fill at CC-14.
