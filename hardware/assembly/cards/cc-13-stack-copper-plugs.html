Stack the copper plugs

Cold core · 13/15Kitchen edition

3 printed plugs close the wall's two lane slots, each gripping its slot like a binder clip — and the tube is the gap between end arches.

  1. Sort them by lane first. lower then middle tile the west lane — the PRV vent crosses UNDER the copper there; top is the whole port stack, because one line crosses that lane.
  2. Slide each down its own slot from above — web in the slot, wall in the gap between the flanges: the binder-clip grip.
  3. Arches seat the tubes — never crushing. A plug's bottom arch takes the upper half of the line under it.
  4. Both stacks close flat at the wall top — only lower arches at both ends.
  5. Leave the slivers to the pour — the rounded slot bottom and the bands round each tube fill at CC-14.
