Bolt the compressor
down to the slab

Enclosure · 03/9Kitchen edition

The one body the box fastens — four printed posts, one up through each foot.

⚠
120 V terminals + a PTC at 140–150 °C stand bare in the power box. Nothing lands there until WR.
  1. Power box to the front. The bolt pattern is symmetric and the box is not — it is the only thing that tells the two ends apart.
  2. Lower it onto the four posts — each rises through a ⌀14 plate hole to the plate’s crown. The posts locate it.
  3. One M3 down into each post, bearing on the grommet’s steel bushing — never on the rubber.
  4. Loop slack, before and after. It arrives brazed to the charged loop (RL-08) and is never taken off it.

Upright, plate down, power box forward — the oil sits in the can’s bottom and the pickup is gravity-fed, so a yaw is the only freedom it has.
Nothing else on this floor is fastened. The condenser and the cold core are held by the slab and by each other, so this plate is what the stratum gets squared off.
