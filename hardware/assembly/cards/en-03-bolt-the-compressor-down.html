Enclosure · 03/9The appliance

The one body the box fastens — four posts.

⚠
Keep the donor terminal cover. It must be intact and secure over the 120 V terminals + PTC before power.
  1. Covered power box to the front. Inspect the moulded cover: present, uncracked, unmodified, donor retention secure.
  2. Lower it onto the four posts — each rises through a ⌀14 plate hole. The posts locate it.
  3. Fender washer over each post, then one M5. Draw down until the washer lands on the crown, then stop — the post sets the squeeze.
  4. Loop slack, before and after. It arrives brazed to the charged loop (RL-08) and is never taken off it.
