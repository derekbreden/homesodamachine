Bond the tank probe +
route the sensor leads

Cold core · 10/16Kitchen edition

The compressor cycles on the vessel wall, not the coil — the DS18B20 reads the tank OD under a foil patch, and both probes' leads leave through the shared slot.

  1. Tape the DS18B20 flat against the vessel OD under a patch of 3M 425 foil — bare TO-92, leads heat-shrunk — the setpoint sensor.
  2. Route both probes' 3-conductor leads up and out the shared slot, alongside the four lines (CC-12).
  3. Seat the leads for sealing: the copper-plug clamp (CC-13) and the foam over-pour (CC-14) must close around them.
  4. They join SIG-1 at the cold-core exit — the IO26 1-wire bus; the 4.7 kΩ pull-up lives on the board, nothing in the loom.
No air path may follow the leads inward. That path — not the sensor — is the only way condensation reaches a potted probe.
