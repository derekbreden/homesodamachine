Faucet + umbilical · 03/4The appliance

Foam goes on the blue tube only — every degree of warm-up costs dissolved CO2, and the flavor lines run warm. One braid segment follows each foam segment, up the run from the bare wall end.

  1. Lay the SIG-6 ribbon in first — it rides inside every braid segment that follows.
  2. A foam segment up the blue tube, butted, no gap. Friction high? Water, never solvents.
  3. Then a braid segment over all four, butted to the braid above — it crosses bare tube and its own foam, nothing seated.
  4. Five of each over 1425 mm — bare 40 at the body, 75 at the wall. The trim takes one of each.
