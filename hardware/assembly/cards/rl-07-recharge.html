RL-07

Mass-metered recharge

Refrigerant loop · 07/8Kitchen edition

The scale is the meter: what the can loses, the loop gains — exact method, target still in calibration.

  1. Can on the scale — tare. Plumb can → manifold → BPV31 flare port; open the BPV31.
  2. Open the can valve. R-600a flows into the vacuum on its own vapor pressure; the readout falls.
  3. Close at the target Δ. The can's lost grams are the loop's charge — Δmass is the measurement.
  4. Button up: manifold valve, BPV31, hose off, cap the flare port. The valve stays clamped for life.
Target is an open item — not the factory mass: the wound coil holds more volume than the finger-plate it replaced. Start at factory + a small overage; iterate 1–2 g against frost pattern + suction superheat on RL‑08.
