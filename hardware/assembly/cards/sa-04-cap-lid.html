Sub-assembly · 04/9The appliance

The same cap and lid, populated. All of it goes on while the pair is loose on the bench — the shell comes under it afterwards, and the core goes in the box after that.

Screwed down

G Ganen pump. M3 × 20 and a ⌀9 washer through the slot in each of the 4 rubber feet, the screw toward the slot’s outer end so the washer clears the pad’s raised shoulder, through the lid, into a deck-mount column’s insert in the cap beneath. Snug, not torqued — the pad is the isolator.

Pressed in

3 valves — V-K, V-A, V-B. Each presses into four blind sockets in a broad plinth. The valve rests beside its open port channel, with no screws or adhesive.

Zip-tied into printed ribs

2 chains — suction, discharge. Tie lying open across the channel first, chain in second. 6″ on each chain’s hex. Metal only — never a collet body.

1 rib and 2 posts leave here empty: their runs end on pieces this bench has not got. fluid-14 is tied in at SA‑07, water-3 and fluid-18 at IP‑02 and IP‑06.

Done whenthe pump is down on all four, every valve is seated arrow-aft, both chains zip-tied and flush-cut.
