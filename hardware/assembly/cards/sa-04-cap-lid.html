Sub-assembly · 03/9Kitchen edition

The same cap and lid, populated. All of it goes on while the pair is loose on the bench — the shell comes under it afterwards, and the core goes in the box after that.

Screwed down

SeaFlo 22-series pump. M3 × 16 and a washer through each of the 4 rubber foot pads, through the lid, into a deck-mount column’s insert in the cap beneath. Snug, not torqued — the pad is the isolator.

Pressed in

3 valves — V-K, V-A, V-B. Each drops into a set of four printed cradle bosses on this face — one under each corner post, blind sockets, nothing bonded and no screw. The valve body hangs in the gap between them.

Strapped into printed ribs

2 chains and 0 run — suction, discharge, water-3. Tie lying open across the channel first, body or tube in second. 6″ on each chain’s hex, 4″ on the run. Metal only — never a collet body.

1 rib leaves here empty. fluid-14 runs from V-F on the front top onto this lid’s reservoir-A fill bore, and there is no manifold on this bench — the tie goes on at SA-07.

Done when the pump is down on all four, every valve is seated arrow-aft, both chains and the one run strapped and flush-cut.
