WR-01

Chassis-ground bonds

Wiring · 01/6Kitchen edition

Three exposed-metal parts, three green bonds, one stack — Class I: a fault trips the breaker, not the user.

⛔
Single point. No chassis backbone — every bond lands on the one stack, earthed via AC-1 G (WR-02).
  1. Pressure vessel — ring under the top-cap SHCS closest to the shelf; route up the SIG-1 wire path.
  2. Compressor body — ring under an M3 foot screw, on the grommet's steel bushing, never in the rubber. Outside the shroud.
  3. Compressor shroud — ring at the ⌀ ~6 mm earth-bond hole beside the AC pass-through — run AC-6.
  4. Faucet SS plate — do not land. No bolt on it, and it ships loose. Stage the wire flagged (wiring.md open 4).
  5. Meter: ohms-low between every bonded surface and the bus.
Tools plumbed chassis from IP-06 · ring/fork crimper · 16 AWG green · multimeter
Ring terminals at the bus, ring or fork at the target — the stack clamps them all under one screw.
Done whenThree bonds on the stack, the faucet wire staged, and ohms-low from every bonded surface to the bus.
