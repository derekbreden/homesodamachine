Sub-assembly · 04/4Kitchen edition

SA-01 coming down over the bottom piece with SA-03 already standing in it. Everything either half owes is done first — what is left is the joint and one plug.

Joined as they close

SeaFlo 12 V pair, run DC-8. The pump stands on the core in the bottom half and its driver is on the +X wall of the top one, so the lead is landed as the two come together and not after. Dress it clear of the seam lip before the pieces touch.

Screwed into heat-sets

4 X-axis screws across the Z seam. The bottom piece’s three-sided lip telescopes +Z into the top; its D-pins drop into the bottom’s socket pods as it comes down. Screws drive in from the ±X exterior faces, one per side wall, into ruthex shorts set at SA-01.

Nothing else

No body is mounted at this moment. The core spans this seam and the bay above it is closed by it. A hand inside after this is a hand through the Y-seam mouth only.

The mouth facing the camera is what the front half takes next.

Done when the halves are on their pins, the seam screws driven from the flanks, the pump lead landed.
