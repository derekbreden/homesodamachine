ES-06

Land the RELAYS
J5 loom

Electronics shelf · 06/7Kitchen edition

The one loom whose both ends live on the shelf: XH housing at the board's J5, screw terminals at both Teyleten modules.

  1. Seat the housing square on the J5 (RELAYS) wafer — pins GND / V5 / IO2 / IO19 on the silk.
  2. LV-1: IO19 → relay #1 IN — asserted through the on-board gas interlock (U15): no compressor while the MQ-6 reads gas.
  3. LV-2: IO2 → relay #2 IN. Boot-safe: the opto's LED load holds IO2 low, which download mode also wants.
  4. LV-3: V5 + GND, teed at the relay end to both modules' VCC / GND terminals.
  5. Every other loom lands later — J1–J4, J6–J9, J11, J13 are built at CA-01 and land at WR-04 / WR-05.
Tools small flat screwdriver · J5 loom from CA-01
Coil 5 V comes off the board's K7805 rail through J5 — opto isolation keeps it electrically separate from the mains contacts. 22 AWG, ~150 mm.
J4 + J7 share the 7P housing. When their looms land (WR-05), the loom label — not the fit — is the misplug guard.
Done whenHousing seated square on the J5 wafer; IN, VCC, and GND landed at both modules' screw terminals.
