PV-09

Close the vessel —
float in, top plate welded

Pressure vessel · 09/14Kitchen edition

Closure is a sequence: rod (PV-06), then the donut, then the plate — and the donut is captive for good.

⛔
The rod must never hold the fillet root open — it locates the plate. Confirm seated depth against the spacer.
  1. Donut first — over the rod, through the open top. Captive after this: tack below, register above.
  2. Top plate, inside face down — the register (PV-03) takes the rod tip as the plate seats 1/4″ deep.
  3. Binds on the tip? Open that cap's pocket to 5/32″ — never force it. This joint is a pressure weld.
  4. Weld the same recessed corner fillet — tacks, settings, trail-off, color read: all per PV-08.
Tools X1 Pro · argon · 1/4″ spacer · YXQ float · prep per PV-07
Captive by geometry — donut between the rod tack below and the top-plate register above. No fastener touches it.
Done whenPlate at seated depth, donut captive, full-perimeter fillet — PT (PV-10) + hydro (PV-11) are the proof.
