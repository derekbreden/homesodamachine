CC-09

Stage the cavity
— elbow in, vessel down

Cold core · 09/16Kitchen edition

Order matters: the CO2 elbow enters through the rear support-arch doorway before the vessel drops — once the tank is down, the arch is under it.

  1. Angle the PP0308E elbow in from above, through the doorway in the rear support arch — its ⌀15 body seats in the arch bore, free port up for the CO2 drop.
  2. Lower the vessel bottom-plate-down into the center cavity — PRV shroud already on Port 4 (PV-14), coil + probe bonded (CC-03).
  3. Seat the rim on the ring plateau — 30 mm above the floor. The bottom-plate fittings hang in open volume inboard of the ring.
  4. Final tail check: rotate until both copper tails lie at the slot wall — inlet low, outlet high. CC-12 gives them their slot heights.
