FS-05

Weigh, label, hand off

Finish + ship · 05/5Kitchen edition

The scale gates the carrier, the label rides the top face, the handoff ends with the founder's email.

  1. Zero, weigh, tape — scale level and zeroed empty, gross to 0.1 kg; longest external dimension per axis (dim-weight reads the outside, not the void). Both → ship-dims.txt.
  2. Check the gate — over 70 lb / ~32 kg residential ground escalates to LTL. None anticipated at 15–20 kg; the measurement decides.
  3. Label the top face — carrier portal, label-pouch adhesive, never loose-taped; the dock scanner reads it without rotating the carton.
  4. Hand off + email same day — scan out to shipped.txt; the email goes from derek@homesodamachine.com, naming the serial, rear photo (FS-03) attached.
