  1. Match the handedness — left reservoir, left pocket; the parts mirror. The bulkhead elbow aims at the slot wall, at its flavor-line hole (CC-12).

  3. Land on the corner support posts. The elbow hanging below the floor clears the pocket floor by 1 mm and bears no load.
  4. Check the top and the far side: cap face 0.5 mm under the pocket wall top; far wall riding the reed channel — CC-06's magnet path.
