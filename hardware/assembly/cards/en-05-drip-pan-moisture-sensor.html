Drip pan
+ moisture sensor

Enclosure · 05/8Kitchen edition

The backflow preventer's atmospheric vent terminates inside the cabinet — a drip means a failed check, and the wet probe in this pan is how the ESP32 hears it.

  1. Lay the moisture probe flat in the basin, on the floor inside the coves, and run its leads out over the rim.
  2. Slide the tray in through the −X wall, rim first, east until it stops. The rim rides the two rails printed on that wall’s inner face; the tray’s two 45° haunches sit down between their inboard edges and centre it; and the stop bar across the rails’ east ends is what it comes to rest against. Home is when the rim’s west edge is flush with the wall’s inner face. Nothing fastens it — it draws straight back out the same way to be emptied. Seated, it stands under the vent’s fall, which the scorecard’s fall rule verifies.
  3. Route the leads toward the shelf's landing point — loose for now; SIG-9 lands at the wiring bench (WR).
Order matters. The probe goes in the tray before the tray goes in the machine — once it is on its rails the basin is under the chain, and a hand reaching in to lay a plate flat is reaching past the ASSE fittings to do it.
