Drip pan
+ moisture sensor

Enclosure · 05/8Kitchen edition

The backflow preventer's atmospheric vent terminates inside the cabinet — a drip means a failed check, and the wet probe in this pan is how the ESP32 hears it.

  1. VHB the rail pair to the foam-cap top, rails fore-and-aft, home stops forward, aft ends on the cap’s rear edge.
  2. Slide the basin in from the back until its front wall butts the stops — that lands it under the vent’s fall, which the scorecard’s fall rule verifies.
  3. Lay the moisture probe flat on the floor, inside the coves.
  4. Route the leads toward the shelf's landing point — loose for now; SIG-9 terminates at the wiring bench (WR).
Order matters. Rails + basin + sensor go in before the back panel (EN-07) and the shelf (EN-08) — both reach into this band, and the rails bond from above while the bay is still open.
