EN-05

Drip pan
+ moisture sensor

Enclosure · 05/8Kitchen edition

The backflow preventer's atmospheric vent terminates inside the cabinet — a drip means a failed check, and the wet probe in this pan is how the ESP32 hears it.

  1. Set the basin in the rear strip east of where the controller board lands, back face on the cap’s rear edge — that puts it under the vent’s fall, which the scorecard’s fall rule verifies. What carries it is not designed yet: it takes hold of the basin’s own flange, and nothing may stand under the floor.
  2. Lay the moisture probe flat on the floor, inside the coves.
  3. Route the leads toward the shelf's landing point — loose for now; SIG-9 lands at the wiring bench (WR).
Order matters. Basin + sensor go in before the back panel (EN-07) and the shelf (EN-08) — both reach into this band, and this one seats from above while the bay is still open.
