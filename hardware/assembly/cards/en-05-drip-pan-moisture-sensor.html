Drip pan
+ moisture sensor

Enclosure · 05/8Kitchen edition

The backflow preventer's atmospheric vent terminates inside the cabinet — a drip means a failed check, and the wet probe in this pan is how the ESP32 hears it.

  1. VHB the rail pair to the foam-cap top, in the rear strip behind where the shelf row will land — rails fore-and-aft, home stops forward, aft ends on the cap’s rear edge.
  2. Slide the basin in from the back along the shelves until its floor flange butts the stops. That is under the vent’s fall — the tip hangs over the strip pointing straight down, and the fall into the basin is what the assembly scorecard’s fall rule verifies.
  3. Lay the moisture probe flat in the basin.
  4. Route the leads toward the shelf's landing point on the same deck — loose for now; SIG-9 terminates at the wiring bench (WR).
Order matters. Rails + basin + sensor go in before the back panel (EN-07) and the shelf (EN-08) — both reach into this band, and the rails bond from above while the bay is still open.
