Drip pan
+ moisture sensor

Enclosure · 05/8Kitchen edition

The backflow preventer's atmospheric vent terminates inside the cabinet — a drip means a failed check, and the wet probe in this pan is how the ESP32 hears it.

  1. Seat the pan on the foam-cap top, in the rear strip behind where the shelf row will land.
  2. Under the vent's fall: the vent faces the front of the cabinet, and the fall lands 4 mm behind the shelf's rear edge.
  3. Lay the moisture probe flat in the basin.
  4. Route the leads toward the shelf's landing point on the same deck — loose for now; SIG-9 terminates at the wiring bench (WR).
Order matters. Pan + sensor go in before the back panel (EN-07) and the shelf (EN-08) — both reach into this band, and the pan seats from above while the bay is still open.
