FS-01

Wipe down +
final inspection

Finish + ship · 01/5Kitchen edition

The burn-in soak leaves fingerprints and shop dust; the customer's unboxing finds whatever you don't.

  1. Wipe every exposed face — rear + inlets, Zone C door + surround, grilles, both sides, front, bottom — microfiber + isopropyl.
  2. Swab and wash — foam-tip swab the funnel cavity; the silicone funnel wipes down separately and seats back once the unit is dry.
  3. Inspect against the checklist (right) — sightlines first: the Zone C door and the rear-panel cutouts.
  4. Repair in place where possible — light buff, reseat a grommet, swap a marked screw, reflow a proud insert. Anything else returns upstream: no known defect ships.
