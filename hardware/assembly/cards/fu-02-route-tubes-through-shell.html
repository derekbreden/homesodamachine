Faucet + umbilical · 02/5Kitchen edition

The printed gooseneck is the dispense head and LLDPE is the wet path end to end. The tubes go in once — faucet and umbilical leave this bench as one permanent unit.

  1. Blue first: brass stiffener fully in, then into the Westbrass upstream compression port.
  2. The factory ferrule + nut clamp LLDPE around brass — hand-snug + 1/4 turn with a wrench.
  3. Black ×2: push dry through the pill slot, up the gooseneck's dispense channel to the printed tip.
  4. Rear ends of all three: bare and square-cut — each pushes into its PP1208E collet at install.
One stiffener per build — the flavor tubes enter no compression port; tip-bore interference + the pill slot hold them.
