Faucet + umbilical · 02/4Kitchen edition

Mount hardware goes on the bare shank before the blue joint. The tubes and fitted SIG-6 then stay with the faucet.

  1. Gasket up; donor washer + nut on loosely — leave the countertop + under-counter-plate gap above.
  2. Blue in: stiffener, lower compression port, then hand-snug + 1/4 turn with a wrench.
  3. Water out: seat TPU thimble + 3/8″ tube, bottom on the thimble cap; square-cut flush at the tip.
  4. Black ×2: pill slot to printed tip — push dry; no compression fitting.
  5. Fit SIG-6; leave the three rear tails bare and square for their PP1208E collets.
One stiffener per build — the flavor tubes enter no compression port; tip-bore interference + the pill slot hold them.
