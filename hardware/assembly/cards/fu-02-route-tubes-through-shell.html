Faucet + umbilical · 02/4The appliance

Start with the bare donor, separate prints, tubes and unterminated SIG-6. Route them before the plate closes.

  1. Follow shell ASSEMBLY.md: thimble + soda tube, flavor pair + ribbon and lever; then close the plate and fit the display.
  2. Gasket over flavor tails, ribbon + shank; donor washer + nut on loosely, leaving the counter gap above.
  3. Blue in: stiffener, lower compression port, then hand-snug + 1/4 turn with a wrench.
  4. Crimp SIG-6's wall-end RJ11 plug. Leave the three tube tails bare and square for their PP1208E collets.
Check outlet position, pull retention and flow. The tubes and curved lap retain the neck, which closes without screws.
