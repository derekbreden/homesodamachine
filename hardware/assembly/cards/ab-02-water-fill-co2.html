Fill, restart
and measured pressure

Acceptance · 02/7The appliance

SeaFlo + IRM-90 baseline. Require jet fit/weld qualification and commissioned acceptance controls first.

  1. Fill with the faucet closed. Run the commissioned V-K and relay #2 control. Log pump inlet, pump outlet and carbonator pressure.
  2. Record volume, time, volts and amps. CHI must stop the fill. Dispense, then prove refill and restart. No fill-time limit is qualified from free-flow specifications.
  3. Walk the joints wet. No leaks; ASSE pan dry. Record reverse sealing of the downstream gas check in the bench fixture.
  4. Hold for 2 min. Read a gauge. No PRV weep. If relief opens, identify the pressure source; refill can raise vessel pressure above the gas setting.
