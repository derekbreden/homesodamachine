AB-02

First water fill
+ CO2 at 90 PSI

Acceptance · 02/7Kitchen edition

First water in service, first CO2 against a wet vessel — the reeds run the fill; your job is joints.

  1. Open the water side. Empty reed + closed faucet arms the refill; command “fill carbonator” — the SeaFlo energizes (relay #2, GPIO 2).
  2. Watch the fill — tens of seconds to the high reed, not minutes; the pump stops itself. Overrun = reed fault, stuck float, or a pump-pull short-circuit.
  3. Walk the joints wet — inlet, slot transitions, top-plate elbow, vent pan: all dry.
  4. Hold 90 PSI for 2 min — in-rush hiss dies to silence as the stone bubbles. No PRV weep, no vent telltale. PRV open = WR1110 fault; a wet vent = cross-contamination.
