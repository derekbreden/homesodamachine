Water path — rear panel
to cold core

Internal plumbing · 02/6Kitchen edition

Bulkhead → V-K → adapters → ASSE 1022 → hose → pump → cold core: 3/8″ holds to the pump inlet.

⛔
The vent drips to atmosphere — never into a drain. The weep is the backflow telltale; the pan waits under its fall.
  1. V-K + water-1/2: mount V-K (fill/shutoff) on its west cradle, arrow east, wire DC-9. Then two R3 LLDPE hops: bulkhead collet → V-K inlet (water-1, down the west lane), V-K outlet → PP010822E (water-2). Square cut, click, tug each.
  2. Tape + snug + 1: PP010822E → GAGIRA → ASSE MPT — arrow away from the customer.
  3. FFL38BARB38 untaped — the flare gasket seals. Snug + 1 against the chrome flat.
  4. ~12″ silicone, clamps on first — clamp ~1/2″ in from each barb tip; torque till it pillows.
  5. Off the pump: MAACFLOW, GASHER arrow away, PP010822E — LLDPE over the pump to the +Z stub.
Tools PTFE tape · wrench · hex driver · scissors · Mudder cutter
asse1022-assembly — four fittings, one piece; vent stub down, barb to the pump.
Done whenClosed dry, rear panel to cold core — every NPT snug + 1, hose pillowed at both clamps, vent stub over the pan. Water arrives at AB-02.
