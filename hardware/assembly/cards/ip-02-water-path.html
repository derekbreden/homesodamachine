Internal plumbing · 02/6Kitchen edition

Bulkhead → ASSE → split → V-K → pump → core: all 1/4″ LLDPE — the only 3/8″ is a clamped stub at each pump barb.

⛔
The vent drips to atmosphere — never into a drain. The weep is the backflow telltale.
  1. Chain, tape + snug + 1: PP010822E → GAGIRA → ASSE MPT, arrow away. Outlet, no tape.
  2. water-1/2/3/4: bulkhead in, east down the strip to the split, branch to V-K, V-K west to the suction.
  3. Pump barbs are molded in — nothing threads on. Each side: ~40 mm reinforced PVC, 2× SAE #6 clamps, R15.9.
  4. Discharge chain (GASHER arrow away): lies flat at the barb's height, collet west. water-6 holds level, water-5 falls once to the inlet.
Tools PTFE tape · wrench · nut driver · cutter · technique GT-01 / GT-02
