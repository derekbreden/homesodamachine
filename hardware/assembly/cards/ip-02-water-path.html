IP-02

Water path — rear wall
to cold core

Internal plumbing · 02/6Kitchen edition

Bulkhead to core, all 1/4″ LLDPE — the only 3/8″ is a clamped stub at each pump barb.

⛔
The vent drips to atmosphere — never into a drain. That weep is the backflow telltale.
  1. Chain: tape + snug + 1, arrow away. Flare: no tape.
  2. No water-1 — bulkhead collet and chain inlet meet face to face. water-2: a straight 56 mm to the split.
  3. water-3 off the split's DOWN branch — east over the cap into V-K, 3 corners. water-4 fires aft 15 mm, no corner.
  4. Pump barbs are molded in — PVC stubs, 4× SAE #6: 62 mm suction, 35 mm discharge, R15.9 mm.
  5. Discharge chain lies flat, barb aft, collet forward; water-5 falls onto water-in in 2 turns.
Tools PTFE tape · wrench · nut driver · cutter · technique GT-01 / GT-02
asse1022-assembly — five fittings, one piece; 1/4″ PTC at both ends, vent stub straight down.
Done whenClosed dry, back wall to core — every NPT snug + 1, the flare drawn on its swivel, four clamps closed, vent stub over the pan.
