AB-07

Drain + air-purge
for transit

Acceptance · 07/7Kitchen edition

The burn-in refilled everything AB-04 emptied — the unit is wet when the soak ends, and this is the last bench that can dry it.

  1. Water closed, CO2 still on. The residual head is what pushes the carbonator out through the dispense path.
  2. Drain the carbonator into the bench catch until it runs dry and the low reed sits asserted.
  3. Purge both channels — the AB-04 cycle, funnel dry and open to air; each reservoir empties out its nozzle.
  4. Bleed and disconnect: close the CO2, crack the faucet to atmosphere, pull both rig lines.
⛔
FS-02 verifies dry — it has no drain. A unit that still holds liquid repeats this card.
