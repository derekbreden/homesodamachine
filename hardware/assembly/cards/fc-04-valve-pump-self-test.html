FC-04

Valve + pump self-test

Commissioning · 04/5Kitchen edition

One command walks every coil and motor — listen for the release as much as the click.

  1. Trigger the self-test at the monitor prompt — V-A through V-J in turn, then the fan, then both pumps.
  2. Count 10 distinct clicks — each Beduan coil energizes ~250 ms and releases. A missing release click is a stuck-on coil.
  3. Hear the fan — a 1 s spin-up of the 12 V axial on the side wall, then coast-down.
  4. Watch both heads turn — Pump A forward ~1 s, then Pump B; silicone heads dry, no flavor loaded yet.
  5. Any failure is loom wiring, a device fault, or a board fault — resolve and re-run before FC-05.
Bench serial console — no other tooling; the chassis is the fixture
Click, then release. A coil with no release click is stuck on — stop and trace it before moving down the sequence.
