RL-08

First run-up + leak check

Refrigerant loop · 08/8Kitchen edition

First breath: right current, cold suction, and silence from the detector at every joint.

⛔
A leak = full redo — vent → recut → argon → rebraze → vacuum → recharge. Never repair under charge.
  1. Energize through the inline fuse (5 A fast-blow) — the first run-up has no prior on-state to wait out.
  2. Expect ~1 A running; the suction line drops cold within a minute or two.
  3. Sweep every joint — detector or soap: both tie-ins, BPV31 saddle + flare cap, every threaded connection.
  4. 3 min off before restart — or the motor stalls against head pressure until the overload trips.
