PV-06

Tack-weld the float rod
to the bottom plate

Pressure vessel · 06/14Kitchen edition

The register (PV-03) does the locating: the rod stands square on the donut-wall axis — the tack freezes what the pocket fixed.

  1. Seat the rod in the register. The 9/64″ pocket is a snug slip on the 1/8″ rod — self-locates the base, holds it square.
  2. Tack it vertical at the base — on the inside face, the side that will face into the vessel.
  3. Same welding session as the plate welds (PV-08, PV-09) — heat the welder once. Settings per PV-08.
  4. Respect the length. At closure the tip enters the top register without bottoming — the rod locates the plate, never holds the fillet root open (PV-09).
131.1 mm = tube 152.4 − both 1/4″ recesses − both 1/4″ plates + both 0.100″ registers − 1 mm clearance.
Tools X1 Pro (settings per PV-08) · argon · rod from PV-05 · plate from PV-04
Done whenRod tacked square, base fully seated in the register — tip free to enter the top register at closure.
