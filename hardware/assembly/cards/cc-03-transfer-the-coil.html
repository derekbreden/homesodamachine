CC-03

Transfer the coil
+ set the band

Cold core · 03/15Kitchen edition

Copper onto foil: the 3M 425 skin from CC-02 is the tank↔coil thermal interface, and the coil's springback is what clamps it down.

  1. Slip the coil on. Off the mandrel it runs 3 mm under the tank OD — work it down evenly, don't scar the foil.
  2. Set the band: 15 mm of bare vessel below it, 18 mm above. Inlet tail low, outlet tail high.
  3. Ride the bridge — the reeds stand in the printed reed-bridge under the foil (CC-02). Walk each crossing wrap up its ramps; no wrap sits on a plateau edge.
  4. Press every wrap snug onto the foil. The groove clocked the tails; rotate so each aims at its own lane — inlet port, outlet west (CC-10).
Materials wound coil from CC-01 · skinned vessel from CC-02 · 3M 425 tape B07BTW7C2N for patch-ups
Springback is your friend — 1–3 mm of relax makes the slip-on possible; the 3 mm stretch makes the clamp.
Done whenCoil clamped on the foil, bare band 15 low / 18 high, no wrap on a reed, one tail on each lane.
