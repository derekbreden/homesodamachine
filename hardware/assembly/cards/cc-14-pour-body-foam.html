CC-14

Pour the body foam

Cold core · 14/15Kitchen edition

One pour finds every open cavity in parallel. What it must not leave behind is a void at a probe.

⚠
Nitrile gloves on. The PU foam's isocyanate component is a skin sensitizer.
  1. Mix the two-part foam 1:1 in measured shots. A build consumes ~5 L (~4 L cavity + waste).
  2. Pour all at once into the open top — no lid, no down-channels. Outer gap, corner pockets and tank cavity fill in parallel. The bag pockets and reed channels take none — a reservoir fills its pocket at 0.5 mm all round.
  3. The long traverse is around the coil at the reservoir sides — ~110 mm of arc through the embedded wraps. Beads squeeze out at the lane slots and every tight tube exit.
Materials PU foam kit B08R7TX8QJ · cups · sticks · gloves
⛔
A void at a probe is the defect. The pour must fully wet each probe and its lead entry — trapped air condenses and frosts on the cold metal. Insulation defect first, probe-fouling second.
Done whenEvery cavity risen full in one pour, both probes wetted in with no void, both reed channels open, squeeze-out trimmed after cure.
