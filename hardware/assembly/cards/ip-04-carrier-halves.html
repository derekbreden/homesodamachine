IP-04

Flavor manifold
— the moving carrier

Internal plumbing · 04/8The appliance

Bench work on loose enclosure-front-top. Everything enters through the open rear and seats outward into the flanks.

  1. Seat the 4 moving tees (Y-C, Y-D, Y-F, Y-G) from aft, each forward to release.
  2. Join the 2 halves — left at release, right at park, each pushed outward flush. Close the lap with 2 M3 × 8 from the empty bay.
  3. Aft valves up from underneath — V-C, V-D, V-G, V-J, posts clear of the tray, then home.
  4. Tie and spring. 8 ties, two a tee, all heads tee-side. Then 2 springs down the inner wells.
  5. Fore valves and bowed stubs last — V-E, V-F, V-H, V-I, then a bench-fit blank per tee, 12 mm between sleeve faces.
Tools bent-nose pliers · 4″ ties · flush cutter · 2.5 mm hex
The carrier block, looking down — schematic. Four tees tied to one body that spans the piece, a grip out each flank, two springs returning it aft, and the lap at centre closed by two screws. The plate under it belongs to front-top and never moves.
Done whenBoth tabs move and settle together over full travel, every tee on the common bearing plane, and the empty carrier returns to park on its own.
