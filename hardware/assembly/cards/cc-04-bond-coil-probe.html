CC-04

Bond the coil probe +
close the foil over the coil

Cold core · 04/15Kitchen edition

The coil's outlet (high) end is the suction side — refrigerant leaving as gas, the coldest metal. The freeze-protect probe bonds there, under the foil, before the skin closes.

  1. Find the suction end — the outlet tail, high on the vessel; CC-03 clocked it at the west lane.
  2. Tuck the DS18S20 flat against the copper — bare TO-92, leads heat-shrunk — and tape it down under the foil. The −8 °C cutoff sees the coldest point only through this bond.
  3. Close the foil skin over the coil's outlet end — probe underneath, foil tight, no loose flap.
  4. Lead the 3-conductor cable toward the front wall. It routes out the port-lane slot with the tank probe's and the carbonator reeds' (CC-12).
Materials DS18S20+ (DigiKey DS18S20+-ND) · 3M 425 foil tape B07BTW7C2N · coil on the vessel from CC-03
