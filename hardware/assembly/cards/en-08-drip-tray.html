EN-08

Slide the drip tray
in through the −X wall

Enclosure · 08/9Kitchen edition

The backflow vent terminates inside the cabinet — a drip is a failed check, and this wet probe is how the ESP32 hears it. The rails are on back-top's −X wall.

  1. Lay the moisture probe flat in the basin, on the floor inside the coves, and run its leads out over the rim.
  2. Slide the tray in through the −X wall, rim first, east until it stops. The rim rides the two rails; the 45° haunches centre it between their inboard edges; the stop bar takes it. Home is the rim’s west edge flush with the wall’s inner face. Nothing fastens it — it draws back out the same way to be emptied.
  3. Route the leads toward the shelf's landing point — loose for now; SIG-9 lands at the wiring bench (WR).
Order matters. The probe goes in the tray first — once the tray is on its rails the basin is under the chain, and a hand laying a plate flat is reaching past the ASSE fittings.
