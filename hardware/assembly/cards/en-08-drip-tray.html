EN-08

Slide the drip tray
in through the −X wall

Enclosure · 08/9Kitchen edition

The backflow vent terminates inside the cabinet — a drip is a failed check, and this wet probe is how the ESP32 hears it. Its channels are on back-top's −X wall.

  1. Lay the moisture probe flat in the basin, on the floor inside the coves, and run its leads out over the rim.
  2. Slide the tray in through the −X wall, rim first, east until it stops. The rim runs in a channel a side — rail under, lap over — and the 45° haunches centre it on the rails' inboard edges. Home is the haunch against the stop bar. No fastener; it draws west to be emptied.
  3. Route the leads toward the shelf's landing point — loose for now; SIG-9 lands at the wiring bench (WR).
Order matters. The probe goes in first — once the tray is in its channels the basin is under the chain, and a hand laying a plate flat is reaching past the ASSE fittings.
