EN-08

Slide the ASSE drip pan
in through the −X wall

Enclosure · 08/9Kitchen edition

The backflow vent terminates inside the cabinet — a drip is a failed check, and this wet probe is how the ESP32 hears it. Its sleeve is on back-top's −X wall.

  1. Lay the moisture probe flat in the pan, on the floor inside the coves. Bring its two leads above the rim and turn them west through the sleeve's open-top wall notch; leave a service loop in the cabinet.
  2. Slide the tray in through the −X wall, east end first, until it stops. It lies on the sleeve's floor and its rim runs under the sleeve's lid. Home is the tray against the backstop. No fastener; its west end stands proud of the skin.
  3. Route the leads toward the shelf's landing point — loose for now; SIG-9 lands at the wiring bench (WR).
Order matters. The probe goes in first — once the tray is in its sleeve the pan is under the chain, and a hand laying a plate flat is reaching past the ASSE fittings.
