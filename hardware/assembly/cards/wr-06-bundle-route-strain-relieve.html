WR-06

Bundle, route,
strain-relieve

Wiring · 06/6Kitchen edition

Six bundles by zone, black-braided-sleeved or zip-tied — and strain relief at every edge a cable crosses.

  1. Shelf → cold core: J4 + J6 + J7 looms, the J11 gas drop, and the pressure-vessel bond — one sleeved bundle along the back wall to the penetrations.
  2. Shelf → manifold: the J1 + J2 trunks, DC-3, DC-5, and the J8 loom; fan-out at the manifold — DC-8 leaves it alone for the fan.
  3. Shelf → compressor: the SJOOW lead runs alone — zip-tie tie-downs only; the jacket takes the strain.
  4. Forward + up: J9 to the front face; J3 + the SS-plate bond ride the umbilical — sleeve shelf-side, separator at the bulkhead.
  5. Relieve every transition — shelf exit, cold-core, manifold, bulkhead; anchors below the cable.
Tools PET braided sleeve · zip ties + tensioner · hot-glue gun
Fan-outs happen at the device cluster, never at the board — one COM / GND rides each trunk and explodes in its Wago at the manifold or reservoir.
Done whenEvery bundle sleeved or tied by zone, every transition relieved — safe to apply 120 VAC, and none has been.
