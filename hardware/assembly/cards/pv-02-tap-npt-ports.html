PV-02

Tap 1/4″-18 NPT
— four ports

Pressure vessel · 02/14Kitchen edition

Hand-cut taper threads — 2 ports per plate × 2 plates. The chucked spring guide keeps the tap square; the wrench does the cutting.

  1. Disc in the pocket, fresh MDF under. Pilot under the chuck; two C-clamps lock the stack to the table.
  2. Spring guide in the chuck, tip in the tap's divot — spring compressed ~1/4″. Tap Magic into the pilot + the first inch of the tap.
  3. Wrench CW, slow: ½ turn forward, ¼ back to break the chip; re-fluid every few turns. Harder as the taper bites — correct, not a problem.
  4. Stop at 4.5 turns of engagement — you'll feel it leave the free-cutting phase. Back out clean.
Tools LingGan M35 1/4-18 NPT tap · Drill America wrench · B&S spring guide · Tap Magic · WEN 4208T
Single-use rig (snapshot 2026-05-03) — the tap buries into the sacrificial MDF on breakthrough. The batch fixture is a downstream design step.
