EN-02

Mount the compressor
with its shroud

Enclosure · 02/8Kitchen edition

Shroud first, on the bench. Once the compressor is bolted down, this cover barely fits.

⚠
120 V terminals + a PTC at 140–150 °C in normal use live under this cover.
  1. Gland into the 7/8″ hole, locknut snug, aimed at the electronics column. Then lower the shroud over the terminal block + PTC — ≥10 mm clear every side.
  2. Compressor down on its four floor pads — M3 through each donor grommet's steel bushing; never crush a grommet.
  3. Pin the shroud, don't clamp it — M3 × 10 front, M3 × 8 + washer rear, through ⌀4.5. The rear is a one-way trip: the MQ-6 lands 1.00 mm behind it (WR-05).
