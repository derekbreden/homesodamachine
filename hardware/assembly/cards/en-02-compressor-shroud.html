Mount the compressor
with its shroud

Enclosure · 02/8Kitchen edition

Shroud first, on the bench. The compressor stays in the charged loop on its cart — once it is bolted down, this cover barely fits.

⚠
120 V terminals + a PTC at 140–150 °C in normal use live under this cover.
  1. Gland into the 7/8″ hole, locknut snug — aimed where the AC run lands, the electronics column.
  2. Lower the shroud over the terminal block + PTC. Confirm ≥10 mm clearance on every side.
  3. Into the cabinet: shroud to the floor at its two base holes, torque-limited; compressor bolted at the middle-bottom bosses.
