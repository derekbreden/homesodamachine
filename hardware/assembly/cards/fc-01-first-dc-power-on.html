Commissioning · 01/5Kitchen edition

A re-look, then first light — the never-powered chassis wakes one rail at a time under PSU control.

⚠
Mains hits the PSU primary when the outlet goes live — no device in series; ground-fault protection deferred. Never direct wall power.
  1. Walk it cold — checklist at right. A re-look, not a re-test; anything moved returns to wiring.
  2. Power via the bench PSU outlet. Firmware boots all-off; the outlet is the hardware backstop.
  3. Prove the rails in order — 12 V → 5 V → 3.3 V, meter on each point (map below). Any rail out: kill the PSU, back to wiring.
  4. Read cold idle — tens of mA. Relay #1 dark; recheck 5 V at full logic load after FC-03.
