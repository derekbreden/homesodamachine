IP-03

Flavor manifold
— valves and tees

Internal plumbing · 03/6Kitchen edition

Build to the truth table: fluid-topology.md names every valve (V-A…V-J), every junction, every segment — the manifold matches it segment-for-segment.

  1. Lay out all 10 Beduan valves with their inlet/outlet legends visible — NC solenoids, flow I→O only.
  2. Lay out the 6 PP0208E Tees between them — Y-A, Y-B, Y-C, Y-D, Y-F, Y-G, in 4 limbs. A limb is one lane: valves and tees butted collet to collet down one column.
  3. Pre-cut all 26 segments to the topology tables — 11 butt-lengths two collets swallow whole, 4 hairpins across the fold. Square-cut both ends, tag each by segment number.
Nothing carries a junction — no tee seats on a body or a tray; each hangs on the collets it joins. And neither reservoir has one: each carries two mouths of its own.
Stock + tools Mudder cutter · masking tape · 1/4″ LLDPE reel
The pack, folded — four limbs across two pump rosettes, every body butted collet to collet down its own column and no elbow anywhere in it. The fold turns about the line the four barb tees' front collets stand on; the four connections that cross it are the hairpins.
Done when10 valves + 6 Tees laid out on their four limbs; 26 segments cut square and tagged by number — ready to build outward (IP-04).
