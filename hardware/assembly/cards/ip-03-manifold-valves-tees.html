IP-03

Flavor manifold
— valves and tees

Internal plumbing · 03/8The appliance

Build to the truth table: fluid-topology.md names every valve (V-A…V-J), every junction, every segment — the manifold matches it segment-for-segment.

  1. Lay out all 10 Beduan valves with their inlet/outlet legends visible — NC solenoids, flow I→O only.
  2. Lay out the 6 PP0208E Tees between them — Y-A, Y-B, Y-C, Y-D, Y-F, Y-G, in 4 limbs. A limb is one lane of valves, tees and their connecting tubes.
  3. Pre-cut all 26 segments — 3 butts two collets swallow whole, 4 hairpins across the fold. Square both ends, tag by number.
4 of those tees move. Y-C, Y-D, Y-F, Y-G ride the sliding carrier rather than the body, and the four bowed stubs that reach them are bench-fit, not cut to a reported length. Lay them out with the rest and leave them; IP-04 builds that block.
Stock + tools Mudder cutter · masking tape · 1/4″ LLDPE reel
The pack, folded — four limbs across two pump rosettes. Four hairpins cross the fold; the four bowed stubs let the carrier tees move relative to the fixed fore valves.
Done when10 valves and 6 tees are laid out on their 4 limbs, and 26 segments cut square and tagged — IP-04 builds the carrier.
