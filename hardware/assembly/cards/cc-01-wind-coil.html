CC-01

Wind the evaporator coil
on the mandrel

Cold core · 01/15Kitchen edition

1/4″ OD × 0.031″ wall ACR copper, wound into the printed mandrel's helical groove. The groove is the pitch — fill it and the coil is right.

  1. Cut the copper: ~17 ft. The full groove (12.7 ft) plus a 2 ft straight tail at each end.
  2. Anchor the start with one 2 ft tail standing radially proud at the groove's inlet end.
  3. Wind tight into the groove, single layer — no gaps, no crossovers, seated to the groove bottom.
  4. Exit radially at the far end with the second 2 ft tail. Both tails purely radial — no vertical jog.
  5. Slide the coil off. Expect 1–3 mm radial springback — it still runs 3 mm under the tank OD and stretches to clamp (CC-02).
