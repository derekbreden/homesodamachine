CC-01

Wind the evaporator coil
on the mandrel

Cold core · 01/15Kitchen edition

1/4″ OD × 0.031″ wall ACR copper, wound into the printed mandrel's helical groove. The groove is the pitch — fill it and the coil is right.

  1. Cut the copper: 15.84 ft (4.83 m) — the wrap as it lands on the tank (13.41 ft) plus a 335.5 mm inlet and 404.6 mm outlet tie-in allowance. The groove itself holds only 12.72 ft; the wrap grows coming off the mandrel.
  2. Anchor the start with the 335.5 mm inlet tail standing radially proud at the groove's inlet end.
  3. Wind tight into the groove, single layer — no gaps, no crossovers, seated to the groove bottom.
  4. Exit radially with the 404.6 mm outlet tail — both tails purely radial, no vertical jog.
  5. Slide the coil off. Expect 1–3 mm radial springback — it still runs 3 mm under the tank OD and stretches to clamp (CC-03).
