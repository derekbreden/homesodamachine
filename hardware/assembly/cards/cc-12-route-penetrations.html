Cold core · 12/15Kitchen edition

Every fluid line leaves by the top, one cap conduit each. The −X face is mated flat to the base: 2 reed cables and two lane slots, nothing else.

  1. Filled high, drawn low — all three. Nothing entering can leave without crossing the liquid.
  2. CO2 down the port lane — the one conduit that feeds, in at the ring's 225° slot. Tug-test first.
  3. Carbonated water up on no bore at all — that same slot, then beside the coil to its conduit.
  4. Water in + both draws climb the forward band (8 mm). A fill has no run — conduit straight onto the cap bore.
  5. Front wall last. Both cables up the port lane, sensor leads up its slot, dressed so CC-13's clamp and CC-14's pour shut on them. No air path in.
