Cold core · 12/15Kitchen edition

Seven lines and four cables cross the shell — every tube crossing 1/4″ OD, transitions warm-side, CO2 alone from above.

  1. Flavor lines ×2 + water outlet — each flavor tube through its low wall hole into the reservoir elbow's PTC port; the outlet out the ring, then its own hole.
  2. Slot, low to high: copper in 59.75, copper out 67.75, water in 75.75, PRV 83.75 — one 8 pitch.
  3. Water inlet + PRV — PP010822E back to NPT into the Port 2 elbow; the vent press-fits the shroud cap's ⌀6.35 hole.
  4. Four cables up the slot — probe leads to SIG-1, reeds to J7, dressed so the plug clamp (CC-13) and over-pour (CC-14) close around them. No air path in.
Parts 1/4″ OD LLDPE · JG PP010822E adapters
