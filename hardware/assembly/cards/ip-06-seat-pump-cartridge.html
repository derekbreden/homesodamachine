IP-06

Seat the pump cartridge

Internal plumbing · 06/8The appliance

Only after the chassis is closed (EN-08). The cartridge is built at SA-09 and stays out of the bay until now — this is its one insertion method, and both hands do it at once.

⛔
Never push a tube into a standing collet. The sleeves come down first and the tube goes in through a held-open collet. A tube driven against a closed sleeve tears its grip and the joint never holds again.
  1. Present all 4 tubes through the fixed plate's 4 passages, cartridge square to the bay mouth.
  2. Squeeze — each hand spans one pocket and its tab. Cartridge aft, carrier fore, until all four tubes bottom at the fore stop. Sleeves fully down, plate touching, tubes in 10 mm. The face is still 2.15 mm shy.
  3. Relax both hands together. The springs take the carrier aft, the sleeves extend over the tubes and the nose opens its 0.5 mm of air. Let go of both at the same moment — one tab released early cocks the carrier.
  4. Push through the last 2.15 mm until the face is flush. The same tubes now sit 11.65 mm deep.
  5. Tug every tube. Four gentle pulls, straight out. Neither tab may read park.
Tools bare hands · shop light down the bay
One tube, both stops — mm · schematic, side on. The plate never moves; the cartridge and the carrier move toward each other and then apart.
Done whenThe face is flush all round, both tabs settled at the same stop, and all four tubes hold a gentle tug — ready to plug at WR-04.
