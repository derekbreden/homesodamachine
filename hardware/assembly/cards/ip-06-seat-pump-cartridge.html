IP-06

Seat the pump cartridge

Internal plumbing · 06/8The appliance

Only after the chassis is closed (EN-08). Built at SA-09, out of the bay until now. Both hands at once.

⛔
Never drive a tube at a standing collet. The sleeve goes down first and the tube enters a held-open collet. A tube pushed against a closed sleeve tears its grip for good.
  1. Present all 4 tubes through the plate's 4 passages.
  2. Squeeze — each hand spans one pocket and its tab — until all four bottom at the fore stop, in 10 mm. The face is 2.15 mm shy.
  3. Relax both together. Springs take the carrier aft, sleeves extend, the nose opens 0.5 mm. One tab let go early cocks the carrier.
  4. Push the last 2.15 mm flush; the tubes end 11.65 mm deep.
  5. Tug all four. Neither tab may read park.
One tube, both stops — mm · schematic, side on. The plate never moves; the cartridge and the carrier move toward each other and then apart.
Done whenThe face is flush all round, both tabs settled at the same stop, and all four tubes hold a gentle tug — ready to plug at WR-04.
