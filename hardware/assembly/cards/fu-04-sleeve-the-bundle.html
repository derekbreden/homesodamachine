FU-04

Sleeve
the bundle

Faucet + umbilical · 04/5Kitchen edition

Three round tubes in a sleeve pack one way — a triangle. The wall's 3 unions stand in a different one.

  1. Pack the tubes as they fall — foamed blue and two bare black, into the natural dense-pack. Any corner will do.
  2. Lay the signal cable in the void between the tubes and the sleeve wall — SIG-6: TX / RX / 5 V / GND.
  3. Wind the spiral wrap on: from just above the under-counter plate down to ~3″ above the wall end.
  4. Leave that last 3″ un-sleeved — the installer flexes the three onto unions 31.36 mm apart, blue into the blue-ringed one at the row's east end.
Spiral, never braid. Both ends are terminated before this step, so braid could only be threaded up from the wall end over five butted foam segments — the one thing the sleeve prevents.
Materials BNTECHGO 28 AWG 4-conductor ribbon B07PNPHWMG · spiral wrap 1″ nominal, ~2.5–3 m (bom §11, SKU TBD)
The pack is a shape, not a map. Two bulkheads sit on one line and the west one 62.88 mm under it; the bare tails flex apart to reach all three — the blue ring is the only thing that has to match.
Done whenOne spiral-wrapped bundle — three tubes dense-packed, ribbon inside, last 3″ free at the wall end.
