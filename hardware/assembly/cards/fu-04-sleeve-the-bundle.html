FU-04

Sleeve
the bundle

Faucet + umbilical · 04/5Kitchen edition

Three tubes pack the same triangle as the rear-panel bulkhead cluster — present the bundle to the panel and each tube already faces its bulkhead.

  1. Triangle-pack the tubes: foamed blue at the top vertex, the two black flavor lines below.
  2. Lay the signal cable in the void between the tubes and the sleeve wall — SIG-6: TX / RX / 5 V / GND.
  3. Slide the sleeve over the full bundle: from just above the under-counter plate down to ~3″ above the rear-panel end.
  4. Leave that last 3″ un-sleeved — the installer flexes the tubes apart for the three-bulkhead push-connect.
Sleeve selection is open: braided polyester must be pre-threaded; spiral wrap installs over an assembled bundle.
Materials BNTECHGO 28 AWG 4-conductor ribbon B07PNPHWMG · sleeve — braided or spiral, TBD
Blue rides the top vertex — matching the blue-ringed bulkhead at the top of the panel triangle.
Done whenOne sleeved bundle — blue at the vertex, ribbon inside, last 3″ free at the panel end.
