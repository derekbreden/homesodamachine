Foam the
carbonated-water tube

Faucet + umbilical · 03/5Kitchen edition

Foam goes on the blue tube only. Chilled CO2-saturated water rides it to the faucet, and every degree of warm-up costs dissolved CO2. Flavor lines run warm and stay bare.

  1. Slide CARGEN 1-ft segments onto the blue tube — a snug interference fit over the 1/4″ OD.
  2. Friction high? A wipe of water. Never solvents — nitrile is solvent-sensitive.
  3. Butt the segments together, no gap. The sleeve (FU-04) holds the butts compressed.
  4. Five segments — 1425 mm of the blue tube's 1540, bare 40 mm at the compression end and 75 at the wall end. A nominal kitchen keeps four; the installer pulls one off with the trim.
