RL-04

Tie in the suction line

Refrigerant loop · 04/8Kitchen edition

Both lines are 1/4″ OD — a direct sweat join. The coil's outlet becomes the compressor's suction line.

⚠
Braze only with argon flowing (RL-03) — the sweep carries residual fuel out ahead of the torch. Wet rag on the stub, hard against the plug face — the plug is printed PETG (Tg ~80 °C) about 68 mm down the copper, and it will not survive an unshielded braze.
  1. Scotch-Brite to bright copper — both tube ODs and inside the coupling sockets.
  2. Stage the joint: the coil-outlet stub leaves the front wall on the west lane — the compressor's own flank — and meets the factory suction cut there.
  3. Slip the coupling over both ends — 1/4″ into 1/4″, sweat × sweat, no adapter.
  4. Wrap the soaked rag on the stub, tight to the plug face and reaching back toward the joint. Keep it wet the whole braze; a rag that steams dry has stopped working.
  5. Braze both mouths dry — no flux. BCuP-5's phosphorus is the flux; heat under the flowing argon, flame aimed away from the core. Check the plug face after: no gloss, no sag, no scorch smell.
Tools 1/4″ ACR slip coupling (sweat × sweat) · BCuP-5 15 % Ag 1/16″ · Bernzomatic TS8000 + MAP-Pro · Scotch-Brite maroon · wet cotton rag
Why dry: flux residue inside a sealed loop is corrosive and plugs the cap tube or drier screen — BCuP-5's phosphorus does the fluxing on copper-to-copper.
Done whenCoil outlet joined to the factory suction line — a complete fillet ring at both coupling mouths, argon never interrupted.
