CC-06

Pour the cap foam
— both caps

Cold core · 06/15Kitchen edition

Each cap is a foam-filled cup, poured bolted to the shell's top face — mouth up, lid on.

⚠
Nitrile gloves on — isocyanate sensitizes skin.
  1. Label TOP / BOTTOM first — only the top cap carries the 7 conduit and 12 deck columns.
  2. Ruthex M3 short into every deck column, same iron as CC-05. Not the power column's — those go in the +X wall at EN-01.
  3. Bolt one stack down: cap floor on the shell face, lid pads-into-the-cup, 6 M3 × 25 into the CC-05 inserts. The screws are the clamp.
  4. Mix 1:1, pour through the lid's ⌀20 hole — air out the two ⌀6 vents. Trim flush — cut to the plate, never into a lid cradle. Unbolt, repeat.
Materials PU foam B08R7TX8QJ · M3×25 ×6 · ruthex M3 short ×8 · gloves
foam-cap-top — conduits and 5 deck-mount stations in the cup; installs rotated 180° (CC-15).
Done whenBoth caps cured full and unbolted, foam trimmed flush at pour + vents — every conduit bore open end to end and every deck insert flush.
