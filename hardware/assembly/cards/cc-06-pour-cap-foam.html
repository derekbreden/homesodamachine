CC-06

Pour the cap foam
— both caps

Cold core · 06/15Kitchen edition

Each cap is a foam-filled cup, poured bolted to the shell's top face — mouth up, lid on.

⚠
Nitrile gloves on — isocyanate sensitizes skin.
  1. Match the lids, label TOP / BOTTOM — the lid with the extra ⌀6.5 mm hole rides the CO2-bossed cap.
  2. Bolt one stack down: cap floor on the shell face, lid pads-into-the-cup, six M3 × 25 into the CC-05 inserts. The screws are the clamp — foam lifts a loose lid.
  3. Mix the two-part foam 1:1, then pour through the lid's ⌀20 mm hole — air out the two ⌀6 mm vents.
  4. Trim flush, unbolt, repeat for the second cap — same fixture; the bottom cap flips at CC-15.
Materials PU foam B08R7TX8QJ · M3×25 ×6 · cups · sticks · gloves
foam-cap-top — the CO2 boss marks the top cap; it installs rotated 180° (CC-15).
Done whenBoth caps cured full and unbolted, foam trimmed flush at pour + vents — and the top cap's CO2 bore is open end to end.
