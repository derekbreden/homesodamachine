Faucet + umbilical · 05/5Kitchen edition

The faucet + umbilical ships as one permanently-attached unit; everything the installer needs at the countertop rides in the same bag.

  1. Coil the umbilical loosely — 8–12″ loop diameter, faucet at one end, bare tails + cable at the other.
  2. Add the kit (table at right): keyhole plate, factory shank nut + washer, tube cutter.
  3. Gasket check: the TPU gasket rides the shank from FU-02 — never loose in the kit.
  4. Bag, seal, label: build number + FAUCET-UMBILICAL-SUBASSEMBLY — set aside for finish-pack-ship (FS).
The bag opens once at WR — the signal cable's rear end terminates at the shelf, then the bag re-closes.
