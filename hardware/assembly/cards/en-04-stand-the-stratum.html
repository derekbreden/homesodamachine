Stand the condenser
on the compressor’s tangent

Enclosure · 04/9Kitchen edition

An oblong can has no flat face. The condenser stands east of the compressor and meets it along the shell’s own +X tangent — one line, not a plane.

  1. Bring the intake face up to that tangent with nothing between them. The discharge stub is made on the same line, so the joint and the mating are one reading — a gap here is a gap in the loop.
  2. Both on the floor slab, packed forward. The aft plane the pair presents is what the core mates against at EN-05.
  3. Read the fan against the donor’s native orientation and record which face it is on before it is buried.
  4. Level the pair. The flavor pack sets down across both crowns at IP-03; leave the floor beneath clear.
