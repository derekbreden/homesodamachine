RL-06

Pull vacuum

Refrigerant loop · 06/8Kitchen edition

First proof of the brazes — and the handoff: argon may stop only because the pump takes over.

  1. Brazes complete → argon off at the regulator, BPV31 closed. The loop-open period ends here.
  2. Swap at the flare port: argon hose off, manifold's 1/4″ SAE flare on. Reopen the BPV31.
  3. Pull to 500 microns or below; hold ≥ 15 min on the pump.
  4. Valve off: no rise for 15 min = tight. A rise = moisture (pump longer) or a leak (find + fix).
The drier's dry blanket hands off here — argon stops only as vacuum begins, never earlier (RL-03).
