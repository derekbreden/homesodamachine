Solder + heat-set bench Hakko FX-888D · one iron, two jobs — brass into PETG, and solder

Tool station · 09/13Kitchen edition

Most of what this iron does is not soldering. A ruthex brass short presses into a printed PETG pocket under the insert tip — twelve in the foam shell, six per reservoir, one per deck-mount column. The solder work is two reed columns.

ShrinkCA-01 · GT-05 QWORK mini heat gun the gun, never a flame 200–450 °Cto full recovery A collar on every cut braid end; a band between every reed 1/2″ braid on most bundles, 3/4″ on the manifold trunk Lacing makes a column stiff enough to drop in; a collar stops braid fraying from its first cut.
⛔
Prove every reed before you lace it — closed with the donor magnet alongside, open with it away. One that fails after lacing costs the column.
The mat is the bench — heat-resistant silicone under the iron, magnetic section holding the M3s and the loose inserts.
The board never meets the iron. It arrives ESD-handled, sits on the mat, and leaves on four SHCS driven into the cap's inserts (ES-03).
Have in hand
Heat, not force — the printed pocket aims the insert, the iron carries it down, and the relief beneath keeps a long screw's tip off PETG.
Leave the bench whenEvery insert flush with its boss face and its bore open, both reed columns laced stiff with all eight reeds proved, and every braid end collared.
