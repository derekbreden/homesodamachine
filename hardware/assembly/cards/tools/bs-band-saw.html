Band saw + cut-off WEN BA4555 · three 316L rods, two lengths, and every end a seating face

Tool station · 02/13Kitchen edition

One operation, three rods. The whole of this machine's job in the appliance is PV-05 — horizontal cutoff, vise and length stop, 1/8″ 316L stock. No rod end is a free end: each one lands in a register or a boss, so square is what you came for and speed is not.
