Laser welder XLaserlab X1 Pro · the tack, the prep, and the two closure fillets

Tool station · 03/13Kitchen edition

The joint is a corner, not an edge. The plate seats 1/4″ below the tube end, so the bore and the plate's outer face form an internal corner and the beam fires into it onto the plate's mass — which backs the puddle. Aimed at the bare 0.065″ tube edge instead, a focused laser cuts back rather than fuses. All four run in one session: heat the welder once.

Operation Mode Set to What it is really for
TackPV-06 Tack, closure recipe rod vertical on the bottom plate's inside face Rod cut to 131.1 mm, base in its register its top must enter the top-plate register without bottoming out The register does the locating — it stands the rod on the donut-wall axis. The tack freezes what the pocket already fixed.
PrepPV-07 X1 cleaning mode or ~30 s per joint with Scotch-Brite 7447 The bore's 1/4″ band, and the plate's outer face not the cut edge, not the plate OD — both buried in the slip joint Clean bare metal, not tooth. Coarse grit on the plate's outer face rounds the perimeter edge — which is the fillet root.
BottomPV-08 60 % · 80 Hz × 2 mmwire 12 mm/s · argon 2 s pre/post · ER316L .030 Plate recessed 1/4″ on the spacer, 8-tack opposite-side-bisecting trail-off motion at end of bead Fuse the corner fully and seal the ~0.005″ radial slip. Keep heat moving — the proud lip is unbacked above the weld and distorts if you dwell.
ClosurePV-09 same recipedonut slid onto the rod first Plate down to its 1/4″ recess against the spacer if the register binds on the rod tip, open that one cap to 5/32″ The register captures the rod tip as the plate seats; the donut is captive between the two for good.
⚠
Handheld class-4 beam — hands are in the work envelope the whole time. TIG gloves on, nothing reflective in the line of fire.
⛔
The rod must never hold the fillet root open — it locates the plate, it does not support it. Confirm seated depth against the spacer.
Don't let go — trigger held, lift straight up at the end of a bead, so the retract cycle fires in mid-air and the wire never re-fuses. Colour inside: yellow/brown passivates out; black scale does not.
Have in hand X1 Pro + argon size-80 / RX Weld · ER316L .030 · 1/4″ spacer · Scotch-Brite 7447 (stainless-only) · V-pads · C110 chill bar · TIG gloves
Why the plate sits low — a fillet driven into a corner backed by 1/4″ of plate is a weld the hand can hold; the same beam aimed at a bare 0.065″ edge is a cut.
Leave the welder whenBoth closure fillets run full-circumference with the plates at their seated depth, the rod captive and not load-bearing, and no black scale inside — ready for dye-penetrant (PV-10).
