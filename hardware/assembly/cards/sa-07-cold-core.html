SA-07

Cold core, plumbed

Sub-assembly · 07/7Kitchen edition

The core off its own bench, with the box nowhere near it: shell poured, both caps down, SA-04’s crown on top. Every one of the 7 cap conduits stands a tube and most of them end in the air.

Pushed home in the cap

7 conduits, one line each. Every fluid line the core takes or gives leaves by the top; the −X face carries nothing but the two reed cables. Cut square, in past the countersink to the shoulder.

Left hanging

5 far ends — co2-2, carb-1, fluid-16, fluid-24, fluid-26. The gas line and the riser wait on bodies the rear wall carries; the three reservoir lines on valves the flavor manifold has not brought. Dress each back over the crown.

Closed on the core

water-5 and fluid-14 — the other 2. Both mouths of the first are on this unit, discharge chain to the water-in conduit. The second is strapped into the cap’s own rib at SA-04 and reaches V-F much later.

The evaporator’s two coppers are not here: they cross at the lane slots and are brazed with the machine standing.

Done when every conduit stands its own tube and each one tug-tests home.
