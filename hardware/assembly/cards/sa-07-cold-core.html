SA-07

Cold core, plumbed

Sub-assembly · 07/9Kitchen edition

The core off its own bench, with the box nowhere near it: shell poured, both caps down, SA-04’s crown on top. Every one of the 9 cap conduits stands a tube and most of them end in the air.

Pushed home in the cap

9 conduits, one line each. Every fluid line the core takes or gives leaves by the top; the −X face carries nothing but the two reed cables. Cut square, in past the countersink to the shoulder.

Left hanging

6 far ends — every conduit’s but the water-in. The gas line and the riser wait on bodies the rear wall carries; the four reservoir lines on valves the flavor manifold has not brought. Dress each back over the crown.

Closed on the core

water-in — the other 1. Both its mouths are on this unit: up the shell from the carbonator, out the conduit, and into the discharge chain’s own collet on the crown.

The evaporator’s two coppers are not here: they cross at the lane slots and are brazed with the machine standing.

Done when every conduit stands its own tube and each one tug-tests home.
