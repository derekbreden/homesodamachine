RL-02

Vent the factory R-600a

Refrigerant loop · 02/8Kitchen edition

The loop opens for the first time, through a valve that never comes off — decompressed fully before any flame.

⚠
R-600a is flammable — LFL ~1.8 % in air. Vent outdoors or under a vent hood, no ignition sources within 3 m.
  1. Find the process tube — the short copper stub off the compressor, pinched-and-brazed shut at the factory.
  2. Clamp the BPV31 saddle on and pierce. It stays for life — the single permanent service point.
  3. Open the valve, vent to atmosphere. Let the loop decompress fully — no rushing the hiss.
  4. Confirm fully vented: gauge atmospheric, no further hiss, no propane-like smell at the valve.
Tools Supco BPV31 bullet-piercing valve B00DM8J3MI · manifold gauge
Legal: EPA Section 608 carves natural refrigerants out of the venting prohibition — venting R-600a is lawful, no certification required.
The BPV31 never comes off — vent (here) · argon (RL-03) · vacuum (RL-06) · recharge (RL-07) all pass through this one flare port.
Done whenGauge reads atmospheric, no further hiss, no propane-like smell at the valve — only then may anything be cut or heated.
