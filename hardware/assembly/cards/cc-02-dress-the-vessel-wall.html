Dress the vessel wall
— reeds, probe, foil

Cold core · 02/15Kitchen edition

Last look at bare 316L. Everything that touches the steel goes on now — foil, coil and foam bury this wall.

⛔
Prove both reeds before the foil closes. A dead reed is found after the pour, with no way back to this wall.
  1. Reed bridge on the register line — PV-03's azimuth, the shell's ±X; the gauge sets its bottom edge at 46.12.
  2. Reeds into the pockets, glass on bare steel — vertical. Pockets set 67.12 CLO / 95.25 CHI; magnet-prove each.
  3. DS18B20 flat on the OD — the setpoint sensor, in the bare band below the wind.
  4. Skin the OD — one continuous 3M 425 layer over the wind band, burnished down onto both ramps.
