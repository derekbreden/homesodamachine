Pressure vessel · 14/14Kitchen edition

After passivation the vessel dresses. Every port's first move is the same — a 316L 90° elbow turning the line lateral — then each port's own stack.

  1. Elbows on all four ports, MNPT into the plate FNPT — PTFE anti-seize on every SS-on-SS joint. Port 4's arrives in the PV-13 subassembly.
  2. Port 4: thread the cured subassembly M-end in. Dedicated port, no tee, no shared line — the relief path stays unobstructed.
  3. Port 1: barb inward, ~3″ silicone stub to the FERRODAY 0.5 µm stone in the water column. Elbow↔barb order: open — measure at fitting.
  4. Ports 2 + 3: the elbow is the whole stack — check valve + adapters land at cold-core.
Open item: confirm the stacks seat with the ports recessed 1/4″.
