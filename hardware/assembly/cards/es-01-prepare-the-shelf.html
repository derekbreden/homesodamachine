ES-01

Prepare the shelf

Electronics shelf · 01/7Kitchen edition

The shelf is the top foam cap. One printed part stages here — the AC hub — and every module lands on a column already standing in the cap, insert set before the pour.

  1. Print the ac-hub and dry-fit one Wago into each of its three wells, butt-end down, to confirm the press fit.
  2. No insert goes in here. Every insert this shelf uses is a ruthex short in a deck-mount column of the top cap, set before the pour at CC-06. Nothing on this bench meets the iron.
  3. Confirm the columns are clear — flash trimmed, bores open, each insert flush with its column top.
  4. Relay #2 + the DC distribution block have no station — stage them loose.
