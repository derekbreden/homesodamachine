Finish + ship · 04/5Kitchen edition

Bottom-up pack, top-down unboxing — the guide is met first in the kit, the front face first in the carton.

  1. Stand the carton, TOP arrow up — bottom foam end-cap in, recess up. Kit stacks per the table at right.
  2. Lower the appliance rear-to-REAR — umbilical cluster faces the marked rear wall; top end-cap mates the corners + funnel surround.
  3. Fill the side voids — faucet bag coiled 8–12″, faucet body toward carton TOP; kit box documentation face up. Wedge both with foam corner-blocks.
  4. Seal — short flaps then long; kraft tape across all three top seams, then the bottom. A $7,500 D2C carton telegraphs care.
