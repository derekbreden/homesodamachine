Mount the PSU,
relays, PCBA

Electronics shelf · 03/7Kitchen edition

Each part onto its boss pattern — M3 × 8 SHCS into the heat-set inserts, torqued by feel. The board is the one ESD-handled part.

  1. PSU first — the Mean Well IRM-90-12ST down onto its four power-tray bosses.
  2. Relays — relay #1 (compressor switch) on the power-tray standoffs; relay #2 (diaphragm-pump switch) at its staged position.
  3. Controller PCBA, ESD-handled — four SHCS through the isolated corner holes MH1–MH4; heads seat on pads the pours keep clear.
  4. Orientation is the tray frame: USB-C (J14) flush at the west board edge, J10 screw throats facing east — both edges left reachable.
Nothing is powered or flashed here — pre-flash happens over J14 at FC-02, after install.
Tools M3 × 8 SHCS (bom.md §13) · ESD mat
pcba-assembly — the JLCPCB-assembled board (85.05 × 72.85 mm) on its four corner standoffs; every field interface is a labeled edge connector.
The tray frame is the board's pcb frame — every boss centre is its MH coordinate verbatim, so the board only seats one way.
Done whenPSU, both relays, and the board seated on their patterns, torqued by feel — J14 west + J10 east edges clear.
