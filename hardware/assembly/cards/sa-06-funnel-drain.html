Funnel
drain stub

Sub-assembly · 06/9The appliance

The funnel, inverted, with the one joint it never gives up. This pair goes to the dishwasher together — every other joint on the funnel is one the customer opens.

Pushed in

1/4″ LLDPE stub — 27.75 mm cut square. In through the spout until it stops on the ramp tip: 12 mm of it inside the silicone. The rest stands proud and is what the union takes — 15.75 mm of grip, so nothing of the stub is ever in the open.

Clamped on

LOKMAN worm-gear clamp, one band. Band centred on the spout’s straight land with a shoulder of silicone showing either side, housing turned off the collar so a driver comes in level. Snug, then a quarter turn — the wall is 2 mm of silicone and it is the seal.

Nothing else

No adhesive, no printed part. The spout is the whole fixture. Wash the funnel with the stub on it — both parts are dishwasher stock.

The John Guest union is not on this bench. It stays in the machine on the far end of fluid-4, and the stub goes into its collet at final staging — EN-09.

Done when the stub bottoms on the ramp tip, the band sits wholly on the straight land, and a firm pull on the stub does not move it.
