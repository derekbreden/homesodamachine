Sub-assembly · 06/9The appliance

The 455.44 mL silicone funnel, its sliding PET-GF frame, and the drain elbow's cradle. Check the hook bearing and plug seal in the printed assembly.

Captured by the enclosure

Slide the frame into front-top. Back-top captures the opposite rails as the enclosure closes. Keep the rail bearing faces square and clear of support residue.

Flat underside

3 mm web beneath the silicone plug, pierced by the 11.25 mm drain hole and the cradle's two wing slots. The full-width end corbels include both rail wings.

Drain elbow

Snap the cradle up under the frame with the PP0308E elbow in it. Pinch both wings in until the hooks pass the slots, then let them spring out over the web. The drain stub stands in the elbow's upper collet; fluid-4 leaves its aft collet for V-B.

Removable silicone

The silicone lifts out from above. Its block rests on the cradle's hooks inside the frame's socket, its land sliding on and off the drain stub.

Native clearances pass. Check the assembled frame and drain joint before fluid commissioning. See the funnel integration review.
