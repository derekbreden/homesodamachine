  3. Reservoir A takes its own two mouths: V-F out onto the fill (14), the draw up into V-E (16). No junction on either.
  4. Shared upstream last: the split’s forward run → flow regulator → V-A (1 + 2); funnel bottom → V-B. LLDPE through each pump's BPT barbs, zip-tied; female fastons onto DC-5.
  5. Land the manifold above the compressor — runs in the printed channels, zip-tied at every pinch-point.
