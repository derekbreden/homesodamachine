Flavor manifold
— pumps and channels

Internal plumbing · 04/7The appliance

Build on loose front-top; keep the cartridge separate. 10 valves, 6 tees. B mirrors A.

  1. Retain the 4 moving tees: Y-C, Y-D, Y-F, Y-G, two ties each in the spring-loaded carrier, branches fore. Fit valve rows + bowed black-LLDPE links per §3.
  2. Build the cartridge (SA-09). Seat both pumps, silicone outlets +Y. Insert 1/4″ OD LLDPE into both silicone ends; zip-tie the joints. Mark the 4 free tips 17.53 mm aft of the rigid outlet-axis casing datum; pump-end engagement is extra stock.
  3. Fit the female pogo half and leads before closing the top clamp (WR-04). Draw the 2 M3 × 60 screws evenly. Pumps firm, no vertical play; motor ends + terminals open.
  4. Land the fixed limbs: A fill/draw → V-F/V-E; B fill/draw → V-I/V-H, directly. Split → flow regulator → V-A; retained PP0308E drain elbow → V-B via fluid-4.
  5. Dress fixed spans in their channels; keep bows and tee-side hairpins free. Leave the cartridge out until chassis closure; seat with power off and check tubes + contacts at IP-05.
Tools Mudder cutter · faston crimper · zip ties
Segment numbers from the topology tables; both pumps seat in the fitted lower cradle. The top clamp holds them with 2 M3 × 60 screws.
