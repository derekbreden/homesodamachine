GT-01

NPT joints — tape,
engagement, witness

Technique · 01/5Kitchen edition

Tapered threads seal on the taper; the PTFE is anti-seize and void-fill, not a gasket. One discipline for every NPT joint in the appliance.

  1. Tape: 2–3 wraps, clockwise viewed into the thread — driving tightens the wrap — first thread bare (no shreds in the stream).
  2. Tape stops by half the engagement. Tape past half is a stress concentration (IP-06's witness rule).
  3. Drive snug + 1 turn past hand-tight, wrench backed up on the mating hex.
  4. SS-on-SS always gets tape — stainless galls dry; the PTFE is what lets the joint ever come apart again.
  5. Witness: PTFE ≤ half the engagement, none at the mouth, dry at the wet check.
Materials Millrose PTFE tape B07C9ZV4PG · backup wrench
Fresh-tapped ports (PV-02): target 4.5 turns of engagement — a test fitting sits snug-firm with 2–3 threads showing.
Done whenEvery NPT joint snug + 1 with a backed-up wrench, first thread bare, tape stopped by half — and dry at the wet check.
