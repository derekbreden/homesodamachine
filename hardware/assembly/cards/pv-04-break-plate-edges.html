PV-04

Break the plate edges
— asymmetric

Pressure vessel · 04/14Kitchen edition

One laser-cut perimeter, two different edges — because only one of them is a weld surface.

  1. Inside-face edge: chamfer freely. It's the lead-in that lets the plug find center in the ~0.005″ radial slip — and it ends up inside the vessel, where no beam reaches it.
  2. Outside-face edge: break the burr only. No chamfer — that corner is the fillet root (PV-08/09). Chamfering it widens the root gap the laser has to bridge, working against penetration.
  3. Keep the faces apart. The register (PV-03) marks the inside face; mark the outside face if it isn't obvious at a glance. Each plate carries its orientation from here on.
Tools Noga NG8150 swivel-blade deburr · both plates
Same tool, opposite intent — one edge is a lead-in, the other is the corner the weld exists to fill.
Done whenBoth plates: inside edge chamfered, outside edge burr-broken sharp, orientation unmistakable.
