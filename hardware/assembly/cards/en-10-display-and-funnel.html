Loose front-top, unpowered. Contact first; valves and frame follow. Finish before EN-05.

  1. ESD-safe: seat the glass, TPU ring on its border. Keep the ring off the screen. Flex the cover's middle, enter both wings, release onto its back datum; check capture and flushness. Leads loose.
  2. Feed the ~350 mm fixed DC-5 ribbon through the lead bore. Solder and sleeve each male tail while the half is free. Marked end at −X: 2 M1.4×5 screws into the EN-01 inserts. Retain the ribbon in the ridge clip; secure J13 unplugged for WR-04.
  3. Snap the elbow cradle under the frame. Elbow vertical, pinch the wings through the slots until the hooks hold. Cut the raw stub 25.79 mm square; push down the drain hole fully into the elbow's upper collet. Keep dry.
  4. Slide the frame in before closure. Back-top captures its opposite rails. Deburr without rounding their bearing faces.
