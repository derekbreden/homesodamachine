CA-01

Build a harness — cut,
crimp, sleeve, test

Cable assemblies · 01/2Kitchen edition

Built complete and tested off the chassis. The repair is swap-the-assembly — so build jig-and-test, all-black.

  1. Cut to length from the spool — schedule length + a service loop. All-black but AC.
  2. Strip to barrel, terminate: ferrule→Wago · faston→tab · ring→ground · XH→housing. No solder splices.
  3. Fan out at the device end: one rail wire out, exploded in the Wago there — never N parallel from the board.
  4. Sleeve in PET braid, heat-shrink every cut end; comb parallel; flush-cut ties.
  5. Test pin-to-pin vs the schedule — no adjacent shorts, commons exact. Label by name; bag for WR.
Tools Preciva 28–5 · Haisstronica 22–10 · iCrimp SN-2549 · Klein stripper · heat gun · meter · technique GT-03 / GT-04 / GT-05
A failed termination makes the whole assembly suspect — swap it end-to-end, never trace and re-crimp one conductor.
