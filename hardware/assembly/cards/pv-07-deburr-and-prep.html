PV-07

Deburr the tube + prep
the weld surfaces

Pressure vessel · 07/14Kitchen edition

The fillet sees two surfaces — the bore's 1/4″ band, the plate's outer face. The rest is about getting the plate to seat.

  1. Deburr both tube ends, ID + OD — one sitting while the tube is loose. Light: the 0.065″ wall has little to give.
  2. It buys insertion. ~0.005″ of radial slip — a rolled-over burr catches the plate part-way down.
  3. Prep the two fillet surfaces — ~30 s per joint, 7447 pad or X1 cleaning mode. Bare metal, not tooth.
  4. Skip what's buried (cut edge, plate OD) — and no coarse grit: 80-120 rounds the root corner (PV-04).
  5. Pads stay stainless-only — embedded free iron outlives the citric soak (PV-12).
Tools Noga NG8150 · Scotch-Brite 7447 (SS-only) · X1 cleaning mode
Shaded = prep zones. Bore band below each end + plate outer face — the only surfaces the beam touches.
Done whenEnds burr-free ID + OD; bright metal on both bore bands + plate outer faces; root corners still sharp.
