GT-03

Crimp lugs — ferrules,
forks, fastons, rings

Technique · 03/5Kitchen edition

Four terminal families, one law: the right die on the right barrel at the right gauge, then the tug. No solder splices anywhere in the harnesses (CA-01).

  1. Strip to the barrel: bare copper the length of the crimp barrel, no more — strands proud of a ferrule get trimmed, insulation inside a barrel is a false crimp.
  2. Match die to terminal and gauge — the die stamps its gauge; a loose die makes a shiny crimp that meters fine and fails warm.
  3. Crimp the barrel on copper only. Insulated skirts grip the jacket — strain relief, not the connection.
  4. Tug every crimp as it's made. One that moves is scrap — cut it off and redo; never re-squeeze.
  5. Failed assembly? Swap end-to-end — never trace and re-crimp one conductor (CA-01).
Tools crimper set per CA-01 · Klein stripper · flush cutters
The tug is the acceptance test — a crimp that holds the tug and meters Ω-low is done; anything else is scrap, immediately.
