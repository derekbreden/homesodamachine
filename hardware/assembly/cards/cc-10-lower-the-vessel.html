Lower the vessel
— elbow already on it

Cold core · 10/15Kitchen edition

The CO2 elbow is made up on the vessel at the bench, not fitted in the cavity — the ring's notch is open to its top face, so the elbow rides down with the tank.

  1. Build the CO2 leg on the bench: PP010822E into the bottom-plate TAISHER elbow, a 1/4″ stub, then the PP0308E — free port up. Tug-test now; nothing is reachable later.
  2. Lower the vessel bottom-plate-down, elbow aimed at the rear notch — 18 mm wide, cut clear through the ring's plateau, so the elbow rides down in it.
  3. Seat the rim on the ring plateau — 30 mm above the floor; the bottom-plate fittings hang in open volume inboard of the ring.
  4. Final tail check: rotate until both copper tails lie at the slot wall — inlet low, outlet high (CC-12).
