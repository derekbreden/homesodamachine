Cold core · 10/15Kitchen edition

Both bottom-plate legs are made up at the bench, never in the cavity — inboard of the ring they descend through open space as the tank seats.

  1. Make up both bottom-plate legs at the bench: a TAISHER elbow per port, PP010822E adapter turned on, the CO2's collet clocked to the ring's own reach. Tug-test now.
  2. Lower the vessel bottom-plate-down. Nothing has to clear a notch — inboard of the ring the volume is open to the floor.
  3. Seat the rim on the ring plateau — 30 mm above the floor, flat all round.
  4. Final tail check: rotate until each copper tail lies on its own lane — inlet port, outlet west (CC-12).
