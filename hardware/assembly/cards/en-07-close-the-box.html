Close the box

Enclosure · 07/9Kitchen edition

The box closes around the pack, not the other way round. Bodies stand on open floor; the pieces come down over them.

  1. Front column first — front-top down over front-bottom, D-pins into the pods as the lip telescopes up. It passes over the flavor pack, not onto it.
  2. Then the Y seam — a proud tongue on the walls and ceiling, a shiplap inside the floor so the slab stays flat under the core.
  3. Back column last — back-top. It is the lid over the service bay; lifting it is the only way in.
  4. Every seam screw from a ±X exterior face — 4 per Z seam, both ends of each side wall, so a seam cannot hinge open at the end it was not pinned at.
  5. Then look inside: collets, stub and C14 tabs all reachable — nothing routed yet.
When back-top closes is an open item. Check the traveller.
