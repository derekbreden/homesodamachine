  1. Front column — the collet plate goes into front-top first, down the slot in its bay floor from the seam face, onto that floor’s top. Then front-top slides AFT onto front-bottom, in from the front, returns home on their stop blocks, carrying the flavor pack in over the stratum’s crowns — and the steel with it.
  2. Back column, over a bare tub — back-top slides AFT onto an empty back-bottom. Its +Y wall sweeps the whole depth on the way in, so nothing stands there yet — the bay rides IN it: chain, meter, ceiling, power column.
  3. The back assembly rides FORE over the core — the Y-seam mouth swallows the core on its cart, hold-down feet riding the cap, and the motion ends as the Y seam’s own telescope: tongue in, scarf closed, plugs in their slots.
  4. Four M3×10 from the ±X exteriors — the floor pair pins the bottoms, the ceiling pair the tops. They also lock both slides: both tops leave FORE, the way they came. Back-top’s way out runs into front-top; front-top’s runs into open air, and the ceiling pair alone is what stands across it.
  5. Then look inside: collets, stub and C14 tabs all reachable — nothing routed yet.
Service is the same door backwards. Four screws, ride the back assembly aft off the core, and the bay is bench work again.
