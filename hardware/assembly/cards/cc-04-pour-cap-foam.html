CC-04

Pour the cap foam
— both caps

Cold core · 04/15Kitchen edition

Each cap is a 16 mm-tall foam-filled cup, poured on the bench: cup mouth-up, lid sealing the open face from above, foam in through the lid.

⚠
Nitrile gloves on. The PU foam's isocyanate component is a skin sensitizer — gloves for every mix and pour.
  1. Set both cups mouth-up, match the lids — the lid with the extra ⌀6.5 mm hole rides the CO2-bossed top cap; the boss walls its bore off from the pour.
  2. Mix the two-part foam 1:1 in a graduated cup — measured shot, stirred, poured while liquid.
  3. Pour through the lid's ⌀20 mm hole. Air escapes the two ⌀6 mm vents; foam expands to fill and cures.
  4. Pour both caps in parallel; trim flush after cure — proud foam comes off level with the lid.
Materials 2 lb 2-part closed-cell PU foam B08R7TX8QJ · graduated cups · stir sticks · nitrile gloves
foam-cap-top — the CO2 boss marks the top cap.
Done whenBoth caps cured full, foam trimmed flush at pour + vents — and the top cap's CO2 bore is open end to end.
