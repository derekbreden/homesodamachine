Sub-assembly · 05/9Kitchen edition

SA-01 slides AFT onto a bare bottom piece — entering over its open fore end, mouth on shoulder, until both returns land on their stop blocks. The tub is empty by design: this piece’s own +Y wall sweeps the column’s whole depth on the way in.

The bay rides the top

Everything the service bay needs is already aboard SA-01. The six connection bodies, the power column, the tap-water chain in its anchor, the meter under the ceiling, the panel in its dados. Nothing waits on the floor; SA-04 and its core ride in later, through the Y-seam mouth, at EN-07.

No screw on this seam

The rails are the joint. The bottom’s flared heads ride the top’s channels the whole travel; lifted, the 45° pairs wedge shut along both runs. The −X return stops fore of the PRV chase’s rib, the +X at the rear horizon — both landing is what home is. The Y seam’s screws, later, are what lock the way back out.

Nothing else

No body is mounted at this moment, and none is inside. The pump lead (DC-8) and every crossing run are made up through the open mouth after the core rides in.

4 runs will hang out of that mouth once the core is in — fluid-18 off the +Y wall of back-top, fluid-16, fluid-24, fluid-26 off the core. The core, then the front half, take that mouth next.

Done when the halves are on their pins, the seam screws driven from the flanks, the pump lead landed.
