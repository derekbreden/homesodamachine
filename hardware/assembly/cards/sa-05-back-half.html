Enclosure back half

Sub-assembly · 05/9Kitchen edition

SA-01 coming down over the bottom piece with SA-04 already standing in it. Everything either half owes is done first — what is left is the joint and one plug.

Joined as they close

SeaFlo 12 V pair, run DC-8. The pump stands on the core in the bottom half and its driver on the +X wall of the top one, so the lead is landed as the two come together. Dress it clear of the seam lip first.

Seated on the barbed lip

No screw crosses the Z seam. The bottom piece’s three-sided lip telescopes +Z into the top, which lands a few millimetres aft of home — barbs through their notches, dry pins into their slots — and slides forward until the pins stop. The barbs ride under the lip’s roof and hold the seam shut; the Y-seam screws at EN-07 are what stop the slide backing out.

Nothing else

No body is mounted at this moment. The core spans this seam and the bay above it is closed by it. A hand inside after this is a hand through the Y-seam mouth only.

4 runs hang out of that mouth — fluid-18 off the +Y wall of back-top, fluid-16, fluid-24, fluid-26 off the core, each pushed home while its own piece was open. The front half takes that mouth next.

Done when the halves are on their pins, the seam screws driven from the flanks, the pump lead landed.
