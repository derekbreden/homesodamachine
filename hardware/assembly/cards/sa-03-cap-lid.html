Sub-assembly · 03/4Kitchen edition

The same core with its lid populated. All of it goes on while the core is loose on the bench, before it is lowered into the back bottom piece.

Screwed down

SeaFlo 22-series pump. M3 × 16 and a washer through each of the 4 rubber foot pads, through the lid, into a deck-mount column’s insert in the cap beneath. Snug, not torqued — the pad is the isolator.

Pressed in

3 valves — V-K, V-A, V-B. Each drops into a set of four printed cradle bosses on this face — one under each corner post, blind sockets, nothing bonded and no screw. The valve body hangs in the gap between them.

Strapped into printed ribs

2 chains and 2 runs — suction, discharge, water-3, fluid-14. Tie lying open across the channel first, body or tube in second. 6″ on each chain’s hex, 4″ on each run. Metal only — never a collet body.

fluid-14 leaves here with its far end hanging: the cap conduit is made and strapped now, V-F takes the other end much later.

Done when the pump is down on all four, every valve is seated arrow-aft, and both chains and both runs are strapped and flush-cut.
