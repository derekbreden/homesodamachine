CC-06

Build the reed columns

Cold core · 06/16Kitchen edition

Each reservoir's level gauge is four reed switches outside the syrup, soldered into one stiff column that drops into the shell's channel. The float's magnet (CC-07) does the rest. Build two — A and B.

  1. Cut the spine: 5 × 22 AWG black silicone conductors — 4 reed signals + 1 shared common — running the length of the column past all four reeds.
  2. Solder each reed in at ~45 mm pitch — one lead to its signal conductor, the other to the shared common. Reed 1 sits low.
  3. Lace it — heat-shrink between reeds — into one pre-assembled column, stiff enough to drop in as a piece.
  4. Prove every reed with the donor magnet: closed (signal ↔ common) with the magnet alongside, open with it away.
