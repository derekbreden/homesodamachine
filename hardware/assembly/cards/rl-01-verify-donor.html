RL-01

Verify the donor
+ factory charge

Refrigerant loop · 01/8Kitchen edition

One label decides the whole session: the donor must run R-600a, and its printed factory charge is the number the recharge target builds from (RL-07).

  1. Read the back-panel rating label — refrigerant type and charge mass. The type must be R-600a.
  2. Record the factory charge: 15 g Unit A · 23 g Unit B, both per manufacturer manual.
  3. Ignore the compressor casting. 48.5-2 and 45 are body cast-stamps — capacity-index codes, not charge masses.
  4. Any HFC = full stop. R-134a, R-410a: Section 608 certification to vent, and the cold-core architecture changes — this procedure does not apply.
