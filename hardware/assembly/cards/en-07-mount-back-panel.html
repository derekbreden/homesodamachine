Mount the populated
back panel

Enclosure · 07/8Kitchen edition

Every connection except CO2 lands on this face, above the cold core. The panel manages connections — it holds no pressure.

  1. Lift the populated panel (EN-01) and seat it against the rear of the shell.
  2. Fasten per the enclosure-CAD screw pattern — still open; working position M3 SHCS into ruthex.
  3. Confirm the blue ring at the top vertex. The install rule — blue tube into the blue ring — needs it where the eye expects.
  4. Confirm nothing is routed yet — stubs wait for the plumbing bench (IP), C14 pins for wiring (WR).
No gasket. Each bulkhead's EPDM O-ring seals around its own tube — panel-to-shell is mechanical capture only.
