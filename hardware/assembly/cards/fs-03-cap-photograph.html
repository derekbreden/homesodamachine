Cap the inlets
+ photograph

Finish + ship · 03/5Kitchen edition

Caps keep debris out of the wetted path and tell the installer where to connect; the photos are the unit's as-shipped memory.

  1. Cap both fluid inlets — both on the rear wall, and neither is a thread. Water: the PP1208E's outboard collet, upstream of the backflow. CO2: rubber over the ABU44's 5/16″ collar. Off at install; the water cap never goes back on.
  2. Leave the umbilical row bare — the PP1208E grab-ring collets keep debris out for the transit-only window.
  3. Two photos minimum — table at right, good light at the plaque face.
  4. Archive both → logs/<serial>/finish/, beside the burn-in logs. The rear-wall shot rides in the tracking email (FS-05).
