FS-03

Cap the inlets
+ photograph

Finish + ship · 03/5Kitchen edition

Caps keep debris out of the wetted path and tell the installer where to connect; the photos are the unit's as-shipped memory.

  1. Cap both fluid inlets — both on the rear wall. Water: the PP1208E's outboard push-to-connect collet, the one upstream of the backflow the supply lands on. CO2: press-on rubber over the DERPIPE's 5/16″ collar, east of centre and below the umbilical row. Neither is a thread. Off at install; the water cap never goes back on.
  2. Leave the umbilical row bare — the PP1208E grab-ring collets keep debris out for the transit-only window.
  3. Two photos minimum — rear: plaque centered, serial + QR readable, signature legible, good light; 3/4 front: front face, top funnel, one side.
  4. Archive both → logs/<serial>/finish/, beside the burn-in logs. The rear shot rides in the tracking email (FS-05).
