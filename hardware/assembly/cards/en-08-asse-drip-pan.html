EN-08

Slide the ASSE drip pan
in through the −X wall

Enclosure · 08/9The appliance

The backflow vent terminates inside the cabinet — a drip is a failed check, and this probe is how the ESP32 hears it. The sleeve is on back-top's −X wall; the pan docks on it.

  1. Dress the pan. Press the pogo pair's female half into the east wall's pocket from inside, nose first, to its shoulders. Solder the probe's two leads to its tails, pot the pocket's mouth, and lay the probe flat on the floor inside the coves.
  2. Slide the pan in through the −X wall, east end first, until it stops against the backstop. The last millimetre is the dock's and the magnets take it.
  3. Route the sleeve's two leads loose toward the power column; SIG-9 lands at the wiring bench (WR).
Order matters. Probe first: once the pan is in its sleeve it is under the chain, and a hand laying a plate flat reaches past the ASSE fittings. The male half went in at EN-02.
