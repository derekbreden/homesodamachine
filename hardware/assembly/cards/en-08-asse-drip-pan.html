Slide the ASSE drip pan
in through the −X wall

Enclosure · 08/9The appliance

The backflow vent terminates inside the cabinet — a drip is a failed check, and this probe is how the ESP32 hears it. The sleeve is on back-top's −X wall; the plate lies loose on one continuous lead.

  1. Retain the lead on the dry wall. Press the plate's continuous two-wire lead through the printed clip aft of the sleeve. Leave the withdrawal-and-lift loop on the pan side; route the fixed end to the dry LM393 board.
  2. Start the pan through the −X wall, east end first. Lay the plate flat inside the coves, lead rising over the aft edge; feed slack back toward the clip while pushing the pan against its solid backstop.
  3. Prove the service motion. Draw until the plate is reachable, lift it completely out, then remove the empty pan. Reverse those motions to reinstall.
No wet-wall connection. The pan is one watertight print. Its cable clip is in dry enclosure material, and its recessed channel ramps back to the wall face at both ends.
