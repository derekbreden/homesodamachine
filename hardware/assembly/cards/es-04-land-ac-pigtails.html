ES-04

Land the AC pigtails

Electronics shelf · 04/7Kitchen edition

Every 120 V conductor in the appliance passes through these three levers: one in-leg from the C14 pigtail, two out-legs each. Seat, lock, then meter.

  1. Open each 221-413 lever, seat the staged ferrule, lock it down — legs per the diagram.
  2. Land the AC-2 forks on the PSU primary screw terminals; AC-2 G on the PSU chassis ground stud.
  3. Land the AC-3 fork on relay #1's contact input — COM on the Teyleten silk.
  4. Relay #1's NO stays empty — AC-4, the compressor SJOOW's switched-H, forks onto it at WR-02.
  5. Meter every bay: continuity from the AC-1 stub to each named out-leg.
Tools small flat screwdriver · multimeter · staged pigtails from ES-02
Lock each lever, then meter — continuity from the AC-1 stub to every named out-leg, bay by bay.
Done whenThree levers locked, every bay metered through, AC-2 + AC-3 terminated at the PSU and relay #1.
