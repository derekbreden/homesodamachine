Cut the three
LLDPE tubes

Faucet + umbilical · 01/5Kitchen edition

One cut per tube, all three the same length — the umbilical bundles them in one sleeve and they terminate at the same end face.

⛔
Do not cut tight. The factory length is sized long with the installer-trim allowance baked in — the second cut happens at the customer's cabinet, not here.
  1. Cut 1× blue + 2× black with the Mudder cutter — square end, no burr, one cut each.
  2. Cut to the design length — still an open item: countertop thickness, faucet-to-cabinet offset, and rear-panel position sum into it.
  3. The installer makes the second cut, trimming the rear-panel end to the actual cabinet depth before the push-connect.
