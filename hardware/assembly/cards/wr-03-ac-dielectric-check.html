The gate between the AC build and the first DC conductor: what must conduct does, what must not stays open.

⚠
C14 off line, multimeter only. Any fail — find and fix before WR-04.
  1. Earth end-to-end: C14 earth pin to every chassis-ground target from WR-01.
  2. H/N through-paths: C14 hot → block H · C14 neutral → block N · block H → relay #1 COM.
  3. Jumper the relay closed (across the input opto): block H reads through to the compressor hot spade.
  4. H↔N open, relay open. Closed it reads the winding — ~10–30 Ω for a 100 W hermetic.
  5. Leakage: every current-carrying conductor to every ground target — open, no exceptions.
