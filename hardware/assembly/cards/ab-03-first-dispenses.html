First dispenses — water,
flavor A, flavor B

Acceptance · 03/7Kitchen edition

Chill first, then three pours — plain carbonated water to the glass, then a metered ratio test on each channel into the cylinder.

  1. Wait out first chill — ~20 °C tap → 2 °C takes tens of minutes to the first compressor-off. Coil hitting −8 °C first = short charge.
  2. Pour water only — wall ≤ 4 °C, compressor off: full pour, 12 oz glass, thermocouple in at once.
  3. Read the glass — table at right. A warm pour is heat pickup on the chilled run: CARGEN sleeve or foil bond.
  4. Meter A, then B — the cylinder catches ~250 mL water + ~12.5 mL concentrate per channel. Pumps audible — no slip, no stall.
