Acceptance · 03/7The appliance

Chill first, then first- and successive-glass carbonation, followed by a metered ratio test on each flavor channel.

  1. Record first chill to the 2 °C wall setpoint. Log both probes and elapsed time; keep the −8 °C freeze protection.
  2. Pour water only — wall ≤ 4 °C, compressor off: full pour, 12 oz glass, thermocouple in at once.
  3. Compare carbonation to the strong reference at matched temperature. Repeat three 12 oz pours; log cadence, glass temperature, refills and recovery.
  4. Meter A, then B — the cylinder catches ~250 mL water + ~12.5 mL concentrate per channel. Pumps audible — no slip, no stall.
