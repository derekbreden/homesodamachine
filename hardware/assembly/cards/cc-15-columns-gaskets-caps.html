All three pours cured — now the core closes: columns into the still-open channels, gaskets on, two foam-filled caps clamped with twelve screws.

  1. Drop the pre-soldered reed columns into the reed channels — the bottom shelf catches them; cables run out their low wall holes to J6 / J7.
  2. First TPU gasket on the body's top edge — perimeter ring with 8 × 8 mm pads at the six screw positions.
  3. Top cap over the gasket, rotated 180° about Z — its CO2 bore lands over the line standing in the body. The pattern bolts either way; the bore tells you which is right. Six M3 × 25 down into the top-face inserts.
  4. Second gasket + bottom cap mouth-down under the body — six M3 × 25 up into the bottom-face inserts. The caps trap the columns from above.
