FC-03

Sensor health walkthrough

Commissioning · 03/5Kitchen edition

Walk the firmware's periodic health frame line by line — every input proved live and in the log.

  1. Open the monitor — pio device monitor -e esp32dev.
  2. Read the bus — every address ACKs (plate). Missing 0x5A = J8 loom / MPR121; any other = board fault.
  3. Prove the probes — two families only: 0x28 tank + 0x10 coil, both ±2 °C of room. One address = parasitic-power miswire, J4 loom.
  4. Provoke the rest — magnet to each reed, spin the impeller, touch sleeves + display, damp the pads.
  5. Wait out the MQ-6 — ~60 s warm-up, then clean-air baseline; out-of-bounds blocks FC-04.
