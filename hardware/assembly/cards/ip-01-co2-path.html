IP-01

CO2 path — front panel
to cold core

Internal plumbing · 01/6Kitchen edition

Two NPT joints in series — check, then regulator — hang off the front panel; one LLDPE run closes into the cold core. Dry path first.

  1. DERPIPE bulkhead NPT-side inboard (EN). Tape it: 2–3 wraps clockwise, first thread bare.
  2. GASHER check on, arrow away from the bulkhead. Snug + 1 turn past hand-tight, backed up on the DERPIPE hex.
  3. WR1110 “IN” (taped) onto the GASHER — same snug + 1. It hangs on threads until its EN bracket.
  4. Tape “OUT”; first PP010822E on — the NPT→PTC transition, warm side of the foam boundary.
  5. LLDPE cut square — no chamfer, no angle — up and over into the cold-core CO2 input. Click, then tug both ends.
Tools Millrose PTFE tape · crescent wrench + backup · Mudder cutter
Closed dry — charging is AB-02. Vessel-side of the boundary (elbow, barb, silicone, sparge stone) is already sealed in the foam (CC).
Done whenClosed dry, DERPIPE to cold core — both NPT joints past hand-tight, both PTC ends clicked home and tug-tested.
