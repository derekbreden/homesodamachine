Internal plumbing · 01/7The appliance

Five gray-acetal PI010822S adapters. Four on the warm chain, one on the bottom gas elbow.

  1. Check ABU44 in the +Y wall’s ⌀17.3 bore. Keep the WR1110 bracket.
  2. Fit PI010822S to WR1110 IN/OUT and check inlet. Check arrow points toward the carbonator.
  3. Coupling on the check’s male outlet, then PI010822S. Seal the NPT joints with supported wrench flats.
  4. Thread a 6-inch tie through the empty check cradle. Seat the metal inlet boss and close the tie. Fit hoses, seat and tug-test ends. Record fit and check qualification per the procedure.
Tools PTFE tape · 2× crescent · tubing cutter · made-up fit record
