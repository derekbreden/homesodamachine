PV-05

Cut the level rods
— three per appliance

Pressure vessel · 05/14Kitchen edition

All three level-sensing rods cut from the same 1/8″ (3.175 mm) 316L stock, 12″ sticks — square, deburred, on-length.

  1. Measure and mark from a squared stock end — 131.1 mm once, 174 mm twice.
  2. Cut square. Every rod end is a seating face — drilled register (PV-06) or printed boss (CC-08).
  3. Deburr both ends to a clean edge. A burr falsifies the seat and scores the float bore.
  4. Verify length — err short, never long. A long rod holds the top plate (PV-09) or the reservoir cap off its seat. Built-in clearance: 1 mm. That is the whole budget.
