Witness and tidy
every joint

Internal plumbing · 06/6Kitchen edition

A hands-on pass over IP-01…05 — every joint gets a hand on it, then the runs tie down for transport.

  1. Tug-test every PTC connection — walk the CO2 path, the water path, the manifold, the risers.
  2. Pump-port clamps: four, two per stub — one on the pump’s barb, one on the adapter’s. Past finger-tight, PVC pillowed between band and barb.
  3. NPT joints: past hand-tight, PTFE showing at no more than half the engagement — excess past half is a stress concentration.
  4. The vent column: stub hangs straight down; clear from its tip to the drip pan, nothing in the fall.
  5. Pumps seated, leads clear for spade termination at WR; zip-tie every span over ~6″ — at bend transitions and the pump-mount boundary.
Tools hands · eyes · zip ties, flush-cut
Three checks a hand and an eye make dry — no pressure, no power, before WR touches a lead.
Done whenEvery joint witnessed dry and tied down — the plumbing does not change after this card. WR lands the leads; AB brings the water.
