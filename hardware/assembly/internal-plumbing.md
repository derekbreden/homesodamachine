# Internal Plumbing

The production procedure for closing every internal fluid path inside the assembled enclosure — CO2 line from the front-panel inlet to the carbonator sparge port, filtered tap-water from the rear-panel inlet through the backflow preventer to the carbonator water-inlet port, the full two-channel flavor manifold (source-select valves, peristaltic pumps, reservoir/nozzle outputs), and the three-tube riser that lands on the back-panel umbilical bulkheads. Comes in after the mechanical chassis is closed up by [`enclosure-mechanical.md`](/hardware/assembly/enclosure-mechanical.md) and feeds [`wiring.md`](/hardware/assembly/wiring.md) and the first powered run at [`firmware-and-commissioning.md`](/hardware/assembly/firmware-and-commissioning.md).

Design intent and the path-by-path enumeration live in [`/hardware/future.md`](/hardware/future.md) "Carbonation subsystem" + "Flavor subsystem" + "Backflow vent monitoring". The valve / Y-junction / tube-segment truth table this manifold builds to is [`/hardware/topology/fluid-topology.md`](/hardware/topology/fluid-topology.md) — the manifold matches that file segment-for-segment. Cold-core internal plumbing (everything inside the foam shell) is already complete per [`cold-core.md`](/hardware/assembly/cold-core.md). Refrigerant lines are already brazed and charged per [`refrigerant-loop.md`](/hardware/assembly/refrigerant-loop.md). Above-counter umbilical (faucet body, 3-tube bundle, foam insulation, sleeve) is already built per [`faucet-and-umbilical.md`](/hardware/assembly/faucet-and-umbilical.md).

## Scope

In: mechanical chassis assembled per [`enclosure-mechanical.md`](/hardware/assembly/enclosure-mechanical.md) — cold core seated against the back wall with coil stubs brazed and its eight penetrations (1/4" OD tubing) presented warm-side, all eight on its front face, back-panel bulkheads (3× PP1208E umbilical + the water-inlet PP1208E 1/4" JG QC) installed plus front-panel CO2 DERPIPE PTC installed, the electronics shelf seated on the foam-cap top but unpowered, internal drip pan with moisture sensor mounted under the future backflow-preventer vent location. Plus the parts listed under "Inputs per appliance" below.

Out: every internal fluid path closed and primed-ready — fully connected, fully torqued, fully clamped, leak-witnessed dry where possible, but **not yet primed with water or CO2**. Fluid charging happens at [`acceptance-and-burn-in.md`](/hardware/assembly/acceptance-and-burn-in.md). Specifically: CO2 path from the front-panel DERPIPE bulkhead through the GASHER check, WR1110 secondary regulator, first PP010822E PTC-to-NPT, into the cold core's front-face CO2 bore, landing on the adapter already made up on the vessel's bottom-plate CO2 elbow; water path from the rear-panel PP1208E 1/4" JG QC bulkhead (customer-facing — customer plugs the install-kit 1/4" LLDPE in at install time; the install-kit Waterdrop 15UC-UF filter mounts customer-side, inline upstream of this bulkhead, per [`/hardware/ledger/bom.md`](/hardware/ledger/bom.md) §3) → 1/4" LLDPE inside the cabinet → PP010822E 1/4" PTC × 1/4" NPT M → GAGIRA 316L SS 3/8" NPT F × 1/4" NPT F reducing coupling threading onto the ASSE 1022's 3/8" MPT inlet → Multiplex 19-0897 backflow preventer → PI4512F6S + PP061208W (3/8" MFL → 1/4" PTC) → 1/4" LLDPE → water-split PP0208E tee → V-K fill/shutoff → 1/4" LLDPE → PP450822E + MAACFLOW → reinforced-PVC stub over the SeaFlo's molded 3/8" suction barb → SeaFlo pump → a second stub off its molded discharge barb → the made-up discharge chain (MAACFLOW → GASHER check → PP450822E) lying level with that barb across the strip → 1/4" LLDPE → the cold core's front-face water inlet; flavor manifold built per [`/hardware/topology/fluid-topology.md`](/hardware/topology/fluid-topology.md) (V-C/D source-select → pumps → reservoir/nozzle valves); three risers (carbonated-water from the cold-core bottom-plate outlet stub, flavor A nozzle, flavor B nozzle) exiting the enclosure top, terminating at the three back-panel PP1208E umbilical bulkheads; Multiplex atmospheric vent terminating in the internal drip pan over the moisture sensor.

Not in scope: cold-core internal plumbing — the TAISHER vessel-port elbows, the silicone tube + sparge stone, the bottom-plate PP010822E PTC adapters on the water-outlet and CO2 ports — all installed inside the cold core during [`cold-core.md`](/hardware/assembly/cold-core.md). Refrigerant lines — already done in [`refrigerant-loop.md`](/hardware/assembly/refrigerant-loop.md). Above-counter umbilical — already done in [`faucet-and-umbilical.md`](/hardware/assembly/faucet-and-umbilical.md). Customer-side tap and CO2 connections — install-time, not assembly. Solenoid and pump wiring — owned by [`wiring.md`](/hardware/assembly/wiring.md); this doc lands every electrical actuator in its plumbed position but leaves the leads loose for the wiring step.

## Procedure

Order of work: CO2 path first (dry, simpler — four NPT joints inside the enclosure and two short tube hops, no soft hose), then the water path (more joints, mixed barb/NPT/PTC), then the flavor manifold (ten valves, two Y-dividers + six Tees, two pumps — the bulk of the bench labor). Three risers up to the back-panel umbilical bulkheads last, after the manifold is closed so the riser routing has the rest of the enclosure laid out around it.

### 1. CO2 path: front-panel DERPIPE → WR1110 → cold-core CO2 stub

Starting at the front panel: confirm the DERPIPE 5/16"-tube × 1/4" NPT push-to-connect bulkhead is installed per [`enclosure-mechanical.md`](/hardware/assembly/enclosure-mechanical.md) (NPT side facing inboard). Wrap PTFE tape on the inboard NPT threads (2–3 wraps, clockwise as viewed into the thread, leaving the first thread tape-free). Thread the GASHER 1/4" NPT SS check valve onto the DERPIPE inboard NPT — arrow on the check valve body pointing **away** from the bulkhead, i.e. inflow from the front panel, outflow toward the WR1110. Snug + 1 turn past hand-tight with the wrench backed up on the DERPIPE hex.

The check is the only thing that hangs off the bulkhead. **The WR1110 does not thread onto it.** The band this chain lives in — between the compressor's top and the pump row's underside — is the machine's whole width and only one fitting deep, so a rigid chain run straight in off the wall meets the source-select tray's underside before the regulator's 57 mm are up. The regulator lies ACROSS that band instead, on its own cradle, and a tube crosses to it. Its cradle is owned by [`enclosure-mechanical.md`](/hardware/assembly/enclosure-mechanical.md) — see Open items.

Wrap PTFE tape on the GASHER's outlet stub and thread a PP450822E 1/4" NPT F × 1/4" PTC adapter onto it. Wrap the WR1110's inlet (the side stamped "IN") and its outlet ("OUT") and thread a PP010822E 1/4" PTC × 1/4" NPT M adapter into each. All three joints snug + 1 turn, wrench backed up on the neighbouring hex. Every NPT-to-PTC transition on this path has now happened on the warm side of the cold core, consistent with the foam-shell boundary rule from [`/hardware/printed-parts/cold-core/foam-shell/README.md`](/hardware/printed-parts/cold-core/foam-shell/README.md) "Build decision".

Cut a short length of 1/4" OD LLDPE for the check-to-regulator hop and push it home in both collets. Tug-test both ends.

Cut a second length of 1/4" OD LLDPE from the bulk reel — long enough to route from the regulator's outlet adapter, back through the machine to the cold core's front face, and into the CO2 bore there (a Ø[6.5 mm](COTWO_TUBE_D) hole through the −Y wall at x=[+19.05 mm](COTWO_INLET_X) beside the water outlet, per [`cold-core.md`](/hardware/assembly/cold-core.md) step 5). The far end lands on the PP010822E collet already made up under the vessel's bottom plate — push it home blind and tug-test it hard; nothing reaches that joint again. Cut both tube ends square with the Mudder cutter — square cuts are load-bearing for PTC sealing, no chamfer needed but no angled cut. Push one end fully into the PP010822E PTC port (audible click + tube bottoms against the internal stop). Push the other end into the cold-core CO2 input. Tug-test both ends.

The CO2 path is now closed dry from the front panel to the cold core. Vessel-side everything (the TAISHER vessel-port elbow, its PP010822E adapter, the LTWFITTING barb adapter, the silicone tube, the sparge stone) is already inside the foam shell from [`cold-core.md`](/hardware/assembly/cold-core.md) step 5.

### 2. Water path: rear-panel PP1208E → filter → adapter chain → ASSE 1022 → 1/4" split → V-K → SeaFlo → cold-core water stub

The customer-facing rear-panel water inlet is a **1/4" JG QC bulkhead** (PP1208E — same SKU as the umbilical cluster). At install time the customer plugs the install-kit 1/4" LLDPE into the outboard PTC side — no factory work on that side beyond confirming the bulkhead is installed and the panel-side EPDM O-ring is seated. The factory work below closes everything inboard of the bulkhead. **From the ASSE 1022 outlet on, the path is all 1/4" LLDPE — it steps back up to 3/8" only at the SeaFlo's own two barbs.**

The PP010822E → GAGIRA → ASSE 1022 → PI4512F6S → PP061208W run and its vent stub are modeled as one piece in [`/hardware/reference/asse1022-assembly/`](/hardware/reference/asse1022-assembly/) — the stack-up, the three terminals (1/4" PTC in, 1/4" PTC out, vent), and which way the vent has to face.

Confirm the PP1208E is installed on the rear panel per [`enclosure-mechanical.md`](/hardware/assembly/enclosure-mechanical.md) with the 1/4" QC on both sides (push-to-connect, no tools required at install). The Waterdrop 15UC-UF filter is customer-side hardware — it ships in the install kit and mounts inline on the customer's 1/4" LLDPE upstream of this bulkhead ([`/hardware/ledger/bom.md`](/hardware/ledger/bom.md) §3) — so no factory work touches it.

Cut a length of 1/4" OD LLDPE from the bulk reel to route from the PP1208E's inboard PTC face to the ASSE 1022's inlet. This is segment **water-1**, modeled port to port in [`_lines.py`](/hardware/printed-parts/enclosure/enclosure-assembly/_lines.py) (developed length + bend count in the built scorecard): a short R3 pigtail off the bulkhead, forward and into the 1/4" PTC inlet on the ASSE chain's west end. Square-cut both ends with the Mudder cutter, push fully into both PTC ports, tug-test.

The ASSE 1022 inlet stack is factory-made up on the chain (modeled in the asse1022-assembly reference): the PP010822E's 1/4" NPT M side threads into the 1/4" NPT F side of the GAGIRA 316L SS reducing coupling (PTFE tape, snug + 1 turn past hand-tight); the GAGIRA's 3/8" NPT F side threads onto the ASSE 1022's 3/8" MPT inlet (tape on the MPT threads, snug + 1 turn). Multiplex flow arrow points away from the customer side. On the outlet, two John Guest fittings make up the flare-to-1/4" stack: the **PI4512F6S** 3/8" FFL × 3/8" PTC adapter draws up onto the ASSE 1022's 3/8" MFL outlet via its chrome swivel nut — flat-faced flare seal, no PTFE tape, quarter turn past hand-tight on the swivel only (never turn the body against the flare) — and the **PP061208W** 3/8"-stem × 1/4" PTC reducer pushes into its collet. The 3/8" run begins and ends inside those two fittings; the reducer's 1/4" PTC collet faces east down the aft strip, and the water leaves the backflow preventer at 1/4" OD.

Cut a length of 1/4" OD LLDPE for segment **water-2**: the outlet stack's collet east down the aft strip, then down the east pocket and south into the **water split** — a JG PP0208E 1/4" union tee (modeled in [`/hardware/reference/water-split/`](/hardware/reference/water-split/)) tube-hung on this run. Water-2 pushes into the split's **north-facing run collet**; the run carries straight through to the **south collet** for the flavor tap, and the **west-facing branch** turns V-K's share off it. Square-cut, push fully home, tug-test.

Mount **V-K** — the Beduan 12 V NC fill/shutoff solenoid — on its cradle in the aft strip's east void, arrow pointing north (+Y) (`BEDUAN_POS` / `BEDUAN_YAW` in [`_contents.py`](/hardware/printed-parts/enclosure/enclosure-assembly/_contents.py)); wire its coil per run DC-9 in [`ac-wiring-schedule.md`](/hardware/wiring/ac-wiring-schedule.md). Normally closed, so a leak alarm or a power loss stops all water reaching the carbonator. Cut segment **water-3** — the split's north run collet the short hop into V-K's inlet — square-cut, push fully home, tug-test.

Cut segment **water-4**: V-K's outlet a short bump north, then west across the aft strip and south into the SeaFlo suction, running under the ASSE overhang. At the pump end the 1/4" LLDPE steps up to the 3/8" suction barb through a PP450822E (1/4" PTC × 1/4" NPT **F**) + MAACFLOW (1/4" NPT M × 3/8" hose barb) pair, and then a stub of reinforced PVC over the barbs. **The pump's barbs are molded into its head casting** — there is no port thread, nothing unscrews, and SEAFLO's catalog states the 21F001 90° barbed fitting will not fit a standard barb head. So the MAACFLOW's barb and the pump's barb do not join to each other: cut ~40 mm of the 3/8" ID reinforced PVC, slip it over the pump's suction barb and over the MAACFLOW's barb, and close **two SAE #6 316 SS clamps** on it, one over each barb, screw heads turned where a driver reaches them. Square-cut the LLDPE and push fully into the PTC port; PTFE tape the two NPT joints, snug + 1 turn. This adapter and the pump's own barbs are the only 3/8" on the suction side.

The flavor manifold draws its tap water off the split's **south run**: 1/4" OD LLDPE → neoFit flow regulator → V-A ([`/hardware/topology/fluid-topology.md`](/hardware/topology/fluid-topology.md) tube segment 1). No 3/8" barb tee, no reducers — the split is already 1/4".

The Multiplex atmospheric vent (1/4" barb, on the body) leans aft and down — the chain is rolled about its own flow axis (`ASSE1022_ROLL` in [`_contents.py`](/hardware/printed-parts/enclosure/enclosure-assembly/_contents.py)), which lifts the body's underside off the pan below it without moving either tube port. It gets a short stub of Sealproof 1/4" ID × 3/8" OD clear PVC ([`/hardware/ledger/bom.md`](/hardware/ledger/bom.md) §3) pushed fully home on it. The drip falls off that tip into the internal drip pan + moisture sensor that [`enclosure-mechanical.md`](/hardware/assembly/enclosure-mechanical.md) put on rails over the cold core's foam-cap top. Cut the stub to the overhang the placement leaves for it — `VENT_STUB_REACH` in [`asse1022_assembly.py`](/hardware/reference/asse1022-assembly/asse1022_assembly.py) — and keep the fall's column clear when you dress the C14's cordage through the band under the body. **Vent must drip to atmosphere, not be plumbed into a drain** — the vent is the mechanical telltale per [`/hardware/future.md`](/hardware/future.md) "Backflow vent monitoring". Two LOKMAN clamps on this run (vent barb + termination, if a termination barb is fitted — bare drip is also acceptable; the receiving pan does not need a tight fit).

Make up the **discharge chain** on the bench as one piece — MAACFLOW → GASHER → PP450822E, modeled in [`/hardware/reference/seaflo-discharge-chain/`](/hardware/reference/seaflo-discharge-chain/), 83.4 mm made up. The MAACFLOW's 1/4" NPT M threads into the GASHER's female socket (PTFE tape, snug + 1) with the check's arrow pointing **away** from the pump; the GASHER's male stub threads into the PP450822E's female (PTFE tape, snug + 1). Lay that chain **horizontally** across the strip ahead of the cold core's front face, level with the pump's discharge barb and 20 mm west of it — MAACFLOW barb east, facing the pump; PP450822E collet west, facing the core (`disch_chain_pos` in [`_contents.py`](/hardware/printed-parts/enclosure/enclosure-assembly/_contents.py)); its bracket is an open item. Then cut ~60 mm of the 3/8" ID reinforced PVC for segment **water-6**: slip it over the pump's discharge barb, curve it south over the foam cap's front edge and **west along the strip at the barb's own height**, and slip it over the chain's MAACFLOW barb. **Two SAE #6 316 SS clamps**, one over each barb. The hose takes a 15.9 mm bend radius — do not force it tighter than that turn, and do not let the curve rest on the cap edge.

Cut segment **water-5** from the 1/4" OD LLDPE reel. The chain's PP450822E collet looks **west** off the far end of the laid-down chain, well above the port it feeds: west off the collet clear of the chain's own end, **down** the one fall this water takes, then back east onto the inlet's own line to push north into the cold-core water-inlet stub on the core's front face. Its height only ever descends — the chain hands the water over at the pump's discharge level and the inlet is below that, so nothing on this line climbs. It also stays out of the riser's way by staying high and west: the riser's climb owns the strip's floor east of the meter, and this run never enters it. Square-cut both ends, push fully into the PTC ports at both ends, tug-test.

The water path is now closed dry from the rear panel to the cold core. Vessel-side everything (the second cold-side PP010822E, the TAISHER vessel-port elbow, the top-plate water-inlet NPT joint) is inside the foam shell from [`cold-core.md`](/hardware/assembly/cold-core.md) step 5.

### 3. Flavor manifold: V-A through V-J + Y-A through Y-H + pumps

Build to [`/hardware/topology/fluid-topology.md`](/hardware/topology/fluid-topology.md) segment-for-segment. The truth table there names every valve, every Y-junction, and every tube segment by number; this step describes the build cadence around that truth table, not its content.

Pre-build sequencing — bench sub-assembly vs. in-place build is **TBD; see Open items**. The remainder of this step is written for a bench sub-assembly that drops in afterward, with the understanding that an in-place build of the same parts list would carry the same ordering arguments.

On the bench, lay out all ten Beduan solenoid valves with their inlet/outlet legends visible. Lay out the two PP2308E Y-dividers and six PP0208E Tees between them in the topology positions. Pre-cut every tube segment from the [`/hardware/topology/fluid-topology.md`](/hardware/topology/fluid-topology.md) "Tube Segments" tables to length from the 1/4" OD LLDPE reel, square-cut both ends with the Mudder cutter, tag each segment with masking tape labelled by segment number from the topology doc.

Build outward from the two channel-A and channel-B pump rosettes:

- For each channel, push the appropriate segment into the pump's inlet PTC (segment 11 for channel A, segment 21 for channel B in the topology doc), then continue building back through Y-C → V-C + V-E for channel A (mirror Y-F → V-D + V-H for channel B), and forward from each pump's outlet through Y-D + Y-E + V-F + V-G for channel A (mirror Y-G + Y-H + V-I + V-J for channel B).
- The shared upstream (tap-water V-A + hopper V-B + their merge through Y-A and Y-B) builds in last. V-A's inlet comes from the **water split**'s south run — the JG PP0208E 1/4" tee on the ASSE 1022's 1/4" outlet — → 1/4" OD LLDPE → flow regulator → V-A inlet per [`/hardware/topology/fluid-topology.md`](/hardware/topology/fluid-topology.md) tube segment 1. V-B's inlet is the funnel bottom of the user-facing top-face hopper.

The two Kamoer KPHM400 peristaltic pumps mount in the enclosure (mount mechanism is an open item). Route 1/4" OD LLDPE through each pump's BPT barbs and around the rotor — LLDPE fits the barbs directly, zip-tied tight. Crimp a female faston receptacle onto each DC-5 harness conductor (+ and GND); these land on the pump-motor spade tabs, joining the harness at [`wiring.md`](/hardware/assembly/wiring.md).

The pump enclosure-mount mechanism is **TBD; see Open items** — top-of-Zone-C access beneath the funnel per [`/hardware/printed-parts/zone-c/README.md`](/hardware/printed-parts/zone-c/README.md), mechanism undrawn. The exact mechanism (push-fit + detent, screwed mounting plate, latch + cam) is not specified in the parts already committed.

Land the assembled flavor manifold into its enclosure position above the compressor and below the cold-core +Y face per the enclosure layout in [`/hardware/future.md`](/hardware/future.md) "Enclosure layout". Route tube segments through the pre-printed tube channels in the enclosure shell; zip-tie at every bundle pinch-point and at every long unsupported span.

Three manifold outlets exit upward to the back-panel umbilical bulkheads in step 4:

- **Carbonated water riser** comes from the cold-core's bottom-plate carbonated-water outlet stub (already plumbed out of the cold core's water-outlet penetration per [`cold-core.md`](/hardware/assembly/cold-core.md) step 5; bottom-plate Port 3 → PP010822E PTC adapter → 1/4" OD blue LLDPE up out of the cold core). Build does not pick up this line in the flavor manifold step — it goes straight from the cold core to the back-panel blue-ringed PP1208E in step 4 below.
- **Flavor A riser** comes from V-G outlet (the "pump to nozzle A" valve, segment 18 in the topology doc terminating at "Nozzle A").
- **Flavor B riser** comes from V-J outlet (segment 28 terminating at "Nozzle B").

### 4. Risers up to back-panel umbilical bulkheads

Three risers exit the enclosure's top face and land on the three back-panel PP1208E umbilical bulkheads per [`/hardware/printed-parts/enclosure/back-panel/README.md`](/hardware/printed-parts/enclosure/back-panel/README.md). The bulkheads are arranged in a triangular cluster on the back panel, with the **blue-ringed bulkhead at the top vertex of the triangle** for the carbonated-water tube.

- **Carbonated-water riser:** 1/4" OD blue LLDPE from the cold-core bottom-plate outlet to the blue-ringed PP1208E, in two lengths with the **DIGITEN flow meter** made up between them. The line cannot leave the cold core's face head-on — refrig-2 climbs the same station on its way to the evaporator inlet and stands ~9.5 mm off the collet — so the first length turns **west inside that gap**, climbs the core's front face west of the bag fall and of the shared slot, and pushes east into the meter's inlet collet. The meter lies in the strip ahead of the core's front face **at the water inlet's own height** — the one band of that strip nothing stands in — so the riser climbs to it and stops instead of running the whole face, and water-5, which crosses high and west, never enters its lane. The second length leaves the meter's outlet, **climbs into the lane under the SeaFlo** (there is no lane over it: the pump's crown and the enclosure ceiling leave a 1/4" tube nothing to pass through), runs aft along that lane the length of the machine, and climbs to the bulkhead in the **column east of the ASSE 1022** — the tap-water line to the split crosses the blue bulkhead's own southward approach, so the riser comes up beside it and turns in at the last. Square-cut every end, push fully into all four PTC ports, tug-test each. The CARGEN nitrile-rubber pipe insulation that wraps this line ([`/hardware/ledger/bom.md`](/hardware/ledger/bom.md) §9, 1/4" ID × 3/8" wall) goes on as one piece per length, cut to that run and slid on from the open end before either collet goes down (Open #7 below) — not in the 1-ft segments the above-counter umbilical uses. Insulation continuity from the cold-core foam-shell exit to the inboard face of the blue-ringed bulkhead is the target — the cold-core foam exit is where the thermal mass of the cold core ends and the chilled water enters its multi-meter run to the faucet.
- **Flavor A riser:** 1/4" OD black LLDPE from V-G outlet up to the second (left vertex, by convention) PP1208E. No insulation — flavor lines run warm-in, warm-out at low duty cycle.
- **Flavor B riser:** 1/4" OD black LLDPE from V-J outlet up to the third (right vertex) PP1208E. No insulation.

Black-into-either-black is unambiguous downstream of the manifold (per [`/hardware/printed-parts/enclosure/back-panel/README.md`](/hardware/printed-parts/enclosure/back-panel/README.md) "Umbilical port — tube identification"): both flavor tubes route through the same panel-side bundle and the user does not need to distinguish them at the panel.

### 5. Witness and tidy

Walk every joint built in this procedure. Tug-test every PTC connection. Confirm every LOKMAN clamp on the SeaFlo barb adapters is past finger-tight and the tube has pillowed. Confirm every NPT joint is past hand-tight and has PTFE tape showing at no more than half of the thread engagement (excess tape past half-engagement is a stress concentration). Confirm the Multiplex atmospheric vent stub leans aft and down as the chain's roll leaves it, and that the column straight down from its tip reaches the drip pan with no obstruction. Confirm both pumps sit in their enclosure position with leads routed clear for spade termination at [`wiring.md`](/hardware/assembly/wiring.md).

Zip-tie any tube run with a span over ~6" between fixturing points. The enclosure's printed tube channels carry most of the line, but zip-ties at the bend transitions and at the pump-mount boundary stabilize the manifold during transport.

The internal plumbing is now ready for [`wiring.md`](/hardware/assembly/wiring.md) (the wiring step lands every solenoid lead, every pump lead, every sensor lead in its electrical termination on the electronics shelf, but the plumbing itself does not change after this step).

## Open items

Procedure-level gaps that need answers before unit 1 ships:

1. **Pump enclosure-mount mechanism.** Tool-free swap from the top of Zone C, beneath the funnel, per [`/hardware/printed-parts/zone-c/README.md`](/hardware/printed-parts/zone-c/README.md) is undrawn. Candidate mechanisms: (a) push-fit + detent against a printed seat in the pump bay — relies on detent geometry alone; (b) screwed mounting plate in the bay with M3 SHCS + heat-set inserts — defeats the tool-free swap; (c) cam-latch or lever that engages a printed feature on the mount — requires a TBD off-the-shelf latch. Pick once the mount geometry is drawn.
2. **Flavor manifold: bench sub-assembly vs. in-place build.** Procedure as written assumes bench sub-assembly; pre-cut every segment per [`/hardware/topology/fluid-topology.md`](/hardware/topology/fluid-topology.md), build outward from the pump rosettes on the bench, then drop the assembled manifold into the enclosure. Plausibility: the manifold's footprint is ~150 × 200 mm above the compressor; the enclosure has access from the top face (open during this step, before the funnel installs) and the front face. An in-place build of the same parts list is geometrically defensible — every joint is reachable from the front or top access faces — and avoids the lift-and-place step that a bench manifold requires (the manifold has 10 valves across 28 tube segments; the lift puts every termination at risk of an unseat). Decision is downstream of the first unit's bench cycle time vs in-place cycle time.
3. ~~**Tap-water tap-point into the flavor manifold (V-A inlet).**~~ **CLOSED.** The flavor tap comes off the **water split** — the JG PP0208E 1/4" union tee on the ASSE 1022's 1/4" outlet (`reference/water-split/`): its south run collet → 1/4" OD LLDPE → neoFit flow regulator → V-A ([`/hardware/topology/fluid-topology.md`](/hardware/topology/fluid-topology.md) tube segment 1). The ASSE leaves at 1/4", so the split and the tap branch are both plain 1/4" PTC — no 3/8"-to-1/4" branch chain. See [`/hardware/ledger/bom.md`](/hardware/ledger/bom.md) §3.
4. ~~**PP0308E elbow count in the warm-side line runs.**~~ **CLOSED.** [`/hardware/ledger/bom.md`](/hardware/ledger/bom.md) §4 now enumerates [10](https://www.freshwatersystems.com/products/john-guest-union-elbow-black-polypropylene-1-4) per build against the tray and pump geometry: 6 on the elbowed valve-manifold boundary ports (source-select 4, bag-circuit 2) + 4 on the two Kamoer pump outlet pairs.
5. ~~**Clamp quantity per build.**~~ **CLOSED.** Five: **four SAE #6 316 SS** on the two pump-port stubs (two per stub — one over the pump's molded barb, one over the adapter's) and **one LOKMAN 10–16 mm** on the vent stub. [`enclosure-mechanical.md`](/hardware/assembly/enclosure-mechanical.md) owns the part. Flagged here because this procedure is downstream of that mechanical step but depends on its existence; coordinate with the mechanical doc.
7. ~~**Cabinet-internal carbonated-water insulation segment count.**~~ **CLOSED — cut-to-length pieces, not 1-ft segments.** The cold-core-exit → blue-ringed-bulkhead run has a fixed length per build, is closed entirely in-shop and is never field-trimmed, so the 1-ft granularity that serves the above-counter umbilical buys nothing here and would leave up to 12" of bare tube on the coldest part of the line.
