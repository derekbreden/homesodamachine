# Internal Plumbing

The production procedure for closing every internal fluid path inside the assembled enclosure — CO2 line from the front-panel inlet to the carbonator sparge port, filtered tap-water from the right-side-panel inlet through the backflow preventer to the carbonator water-inlet port, the full two-channel flavor manifold (source-select valves, peristaltic pumps, reservoir/nozzle outputs), and the three-tube riser that lands on the back-panel umbilical bulkheads. Comes in after the mechanical chassis is closed up by [`enclosure-mechanical.md`](/hardware/assembly/enclosure-mechanical.md) and feeds [`wiring.md`](/hardware/assembly/wiring.md) and the first powered run at [`firmware-and-commissioning.md`](/hardware/assembly/firmware-and-commissioning.md).

Design intent and the path-by-path enumeration live in [`/hardware/future.md`](/hardware/future.md) "Carbonation subsystem" + "Flavor subsystem" + "Backflow vent monitoring". The valve / Y-junction / tube-segment truth table this manifold builds to is [`/hardware/topology/fluid-topology.md`](/hardware/topology/fluid-topology.md) — the manifold matches that file segment-for-segment. Cold-core internal plumbing (everything inside the foam shell) is already complete per [`cold-core.md`](/hardware/assembly/cold-core.md). Refrigerant lines are already brazed and charged per [`refrigerant-loop.md`](/hardware/assembly/refrigerant-loop.md). Above-counter umbilical (faucet body, 3-tube bundle, foam insulation, sleeve) is already built per [`faucet-and-umbilical.md`](/hardware/assembly/faucet-and-umbilical.md).

## Scope

In: mechanical chassis assembled per [`enclosure-mechanical.md`](/hardware/assembly/enclosure-mechanical.md) — cold core seated against the back wall with coil stubs brazed and its seven penetrations (1/4" OD tubing) presented warm-side, back-panel bulkheads (3× PP1208E umbilical + the water-inlet PP1208E 1/4" JG QC) installed plus front-panel CO2 DERPIPE PTC installed, the electronics shelf seated on the foam-cap top but unpowered, internal drip pan with moisture sensor mounted under the future backflow-preventer vent location. Plus the parts listed under "Inputs per appliance" below.

Out: every internal fluid path closed and primed-ready — fully connected, fully torqued, fully clamped, leak-witnessed dry where possible, but **not yet primed with water or CO2**. Fluid charging happens at [`acceptance-and-burn-in.md`](/hardware/assembly/acceptance-and-burn-in.md). Specifically: CO2 path from the front-panel DERPIPE bulkhead through the GASHER check, WR1110 secondary regulator, first PP010822E PTC-to-NPT, into the cold core's +Z penetration, landing on the cold core's internal CO2 sparge stub at the foam-shell boundary; water path from the right-side-panel PP1208E 1/4" JG QC bulkhead (customer-facing — customer plugs the install-kit 1/4" LLDPE in at install time; the install-kit Waterdrop 15UC-UF filter mounts customer-side, inline upstream of this bulkhead, per [`/hardware/ledger/bom.md`](/hardware/ledger/bom.md) §3) → 1/4" LLDPE inside the cabinet → PP010822E 1/4" PTC × 1/4" NPT M → GAGIRA 316L SS 3/8" NPT F × 1/4" NPT F reducing coupling threading onto the ASSE 1022's 3/8" MPT inlet → Multiplex 19-0897 backflow preventer → flare38-14ptc (3/8" MFL → 1/4" PTC) → 1/4" LLDPE → water-split PP0208E tee → V-K fill/shutoff → 1/4" LLDPE → PP010822E + MAACFLOW at the SeaFlo 3/8" suction barb → SeaFlo pump → discharge MAACFLOW → GASHER check → PP010822E → cold core's +Z slot landing on the cold core's internal water-inlet stub; flavor manifold built per [`/hardware/topology/fluid-topology.md`](/hardware/topology/fluid-topology.md) (V-C/D source-select → pumps → reservoir/nozzle valves); three risers (carbonated-water from the cold-core bottom-plate outlet stub, flavor A nozzle, flavor B nozzle) exiting the enclosure top, terminating at the three back-panel PP1208E umbilical bulkheads; Multiplex atmospheric vent terminating in the internal drip pan over the moisture sensor.

Not in scope: cold-core internal plumbing — the in-cavity PP0308E elbow, the TAISHER vessel-port elbows, the silicone tube + sparge stone, the bottom-plate PP010822E PTC adapter on the water-outlet port — all installed inside the cold core during [`cold-core.md`](/hardware/assembly/cold-core.md). Refrigerant lines — already done in [`refrigerant-loop.md`](/hardware/assembly/refrigerant-loop.md). Above-counter umbilical — already done in [`faucet-and-umbilical.md`](/hardware/assembly/faucet-and-umbilical.md). Customer-side tap and CO2 connections — install-time, not assembly. Solenoid and pump wiring — owned by [`wiring.md`](/hardware/assembly/wiring.md); this doc lands every electrical actuator in its plumbed position but leaves the leads loose for the wiring step.

## Procedure

Order of work: CO2 path first (dry, simpler — only two NPT joints inside the enclosure, no soft hose), then the water path (more joints, mixed barb/NPT/PTC), then the flavor manifold (ten valves, two Y-dividers + six Tees, two pumps — the bulk of the bench labor). Three risers up to the back-panel umbilical bulkheads last, after the manifold is closed so the riser routing has the rest of the enclosure laid out around it.

### 1. CO2 path: front-panel DERPIPE → WR1110 → cold-core CO2 stub

Starting at the front panel: confirm the DERPIPE 5/16"-tube × 1/4" NPT push-to-connect bulkhead is installed per [`enclosure-mechanical.md`](/hardware/assembly/enclosure-mechanical.md) (NPT side facing inboard). Wrap PTFE tape on the inboard NPT threads (2–3 wraps, clockwise as viewed into the thread, leaving the first thread tape-free). Thread the GASHER 1/4" NPT SS check valve onto the DERPIPE inboard NPT — arrow on the check valve body pointing **away** from the bulkhead, i.e. inflow from the front panel, outflow toward the WR1110. Snug + 1 turn past hand-tight with the wrench backed up on the DERPIPE hex.

Wrap PTFE tape on the WR1110 inlet (the side stamped "IN"). Thread it onto the GASHER outlet. Same snug + 1 turn. Both fittings now sit in series on a short rigid NPT stub off the front panel — the WR1110 body hangs off the GASHER, supported by the threads only at this stage. Final mechanical fixturing of the WR1110 body (the regulator weighs enough to stress the NPT stub under transport vibration) is owned by [`enclosure-mechanical.md`](/hardware/assembly/enclosure-mechanical.md) via a printed bracket against the front-panel inner face — see Open items.

Wrap PTFE tape on the WR1110 outlet ("OUT" side). Thread the first PP010822E 1/4" PTC × 1/4" NPT M adapter onto it. The NPT-to-PTC transition has now happened on the warm side of the cold core, consistent with the foam-shell boundary rule from [`/hardware/printed-parts/cold-core/foam-shell/README.md`](/hardware/printed-parts/cold-core/foam-shell/README.md) "Build decision".

Cut a length of 1/4" OD LLDPE from the bulk reel — long enough to route from the PP010822E outlet, up and over any routing obstacles, and into the cold-core CO2 input on the foam-cap stack (the CO2 inlet enters from above through the cap boss + cap-lid Ø[6.5 mm](COTWO_TUBE_D) hole at x=0, y=[-72.75 mm](COTWO_INLET_Y) per [`cold-core.md`](/hardware/assembly/cold-core.md) step 4). Cut both tube ends square with the Mudder cutter — square cuts are load-bearing for PTC sealing, no chamfer needed but no angled cut. Push one end fully into the PP010822E PTC port (audible click + tube bottoms against the internal stop). Push the other end into the cold-core CO2 input. Tug-test both ends.

The CO2 path is now closed dry from the front panel to the cold core. Vessel-side everything (the in-cavity PP0308E elbow, the second cold-side PP010822E, the TAISHER vessel-port elbow, the LTWFITTING barb adapter, the silicone tube, the sparge stone) is already inside the foam shell from [`cold-core.md`](/hardware/assembly/cold-core.md) step 4.

### 2. Water path: rear-panel PP1208E → filter → adapter chain → ASSE 1022 → 1/4" split → V-K → SeaFlo → cold-core water stub

The customer-facing rear-panel water inlet is a **1/4" JG QC bulkhead** (PP1208E — same SKU as the umbilical cluster). At install time the customer plugs the install-kit 1/4" LLDPE into the outboard PTC side — no factory work on that side beyond confirming the bulkhead is installed and the panel-side EPDM O-ring is seated. The factory work below closes everything inboard of the bulkhead. **From the ASSE 1022 outlet on, the path is all 1/4" LLDPE — it steps back up to 3/8" only at the SeaFlo's own two barbs.**

The PP010822E → GAGIRA → ASSE 1022 → flare38-14ptc run and its vent stub are modeled as one piece in [`/hardware/reference/asse1022-assembly/`](/hardware/reference/asse1022-assembly/) — the stack-up, the three terminals (1/4" PTC in, 1/4" PTC out, vent), and which way the vent has to face.

Confirm the PP1208E is installed on the rear panel per [`enclosure-mechanical.md`](/hardware/assembly/enclosure-mechanical.md) with the 1/4" QC on both sides (push-to-connect, no tools required at install). The Waterdrop 15UC-UF filter is customer-side hardware — it ships in the install kit and mounts inline on the customer's 1/4" LLDPE upstream of this bulkhead ([`/hardware/ledger/bom.md`](/hardware/ledger/bom.md) §3) — so no factory work touches it.

Cut a length of 1/4" OD LLDPE from the bulk reel to route from the PP1208E's inboard PTC face to the ASSE 1022's inlet. This is segment **water-1**, modeled port to port in [`_lines.py`](/hardware/printed-parts/enclosure/enclosure-assembly/_lines.py) (developed length + bend count in the built scorecard): a short R3 pigtail off the bulkhead, forward and into the 1/4" PTC inlet on the ASSE chain's west end. Square-cut both ends with the Mudder cutter, push fully into both PTC ports, tug-test.

The ASSE 1022 inlet stack is factory-made up on the chain (modeled in the asse1022-assembly reference): the PP010822E's 1/4" NPT M side threads into the 1/4" NPT F side of the GAGIRA 316L SS reducing coupling (PTFE tape, snug + 1 turn past hand-tight); the GAGIRA's 3/8" NPT F side threads onto the ASSE 1022's 3/8" MPT inlet (tape on the MPT threads, snug + 1 turn). Multiplex flow arrow points away from the customer side. On the outlet, the **flare38-14ptc** 3/8" female-flare × 1/4" PTC adapter draws up onto the ASSE 1022's 3/8" MFL outlet via its chrome swivel nut — flat-faced flare seal, no PTFE tape. Its 1/4" PTC collet faces east down the aft strip, and the water leaves the backflow preventer at 1/4" OD.

Cut a length of 1/4" OD LLDPE for segment **water-2**: the flare38-14ptc's collet east down the aft strip, then south and down into the **water split** — a JG PP0208E 1/4" union tee (modeled in [`/hardware/reference/water-split/`](/hardware/reference/water-split/)) tube-hung on this run. Water-2 pushes into the split's west-facing branch; the split's two run collets feed V-K (north) and the flavor tap (south). Square-cut, push fully home, tug-test.

Mount **V-K** — the Beduan 12 V NC fill/shutoff solenoid — on its cradle in the aft strip's east void, arrow pointing north (+Y) (`BEDUAN_POS` / `BEDUAN_YAW` in [`_contents.py`](/hardware/printed-parts/enclosure/enclosure-assembly/_contents.py)); wire its coil per run DC-9 in [`ac-wiring-schedule.md`](/hardware/wiring/ac-wiring-schedule.md). Normally closed, so a leak alarm or a power loss stops all water reaching the carbonator. Cut segment **water-3** — the split's north run collet the short hop into V-K's inlet — square-cut, push fully home, tug-test.

Cut segment **water-4**: V-K's outlet a short bump north, then west across the aft strip and south into the SeaFlo suction, running under the ASSE overhang. At the pump end the 1/4" LLDPE steps up to the 3/8" suction barb through a PP010822E (1/4" PTC × 1/4" NPT M) + MAACFLOW (1/4" NPT M × 3/8" hose barb) pair — the same barb adapter the discharge side uses, LOKMAN worm-gear clamped. Square-cut the LLDPE and push fully into the PTC port; PTFE tape the two NPT joints, snug + 1 turn. This adapter and the pump's own barbs are the only 3/8" on the suction side.

The flavor manifold draws its tap water off the split's **south run**: 1/4" OD LLDPE → neoFit flow regulator → V-A ([`/hardware/topology/fluid-topology.md`](/hardware/topology/fluid-topology.md) tube segment 1). No 3/8" barb tee, no reducers — the split is already 1/4".

The Multiplex atmospheric vent (1/4" barb, on the body) faces the front of the cabinet and gets a short stub of Sealproof 1/4" ID × 3/8" OD clear PVC ([`/hardware/ledger/bom.md`](/hardware/ledger/bom.md) §3) pushed fully home on it. The drip falls off that tip onto the internal drip pan + moisture sensor that [`enclosure-mechanical.md`](/hardware/assembly/enclosure-mechanical.md) seated on the cold core's foam-cap top, 51.6 mm below and 4 mm behind the electronics shelf's rear edge. Cut the stub to the overhang the placement leaves for it — `VENT_STUB_REACH` in [`asse1022_assembly.py`](/hardware/reference/asse1022-assembly/asse1022_assembly.py) — and keep the fall's column clear when you dress the C14's cordage through the band under the body. **Vent must drip to atmosphere, not be plumbed into a drain** — the vent is the mechanical telltale per [`/hardware/future.md`](/hardware/future.md) "Backflow vent monitoring". Two LOKMAN clamps on this run (vent barb + termination, if a termination barb is fitted — bare drip is also acceptable; the receiving pan does not need a tight fit).

Thread the MAACFLOW 1/4" NPT M × 3/8" hose-barb adapter into the SeaFlo's 3/8" outlet — PTFE tape on the NPT side facing the pump. (The SeaFlo outlet uses a barbed-to-NPT fitment; verify the adapter direction against the pump's outlet port type at the bench.) Thread the GASHER 1/4" NPT SS check valve (the second of the pack) onto the MAACFLOW's 1/4" NPT face, PTFE tape, arrow pointing away from the pump. Thread the first PP010822E 1/4" PTC × 1/4" NPT M onto the GASHER outlet, PTFE tape, same convention as the CO2 path.

Cut a length of 1/4" OD LLDPE from the bulk reel — route from the PP010822E outlet up over the SeaFlo, across to the +Z face of the cold core, and into the cold-core water-inlet stub at the +Z slot. Square-cut both ends, push fully into PTC ports at both ends, tug-test.

The water path is now closed dry from the rear panel to the cold core. Vessel-side everything (the second cold-side PP010822E, the TAISHER vessel-port elbow, the top-plate water-inlet NPT joint) is inside the foam shell from [`cold-core.md`](/hardware/assembly/cold-core.md) step 4.

### 3. Flavor manifold: V-A through V-J + Y-A through Y-H + pumps

Build to [`/hardware/topology/fluid-topology.md`](/hardware/topology/fluid-topology.md) segment-for-segment. The truth table there names every valve, every Y-junction, and every tube segment by number; this step describes the build cadence around that truth table, not its content.

Pre-build sequencing — bench sub-assembly vs. in-place build is **TBD; see Open items**. The remainder of this step is written for a bench sub-assembly that drops in afterward, with the understanding that an in-place build of the same parts list would carry the same ordering arguments.

On the bench, lay out all ten Beduan solenoid valves with their inlet/outlet legends visible. Lay out the two PP2308E Y-dividers and six PP0208E Tees between them in the topology positions. Pre-cut every tube segment from the [`/hardware/topology/fluid-topology.md`](/hardware/topology/fluid-topology.md) "Tube Segments" tables to length from the 1/4" OD LLDPE reel, square-cut both ends with the Mudder cutter, tag each segment with masking tape labelled by segment number from the topology doc.

Build outward from the two channel-A and channel-B pump rosettes:

- For each channel, push the appropriate segment into the pump's inlet PTC (segment 11 for channel A, segment 21 for channel B in the topology doc), then continue building back through Y-C → V-C + V-E for channel A (mirror Y-F → V-D + V-H for channel B), and forward from each pump's outlet through Y-D + Y-E + V-F + V-G for channel A (mirror Y-G + Y-H + V-I + V-J for channel B).
- The shared upstream (tap-water V-A + hopper V-B + their merge through Y-A and Y-B) builds in last. V-A's inlet comes from the **water split**'s south run — the JG PP0208E 1/4" tee on the ASSE 1022's 1/4" outlet — → 1/4" OD LLDPE → flow regulator → V-A inlet per [`/hardware/topology/fluid-topology.md`](/hardware/topology/fluid-topology.md) tube segment 1. V-B's inlet is the funnel bottom of the user-facing top-face hopper.

The two Kamoer KPHM400 peristaltic pumps mount in the enclosure (mount mechanism is an open item). Route 1/4" OD LLDPE through each pump's BPT barbs and around the rotor — LLDPE fits the barbs directly, zip-tied tight. Crimp a female faston receptacle onto each DC-5 harness conductor (+ and GND); these land on the pump-motor spade tabs, joining the harness at [`wiring.md`](/hardware/assembly/wiring.md).

The pump enclosure-mount mechanism is **TBD; see Open items** — top-of-Zone-C access beneath the funnel per [`/hardware/printed-parts/zone-c/README.md`](/hardware/printed-parts/zone-c/README.md), mechanism undrawn. The exact mechanism (push-fit + detent, screwed mounting plate, latch + cam) is not specified in the parts already committed.

Land the assembled flavor manifold into its enclosure position above the compressor and below the cold-core +Y face per the enclosure layout in [`/hardware/future.md`](/hardware/future.md) "Enclosure layout". Route tube segments through the pre-printed tube channels in the enclosure shell; zip-tie at every bundle pinch-point and at every long unsupported span.

Three manifold outlets exit upward to the back-panel umbilical bulkheads in step 4:

- **Carbonated water riser** comes from the cold-core's bottom-plate carbonated-water outlet stub (already plumbed out of the cold core's water-outlet penetration per [`cold-core.md`](/hardware/assembly/cold-core.md) step 4; bottom-plate Port 3 → PP010822E PTC adapter → 1/4" OD blue LLDPE up out of the cold core). Build does not pick up this line in the flavor manifold step — it goes straight from the cold core to the back-panel blue-ringed PP1208E in step 4 below.
- **Flavor A riser** comes from V-G outlet (the "pump to nozzle A" valve, segment 18 in the topology doc terminating at "Nozzle A").
- **Flavor B riser** comes from V-J outlet (segment 28 terminating at "Nozzle B").

### 4. Risers up to back-panel umbilical bulkheads

Three risers exit the enclosure's top face and land on the three back-panel PP1208E umbilical bulkheads per [`/hardware/printed-parts/enclosure/back-panel/README.md`](/hardware/printed-parts/enclosure/back-panel/README.md). The bulkheads are arranged in a triangular cluster on the back panel, with the **blue-ringed bulkhead at the top vertex of the triangle** for the carbonated-water tube.

- **Carbonated-water riser:** 1/4" OD blue LLDPE from the cold-core bottom-plate outlet → up through the routing channel on the +Z side of the cold core → into the blue-ringed PP1208E from the inboard PTC face. Square-cut both ends, push fully into both PTC ports, tug-test. The CARGEN nitrile-rubber pipe insulation that wraps this line ([`/hardware/ledger/bom.md`](/hardware/ledger/bom.md) §9, 1/4" ID × 3/8" wall) is installed in segments along this run inside the cabinet, butted along the length — same install-by-segment ergonomics as the above-counter umbilical per [`/hardware/printed-parts/enclosure/back-panel/README.md`](/hardware/printed-parts/enclosure/back-panel/README.md) "Umbilical bundle construction", scaled to the cabinet-internal portion of the run. Insulation continuity from the cold-core foam-shell exit to the inboard face of the blue-ringed bulkhead is the target — the cold-core foam exit is where the thermal mass of the cold core ends and the chilled water enters its multi-meter run to the faucet.
- **Flavor A riser:** 1/4" OD black LLDPE from V-G outlet up to the second (left vertex, by convention) PP1208E. No insulation — flavor lines run warm-in, warm-out at low duty cycle.
- **Flavor B riser:** 1/4" OD black LLDPE from V-J outlet up to the third (right vertex) PP1208E. No insulation.

Black-into-either-black is unambiguous downstream of the manifold (per [`/hardware/printed-parts/enclosure/back-panel/README.md`](/hardware/printed-parts/enclosure/back-panel/README.md) "Umbilical port — tube identification"): both flavor tubes route through the same panel-side bundle and the user does not need to distinguish them at the panel.

### 5. Witness and tidy

Walk every joint built in this procedure. Tug-test every PTC connection. Confirm every LOKMAN clamp on the SeaFlo barb adapters is past finger-tight and the tube has pillowed. Confirm every NPT joint is past hand-tight and has PTFE tape showing at no more than half of the thread engagement (excess tape past half-engagement is a stress concentration). Confirm the Multiplex atmospheric vent stub faces the front of the cabinet and the column straight down from its tip reaches the drip pan with no obstruction. Confirm both pumps sit in their enclosure position with leads routed clear for spade termination at [`wiring.md`](/hardware/assembly/wiring.md).

Zip-tie any tube run with a span over ~6" between fixturing points. The enclosure's printed tube channels carry most of the line, but zip-ties at the bend transitions and at the pump-mount boundary stabilize the manifold during transport.

The internal plumbing is now ready for [`wiring.md`](/hardware/assembly/wiring.md) (the wiring step lands every solenoid lead, every pump lead, every sensor lead in its electrical termination on the electronics shelf, but the plumbing itself does not change after this step).

## Open items

Procedure-level gaps that need answers before unit 1 ships:

1. **Pump enclosure-mount mechanism.** Tool-free swap from the top of Zone C, beneath the funnel, per [`/hardware/printed-parts/zone-c/README.md`](/hardware/printed-parts/zone-c/README.md) is undrawn. Candidate mechanisms: (a) push-fit + detent against a printed seat in the pump bay — relies on detent geometry alone; (b) screwed mounting plate in the bay with M3 SHCS + heat-set inserts — defeats the tool-free swap; (c) cam-latch or lever that engages a printed feature on the mount — requires a TBD off-the-shelf latch. Pick once the mount geometry is drawn.
2. **Flavor manifold: bench sub-assembly vs. in-place build.** Procedure as written assumes bench sub-assembly; pre-cut every segment per [`/hardware/topology/fluid-topology.md`](/hardware/topology/fluid-topology.md), build outward from the pump rosettes on the bench, then drop the assembled manifold into the enclosure. Plausibility: the manifold's footprint is ~150 × 200 mm above the compressor; the enclosure has access from the top face (open during this step, before the funnel installs) and the front face. An in-place build of the same parts list is geometrically defensible — every joint is reachable from the front or top access faces — and avoids the lift-and-place step that a bench manifold requires (the manifold has 10 valves across 28 tube segments; the lift puts every termination at risk of an unseat). Decision is downstream of the first unit's bench cycle time vs in-place cycle time.
3. ~~**Tap-water tap-point into the flavor manifold (V-A inlet).**~~ **CLOSED.** The flavor tap comes off the **water split** — the JG PP0208E 1/4" union tee on the ASSE 1022's 1/4" outlet (`reference/water-split/`): its south run collet → 1/4" OD LLDPE → neoFit flow regulator → V-A ([`/hardware/topology/fluid-topology.md`](/hardware/topology/fluid-topology.md) tube segment 1). The ASSE leaves at 1/4", so the split and the tap branch are both plain 1/4" PTC — no 3/8"-to-1/4" branch chain. See [`/hardware/ledger/bom.md`](/hardware/ledger/bom.md) §3.
4. ~~**PP0308E elbow count in the warm-side line runs.**~~ **CLOSED.** [`/hardware/ledger/bom.md`](/hardware/ledger/bom.md) §4 now enumerates [11](https://www.freshwatersystems.com/products/john-guest-union-elbow-black-polypropylene-1-4) per build against the tray and pump geometry: 1 in-cavity CO2 bend + 6 on the elbowed valve-manifold boundary ports (source-select 4, bag-circuit 2) + 4 on the two Kamoer pump outlet pairs.
