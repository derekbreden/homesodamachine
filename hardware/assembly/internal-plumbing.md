# Internal Plumbing

The production procedure for closing every internal fluid path inside the assembled enclosure — CO2 line from the inlet on the +Y wall of back-top to the cold core's `co2-in` cap conduit, filtered tap-water from the +Y wall's inlet through the backflow preventer to the `water-in` cap conduit, the full two-channel flavor manifold (source-select valves, peristaltic pumps, reservoir fills and draws, gooseneck outputs), and the three risers that land on the umbilical bulkheads on the +Y wall. **§3's fixed valves, moving tee carrier and flexible links are assembled on the bench into `enclosure-front-top` while that piece is still loose.** The rest stages where the slide order puts it ([`enclosure-mechanical.md`](/hardware/assembly/enclosure-mechanical.md) §6): work on `enclosure-back-top`'s own walls — the tap-water chain in its anchor, the water split in its rib — happens on that piece on the bench before its column closes; work on the cold core's lid — both pump chains, the pump's stubs — happens on the core's open cart before it rides in; the crossing runs and the risers are made up through the open Y-seam mouth after the ride; and only the pump cartridge's connection cycle waits for a fully closed chassis. Feeds [`wiring.md`](/hardware/assembly/wiring.md) and the first powered run at [`firmware-and-commissioning.md`](/hardware/assembly/firmware-and-commissioning.md).

Design intent and the path-by-path enumeration live in [`/hardware/README.md`](/hardware/README.md) "Carbonation" + "Flavor" + "Safety". The valve / Y-junction / tube-segment truth table this manifold builds to is [`/hardware/topology/fluid-topology.md`](/hardware/topology/fluid-topology.md) — the manifold matches that file segment-for-segment. Cold-core internal plumbing (everything inside the foam shell) is already complete per [`cold-core.md`](/hardware/assembly/cold-core.md). Refrigerant lines are already brazed and charged per [`refrigerant-loop.md`](/hardware/assembly/refrigerant-loop.md). Above-counter umbilical (faucet, 3-tube bundle, foam insulation, sleeve) is already built per [`faucet-and-umbilical.md`](/hardware/assembly/faucet-and-umbilical.md).

## Scope

In: the slide order's stages per [`enclosure-mechanical.md`](/hardware/assembly/enclosure-mechanical.md) §6 — `enclosure-back-top` populated on the bench (3× PP1208E umbilical + the water-inlet PP1208E 1/4" JG QC + the CO2 neoFit ABU44 + the RJ11 keystone + the C14; electronics bay on its wall; unpowered), the cold core charged and staged on its cart with its [7](CAP_FLUID_LINES) cap conduits standing proud of the top lid, and — for the runs made up at the mouth and the pump cartridge's connection cycle — the ridden-together chassis. The ASSE drip pan with moisture sensor goes into its sleeve through the −X wall after the box closes. Plus the parts listed under "Inputs per appliance" below. **§3 runs ahead of all of it** — its fixed valve rows and moving carrier mechanism are assembled on `enclosure-front-top` before that piece joins anything, so what they need off this list is the printed set and the parts, not a chassis.

**Every warm-side fluid termination on the cold core is a cap conduit.** All [7](CAP_FLUID_LINES) of them are Ø[6.8 mm](CAP_CONDUIT_D) bores up the cap's own columns, opening upward on the lid's outer face — so a line reaching one arrives at the deck the electronics bay stands on, not at a body face, and leans into a countersunk lip on the way in. Three are the carbonator's own (`water-in`, `co2-in`, `carb-water-out`) and four are the reservoirs' (`reservoir-a`, `reservoir-a-fill`, `reservoir-b`, `reservoir-b-fill`). No fluid line crosses a shell wall: the core's front face is mated against the refrigeration base — the condenser flush on it, the compressor's plate inset at each end, because the condenser is the deeper of the two and the pair is struck on one centre before it is yawed — so a bore struck there would open into that base rather than into the machine. What is left on that face is the copper/PRV slot on each lane, and neither of the two is this procedure's — both reed cables leave by the lid, each up the channel its own column stands in.

Out: every internal fluid path closed and primed-ready — fully connected, fully torqued, fully clamped, leak-witnessed dry where possible, but **not yet primed with water or CO2**. Fluid charging happens at [`acceptance-and-burn-in.md`](/hardware/assembly/acceptance-and-burn-in.md). Specifically: CO2 path from the +Y wall's ABU44 bulkhead, `co2-0` to the WR1110 secondary regulator, `co2-1` to the downstream GASHER check, and `co2-2` to the `co2-in` cap conduit, with gas-rated adapters and the made-up check placement qualified as described in §1; water path from the +Y wall's PP1208E 1/4" JG QC bulkhead (customer-facing — customer plugs the install-kit 1/4" LLDPE in at install time; the install-kit Waterdrop 15UC-UF filter mounts customer-side, inline upstream of this bulkhead, per [`/hardware/ledger/bom.md`](/hardware/ledger/bom.md) §3) → the ASSE chain's own inlet collet, which the bulkhead's inboard collet meets face to face → PP010822E 1/4" PTC × 1/4" NPT M → GAGIRA 316L SS 3/8" NPT F × 1/4" NPT F reducing coupling threading onto the ASSE 1022's 3/8" MPT inlet → Multiplex 19-0897 backflow preventer → PI4512F6S + PP061208W (3/8" MFL → 1/4" PTC) → `water-2` → water-split PP0208E tee → `water-3` → V-K fill/shutoff, whose outlet the PP450822E + MAACFLOW suction chain's collet meets face to face → reinforced-PVC hose onto the G Ganen suction barb → G Ganen pump → a second hose off its discharge barb → the made-up discharge chain (MAACFLOW → GASHER check → PP450822E) lying fore-and-aft on the pump's west flank → `water-5` → the `water-in` cap conduit; flavor manifold built per [`/hardware/topology/fluid-topology.md`](/hardware/topology/fluid-topology.md) (V-C/D source-select → pumps → reservoir fill/draw and dispense valves), including the four runs onto the reservoirs' own fill and draw conduits; three risers (carbonated water off the `carb-water-out` conduit, flavor A, flavor B) climbing to the three PP1208E umbilical bulkheads on the +Y wall; Multiplex atmospheric vent terminating in the ASSE drip pan over the moisture sensor.

Not in scope: cold-core internal plumbing — the TAISHER carbonator-port elbows, the bottom-plate PTC adapters (PI010822S for CO2, PP010822E for water), and every line run inside the shell between a carbonator port and its cap conduit — all installed inside the cold core during [`cold-core.md`](/hardware/assembly/cold-core.md). The plain bottom CO2 opening and the top water-inlet jet are specified by [`pressure-vessel.md`](/hardware/assembly/pressure-vessel.md) and [`water-inlet-jet.md`](/hardware/assembly/water-inlet-jet.md). Refrigerant lines — already done in [`refrigerant-loop.md`](/hardware/assembly/refrigerant-loop.md). Above-counter umbilical — already done in [`faucet-and-umbilical.md`](/hardware/assembly/faucet-and-umbilical.md). Customer-side tap and CO2 connections — install-time, not assembly. Solenoid and pump wiring — owned by [`wiring.md`](/hardware/assembly/wiring.md); this doc lands every electrical actuator in its plumbed position but leaves the leads loose for the wiring step.

## Procedure

The section numbers follow the three fluid systems; the physical build does not. Run §3 steps 1–5 first on loose `enclosure-front-top`, then close the enclosure around that subassembly. Make the CO2 and water paths at the bench/cart stages their bodies expose, complete §3 step 6 when the closed chassis accepts the pump cartridge, and land the three risers last after the manifold and service bay establish their routing space.

### 1. CO2 path: the +Y wall's ABU44 → WR1110 → GASHER → `co2-in` cap conduit

The required flow order puts the check **after the regulator**, with its arrow toward the carbonator. The bottom gas port is below the water line, so the check must stop reverse water flow before it reaches the WR1110. Confirm the ABU44 bulkhead is clamped through the +Y wall per [`enclosure-mechanical.md`](/hardware/assembly/enclosure-mechanical.md); the customer's red tether enters its outboard collet and `co2-0` enters its inboard collet.

**Use PI010822S gray-acetal adapters at all five CO2 NPT-to-tube transitions.** [John Guest specifies this part for CO2 beverage gas, air and inert gases](https://www.johnguest.com/ca/en/od-tube-fittings/acetal-gray/threaded-connectors/male-connector-nptf-thread). The [John Guest catalog](https://www.johnguest.com/sites/default/files/files/JohnGuest-Fluid%20System-Catalog-2025.pdf) excludes the PP range from compressed-air service; PP010822E and PP450822E belong on the water paths here.

Make up the warm components with supported wrench flats and thread sealant applied according to the fitting instructions:

1. PI010822S into WR1110 **IN**, and a second into **OUT**.
2. A third PI010822S into the GASHER's female inlet; verify the supplied body's flow arrow points from that socket toward its male outlet.
3. One LTWFITTING 316 1/4-inch FNPT coupling onto the check's male outlet, then a fourth PI010822S into the coupling's other end. The fifth PI010822S is on the cold-core bottom gas elbow, fitted in [`cold-core.md`](/hardware/assembly/cold-core.md).

**The downstream check placement and three warm red hose routes require a made-up fit record.** The nominal CAD layout keeps WR1110 in its existing bracket and places the check alongside it above the G Ganen pump, supported at its round inlet boss by a tied ceiling cradle. The regulator-to-check hose turns forward in a U; the check outlet returns through the east lane to `co2-in`. [`_gas_chain.py`](/hardware/manifold-layout/_gas_chain.py) includes the four warm adapters and a provisional coupling envelope. The coupling dimensions and actual PI010822S made-up reach remain unmeasured. Verify the completed stack, support, wrench clearance, tube insertion lengths and hose bends against the enclosure before assembly; the CAD clearance checks do not qualify those received parts. Keep the WR1110 bracket and enclosure envelope fixed.

**What holds the regulator is a rib printed off the top wall** (`enclosure._tube_anchors`, [`printed-parts/enclosure/enclosure/README.md`](/hardware/printed-parts/enclosure/enclosure/README.md) "Tube anchors"), bored concentric with the **barrel between its two wrench hexes** and stopped on the barrel's axis plane so its lip is a flat face one wall deep. Thread a **6" zip tie** through the cavity in the middle of that rib and leave it lying open before the regulator goes up — the channel's roof is the rib's own back, and there is no reaching under it afterwards. Lift the barrel into the bore until it beds all the way round, close the tie round barrel and rib together, and flush-cut it — barrel and rib make an [83.7 mm](WR1110_LOOP) loop, past what a 4" tie closes. **Cinch on the barrel only** — never on either wrench hex, which is where a socket has to go to break the joint. The seat opens downward, so **the zip tie is what carries the regulator** and the bore is what it pulls into: tug-test it, the same as the flow meter's two anchors.

Cut the three runs from the **red 1/4-inch LLDPE reel** using the qualified placement:

- **`co2-0`:** ABU44 inboard collet → WR1110 IN adapter.
- **`co2-1`:** WR1110 OUT adapter → GASHER female-inlet adapter.
- **`co2-2`:** PI010822S on the GASHER outlet coupling → `co2-in` cap conduit → the cold-core gas elbow's PI010822S.

Square-cut every tube end, allow the measured insertion depth at each collet, push fully home and tug-test. The cap conduit guides a continuous tube; it is not a sealing fitting. Thread one **6-inch 18 lb zip tie** through the empty local check cradle before placing the made-up check. Seat its round metal inlet boss in the cradle, close the tie and flush-cut it, keeping the adapter and wrench flats clear. The nominal cradle loop exceeds the documented closed-loop reach of a 4-inch tie. Retain the crossing runs without loading a plastic collet, and record clearance with the electronics deck and enclosure closed. The downstream portion must tolerate carbonator pressure and wet reverse exposure. Verify the owned check's materials and reverse sealing per Open items before qualifying it for this duty.

Below the conduit, the line and bottom elbow are already installed by [`cold-core.md`](/hardware/assembly/cold-core.md). The elbow feeds the plain Port 1 opening. Gas enters below the water only while the regulator-to-carbonator pressure difference permits flow, and rises toward the headspace. There is no internal barb, silicone hose or stone. Pressure and reverse-leak checks are performed at [`acceptance-and-burn-in.md`](/hardware/assembly/acceptance-and-burn-in.md).

### 2. Water path: the +Y wall's PP1208E → adapter chain → ASSE 1022 → split → V-K → G Ganen → `water-in` cap conduit

The customer-facing water inlet on the +Y wall is a **1/4" JG QC bulkhead** (PP1208E — same SKU as the umbilical cluster). At install time the customer plugs the install-kit 1/4" LLDPE into the outboard PTC side — no factory work on that side beyond confirming the bulkhead is installed and the wall-side EPDM O-ring is seated. The factory work below closes everything inboard of the bulkhead. **From the ASSE 1022 outlet on, the path uses 1/4" LLDPE, with 3/8" ID reinforced-PVC hoses connecting the G Ganen pump to its suction and discharge chains.**

**Every length in this section is cut off the white reel** — `water-2` through `water-5` and the flavour circuit's own tap-water leg `fluid-1`/`fluid-2` below, up to V-A. The two 3/8" pump stubs are the exception and are reinforced clear PVC over the pump and adapter barbs.

**Mount the G Ganen on its four identical purchased rubber feet while the cap is accessible.** Their nominal 7 mm pads slide along the casing rails and can be removed. The slot centres stand at reference X = 9.5 and 67.5 mm, Y = ±38.5 mm, with each clip fully engaged at its rail end. These are selected full-engagement positions, not claimed hard stops. Reference +X runs from the motor/head junction toward the motor rear. Seat the bearing faces on the lid and place each M3 × 20 screw 1.5 mm outward within its slot, so its Ø9 × 0.8 mm washer clears the raised shoulder. Align those screw axes with the printed cap columns. Verify actual screw passage, flat washer seating, engagement and rubber compression as the mount closes. The installed pump sends flow toward **enclosure −X**: suction faces east and discharge faces west. The [installation reference](/hardware/reference/g-ganen-pump/installation/README.md) supplies the measured ports, mounting axes and native checks.

**The whole tap-water head end runs fore and aft in the west lane, on two storeys.** The ASSE chain hangs in the lane west of the pump, hugging the cold core's west face, inlet aft at the +Y wall and flow running forward. `water-2` carries the water forward off its outlet and a step down onto the tap's own storey, where the split stands; the flow regulator stands directly OVER the split on that same column, and `fluid-1` is the hairpin between the two. Both bodies' flows lie fore and aft along the lane, so the only mouth in the head end that is not on that axis is the split's branch, which looks straight down at the storey the pump and the manifold stand on.

The PP010822E → GAGIRA → ASSE 1022 → PI4512F6S → PP061208W run and its vent stub are modeled as one piece in [`/hardware/reference/asse1022-assembly/`](/hardware/reference/asse1022-assembly/) — the stack-up, the three terminals (1/4" PTC in, 1/4" PTC out, vent), and which way the vent has to face.

Confirm the PP1208E is installed on the +Y wall per [`enclosure-mechanical.md`](/hardware/assembly/enclosure-mechanical.md) with the 1/4" QC on both sides (push-to-connect, no tools required at install). The Waterdrop 15UC-UF filter is customer-side hardware — it ships in the install kit and mounts inline on the customer's 1/4" LLDPE upstream of this bulkhead ([`/hardware/ledger/bom.md`](/hardware/ledger/bom.md) §3) — so no factory work touches it.

**There is no `water-1`.** The chain stands off the +Y wall by exactly what the union reaching through it leaves, so the bulkhead's inboard collet and the ASSE chain's inlet collet meet face to face down one axis with nothing between them to turn. The first tube in the machine is a length of stock cut to the two grips and swallowed whole by them: square-cut both ends, push fully into both collets, tug-test.

The ASSE 1022 inlet stack is factory-made up on the chain (modeled in the asse1022-assembly reference): the PP010822E's 1/4" NPT M side threads into the 1/4" NPT F side of the GAGIRA 316L SS reducing coupling (PTFE tape, snug + 1 turn past hand-tight); the GAGIRA's 3/8" NPT F side threads onto the ASSE 1022's 3/8" MPT inlet (tape on the MPT threads, snug + 1 turn). Multiplex flow arrow points away from the customer side. On the outlet, two John Guest fittings make up the flare-to-1/4" stack: the **PI4512F6S** 3/8" FFL × 3/8" PTC adapter draws up onto the ASSE 1022's 3/8" MFL outlet via its chrome swivel nut — flat-faced flare seal, no PTFE tape, quarter turn past hand-tight on the swivel only (never turn the body against the flare) — and the **PP061208W** 3/8"-stem × 1/4" PTC reducer pushes into its collet. The 3/8" run begins and ends inside those two fittings; the reducer's 1/4" PTC collet faces **forward** down the west lane, and the water leaves the backflow preventer at 1/4" OD.

**Lay the made-up chain into its anchor.** The −X wall carries a stepped cradle for it (`enclosure._asse_cradle`, [`printed-parts/enclosure/enclosure/README.md`](/hardware/printed-parts/enclosure/enclosure/README.md) "Tap-water cradle") — three 120° V sections, one per section of the chain, cut so the brass barrel sits deepest and the steps either side of it are faces square to the axis. Bring the chain in from the east and press it west into the V until the barrel's two lower flats bed on the anchor's two faces; the barrel's forward end face comes up against the step under the swivel nut with `ASSE_STEP_SLIP` of play, and aft the inlet collet butts the bulkhead's, which is where the run's own length is taken up. **The vent's clock is the fit.** The Multiplex's hex does not spin on the run, so a barrel bedded flat in the V has its vent pointing at the tray by construction — a chain that will not sit down in the anchor is a chain rolled off its flats, and forcing it is how the drip ends up outside the coves.

Then close the mouth with **two 8" 50 lb zip ties** — barrel and anchor make a 105 mm loop, past what a 4" tie closes, and an 8" is a 50 lb tie at 0.19" across the zip tie, which is why this cavity is cut wider than the others on the box — one in each band the vent leaves clear on the barrel, each **threaded down the bore through the anchor's back before the chain goes in**, then laid across the channel the deck's own storey leaves under the top wall, so the chain comes down onto the zip tie rather than the zip tie being fished over it afterwards. Bring each end round under the barrel and up its east flank to buckle. **Brass barrel only** — a tie cinched on the JG acetal flare adapter or the PP reducer takes a collet out of round. Black UV-nylon, flush-cut, no proud tail, per [`cable-assemblies.md`](/hardware/assembly/cable-assemblies.md). They stop the chain leaving the anchor eastward and nothing else: what carries it is the V, on two flats of the fitting's own hex, so a cut tie is a service step and not a failure.

**Set the water split into its rib.** A JG PP0208E 1/4" union tee (modeled in [`/hardware/reference/water-split/`](/hardware/reference/water-split/), using the measured fitting reference shared with the manifold's six junctions). The −X wall of `enclosure-back-top` prints a seat for it, bored for the round barrel between the tee's hub and its supply collet, with a zip tie channel behind the bore ([`enclosure_assembly.py`](/hardware/manifold-layout/enclosure_assembly.py) `BODY_ANCHOR_SITES`). Thread one **6 inch, 18 lb zip tie** through the channel before seating the fixed collar. Close it around the collar and rib, with the lock on the exposed +X crown; the shortest loop is [74.9 mm](SPLIT_LOOP). Keep the complete 2.5 mm tie band on the fixed collar and the terminal collet free to move. Its two run collets lie fore and aft, supply aft and flavor tap forward, and its **branch is rolled to look straight down**.

Cut segment **water-2**: the chain's outlet collet aft on its own level stub, then one lean forward, west and down onto the split's storey, and a level stub into the supply collet — two corners, because the split stands a step under the chain and a lane inboard of it. Both stubs are level and long enough for their own corner to seat in. Square-cut, push fully home, tug-test.

Mount **V-K** — the Beduan 12 V NC fill/shutoff solenoid — on the cold core's crown on the machine's east flank, forward of the suction chain and **firing aft** into the collet that feeds the pump; its own frame already runs the flow inlet-forward / outlet-aft, so it takes no turn. Wire its coil per run DC-9 in [`ac-wiring-schedule.md`](/hardware/wiring/ac-wiring-schedule.md). Normally closed, so a leak alarm or a power loss stops all water reaching the carbonator. Cut segment **water-3** — off the split's **downward branch collet**, falling once on the branch's own column and **past** the lane it feeds, then forward and west in one leg into the channel between the enclosure's west wall and the cold core's shell, rising onto the core's front lane as it goes, east across the machine beneath the funnel union's foot and through the cap's own side post, and one lean forward onto V-K's column. Four corners, every one at stock. The fall ending under that lane rather than on it is what stands the split and the flow regulator as low as they do: what a fall costs the fitting above it is its corner's tangent and not its length, so a corner that bottoms out low starts low. Square-cut, push fully home, tug-test.

Make up the **suction chain** on the bench — a PP450822E (1/4" PTC × 1/4" NPT **F**) + MAACFLOW (1/4" NPT M × 3/8" hose barb) pair, PTFE tape on its one NPT joint, snug + 1 turn. Thread a **6" zip tie** through the rib on the cold core's cap lid east of the pump and leave it lying open across the channel — it goes in before the chain, the same way its twin's does. Lay the chain into that rib, its bore taking the **MAACFLOW's hex**: that hex is the only metal on this chain a zip tie may close on, and it is shorter than the rib, so the rib overhangs onto the barb one side and the PP socket the other and touches neither. Close the tie over the hex and flush-cut it. **The rib carries the chain and the tie only holds it down.** Cinch on the **MAACFLOW hex only** — never on the PP450822E's socket, hex or collet, which go out of round. **There is no segment to cut here.** V-K's outlet and this chain's collet meet face to face on one plane and one column — V-K's cradle seat and the source row's own depth are what put them there — so the tube between them is a length of stock cut to the two grips and swallowed whole by both, the same joint the rear bulkhead makes with the ASSE chain's inlet.

Fit segment **water-7** between the pump's **east-facing suction barb** and the suction chain's **aft-facing MAACFLOW barb**. Its generated exposed route is ~[48 mm](SUCTION_STUB_LEN); the hose blank also needs the actual engagement over both barbs. The G Ganen reference measures each barb independently, and its observed external profile is an insertion allowance to test during assembly. Seat the 3/8" ID reinforced PVC, preserve the drawn bend, and close two SAE #6 316 SS clamps, one over each barb, with their screw heads accessible. Confirm insertion, retention and a clear hose bore on the received pump.

Bend it on a former or off the reel's own set; a hairpin pinched by hand kinks at the crown.

From the regulator's outlet, segment **`fluid-2`** runs aft, turns east and crosses the lane **level, on the outlet's own storey** — `water-2` comes down that same lane from the chain overhead and passes beneath it, and a run that has spent no height yet is the one that clears it — then comes forward and down the strip west of the nozzle-A line in one leg, and leans east through the window the funnel drain leaves — under the drain's lane and over its turn onto V-B — into V-A's inlet. Four corners ([`/hardware/topology/fluid-topology.md`](/hardware/topology/fluid-topology.md) tube segments 1 and 2). No 3/8" barb tee, no reducers — the split is already 1/4".

Cut the stub to the overhang the placement leaves for it — `VENT_STUB_REACH` in [`asse1022_assembly.py`](/hardware/reference/asse1022-assembly/asse1022_assembly.py) — and keep the fall's column clear when you dress the C14's cordage through the band under the body. **Vent must drip to atmosphere, not be plumbed into a drain** — the vent is the mechanical telltale per [`/hardware/README.md`](/hardware/README.md) "Safety". Two LOKMAN clamps on this run (vent barb + termination, if a termination barb is fitted — bare drip is also acceptable; the receiving pan does not need a tight fit).

### 3. Flavor manifold: V-A through V-J + Y-A, Y-B, Y-C, Y-D, Y-F, Y-G + pumps

Build to [`/hardware/topology/fluid-topology.md`](/hardware/topology/fluid-topology.md) segment-for-segment. The truth table there names every valve, every Y-junction, and every tube segment by number; this step describes the build cadence around that truth table, not its content.

**This step begins on the bench, on `enclosure-front-top` alone, before that piece joins anything.** The two valve trays hold the fixed valve rows. The wall behind the release plate journals the four pump-side tees in X and Z while leaving their Y stroke free, and the tee carrier couples those four bodies so they travel together. Their tee-side hairpin ends and the four bowed tee-to-valve stubs travel with them; the fixed ends of those eight flexible members do not.

**V-A and V-B are not this bench's.** They stand on the cold core's cap in the back column and press into their own cradles there (`_cold_core_interface.cap_cradles`), so the two runs carrying them forward to Y-A and Y-B are the only joints in the pack that cross the Y seam — see Open items 2.

Lay out all ten Beduan solenoid valves with their inlet/outlet legends visible and the six PP0208E tees in the topology positions. The four bowed links have 17.251 mm of exposed developed path. Their blanks also include the insertion at both real fittings and are bench-fit at step 5. Cut every other segment from its reported cut length. Cut **from the black reel, with one exception: `fluid-3`, V-A's outlet into Y-A, comes off the white reel** — it is the last of the tap water, and everything downstream of Y-A has met the funnel's syrup. Square-cut both ends with the Mudder cutter and tag each segment with its topology number.

Build the carrier block in this order:

1. **Seat the four moving tees.** With both valve rows and the carrier absent, insert Y-C, Y-D, Y-F and Y-G from aft into the tee wall's branch journals. Move each tee forward to release. The branch collet remains against the fixed release face while the bare tee waits for its carrier.
2. **Load each spring and install its carrier half, left first.** Both valve rows, Y-A/Y-B and all flexible links remain absent. Place the spring in the closed moving cup and hold it axially at 12.15 mm with the 2 mm-thick pusher. Bring the half through the open rear, 70 mm above its final height and 17.15 mm inboard. Lower it behind the seated tees at Y+33 mm, shift it outward 13.90 mm, slide it fore 28.50 mm, then seat it outward the final 3.25 mm at the aft stop. Withdraw the pusher 15.435 mm inboard and lift it 70 mm beside the outer tee; the spring expands into its integral fixed cup. Reuse the same tool for the right half. The right shelf enters its receiver during the fore slide, and the broad rear wall retains the joint during the final outward seat. The shelf needs no separate lift or twist. The opposed rims capture X, and the flank recesses guide Y. The [assembly view](/hardware/printed-parts/enclosure/tee-carrier/simple-carrier-study/assembly-sequence.png) shows the joint motions.
3. **Install the aft valves.** Move the joined carrier fore to release, so each tee arm sits in its trough, and keep the carrier and tees there. Bring V-C, V-D, V-G and V-J up from the open underside with their posts clear of the aft tray, then press their posts fully into the sockets. Match the topology's port and coil clocks.
4. **Tie the tees.** Thread one 4-inch tie around each run arm through the carrier slots: two ties per tee, eight total. The outer tees' heads face inboard and the inner tees' heads face outboard, into the open gaps between tees. Draw both ties evenly and flush-cut the tails. Each strap bears on the station web between its slots and crosses the aft face; keep each head on the tee side. Check equal tab travel, both spring ends seated in their cups and empty return to the 4.50 mm aft limit before fitting the flexible links.
5. **Install the fore valves and the four bowed flex stubs.** Press V-E, V-F, V-H and V-I into the fore tray and retain the seated valves with their ties. Bench-fit one black-LLDPE blank for each Y-C↔V-E, Y-D↔V-F, Y-F↔V-H and Y-G↔V-I joint to the [current manifold path](/hardware/manifold-layout/README.md): 17.251 mm exposed developed length and 15.251 mm vertical separation. The blank includes both fitting engagements. Keep all four bows clear through the complete 4.50 mm carrier travel and leave the tee-side end free to move with the carrier.
6. **Connect the pump cartridge only after the chassis is closed.** Present its four tubes through the fixed release plate. Each hand spans the cartridge pocket and carrier tab on the same side; squeeze the cartridge aft and carrier fore until all four tubes bottom at the fore stop. Each tube projects 10 mm beyond its fully depressed sleeve face while the cartridge is 2 mm short of seating. Relax both hands together and push the cartridge through its final 2 mm until fully seated. The carrier settles at connected, 2 mm aft of release, with its sleeves extended and 0.5 mm of nose air. The same tubes bottom 11.50 mm beyond the extended sleeves.

For removal, brace the enclosure and pull the cartridge. The four tied tees follow it fore; the plate takes up the 0.5 mm nose gap and depresses the branch sleeves through their measured 1.50 mm stroke. At the fore stop the plate holds the sleeves continuously while the tubes leave. Once they clear, the empty carrier returns to the 4.50 mm aft limit, 2.50 mm beyond connected. Check both tabs move and settle together, and prove all four tubes are gripped with a gentle tug after insertion. Spring retention, return tension and assembled rigidity are observations of the complete enclosure trial.

Build the fixed limbs around that carrier block. Y-A and Y-B remain on the two inner axes, with their branches facing one another across the mirror plane. V-A's inlet comes from the water split through the flow regulator; V-B's inlet is the funnel. Neither reservoir has a junction: V-F/V-E reach reservoir A's fill/draw conduits and V-I/V-H reach reservoir B's directly. The four spine hairpins keep their fixed valve ends where the trays put them while their Y-C/Y-D/Y-F/Y-G ends follow the carrier; do not tie those moving ends to the enclosure.

**The two valve trays take the decks with the piece in the hand.** The fold leaves eight of the ten valves standing on two planes, four to a plane, and each plane takes one plate of four seats — printed into `enclosure-front-top` itself, wall to wall ([`printed-parts/enclosure/valve-tray/`](/hardware/printed-parts/enclosure/valve-tray/README.md)). Every valve's four corner posts press into their own sockets; nothing bolts down and nothing is bonded. The seats are SUNK in these plates rather than stood on them, so the face itself is what a valve's round body boss lands on, the same way on both. The two plates face each other with both decks between them and each runs wall to wall, so what carries the manifold once it is in the enclosure is the box itself, not the tube between the valves.

**Build the Kamoer cartridge upright on the bench.** Lower both complete KPHM600-SW3B17 pumps into their fitted wells, with the silicone outlets facing the open +Y side. Lower the broad cap over the motors; its fitted collars locate the pumps and its raised crown leaves the motor ends and terminals open. Draw its [2](CAP_SCREWS) top-access M3×[60](CAP_SCREW_LEN) screws evenly into the cradle's centre heat-sets. Check that both pumps are firmly held without vertical play, as recorded for Derek's accepted assembly. The [fitted-well audit](/hardware/reference/kamoer-kphm400/fitted-well-audit/README.md) identifies the retained local fit. Leave the assembled cartridge out of the bay until the chassis is closed; connect it using step 6, including the final 2 mm seating motion.

Route each pump's silicone tube around its rotor, then insert 1/4" OD LLDPE into both silicone ends and zip-tie the silicone onto the LLDPE. The four LLDPE ends standing aft off the face pass through the fixed release plate and enter the moving branch collets. Set each free tip to the current seated tube-bottom plane, Y94.690 mm in the enclosure, or [17.53 mm](PUMP_TUBE_PROJECTION) aft of the modeled rigid outlet-axis station. Measure from that casing datum; the silicone mouth and inserted LLDPE stub can move. At the fore stop, these tubes bottom while the cartridge is 2 mm short of seating; release the grips and push the cartridge through the final 2 mm. Fit the pump-end joint before marking the free tips; its engagement is additional stock. Crimp a female faston receptacle onto each conductor of DC-5's cartridge cord, the 28 AWG 4P ribbon, each conductor folded back on itself to fill the barrel; these four stay on the pump-motor tabs, and the cord joins the enclosure at the pump jack behind the display per [`wiring.md`](/hardware/assembly/wiring.md).

The fixed valve rows and carrier mechanism are already inside the front-top piece that carries them, and the position they take above the compressor and below the cold-core +Y face ([`/hardware/README.md`](/hardware/README.md) "Enclosure (back to front)") is the one they arrive in when [`enclosure-mechanical.md`](/hardware/assembly/enclosure-mechanical.md) closes the front column. Route each fixed span through the piece's own tube channels while it is reachable. Do not place a tie across any carrier guide, service arm, bowed stub, or moving hairpin end.

**Four of the manifold's runs land on the cold core's own cap conduits**: **`fluid-14`** off V-F onto `reservoir-a-fill`, **`fluid-24`** off V-I onto `reservoir-b-fill`, and the two draws — **`fluid-16`** from `reservoir-a` into V-E and **`fluid-26`** from `reservoir-b` into V-H. Dress and tag their front-top ends while the piece is on the bench; land the cold-core ends while the core remains on its open cart, before the back assembly rides over it. A fill conduit opens into that reservoir's headspace; a draw conduit reaches the floor bulkhead at the bottom of its wet V. Keep the carrier-side hairpin ends free while those fixed tails are dressed.

Three more outlets exit upward to the umbilical bulkheads on the +Y wall in step 4:

- **Carbonated water riser** comes off the cold core's `carb-water-out` **cap conduit**, standing proud of the lid (already plumbed inside the shell per [`cold-core.md`](/hardware/assembly/cold-core.md) step 5). Build does not pick up this line in the flavor manifold step — it goes straight from the cold core to the +Y wall's blue-ringed PP1208E in step 4 below.
- **Flavor A riser** comes from V-G outlet (the "pump to nozzle A" valve, segment 18 in the topology doc terminating at "Nozzle A").
- **Flavor B riser** comes from V-J outlet (segment 28 terminating at "Nozzle B").

### 4. Risers up to the umbilical bulkheads on the +Y wall

Three risers climb to the three PP1208E umbilical bulkheads on the +Y wall per [`/hardware/printed-parts/enclosure/y-wall-of-back-top/README.md`](/hardware/printed-parts/enclosure/y-wall-of-back-top/README.md). **The three stand on one row** high on the +Y wall, under the umbilical and above the water inlet: flavor B outboard on the west, flavor A beside it, and the **blue-ringed bulkhead** for carbonated water east of the pair.

Aft of the meter, **`carb-1`'s own crossing is anchored** where it runs under the top wall: a rib printed off that wall carries a bore concentric with the tube (`enclosure._tube_anchors`), stopped on the tube's axis plane so its lip is a flat face one wall deep. Lay the tube up into the bore and close it with **one 4" zip tie** through the cavity in the middle of the rib, round under the tube and back — tube and rib make a [39.7 mm](CARB_1_LOOP) loop, the shortest on the box. Its fore/aft gap is 22 mm and its developed length is about 23 mm; keep both ends on the collets' axes. Confirm the housing's physical flow arrow points from `carb-1` toward `carb-2`. Square-cut every end, push fully into all four PTC ports, tug-test each. The CARGEN nitrile-rubber pipe insulation that wraps this line ([`/hardware/ledger/bom.md`](/hardware/ledger/bom.md) §9, 1/4" ID × 3/8" wall) goes on as one piece per length, cut to that run and slid on from the open end before either collet goes down (Open #7 below) — not in the 1-ft segments the above-counter umbilical uses. Insulation continuity from the cap conduit's mouth to the inboard face of the blue-ringed bulkhead is the target — that mouth is where the thermal mass of the cold core ends and the chilled water enters its multi-meter run to the faucet.
- **Flavor A riser:** segment **`fluid-18`**, 1/4" OD black LLDPE off V-G's outlet. Thread one 6" zip tie through the post's window before seating the tube; bed its complete 8.8 mm straight bearing, close the tie over the post's crown and flush-cut it. - **Flavor B riser:** segment **`fluid-28`**, the mirror off V-J's outlet into the west bulkhead, and the shorter of the two because its valve already stands on that side. Four corners. No insulation.

Black-into-either-black is unambiguous downstream of the manifold (per [`/hardware/printed-parts/enclosure/y-wall-of-back-top/README.md`](/hardware/printed-parts/enclosure/y-wall-of-back-top/README.md) "Umbilical port — tube identification"): both flavor tubes route through the same wall-side bundle and the user does not need to distinguish them at the wall.

### 5. Witness and tidy

Walk every joint built in this procedure. Tug-test every PTC connection. Check all four SAE #6 clamps on the G Ganen/MAACFLOW hose joints for seated hose, secure retention and an unobstructed bore; the vent uses its separate LOKMAN clamp. Confirm every NPT joint is past hand-tight and has PTFE tape showing at no more than half of the thread engagement (excess tape past half-engagement is a stress concentration). Confirm the Multiplex atmospheric vent stub leans aft and down as the chain's roll leaves it, and that the column straight down from its tip reaches the ASSE drip pan with no obstruction. Confirm both pumps sit in their enclosure position with leads routed clear for spade termination at [`wiring.md`](/hardware/assembly/wiring.md).

Zip-tie any tube run with a span over ~6" between fixturing points. The enclosure's printed tube channels carry most of the line, but zip ties at the bend transitions and at the pump-mount boundary stabilize the manifold during transport.

The internal plumbing is now ready for [`wiring.md`](/hardware/assembly/wiring.md) (the wiring step lands every solenoid lead, every pump lead, every sensor lead in its electrical termination on the electronics bay, but the plumbing itself does not change after this step).

1. **Pump enclosure-mount mechanism.** The cartridge has two fitted lower wells and one broad cap, closed by [2](CAP_SCREWS) M3×[60](CAP_SCREW_LEN) screws from above. The raised crown surrounds the motor ends with open terminal wells. Derek confirms firm retention and no vertical play in his accepted assembled pair; the local comparison and current cap checks are linked from the [physical-fit record](/hardware/reference/kamoer-kphm400/physical-fit.json). 2. **The pack's two seam-crossing runs still need a motion gate.** V-A and V-B stand on the cold core's cap while Y-A and Y-B ride in the front-top manifold, so their two runs cross the Y seam. Their assembly moment is the open-cart stage before the back assembly rides over the core, with both mouths still reachable. [`_lines.py`](/hardware/manifold-layout/_lines.py) draws neither run, so the tree still lacks a geometry check that proves the available reach and the closing sweep.
3. ~~**Tap-water tap-point into the flavor manifold (V-A inlet).**~~ **CLOSED.** The flavor tap comes off the **water split** — the JG PP0208E 1/4" union tee one tube-length forward of the ASSE 1022's outlet (`reference/water-split/`): its forward run collet → `fluid-1` → neoFit flow regulator → `fluid-2` → V-A ([`/hardware/topology/fluid-topology.md`](/hardware/topology/fluid-topology.md) tube segments 1 and 2). 4. ~~**Union-elbow count in the warm-side line runs.**~~ **CLOSED — none.** The placed pack turns no line on a fitting: three interior joints are face-to-face butts, four carrier-tee-to-fore-valve joints are bowed flex stubs, every junction is a tee, and each pump tube enters a tee's branch.
