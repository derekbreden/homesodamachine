# Internal Plumbing

The production procedure for closing every internal fluid path inside the assembled enclosure — CO2 line from the rear-wall inlet to the cold core's `co2-in` cap conduit, filtered tap-water from the rear-wall inlet through the backflow preventer to the `water-in` cap conduit, the full two-channel flavor manifold (source-select valves, peristaltic pumps, reservoir fills and draws, nozzle outputs), and the three risers that land on the rear-wall umbilical bulkheads. Comes in after the mechanical chassis is closed up by [`enclosure-mechanical.md`](/hardware/assembly/enclosure-mechanical.md) and feeds [`wiring.md`](/hardware/assembly/wiring.md) and the first powered run at [`firmware-and-commissioning.md`](/hardware/assembly/firmware-and-commissioning.md).

Design intent and the path-by-path enumeration live in [`/hardware/future.md`](/hardware/future.md) "Carbonation subsystem" + "Flavor subsystem" + "Backflow vent monitoring". The valve / Y-junction / tube-segment truth table this manifold builds to is [`/hardware/topology/fluid-topology.md`](/hardware/topology/fluid-topology.md) — the manifold matches that file segment-for-segment. Cold-core internal plumbing (everything inside the foam shell) is already complete per [`cold-core.md`](/hardware/assembly/cold-core.md). Refrigerant lines are already brazed and charged per [`refrigerant-loop.md`](/hardware/assembly/refrigerant-loop.md). Above-counter umbilical (faucet body, 3-tube bundle, foam insulation, sleeve) is already built per [`faucet-and-umbilical.md`](/hardware/assembly/faucet-and-umbilical.md).

## Scope

In: mechanical chassis assembled per [`enclosure-mechanical.md`](/hardware/assembly/enclosure-mechanical.md) — cold core standing on the floor behind the refrigeration base, its front face mated flush against the condenser, coil stubs brazed, and its [7](CAP_CONDUITS) cap conduits standing proud of the top lid; rear-wall bulkheads installed (3× PP1208E umbilical + the water-inlet PP1208E 1/4" JG QC + the CO2 DERPIPE PTC); the electronics shelf seated on the foam-cap top but unpowered; internal drip pan with moisture sensor mounted under the future backflow-preventer vent location. Plus the parts listed under "Inputs per appliance" below.

**Every warm-side fluid termination on the cold core is a cap conduit.** All [7](CAP_CONDUITS) of them are Ø[6.5 mm](CAP_CONDUIT_D) bores up the cap's own columns, opening upward on the lid's outer face — so a line reaching one arrives at the deck the electronics shelf stands on, not at a body face, and leans into a countersunk lip on the way in. Three are the carbonator's own (`water-in`, `co2-in`, `carb-water-out`) and four are the reservoirs' (`reservoir-a`, `reservoir-a-fill`, `reservoir-b`, `reservoir-b-fill`). No fluid line crosses a shell wall: the core's front face is mated against the refrigeration base — the condenser flush on it, the compressor's plate inset at each end, because the condenser is the deeper of the two and the pair is struck on one centre before it is yawed — so a bore struck there would open into that base rather than into the machine. What is left on that face is the two reed cables and the copper/PRV slot above them, and none of the three is this procedure's.

Out: every internal fluid path closed and primed-ready — fully connected, fully torqued, fully clamped, leak-witnessed dry where possible, but **not yet primed with water or CO2**. Fluid charging happens at [`acceptance-and-burn-in.md`](/hardware/assembly/acceptance-and-burn-in.md). Specifically: CO2 path from the rear-wall DERPIPE bulkhead through the GASHER check threaded onto its inboard stub, `co2-1` across to the WR1110 secondary regulator, and `co2-2` down onto the `co2-in` cap conduit; water path from the rear-wall PP1208E 1/4" JG QC bulkhead (customer-facing — customer plugs the install-kit 1/4" LLDPE in at install time; the install-kit Waterdrop 15UC-UF filter mounts customer-side, inline upstream of this bulkhead, per [`/hardware/ledger/bom.md`](/hardware/ledger/bom.md) §3) → the ASSE chain's own inlet collet, which the bulkhead's inboard collet meets face to face → PP010822E 1/4" PTC × 1/4" NPT M → GAGIRA 316L SS 3/8" NPT F × 1/4" NPT F reducing coupling threading onto the ASSE 1022's 3/8" MPT inlet → Multiplex 19-0897 backflow preventer → PI4512F6S + PP061208W (3/8" MFL → 1/4" PTC) → `water-2` → water-split PP0208E tee → `water-3` → V-K fill/shutoff → `water-4` → PP450822E + MAACFLOW suction chain → reinforced-PVC stub over the SeaFlo's molded 3/8" suction barb → SeaFlo pump → a second stub off its molded discharge barb → the made-up discharge chain (MAACFLOW → GASHER check → PP450822E) lying fore-and-aft on the pump's west flank → `water-5` → the `water-in` cap conduit; flavor manifold built per [`/hardware/topology/fluid-topology.md`](/hardware/topology/fluid-topology.md) (V-C/D source-select → pumps → reservoir fill/draw and nozzle valves), including the four runs onto the reservoirs' own fill and draw conduits; three risers (carbonated water off the `carb-water-out` conduit, flavor A, flavor B) climbing to the three rear-wall PP1208E umbilical bulkheads; Multiplex atmospheric vent terminating in the internal drip pan over the moisture sensor.

Not in scope: cold-core internal plumbing — the TAISHER vessel-port elbows, the silicone tube + sparge stone, the bottom-plate PP010822E PTC adapters, and every line run inside the shell between a vessel port and its cap conduit — all installed inside the cold core during [`cold-core.md`](/hardware/assembly/cold-core.md). Refrigerant lines — already done in [`refrigerant-loop.md`](/hardware/assembly/refrigerant-loop.md). Above-counter umbilical — already done in [`faucet-and-umbilical.md`](/hardware/assembly/faucet-and-umbilical.md). Customer-side tap and CO2 connections — install-time, not assembly. Solenoid and pump wiring — owned by [`wiring.md`](/hardware/assembly/wiring.md); this doc lands every electrical actuator in its plumbed position but leaves the leads loose for the wiring step.

## Procedure

Order of work: CO2 path first (dry, simpler — three NPT joints inside the enclosure and two tube runs, no soft hose), then the water path (more joints, mixed barb/NPT/PTC), then the flavor manifold (ten valves, six Tees, two pumps — the bulk of the bench labor). Three risers up to the rear-wall umbilical bulkheads last, after the manifold is closed so the riser routing has the rest of the enclosure laid out around it.

### 1. CO2 path: rear-wall DERPIPE → GASHER → WR1110 → `co2-in` cap conduit

**The gas comes in at the rear wall,** on the umbilical row's own storey one column east of the blue-ringed carbonated-water union, and across the wall from the water inlet on the west — the same wall the customer reaches at install, so tank, tap and umbilical all land on one face. Confirm the DERPIPE 5/16"-tube × 1/4" NPT push-to-connect bulkhead is clamped through the rear wall per [`enclosure-mechanical.md`](/hardware/assembly/enclosure-mechanical.md), NPT side facing inboard. Wrap PTFE tape on the inboard NPT threads (2–3 wraps, clockwise as viewed into the thread, leaving the first thread tape-free). Thread the GASHER 1/4" NPT SS check valve onto that stub — arrow on the check body pointing **away** from the wall, i.e. inflow from outside, outflow forward toward the WR1110. Snug + 1 turn past hand-tight with the wrench backed up on the DERPIPE hex. That joint is made-up thread and carries no line.

The check is the only thing that hangs off the bulkhead. **The WR1110 does not thread onto it.** The regulator stands apart, fed and drained by tube, one short hop forward of the check on the same axis and the same height — which is the fitting count [`/hardware/ledger/bom.md`](/hardware/ledger/bom.md) §4 buys.

**What holds the regulator is a rib printed off the top wall** (`enclosure._tube_anchors`, [`printed-parts/enclosure/enclosure/README.md`](/hardware/printed-parts/enclosure/enclosure/README.md) "Tube anchors"), bored concentric with the **barrel between its two wrench hexes** and stopped on the barrel's axis plane so its lip is a flat face one wall deep. Thread a **6" zip tie** through the cavity in the middle of that rib and leave it lying open before the regulator goes up — the channel's roof is the rib's own back, and there is no reaching under it afterwards. Lift the barrel into the bore until it beds all the way round, close the tie round barrel and rib together, and flush-cut it — barrel and rib make an [84.1 mm](WR1110_LOOP) loop, past what a 4" tie closes. **Cinch on the barrel only** — never on either wrench hex, which is where a socket has to go to break the joint. The seat opens downward, so **the strap is what carries the regulator** and the bore is what it pulls into: tug-test it, the same as the flow meter's two saddles.

Wrap PTFE tape on the GASHER's outlet stub and thread a PP450822E 1/4" NPT F × 1/4" PTC adapter onto it. Wrap the WR1110's inlet (the side stamped "IN") and its outlet ("OUT") and thread a PP010822E 1/4" PTC × 1/4" NPT M adapter into each. All three joints snug + 1 turn, wrench backed up on the neighbouring hex. Every NPT-to-PTC transition on this path has now happened on the warm side of the cold core, consistent with the foam-shell boundary rule from [`/hardware/printed-parts/cold-core/foam-shell/README.md`](/hardware/printed-parts/cold-core/foam-shell/README.md) "Build decision".

Cut segment **`co2-1`** from the 1/4" OD LLDPE reel — the check-to-regulator hop, a straight length on one axis with no corner in it. Push it home in both collets. Tug-test both ends.

Cut segment **`co2-2`**: off the regulator's outlet adapter, forward along the deck, east onto the port lane's own column, and straight down it onto the **`co2-in` cap conduit**, which stands proud of the cold core's top lid on the machine's east flank, in the window between V-K's own footprint and the controller board's. Two corners, both at the arc 1/4" LLDPE wants; the run holds the deck's height until it drops. The line leans into the conduit's countersunk lip rather than meeting a fitting. Cut both ends square with the Mudder cutter — square cuts are load-bearing for PTC sealing, no chamfer needed but no angled cut. Push the collet end fully home (audible click + tube bottoms against the internal stop), lay the far end into the conduit, and tug-test both.

**`co2-2`'s crossing is anchored** where it runs under the top wall, one cap conduit aft of `carb-1`'s and in the same kind of rib: a bore concentric with the tube, stopped on the tube's axis plane. Lay the tube up into it and close it with **one 4" zip tie** through the cavity in the middle of the rib, round under the tube and back — tube and rib make a [40.1 mm](CARB_1_LOOP) loop, the same as `carb-1`'s. The bore is what locates the run; the strap only stops it leaving.

The CO2 path is now closed dry from the rear wall to the cold core's crown. Everything below that conduit is already inside the shell from [`cold-core.md`](/hardware/assembly/cold-core.md) step 5: the line falls the shell's whole height down the port lane, turns once in the open onto the bore through the tank support ring, and bottoms on the PP010822E collet made up under the vessel's bottom plate — and from there the TAISHER elbow, the LTWFITTING barb adapter, the silicone stub and the sparge stone hang in the water column, so the gas enters **below** the liquid and dissolves on the way up.

### 2. Water path: rear-wall PP1208E → adapter chain → ASSE 1022 → split → V-K → SeaFlo → `water-in` cap conduit

The customer-facing rear-wall water inlet is a **1/4" JG QC bulkhead** (PP1208E — same SKU as the umbilical cluster). At install time the customer plugs the install-kit 1/4" LLDPE into the outboard PTC side — no factory work on that side beyond confirming the bulkhead is installed and the wall-side EPDM O-ring is seated. The factory work below closes everything inboard of the bulkhead. **From the ASSE 1022 outlet on, the path is all 1/4" LLDPE — it steps back up to 3/8" only at the SeaFlo's own two barbs.**

**The whole tap-water head end lies on one axis in the west lane.** The ASSE chain hangs fore and aft in the lane west of the pump, hugging the cold core's west face, inlet aft at the rear wall and flow running forward; the split stands one tube-length forward of its outlet on that same line, and the flow regulator one tube-length forward of the split. So `water-2` and `fluid-1` are lengths of stock with no corner in either, and the only mouth on the run that is not on that axis is the split's branch, which looks straight down at the storey the pump and the manifold stand on.

The PP010822E → GAGIRA → ASSE 1022 → PI4512F6S → PP061208W run and its vent stub are modeled as one piece in [`/hardware/reference/asse1022-assembly/`](/hardware/reference/asse1022-assembly/) — the stack-up, the three terminals (1/4" PTC in, 1/4" PTC out, vent), and which way the vent has to face.

Confirm the PP1208E is installed on the rear wall per [`enclosure-mechanical.md`](/hardware/assembly/enclosure-mechanical.md) with the 1/4" QC on both sides (push-to-connect, no tools required at install). The Waterdrop 15UC-UF filter is customer-side hardware — it ships in the install kit and mounts inline on the customer's 1/4" LLDPE upstream of this bulkhead ([`/hardware/ledger/bom.md`](/hardware/ledger/bom.md) §3) — so no factory work touches it.

**There is no `water-1`.** The chain stands off the rear wall by exactly what the union reaching through it leaves, so the bulkhead's inboard collet and the ASSE chain's inlet collet meet face to face down one axis with nothing between them to turn. The first tube in the machine is a length of stock cut to the two grips and swallowed whole by them: square-cut both ends, push fully into both collets, tug-test.

The ASSE 1022 inlet stack is factory-made up on the chain (modeled in the asse1022-assembly reference): the PP010822E's 1/4" NPT M side threads into the 1/4" NPT F side of the GAGIRA 316L SS reducing coupling (PTFE tape, snug + 1 turn past hand-tight); the GAGIRA's 3/8" NPT F side threads onto the ASSE 1022's 3/8" MPT inlet (tape on the MPT threads, snug + 1 turn). Multiplex flow arrow points away from the customer side. On the outlet, two John Guest fittings make up the flare-to-1/4" stack: the **PI4512F6S** 3/8" FFL × 3/8" PTC adapter draws up onto the ASSE 1022's 3/8" MFL outlet via its chrome swivel nut — flat-faced flare seal, no PTFE tape, quarter turn past hand-tight on the swivel only (never turn the body against the flare) — and the **PP061208W** 3/8"-stem × 1/4" PTC reducer pushes into its collet. The 3/8" run begins and ends inside those two fittings; the reducer's 1/4" PTC collet faces **forward** down the west lane, and the water leaves the backflow preventer at 1/4" OD.

**Lay the made-up chain into its trough.** The −X wall carries a stepped cradle for it (`enclosure._asse_cradle`, [`printed-parts/enclosure/enclosure/README.md`](/hardware/printed-parts/enclosure/enclosure/README.md) "Tap-water cradle") — three 120° V sections, one per section of the chain, cut so the brass barrel sits deepest and the steps either side of it are faces square to the axis. Bring the chain in from the east and press it west into the V until the barrel's two lower flats bed on the trough's two faces; the barrel's forward end face comes up against the step under the swivel nut with `ASSE_STEP_SLIP` of play, and aft the inlet collet butts the bulkhead's, which is where the run's own length is taken up. **The vent's clock is the fit.** The Multiplex's hex does not spin on the run, so a barrel bedded flat in the V has its vent pointing at the tray by construction — a chain that will not sit down in the trough is a chain rolled off its flats, and forcing it is how the drip ends up outside the coves.

Then close the mouth with **two 8" 50 lb zip ties** — barrel and trough make a 100 mm loop, past what a 4" tie closes, and an 8" is a 50 lb tie at 0.19" across the strap, which is why this cavity is cut wider than the others on the box — one in each band the vent leaves clear on the barrel, each **threaded down the bore through the trough's back before the chain goes in**, then laid across the channel the deck's own storey leaves under the top wall, so the chain comes down onto the strap rather than the strap being fished over it afterwards. Bring each end round under the barrel and up its east flank to buckle. **Brass barrel only** — a tie cinched on the JG acetal flare adapter or the PP reducer takes a collet out of round. Black UV-nylon, flush-cut, no proud tail, per [`cable-assemblies.md`](/hardware/assembly/cable-assemblies.md). They stop the chain leaving the trough eastward and nothing else: what carries it is the V, on two flats of the fitting's own hex, so a cut tie is a service step and not a failure.

Cut segment **water-2**: the chain's outlet collet forward down the lane into the **water split** — a JG PP0208E 1/4" union tee (modeled in [`/hardware/reference/water-split/`](/hardware/reference/water-split/)), seated on its own supply collet on the chain's line and tube-hung on this run. Its two run collets stay on that axis, supply aft and flavor tap forward, and its **branch is rolled to look straight down**. So water-2 has no corner in it at all: the two mouths face each other down one line, and what the length has to be is enough tube for both collets to take hold of. Square-cut, push fully home, tug-test.

Mount **V-K** — the Beduan 12 V NC fill/shutoff solenoid — on the cold core's crown on the machine's east flank, forward of the suction chain and **firing aft** into the collet that feeds the pump; its own frame already runs the flow inlet-forward / outlet-aft, so it takes no turn. Wire its coil per run DC-9 in [`ac-wiring-schedule.md`](/hardware/wiring/ac-wiring-schedule.md). Normally closed, so a leak alarm or a power loss stops all water reaching the carbonator. Cut segment **water-3** — off the split's **downward branch collet**, dropping out of the west lane onto the cap, then east across the machine and forward to turn into V-K's inlet. Three corners. Square-cut, push fully home, tug-test.

Make up the **suction chain** on the bench — a PP450822E (1/4" PTC × 1/4" NPT **F**) + MAACFLOW (1/4" NPT M × 3/8" hose barb) pair, PTFE tape on its one NPT joint, snug + 1 turn. Thread a **6" zip tie** through the rib on the cold core's cap lid east of the pump and leave it lying open across the channel — it goes in before the chain, the same way its twin's does. Lay the chain into that rib, its bore taking the **MAACFLOW's hex**: that hex is the only metal on this chain a strap may close on, and it is shorter than the rib, so the rib overhangs onto the barb one side and the PP socket the other and touches neither. Close the tie over the hex and flush-cut it. **The rib carries the chain and the tie only holds it down.** Cinch on the **MAACFLOW hex only** — never on the PP450822E's socket, hex or collet, which go out of round. Then cut segment **water-4**: V-K's outlet straight aft into the chain's collet. Both mouths lie on one plane and one column — V-K's cradle seat is what puts them there — so this is a length of tube and not a route, no corner. Square-cut the LLDPE and push fully into the PTC port.

Then cut segment **water-7**, ~[60 mm](SUCTION_STUB_LEN) of the 3/8" ID reinforced PVC. **The pump's barbs are molded into its head casting** — there is no port thread, nothing unscrews, and SEAFLO's catalog states the 21F001 90° barbed fitting will not fit a standard barb head. So the MAACFLOW's barb and the pump's barb do not join to each other: slip the stub over the pump's **east-facing** suction barb, let it turn and drop outboard to the chain's **aft-facing** MAACFLOW barb below, and close **two SAE #6 316 SS clamps** on it, one over each barb, screw heads turned where a driver reaches them. This stub and the pump's own barb are the only 3/8" on the suction side.

The flavor manifold draws its tap water off the split's **forward run collet**, and that run is straight too: segment **`fluid-1`** is one tube-length forward into the neoFit flow regulator's inlet, on the same axis and with no corner — ASSE, split, regulator down one line. From the regulator's outlet, segment **`fluid-2`** crosses the machine east and aft onto the folded deck and into V-A's inlet, four corners ([`/hardware/topology/fluid-topology.md`](/hardware/topology/fluid-topology.md) tube segments 1 and 2). No 3/8" barb tee, no reducers — the split is already 1/4".

The Multiplex atmospheric vent (1/4" barb, on the body) points at the floor: the chain's placement is a yaw and nothing else, and the vent has to face down because it weeps to atmosphere and that drip is the machine's cross-contamination telltale. The chain stands on the panel deck's own storey, the one its bulkhead crosses the back wall on, and the basin hangs off it — the rim one `VENT_GAP` of air under the chain's underside and the floor one `PAN_Z` below that ([`enclosure_assembly.py`](/hardware/manifold-layout/enclosure_assembly.py) `build_asse`, `pan_floor`) — so the drip falls the gap the basin was drawn for. It gets a short stub of Sealproof 1/4" ID × 3/8" OD clear PVC ([`/hardware/ledger/bom.md`](/hardware/ledger/bom.md) §3) pushed fully home on it. The drip falls off that tip into the internal drip pan + moisture sensor that [`enclosure-mechanical.md`](/hardware/assembly/enclosure-mechanical.md) put in channels over the pump's casting. Cut the stub to the overhang the placement leaves for it — `VENT_STUB_REACH` in [`asse1022_assembly.py`](/hardware/reference/asse1022-assembly/asse1022_assembly.py) — and keep the fall's column clear when you dress the C14's cordage through the band under the body. **Vent must drip to atmosphere, not be plumbed into a drain** — the vent is the mechanical telltale per [`/hardware/future.md`](/hardware/future.md) "Backflow vent monitoring". Two LOKMAN clamps on this run (vent barb + termination, if a termination barb is fitted — bare drip is also acceptable; the receiving pan does not need a tight fit).

Make up the **discharge chain** on the bench as one piece — MAACFLOW → GASHER → PP450822E, modeled in [`/hardware/reference/seaflo-discharge-chain/`](/hardware/reference/seaflo-discharge-chain/), [83.4 mm](DISCHARGE_CHAIN_LEN) made up. The MAACFLOW's 1/4" NPT M threads into the GASHER's female socket (PTFE tape, snug + 1) with the check's arrow pointing **away** from the pump; the GASHER's male stub threads into the PP450822E's female (PTFE tape, snug + 1). Thread a **6" zip tie** through the rib on the cold core's cap lid, west of the pump, and leave it lying open across the channel — it goes in before the chain does, because the channel's roof is the rib's own back and there is no reaching under it afterwards. Then lay the chain **fore-and-aft into that rib**, its bore taking the GASHER check's hex — **MAACFLOW barb aft**, looking back at the barb it takes; **PP450822E collet forward**, looking down the deck at the cap conduit it feeds. The bore takes any clock, so the check's hex needs no orienting; what it owes is the arrow. Close the tie over the barrel and flush-cut it. **The rib carries the chain and the tie only holds it down** — cut the tie and the chain still lies where it lies. Then cut segment **water-6**, ~[58 mm](DISCHARGE_STUB_LEN) of the 3/8" ID reinforced PVC: slip it over the pump's **west-facing** discharge barb, take it round the one corner onto the chain's **aft-facing** MAACFLOW barb, and close **two SAE #6 316 SS clamps**, one over each barb. The chain lies lower than the barb, so the stub turns in plan and falls on the same leg. The hose takes a [15.9 mm](PVC_BEND_R) bend radius — do not force it tighter than that turn, and keep the sweep off the wall.

Cut segment **water-5** from the 1/4" OD LLDPE reel — the tap-water path's only descent. The chain's PP450822E collet looks **forward** off the far end of the laid-down chain, and the `water-in` cap conduit opens on the lid a short reach ahead of it and a little to the east. The tube leaves the collet straight on its own axis and comes down onto the bore on the vertical it is drilled at — two gentle turns rather than one square one, each taking the full arc 1/4" LLDPE wants, so this line turns at stock, and its height only ever descends. Square-cut both ends, push fully home at the collet, lay the far end into the conduit, tug-test both.

The water path is now closed dry from the rear wall to the cold core's crown. Everything below that conduit is already inside the shell from [`cold-core.md`](/hardware/assembly/cold-core.md) step 5 — the top-plate TAISHER elbow with its PP010822E made up on the lateral female end, the tube running the shallow band between the top plate and the cap's floor, and the climb up the conduit. The carbonator is **filled high**: the water arrives above the liquid and falls into the headspace against the CO2 back-pressure.

### 3. Flavor manifold: V-A through V-J + Y-A, Y-B, Y-C, Y-D, Y-F, Y-G + pumps

Build to [`/hardware/topology/fluid-topology.md`](/hardware/topology/fluid-topology.md) segment-for-segment. The truth table there names every valve, every Y-junction, and every tube segment by number; this step describes the build cadence around that truth table, not its content.

Pre-build sequencing — bench sub-assembly vs. in-place build is **TBD; see Open items**. The remainder of this step is written for a bench sub-assembly that drops in afterward, with the understanding that an in-place build of the same parts list would carry the same ordering arguments.

On the bench, lay out all ten Beduan solenoid valves with their inlet/outlet legends visible. Lay out the six PP0208E Tees between them in the topology positions — **four limbs**, each one line of valves and tees butted collet to collet down its own column, the pack folded in two about the plane the four barb tees' front collets stand on ([`fluid-topology-limbs.mmd`](/hardware/topology/fluid-topology-limbs.mmd)). Pre-cut every tube segment from the [`/hardware/topology/fluid-topology.md`](/hardware/topology/fluid-topology.md) "Tube Segments" tables to length from the 1/4" OD LLDPE reel, square-cut both ends with the Mudder cutter, tag each segment with masking tape labelled by segment number from the topology doc.

Build outward from the two channel-A and channel-B pump rosettes:

- **Each pump barb takes a tee by its BRANCH**, which lays that tee's run across the head's face as the outboard half of a limb. Drop Y-C on pump B's suction and Y-F on pump A's (segments 11 and 21 in the topology doc), Y-D on pump B's discharge and Y-G on pump A's (segments 12 and 22). A barb is not a quick-connect: the tee seats on it directly, with no fitting and no tube between them.
- Build each limb outward from its barb tee, collet to collet: back through Y-C → V-C + V-E for channel A (mirror Y-F → V-D + V-H for channel B), and forward from Y-D through V-F and V-G for channel A (mirror Y-G → V-I + V-J for channel B). **Neither reservoir has a junction** — each carries two mouths of its own, so V-F and V-E reach reservoir A's fill and draw directly, and V-I and V-H reach reservoir B's, with nothing standing between.
- The shared upstream (tap-water V-A + hopper V-B + their junction with the selects pair through Y-A and Y-B) builds in last. Y-A and Y-B stand on the two inner limbs' own axes, one valve forward of the select each feeds, and their branches face each other across the mirror plane and meet on the crossbar. V-A's inlet comes from the **water split**'s forward run — flow regulator → V-A per [`/hardware/topology/fluid-topology.md`](/hardware/topology/fluid-topology.md) tube segments 1 and 2. V-B's inlet is the funnel bottom of the user-facing top-face hopper.

The two Kamoer KPHM400 peristaltic pumps mount in the enclosure (mount mechanism is an open item). Route 1/4" OD LLDPE through each pump's BPT barbs and around the rotor — LLDPE fits the barbs directly, zip-tied tight. Crimp a female faston receptacle onto each DC-5 harness conductor (+ and GND); these land on the pump-motor spade tabs, joining the harness at [`wiring.md`](/hardware/assembly/wiring.md).

The pump enclosure-mount mechanism is **TBD; see Open items** — top-of-Zone-C access beneath the funnel per [`/hardware/printed-parts/zone-c/README.md`](/hardware/printed-parts/zone-c/README.md), mechanism undrawn. The exact mechanism (push-fit + detent, screwed mounting plate, latch + cam) is not specified in the parts already committed.

Land the assembled flavor manifold into its enclosure position above the compressor and below the cold-core +Y face per the enclosure layout in [`/hardware/future.md`](/hardware/future.md) "Enclosure layout". Route tube segments through the pre-printed tube channels in the enclosure shell; zip-tie at every bundle pinch-point and at every long unsupported span.

**Four of the manifold's runs land on the cold core's own cap conduits**, and they are cut and closed with the rest of the manifold, not in step 4: **`fluid-14`** off V-F's outlet aft and down onto `reservoir-a-fill`, **`fluid-24`** off V-I's outlet onto `reservoir-b-fill`, and the two draws — **`fluid-16`** up off `reservoir-a` into V-E's inlet and **`fluid-26`** up off `reservoir-b` into V-H's inlet. A fill conduit lands on a bore in that reservoir's own cap, opening on its **headspace above the liquid**; a draw conduit stands over the **bulkhead at the bottom of the wet V**, the lowest drainable point in the cavity. Nothing that enters a reservoir can leave it without crossing the liquid, which is what the air-purge and clean-flush service modes in [`fluid-topology.md`](/hardware/topology/fluid-topology.md) run on.

Three more outlets exit upward to the rear-wall umbilical bulkheads in step 4:

- **Carbonated water riser** comes off the cold core's `carb-water-out` **cap conduit**, standing proud of the lid (already plumbed inside the shell per [`cold-core.md`](/hardware/assembly/cold-core.md) step 5). Build does not pick up this line in the flavor manifold step — it goes straight from the cold core to the rear-wall blue-ringed PP1208E in step 4 below.
- **Flavor A riser** comes from V-G outlet (the "pump to nozzle A" valve, segment 18 in the topology doc terminating at "Nozzle A").
- **Flavor B riser** comes from V-J outlet (segment 28 terminating at "Nozzle B").

### 4. Risers up to rear-wall umbilical bulkheads

Three risers climb to the three rear-wall PP1208E umbilical bulkheads per [`/hardware/printed-parts/enclosure/back-panel/README.md`](/hardware/printed-parts/enclosure/back-panel/README.md). **The three stand on one row** high on the rear wall, under the umbilical and above the water inlet: flavor B outboard on the west, flavor A beside it, and the **blue-ringed bulkhead** for carbonated water east of the pair.

- **Carbonated-water riser:** 1/4" OD blue LLDPE from the cold core's `carb-water-out` cap conduit to the blue-ringed PP1208E, in two lengths with the **DIGITEN flow meter** made up between them. The conduit opens **up** through the lid on the machine's east flank; segment **`carb-1`** climbs off it and leans inboard and aft over the deck, two corners, closing forward into the meter's inlet collet. The meter lies **fore-and-aft on the riser's own axis** at the port row's height, its pigtail boss facing **+X** toward the board it plugs into — its two collets are the riser's, so the body splits the run in two rather than hanging off it. It hangs in **two saddles printed off the top wall**, one over each collet barrel with nothing over the round body between them (`enclosure._digiten_saddles`, [`printed-parts/enclosure/enclosure/README.md`](/hardware/printed-parts/enclosure/enclosure/README.md) "Flow-meter saddles"): lift each barrel up into its bore until it beds all the way round, and close each saddle with **one 4" zip tie** threaded through the cavity in the middle of the rib over the bore and round under the barrel — arm and saddle make a 55 mm loop. **Both straps carry the meter** — a seat that opens downward holds nothing on its own — so tug-test each one and leave the outer [6 mm](DIGITEN_COLLET_FREE) of every barrel clear of the saddle, which is the collet ring the tube comes back out of. Aft of the meter, **`carb-1`'s own crossing is anchored** where it runs under the top wall: a rib printed off that wall carries a bore concentric with the tube (`enclosure._tube_anchors`), stopped on the tube's axis plane so its lip is a flat face one wall deep. Lay the tube up into the bore and close it with **one 4" zip tie** through the cavity in the middle of the rib, round under the tube and back — tube and rib make a [40.1 mm](CARB_1_LOOP) loop, the shortest on the box. The bore is what locates the run; the strap only stops it leaving. Segment **`carb-2`** is then a straight: the meter's outlet collet and the bulkhead's inboard collet face each other down one line at one height, so that length has no corner in it and is cut to the two grips. Square-cut every end, push fully into all four PTC ports, tug-test each. The CARGEN nitrile-rubber pipe insulation that wraps this line ([`/hardware/ledger/bom.md`](/hardware/ledger/bom.md) §9, 1/4" ID × 3/8" wall) goes on as one piece per length, cut to that run and slid on from the open end before either collet goes down (Open #7 below) — not in the 1-ft segments the above-counter umbilical uses. Insulation continuity from the cap conduit's mouth to the inboard face of the blue-ringed bulkhead is the target — that mouth is where the thermal mass of the cold core ends and the chilled water enters its multi-meter run to the faucet.
- **Flavor A riser:** segment **`fluid-18`**, 1/4" OD black LLDPE off V-G's outlet, up out of the manifold and aft across the machine into the middle bulkhead of the row — the longest single run in the appliance, four corners. No insulation — flavor lines run warm-in, warm-out at low duty cycle.
- **Flavor B riser:** segment **`fluid-28`**, the mirror off V-J's outlet into the west bulkhead, and the shorter of the two because its valve already stands on that side. Four corners. No insulation.

Black-into-either-black is unambiguous downstream of the manifold (per [`/hardware/printed-parts/enclosure/back-panel/README.md`](/hardware/printed-parts/enclosure/back-panel/README.md) "Umbilical port — tube identification"): both flavor tubes route through the same wall-side bundle and the user does not need to distinguish them at the wall.

### 5. Witness and tidy

Walk every joint built in this procedure. Tug-test every PTC connection. Confirm every LOKMAN clamp on the SeaFlo barb adapters is past finger-tight and the tube has pillowed. Confirm every NPT joint is past hand-tight and has PTFE tape showing at no more than half of the thread engagement (excess tape past half-engagement is a stress concentration). Confirm the Multiplex atmospheric vent stub leans aft and down as the chain's roll leaves it, and that the column straight down from its tip reaches the drip pan with no obstruction. Confirm both pumps sit in their enclosure position with leads routed clear for spade termination at [`wiring.md`](/hardware/assembly/wiring.md).

Zip-tie any tube run with a span over ~6" between fixturing points. The enclosure's printed tube channels carry most of the line, but zip-ties at the bend transitions and at the pump-mount boundary stabilize the manifold during transport.

The internal plumbing is now ready for [`wiring.md`](/hardware/assembly/wiring.md) (the wiring step lands every solenoid lead, every pump lead, every sensor lead in its electrical termination on the electronics shelf, but the plumbing itself does not change after this step).

## Open items

Procedure-level gaps that need answers before unit 1 ships:

1. **Pump enclosure-mount mechanism.** Tool-free swap from the top of Zone C, beneath the funnel, per [`/hardware/printed-parts/zone-c/README.md`](/hardware/printed-parts/zone-c/README.md) is undrawn. Candidate mechanisms: (a) push-fit + detent against a printed seat in the pump bay — relies on detent geometry alone; (b) screwed mounting plate in the bay with M3 SHCS + heat-set inserts — defeats the tool-free swap; (c) cam-latch or lever that engages a printed feature on the mount — requires a TBD off-the-shelf latch. Pick once the mount geometry is drawn.
2. **Flavor manifold: bench sub-assembly vs. in-place build.** Procedure as written assumes bench sub-assembly; pre-cut every segment per [`/hardware/topology/fluid-topology.md`](/hardware/topology/fluid-topology.md), build outward from the pump rosettes on the bench, then drop the assembled manifold into the enclosure. Plausibility: the manifold's footprint is ~150 × 200 mm above the compressor; the enclosure has access from the top face (open during this step, before the funnel installs) and the front face. An in-place build of the same parts list is geometrically defensible — every joint is reachable from the front or top access faces — and avoids the lift-and-place step that a bench manifold requires (the manifold has 10 valves across 26 segments, eleven of them collets butted face to face; the lift puts every termination at risk of an unseat). Decision is downstream of the first unit's bench cycle time vs in-place cycle time.
3. ~~**Tap-water tap-point into the flavor manifold (V-A inlet).**~~ **CLOSED.** The flavor tap comes off the **water split** — the JG PP0208E 1/4" union tee one tube-length forward of the ASSE 1022's outlet (`reference/water-split/`): its forward run collet → `fluid-1` → neoFit flow regulator → `fluid-2` → V-A ([`/hardware/topology/fluid-topology.md`](/hardware/topology/fluid-topology.md) tube segments 1 and 2). The ASSE leaves at 1/4", so the split and the tap run are both plain 1/4" PTC — no 3/8"-to-1/4" branch chain. See [`/hardware/ledger/bom.md`](/hardware/ledger/bom.md) §3.
4. ~~**Union-elbow count in the warm-side line runs.**~~ **CLOSED — none.** The placed pack turns no line on a fitting: every valve is butted collet to collet down its limb, every junction is a Tee, and each pump barb is taken by a tee's branch. [`/hardware/ledger/bom.md`](/hardware/ledger/bom.md) carries no per-unit elbow row, and [`_bom_sync.py`](/hardware/scripts/_bom_sync.py) asserts against `manifold_layout.JOINS` so that a posed elbow puts the row back rather than going uncounted.
5. ~~**Clamp quantity per build.**~~ **CLOSED.** Five: **four SAE #6 316 SS** on the two pump-port stubs (two per stub — one over the pump's molded barb, one over the adapter's) and **one LOKMAN 10–16 mm** on the vent stub. The two sizes are not interchangeable — the reinforced PVC stretches to ~17 mm over a barb, past the LOKMAN's 16 mm close. [`/hardware/ledger/bom.md`](/hardware/ledger/bom.md) §3 commits both lines.
6. **A cradle for the WR1110 body.** The regulator threads onto nothing — it stands on its own, fed and drained by tube, so nothing holds it at all until a cradle exists. It has a station to be held at: [`enclosure_assembly.py`](/hardware/manifold-layout/enclosure_assembly.py) seats it on its own inlet collet one `co2-1` forward of the check's stub, and the scorecard carries its clearances to the neighbours around it; its `held` row reads `none`. So a cradle has a measured datum to reach and measured room to stand in; what it does not have is a body. [`enclosure-mechanical.md`](/hardware/assembly/enclosure-mechanical.md) owns the part. Flagged here because this procedure is downstream of that mechanical step but depends on its existence; coordinate with the mechanical doc.
7. ~~**Cabinet-internal carbonated-water insulation segment count.**~~ **CLOSED — cut-to-length pieces, not 1-ft segments.** The cold-core-exit → blue-ringed-bulkhead run has a fixed length per build, is closed entirely in-shop and is never field-trimmed, so the 1-ft granularity that serves the above-counter umbilical buys nothing here and would leave up to 12" of bare tube on the coldest part of the line. The riser is authored in [`_lines.py`](/hardware/manifold-layout/_lines.py) as `carb-1` and `carb-2`, and the flow meter between them means **two pieces, not one** — each cut to its own run's developed length, which the enclosure-assembly build prints per line, and slid on before the collets go down. [`bom.md`](/hardware/ledger/bom.md) §9 carries 84 in of CARGEN per build: the 24 in this riser takes, which still covers both pieces, plus the umbilical's five 1-ft segments.
