# J2 · Manifold B bench assembly

[Interactive 3D map and build sequence](https://homesodamachine.com/cost#j2-harness)
in the cost page's assembly section. The website reads the pin map, steps and
time estimate from [`j2.json`](j2.json).

The reference is the complete integrated-machine J2 harness: V-I, V-J,
condenser fan and V-K. In the [Lillium build](../lillium-cutover.md#7-wiring),
FAN and OUT3 are folded back and labelled, with only V-I and V-J connected.
The circuit assignments come from the main board and wiring schedule.
The ribbon-edge assignment and illustrated dressing are proposed bench
conventions; neither records the physical details of the completed sample.

## Ribbon and contact map

Cut two 650 mm lengths of 22 AWG black 3P silicone ribbon. Name them A and B.
On each, conductor 1 is the white-striped edge; count away from that edge
before separating conductors. Keep each stripe visible on exposed ends.

Identify housing cavity 1 by its circuit mark and the board's COM pin.
The wire-entry and mating-face views mirror each other. The interactive map
shows both views with the same electrical numbering.

| J2 cavity | Net | Ribbon lead | Bench cut | Far end |
| ---: | --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| 1 | COM / +12 V | A1, white stripe | 300 mm | Ferrule into Wago 221-415 |
| 2 | FAN | A2 | 650 mm provisional | Fan negative; donor interface unlocated |
| 3 | OUT4 | A3 discarded | — | Empty cavity, no contact |
| 4 | OUT3 | B1, white stripe | 150 mm | V-K negative, female disconnect |
| 5 | OUT2 | B2 | 550 mm | V-J negative, female disconnect |
| 6 | OUT1 | B3 | 550 mm | V-I negative, female disconnect |

Peel away and discard A3. Peel B1 away for the short V-K branch before
gathering A1, A2, B2 and B3 into the descending trunk. Five
SXH-001T-P0.6 contacts seat in one XHP-6, in positions 1, 2, 4, 5 and 6.

COM feeds one Wago port. Four additional black bulk-wire branches, each
ferruled at the Wago, feed V-I positive, V-J positive, fan positive and V-K
positive. Prepare four 450 mm branch blanks per the wiring schedule and trim
at fitting. The native junction review recommends 300 mm for V-I, V-J and
V-K and provisionally 450 mm for the fan positive. These shared positive
branches are separate from each device's switched return to J2.

## One complete bench job

1. **Cut and identify — 2 minutes.** Identify both striped edges, label the ribbon
   groups, peel and trim to the table, and prepare positive branch stock.
2. **JST end — 3 minutes.** Splay, strip, crimp, inspect, seat and lightly tug
   the five contacts. Verify the deliberately empty middle cavity.
3. **Slip-on dressing — 1 minute.** Slide on nominal 1/2-inch black PET mesh
   and two heat-shrink end collars before fitting large far-end terminals.
   Stage two black zip ties, one at each mesh end. Keep the installed shared
   flat ribbon corridor bare.
4. **Far ends and final dressing — 3 minutes.** Crimp five ferrules and six
   valve disconnects; land all five Wago ports. The two fan connections remain
   provisional pending identification of its donor lead. Set the heat-shrink
   collars, close the ties and flush-cut tails. Exact sleeve length, collar
   sizes and tie locations remain a bench layout proposal.
5. **Ring out and label — 1 minute.** With board and devices disconnected,
   verify all five populated pin destinations, continuity through COM to
   the four positives, and isolation of every separate net. Check cavity 3
   empty. Label the housing `J2 · MANIFOLD B`.

**Unit 25 estimate: 10 attended minutes** for one practiced person completing
the harness start to finish with the same hand tools. The map, cut lengths,
terminal choices and material locations are settled; shared batch setup is
amortized. Individual steps are allocations of this complete bench job.

The work-content estimate allows roughly 5–6 minutes for five JST contacts and
thirteen far-end crimps (five ferrules and eight device disconnects), and
4–5 minutes for cutting, dressing, Wago assembly, continuity and labeling.
Five minutes would leave too little time for the complete job; fifteen minutes
is an upper planning allowance. The cost page uses ten minutes, or $16.67 at
the ledger's $100/hour rate. This is a theoretical cycle estimate, unmeasured.
Development, lookup, learning and in-chassis fitting are outside the
recurring cycle.

The builder reports approximately one hour for the first complete reference
piece, mostly spent looking up the build map. That observation records
development context; no individual production steps have been timed.

The [labor ledger](../../ledger/labor.md#6-wiring) includes this ten-minute
J2 job once among its eleven complete bench harnesses. The cost page's Wiring
section visualizes J2 and the five-minute J13 pump harness; the remaining
harnesses have individual time estimates. Installation and AC checks have
separate allowances.

## Termination and dressing limits

The [cable schedule](../cable-assemblies.md) calls for 4.8 mm and 6.3 mm female
disconnects but does not identify the width for each valve. Match each actual
tab before selecting the disconnect. Both fan-end disconnects in the schedule
are provisional: the [native routing evidence](../../wiring/manifold-junction-clearance-check.json)
ends at the condenser crown and does not locate the donor lead or connector.

The nominal mesh size is unqualified on the flat ribbon. The
[installed wiring schedule](../../wiring/ac-wiring-schedule.md#loom-terminations)
keeps J1 and the four descending J2 conductors bare in their shared straight
corridor. The 3D workbench illustrates components and electrical topology,
not a qualified installed sleeve path, terminal boot envelope or cable length
scale.

The [installed enclosure wiring model](../../wiring/README.md) places the same
pin identities in the populated machine and records each route's current status.
