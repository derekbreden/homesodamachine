# J13 · Pumps fixed-half bench assembly

[Interactive 3D harness](https://homesodamachine.com/cost#j13-harness) in the
cost page's wiring assembly section. The conductor records, sequence and
unit-25 estimate live in [`j13.json`](j13.json).

This finished piece is one 350 mm 22 AWG black 4P silicone ribbon, with four
JST-XH contacts in an XHP-4 at the PCB end and four insulated solder joints on
the fixed male pogo half at the other end. Its four conductors remain separate
throughout. The cartridge-side half and its motor-tab leads are a separate
bench assembly.

## End-to-end conductor map

The white-striped edge is A1. Count away from that edge before splitting the
ribbon. This stripe assignment is the bench convention for this guide, not
an observation of an assembled sample. The electrical and solder-tail orders
come from the [cable schedule](../cable-assemblies.md#contact-pair--dc-5) and
[J13 board declaration](../../pcb/pcba/pcba.tsx).

Read pogo order from **machine +X toward machine −X**, using the pair's
attracting orientation. With the pair mated by hand, mark the corresponding
ends of both halves that will stand at machine −X. Face-on views from the
two sides reverse the apparent left-to-right order. The marked end identifies
BM1; the opposite end identifies striped AM2.

## One complete bench job

1. **Cut and identify — 0.5 minute.** Cut one 350 mm 4P ribbon. Identify
   A1–A4 and split the pogo end back 20 mm. Mark the connector half's machine
   −X end using its mated orientation.
2. **JST end — 1.5 minutes.** Splay and strip the four ends, crimp four
   SXH-001T-P0.6 contacts and inspect both barrels. Seat and lightly tug each
   contact in XHP-4 cavities 1–4. Cavity 1 matches the native PCB's AM2 label
   and the housing circuit-1 mark.
3. **Pogo end — 2 minutes.** Slip on four individual heat-shrink sleeves
   before soldering. With front-top loose, feed the free pogo end through the
   empty bulkhead lead bore. Tin and solder each conductor to its mapped male tail.
   Slide and shrink each sleeve over its own joint.
4. **Ring out and label — 1 minute.** With the piece disconnected, check each
   JST cavity to its matching male pogo pin and all six conductor pairs for
   isolation. Inspect the four insulated joints and JST contact retention.
   Label the housing `J13 · PUMPS` and stage it for installation.

**Unit 25 estimate: 5 attended minutes** for a practiced person completing
this piece start to finish, with the settled map, materials and hand tools
and loose front-top ready. Shared setup is amortized. Ten minutes is the upper planning allowance.
The step allocations are theoretical and unmeasured. Development, lookup,
bulkhead mounting, cabinet routing and the separate cartridge-side cable are
outside this bench cycle.

## Slip-on parts and installation boundary

Each solder joint gets its own heat-shrink sleeve. The fixed flat ribbon
stays bare at the contact pair, through the bulkhead passage and along the
bulkhead crown below the funnel frame; this piece has no mesh collars or cable ties.

The [front-top assembly](../enclosure-mechanical.md) requires feeding the free
pogo end forward through the empty bulkhead's lead bore **before** soldering
the male half. The visual shows the finished electrical piece outside the
bulkhead so its four tails remain visible. On the machine, finish those
joints with the half outside its seat, then insulate, draw back the slack and
mount the male half. The five-minute bench job assumes front-top is loose and
ready; passing the bare cable end through its bore before soldering belongs
to that cycle. Drawing back the mounting slack, securing the half with its
fasteners and completing the installed routing have separate assembly allowances.

The accepted [physical connector observation](../../reference/yyfkgcp-pogo-4p/physical-observations.json)
covers fit and mating/compression in the printed test piece. It does not
record four-channel continuity of a completed J13 harness. Remove appliance
power before mating or parting the cartridge service contact.

The [installed enclosure wiring model](../../wiring/README.md) places the same
pin identities in the populated machine and records each route's current status.
