# J13 · Pumps bench assembly

[Interactive 3D harness](https://homesodamachine.com/cost#j13-harness) in the
cost page's wiring assembly section. The conductor records, sequence and
unit-25 estimate live in [`j13.json`](j13.json).

This finished piece is one 500 mm 22 AWG black 4P silicone ribbon, with four
JST-XH contacts in an XHP-4 at the PCB end and four insulated female Faston
receptacles at the pump end, two on each pump's motor tabs. Its four conductors
remain separate throughout. The ribbon runs from J13 below the funnel frame,
through front-top's common rear electrical passage below the display ribbon,
and through the integral upper mount's transverse channel into the two motor
terminal rooms.

## End-to-end conductor map

The white-striped edge is A1. Count away from that edge before splitting the
ribbon. This stripe assignment is the bench convention for this guide, not
an observation of an assembled sample. The electrical order comes from the
[cable schedule](../cable-assemblies.md#pump-leads--dc-5) and
[J13 board declaration](../../pcb/pcba/pcba.tsx).

| JST cavity / PCB label | Ribbon lead | Pump-end pair | Faston receptacle on |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 · AM2 | A1 · white stripe | +X pair | Pump A motor tab 2 |
| 2 · AM1 | A2 | +X pair | Pump A motor tab 1 |
| 3 · BM2 | A3 | −X pair | Pump B motor tab 2 |
| 4 · BM1 | A4 | −X pair | Pump B motor tab 1 |

The pump ribbon passes the common rear electrical mouth with its striped edge at
**machine +X**, below the display ribbon. Inside the integral upper mount, the
striped pair turns +X through the transverse channel to pump A; the other pair
turns −X to pump B.

## One complete bench job

1. **Cut and identify — 0.5 minute.** Cut one 500 mm 4P ribbon. Identify
   A1–A4, peel the pump end into its two pairs back 70 mm, A1/A2 for pump A
   and A3/A4 for pump B, and part each pair's last 20 mm.
2. **JST end — 1.5 minutes.** Splay and strip the four ends, crimp four
   SXH-001T-P0.6 contacts and inspect both barrels. Seat and lightly tug each
   contact in XHP-4 cavities 1–4. Cavity 1 matches the native PCB's AM2 label
   and the housing circuit-1 mark.
3. **Faston end — 2 minutes.** With front-top loose, feed the free pump end
   forward through the common rear electrical mouth and out underneath. Keep
   the XHP-4 aft of the mouth. Strip the four ends and crimp four insulated
   female Faston receptacles, matched to the motor tabs' width. Inspect and
   tug each crimp.
4. **Ring out and label — 1 minute.** With the XHP-4 unplugged and the Fastons
   not yet landed, check each JST cavity to its Faston and all six conductor
   pairs for isolation. Inspect the JST contact retention. Label the housing
   `J13 · PUMPS`.

**Unit 25 estimate: 5 attended minutes** for a practiced person completing
this piece start to finish, with the settled map, materials and hand tools,
front-top loose. Shared setup is amortized. Ten minutes
is the upper planning allowance. The step allocations are theoretical and
unmeasured. Development, lookup, landing the Fastons, installing the pumps and retainer, and
cabinet routing are outside this bench cycle.

## Slip-on parts and installation boundary

The piece has no heat-shrink sleeves, mesh collars or cable ties. Its flat
ribbon stays bare below the funnel frame, through the shared rear electrical
mouth and through the transverse channel to the motor terminal rooms.

Prepare both pumps' tube tips in the loose lower retainer and remove the tube
setting jig. With front-top loose and the bare pump end threaded through its
common passage, complete and ring out the harness, then land the Fastons while
the pumps' tabs are accessible below the shell. Raise the prepared pump and
retainer unit motor-first, drawing wire slack aft through the shared mouth.
Keep the leads in the terminal channel and clear of pump bearings and retainer
seats, then secure the retainer from underneath. This happens before plumbing
and enclosure closure ([internal plumbing §3](../internal-plumbing.md#3-flavor-manifold-v-a-through-v-j--y-a-y-b-y-c-y-d-y-f-y-g--pumps)).

The five-minute bench job covers threading, crimps, ring-out and labeling with
front-top loose. Landing the Fastons, installing the pumps and retainer, and
dressing the free reserve have separate assembly allowances. The J13 housing
seats through the funnel opening after enclosure closure.

Unplug J13 by its housing before separating enclosure pieces. The
[installed enclosure wiring model](../../wiring/README.md) places the same
pin identities in the populated machine and records each route's current status.
