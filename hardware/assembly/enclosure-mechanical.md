# Enclosure Mechanical Assembly

The production procedure for standing the machine's pack inside the printed enclosure and closing it around the pack: refrigeration stratum on the floor, cold core behind it, the power column against the +X wall, the collet plate in through front-top's seam face, the four quadrants telescoped and cross-pinned, the ASSE drip pan in through its own slot, the display let into the facet. No internal plumbing runs, no AC/DC wiring runs — those are downstream in [`internal-plumbing.md`](/hardware/assembly/internal-plumbing.md) and [`wiring.md`](/hardware/assembly/wiring.md). The output is the mechanical canvas everything else lands on.

The arrangement is [`enclosure_assembly.py`](/hardware/manifold-layout/enclosure_assembly.py): it places every body and sizes the box around them, and `enclosure-assembly.scorecard.json` beside it carries every port, every run and every clearance the placement holds. Design intent lives in [`/hardware/README.md`](/hardware/README.md) "Enclosure (back to front)", "Refrigeration", "User-facing surfaces", and "Safety". Part-level READMEs are the source of truth for every component this procedure handles; this document is the build-cadence wrapper.

## The box

Four printed PET-GF15 quadrants — `enclosure-front-bottom`, `enclosure-front-top`, `enclosure-back-bottom`, `enclosure-back-top` — measuring [215 × 462 × 361 mm](BOX_SIZE) over the outside ([`enclosure.py`](/hardware/printed-parts/enclosure/enclosure/enclosure.py)), plus the `enclosure-pump-cartridge` that slides out of the front pair's bay with both pumps aboard and the flat-printed ceiling panel that slides into back-top. Every standing face is a wall of a quadrant: the rear face is the back pieces' own +Y wall, the display facet is a 45° chamfer in `enclosure-front-top`'s top-front arris, the flat front below the facet is the pump cartridge's face, and the funnel opening is cut in the top wall, both top pieces taking their share of it. Only the broad rear ceiling field is separate, because printing it in the mouth-down back-top would bridge the open service bay at the top of a 195 mm print.

- **The Y seam is stated at [200](Y_SEAM).** The front pieces' +Y walls telescope into the back pieces — a proud tongue on the side walls and ceiling, and a full-thickness, 45°-scarfed tongue inside the floor slab, so the floor stays flat where the cold core rides across it. Interlocking screw bosses cross-pin the seam from the ±X exteriors, at a level for each end of each piece.
- **The Z seam is one stated plane, both columns, at [160](Z_SEAM_FRONT)** — the seam line runs level round the box and the four pieces meet at a four-way corner on each side wall. Both columns run solid there (the refrigeration stratum and flavour deck forward, the cold core and service bay aft), so the seam runs through each on the lane its lip's ring keeps of the pack. The bottom pieces carry the lip and the socket collars, the top pieces the pins, and four X-axis screws cross each seam.
- **The wall is a fact about a face, not about the box.** [3 mm](WALL_T) is the section a piece starts from and every exterior wall carries at least [6 mm](LIP_UNDERWALL), each grown INWARD — the stated silhouette and `interior_x` both stand still, and only the piece carrying a section knows about it. A bottom piece gets its [6 mm](LIP_UNDERWALL) for nothing down all three of its lipped sides, the lip's own skin carried to the slab. On the top pieces it is stated per face: [9 mm](FRONT_TOP_FLANK) on `enclosure-front-top`'s ±X flanks, [9 mm](BACK_TOP_FLANK) on `enclosure-back-top`'s, [6 mm](BACK_TOP_WALL) on its +Y wall and 6 mm on its two fixed ceiling strips. The removable ceiling carries an 11 mm structural envelope and 6 × 6 mm tongues, all inward of the same exterior top; its pockets preserve the unchanged z = 352 pack lane. The front face is [9 mm](FRONT_WALL); the complete flavour pack stands behind it with the removable pump face on that same exterior plane. **The floor is the one section that grows OUTWARD** — [6 mm](FLOOR_T) of slab under both bottom pieces, and the stated height is struck to its underside, so the slab stands in the silhouette rather than in the cavity and everything the pack sets on the floor stays where it was. What noses into a section is never moved for it: the wall gives that station back as a stated, 45°-chamfered relief — a Wago well, the ASSE drip pan's sleeve, the PRV chase's rib, the CO2 nut's clamp stack — and `back_wall_t_at`, `box-front` and `back-top-flank-clear` are what read those back against the pack.
- Each quadrant beds on its own Z− face — the bottoms on their closed floors, the tops on their seam rims, mouth down. The lower pump cradle and top clamp each take their own plate; `bed-fit` on the card holds all six printed pieces to the H2C's bed.

**Width is a stated bound, not a consequence of what stands inside.** [215 mm](APPLIANCE_W), struck symmetric about x = 0 — the axis the whole pack is centred on — exactly as the height and the rear plane are stated. No body sets it: a narrower body taking a wider one's place does not make the machine narrower, and a pack that outgrows it reads red on `box-width` rather than quietly resizing the appliance. What that check requires OF a body on the floor is one [14 mm](SIDE_BAND) boss chain of clearance to the ±X walls **at the depths the seam's bosses stand there** — a body standing on the slab spans the interior wall to wall, so a wall on its face would leave the seam's mouths, plugs and socket collars nowhere to stand. Held off by their own reach, every one of them seats at full section and the bodies seat against the band. The reach is in Y and in Z as much as in X: each boss is a block [16.2 mm](SOCKET_OD) across and the same tall at its own station, so beside one — and over or under one — the band is the wall's own air, which is where a body hung on the flank is mounted rather than held off. What fences that flank at every height is not a boss at all but the standing corner's own relief. The widest thing on the slab is the **cold core**: yawed a quarter turn so its short face crosses the machine, it still measures [181](CORE_X) against the mated compressor and condenser's [174](STRATUM_X).

## Scope

In: the integrated refrigerant-loop assembly (output of [`refrigerant-loop.md`](/hardware/assembly/refrigerant-loop.md) — cold core with wound coil + plumbed compressor + condenser/fan, charged and run-up-verified); the six printed enclosure pieces and the laser-cut collet plate (the nameplate is not one of them — it goes on at [`finish-pack-ship.md`](/hardware/assembly/finish-pack-ship.md) §3, after the machine is dry) ([`/hardware/printed-parts/enclosure/`](/hardware/printed-parts/enclosure/)); the six bodies seated through the +Y wall of back-top (three umbilical PP1208E unions, the tap-water PP1208E, the C14 mains inlet, and the CO2 neoFit ABU44) each over a printed bulkhead ring, blue on the carbonated-water one per [`/hardware/printed-parts/enclosure/y-wall-of-back-top/README.md`](/hardware/printed-parts/enclosure/y-wall-of-back-top/README.md); the ASSE drip pan + moisture sensor for the backflow vent; the Waveshare 4.3B display; the bench-built unpowered power column (output of [`power-column.md`](/hardware/assembly/power-column.md)).

Out: a complete mechanical chassis — refrigeration stratum standing on the floor slab with the compressor bolted down to it, cold core seated flat on that slab behind it, packed off the +Y wall with the loop's two drawn legs crossing the lane in front of it, the power column standing on the core's cap against the +X wall, the four quadrants telescoped and cross-pinned with every +Y wall's body installed (`enclosure-back-top` last — see Open items 4), the collet plate standing in its slot through the bay floor, its top edge up on the cap's land and bearing on the tee wall, the pump bay open, ASSE drip pan in its sleeve, display in its facet. Ready for [`internal-plumbing.md`](/hardware/assembly/internal-plumbing.md) and [`wiring.md`](/hardware/assembly/wiring.md).

Not in scope: internal plumbing runs through the cabinet — the CO2 chain off the ABU44, the tap-water chain off its union, the flavor manifold and the three risers — see [`internal-plumbing.md`](/hardware/assembly/internal-plumbing.md); AC + DC + signal wiring runs — see [`wiring.md`](/hardware/assembly/wiring.md); installing the wound coil onto the carbonator (already done in [`cold-core.md`](/hardware/assembly/cold-core.md) step 1); refrigerant-loop integration (already done in [`refrigerant-loop.md`](/hardware/assembly/refrigerant-loop.md)); the nameplate's signing and application, and seating the silicone funnel in the funnel opening — both happen at [`finish-pack-ship.md`](/hardware/assembly/finish-pack-ship.md), not here.

## Procedure

**The box closes in slides, and the slides fix the order.** Each column's top slides onto its bottom on the Z seams' hooked rails, and **the two columns are mirrored**: front-top enters fore of home, in open air ahead of the box, and draws AFT; back-top enters aft of home, in open air behind it, and draws FORE. Each arrives from the end of the box it stands at, so neither is lifted over the other. A top slides over everything its bottom holds, so the front column closes over the seated stratum with the flavour pack riding the moving piece (§4), and the back column closes over **nothing**: its tub is bare when back-top comes on (§6), because the cold core cannot pass under that piece's own +Y wall. The core enters afterward, through the mouth — the closed back assembly rides forward over the core where it stands tethered to the stratum, one continuous motion that ends in the Y seam's own telescope — and the four Y-seam screws close the box. The same four open it. Only the ASSE drip pan goes in from outside, last, through its own slot — which is what that slot is for.

### 1. Stage the printed pieces

Print-inspect all seven pieces — the four quadrants, the lower pump cradle and its top clamp, and the ceiling panel that closes `enclosure-back-top`'s roof ([`/hardware/printed-parts/enclosure/ceiling-panel/`](/hardware/printed-parts/enclosure/ceiling-panel/README.md)) — plus this unit's nameplate, and stage the collet plate (the laser-cut 1/8" 316 flat, [`/hardware/manifold-layout/`](/hardware/manifold-layout/README.md) `collet-plate.dxf`) with them. The plate goes into `enclosure-front-top` at §4, before that column closes; the pump cartridge goes in downstream; the pumps they serve are landed in [`internal-plumbing.md`](/hardware/assembly/internal-plumbing.md). Wipe down every interior face; remove brim residue and any stray support material at the +Y wall's bores, the rails' heads and channels — a bead in a channel is a 100 mm slide that binds at one station — the Y seam's lips and socket collars, and — above all — **the floor slab the cold core lands on**. The core bears on that slab across its whole footprint, so a bead of brim under it is a high spot, not a blemish. Both halves of the floor joint print on their bed faces: the front tongue is full thickness until its 45° scarf nose, and the back keeps the matching bed-side wedge. Set the nameplate aside in the unit's build folder with its two M3×8; it does not go on at this step (see [`finish-pack-ship.md`](/hardware/assembly/finish-pack-ship.md)). **The display's two printed parts go in that folder with it**, not loose on the bench: the cover plate ([`/hardware/printed-parts/enclosure/display-cover/`](/hardware/printed-parts/enclosure/display-cover/README.md)) and, under it, the TPU gasket ring that goes between the plate's lap and the display's glass ([`/hardware/printed-parts/enclosure/display-gasket/`](/hardware/printed-parts/enclosure/display-gasket/README.md)). Both go on at §8, and the ring is soft, black and one wipe from being swept off a bench unnoticed — a display closed without it is a display the cover plate does not hold.

Install ruthex M3 heat-set inserts everywhere the pieces call for them — the Y seam's four socket collars (the Z seams take no screw and no insert: their retention is the rails' own hooked heads), the C14's two in the fore face of the +Y wall's inlet tunnel, the two bored under the display facet's inset floor on `enclosure-front-top` that the cover plate's screws pull against, the four posts standing off the floor slab under the compressor's feet, the two in the condenser fin's fingers on `enclosure-front-bottom`'s +X wall that the block's aft flanges are drawn down onto, the nameplate's two on the +Y wall, and the [17](EAST_BOSSES) +X wall bosses the power column bolts to. Standard heat-set procedure: soldering iron on the insert, press straight down until flush. **The floor's four are M5** and every other insert in the box is M3 — a second tip off the same T18 kit, and the only station in the appliance that takes it. All inserts go in *before* anything is in the box, while there is bench access from every face.

**The ceiling is the first part installed in back-top.** Before any connection body, anchor tenant or power-column body is mounted, slide the bare panel aft through the open Y-seam mouth with both tongues fitted in their dados until its aft edge lands on the +Y wall. The uninterrupted dados are the complete joint; the panel takes no insert, bore or separate keeper hardware. This ceiling is the last part removed from a stripped back-top and the first part installed in a new one.

Dry-fit the seams with the box empty: slide each column's top onto its bottom — front-top entering fore of front-bottom's front wall and running AFT to its stop blocks, back-top entering aft of back-bottom's rear wall and running FORE to its own — then part them again and telescope front-bottom into back-bottom, front-top into back-top. Confirm every slide runs its whole travel without shaving, every foot lands on its stop block, and every Y-seam plug drops into its socket. **Then take it all apart again** — the pack goes in first.

### 2. Seat the +Y wall's six connection bodies

All six land in `enclosure-back-top`, on the bench, before that piece goes anywhere near the pack. This is the easier work surface, and it lets each bulkhead's torque load react against a wall that is unobstructed from inside.

**Every hole in this wall is struck on its own fitting's mouth**, so a bore and the barrel that passes it cannot land on two different columns ([`enclosure_assembly.py`](/hardware/manifold-layout/enclosure_assembly.py) `y_wall_ports`). The stations:

| Body | Station (x, z) | Wall opening | Seating |
|---|---|---|---|
| Umbilical unions × 3 (PP1208E) | [-78.07 / -37.81 / -37.81](UMBILICAL_STATIONS) at z [336.2](PORT_ROW_Z) | Ø[18](PORT_HOLE_D) round | Flange on the OUTER face, threading through, nut clamped inside |
| Tap-water union (PP1208E) | x [-78.07](WATER_BACK_X), z [336.2](WATER_BACK_Z) | Ø[18](PORT_HOLE_D) round | Same; its inboard collet is what the ASSE chain butts against |
| C14 mains inlet | [x 66.9, z 336.2](C14_BACK) | Rounded rectangle | Flange on the inlet tunnel's fore face, two M3 into that face, shroud back up its bore |
| CO2 ABU44 bulkhead | [x 2.45, z 336.2](CO2_BACK) | Ø[17.86](CO2_HOLE_D) round | Flange on its red ring, panel nut inside; the GASHER stands one hop of tube ahead of its inboard collet in [`internal-plumbing.md`](/hardware/assembly/internal-plumbing.md) |

**The CO2 comes in at the +Y wall**, not at the front — carbonator, tap and umbilical all land on the one face the customer reaches at install. No connection is cut in the front wall: its one opening is the pump bay, and the pump cartridge's face closes that.

**The drip pan's dock goes into the same piece on the same bench.** Solder two leads to the tails of the male half of the JHYOSSTHI magnetic pogo pair ([`/hardware/reference/jhyossthi-pogo-dock/`](/hardware/reference/jhyossthi-pogo-dock/)), feed them down the lead channel that opens in the floor of the pan sleeve's backstop, and press the pill into the backstop's pocket from the **east** — nose first, west, until its shoulders meet the window's. Pot the pocket's mouth behind it. The pump will stand a millimetre off that face once the core is in, so this is the one moment the pocket is reachable; the leads hang free under the sleeve until [`wiring.md`](/hardware/assembly/wiring.md).

The four PP1208E unions stand in a **rectangle** on two columns [40.26 mm](UMBILICAL_PITCH) apart: the carbonated-water and tap-water unions on the upper storey at z [336.2](PORT_ROW_Z), the two flavor unions on their own below. A JG bulkhead nut is [22.86](PORT_NUT_D) mm across the face and a chain of three made-up nuts occupies [82.58](PORT_CHAIN_3) mm, so the pitch leaves a socket room to get on each nut with its neighbour already made up. The C14's flange is [49.77](C14_FLANGE_W) mm wide and stands east of them on its own storey.

Install, in this order:

- **The four JG unions** — flange + EPDM O-ring bearing on the wall's outer face, threading through the bore, nut drawn up from inside. Mechanical capture only; no wall-side gasket. Push-to-connect on both sides, so no tool touches either collet. Confirm the blue-ringed union is the one at [-37.81](UMBILICAL_CARB_X) — the user-facing rule at install is "blue tube into the blue-ringed bulkhead" per [+Y wall of back-top "Umbilical port — tube identification"](/hardware/printed-parts/enclosure/y-wall-of-back-top/README.md), and that rule only works if the ring is where the customer expects it.
- **The C14 inlet** — it lands from *inside*: flange against the fore face of the printed tunnel standing round the cutout, two M3 into that face either side of the bore, and its moulded shroud reaches back up the bore toward the wall. Nothing on this station stands proud of the rear face. Drawn home, the C13 cord housing nests [3–5 mm](AC_RECESS_DEPTH) down the same bore. Solder-tab pins face into the cabinet.
- **The ABU44** — a 1/4" collet on each face, the red bulkhead ring dropped into its rim before the fitting goes in, flange bearing on that ring and the panel nut made up inside. Nothing threads onto either collet here.

Set the populated back-top piece aside; the power column joins it at step 5, and it closes in step 6.

### 3. Stand the refrigeration stratum on the floor

The compressor body comes in as part of the integrated refrigerant-loop assembly with all refrigerant lines already brazed to it. **It is never separated from that assembly at any point in this procedure.**

**The compressor is the one body in the box that is bolted down to it.** `enclosure._floor_bosses` stands a post on the floor slab under each of the four holes in the compressor's own mounting plate, and each post rises *through* the rubber grommet in that hole to just under the grommet's crown — so the posts locate the body before a screw is turned, and the standoff a screw crosses is the one the plate asked for rather than a number typed anywhere. **The post is the sleeve the mount needs**: Ø12 standing in a Ø14 bore, the rubber round it working in shear, and its crown the surface the washer comes to rest on after squeezing the top flange [0.4](GROMMET_SQUEEZE) mm. Sequence:

1. Set the assembly down with the compressor's **covered power box facing the front**. Its bolt pattern is symmetric about the plate's centre and the power box is not, so the box is the only feature that tells the two ends apart ([`/hardware/reference/compressor/README.md`](/hardware/reference/compressor/README.md) "Frame") — a plate that will not take all four posts is on the wrong end. Confirm the donor's moulded terminal cover is present, uncracked, unmodified, and securely retained; it stays with the compressor throughout the build.
2. Lower it straight down onto the four posts, the cold-core assembly steady on its cart with no tension on the refrigerant lines.
3. Drop an M5 × 25 fender washer over each post and drive one M5 into its ruthex insert. **Draw it down until the washer lands on the post's crown, then stop.** The post's crown stands [0.4](GROMMET_SQUEEZE) mm under the grommet's, so the washer squeezes the top flange by that much on its way and the post takes the load after it — the preload is printed in and no torque figure sets it. A screw that still turns freely has not reached its post; one that needs a bar on it is being driven into a stop that is already home. The washer spans the grommet's bore and lands on nothing but rubber and post, so the can still stands on its four grommets.

The condenser+fan stands east of it, hard against the depth the +X wall's own furniture leaves free, and **off the slab on its own mount**. **An oblong can has no flat face.** The compressor's discharge stub stands on the tangent at its own +X extreme, and the block's header is re-dressed to that stub's column and plane ([`compressor.stations()`](/hardware/reference/compressor/README.md)) — so the lane the block's east position opens is crossed by one straight length of tube and nothing about the two bodies has to touch. (See Open items 1 for the openings its air still has no route through.)

Both of the block's Y faces stand 20 mm back over its whole width, leaving 0.4 mm of folded sheet at the crown and at the base of each ([`/hardware/reference/condenser-block/README.md`](/hardware/reference/condenser-block/README.md)). Those four flanges are what the box takes:

4. **Feed the aft end onto the fin.** A single fin stands off the +X wall in the bay's east lane with a finger reaching west off it at either end (`enclosure._cond_mount`). Bring the block in with those fingers entering its aft recess, then push it **forward** until the two fore flanges are 3 mm into the grooves of the front wall's two rails (`enclosure._cond_cradle`) and its fore face is flat against the rails' shoulder. **The rails are the datum in Y and their grooves in Z** — the same bargain the gas sensor's card slot strikes. The block is now standing on the lower rail's crown and the lower finger, 9 mm clear of the slab — the depth the lower finger's own bore takes.
5. **Two screws close it.** Both go **down** the one vertical line the donor drilled through its aft flanges. Drive an M3 × 8 through the crown flange's Ø5 hole into the upper finger's ruthex, then one through the base flange's into the lower finger's. **The lower one is reached from behind** — in through the open aft face of that recess, on a long ball-end key leaned aft of the upper finger, with the bay's east lane and the core's bay still clear. Snug both; the sheet is four tenths.

**The gas sensor goes in before either body does.** The MQ-6 card lies ALONG the −X strip and drops straight down into the two grooved posts printed on the floor of it (`enclosure._west_cradle`), can facing WEST into its well in the flank and header facing EAST into the bay — down until the card lands on the shoulder at the foot of each groove, and there is no screw because the card has no hole. Do it with the front-bottom piece still open on the bench: once the compressor is down, the slot is a reach past a charged assembly for no reason. Its loom lands later, in [`wiring.md`](/hardware/assembly/wiring.md). **Card in the wrong way round is the one error this slot allows** — the can must go into the wall's well, with the four pins standing out into the open bay where the loom can reach them.

**The flavor pack sets down on their crown.** [`manifold_layout`](/hardware/manifold-layout/manifold_layout.py)'s ten valves, six tees and two Kamoer pumps come in as one folded pack that rests on its own four spine hairpins across the two crowns, pump-head faces standing clear above them. **It arrives inside `enclosure-front-top`,** made up and plumbed on the bench at [`internal-plumbing.md`](/hardware/assembly/internal-plumbing.md) §3: eight of its valves press into the two **valve trays** that piece carries — a plate of four seats per deck, wall to wall ([`printed-parts/enclosure/valve-tray/`](/hardware/printed-parts/enclosure/valve-tray/README.md)) — so the pack slides in with the column at §4 rather than being set in before it. What this step owes it is a level pair of crowns and a clear floor beneath for it to land on.

### 4. Close the front column

**The collet plate goes into `enclosure-front-top` first, through that piece's Z− face** — the seam face the bottom piece mates on, and the face the part beds on off the printer. Set the piece on its ceiling with the seam face up — the pose SA-02 draws it in — and feed the cut steel into the slot that passes through the bay floor (`enclosure._plate_slot`), holes forward-facing — the plate is a rectangle, so either end leads and only the holes say which face is fore. The slot is one width and holds nothing back: the steel goes down it — up, in the machine's own frame — until its own **top edge** lands on the land `enclosure._plate_cap` carries off the tee wall's fore face, [1 mm](PLATE_CAP_LAND) of flat running wall to wall at [216.49 mm](PLATE_CAP_Z). **That land is the plate's only stop and its Z datum**, so seated means the top edge is up against it along its whole length — run a finger down the joint and feel no gap. Over the two outer tails the guide cheeks' own heads carry the same plane out to the side walls. The fore cheeks keep the steel from pitching toward the opening and their returns wrap outside its ends into fixed side-wall stock; each cheek is a wedge in plan, thickest where it is rooted in that side wall, which is where the moment is carried, and each stands the whole storey to the bay's ceiling. **The steel lands in front of the wall that carries the four anchor tees** — `enclosure._tee_wall`, front-top's own section aft of the plate, wall to wall and the whole height of the bay, one bore per tee closed on the round collar that tee's branch arm carries. The plate's aft face is that wall's fore face, struck as one figure, so the steel bears back on the wall across its whole face and every bore is stopped at its fore mouth by steel.

**Nothing fastens it, and nothing has to.** The slot takes its two long faces, the cap's land stops it, the wedge cheeks take the pitch, the tee wall takes the push, and the piece's own Z− face is the only way in or out. Its ends stand [4.5 mm](PLATE_STEP_IN) off the cavity-side walls at **every** height: the steel ends at x = ±100.0, its constant-width slot ends at x = ±100.2, and 4.3 mm of printed return plus the 3 mm outer wall remains beyond each end. Each fixed Y− cheek bears on 10 mm of the widened tail. **The part is a plain rectangle**: [4](PLATE_CORNERS) corners and [4](PLATE_HOLES) holes. Check the steel against `collet-plate.dxf` before you feed it. **The steel is not captive until the column closes**: seat it, then keep the piece seam-face-up until §4 takes it, because righted on its own the plate slides back down the way it came in. **Closing the column is what catches it** — front-bottom carries a foot under each end of the steel at the seam, its top face the seam plane itself, so foot and flank are one continuous bearing land [9.5 mm](PLATE_LAND) wide per end and the plate stands on it at nominal with nothing to settle onto. The piece prints seam-face-down; turned over once it is the bench pose for this step and for the trays.

`enclosure-front-top` **slides AFT onto `enclosure-front-bottom`** — brought in from the front, standing [104.3 mm](RAIL_TRAVEL_FRONT) proud of the bottom piece's front wall and drawn straight back, mouth riding the shoulder, until both feet land on their stop blocks aft of the tee wall. This is the piece that carries the display facet and the front half of the funnel opening, and **it arrives carrying the flavour pack** — made up into its two valve trays and its tee wall on the bench at [`internal-plumbing.md`](/hardware/assembly/internal-plumbing.md) §3 — **and the collet plate**. So this motion is what lands the pack: the four spine hairpins sweep in over the stratum's crowns with the piece and settle over them as it stops, and what this step owes them is that lane left clear. A slide that binds mid-travel is something standing in the channel's lane; a slide that will not close the last millimetre is a foot meeting something on a stop block's face.

The cold core stays **on its cart** through this step, tethered to the stratum by the two drawn refrigerant legs hanging out the open back. Keep the cart tracking the bench so neither leg is ever in tension; the core's own seating is §6's ride.

### 5. Stand the power column on the +X flank

The bench-built power column's five bodies stand against the **+X wall**, and their feet come down onto the cold core's cap when the piece carrying them closes. Each is turned so its own mounting plane — the PSU's potted base, the main board's underside, the relay's and the hub plate's undersides, the ground stud's landing face — faces that wall and lands on **one common seat**, the plane the refrigeration stratum's own east face defines. That is what puts the whole group clear of the Y seam's posts, pods and plugs in one test rather than five. **No tray stands under any of them.**

**What holds each one is a printed boss per hole.** `enclosure._east_bosses` grows [17](EAST_BOSSES) of them off that wall's inner face — one for every hole in every body's own pattern, each reaching out to that body's own mounting plane and bored back from its tip for a ruthex M3 short, so the standoff a screw crosses is what the body asked for rather than a number typed anywhere. Each boss carries a flat-bottomed D stem on a full-width 45° corbel; the stem always reaches the hole, while `wall_mounts` holds only a wedge that crosses an installed body back by 1 mm from that body's exact envelope. The screw goes the other way: in through the body from the room. Pattern and screw schedule are [`power-column.md`](/hardware/assembly/power-column.md)'s.

**Every one of those bosses is on `enclosure-back-top`**, which is why this step is bench work on that piece and not work inside a standing box: the bodies are offered up to its wall and screwed down there, alongside the six connection bodies of step 2, and they come down with it.

The column, aft to fore:

- **Mean Well IRM-90-12ST PSU** — lying on its side against the wall so only its [33.5 mm](PSU_DEPTH) depth reaches into the lane and its [109 mm](PSU_LENGTH) long axis runs fore and aft. Its aft face stands just clear of the rear seam, under the C14 inlet's own column.
- **Teyleten relay #2** stands ON END in the band between the main board and the brick — the only pose a third body fits that depth in. **Teyleten relay #1** lies on the main board's crown, and the **ground ring-terminal stack** takes the same seat one clearance forward of it. The **five Wago lever nuts** press into wells printed on the wall itself over the brick's crown; nothing bolts them.
- **Main board** forward of the brick on the same seat, its long edge fore and aft down the flank so only its thickness and components reach inboard. Handle it ESD-safe; the four holes are its electrically isolated MH1–MH4, and the screw heads seat on the top-face pads.

The power column is **unpowered** at this step: the AC pigtails from the wall's own three mains Wagos hang free and get terminated at the C14's solder-tab pins, which stand on the same wall just aft of the row, in [`wiring.md`](/hardware/assembly/wiring.md). The compressor's earth-bond lead routes toward the ground stack and waits.

The **five device-cluster lever nuts** are not on this column — each presses into a well on the flank its own cluster stands against, and they go in with the looms rather than with the column: [`cable-assemblies.md`](/hardware/assembly/cable-assemblies.md) step 3, stationed in [`ac-wiring-schedule.md`](/hardware/wiring/ac-wiring-schedule.md) "Loom terminations".

Confirm the main board's two exposed edges are clear to a hand and a plug, and that nothing on this flank stands in front of a +Y wall body's own reach inboard.

### 6. Close the box

The box closes in two slides and four screws, and everything in the service bay is aboard before the first one moves.

1. **Back column** — `enclosure-back-top`, populated per steps 2 and 5 and carrying the ceiling retained at §1, **slides FORE onto a bare `enclosure-back-bottom`** — entering aft of home, standing [237.9 mm](RAIL_TRAVEL_BACK) proud of the bottom piece's rear wall, mouth on shoulder, until both feet land on their stop blocks at the Y-seam end of their runs. Both flanks carry the same run: the rail passes straight through the PRV chase's station, which keeps out of the joint's own band for that storey. The tub under it is EMPTY, and that is the design and not a convenience: this piece's whole fore band sweeps the depth of the column on its way in, so nothing may stand in the tub yet — `z-slide-back-clear` is the card's reading of the travel. Everything the bay needs from this piece — the six connection bodies, the power column, the tap-water chain in its anchor, the meter under its ceiling — is already riding it.

2. **The back assembly rides forward over the core.** Stand the closed back column behind the core's cart, mouth facing the core's aft face, and bring it FORE — the Y-seam mouth swallowing the core, the slab passing under it, the hold-down feet's eased tips riding over the cap's crown — one continuous motion that runs the column's whole depth and ends as the Y seam's own telescope: over the last [13.1 mm](Y_OVERLAP) the front assembly's proud tongue enters the back's mouth, the floor's scarfed tongue closes inside the slab, and the four Y-seam plugs drop into their slots. The core is seated when the motion stops: its aft face on the rear seam band, its front corners already in front-bottom's pocket blocks, the brackets' feet flat on its cap — 0 by intent. **Set the core's lid tenants before this ride**: the water pump and everything else that stands on the cap goes on at [`internal-plumbing.md`](/hardware/assembly/internal-plumbing.md) while the core is still on the open cart, where every face of it is bench work.

3. **Four screws.** M3×10 SHCS from the ±X exteriors at both levels — the floor pair pinning the two bottom pieces, the ceiling pair the two tops. These four are the whole of the box's fastening, and they are also the slides' lock: each column's top can only leave the way it came in, FORE. Back-top's way out runs into front-top, which now stands in it; front-top's runs into open air, and the ceiling pair is what stands across it.

   **This is what shuts the core down.** Two brackets (`enclosure._core_holds`) stand in the [3 mm](REAR_SEAM_CLEAR) band behind the core, one either side of the flavour unions' column, each with its foot [12 mm](CORE_HOLD_REACH) forward onto the aft edge of the cap. Each clears the flavour-A union beside it by [8.76 mm](CORE_HOLD_CLEAR), so a bulkhead nut run down crooked is the one thing in the bay that fouls one. With the screws driven, the core is closed in on every face: slab under, blocks ahead, +Y wall aft, brackets over.

**Service is the same door swinging the other way**, and its first move needs no cart: **the ceiling pair alone frees front-top**, which draws fore off its rails with the machine standing where it is, taking the flavour pack, the trays, the tee wall and the collet plate with it. All four out and the back assembly rides aft off the core — the whole bay open on the cart again — or, with the core out, either top slides fore off its own column. No spudger, no flex clip, no hidden fastener.

With the box closed, confirm from inside: the four JG unions' cabinet-side collets, the ABU44's inboard collet and the C14's solder-tab pins are all exposed and reachable; the compressor's retained donor cover and factory-external electrical interface are reachable for the AC run arriving from the power column; and **no plumbing or wiring has been routed through any bulkhead at this point.**

**The collet plate is already aboard**, fed into front-top through that piece's Z− face at §4 and closed in by this column's own mouth. Confirm through the open bay that the steel stands square in front of the tee wall with its [4](PLATE_HOLES) holes forward-facing and every bore stopped at its fore mouth by it.

The bay itself rides open — the pump cartridge, with both pumps zip-tied to its deck, goes in at [`internal-plumbing.md`](/hardware/assembly/internal-plumbing.md), its four barb tubes threading the plate's holes into the anchor tees' collets, and from then on the face is the front of the machine: pull the pump cartridge while bracing the box and the tees run forward in their bores until the plate takes each collet's nose and the grip opens; push it home and they click back in.

### 7. Slide the ASSE drip pan in through the −X wall

The ASSE drip pan's sleeve is printed on `enclosure-back-top`'s −X wall, so this follows that piece down whenever it closes.

Dress the pan first ([`asse-drip-pan/README.md`](/hardware/printed-parts/enclosure/asse-drip-pan/README.md) "The dock"): press the female half of the magnetic pogo pair into the pocket in the pan's east wall from inside the cavity, nose first, until its shoulders stop it; solder the moisture sensor's two leads to the tails standing proud of the boss; pot the pocket's mouth over the joints; then lay the sensor flat on the floor inside the coves. Pan, plate, leads and dock are one part from here on, and leave the machine together. The probe goes in the pan before the pan goes in the machine: once it is in its sleeve the pan is under the ASSE chain, and a hand laying a plate flat in it would be reaching past those fittings to do it.

Then **slide the pan in through the slot in the −X side wall**, east end first, until it stops. The slot is one opening in two rectangles, cut on the pan's own section — the pan's body below the flange's underside, the rim above it — and the same two rectangles are the berth behind it. The pan's floor lands on the sleeve's floor and its rim runs under the sleeve's lid, so it goes in flat and stays flat. **Home is the pan against the backstop**, the solid east end of the sleeve, which its own wall and its east rim meet together. The lid holds the pan down as well as the floor holds it up, so west through the slot is the only way it leaves.

**The sleeve carries it, floor to floor, and the dock holds it home.** Nothing fastens the pan: it draws west back out the same slot to be emptied, and its west end stands proud of the machine's skin to be taken hold of — thumb on the flange's top, fingertip under the floor. The last millimetre of the push is the dock's: the pads in the pan's east wall meet the pins in the backstop and the pair's magnets take the pan the rest of the way and keep it there; a pull parts them again, and nothing trails the pan out. Seated, it stands under the fall off the Multiplex 19-0897's atmospheric vent tip, which `enclosure_assembly.check_vent_lands` holds to the pan's flat floor, inside the coves, so the drip lands on the moisture plate and not on a wall it runs down the outside of. Route the male half's two leads — they leave the sleeve under its floor — toward the power column and leave them loose; termination happens in [`wiring.md`](/hardware/assembly/wiring.md) (run SIG-9 in [`/hardware/wiring/ac-wiring-schedule.md`](/hardware/wiring/ac-wiring-schedule.md)).

### 8. Let the display into the facet, and clear the funnel opening

The **display** goes into the 45° facet chamfered across the top-front arris of `enclosure-front-top` — a solid surface running the box's full width, with the glass centred on it and flat facet either side. The face is let down two steps: an inset the cover plate fills, and inside that the bezel counterbore the glass seats in, with the body hanging behind it through the PCB cut-through. The glass is the datum. Handle it ESD-safe.

**The gasket ring goes on the glass before the plate goes anywhere near it.** The TPU ring is cut to the glass's own outline outside and to the plate's window inside, so laid down square it lands on the glass's border and nothing else — clear of the screen, short of the counterbore's wall. What it fills is the step between the two seats: the plate's underside lies on the inset floor and the glass's front face [1 mm](DISPLAY_GASKET_T) below that, which is the ring's own thickness. Without it the plate's lap passes over the glass on air, the two screws hold the plate and not the display, and the 45° face a customer wipes is open at the glass's edge.

Then **lay the cover plate into the inset and drive its two screws.** The plate is a border — [6 mm](DISPLAY_BORDER) across top and bottom, wider at each side where a screw stands — and it laps the glass by [3 mm](DISPLAY_INSET_LAP) all round, which is the ring's own width, so what holds the display in is the plate bearing down through that ring, and what holds the plate is two M3 × 8 SHCS into heat-sets set in the facet's own material. **The finished face has nothing standing proud of it.** The plate's top lies in the 45° plane and each head lands in a ⌀[6.15](DISPLAY_CBORE_D) counterbore [0.2 mm](DISPLAY_SEAT_RECESS) under it — the seat the cold core's cap lids use, the same screw and the same recess. That is why the plate is not one thickness throughout: the head seat is deeper than the plate, so the section thickens under each screw by the counterbore's own depth and drops into a pocket in the inset floor, which leaves the land under the head at the plate's own section. Leave the display's RS485 and power leads loose for [`wiring.md`](/hardware/assembly/wiring.md).

The **funnel opening is an opening, not a part.** `enclosure._funnel_hole` cuts it in the top wall behind the display facet, sized off the placed funnel's own collar, and the opening crosses the Y seam — so both top pieces carry their share of it and there is no screw pattern to land. Deburr and wipe the collar seat: the removable silicone funnel's brim underside rests on this face, and brim residue or a stray support nib holds the funnel proud and crooked in its frame. Then sight straight down the opening and confirm the fall corridor is clear: what stands under it is the flavour manifold's two valve decks and, behind them, the cold core's cap. What drops through it is `fluid-4`, drawn 1/4" LLDPE from the funnel's spout down to the inlet of V-B, the funnel gate in its cradle on that cap — the machine's one gravity feed, so what the corridor owes it is a clear fall. It falls west of the source pair rather than between them, and the two bodies it runs nearest on the way down are [coil-v-b](FUNNEL_NEAR_A) and [coil-v-a](FUNNEL_NEAR_B), which the [Ø6.35](FUNNEL_TUBE_D) tube clears by **[1.226 mm](FUNNEL_LANE_SIDE)** at its tightest, and `clearance-floor` — which holds every drawn run off everything its own line does not join it to — [currently passes](FUNNEL_GATE_STATUS) with this corridor in the count. Nothing holds it open: the coils are placed bodies and the tube is drawn between them, so that air is what the arrangement leaves rather than a feature anyone cut. Treat a stray nib or a tube off its drawn line as a defect, not a tolerance. The funnel itself ([`/hardware/printed-parts/zone-c/README.md`](/hardware/printed-parts/zone-c/README.md)) is seated at finish-and-pack, after the wipe-down, not here.
