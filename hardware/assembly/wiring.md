# Wiring

The procedure for executing every electrical run in the appliance — chassis ground bonds, AC mains, the cabinet-side [12 V](DC_BUS_V) branches, and signal looms — once the enclosure is plumbed and the electronics bay is installed. The schedule of runs is [`/hardware/wiring/ac-wiring-schedule.md`](/hardware/wiring/ac-wiring-schedule.md); this doc is the build-order procedure for executing that schedule, with AC, DC, and signal runs interleaved by physical zone.

The routed groups above the cold-core lid carry [16 AWG](AWG_AC_MAIN) mains, [12 V](DC_BUS_V) power, and [22 AWG](AWG_DC_BRANCH) signal in close proximity. Topology lives in [`/hardware/wiring/power.mmd`](/hardware/wiring/power.mmd), [`/hardware/wiring/esp32-pinout.mmd`](/hardware/wiring/esp32-pinout.mmd), and [`/hardware/wiring/valve-control.mmd`](/hardware/wiring/valve-control.mmd). Every low-voltage run lands on a labeled edge connector of the main board ([`/hardware/pcb/pcba/pcba.tsx`](/hardware/pcb/pcba/pcba.tsx), the canonical pin map) or on a relay module's terminals.

The field harnesses are **not** wired in place conductor-by-conductor — they are **pre-fabricated as cable assemblies** on the bench (cut-to-length all-black silicone, ferruled/crimped, black-braided-sleeved, continuity-tested), then landed here. The fabrication procedure is [`cable-assemblies.md`](/hardware/assembly/cable-assemblies.md); a failed harness is swapped as a whole assembly, never repaired conductor-by-conductor. Wire is all-black throughout **except the AC mains** (black hot / white neutral / green ground — a safety convention, not a service aid).

The PCB lies flat on the cold-core lid. The PSU and both relays mount to back-top’s dry west sidewall, and the meter travels on its east-wall carriers. Keep the funnel frame off while making the final connections between quadrants. Fit the frame after the tubing and leads are dressed; it carries no wiring. The rear ceiling is continuous. The [installed routing review](/hardware/wiring/installed/above-core-review/README.md) records the nominal conductor paths and its remaining evidence limits.

## Scope

In: a chassis that exits [`internal-plumbing.md`](/hardware/assembly/internal-plumbing.md) — cold core ridden into the enclosure, valve manifold mounted, all water + CO2 + flavor lines plumbed, the pump cradle home with the J13 ribbon's Fastons on its motor tabs and its housing seated at J13; electronics bay installed per [`electronics-bay.md`](/hardware/assembly/electronics-bay.md) + [`enclosure-mechanical.md`](/hardware/assembly/enclosure-mechanical.md), populated with the C14 inlet pigtails, AC distribution block, Mean Well IRM-90-12ST PSU, both Teyleten relays, the main board with its on-board [5 V](LOGIC_V) + [3.3 V](MCU_V) logic rails, the [12 V](DC_BUS_V) distribution block, and ground bus — the column-internal runs (DC-1/2/4, LV-1/2/3) done, unpowered, and the remaining field looms awaiting connection; compressor bolted down to the enclosure floor on its four posts with the donor's moulded cover securely retained over its terminal block and PTC module, its factory-external electrical interface accessible without disturbing the cover, and its body bond unlanded, condenser + fan standing east of it on the shell's own tangent. The bagged faucet-and-umbilical sub-assembly (output of [`faucet-and-umbilical.md`](/hardware/assembly/faucet-and-umbilical.md)) arrives with its completed RJ11 plug. Connect it to the rear keystone for end-to-end testing. This procedure terminates only the inboard SIG-6 loom, from J3 to the keystone's 110 IDC; the umbilical cable stays intact.

Wire stock and small parts: the pre-fabricated cable assemblies from [`cable-assemblies.md`](/hardware/assembly/cable-assemblies.md) — one labeled loom per board connector (J1–J4, J6, J7, J9, J11, J13), each ending in a JST XH [2.5 mm](JST_PITCH) housing at the main board end; [16 AWG](AWG_AC_MAIN) silicone hookup wire for AC + ground (green for ground, plus the appliance-wire colors used in [`/hardware/wiring/ac-wiring-schedule.md`](/hardware/wiring/ac-wiring-schedule.md) §AC mains); [16 AWG](AWG_AC_BRANCH_U) solid black/white UL1007 for fixed AC-2/3 and DC-1/2/4, per the wiring schedule's conductor-construction table; [18 AWG](COMP_LEAD_AWG) SJOOW 3-conductor jacketed cable for the compressor lead; ferrules sized for [16/22 AWG](AWG_TRIPLE); ring terminals for the ground bus + the compressor's terminal-box earth screw; female-disconnect (Faston) terminals for the diaphragm pump, valves, and condenser fan; the current donor compressor's factory-external connector is Open item 1; black PET braided sleeve + zip ties for bundling; Wago 221 lever nuts for the device-end fan-outs (221-420 at MANIFOLD A, 221-415 at MANIFOLD B, 221-413 at the sensor commons and power row).

Out: every run in [`/hardware/wiring/ac-wiring-schedule.md`](/hardware/wiring/ac-wiring-schedule.md) executed and continuity-tested. AC runs AC-1 through AC-6 landed, with the C14 ground pin bonded to the central chassis-ground point and onward to the two specified metal destinations (carbonator, compressor body). The current donor's connector is Open item 1, and the faucet under-counter plate's bond is deferred. Cabinet-side DC runs DC-3 and DC-5 through DC-9 landed. The inboard SIG-6 loom lands from J3 to the rear keystone; the other signal runs are landed at both ends. SIG-7 (4.3" enclosure display) is a [~400 mm](SIG_DISPLAY_LEN) internal run from the electronics bay to the front face; SIG-6 (1.47" faucet display, direct TTL UART) rides the umbilical up to the above-counter faucet, while SIG-4 (flow meter) is a short internal run in the electronics-bay zone. A dielectric / continuity check passes on the AC side.

Not in scope: applying [120 VAC](AC_LINE_V); flashing firmware; bringing up the [12 V](DC_BUS_V) rail; any sensor probe-into-water service — see [`firmware-and-commissioning.md`](/hardware/assembly/firmware-and-commissioning.md). The column-internal runs — see [`electronics-bay.md`](/hardware/assembly/electronics-bay.md). The compressor terminal block's clip-on PTC start relay / overload module and moulded cover are donor-side subassemblies preserved during teardown ([`/hardware/reference/ice-maker/README.md`](/hardware/reference/ice-maker/README.md) "Powering and control"). The cover stays installed: the appliance lead terminates at the donor assembly's factory-external electrical interface.

## Inputs per appliance

Wire stock, connectors, and termination consumables are in [`/hardware/ledger/bom.md`](/hardware/ledger/bom.md) §11 — that one section carries all of it: the 16 AWG AC + ground stock, the 22 AWG DC/signal stock, the Wago 221 lever nuts, the spade and ring terminals, the ferrules, the heat-shrink, and the braided sleeve. The AC hardware the runs land *on* is §5 (C14 inlet, line cord, the GEARit SJOOW compressor lead, the thermal fuse). Status in [`/hardware/ledger/purchases.md`](/hardware/ledger/purchases.md). The runs themselves are in [`/hardware/wiring/ac-wiring-schedule.md`](/hardware/wiring/ac-wiring-schedule.md) §"Run table".

Tooling: ratcheting crimper for insulated and uninsulated terminals + ferrules + Faston disconnects + JST XH (one tool covers all four with the appropriate die per terminal family); wire strippers sized for [16/22 AWG](AWG_TRIPLE); small flat screwdriver for Wago 221 levers and the relay screw terminals; PEX-style sidecutters for trimming; a hot-glue gun for strain relief at intermediate tie points where the run crosses a printed-part edge; cable-tie tensioner (optional); multimeter for the continuity + dielectric checks at step 3.

## Procedure

Execution order: chassis-ground bonding, then AC, then the cabinet-side DC, then signal, with a static dielectric / continuity check on the AC side gated between AC completion and DC energization. Within each phase, runs are executed in the run-number order from [`/hardware/wiring/ac-wiring-schedule.md`](/hardware/wiring/ac-wiring-schedule.md).

### 1. Chassis-ground bonds (single-point ground at the electronics bay)

Establish the single-point chassis ground first. Chassis grounding is via discrete green wires from the exposed metal parts back to a central ground bus in the electronics bay. Per [`/hardware/wiring/ac-wiring-schedule.md`](/hardware/wiring/ac-wiring-schedule.md) "Grounding strategy" all bonds are [16 AWG](AWG_AC_MAIN) green-insulated, with ring terminals at both ends.

Bond, from each target to the ground bus in the electronics bay:

- **Carbonator** — the bond needs a metal landing on the stainless shell. The printed foam-cap screw closes on a heat-set insert in plastic and defines no contact with that steel. The [installed wiring model](/hardware/wiring/README.md) carries `carbonator.PE` as an unlocated landing.
- **Compressor body** — ring terminal under the compressor's own earth screw at its terminal box ([`enclosure-mechanical.md`](/hardware/assembly/enclosure-mechanical.md) §3). **Not under a floor screw.** The compressor stands on four rubber grommets and the posts under them are printed PET-GF, so a ring on one of those heads reaches rubber and plastic and touches no compressor metal at all. The earth screw is on the can. **This is the machine's only bond to the refrigeration metalwork**, and it is run AC-6 in [`/hardware/wiring/ac-wiring-schedule.md`](/hardware/wiring/ac-wiring-schedule.md): the G conductor of the same jacketed lead that carries AC-4 and AC-5 to the donor assembly's factory-external electrical interface, so the bond arrives with the power it protects.
- **Faucet under-counter SS plate** — **not landed in this procedure.** The plate ([`/hardware/cut-parts/faucet/under-counter-plate/`](/hardware/cut-parts/faucet/under-counter-plate/)) has no fastener of its own — it is a Ø[54.55 mm](PLATE_D) disc with a shank pocket and a pill pocket, clamped by the faucet's own factory shank nut — and it ships loose in the install bag, so it does not exist on the appliance at this step. Stage the bus-side ring and leave the conductor coiled and flagged.

At the electronics-bay end, every bond terminates on a single ring-terminal stack at the ground bus. The bus is bonded to the C14 inlet's earth pin via run AC-1's green conductor (C14 earth → AC distribution block → ground bus), executed in step 2.

Prepare [~400 mm](EARTH_BUS_LEN) for the rear PE WAGO-to-stack bond and [~500 mm](EARTH_CARBONATOR_LEN) for the carbonator bond. Their modeled paths are 295.531 mm and 422.415 mm, leaving 104.469 mm and 77.585 mm for terminations and dressing. The carbonator's metal landing remains unlocated; trim at the actual landing. The short AC-1 earth pigtail from C14 to the rear WAGO has its own cut.

At the lid stack, the inlet bond uses the northeast-facing lug, the carbonator bond the northwest-facing lug, and AC-6 the southwest-facing lug. Leave the inlet bond's nominal 6 mm straight entry before its bend and keep the fore paired-tube tie head on its east side. Fasten the stack before dressing AC4-H and AC5-N through the fore connection area. Hold those flexible leads clear of the vertical driver during service; the installed harness is not qualified to remain in that tool path.

Continuity check after this step: ohms-low between every bonded metal surface and the bus.

### 2. AC mains (C14 → distribution → relay #1 → compressor + PSU)

Execute the AC runs in the order they appear in [`/hardware/wiring/ac-wiring-schedule.md`](/hardware/wiring/ac-wiring-schedule.md) "AC mains (120 V)": AC-1, AC-2, AC-3, AC-4, AC-5, AC-6. AC-1 lands the rear C14 inlet directly on the AC distribution block; there is no device in series — ground-fault protection is deferred, see [/future/pie-in-the-sky/gfci.md](/future/pie-in-the-sky/gfci.md). AC-6 is the compressor chassis-ground done in step 1; it is the third conductor of the same 3-conductor bundle. Build the bundle whole here; the green conductor was landed at the bus end in step 1.

Compressor lead (AC-4 + AC-5 + AC-6): stage a single [18 AWG](COMP_LEAD_AWG) SJOOW 3-conductor lead from [~700 mm](COMP_LEAD_LEN) preparation stock, carrying switched H, N and PE. Its shared jacket uses the existing front floor opening and valve-bank gap. Above the jacket cut, the same three continuous insulated cores fan out to relay #1, the rear neutral WAGO and the lid earth stack. Dress and retain these branches along the [installed model](/hardware/wiring/README.md), then trim the ends. The switched-hot stock budget covers a 606.306 mm main path and a separate 36.777 mm fuse continuation: approximately 643.082 mm total, leaving 56.918 mm for terminations and dressing. At the compressor end, switched H reaches the dressed SF76E lead splice and then the verified hot side of the donor's factory-external interface; N reaches its verified neutral side; PE takes a ring at the compressor's terminal-box earth screw. Preserve the donor cover and factory lead exit. The exact current-donor connector and polarity mapping remain Open item 1.

At the column end of the bundle: H_switched lands on the Teyleten relay #1 NO terminal; N lands at the AC distribution block N pole; G lands on the ground bus. AC distribution-block landings use ferrules into the Wago 221 lever-nut block. The SJOOW switched-hot conductor lands in the relay screw cage with a ferrule; the fixed AC-3 feed is bare solid copper.

AC-1 (C14 inlet → AC distribution block) is [~150 mm](AC1_LEN) of solid black hot, white neutral and green earth, soldered to the inlet's tab pins at the C14 end and stripped bare into the Wago block at the distribution end; H, N, and G land directly on the block. AC-2 (distribution block → PSU primary) is [~200 mm](AC2_LEN), solid black hot and white neutral directly in the WAGO and PSU screw cages. The encapsulated Class II PSU has no earth terminal; exposed metalwork bonds to the separate ground bus. AC-3 is the hot-pickup leg from the distribution block into relay #1's contact common.

Color discipline (AC only): [16 AWG](AWG_AC_MAIN), solid PVC on AC-1/2/3 and stranded silicone on the separate earth bonds, black for hot, white for neutral, green for ground. DC and signal wire is all-black (cut-to-length, per [`cable-assemblies.md`](/hardware/assembly/cable-assemblies.md)) — color is reserved for the mains as a safety convention.

### 3. Static dielectric / continuity check on the AC side

After step 2 and before any DC conductor lands, cold-check the AC wiring with the C14 inlet **disconnected from line**, multimeter only. The AC path runs C14 inlet → AC distribution block directly (AC-1), so H and N continuity from the inlet to the distribution block reads end-to-end. Ground-fault protection is deferred — see [/future/pie-in-the-sky/gfci.md](/future/pie-in-the-sky/gfci.md).

- **Continuity (ohms-low) — earth, end-to-end:** C14 earth pin to every metal-part chassis-ground target landed in step 1.
- **Continuity (ohms-low) — H/N, C14 → distribution block:** C14 hot pin to AC distribution block H pole. C14 neutral pin to AC distribution block N pole.
- **Continuity (ohms-low) — H/N, distribution block → downstream loads:** AC distribution block H pole to Teyleten relay #1 common. With relay #1 manually held closed (jumper across the input opto), distribution-block H through the SF76E to the verified hot side of the donor compressor assembly's factory-external electrical interface.
- **Open (ohms-high) — H to N downstream of the distribution block:** distribution block H to distribution block N, both with relay #1 open and with relay #1 closed (with the compressor's motor windings in circuit, this last reads the winding resistance; confirm ~[10](WINDING_R_LOW)–[30 Ω](WINDING_R_HIGH) for a [100 W](COMP_CLASS_W) hermetic, matching the donor compressor's nameplate). With relay #1 open, C14 hot to C14 neutral reads open.
- **Open (ohms-high) — leakage:** Every AC current-carrying conductor to every chassis-ground target. C14 hot pin to chassis-ground, C14 neutral pin to chassis-ground, AC distribution block H pole, AC distribution block N pole, relay #1 NO terminal, and both AC-4/5 conductors at the donor compressor's factory-external interface — each to chassis-ground. No leakage path.

If any check fails, find and fix it before step 4.

### 4. Cabinet-side [12 V](DC_BUS_V) runs (DC-3, DC-5 through DC-9)

The column-internal [12 V](DC_BUS_V) runs (DC-1, DC-2, DC-4) land at [`electronics-bay.md`](/hardware/assembly/electronics-bay.md); the cabinet-side runs land here, in schedule order:

- **DC-3** (relay #2 contact output → G Ganen diaphragm pump) — the relay pigtail reaches the lid-mounted pump and lands on its leads with female disconnects.
- **DC-5** (PUMPS J13 → Kamoer pump A / pump B) — one 500 mm 22 AWG 4P ribbon, already landed whole at the pump cradle's installation ([`internal-plumbing.md`](/hardware/assembly/internal-plumbing.md) §3, step 7): four Fastons on the motor tabs, each pair in its crown groove on the top clamp, the joined ribbon up the clamp's lead slot and through front-top's lead bore, then bare along the bulkhead crown below the funnel frame and through the +X cavity, clear of the manifold Wago and tube approaches, to its labeled XH housing at J13. The conductors run `AM2`, `AM1`, `BM2`, `BM1` from +X to −X through the bore; the +X pair serves pump A and the −X pair pump B ([`cable-assemblies.md`](/hardware/assembly/cable-assemblies.md#pump-leads--dc-5)). Its ring-out is done before the Fastons land. Confirm the housing is seated at J13, and unplug J13 by its housing before separating enclosure pieces.
- **DC-6** (MANIFOLD A J1 → 8 solenoid coils) — a **black sleeved trunk** from the column to the manifold: 9 conductors of 22 AWG black (8 OUT + COM), cut-to-length and carried in 3/4" braided sleeve per [`cable-assemblies.md`](/hardware/assembly/cable-assemblies.md). It breaks out at the manifold: each OUT lands on a female disconnect at its valve; the COM lands in the manifold's 221-420 lever nut, which fans the [12 V](DC_BUS_V) to every valve's other tab. Valve ↔ OUT mapping per [`/hardware/wiring/valve-control.mmd`](/hardware/wiring/valve-control.mmd).
- **DC-7** (MANIFOLD B J2 → 2 solenoid coils) — same pattern on the J2 trunk, 5 of its 6 ways populated (2 OUT + FAN + COM + `OUT3`); the FAN conductor is DC-8, `OUT3` is DC-9, and `OUT4` is a spare board channel left unpopulated.
- **DC-9** (MANIFOLD B J2 `OUT3` + a `COM` tap → V-K) — the water-supply fill/shutoff coil seats in back-top’s east sidewall mount. Route `OUT3` to its female disconnect along the installed path, clear of the pump, meter and soda fittings. Low-side switching like every manifold valve: `OUT3` sinks V-K's − through the on-board TBD62083 #2 (MCP23017 0x21 PA5), and + taps the shared `COM` at the manifold's fan-out, the way the fan's does. V-K stops water reaching the carbonator on a leak alarm or a power loss. V-A mounts on back-top; V-B mounts on the cold-core lid. Their J1 branches follow those carrying pieces and connect through the open roof.
- **DC-8** (condenser fan) — the fan's + ties to the manifold's COM fan-out; the J2 `FAN` conductor sinks the − side through the on-board TBD62083 #2 channel 5 (MCP23017 0x21 PA3 commands it), flyback through the driver's integrated diode to COM. The branch leaves the J2 trunk at the manifold; the `FAN` conductor is [~650 mm](FAN_RUN_LEN) from the +X wall to the side-wall fan. Female Fastons at the fan.

### 5. Signal looms (SIG-1 through SIG-4, SIG-6 through SIG-12)

Execute the SIG runs from [`/hardware/wiring/ac-wiring-schedule.md`](/hardware/wiring/ac-wiring-schedule.md) "Sensors and signal", one pre-fabricated loom per board connector. Every board end is a JST XH [2.5 mm](JST_PITCH) housing seated on its labeled wafer — J4 and J7 share the same 7P housing, so land each by its loom label ([`cable-assemblies.md`](/hardware/assembly/cable-assemblies.md)); a swap would put J4's 3V3/V5 on J7's reed inputs.

- **SENSORS J4 loom** — three runs share it, GND split near the column. **SIG-1**: the 1-wire temperature bus (`IO26` + `3V3` + GND), [22 AWG](AWG_DC_BRANCH), [~650 mm](SIG_ONEWIRE_LEN) to the back of the cold core — a DS18B20 (carbonator-wall, family 0x28) and a DS18S20 (evap-suction, family 0x10) bussed in parallel and told apart in firmware by family code; the [4.7 kΩ](PULLUP_R) data pull-up is on the main board (R9), nothing in the loom. Probes land at the cold core's exit per [`cold-core.md`](/hardware/assembly/cold-core.md). **SIG-4**: the DIGITEN flow meter (`IO25` + `V5` + GND), short internal run — the meter stands on edge between the pump and east gas group, with fore–aft ports and its lead root downward. Its three conductors return to the flat PCB along the installed paths. **SIG-9**: J4 `IO23` and `IO27` are reserved and unpopulated. The visible faucet drain is observed directly.
- **REEDS A J6 loom** — **SIG-10**: reservoir A's 4 level reeds + shared GND, [22 AWG](AWG_SIGNAL), [~600 mm](SIG_REEDS_A_LEN) up reservoir A's own reed channel and out the cap's `reed-cable-a` conduit; GND runs continuously from J6 alongside the four signal wires, with insulated local taps at the reeds. The reeds ride the on-board MCP23017 internal pull-ups (INPUT_PULLUP, magnet pulls LOW) — no resistors in the loom.
- **REEDS B J7 loom** — three runs share it, GND branches in a three-port 221-413 at the cold-core end, with one common wire per reed column. **SIG-2/SIG-3**: the carbonator low/high reeds (`CLO` / `CHI`). **SIG-11**: reservoir B's 4 reeds (`RB1`–`RB4`). Same internal-pull-up pattern.
- **FAUCET J3 loom** — **SIG-6**: direct TTL UART to the gooseneck-mounted ESP32-S3-Touch-LCD-1.47 faucet display. **The run crosses the +Y wall of back-top and the loom is only its inboard half**: XH housing on J3 at one end, 110 IDC on the back of the RiteAV CAT3 USOC keystone at the other ([`../printed-parts/enclosure/y-wall-of-back-top/README.md`](/hardware/printed-parts/enclosure/y-wall-of-back-top/README.md) station 7). Outboard of the jack the BNTECHGO ribbon runs [~1 m](SIG_UMBILICAL_LEN) up the umbilical on its completed plug. Punch down **J3 pin 3/V5 → jack 2 (white/orange label), J3 pin 4/GND → jack 3 (blue), J3 pin 2/IO35 RX → jack 4 (white/blue), J3 pin 1/IO33 TX → jack 5 (orange)**. Leave jack 1 and 6 open. All conductors are black; the colors name the jack's printed USOC labels. The [faucet guide's page 15](https://homesodamachine.com/read/faucet-assembly-guide/faucet-assembly-guide.pdf#page=15) shows the oriented plug and punchdown slots, and [`cable-assemblies.md#faucet-display-sig-6`](/hardware/assembly/cable-assemblies.md#faucet-display-sig-6) gives the complete mapping. Neither half carries components: the series damping + ESD clamps are on the main board ([`cable-assemblies.md`](/hardware/assembly/cable-assemblies.md) "Faucet-display ESD protection").
- **DISPLAY J9 loom** — **SIG-7**: the RS485 link + power to the ESP32-S3-Touch-LCD-4.3B enclosure display (`B` / `A` / GND / `V12`). The A/B pair lands on the 4.3B's onboard SP3485 terminals; `V12` + GND feed its 7–36 V screw input. Internal run, electronics bay → front face.
- **GAS J11 loom** — **SIG-12**: the MQ-6 combustible-gas sensor low in the refrigeration bay, in the two floor posts down its -X strip beside the compressor (`GND` / `V5` / `DOUT` / `AOUT`). The sensor board's header faces EAST, out of the strip and into the bay the front assembly opens onto, so the loom lands on it from the room and runs aft up the -X flank. Its 0–5 V outputs are divided to ESP-safe levels on the main board (R1–R4), so the loom is a plain sensor cable. This loom also feeds the on-board gas→compressor interlock (U15): the compressor relay cannot energize until it is landed and the sensor warmed.

Continuity-test each signal run end-to-end before zip-tying down.

### 6. Bundle, route, and strain-relieve

Bundle by zone:

- **Column-to-cold-core bundle** — the J4 SENSORS loom (SIG-1 + SIG-9 legs), the J6 + J7 reed looms (SIG-2/3/10/11), the J11 gas loom on its drop down the +Y wall, and the carbonator's chassis-ground bond into a single braided-sleeved bundle exiting the back of the column and routing along the +Y wall of the enclosure and forward over the cold core's **lid**, which is where every one of those penetrations is ([`/hardware/printed-parts/cold-core/reservoir/level-sensing.md`](/hardware/printed-parts/cold-core/reservoir/level-sensing.md) for the reed columns).
- **Column-to-manifold bundle** — the J1 + J2 manifold trunks (DC-6/DC-7/DC-8) and DC-3 (diaphragm pump). Braided-sleeve and zip tie; fan-out happens at the manifold.
- **Column-to-compressor bundle** — the [18 AWG](COMP_LEAD_AWG) SJOOW jacketed lead from step 2 is its own bundle; zip-tie tie-down points only.
- **Column-to-front-face bundle** — the J9 DISPLAY loom (SIG-7) and DC-5's 4P ribbon approach the front through the +X half of the enclosure interior. J9 crosses the ridge through its teardrop opening east of the centreline, the side it arrives on, and lands on the 4.3B screw terminals. DC-5 passes inboard of the manifold Wago and tube approaches and lies along the bay bulkhead's crown below the funnel frame, then turns into front-top's lead bore and runs on to the motor tabs in the top clamp. Keep this 4P ribbon bare and its run clear of the funnel frame.
- **Column-to-umbilical bundle** — the J3 FAUCET loom (SIG-6 on the BNTECHGO ribbon) and the under-counter SS plate chassis-ground bond. Both ride the umbilical together up to the under-counter zone; braided sleeve on the column side, separator at the umbilical's bulkhead.
- **Manifold-to-fan branch** — DC-8 leaves the J2 trunk at the manifold and runs alone along the side wall to the condenser fan; female Fastons at the fan.

Provide strain relief at the column exit edge, cold-core penetration, manifold entry and umbilical bulkhead. DC-5's slack, drawn back through the lead bore as the cradle goes home, lies along the bulkhead crown below the funnel frame. Retain the compressor lead at its shared jacket and along the three insulated upper fanouts; keep pull from reaching the terminal landings. Use existing printed-part features. Cable-tie anchors are placed below the cable.

## Output condition

A wired but unenergized chassis:

- The two landable chassis-ground bonds (carbonator, compressor body) land at the single ground bus on the electronics bay; the bus is bonded to the C14 inlet's earth pin. The faucet under-counter plate's conductor is staged and flagged, not landed.
- All AC runs (AC-1 through AC-6) executed and continuity-tested; the AC dielectric / continuity check (step 3) passes
- The compressor's donor moulded cover is intact, unmodified, and securely retained over the terminal/PTC assembly; the appliance lead terminates only at the factory-external electrical interface and the factory lead exit is undamaged
- All DC runs (DC-1 through DC-9) in place — the [12 V](DC_BUS_V) path is contiguous from PSU output to every load, with the fan on the low-side-switched DC-8 branch
- The main board's J1, J2, J3, J4, J6, J7, J9, J11, and J13 looms seated on their labeled wafers and landed at their devices; the J5 relay loom verified seated
- All internal SIG looms landed at both ends; the faucet's pre-fitted plug connects to the rear keystone for the end-to-end SIG-6 check
