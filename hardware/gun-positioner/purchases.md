M5 nuts demand **46**: kit 45 plus **one acquired M5 hex nut**. Use four
acquired 25 mm fender washers at the cable post join/saddle; the remaining
42 M5 head washers fit the kit's 45. M4 nuts demand 25 and M3 nuts demand
36 plus six sacrificial cutting nuts; their kit supplies are 100 and 140.
The two M6 follower nuts/washers come from the kit. M8 sets and shaft end
hardware are supplied with their purchases. Parking pins do not use nuts.

M3 head wells use acquired 7 mm OD x 0.5 mm flat washers. Other print-facing
heads use matching kit flat washers. Check motor-flange head-well clearance
before adding a washer there; supplier-installed screws govern that metal
interface. Spare kit screws of unlisted lengths are not counted as demand.

The acquired M5x50 40-pack supplies sixteen shortened screws. Existing
M3x25 and M3x60 stock supply six each. The [assembly cutting procedure](assembly.md#print-and-prepare)
uses a sacrificial nut around each small screw at the cut to suit the owned
24 TPI metal saw blade. Measure and deburr every finished under-head length.

## Printed material consumption

The [print manifest](../printed-parts/fixtures/pgfun-positioner/print-manifest.json)
reports exact solid volumes, assigned materials, quantities and orientations.
Structural PET-CF17 uses six walls and 100% infill. Camera/control PETG uses
four walls and 25% infill. TPU liners/pads are solid. Manifest mass is
**solid-equivalent**, before supports, skirts and purge; it is not a native
slicer estimate. The final release receipt totals those volumes by material.

The filament is acquired stock, so its additional purchase cash is zero.
Its consumed value is not zero: use the recorded PET-CF17 cost of about
$39.32/kg and PETG $11.20-13.94/kg, with actual sliced/support mass, for the
material charge. Budget roughly $160-200 of stock consumption for this
release; replace that estimate with the native slice when printing.
