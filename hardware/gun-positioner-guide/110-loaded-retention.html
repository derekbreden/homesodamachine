Prove retention with the real load

The brakes must carry the actual gun, wire guide and supported umbilical when motor power or belt support disappears.

AddNo new parts
Tools / setupAccepted torque records · secondary support/catch · camera / indicator log
  1. Record the loaded central pose and each intended extreme pose with its cable routing.
  2. With tube removed and cameras retracted, fit a steel catch tether and rigid stand no more than 5 mm below the cradle; keep ≥10 mm from fixed metal. Remove motor power.
  3. Use the 12.7 µm-graduation indicator on rigid metal; record at 0, 1, 5 and 10 minutes. Separately release belt drive support while preserving the screw brake.
  4. Repeat after initial dry cycling; record any movement and invalidate the coordinate reference if it occurs.
Retention test setup

Laser disabled; tube removed; secondary support present. The test accepts recoverable load support, not 5 µm power-off pose retention.

Ready when

No indicator-resolved growth or camera-visible jump/slip occurs and the catch is not contacted. Lower measured brake torque is at least twice the upper bound of actual backdrive torque. A slipping load or failed torque margin stops loaded motion. Improve passive support/retention and repeat; more motor current cannot repair power-off holding.

This ten-minute test screens coarse gravity retention; it does not establish 5 or 10 µm work-coordinate accuracy.
