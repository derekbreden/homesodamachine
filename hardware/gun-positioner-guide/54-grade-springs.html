Grade the actual spring pairs

Source rate is a setup estimate. Measure the received springs over their used compression range, then match each directional pair before closing a force cage.

Add24 force springs · 3 friction springs · 4 crash springs · numbered result sheet
Tools / setupControlled gauge fixture · quill indicator · guarded metal platen
  1. Number every spring. Seat it between parallel metal platens and record free length and a low-force zero.
  2. Compress slowly through the working range; record force and travel at repeated points and unload to check return.
  3. Fit each local slope Δforce/Δtravel, preserve uncertainty and match the two springs in every directional pair.
  4. Use each axis/direction’s preload in the force-settings table. Set it with measured metal spacers and grade the finished link.
Ready when

Each assigned spring has a measured usable force/deflection range, returns freely and stays clear of coil bind at maximum captured travel. An unknown rate, nonreturning spring or coil-bind approach fails grading. Replace/reassign it or correct the metal compression setting before assembling the link.
