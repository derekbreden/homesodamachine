Make two readable buses

X, Y and Z share UART0. U, V and W share UART1. Each bus has addresses 0, 1 and 2.

AddTwo 330 Ω series resistors · two 1.8 kΩ pull-ups · six address strap pairs
Tools / setupPower unplugged · meter · carrier pin labels
  1. Connect GP0 through 330 Ω to bus A; connect GP1 directly to bus A.
  2. Connect GP4 through 330 Ω to bus B; connect GP5 directly to bus B.
  3. Land X/Y/Z on bus A and U/V/W on bus B; pull each bus to 3.3 V through 1.8 kΩ.
  4. Set the address straps to 0/1/2 within each bus. Label every driver by its axis.
Ready when

A driver appears on one bus and one address. There is no fourth axis sharing either 0/1/2 address. A duplicate address cannot be diagnosed independently. Correct MS1/MS2 straps while unpowered; verify all six by UART before motion.
