Validate optics and the host clock

Each camera needs its own filled fixed-optics receipt or complete VISCA source. The clock receipt binds this Python and the helper build to the session timeline.

  1. Fill each operator receipt from actual focus/exposure/tracking/zoom/white-balance/lens settings, with identity, time and method.
  2. Choose operator or VISCA in each configuration entry, with only its selected transport/source fields populated.
  3. Point clock_check to clock-check.json. Recheck after changing Python, helper build, camera position, lens or optics.
$GP_PY $GP_OBS/observe.py optics check "$GP_SETUP/cam_a-optics.json" --camera-id cam_a
$GP_PY $GP_OBS/observe.py optics check "$GP_SETUP/cam_b-optics.json" --camera-id cam_b
$GP_PY $GP_OBS/observe.py clock-check --out "$GP_SETUP/clock-check.json"
$GP_PY $GP_OBS/observe.py validate-config "$GP_SETUP/cameras.json" --measurement
Ready when

Both optics checks, clock bracket and measurement configuration pass; no required unfilled field or unknown/automatic optics state remains. A stale/wrong-camera receipt, unknown optics or changed clock binding fails measurement readiness. Record actual settings and renew the affected receipt before capture.
