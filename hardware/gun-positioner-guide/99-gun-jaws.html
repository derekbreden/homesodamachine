Pad the metal jaw bars

Four metal bars carry clamp load. Four TPU pads protect the gun body; each pad is backed by metal.

Add4 metal jaw bars / 4 TPU pads · 4 M5 × 25 lower mounts · 4 M5 × 80 through-bolts / two nuts each / two washers
Tools / setup4 mm hex · 8 mm wrench · gun on soft bench support
  1. Bolt the two lower metal bars to the released tray with four M5 × 25 mounts, a washer at both ends and locking nuts; fit their pad faces.
  2. Identify broad gun-body clamping surfaces clear of controls, vents, wire-guide attachment and cable strain relief.
  3. Place the disabled gun on the lower pads while holding it independently.
  4. Fit upper pads/bars and start all four through-bolts loosely.
