Verify all six channels and UART

Repeat the signed motor test on Y, Z, U, V and W. Then verify a six-channel request and the diagnostic response to a missing UART bus.

  1. Run the same +0.0400 / −0.0400 pair on each remaining motor; record connector, sign and completion counts.
  2. Issue the six-axis request below. Observe each requested sign and no unsolicited channel.
  3. Disarm; unplug 24 V and disconnect one UART bus. Restore power and issue clear; it must refuse a healthy ready state.
  4. Unplug 24 V, restore the bus, stop the session and remove the temporary electrical reference.
