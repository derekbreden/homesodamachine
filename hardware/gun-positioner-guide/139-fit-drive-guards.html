Couple the belts and fit guards

Return every physical axis to its central datum before coupling its belt. The three screw brakes remain applied; the guard covers the belt without touching it.

Add6 belt-guards · 12 M3 × 25 / governed washers / locking nuts
Tools / setup24 V and USB unplugged · support under load · small hex key
  1. Support gravity loads and restore X/Y/Z central marks and all three 180 mm actuator lengths.
  2. Couple each belt with the tension/track check from its transmission page.
  3. Transfer the two registered Ø3.4 guard holes from the bulkhead through the matching top angle feet; deburr them and verify M6 washer clearance.
  4. Start each guard fastener loosely. Include the 3.175 mm angle foot in its measured grip and require two full threads beyond the locking nut; seat the guard without bending it.
  5. Hand-turn each supported screw through two turns in each direction.
Ready when

Every belt remains centered and the guards clear belt edges, pulley flanges and brake parts through full rotation. A rubbing guard or shifted datum fails closure. Correct fit mechanically; do not power a rubbing transmission.
