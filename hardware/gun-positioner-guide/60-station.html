Square the common station

The metal rectangle carries the positioner and the existing rotator. Keep the full translating and camera envelopes inside a supported bench area.

Add4040: two × 750 mm, six × 460 mm · metal corners · 325 × 300 mm rotator-platform
Tools / setupSquare · caliper · 6 mm hex · bench clamps
  1. Lay the members flat; start all metal corner fasteners before tightening.
  2. Compare the two diagonals and bring the frame square; seat corners in opposite pairs.
  3. Bolt the rotator platform to both inner crossmembers. Transfer-drill its mounting holes from the existing rotator feet.
  4. Through-bolt the station to the bench and install the existing rotator on the platform.
Ready when

All feet are supported, both diagonals agree within 1 mm, and pushing the frame by hand cannot shift it on the bench. A rocking or sliding frame invalidates the camera datum. Correct bench support and metal corner seating before installing guides.
