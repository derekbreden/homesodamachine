Measure loaded motion and hold temperatures

Fixed-current acceptance includes the motor cases, driver heatsinks and retained brake setting under a repeated loaded sequence and prolonged hold.

AddNo new parts
Tools / setupContact thermometer · documented uncertainty · controller/camera logs
  1. Record each motor-case and driver-heatsink contact temperature at startup.
  2. Repeat the accepted low-speed loaded sequence and holds. Record temperatures every five minutes.
  3. Continue through three consecutive five-minute intervals (15 minutes) with no increasing trend exceeding documented instrument/contact uncertainty.
  4. After the first 30 minutes and hot/cold settling, repeat full-turn brake drag and loaded power-cut checks.
Ready when

Every surface reading plus its uncertainty is ≤50°C; the 15-minute trend, signed response and retention gates all pass. Warning, continued heating, changing drag, lost motion or an unexplained feature jump fails the trial. Stop; a cooling/current/mechanical adjustment starts a new fixed-setting trial series.

A ±2°C tool cannot establish a 1°C plateau. This ceiling protects handling and nearby printed parts; it is not a chip temperature rating.
