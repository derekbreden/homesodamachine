Identify camera A, then camera B

The two cameras can share a product name. Identify each by the inventory change when its labeled USB cable is connected.

  1. Unplug both camera USB cables and run the first inventory command.
  2. Plug in only A and run the second command. Record the one new unique_id and name.
  3. Plug in B and run the third command. Record its one new unique_id and name; label both cables/ports.
$GP_PY $GP_OBS/observe.py cameras --save "$GP_SETUP/cams-none.json"
$GP_PY $GP_OBS/observe.py cameras --new-since "$GP_SETUP/cams-none.json" --save "$GP_SETUP/cams-a.json"
$GP_PY $GP_OBS/observe.py cameras --new-since "$GP_SETUP/cams-a.json"
Ready when

Each connection introduces exactly its intended device, with a distinct recorded unique ID. Multiple new devices or a reused ID makes assignment ambiguous. Repeat that connection/inventory step before capturing frames.
