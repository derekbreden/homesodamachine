Raise the level stages to the seam

The model's camera optical center is an envelope. Align the real image center by adjusting all four metal height posts together, with the PTZ level.

AddNo new parts
Tools / setupActual seam / wire endpoint · camera images · level · 13 mm wrench
  1. With laser disabled, place the real wire endpoint and seam reference in the dry observation workspace.
  2. Adjust all four lower post nuts equally until the real camera image center reaches the seam; keep the plate horizontal.
  3. Lock all post nuts, then trim/deburr surplus stud to no more than 22 mm above the fixed-plate underside.
  4. Set camera/lens spacing and manual focus at the working distance; lock tripod, cassette slots and both return clamps.
Ready when

Both views resolve the required features with rigid locked mounts, no PTZ tilt, no protruding post contact and complementary geometry. An unclear wire endpoint or obstructed view fails observability. Change mount spacing/height or illumination and calibrate again before a response trial.
