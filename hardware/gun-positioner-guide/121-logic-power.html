Keep the controller alive

USB loss must leave the controller powered long enough to detect its watchdog timeout. The unswitched buck supplies that independent logic power.

Add24 V → 5 V buck, ≥0.5 A · 500 mA branch fuse · 1N5819 · VSYS / GND wires
Tools / setupUSB disconnected · motor VM disconnected · voltmeter
  1. Connect buck input + after F0 through its separate 500 mA fuse; input - goes to the common 0 V star.
  2. Power the buck by itself. Adjust and meter its output to 5.0 V, then unplug 24 V.
  3. Route output + → 1N5819 → Pico VSYS, physical pin 39; band faces VSYS.
  4. Connect buck output - to GND. Keep all buck output wiring clear of Pico VBUS.
Ready when

VSYS receives power through the Schottky diode, and 3V3 is present with USB absent while 24 V is on. VBUS has no external buck wire. Missing 3V3 defeats USB-loss protection. Disconnect 24 V and correct buck voltage, diode direction and VSYS/GND routing.
