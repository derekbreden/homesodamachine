Check reset fallback and ENN

Digital UART settings set working current. A reset driver can fall back to its VREF trimmer and straps, so verify the received module's minimum fallback and disable level.

AddNo new parts
Tools / setupMotor plugs disconnected · firmware inhibited · VREF drawing · voltmeter
  1. With VM restored and ENN HIGH, identify the received VREF test point from its drawing.
  2. Measure VREF against module GND and adjust its trimmer to the measured minimum; record each voltage.
  3. Hold Pico RUN low and meter shared ENN with all six modules seated.
  4. Separately enter BOOTSEL and repeat the ENN measurement; preserve physical supports and disconnected motors.
Ready when

ENN remains above 0.7 × measured VIO in RUN-low and BOOTSEL, and each module's minimum VREF is recorded. A carrier pull-down, wrong test point or surviving high fallback current fails reset protection. Remove power and correct the received module/carrier interface before coupling.
