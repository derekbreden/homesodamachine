Build the open roll cradle

Qualified two-bolt split hubs carry torque on plain shafts. Metal plate corners close the frame around the supported output.

Addroll-output-hub · roll-cradle-back · two roll-cradle-side · paired M4 × 50 grade12.9 clamps · plate fasteners and metal corners · 4 M5 × 45 / two washers / locking nut
Tools / setup3 / 4 mm hex · 7 / 8 mm wrenches · square
  1. Fit the qualified output hub and rear plate; start both side members and square them before seating all frame joints.
  2. Start every joint before tightening; bring opposed corners seated progressively.
  3. Restore each qualified clamp joint with both retained bolts and witness marks. Repeating its proof is required after disturbing its grip.
  4. Rotate the supported unloaded frame through its range: yaw/roll ±20°, pitch −20…+10°. Examine each corner clearance.
Ready when

Each hub has a passing torque-proof record and retained axial stack; the unloaded frame has no loose corner or contact inside its intended range. A contacting or loose frame is a failed fit. Correct its metal geometry before attaching a screw actuator.
