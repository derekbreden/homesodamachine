Measure the powered force ceiling

Static switch force and powered motor force are different records. A motor can stall below the static trip setting; record that observed outcome and stop it.

  1. Create a temporary fixture reference at its unloaded center; label this as a test reference, never a gun/tool datum.
  2. Advance one small bounded request at a time while observing force, shuttle travel and actual motion. Start at one-second moves.
  3. Record peak + uncertainty against the named axis/direction ceiling table, before ±1.0 mm capture. Keep the 500 N gauge itself inside range.
  4. If the switch opens, all axes must inhibit. If motion stalls first, issue STOP, record “stall”, abort and do not retry or raise current to force a trip.
Ready when

Both signs at every used rate/load stay below their complete upper force bound, with no shoulder impact or lost load. Separate controlled switch tests prove all-axis latching. Excess force, capture impact, continued motion after an open loop or an unacknowledged stall fails the setting. Support/unplug, correct it and start a new trial.

After every trip/stall, support and deenergize, recenter/inspect the link and restore an actual physical datum. Discard the temporary fixture reference before mounting its drive.
