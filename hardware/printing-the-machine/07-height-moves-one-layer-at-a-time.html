Height

Height moves one layer at a time.

The printer cannot lay half a layer. A height in the drawing is rounded to a whole number of layers, so a change smaller than a layer either vanishes or arrives as a whole layer.

Holds for any filament: it is arithmetic · the example is a PET-GF pocket, 12 November 2026

One pocket roof, four drawingsThree of the four drawn heights print as the same pocket. The fourth prints a whole layer taller.

What was asked

Two test pockets for a connector, one a tenth of a millimetre shorter than the other, to feel which grips better.

What the slice did

A tenth is less than half of a {{petgf:/layer_height}} mm layer. Both wide pockets came out the same height. The narrow pair came out a whole layer apart.

A different variable

Layer height is part of the process, like temperature. Change it and an overhang prints differently, a support sits a different distance under its roof, and each course has a different time to cool. A fit trial that changes it is testing two things.

What we do

Keep the plastic’s own layers. Read the opening from the sliced file, from the top of the last floor course to the underside of the first roof course. When two drawings print the same, say so. To compare heights, draw them a whole layer apart.
