Travel

Every jump carries a bit along.

When the nozzle moves without printing, ASA Aero keeps coming out of it. A bit goes along on every move and is left wherever the nozzle lands. So the float is printed with as few of those moves as its shape allows.

Seen in Bambu ASA Aero · Mark2 · glued Engineering plate · right 0.4 mm hardened nozzleNot tried in: Mark 1

“The one more issue is that every time the nozzle moves and is *not* printing the filament continues to extrude just because of its expansion when heating, and so we end up with bits carried around on each "jump".”
DEREK · 3 OCTOBER 2026, WATCHING THE FIRST PRINT THAT RAN
“So I’m thinking we want to try one piece at a time, and with that piece, just do like 300 walls so that it is all walls and we get concentric the whole way, and no jumps.”
DEREK · THE SAME NIGHT

Close together
