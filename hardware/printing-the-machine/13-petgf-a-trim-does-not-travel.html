First layer · textured PEI

A first-layer trim does not travel.

The printer finds the plate by touching it with its nozzle. On a textured plate it touches the tops of the bumps, so how hard a first layer is pressed in depends on that one plate. The trim is the small correction, found by printing.

Seen in PET-GF · Mark 1 and Mark2 · textured PEI · left 0.4 mm nozzleNot tried in: smooth plates. Carried to PETG on one, this trim failed

Before each print the nozzle touches the plate. On texture it finds the peaks, so the printer then lowers it a hair. Our trim lifts it again.

Forced down into the texture. One plate, 30 August 2026: “more melty pressed in material from first layer into plate”.

Flat roads, joined to each other and keyed to the texture, which stays printed in the face of the part.

Round roads resting on the peaks. They lift, and loose plastic balls up on the nozzle (20 September 2026, next page).

Three days, twenty rungs, two answers

Bambu’s start code says why a textured plate gets a correction: “the nozzle was touching topmost of the texture when homing”. Ours is found by printing. Between 28 and 30 August 2026 a ladder of empty project files was built, z-trim-0.01.3mf to z-trim-0.20.3mf, a hundredth of a millimetre of lift apart, and one at 0.30 to prove the setting did anything. On the first night Derek thought the answer was 0.02, then 0.01. Two days later the ladder had been climbed to its top.

Mark2 prints PET-GF at +{{petgf-setup-pieces:/printers/Mark2/z_trim_mm}} mm and Mark 1 at +{{petgf-setup-pieces:/printers/Mark1/z_trim_mm}} mm: the same model of printer, the same plastic, the same size of nozzle and the same kind of plate.

“it seems one of the confounding variables is which textured PEI plate is used.”
DEREK · 30 AUGUST 2026

Not to another printer. On 8 October 2026 a lower trim, tried once on Mark 1, had been written down as Mark 1’s new rule. “The +0.04 trim was a *special* case for a problem with a *specific* print - not a change to the rule for Mark1.”

Not to another plastic or plate. On 6 November 2026 Mark 1’s PET-GF trim rode along onto a PETG job on a glued smooth plate. “lol, okay, print stopped, it lifted right off.” Then: “No. That should be documented as for pet-gf on textured plate.”
