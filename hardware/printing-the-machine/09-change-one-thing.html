One control, once

The control is the current recipe, printed on the same plate as its variations. Once it has been printed and judged it is not printed again.

About an hour

A coupon plate that takes an hour gets tried the same evening. A trial that takes a day gets tried once.

“This amount of TPU costs less than your tokens, so please just start a print once you are ready.”
DEREK · 13 OCTOBER 2026 · TPU

Both printers

When two printers are loaded and clear, the plate is split across them. One sitting loaded and idle is a wasted evening.

“Both printers are available and its my bed time. Print as you see fit.”
DEREK · 15 OCTOBER 2026

Then write down what happened

Every print keeps its photographs, the file that was sent, and what Derek said when he held it, word for word. When he says a part is good enough, that goes on record against the part, with the flaw he named.

Where this book says Still open, it is usually because a trial changed two things at once and worked. The result is real. Which change did it is not known.
