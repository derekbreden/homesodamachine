A. STORY
Printing began in March 2026 on one Bambu Lab H2C; history holds no earlier printer. ("X1" here is a laser welder, /Users/derekbredensteiner/Developer/homesodamachine/tools/x1_control/README.md; the only X1-class trace is an agent's assumed "typical 256mm print bed" where the H2C has 325 x 320, commit bc0b1729b.) Spring was PET-CF on 0.4 mm hotends: clogs, air-printing, and an induction heating assembly destroyed while poking a clog, which bought the second H2C; a 0.6 mm tungsten-carbide nozzle then printed first try. August moved the exterior to PET-GF15 on the left 0.4 mm nozzle and opened the first-layer question with a 0.01-0.20 mm Z-trim sweep. In September agents began sending jobs, which surfaced breaker trips, dropped sends, idle-filament overloads and unseasoned plate edges. October-November narrowed every rule after PET-GF settings leaked onto PETG three times. It converged on one loop: prepare from the STL, slice with Bambu Studio's command line, read the emitted G-code back, send once, prove the new job id, file Derek's words and photos.

B. LESSONS
1. A first-layer Z trim belongs to one printer, plate, material and nozzle side. Why: homing touches the texture peaks and plates differ; identical H2Cs settled at +0.04 (Mark2) and +0.18 (Mark1). The PET-GF trim rode onto PETG three times (Oct 6 blob, Oct 20 reservoir, Nov 6 chips). Evidence: /Users/derekbredensteiner/Developer/homesodamachine/hardware/printed-parts/z-trim.md; /Users/derekbredensteiner/Developer/homesodamachine/hardware/printed-parts/enclosure/display-cover/prints/2026-11-06-identification-set-mark1/physical-result.json. Derek: "No. That should be documented as for pet-gf on textured plate." (commit 926988d8a); "lol, okay, print stopped, it lifted right off." Stated.
2. Trust only what the machine receives. Why: a Studio re-save silently moved nozzle 290 to 265, the trim and the flow type (commit 72c9b062f); the PET-GF slot is a PET-CF clone, so its type, density and grams are wrong. The review reads the archive: checksum, the emitted G29.1 trim, bead envelope, layer bands, supports against show faces and sealed interiors. Evidence: /Users/derekbredensteiner/Developer/homesodamachine/tools/hsm/print-prep/src/review.rs. Stated in the tools.
3. Keep every bead (model, support, brim) 20 mm inside an unseasoned plate, 80 mm for small plates, measured on emitted paths. Why: never-printed textured PEI rejects first layers; four sends of a bed-filling part failed, then one sacrificial layer at Bambu's stock offset seasoned the edges. Evidence: /Users/derekbredensteiner/Developer/homesodamachine/hardware/printed-parts/fixtures/fake-countertop/print-jobs.json. Derek: "The seasoning worked."; "You can't print that close to the edge of the bed on a plate that hasn't been seasoned properly first." (commit 464f0465c). Margin stated; the seasoning recipe lives only in that record.
4. "Extrusion motor overloaded" has several causes; change one variable. Idle loaded filament: cut at the spool, unload, pull the piece, reload. Clogged hotend: swap. Nozzle too close: two drying cycles, a spare hotend and rethreading failed; +0.05 mm on the right nozzle printed clean. Evidence: /Users/derekbredensteiner/Developer/homesodamachine/hardware/printed-parts/enclosure/display-cover/prints/2026-11-08-identification-set-mark1-right-z005/README.md. Derek: "Let's see if we can change just that one variable of the Z offset and try again." (commit d85cd06c1). Synthesis inferred.
5. A send is proven only by a new job id: one Send per dialog, never repeat an uncertain one. Why: sends during a filament load vanish silently; a just-loaded hot nozzle refuses (0502400D); an identical archive was refused (05004046) until renamed; the dialog can offer the other printer; the printer ignores unsigned MQTT commands. Evidence: /Users/derekbredensteiner/Developer/homesodamachine/tools/bambu-printers.md. Derek: "I don't think errors on the touchscreen need to be dismissed." (commit 2ee77288b). Stated.
6. Record every print and keep the clip count low. Why: the Oct 6 blob that cost a heating assembly ran with timelapse off, leaving no video; later recording stopped at 128 clips with the option On. Evidence: /Users/derekbredensteiner/Developer/homesodamachine/hardware/printed-parts/zone-c/funnel-mold/print-log.md; /Users/derekbredensteiner/Developer/homesodamachine/tools/h2c-timelapse-gc.md. Derek: "maybe its not a space issue but a 128 limit issue" (commit 62ab84c80). Stated; limit unconfirmed.
7. Start or resume the two printers at least 180 s apart. Why: 54 s apart tripped the breaker; 236 s did not. Evidence: /Users/derekbredensteiner/Developer/homesodamachine/hardware/printed-parts/enclosure/tee-readiness/full-enclosure-print/native-slice-reviews/2026-09-28-shared-circuit-stagger/README.md. Stated; load unmeasured.
8. Do not leave a loaded printer idle: split work across both, give a lone job to the one idle-and-loaded longest, purge after idle. Evidence: /Users/derekbredensteiner/Developer/homesodamachine/tools/h2c-purge/README.md; result at 05a3ada62:tools/h2c-purge/trial-v1/result.json. The purged blob can land on the bed. Stated; moisture unproven.
9. Abrasive filament runs only on the left (H2D-type) hotend; the right is induction, H2C-only. Keep spares; never push a clog from below. Evidence: /Users/derekbredensteiner/Developer/homesodamachine/hardware/ledger/tools.md; /Users/derekbredensteiner/Developer/homesodamachine/hardware/printed-parts/faucet/faucet-shell/print-log.md. Derek: "So far, the diamond PCD has been the toughest and most reliable, in other sizes." Stated.
10. The sliced plate type must be the plate on the bed; clear PETG and TPU moved to a glued Engineering Plate. Why: plate type sets Z compensation and the printer checks it (0500-8051). Evidence: /Users/derekbredensteiner/Developer/homesodamachine/hardware/printed-parts/faucet/tpu-o-ring/prints/2026-10-23-black-tpu85a-right06-mark2/cancelled-plate-mismatch/README.md. Derek: "we've had persistent lifting here. So I've gone ahead and switched Mark 1 out to the engineering plate and glued it up." (commit c6ddc49a6). Inferred.
11. Dry on the maker's cycle and feed from a box the spool turns freely in; low air humidity does not prove dry filament, and drying changed nothing when moisture was not the cause. Evidence: tools.md above, "What dries where". Stated.
12. Measure two-nozzle registration with a coplanar coupon and correct it in the slice, not the CAD. Why: after Auto calibration Mark2 still needed X -0.50, Y +0.70 mm. Evidence: /Users/derekbredensteiner/Developer/homesodamachine/hardware/printed-parts/calibration/dual-nozzle-registration/README.md. Stated; per printer and nozzle pair.
13. PET-GF: part fan off except bridges, overhangs and the courses just above; a lone small part needs time between layers. A 0% overhang threshold in Bambu Studio cools every outer wall. Evidence: /Users/derekbredensteiner/Developer/homesodamachine/hardware/printed-parts/cooling/README.md. Derek, on fan-off rounds: "That turned out worse. Exploded basically." Stated.
14. TPU and ASA Aero: place objects close together; no AMS feeds TPU. Evidence: /Users/derekbredensteiner/Developer/homesodamachine/hardware/printed-parts/fixtures/tube-insertion/prints/2026-10-12-tpu-liners-mark1/failure.json. Derek: "With both ASA Aero and TPU I am finding that the distance between objects increases risk of problems on these "unclean start/stop" filaments." Stated; unmeasured.
15. A request to print means the bed is clear; never ask for spool weight; never pause a print to think. Derek: "If I tell you to print something, you print something. I will not tell you to print something if the plate is not clear." (fake-countertop record above). Evidence: /Users/derekbredensteiner/Developer/homesodamachine/hardware/printed-parts/AGENTS.md. Stated.

C. BAKED-IN BASICS
PET-GF feeds from the left external spool and reads PET-CF everywhere. Job names carry the trim (z004, z018). Standing options: Timelapse On, bed leveling On, flow and nozzle-offset calibration Auto, clump probing Off. Every job binds STL, project, archive and G-code hashes to a launch receipt. FAILED just means idle, and "layer 0" is not the failure height. Fits are tested on coupons cropped from the production part, in its pose and layers. A swapped hotend is re-read at the touchscreen. AMS dryers are stopped before launch.

E. SURPRISES
New textured PEI must be seasoned. Two identical printers need trims 0.14 mm apart. A 0.05 mm lift cured an overload that drying and a new hotend did not. Agents print by driving Bambu Connect through macOS accessibility, because the printer ignores unsigned commands. A lone small PET-GF cap uses smooth timelapse plus a prime tower purely as a cooling dwell.
