Fan

PET-GF prints with its fan off except over air, and went brittle when the fan was left on. Soft TPU prints with the fan full on, and its overhanging lips curled the moment it was turned down.

Flow

The reservoir’s recipe feeds a little more plastic than the lines call for. The float’s feeds about half, because that plastic foams as it leaves the nozzle.

Plate

PET-GF goes on bare textured plate. The other three print on a smooth plate with glue, and clear PETG got there by lifting off the textured one.

Pace

Each plastic has a limit on how much can be pushed through the nozzle each second, and the limit sets the speed. Soft TPU’s is a small fraction of PET-GF’s.
