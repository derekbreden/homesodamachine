How this book is kept

The page you read is the file an agent reads.

The book lives in the machine’s repository, beside the parts it describes. Each page is one plain file.

Where it is

The pages are hardware/printing-the-machine/NN-title.html, numbered in reading order. README.md in the same folder is the index: every page’s rule, by plastic, and where to start for the job in hand.

Where its pictures come from

Every photograph was taken at the bench and saved with the print it shows. art/manifest.json lists each one with the file and revision it was copied from. Pictures of the model are drawn from the same geometry the printers get.

Where its numbers come from

Settings in the current recipes are read from their project files each time the book is bound; guide-inputs.json names those files. Numbers from trials are as they were measured, with a date.

To add a page

Start from a print: its date, printer, plastic, plate and nozzle, and a photograph. Say what happened, then the rule it taught, then how far that has been tested. Put it in the part for its plastic. Link the record.

To bind it

tools/run hsm-manual-guides bind printing-the-machine draws every page, stops on any whose content overflows, and writes the PDF this was read from.

What is not in it yet

How far PET-GF leans, and its bridges and fins. Screws and heat-set inserts. Why clear PETG moved to a glued plate, and PETG’s molds. Reading back the file the printer receives. Each has prints on record and no page.

Who made it

The prints, the photographs and the judgements are Derek’s, made with AI agents over nine months of 2026. The pages were written and drawn from that record by Claude (Fable 5.2) in November 2026.
