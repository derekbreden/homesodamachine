Both Kamoer pumps ride the pump cradle under its top clamp, and the cradle is pushed into front-top's bay once, at the factory. The four prepared pump tubes pass the tee wall's fore band through one teardropped Ø11.93 mm hole each into the four PP0208E pump tees. Each tee's branch collet stands extended in that hole and grips its tube like any push-fit, and those four collets hold the cradle home. A tee pushed fore lands its fixed barrel on the bore face, clear of the collet.

The SB443-IN magnet columns in the cradle's back and front-top's bay face, the YYFKGCP pogo contact pair and its M1.4 hardware, the collet plate's pressing face, the cradle's two hand pockets and the customer pump-replacement procedure are gone. The J13 ribbon passes front-top's straight Ø11 mm lead bore and the clamp's lead slot, whose floor rises at 45° to turn the leads up, and runs along two vee-floored crown grooves to four Faston receptacles on the motor tabs. Every physical fault, the pumps' included, is answered by shipping a replacement unit.
