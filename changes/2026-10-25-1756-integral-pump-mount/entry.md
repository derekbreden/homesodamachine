The upper pump inverse is part of front-top. Its broad stock continues into the display housing and roof, with fitted contours confined to the pump pockets and the required wire and tube passages.

Both pumps sit in the loose rectangular retainer while their four tubes and motor leads are prepared. This unit rises motor-first through the open underside of front-top. Three M3 × 60 screws enter from below and engage the inserts in the fixed upper stock. Bottom-open gates admit the prepared tubes.

All four prepared tee, upper-tube and fore-valve groups install straight from aft before the rear valve row and returns. The two adjacent WAGO holders have one broad fore plane that clears this approach. Pump and display leads share one rear passage. The display housing has one flat inner back; the cable exit opens over the bulkhead lip. Two plain cleanouts open the display-wing support pockets into the empty PCB room while retaining 3 mm beneath the glass seat.
