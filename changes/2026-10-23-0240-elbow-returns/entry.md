Each lower return is a NeoFit ATEU44W stem elbow in the tee's lower collet, its stem cut so its shoulder stands on the collet face, one straight LLDPE tube, and a second stem elbow in the rear valve's bottom collet with 3.75 mm of stem below it, so both sockets face each other level. The R14 hairpins are gone. V-G and V-J drop 8 mm to V-C and V-D's height so all four returns are alike; flavor B's riser keeps its plane over the aft row.

The collet plate's release face stands one collet stroke deeper, 4.675 mm behind the bay face, and holds each tee's branch collet pressed home. A pressed collet does not grip: each pump tube slides in on the collet's O-ring and draws straight out when the cartridge is pulled, and the cartridge's magnets hold the tubes in against pressure. No tee moves, and the tee wall's aft face follows the tee 1.5 mm further aft.
