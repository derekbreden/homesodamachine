The cartridge captures the molded pump skirts between upper and lower lips on continuous
front and rear channels. Its closed end joins the channels and lies on the print bed.
The pumps slide in through the opposite end before the unit rises into front-top.
The heads, motors and tube outlets remain open.

The enclosure cavity grows inward from the front and tee walls on corbels. Sloped
transitions over the pump bosses and motors close their rooms into the display housing.
The cartridge's front channel enters a pocket inside the enclosure wall.

The modeled cartridge is connected and clears the enclosure and pump bodies. Its bearing
lips stand along the build direction. This is a geometry concept: enclosure attachment,
lateral pump stops and tube-setting registration remain open, and there is no physical
print or fit result for this cartridge.
