The corner coupon seats under light finger pressure. The frame has a fore and
an aft screw on each front flank to hold that seated fit along the flange.
Each new socket head is recessed below the silicone apron. It threads into the
same full-length heat-set insert used at the fore station, on a ledge rooted
into the sidewall.
