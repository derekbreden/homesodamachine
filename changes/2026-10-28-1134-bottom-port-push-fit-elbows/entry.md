Each bottom-plate port carried a TAISHER street elbow with a push-fit adapter threaded into its side: the gray PI010822S on the CO2 inlet, the black PP010822E on the carbonated-water outlet. Each now carries one black acetal neoFit AME4-N2-E. Its 1/4" NPTF thread enters the plate, and its push-fit socket turns the line 90° and takes the tube itself.

- One part and one taped thread fewer on each port, and about $10 less per port.
- The socket stands 0.431 in over the thread's shoulder, so the two bottom lines run at z 20.8 instead of 17. They keep their slots through the support ring, and the card stays clear.
- The top plate keeps its street elbows: Port 2's jet is welded to its elbow, and Port 4's relief valve threads into its elbow.
- The installed thread engagement is unmeasured. The first-article check on a tapped scrap of plate measures it before the first carbonator.
