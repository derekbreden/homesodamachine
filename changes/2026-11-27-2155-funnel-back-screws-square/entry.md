On a 45° seat an upright screw's pull turns into a sideways push: each back seat pressed back-top's side band outward by between a third of and the whole clamp force, depending on friction. A stiffness model of the published back-top puts that at 0.06 to 0.24 mm of outward movement at the seat for every 100 N of push, the larger figure for the printed walls and gyroid infill.

Each back screw now crosses its seat at the same station, square to it. Its insert opens flush on the seat and presses in from above through the open roof pocket, and back-top's ledge ends higher than it did. The frame's bore leaves the wedge square to it, and the head seats in a trough cut square to the screw, 1.1 mm clear of the silicone brim's rounded corner; the trough leaves the driver a straight way in from above the funnel opening. The raised rear head pads are gone. Both back screws are the M3 × 12 the front ones use. The picture shows the west side; the east is its mirror.
