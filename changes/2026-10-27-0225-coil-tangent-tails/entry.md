The inlet uses a vertical dogleg after its plan offset. The outlet uses two
quarter bends and a vertical straight. All tail bends have a 15 mm centerline
radius, and every junction is tangent. Both tails meet their shell exit stations
at Y=±77.50 mm, Z28.425 mm, pointing toward −X.

The [two-sheet production drawing](/read/cold-core-layout/evaporator-coil-drawing.pdf)
includes tube outlines, toleranced winding and exit dimensions, tangent-station
tables, working lengths, stock cut and the local relief over the reed bridge.
