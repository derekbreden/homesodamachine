The carbonator rod, float magnet and external reed bridge sit on the cold core’s −Y side, opposite both reservoirs’ +Y sensing stations. Both end-cap rod registers and the evaporator’s bridge relief follow that location. Reservoir magnet axes are 144.0 mm from the carbonator reeds.

A dedicated Ø7.5 mm straight passage through the upper cup and the very top lid carries the three carbonator reed conductors. The temperature bus and protective earth retain their separate passage. Above the lid, the reed leads pass beneath the pump and approach the PCB from the west; the longest relocated conductor is 395.7 mm, within its 500 mm preparation blank. Native checks found no intersections with hardware or other conductors.

A 4 mm stainless upper float stop holds the relocated float below the inlet tip. The upright travel envelope includes the full radial rod play and clears the modeled inlet jet by 1.75 mm nominal, or 1.55 mm at the stop’s minimum length. Installed jet projection, stop attachment, float tilt and reed switching still require physical qualification before vessel closure.

The end-cap, vessel-weldment and evaporator drawings, stock/BOM and attended-labor figures, assembly instructions and carbonation animation assets match the sensing position and cable route.
