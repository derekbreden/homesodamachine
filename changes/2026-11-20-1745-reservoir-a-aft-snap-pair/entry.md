Reservoir A's fill and draw lines cross the lid on their way to the reservoir. At the fore station they press down into a pair of integral clips. At the aft station they lay in an open stepped saddle and one tie held them both, threaded through a slot under the saddle.

The aft station now has the same clips as the fore pair. Each is turned along its line's last level turn before the reservoir and ends where that turn ends, so the lines keep their routes. The draw line's clip stands on the taller root. The fill line's is filled square against that root up to its jaw's top, which makes the pair one body with no slot between the two.

The tie leaves the parts list and the assembly steps, and the lid's print needs no blocker for a tie passage. Neither clip has been printed.
