The back-top prints ceiling-down, so the top of the meter receiver is the first of it to print. Beside the meter cap's top hook pocket, a narrow strip of it runs along the east flank, and its top bridged the whole length of the pocket. On the stopped Mark 1 print that first ledge hung loose strands.

The strip's top now climbs from the pocket's edge to the flank at the slope the enclosure's teardrop roofs print at, so each course steps out a little from the wall below it and nothing bridges there. The meter cap's hook tip stops short of it and only moves away from it as the wing lifts.
