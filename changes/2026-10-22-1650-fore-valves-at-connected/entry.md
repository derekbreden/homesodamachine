With each fore valve over the middle of its tee's travel, the upper tube stood 0.75 mm off straight at connected. Its spring pulled the tee forward and, through the bottomed pump tube, pushed the cartridge off its magnets and the collets toward release. On a straight-beam estimate with the collets as clamps that came to about 11 to 40 N a tube, against 60.9 N for all four magnet pairs at their steel rating.

Over the connected axis the upper tube carries nothing in service. At release it bends the tee's whole 1.5 mm stroke, which only adds to the pull. The fore valve tray, the outer coils' yoke pockets and the pogo lead bore's run-out move aft with the valves.
