The cable bank grows directly from the cold-core lid beside the retained PCB.
Its two upward-opening channels share a filled base and a common wall. The
floor, outer walls and shared wall use 3 mm stock; each channel has 2.2 mm
clear depth, a 16 mm span and a 0.75 mm retaining beak.

A full-span 45° heel clears the neighboring corner screw pocket while keeping
the complete floor beneath both cable seats. The original PCB keeps its
mounting datums.

Derek approved the modeled arrangement: "This looks good to me. Approved to implement it or anything like it. I think we're on the right path."

The four-lead channel follows the accepted cable-coupon section. The complete
bank, seven-lead grouping and installed dressing await a physical fit result.
