The production assembly places the scanned exterior in its existing reference frame. The
molded casing roots, mounting holes, motor ventilation and terminal details are visible.
Separate component colors identify the adapter, fasteners, primer and flexible sleeves.
Inserted scanning stubs are excluded; the assembly supplies its routed tubing.

The reference is reconstructed from complementary captures at fixed scale. Held-out native
observations compare the resulting surface with the head, flange, adapter, motor and rigid
casing regions. The [reference review](https://github.com/derekbreden/homesodamachine/blob/main/hardware/reference/kamoer-kphm400/scan-review.md)
links the reconstruction recipe, measurements and limitations.

Derek asked for “a model that is based on the scan” and to “update our production assembly
with this proper model as well.”
