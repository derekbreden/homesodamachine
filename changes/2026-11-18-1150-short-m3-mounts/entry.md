The short black M3 stock fastens the PCB, condenser, nameplate, display cover and status plate. The [mechanical BOM](/hardware/ledger/bom.md#13-mechanical-attach-hardware) owns the allocation.

The PCB screws bear directly on the mounting pads. The board stays on the equipment lid's integral posts. Condenser screws bear through the same flat washers specified at the mains inlet, spanning the donor flange holes.

The nameplate's blind hosts retain closed backing, with matching sealed recesses in the cold-core casing. The front housing's display and status-plate pilots retain regular inserts beneath their head seats.

The Teyleten relay PCB holes take M2 mounting screws, as Derek identifies in the [received-board record](/hardware/reference/teyleten-relay/mounting-fit.json). Printed pins locate those holes. The end-clamp M3 screws enter separate wall hosts outside the PCB and pass through the printed clamps only.

The [nameplate fit record](/hardware/printed-parts/enclosure/nameplate/production-fit.json) retains the mesh review and physical-assembly scope. The [relay mounting record](/hardware/printed-parts/enclosure/enclosure/relay-mount-fit.json) records the external clamp path and its board clearance.
