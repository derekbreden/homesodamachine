Two 45° catches seated the insert in a blind pocket under the frame. In the Mark1 coupon the insert stayed in place and guided the tube. [Coupon fit record](https://github.com/derekbreden/homesodamachine/blob/58f35ce9e1336dfb37f06bd2e3833dc7f65dfe6c/hardware/print-tests/funnel-dispense-snap/prints/mark1/physical-result.json)

The frame printed on its feet and the clip on its flat end. The native slice left the pocket clear of support and built both spring leaves on every clip layer.
