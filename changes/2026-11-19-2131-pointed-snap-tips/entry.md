Each rear cover's leaf carries one planar lead-in from its solid nose to a pointed free edge. On the ASSE, Pisco and water-regulator receivers the chamfered catch is cut down into the ledge's corner, so the hook's nose seats at the catch plane and the whole lead-in stands under the recessed ceiling. The catch faces keep the shared 45° slope and print without support.

The meter's catch climbs from its lip into the sideways pocket. Its cover's point runs to just short of the flank.

Derek: "I'd also like the pointier wing tips we had at one point, or some compromise between those and the blunted tips we have now."
