The raised pad runs forward to the seam rib. Its entire 81.55 mm span continues up to the roof, filling the upper front corner beneath the rear-west funnel screw. The finished pad clears the removable frame by 1.86 mm and the closed front-top joint by 0.25 mm.

From front to rear, the wells hold compressor neutral, compressor hot and diaphragm-pump positive. Each well captures the blank rear half of a 221-413. With the funnel frame out, the installer can close the levers and push the connector into its seat. All six leads follow the seated connectors in the production assembly.

The first Mark1 slice of this bank left all three wells open for 79 layers, then closed each 8.70 mm span with 24 transverse bridge lines. The subsequent production revision adds removable interfaces under all six back-top WAGO roofs and clearance for the supported faces.
