The ten M5 stations have continuous Ø11.6 mm hosts, with 2.6 mm of material around each insert pilot and 3.2 mm around the screw tails. Their bottom blind ends narrow above the complete cylindrical clearances. The CO2 descent and water line follow fitted R14 paths around the hosts; their port endpoints stay fixed.

Both sensing windows keep their full rectangular openings below peaked roofs, with 4.45 mm of header stock. The B cable outlet has a separate peaked roof and a 4.2 mm header. The actual native shell slice has no support bodies and a 21.11 mm minimum full-bead bed margin.

Local solid regions fill the M5 hosts, head bearings and wall/floor roots. The separate bottom cap's ten short, outward-open head pockets use its scoped Normal(auto)/Snug preparation, with 21.02 mm full-bead bed margin. The native print and post-publication surface readings are in [the casing review](/hardware/printed-parts/cold-core/foam-shell/printability-review.json).
