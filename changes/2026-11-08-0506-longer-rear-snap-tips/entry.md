The ASSE, Pisco, flow-meter, water-regulator and rear pump caps use the same longer planar lead-in. Their spring walls, root blends, catches, lateral reach and hardware seats retain their approved geometry.

The enclosure ceiling and cold-core lid carry complete pockets for the extended points. The geometry comparisons show clear seated caps and unchanged lower profiles. The nearby components remain clear in the full machine.

Derek's physical approval applies to the longer regulator tips: “The longer pointed tips on the snaps there worked beautifully.” The other caps carry that profile and need their own physical verification.
