The 10-way manifold connector in the front top has a 15.70 × 30.10 mm opening. Each 3-way connector has an 8.20 × 18.70 mm opening. Six sit in the back top, and four sit on the cold-core lid. The first dimension runs between the gripping cheeks. The second runs from the pocket floor to its roof in print orientation.

The blind seats still take 8.70 mm of the 10-way body and 9.45 mm of each 3-way body. Each pocket prints with a removable tree rooted on its floor. The 5-way manifold opening has its own Mark2 coupon trial.
