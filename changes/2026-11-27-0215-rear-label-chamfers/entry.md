The exterior stands under the rear label channels were difficult to remove. The rear wall now grows into the open 38 mm of each channel on a 45° slope. At DRAIN, the outer rail also carries the part of the enclosure that flares past the label face.

The gold surface in the section is the bottom label. Its matching chamfer has 0.15 mm of clearance measured perpendicular to the enclosure face. Two 1.30 mm-wide flats at the ends of the label sit behind the rails and hold its bore at the fitted height as the column is loaded.
