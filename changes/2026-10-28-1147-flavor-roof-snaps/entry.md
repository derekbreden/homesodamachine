The two 4 mm dispense lines rise onto straight roof runs before reaching the
paired guides at Y354 and Y390. Each station joins both seats with one broad
filled base. The mouths face downward for loading from inside the open back-top.
The roof carries both stations while the funnel frame remains removable.

The neighboring Reservoir B fill tube follows a smooth lower path between its
existing fittings. In the route view, the highlighted tubes carry flavor and
the black tube fills Reservoir B.

Flavor A connects V-G to the east rear socket at X77.5; flavor B connects V-J
to the west socket at X37.25. The identified umbilical tails follow those same
logical assignments.

The 4 mm guides use 4.10 mm bores, 1.52 mm spring walls and 8 mm spans. Their
scaled C2 fit remains provisional pending a printed trial.
