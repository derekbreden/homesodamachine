The bench trials settled the shape of the spring and its mating cheek. The production enclosure now carries that pair at the ASSE valve, Pisco, water regulator and sideways Digiten meter. The Pisco keeps its fitted saddle, shortened fore-and-aft grip and beveled entry past the rear corner. The regulator keeps the 0.40 mm saddle adjustment that Derek reported “fit very well.” The meter has extra clearance on the upper guide where it prints against removable support.

The carbonator lid carries the same working section along both sides of the motor. The motor seats on its rails, then the cover bears on the can before either spring foot bottoms. The sliced cap and lid give about 1.27 mm of hook engagement per side at the modeled motor contact. Fit and vibration with the running pump still need a physical check.
