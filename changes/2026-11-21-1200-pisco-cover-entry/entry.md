Derek’s [fit photos](https://github.com/derekbreden/homesodamachine/blob/4868810416257601dd2a82782c85945eaa940442/hardware/print-tests/rear-equipment-corner/prints/2026-11-20-mark2/physical-result/physical-result.json) show the Pisco cover partway into its receiver, tipped toward the adjacent meter seat. The outside corner of the cover catches the projecting meter shoulder as it swings in.

The meter seat has a flat shoulder over the upper screw-boss passage. Its mating spring and hook end together at the opening for the Pisco cover. The lower spring and the spine run the length of the meter housing. The fit plate carries a roof-down cut of the production enclosure and the matching meter cover.

Derek assembled all three components in the printed corner with their snap covers fitted. The Pisco cover clears the meter shoulder and has the most play when rocked by hand.
