The shell reaches the uniform 7.4 mm equipment lid directly. Its open mouth leaves the upper foam space accessible for the body pour. The vessel and coil stations stay at their fixed datums, within the 253.4 mm installed casing height.

Ten M5 × 100 screws on each face use the same stock as the enclosure seams. Continuous Ø11.6 mm hosts carry the insert pilots and screw tails into the shell walls. The shell, lid and bottom cap each have solid print regions through their screw bearings and supporting roots.

The bottom cap has an open top and a 2 mm outside floor, leaving 17.85 mm of central foam depth. Attach it to the empty shell, invert the shell and distribute the charge through four Ø30 mm underside ports. Two Ø6 mm vents release air. Cure, trim and foil-patch the bottom before turning the casing upright and installing the vessels.
