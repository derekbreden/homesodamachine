The rear nameplate's two lower insert hosts dip into the shell's rear wall. Each takes a recess from outside, and a wall inside the shell closes the recess off from the foam. The west recess's closing wall ended almost flush with the side of the rear west screw host. A narrow strip of the wall's end stayed showing beside the host's round, the height of the wall, with a hairline groove between the two.

A closing wall that ends beside a screw host now runs on to the host's axis. Its inner face meets the host's round, and its end is inside the host. [See the wall on the shell.](https://homesodamachine.com/3d#step:manifold-layout%2Fenclosure-assembly.step?find=solid%3A%20cold-core%2Ffoam-shell%0Abox%3A%20x%3D-80.000%20y%3D453.000%20z%3D176.000%20to%20x%3D-50.000%20y%3D463.000%20z%3D199.000%20%22Nameplate%20closing%20wall%20and%20rear%20west%20screw%20host%22%0Aview%3A%20az%3D-70%20el%3D50)

The closing walls join the casing with the screw hosts, before any passage is cut, so the screw's passage through the host is whole.

The shell's lint reported the strip as a sliver and reports none now. The shell's print plan is sliced from this shell.
