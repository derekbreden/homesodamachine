Back-top prints ceiling-down, and these changes take out faces that would print badly that way. The fore meter's lane to the east wall was a flat beam over open air. It now rises off the wall on a 45° corbel, and each meter seat's beam starts on the seat's own west plane.

On the rear wall, the keystone jack's boss stands a full wall proud of the inner face instead of 0.7 mm, and the jack's pocket runs on through it behind the catches. The lever-nut wells above it stand as one tower to the ceiling. The west core hold's east face moves 0.125 mm east onto the jack pocket's west wall. The saddles' tie windows close at 45°. The added stock meets no other body in the machine.
