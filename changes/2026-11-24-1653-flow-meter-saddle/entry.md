Derek found that the +0.40 mm flow-meter cover fit better than +0.20 mm, and +0.20 mm fit better than the original. Both raised covers showed signs of incomplete seating. The new cover tests whether the meter-facing saddle was making contact too early.

The housing contour, neck passages, ledges and spring roots move 0.40 mm outward with the back of the cover. Each wing now measures 32.45 mm from root to crest, up from 32.05 mm on the earlier +0.40 mm cover. Both hooks and their flat crests retain that cover’s receiver geometry.

When both crests meet the receiver wall in the model, 0.45 mm of clearance remains toward the nominal meter face. The bench fit will show whether this gives the physical housing enough room to seat.
