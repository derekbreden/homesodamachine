The back top prints upside down. The section shows where the supported face meets the compressor-neutral connector. The extra roof clearance brings the nominal gap to 0.40 mm. The backing grows by the same amount. The side walls of each pocket still grip the 221-413 across 8.70 mm with 9.45 mm of insertion depth.

The three compressor and pump connections sit near the front seam. The hot, earth and neutral connections sit farther aft on the same wall. All six mouths face into the enclosure so the supports can be removed before the connectors are installed. [Back-top Mark1 print plan](/parts#material:HSM-0081?view=print)
