When Derek pressed the outlet in the printed corner, the pointed front collar toggled between two positions. The collar with raised flats stayed put on the same regulator and rear collar. The production cover carries those flats along both wings.

The full-length Mark1 cover earned Derek’s verdict. “The +0.20 mm cover is great. Works very well, more than just good enough.”
