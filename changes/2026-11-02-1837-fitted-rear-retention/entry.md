The ASSE cap encloses both axial shoulders of the 1/4 NPT–3/8 NPT adapter's
hex, with 0.15 mm fitted air. Its lower body reaches Y461.078. The ASSE body,
attached outlet fittings and drain sleeve have ±1.5 mm of axial adjustment
around that fixed adapter. Solid stock above the sleeve window joins the
adapter pocket to the cap.

The PISCO cap follows the registered body and exposed outlet neck with
0.15 mm radial and 0.25 mm axial air. One 3 mm upper receiver bearing locates
the largest body ring. The rear bore and the cap's axial stops retain their
positions. Installed service uses a 3.6 mm release drop, outlet-check
withdrawal, then a 45 mm fore slide with the leaves held open.

The meter's cap and receiver follow the housing taper and label-side bevel.
Bosses and screw heads have separate fitted passages. The wire exit and
sixteen-millimetre hardware loading corridor remain open.

[Three complete retention pairs](../../hardware/print-tests/rear-snap-retention/v5/README.md)
exercise these fits in black PET-GF, with production print orientations,
2 mm leaves and centered flathead release entries. The active Mark1 and
Mark2 trial continues; the backed ASSE revision is separately prepared for
a clear plate. Native geometry checks do not establish printed clamp force
or repeated-cycle life.
