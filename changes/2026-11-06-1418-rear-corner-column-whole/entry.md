The mains connectors under the power supply stood partly inside the corner column at the back, and the enclosure cut the column back to make room for them. Connectors go around the mechanics, so they moved instead. The live connector and the neutral beside it sit further in, and the earth connector stands on end, as the neutral already did, clear of the column, the network jack beside it and the relay in front. Their wires follow them.
