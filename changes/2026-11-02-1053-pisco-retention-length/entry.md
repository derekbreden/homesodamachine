The PISCO cap spans Y420.346–462.050 mm, with a full 3 mm receiver end wall
before the rear port land. Its lip captures the outlet neck and its body
bearing extends toward the rear bore. The modeled clearances to the ASSE and
meter caps are 8.146 mm and 4.523 mm.

The five [retention sample pairs](../../hardware/print-tests/rear-snap-retention/v4/README.md)
include the production parent stock, complete spring pockets and axial stops.
The PISCO and ASSE samples include their rear bulkhead holes; the meter sample
includes its wire exit and rear soda bore. Mark1 is printing the pump receiver,
ASSE pair and PISCO pair; Mark2 is printing the pump cap, meter pair and water
regulator pair. Physical fit and spring behavior await this bench trial.

Derek asked: “Do we really not have room for more coverage of the pisco, more y
length on it?” and “print just enough to show those retention points please.”
