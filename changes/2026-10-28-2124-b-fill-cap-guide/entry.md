The B-fill passage uses the printed R14, Ø6.8 mm elbow geometry inside a full-height rectangular block. The cap cup carries the lower passage and three RX-M3×5.7 inserts; the lid closes the channel and takes three recessed M3 × 10 screws. Their fore centers are X−54/−33 at Y219.3, where the inlet becomes straight. The aft screw remains at X−43.5/Y242.3.

The channel crown has 0.25 mm of upward allowance for the supported printed underside. The roof retains 2.20 mm of material, the original PCB retains 1.60 mm of clearance, and the clamped cup/lid faces retain their normal 0.15 mm seating allowance. A 0.5 mm cup-mouth lead-in removes the tapered parting edges.

The block fills its complete footprint to keep narrow foam voids out of the cap. The Ø20 foam-pour opening is at X−73/Y212.6, 3 mm clear of the block. The Reed-B passage remains open through the shared stock.

The external B-fill approach uses the existing valve elbow clocked 15° east. The continuous tube replaces the two fittings buried in the cap and reaches the reservoir plate without moving the PCB.
