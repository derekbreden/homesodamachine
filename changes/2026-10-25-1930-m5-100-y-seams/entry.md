All four Y-seam stations use the same [fully threaded SVLING M5×0.8×100 socket-head screw](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0GHNNYVDH/). The lower stack retains back-bottom / front-bottom / back-bottom ownership. Its head seat rises from Z40.8 to Z60.8 while the insert stays under the Z seam.

The upper stack is front-top's roof head block, back-top's middle block and front-top's lower insert block. The head seats at Z347; the 100 mm shaft passes through the three blocks and its tip ends at Z247 inside the lower block's blind pocket. The insert mouth is Z265. Both end blocks grow from front-top's surrounding wall. The head block carries the complete rounded outer flank, with full stock below the funnel clearance and an opening into the funnel that leaves no narrow collar; tightening seats the head in that piece, and back-top's middle block retains its running clearances. Back-top's middle-block wall root continues into its roof behind the head block's running end. The sections show the west wall; the east joint has the same block sequence.

The two west flavor-tube routes pass inboard of the extended stack on R25 bends. Their low lanes rise 5 mm, with flavor A shifted inboard 7 mm and flavor B 8.5 mm before returning toward their rear rises. Valve and bulkhead connections stay in place.

The [native geometry review](https://github.com/derekbreden/homesodamachine/blob/main/hardware/printed-parts/enclosure/enclosure/y-seam-100-review.json) records valid, single-solid quadrants, clear screw passages, complete 3 mm head-bearing stock and upper blind caps, and interference-free sampled withdrawal of the local seam sections. Both heights keep the full 9.5 mm insert engagement with the specified ±0.8 mm screw-length allowance; the upper pocket retains at least 1.7 mm beyond the longest tip. These geometry readings do not establish physical rigidity, load capacity or lifetime.
