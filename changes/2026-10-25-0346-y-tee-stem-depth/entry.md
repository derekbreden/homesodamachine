The [PP0208E reference](/hardware/reference/tee-connector/README.md) was calipered on the tee in hand: a 1/4" tube bottoms 10.0 mm in from the pressed sleeve face. Both stem elbows now carry that depth, so each stem ends 5.7 mm higher inside its tee.

The elbows keep their poses, and no printed piece changes. At assembly, trim each stem to the tee's marked tube stop.
