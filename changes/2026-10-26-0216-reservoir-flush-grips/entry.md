Each 4 mm reservoir lid captures two liquid-tube spools and one air-tube spool in matching 45° countersinks. Both TPU faces sit flush with the plate. The machine uses four liquid grips and two air grips in TPU 90A.

Each reservoir has a separate straight 4 mm OD / 2.5 mm ID air tube. A printed passage through the foam-cap cup and pour lid keeps insulation off the tube; its upper end stands 6 mm above the casing. A slotted, closed-floor baffle below the reservoir lid obstructs upward splash. Filtering is deferred.

The reservoir bodies, rim gaskets, float datums and B-fill stem tip retain their geometry. Native CAD checks cover the air path and casing clearance, and the float integration check passes.
