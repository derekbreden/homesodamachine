The earlier assembly drawing sent the power and pulse wires through about 13 mm of the clip’s solid base. All three meter leads also dipped into the printed receiver. When the assembly review identified those crossings, Derek asked, “Please fix that.”

The revised routes carry the three leads over the tubes along the east wall, with at least 0.36 mm between the wire insulation and the receiver and 1.48 mm between the insulation and the removable cover in the model. Two smaller bends also clear the lid. The DC1 supply moves 1 mm aft and the J1 common moves 1 mm down.
