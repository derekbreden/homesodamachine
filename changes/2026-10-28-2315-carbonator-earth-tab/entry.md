An M3 × 12 stainless screw and captive-tooth locknut clamp the ring terminal to a 17 mm steel tab welded to the carbonator’s top plate, with the crimp barrel pointing upward. The tab’s Ø3.6 mm bolt axis passes through world X−40, Y347, Z210.05 mm. The raised axis leaves 1.15 mm between a Ø9 mm socket and the vessel rim.

The green earth lead runs from the lid’s ground junction through the existing Ø7.5 mm sensor-and-earth passage in the printed cup and lid. The steel-side ring is crimped first; its opposite end passes through the printed passage bare and receives its lid-side ring afterward. The steel attachment is tightened before the vessel enters the casing, and the connection is completed before the foam pour.
