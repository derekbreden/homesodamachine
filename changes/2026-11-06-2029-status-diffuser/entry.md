The status plate prints in black, white and translucent PETG. The window follows the LED mounting bore and is flush with both faces of the black plate. A black margin separates the window from its white ring. The frame, rule, brand mark and ON lettering share the rear nameplate's style.

The selected light is the CHANZON prewired clear-lens blue LED, with an inline resistor. The acquired EDGELEC blue LED also fits the receiver. The clear lens sends light into the printed PETG, which provides the diffusion.

Derek: “I'd like the translucency to be the same thickness as the rest of the plate.”
