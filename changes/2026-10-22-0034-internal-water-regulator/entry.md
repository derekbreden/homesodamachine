[Locate the regulator and both tubes in 3D](https://homesodamachine.com/3d#step:manifold-layout%2Fenclosure-assembly.step?find=view%3A%20az%3D126%20el%3D40%0Asolid%3A%20flow-regulator%0Asolid%3A%20tube-fluid-1%0Asolid%3A%20tube-fluid-2).

The internal [Hydronix SB-FPV-40](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B01CFOGH6O) supplies V-A's clean-water feed. The regulator rests beside the water pump, slightly inclined toward the rear, on one solid post with a six-inch tie around its fixed outlet socket. Both release collets stay exposed.

The water split keeps its seat. Two tube routes retain R14 bends and axial fitting entries, using about 121 mm more tubing in total. The flow-sensor ground lead passes in front of the regulator and above the pump; the carbonator bond wire passes below the return tube. The Wago locations stay fixed. The needle valve's wall mount is removed.

The regulator occupies the rear bay. The funnel's intended enlargement toward enclosure −Y remains open; the funnel geometry and nominal 531 mL capacity are unchanged by this work.

The [manual integration record](https://github.com/derekbreden/homesodamachine/blob/main/hardware/reference/shok-blok-regulator/installation-review.json) covers static native clearance and closing movement sampled every 2 mm. The current wiring records cover all 102 native wires and exact-solid follow-up of mesh contacts. These are nominal CAD reviews. The regulator envelope and port assignment remain provisional; acquisition and physical fit are not recorded.

Derek: “Please also keep in mind that we still have work to do to enlarge the funnel by increasing its Y axis in the Y- direction, as you consider where and how this regulator might fit *inside* the enclosure instead of the needle valve.”
