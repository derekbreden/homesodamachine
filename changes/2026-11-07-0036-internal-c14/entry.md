The inlet's flange seats in an internal pocket around the cord passage. Washer-backed screws enter blind inserts from inside, and the mounting tunnel joins the rear wall, flank and ceiling as plain stock. The outside presents the cord opening.

The inlet fits before the adjacent power supply and relays. Removing the power supply provides straight tool access to the inner flange screw during service. The install guide carries that order.

The print uses solid deposition through the tunnel and its roots, with supports excluded from the internal pocket, pilots and passage.
