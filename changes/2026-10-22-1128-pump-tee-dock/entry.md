Each pump's two straight LLDPE tubes pass front-top's 3.175 mm collet plate into four purchased John Guest PP0208E tees, one on each pump port, branch on the tube's axis and run upright. In service each tee stands at connected: its branch collet extended with its nose on the plate's aft face and the tube bottomed at the tee's own stop. Pulling the cartridge drags each tee forward its 1.5 mm collet stroke until the plate holds the collet pressed and the tube draws out; pushing the cartridge home returns each tee.

Each tee's branch lies in a tee anchor on front-top's seam plane, a half journal that ends flush with the bulkhead's aft face, and a PET-GF tee cap snaps down on top of the branch. The cap is the anchor cap's snap section, one section its whole length, printed standing on its end with nothing facing the bed.

The fore valves stand fixed over the middle of the tee's travel, so each short upper tube bends 0.75 mm each way. One R14 hairpin joins each tee's lower port to its rear valve; V-G and V-J stand 1.5 mm lower for it, and the tube anchor on back-top downstream of V-J follows. Four SB443-IN pairs in two-high columns seat the cartridge. The TPU receivers, their keepers and the PP0308E dock elbows are gone.
