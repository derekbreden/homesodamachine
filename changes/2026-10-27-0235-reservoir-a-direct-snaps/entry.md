The fore pair uses the selected 6.35 mm C2 bore, 2.40 mm spring section and an 8 mm contact span. The two seats share one full lower base, while the four upper arms remain separate.

The tubes press downward into seats on 13.5 mm centers. The draw tube follows its approved path, and the fill tube follows broad R14 bends through the wider fore lane.
