The fore and aft stations each seat both Reservoir A tubes under one shared nominal tie. Their bearings and tie webs retain 3 mm stock. The aft outer quarter is open beside the shell, and the fore tie head faces east of the dressed ground lead. The core seats from its shell footprint and stack floor; the overhanging anchors do not establish the enclosure dimensions.

The selected free curves use R14 bends through the fixed port and seat points. Their longest developed spans between held points are 192.7 mm for A-fill and 154.5 mm for A-draw. These curves and lengths illustrate routing; they are not tube-cut instructions.

The ground stack sits at X37/Y195. Fasten it before dressing the neighboring AC4-H and AC5-N leads. Its insert surround and blind backing retain their separate [native stock record](https://github.com/derekbreden/homesodamachine/blob/113ac8353065ef660437e222c5dd5bc188b99956/hardware/printed-parts/cold-core/foam-cap/ground-relocation-review.json).
