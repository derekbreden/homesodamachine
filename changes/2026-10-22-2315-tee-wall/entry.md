Front-top's bay bulkhead is one flat slab of PET-GF the bay's whole width and height, 14.321 mm from the bay face to its aft face. Its fore 3.175 mm is the collet plate. Each tee's branch slides in a teardropped Ø17 mm bore from the plate's release face out through the wall's aft face: the scanned collar's Ø16.5 mm envelope and 0.25 mm of running air. Ahead of it a teardropped Ø8.5 mm hole through the plate passes the tube and leaves the face the branch collet presses on.

The bore holds the tee in X and Z and lets it slide its 1.5 mm stroke. The wall's aft face stands 1.454 mm clear of the tee's run barrel even at released, which sets the wall's depth.

The tee anchors on the seam plane, the snap-down tee caps, the 45° underside over them and the magnet bosses are gone; the magnet slots are cut into the wall itself.

Derek: “In several places in that history, we have a much more printable thick flat wall, for the tee to be inserted into, with a counter bore hole for the tee to be inserted into, and then a smaller hole for pressing the collet. What I like about these places in the history is the fewer edges and the thicker wall. If you can see what I mean and implement it, please do so.”
