Back-top's rear wall is relieved behind the six fittings, so their nuts clamp a thinner panel. The relief ended on a level face above the rim, beside the rounded corner of the cold core's pocket. Back-top prints ceiling-down, so that face printed as a bridge in the east corner.

The relief runs out through the rim, and its face is the face of the core's pocket. The two are one plane from the rim to the ceiling, with one straight edge on the east side.

The four bulkhead nuts seat on that face. The Pisco regulator's shoulder seats on it too, so the regulator, its inlet collet, its check and the start of the gas tube stand further aft with the nuts, and the Pisco receiver and its cover follow the regulator.

Derek on the bridge in the print plan: "I would like to avoid this overhang, this pink bridge in this screenshot. I like fewer edges, and consider an opportunity to extend an edge so it becomes flush with another edge and eliminates a cavity - I consider that something I would like to do."
