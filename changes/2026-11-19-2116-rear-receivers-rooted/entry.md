The ASSE and Pisco receivers, both rear corner columns and the west flank's connector wells each take their root from the ceiling recess's own floor. The recess ends at the rear wall's face, the wall's panel relief runs up to it, and it runs round the wells' row.

The meter receiver carries a corbel under its aft shoulder, beneath the Pisco receiver. Its fore shoulder and the lip its cover's upper leaf rides over print on removable support through the open west side; the lip bridges from that support to the aft shoulder. The lane above the lip is open for the leaf to flex in.

The Pisco cover's east lower corner clears the aft corbel over its aft run, and its body runs the full fitted span beneath its shortened leaves.
