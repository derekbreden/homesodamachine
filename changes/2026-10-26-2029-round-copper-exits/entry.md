Two 7.5 mm holes carry the nominal 6.35 mm copper tails at their retained axes. The 2.38 mm wall continues above both holes, joining the broad panel to its rounded corners along the full height. Separate copper-slot covers and their seating ledges are removed.

The 282.4 × 180.4 mm casing, floor, stack planes, printed reservoir fits and cap mounting axes stay fixed. The tails feed through from inside while the core is lowered; broad bends are formed while supporting the copper, and foil collars seal the annuli before the foam pour.

The full-shell production preparation for Mark2 uses the selected faucet A2 scarf treatment with wiping off, placed on a plain long-wall span clear of the insert pockets and ports. Outer-wall motion is 100 mm/s at 1,500 mm/s². The native estimate is 31 hours 37 minutes.
