Back-top carries direct tube snaps for the reservoir-A fill line, both rear flavor lines and the vent. Their filled roots meet the host faces, and the wall-mounted fill snap has a full 45° corbel. Each wing spans the complete 8 mm along its tube.

Three cable hooks hold the DC power pair, J5 relay ribbon and positive V-K lead. The hooks have 3 mm stock and matched seats. Seven wire routes pass through them, preserving their terminal positions and the other installed wiring. J5 keeps its four-conductor web through the hook and fans out around the adjacent valve and tubing.

Derek selected C2 with the **6.35 mm bore** for present use, with smaller bores available for later tuning. The **16 mm 4P clip** fits and retains the ribbon; angling and pulling helps with entry. The 4 mm tube, DC pair and single-wire fits remain estimates.

The closely spaced reservoir fore pair retains its cap. Independent C2 clips at its 7.5 mm pitch intrude into the neighboring tube. Body restraints retain their existing roles.
