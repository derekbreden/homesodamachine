Once the front column is home, seat the continuous reservoir B draw tube in the V-H inlet. Reach through the funnel opening and press the bend toward the bulkhead. The inner and outer wings each have a split so the tube can seat one pair at a time. [Find the entry clip in the assembled machine.](https://homesodamachine.com/3d#step:manifold-layout%2Fenclosure-assembly.step?find=feature%3A%20enclosure-front-top%3A%3Avh-inlet-clip%0Aview%3A%20az%3D68%20el%3D18)

The clip prints as part of the front-top quadrant, with solid infill through the wings and their roots. [Open the Mark 1 print plan.](https://homesodamachine.com/parts#material:HSM-0079?view=print)
