The display's pocket, glass seat, PCB opening and insert pilots move with the display. The display and the plate share the facet's flat run, with equal margins at both side edges and between the two. The plate's flush seat carries pilots for the same short inserts as the cover, its screws level with the cover's.

Under the light a bore sets a blue LED back behind a small aperture, so it stays in shadow when unlit. A channel carries the LED's leads into the display's PCB pocket, where they land on the display's VIN and DOUT0, and the display lights it as it boots. The plate prints in two-colour PETG: the brand mark over a rule, the light in a ring, and ON in the nameplate's letterforms.

Derek: “I really like D. I like the simplicity of it. The machine has one job, and it is to be on. Always. And the light lets you know it's doing it.”
