WAGO-G, the 221-413 on back-top's rear wall below C14, is the machine's one earth junction. Port 1 takes the C14 earth, port 2 the carbonator's bond lead and port 3 the compressor cord's PE core. The bond lead drops from the cap passage straight into port 2; the compressor's PE core rises over the keystone jack and drops into port 3.

The M3 screw, washer, three-ring stack and heat-set insert are gone, with the boss and pilot back-top carried for them and the 16 AWG jumper that tied the stack to WAGO-G. Rings remain where an earth meets metal: under the compressor's terminal-box earth screw and on the drilled carbonator rim.
