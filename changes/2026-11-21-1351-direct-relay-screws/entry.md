A Sutemribor socket-head screw passes through each PCB corner into a ruthex heat-set insert. The inserts sit flush with the wall bearings. Either relay can be removed independently.

Each screw engages the full insert and stops short of its blind floor. The [mounting record](/hardware/printed-parts/enclosure/enclosure/relay-mount-fit.json) gives the measurements from the installed assembly and the printable wall.
