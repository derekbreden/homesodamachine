The front and aft V-G bends align at X−33. The crossover snap on the lid holds the aft run. The closest point on the seated frame is 1.90 mm above the modeled tube, and that gap opens as the frame lifts.

The Mark1 insert stayed seated in its receiver and guided the tube. Attaching the tube there would add a release step every time the frame came out. The production frame has a solid underside at that location and prints on its own.
