The fixed rails follow their three measured faces through the removable-slider
occlusions. Their transverse grooves remain open. The molded wire cover retains
its measured lower corners and ends at native X64.7 mm; the motor and rear cap
supply the rigid surface beyond it. Flexible red and green leads do not extend
that surface.

The half-pipe saddle and upper snap use the approved motor/cylinder band. The
axial stops contact the fixed rail ends. The rough middle/head underside is
excluded from mounting contact, and the clean forward tip is unused as a support.
The layout's native mount review covers the installed cradle and snap.

All 25 reference bodies are valid. On disjoint scan samples, lower-casing p95
surface distances are 0.235 and 0.169 mm for the two exposed views. These are
comparisons to the archived scans, not an absolute scanner tolerance or a
physical snap-fit result. The reference retains the independent port axes and
datum frame.
