The rear inlet follows the approved order: TAP adapter, GAGIRA coupling, downward-venting ASSE body and threaded outlet reducer. The raised tee feeds the pump inlet, water regulator and V-A. Pisco and its stem check occupy the upper gas lane; the meter connects directly to SODA.

Four integral seats and removable snap caps locate the inlet equipment. Both 4 mm flavor lines retain R25 bends; the short white vent return has R55.67 minimum curvature. The PSU mounts under the ceiling, the relays on the west wall, and V-A on its ceiling plinth. Reinforced pump hoses have modeled clamps and trimmed reducers. Water, gas and soda continue through the lid into the cold core; the two flavor lines follow their own retained routes.

The front manifold, pump retainer, funnel frame, Reservoir A anchors and B-fill assembly retain their assembly interfaces. The rear face carries six sliding identification tiles, the accepted nameplate, mains inlet and data jack.

The mechanical layout was committed and pushed before the wiring stage. The installed harness includes 101 conductors, board connector housings and the final three Wagos. Its review considers larger bodies, excludes wires and tubes as wire obstacles, and resolves local contacts at their named electrical attachments. All 120 native wiring bodies read back as valid solids, and every preparation cut covers its installed route.
