The comparison pairs the standard pointed cover with a taller planar lead-in.
The taller cover keeps the same lateral reach, retaining nose, spring walls,
roots and hardware bearings. Its matching receiver extends the flank pockets
upward into solid roof stock; both receivers retain their complete axial stops
and fitted seats.

Mark2 prints both receiver-and-cover pairs. The covers keep their ordinary
layer schedule; fine layers belong only to the tilted receivers. The plate
uses the other articles for cooling time, with no priming tower. Insertion
effort, directional tolerance and repeated release await the physical result.
