Six sensor conductors surround the green earth lead in one straight Ø7.5 mm
passage through the cup and lid at world X−48/Y347. Its mouth remains
straight to the lid face, with the exposed loom bending above it. The carbonator reeds
transfer across the open vessel head before the body foam pour. The
temperature feed reaches the suction-copper probe first and continues to
the bulk-temperature probe.

The earth lead peels off beside the cap into the lid junction at
X−53/Y339. Its steel-side closed ring clamps to the existing proud tube rim:
a radial M3 × 0.5 tapped hole takes a finished M3 × 4 screw and external-tooth
washer. The hole center is 3.5 mm above the recessed top plate, with the
complete screw-tip envelope above the finished closure weld.
