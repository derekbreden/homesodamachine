The upper JG PP0308E elbow and its upward socket move from X−48 to X−33 at Y287.5. The trimmed NeoFit stem elbow stays in its reservoir cap bore at X−43.5/Y233.3, and a direct link of about 15.6 mm of exposed tube joins the two. At X−48 the elbow ran into V-A's low under-head tube; at X−33 it clears that tube by 1.48 mm. The fill tube's tail follows the socket.

The lid carries a broad 2.4 mm cover over the upper elbow's barrel, its top at Z254.75. The barrel bears up against it, and it leaves 0.25 mm nominal air to the PCB's solder tails. The lower fitting's cover keeps 3.05 mm. In the cup, one rectangular corridor joins the two fitting pockets around the link; it removes 336.7 mm³ and adds no stock.

Fit both fittings and the link in the open cup before the lid closes, and mask the pockets and the link for the pour. The elbow sits in the straight entry path to J10's V12 terminal, so both J10 leads are landed on the loose PCB before it is fastened.
