The shared compressor cable runs below the funnel frame, between the existing
valve banks and into the compressor bay. Its three insulated cores keep their
spacing through the bends. Fifteen neighboring wire paths are dressed clear of
the cable. The hardware arrangement is retained.

The compressor earth uses the downward-facing ring lug. The thermal fuse's
reference shows only its first 3 mm of straight lead; the installed model also
carries the dressed leads to two insulated splice regions. The short hot link
from the fuse passes below the neutral and earth fanout. Estimated donor
landings sit beside the retained power-cover envelope.

The modeled longest core is approximately 529 mm. Preparation stock is 600 mm,
with the ends trimmed after routing. The two dressed fuse leads fit within the
reference's 35 mm and 20 mm lengths.

The [compressor-cable review](https://github.com/derekbreden/homesodamachine/blob/main/hardware/wiring/installed/compressor-review.json)
records valid native bodies, separate cores, and no hardware or neighboring-wire
intersections for this cable and its fuse link. These are proposed installed
arrangements using the model's dimensional allowances.
