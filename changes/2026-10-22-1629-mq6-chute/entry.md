The MQ-6 card drops into two grooved posts on the floor, and those posts are what place it. It was also set west until its can bottomed on the wall, through a 3 mm chute cut into the flank's inner skin, and the vent grooves beside that chute were held solid so no thin strip stood between the two openings. The compressor stood 10.8 mm east of the card.

The card now stands one clearance floor, 1 mm, off the flank face. The flank is one plane behind it and the grooves vent like the rest.
