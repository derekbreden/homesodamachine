The full-length four-bar Pisco cover stayed engaged when Derek pressed beside the regulator outlet in the original rear quadrant. He attributed the remaining movement to the wing bending. After swapping the two-bar cover onto the same regulator, he felt “a tiny, tiny amount” more play. The production Pisco has the same wings, hooks and saddle, with the four trial identification grooves filled.
