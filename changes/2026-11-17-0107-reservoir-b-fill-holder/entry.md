The rectangular top prints with the bend holder on its flat back. The lid
lowers over it after the foam cures. Foil tape closes the seam around the
holder, which stays with the continuous fill tube when the lid is lifted.

[Find the holder in the installed model](https://homesodamachine.com/parts#step:manifold-layout%2Fenclosure-assembly.step?find=solid%3A%20cold-core%2Freservoir-b-fill-guide).
