The fill tube seat's free upper jaw moves away from the corbelled lower jaw.
Its inner curve and rounded nose move together, leaving the lower jaw and
corbel at their fitted positions.

The [Mark1 fit print](https://github.com/derekbreden/homesodamachine/blob/f15bb9b4dbab8cd2046ea8e1e8e08d13539381d2/hardware/print-tests/tube-snap-corbel/prints/2026-11-20-mark1-upper-wing-support/print-job.json)
uses removable contoured strips under the upper jaw. Their breakaway feet land
on the lower jaw, and the tube mouth admits a screwdriver beneath the strips.
