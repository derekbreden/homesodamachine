The rear nameplate's two upper insert hosts dip into the lid's rear edge, and the casing clears each with a recess. In the hollow shell a wall closes that recess off from the foam. The same wall was added to the solid lid, where all it did was refill a strip of the rear middle screw's head pocket. The strip left a narrow sliver face on the pocket's round, and it stood where that screw's head seats.

The lid now takes each host's plain recess. The one beside the rear middle screw stands closer to the screw's slot than a wall, so the two open into each other: one pocket on the head's floor, in as far as the screw's axis, where its wall meets the pocket's round square. The screw's head has its whole seat. The pump cap's spring wings end in pointed tips that dip into shallow pockets in the lid's face. The east pocket's aft corner stood so near Reservoir A's air passage that a thin web of lid was left between them. The pocket's floor now runs out to the passage's axis and, behind the cap, aft to it, so both of its walls end square on the passage.
