Each motor seats in its fitted cylindrical guide, with local tracks for the landed terminals. Fixed stops above the flange and a screw-fastened retainer beneath it capture each pump independently. Continuous stock separates their loading openings.

The outlet loading rooms have sloped roofs. Each retainer has broad screw seats and prints with its bearing face on the bed. The motor-side scan defect is replaced by the independently fitted barrel surface.

[See the installed pumps and retainers in the complete machine](https://homesodamachine.com/3d#step:manifold-layout%2Fenclosure-assembly.step?find=solid%3A%20enclosure-pump-retainer-a%0Asolid%3A%20enclosure-pump-retainer-b%0Asolid%3A%20pump-a-motor%0Asolid%3A%20pump-b-motor%0Aview%3A%20az%3D-70%20el%3D15). The [geometry record](https://github.com/derekbreden/homesodamachine/blob/main/hardware/printed-parts/enclosure/enclosure/pump-clamp-fit.json) contains the insertion samples, guide and tool clearances, and underside-area comparison. The assembly guide and parts ledger describe the independent clamps. Physical fit and retention remain to be tried on the printed parts.
