3 mm-rise planar lead-in above the 1.6 mm tool-backed nose. Their matching
receiver ceilings clear the complete points by 0.5 mm. The cartridge's lower
barb uses one 4.6 mm planar lead-in to its retaining corner. Its receiver roof

The 2 mm leaves, rounded roots, catch overlap and centered screwdriver access
remain part of every interface. All caps print on their axial ends. The full
enclosure, cold-core lid, seated cartridge retainer and installation views
carry the matching geometry.

clock. Its three lower-facing planes have 0.25 mm additional vertical seating
allowance. Local corner clearance follows those flats through the hex band;
the upper planes and cylindrical neck seats retain their fits. Both axial
shoulders still retain the adapter, independently of the ASSE's taped joint.
